## Role descriptions (handed down from Spokes 2026)
Here are the roles Spokes 2026 had and their rough responsibilities, as well as recommendations for roles. The bracketed number is the recommended number of people in that role. Roles generally become kinda flexible because of how small the team is, but don't be afraid to give people firm responsibilities.

**Fundraising [2]**
- Track fundraising totals on spreadsheet
- Make approximate timeline for fundraising goals
- Organize fundraising events (bike raffle, bake sale)
- Make email drafts for fundraising
- Meet sometimes outside of weekly meetings to discuss progress and what should be done
- Keep track of grant deadlines and delegate work
- Keeps others accountable on fundraising responsibilities
- Set up crowdfunding site with MIT
- Design and execute campaigns

**Finances & Budget [2-4]**
Note: This role was assigned to two people but it is also the full responsibility of the 4 card holders. I would have 2 of the card holders automatically be the Finances team.
- Acquire travel and pro cards
- Make sure tax exemption forms are up to date
- Keep track of receipts, purchasing before and during trip
- Communicate with Edgerton Center when they ask about purchases
- Communicates with Gear team to purchase gear, and Learning Festival developers to purchase their supplies
- Make itemized budget
- Keep track of spending during trip
- Finalize spending report at end of trip
- Makes decision on purchases to ensure team stays within budget

**Edgerton Contact [1]**
- Basically the go-to person for answering Edgerton's emails
- Can delegate responsibility to other team member if it makes sense

**Car Rental [1]**
- Communicate with Edgerton Center to rent car
- Make sure background checks, other necessary documents are filled out
- Help to handle reporting in case of accident or damage during the trip

**Route Planning [3]**
- Plan the overall route, taking the group's requirements and preferences into mind
- Resolves route issues during the trip as needed
- Makes the daily route for the trip
- Marks out water and lunch stops
- Uses Strava, RideWithGPS, Google Maps for route

**Learning Festivals [2]**
- Make timeline for LF development during the semester
- Checks in with people to see if they need assistance making their LF activities
- Communicate with Route Planning to finalize festivals and dates
- Communicates with LF hosts

**Housing [3]**
- Communicates with MIT alum, WarmShowers hosts, churches to acquire housing
- Books campgrounds

**Social Media [2]**
- Runs social media!
- Makes posters and other graphics
- Communicates with Sonny Oram, Edgerton Communications Officer
- Design Spokes t-shirts and jerseys

**Gear [2]**
Note: it probably makes sense for a gear person to hold the pro card (card to purchase stuff not related to travel). We had one person but you could have like one gear person and then one gear person who is also on finances.
- Creates list of gear needed (bike gear, camping gear, etc)
- Communicates with pro-card holders to purchase gear during the semester
- Communicates with Edgerton and MIT to purchase bikes

**Website [1]**
- Updates website
- Posts sponsor graphics
- Handles website purchasing/subscription as needed

**Meeting Moderator and Notetaker [1]**
- Keeps their eye on the clock during weekly meetings
- Ensures agenda items are discussed
- Sends out agenda
- Main notetaker during weekly meetings

**Training [1]**
- Sets training plan and holds training events

## What happened (from the meeting transcript)
_Summarized from the audio transcript (speakers weren't labeled by name, so most points aren't attributed). The raw transcript is kept outside the repo at `~/spokes_sources/meetings/2026-11-22_first-meeting_transcript.txt`._

**Big picture**
- Main takeaway from the Nov 15 transition meeting: "it's a lot of work." Priorities before Thanksgiving: **fundraising first**, then bikes.
- Roles were picked by going role by role and raising hands. Where too many people signed up, people trimmed their load to about 3–4 roles each (final table above). "Meeting coordinator" is a light role.
- **New role created at this meeting: Food coordinator.** No past team had one. Past teams bought bulk snacks (e.g. a big tub of trail mix), and the van driver called restaurants a day ahead asking for free meals.
- **Each subgroup should have one named lead** for outside contacts, because hosts and LF partners get confused when the person emailing them changes. Idea: split the trip into sections (e.g. quarters), each owned by one person.
- Route planning/housing kept all 4 volunteers. On the trip, the 2026 route team looked ahead every night, and the van driver of the day has time to handle hosts.

**Edgerton**
- Gloria (joining remotely) leads replies to Marvelin and is the Edgerton contact and car-rental lead. Those should be the same person. Car rentals go through Edgerton, not the team's cards. A member said Edgerton doesn't care about drivers being under 21.
- Card holders and finance people should also be at the first Edgerton meeting, and others can join by Zoom. Justin Schmidt is expected to explain the rules at that meeting.
- **This year every Edgerton team needs a safety officer, and maybe a safety report.** Members linked it to a Rocket Team lab incident over the summer. One member is already safety officer for another club and offered to do it.
- Open question: does every sponsorship need Edgerton approval of a sponsorship packet first? Some members said clubs usually skip approval because MIT is slow. **Ask the 2026 team and Edgerton before acting.** Also check before collecting any money outside the Spokes cost object. 2026 also asked Edgerton about its Venmo policy.

**Fundraising**
- **School of Engineering:** Michelle has been talking to SoE communications about MIT's "MIT for America" / "Curiosity with a mission" campaigns, pitching that Spokes goes to rural America. SoE said it would reply after Thanksgiving (see `email/2027/2026-11-23_mit-spokes-soe-mit-for-america.md`).
- **Departments:** divide and conquer by major, with each person emailing their own department ("I am a Course X major") before the end of the semester. Team majors: 6-4 (×2), 6-2, 6-3/2A, 2, 2/11, 8, 11/14.
  - Members said **Course 6 "does not like to give money."**
  - **Course 2 has a funding form only Course 2 students can fill out.** It runs per semester, so plan it for spring.
- **Giving Tuesday (Dec 1):** frame it as "celebrate the giving season by giving to our cause." Point people to the Spokes crowdfunding page, not the general Edgerton fund.
- **Pi Day 24-Hour Challenge:** members disagreed. One said teams "aren't allowed to do 24-hour challenges," and another said "we definitely have to do that." Past teams (2024, 2026) did take part. **Confirm with Edgerton.**
- **Employer matching:** one member's future employer triples employee donations to causes. They will ask whether it applies before their start date.
- **Sponsorship ambitions:** Red Bull (helmets), sunscreen brands, food/nutrition brands (list at the end of the doc). Start social media before the trip so it can be used to win free products.
- **Event ideas:** pledge-per-mile bike-a-thon (donors pledge $ per mile; a member raised ~$5 per lap in a past event), raffles.
- The fundraising subgroup should meet before break and build a company list.

**Food**
- The team wants to **eat healthier than 2026** (fewer sugary drinks and less fast food) and is OK spending a bit more per day, or fundraising for it. Cooking came up and Max is keen, though past teams advised against cooking.

**Gear, bikes and van**
- Want **bikes earlier than 2026** (2026 bought around spring break), but riding outside is limited in Feb–Mar. Option: get fitted before IAP and order at the end.
- The 2026 Trek bike looks like the right choice. Size fittings are at Trek (Alewife; reachable by Red Line). The PT fit comes after you have the bike.
- Gear team will call Trek this week and reply to Trek and Cambridge Bicycle.
- **Van:** interest in a bigger cargo/passenger van (more room, hitch rack), with worries about city driving and parking in San Francisco. Edgerton handles the rental contract. To discuss more.

**Training and health**
- The team wants real training, including injury prevention and long-term joint/knee health. Some 2026 riders didn't train much, and parents are worried about knees.
- A member is in touch with the bike-fit PT and has a nutrition contact.

**Logistics and communication**
- **WhatsApp community with sub-channels per team** (it handles bad rural signal better than iMessage). Use MIT emails. A shared contact doc lists everyone's phone and email.
- **Shared Spokes Google Calendar** for all deadlines.
- **Group photo:** Killian Court with bikes, **Mon Nov 23, 9:00 AM**, jeans + MIT shirt, for the crowdfunding page before Dec 1. Retake in spring with the real bikes and uniforms.
- **Meetings:** weekly; Zoom over winter break, since members will be in several countries.
