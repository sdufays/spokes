# Tonight was the launch party of Spokes! Friends...

_Tonight was the launch party of Spokes! Friends and family from the Bay area and much further away go together at San Francisco’s Crissy Field for introductions to the team, funny jokes and big hugs._

_Tomorrow, we will teach our second class in Oakland. When we finish, we will take the ferry from San Francisco to Vallejo, to finally depart on our trip!_

_On Thursday, we teach our third Learning Festival in Sacramento. Stay tuned for more information on our classes!_
