# So. many. degrees. !!!

[So. many. degrees. !!!](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/54354043035/so-many-degrees "So. many. degrees. !!!")

While on the road, it’s a bit tough some days to keep up with news in the ‘outside world’ due to the limited reception that we have along certain parts of our route. One headline that we missed, but that most certainly didn’t come as a surprise, was that a heat wave has been sweeping the west coast over the past several days. We’ve been absolutely loving the gorgeous scenery in Utah, but wow. It is HOT. On Saturday, we had one of our shortest rides in awhile: 50 miles, from Hanksville, UT to Hite, UT (near the Colorado river).

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/88c1971aea5e0d141e5036b63fc0dc1f/tumblr_inline_mp9n1z6cvq1qz4rgp.jpg)

We had a bit of a leisurely morning, talking and hanging out with Dan and Pat, some new friends from UConn Medical school who we bumped into who are also riding xc (<http://coasttocoastforacure2013.blogspot.com/>), 

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/bc2f0b2ce0f7f0d4face1e8183634bd1/tumblr_inline_mp9ncntAgE1qz4rgp.jpg)

and when we left, I think we all kind of shrugged of the route as trivial. We thought we’d be swimming in the river in nooo time.

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/a29e84400734e6562255b8f9e6c7be68/tumblr_inline_mp9nfmpTyP1qz4rgp.png)

'twas not the case. :P It hit 108 degrees before noon, and other than the few millimeters of shade at the base of the canyons, it was just us and the sun. It usually takes a whole lot of convincing to get me off my bike to take a break, but that day I was literally having to force myself to let 5 miles pass (on my new odometer! Having lots of fun trying to hit 50mph with that - sorry mum ;)) before taking another break (and a giant swig of water). By the time we arrived at Hite, we were all absolutely exhausted (and starving - hellllooooo poptarts), and crashed on a couch in the convenience store at the recreational area. We were 80 miles away and thousands of feet below the next mark of civilization, and were a bit concerned about trying to make the ride in the sun the next day. The solution? Night riding! The store closed at 3:30pm, so the Spokes team, along with Pat and Dan, found a bit of shade where we settled until 1am.

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/9830e41ca12b9da80ec4c62bec905e73/tumblr_inline_mp9mqvQ64e1qz4rgp.jpg)

We managed to get pretty comfy despite the heat :) 

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/799fd4cf9c18141147c07d5d43c7feee/tumblr_inline_mp9mshVl5H1qz4rgp.jpg)

…and we most definitely didn’t go hungry! Ethan and Titiaan prepared some awesome fried rice, I prepared some “iced” (aka very, very room temperature) mochas to get everyone moving in the middle of the nights, and Ethan also prepared a new favorite of mine: cinnamon raisin pancakes :) We also had some Jack Johnson playing for atmosphere. Who knew being semi-stranded in the heat could be so much fun?

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/9be60550ea12f562fb376729bc1ca5a0/tumblr_inline_mp9nm1rpXw1qz4rgp.jpg)

There was another group who we met also going xc, but East -> West. One girl was biking by herself (!), and had been joined by some of her friends for a couple of days who were doing the trip in a car. Turns out you can network in the middle of nowhere - Sam, one of the guys in that group, is headed to Stanford business school to work on renewable energy, so he and Titiaan had a lot to talk about! 

At 2am, our army of bikes set out on the journey to Blanding, UT. The cool thing about riding in the dark is that you really can’t tell if you’re on a hill or not, so the elevation gains in the first part of the ride were a piece of cake!

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/25620442c36f66f0c955baf573c50350/tumblr_inline_mp9nu4cZ2d1qz4rgp.jpg)

We were a bit deceived by the maps though, as we thought that the route was a steady climb until mile 50, downhill from 50-60, and a bit more of a climb from 60-80. The “bit” of a climb at the end was actually the steepest one that we’ve encountered yet, and it really just seemed to go on and on. Needless to say, we were happy to get to Blanding. Pat and Dan very kindly offered to let us use their motel’s pool, and we all agreed that felt like a little bit of heaven. :) 

Today is a big one for the team: 

  * 3 week anniversary of first meeting up
  * State crossing from UT to CO (lots of teaching and jerseys coming up!)
  * Claire’s birthday month (the countdown has begun ;))
  * NATHAN’S FIRST AWESOME VIDEO IS READYYYYY!
