# California & Nevada in 10 days!

[California & Nevada in 10 days!](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/53553271455/california-nevada-in-10-days "California & Nevada in 10 days!")

10 days ago, we taught our first learning festival and hosted our launch party.

10 days later, we have crossed the entire states of California and Nevada!

The journey so far has been epic: early morning sunrise (getting up every day at 05.00am), free ice cream offered by new-made friends and showers in public park sprinklers. 

To give you a little taste for life on the road, enjoy the time-lapse video above!
