# Yesterday was our first day of riding! Turner,...

Yesterday was our first day of riding! Turner, Ethan and Titiaan drove from San Francisco’s Mojo Bicycle Café across Golden Gate Bridge into Marin County. 

The ride was gorgeous: sunlight shone through the Eucalyptus trees in the Presidio; we rode across the Golden Gate Bridge in the fog, with loud fog horns below us, resembling the sound of whales; we climbed 1,000 feet up Conzelman Road to find an amazing view of San Francisco and the Bay.

Standing on top of a big hill, enjoying the view of the city clouded in fog, we had our first ideas: why don’t we make a book on this journey explaining all the natural phenomena we come across. Chapter 1: how do clouds form?

Our second idea was slightly crazier: _can we make the Bay area the world’s largest discotheque?_ With a giant stroboscope on one of the hills, and the ocean tankers honking, this would be a gigantic project. Ethan put it nicely: “This is the biggest smoke machine in the world”. 

This morning, Turner and Titiaan will cycle to Sausalito to meet with John O'Neil, board member of [The Noyce Foundation](http://www.noycefdn.org/). By tonight, most of the team will be in town: Kaitlyn and Nathan are coming up from Santa Cruz; Claire is flying in from Boston. 

May this be an epic journey!
