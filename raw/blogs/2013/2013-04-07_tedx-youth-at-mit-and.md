# TEDx Youth at MIT! and...

[TEDx Youth at MIT! and…](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/47394245946/tedx-youth-at-mit-and "TEDx Youth at MIT! and…")

Turner’s birthday - what an awesome day!

[TEDx Youth Charles River 2013](http://www.ted.com/tedx/events/6604) was held in MIT’s Stata Center this Sunday, April 7th. One of the speakers was [Romi Kadri](https://twitter.com/romikadri), who introduced the Spokes team to [Free Flow bikes, UK](http://www.freeflowbikes.com/), and we couldn’t thank him more: Free Flow is donating bikes to the Spokes team for our trip this summer! 

![image](http://38.media.tumblr.com/a43b8550ca0bda732559491c78e30b4c/tumblr_inline_mkwessukPY1qz4rgp.png)

My personal takeaways from the speakers at this awesome event: 

[Delian A.](https://twitter.com/MITDelian):

MIT sophomore ; [Thiel 20 under 20 finalist ](http://www.thielfellowship.org/); always wears one black and one white shoe :)

“The Magical Bracelet” that monitors blood sugar levels and correspondingly administer insulin ; [Jerry the teddy bear](http://news.medill.northwestern.edu/chicago/news.aspx?id=185320) for diabetic children who the children feel can empathize with their condition ; medication adherence (taking as much medicine as one is prescribed when they are supposed to). Delian’s final message: _**the only way to drive innovations like this in healthcare is for consumers to demand it**_. 

![image](http://31.media.tumblr.com/5971edbbb4d384d1d371b8adcb6d59ba/tumblr_inline_mkwg7lihFI1qz4rgp.jpg)

[Divya Srinivasan](https://twitter.com/divyasrinivasan): 

Founder of [MIT’s GlobeMed Chapter](http://globemed.org/impact/mit/) ; featured in [WBUR-NPR](http://www.wbur.org/), [USA Today](http://www.usatoday.com/), and [The Boston Globe](http://www.bostonglobe.com/) for spurring the mental health task force at MIT ; a cappella singer, runner and dancer. What doesn’t she do?!

The importance of fostering a supportive environment in order to battle mental health disorders on college campuses across the country. Divya left the audience only after sealing a verbal pact with us to tell our family, friends, spouses, and even people we don’t know that we’re here to listen and support. _**Incredible people care about carrying happiness into the future. :)**_

_**![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/c6cd7362bc646997732507f0613d83db/tumblr_inline_mkwgyu2WHi1qz4rgp.jpg)**_

[Sivakami Sambasivam](https://twitter.com/ssivakami): 

![image](http://31.media.tumblr.com/6afaee9eed2ea040b108c5ad060f4cf4/tumblr_inline_mkwk01uXV41qz4rgp.jpg)

Starting with an image of premade cakes at an ice cream shop, Siv made the comparison between the cakes available to buy in an ice cream shop and the jobs featured on career fair lists. Her awe-inspiring talk left the audience pondering the question:  _**“will you order one of the premade cakes, design your own, or go out there and buy your own ingredients?”**_

![image](http://38.media.tumblr.com/a5ce5548d8785fc9759b1378258ace69/tumblr_inline_mkwjvljIO81qz4rgp.png)

[Kevin Rustagi](http://www.linkedin.com/in/kevinrustagi): 

Kevin, cofounder of Kickstarter ultra-success [Ministry of Supply](http://ministryofsupply.com/), focused on sharing ideas: the world is headed toward a point in time when it will be one giant brain, with ideas elegantly connected and what’s now called “small talk” generating the big ideas of tomorrow. “We are all connected - sharing allows us to think _inside_ of someone else.”

The process of innovating and sharing?

**idea (spark) – share (in a trusted circle) – prototype (tell a few more people) – ask questions (but not of many people! Who likes this, and who thinks it’s interesting?) – test (put it out there – lots of people!)**

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/df7c5cbeaf8fae050f7099c26bd235b8/tumblr_inline_mkwl0n0q5l1qz4rgp.jpg)

[Romi Kadri](https://twitter.com/romikadri): 

_**“… focused on remaining creative and adventurous as he applies his network and experience to drive the world’s most valuable, high-impact ideas to market.”**_

…aka the guy who helped [Spokes](http://spokesamerica.org) find our bikes - thank you so much, Romi! 

Romi discussed a disconnect between people with true artistic talent, and people who want to bring products and ideas to market. He brought a whole new definition to the “quality v. quantity” dichotomy - some work their entire lives to create something of high quality, but never quite get it out to the world; others, meanwhile, try idea after idea, in hopes that one will work out. 

Romi definined quality as: 

  1. **Unique**
  2. **Meaningful**
  3. **Experiential**



![image](http://38.media.tumblr.com/caa603b621c89659e478f98198cc3226/tumblr_inline_mkwihqA90C1qz4rgp.jpg)

[Samantha Smith](https://twitter.com/samantha_smith): 

Designer of her own major in government, human nature, and innovation; co-founder of Startup Booster, a company working to grow entrepreneurship in Hungary; co-author of an untitled multi-media book & brand that introduces teens to the topic of major world issues

_**Remove the self from systems analysis**_

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/a8fb19cb668a3a2cc368385dcddb7f71/tumblr_inline_mkwm2o6t2u1qz4rgp.jpg)

[Ian Cinnamon](https://twitter.com/IanCinnamon): 

Author of programming textbook for Mcgraw-Hill (before starting college!) ; creator the 22nd best selling iPhone app ; Stanford bound bundle of awesomeness! 

**Enjoy the game of life, and _love what you do_. **

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/701b588de035245b260409d2e750a86f/tumblr_inline_mkwl5kvETQ1qz4rgp.jpg)

What an amazing collection of people to have in one place!
