# A few days ago we met Jonah, a 19 year...

[ ![

A few days ago we met Jonah, a 19 year old walking across the country! A couple of us stopped on the side of the road to talk for awhile and hear about his incredible journey, and he played his own version of Bob Marley’s Jammin’ for us. He’s going east -&gt; west, so just Nevada and California remain! He’s looking to be the youngest person in history to complete the walking “grand slam,&ldquo; which consists of the Transamerica, Pacific Crest, Appalachian, and Continental Divide trails. We wish him the absolute best of luck, and hope to see him at our reception dinner in Washington, D.C. in September! :\)
