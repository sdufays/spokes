# Official Spokes gear - thank you, edX!

[Official Spokes gear - thank you, edX!](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/54034491618/official-spokes-gear-thank-you-edx "Official Spokes gear - thank you, edX!")

Today, we made the order for our official Spokes gear! 16 days into our trip, we certainly feel like a team, and now we can look the part, too. :) A big thanks to our partners at [edX](https://www.edx.org/) for funding our team apparel! Look out for a post soon about the work that we’re doing with them, and how Spokes students across the country are benefiting from edX’s open source platform. ![image](http://38.media.tumblr.com/3a42b3fae8dcd850d3369eb15f75426e/tumblr_inline_mp2g4rUnpS1qz4rgp.png)
