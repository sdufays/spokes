# Spokes 2014 and What's Driving Me

[Spokes 2014 and What’s Driving Me](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/81126448432/spokes-2014-and-whats-driving-me "Spokes 2014 and What’s Driving Me")

Spokes America is back in action for the summer of 2014. Over the course of the coming months, we’ll use this blog as a log of our thoughts and goals leading up to our trip from San Francisco to Washington, D.C. as well as a record of our successes once we finally hit the road. I hope you’ll follow us on the journey by reading along.

![](http://31.media.tumblr.com/902049c808ea73ea3ddeb38794c894d2/tumblr_inline_n383c5hJIW1s3e7lq.jpg)

As we approach the beginning of April, it struck me earlier today just how quickly the beginning of Spokes’ epic cross-country teaching adventure is approaching. During the school year, the summer always seems far away. Between now and when we put our tires to the road for the first time, we all have a lot to do.

The last few weeks are been pretty crazy for me, balancing everything that’s going in my classes with my rowing and making sure that I’m keeping my fundraising responsibilities for Spokes on track. I finally had a moment yesterday, while I was working on a draft of pitch information, to take a deep breathe and reflect on why making Spokes a reality is so important to me.

Spokes, or any kind of bike trip of this length, is going to be physically taxing. I expect it to push me far outside my comfort zone. 4000 miles is a long way—it feels like a long way when I’ve made the trip by plane from home in New York to the west coast, and I’m sure it’s going to feel like a _very_ long trip as we push the pedals from San Francisco to Washington, D.C. I see two-fold rewards from this challenge.

To start, Spokes is going to do a whole lot of good. I was lucky to have access to great educational resources growing up, and I didn’t know how awesome STEM fields were until I started to visit colleges and saw what an engineering lab looked like and the real change that engineers and scientists create in the world. By offering students an earlier insight into science and tech, I believe that Spokes has the power to change students’ lives. That gets me really excited.

More personally, I’m looking forward to get in touch with the parts of my home country that I have never before experienced. I’m a coastal kid. I was born and raised in New York, go to school in New England, and have made a few visits to the West Coast and Florida. I’ve never cracked into the interior of America, to see the vistas that drove development in the United States and America’s most famous landscapes. I hope that I’ll complete the trip with a new perspective on what it means to be American and what this country is really about.

As I continue to work with Jamin, Caleb, Evan, Cali, Maddie, and Emma to make Spokes a reality, these goals will be in sharp relief in my mind. I’m going to use them to keep me motivated, and I hope that they can inspire you to keep in touch with us on our journey and support our cause. Go Spokes
