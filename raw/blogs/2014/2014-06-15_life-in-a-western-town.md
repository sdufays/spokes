# Life in a Western Town...

[Life in a Western Town…](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/88873934740/life-in-a-western-town "Life in a Western Town…")

Good morning from Carson City, NV! I’d like to send a big thank you to all of you who are following our adventures and continuing to support us! It means a lot to us to know there are people out there curious to know what’s going on and enjoying our posts. We’d also like to thank TI for putting up a post about us on their company blog, which you can read [here](http://e2e.ti.com/blogs_/b/thinkinnovate/archive/2014/06/10/college-students-bike-across-america-to-get-kids-excited-about-engineering.aspx). 

We’re currently taking a rest day before we do our first teaching workshop tomorrow at the local library. Its a beautiful sunny day, not too hot yet, and very relaxing. We’re staying at an RV campground so we look a little out of place with bikes instead of a camper, but that hasn’t stopped us from enjoying the showers, the Wi-Fi and, most importantly, the jacuzzi! 

While I’m chilling here, I’ll post some random photos and share some anecdotes. 

In this one, we’re in San Francisco staying with Caroline Wang and her family, who were kind enough to host us in their backyard for a couple days so we could make our final preparations. Those preparations included, among other things, setting Jamin’s bike seat at  _just_ the right angle for riding. :) 

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/6ff4c2212ea426a550a90a816e5bd8db/tumblr_inline_n781c3BGKn1s3e7lq.jpg)

Days 3, 4 and 5 were a lot of hill climbing to get up and over the Sierras. Sam was just truckin’ through it all and for some reason was still smiling at 5000 feet. This man likes pain. We quickly learned to beat the heat by getting up at 4 AM and finishing our ride by 1 PM. Except its freezing at 4 AM so now I’ve also discovered that its possible to still ride a bike while wearing every article of clothing I packed for this trip. ![](http://31.media.tumblr.com/271e65ee5d24ac3bad26b5c45bec56ea/tumblr_inline_n781exQqxo1s3e7lq.jpg)

One morning as we were leaving camp, we came across this helicopter taking off from a helipad, rising up against the side of a rocky cliff, and lifting up a telephone post with a winch. It started to fly away with the telephone post dangling 50 feet below. It was pretty uplifting. We spent a long time after that arguing about the physics of flying around with heavy poles on pendulums; we concluded that the pilot would have to be always accelerating to keep the helicopter ahead of the pole, and that when he did decelerate, he would have to be ready to compensate for the mass of the pole swinging in front of the helicopter. Other thoughts?![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/33afcd91e36600d898877ea5058a403e/tumblr_inline_n781ilHUpD1s3e7lq.jpg)

And finally, yesterday we passed the California-Nevada border. Its pretty incredible to think we’ve already finished our first state. At the border I learned that planking is not just an ab workout. Soon after crossing over, we passed through Genoa, which, as the sign below says, is Nevada’s oldest settlement. And, as we learned from Dennis and Jeremiah at Bike Habitat in Carson City, it also has Nevada’s oldest bar, famous for hosting musicians like Johnny Cash and Willie Nelson - what? 

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/46c9023219b08a7cfb63bd04ac3f6cfd/tumblr_inline_n781wdlXQ41s3e7lq.jpg)

Stay tuned for more, and don’t forget to follow us on Twitter and check out our Facebook page!
