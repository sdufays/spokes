# Kansas!

[Kansas!](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/92084197995/kansas "Kansas!")

Hello everyone - Emma here! This is a fast update from the team, halfway through northern Kansas. We’re currently in Seneca, Kansas, enjoying a cup of coffee on Main Street - but they’re closing in 5 minutes so I have to type fast. 

Sam covered most of our adventures getting into Kansas, and since then, its been pretty much the same. Every day, we get up, we get on our bikes, and we ride through cornfields. Kansas’ scenery isn’t quite as varying as the West, but it’s great for introspection. The open horizons remind me of the ocean, and it leaves your mind free to wander. Maddie asked me once what I thought about on the bike - my answer is pretty much everything: what I’m going to do in the future, past life events, food I want to eat…and then when I’m out of things to think about, I sing. 

Kansas itself is a nice place; I’ve gotten more waves here than anywhere else. I’ve also encountered fewer speeding drivers and even the trucks seem friendlier. I wonder if being such a heavy farming community makes the residents more amicable and welcoming because that’s how agricultural communities thrive - through cooperation. I also wonder if living in a place that is more crop fields than residential gives you plenty of space to be with your own thoughts, so that when you are with others, you are more ready to enjoy their company? At least that’s how I’ve experienced this landscape.

Anyway, its closing time, so more to come!
