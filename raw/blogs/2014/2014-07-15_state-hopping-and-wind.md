# State Hopping and Wind

[State Hopping and Wind](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/91799701825/state-hopping-and-wind "State Hopping and Wind")

Good evening from St. Francis, KS! Team Spokes had its longest ride of the trip thus far today, 120 miles to journey from Brush! (sic), CO all the way here to the northwest corner of Kansas.

Today’s ride looked a little monotonous. Most of the ride followed US-34 in Northeastern Colorado, a road bordered mostly by various cattle farms and populated by large trucks carrying apparently “fragrant” livestock. I say “looked” and “apparently,” because, continuing with a kind of tradition, I was behind the wheel today for yet another border-crossing day. 

The day’s ride actually added in a surprise state. We were far enough north in Colorado that a route that clipped the southwestern corner of Nebraska was the same length as a route that skipped it altogether. 

Here’s the team at the Nebraska border: 

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/7e30100cc50dc7c6f0deb0e240bee4f1/tumblr_inline_n8qb3kC7wR1s3e7lq.jpg)

Feeling like an excited kid as I set the timer on the camera and saw the approaching train, I made the “blow your whistle” hand-motion to the conductor. Lo and behold, our border crossing got to be accompanied by the awesome whistle of a large freight-train. That was pretty fantastic.

Remember, of course, that we did end up in Kansas today—St. Francis, KS, which is here: 

![image](http://38.media.tumblr.com/0cbf5906da5af3703af03ae6d9360351/tumblr_inline_n8qb7t8aql1s3e7lq.jpg)

Basically, we planned our route so that we would get 10 or so miles in Nebraska and then head on our merry way south to Kansas, where we have about 400 miles of more or less straight east-west riding to do this week. At our second crossing of the day, we stopped for another customary photo…

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/8c613f64fc5a3747d5b3ae982d266cb0/tumblr_inline_n8qbasZNPV1s3e7lq.jpg)

…and, just because I felt like the car and I were forming a special bond, I asked Jamin to snap a pic of me and the Moth, as our friendly black minivan has been christened. The Moth and I have now crossed the California-Nevada, Nevada-Utah, Colorado-Nebraska, and Nebraska-Kansas borders together. Some people just have all the driving rotation luck!

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/fe78ce425682e6862ff9f0b019240350/tumblr_inline_n8qbdjRGmp1s3e7lq.jpg)

After a successful stop at the Loveland, Colorado Library, we’ve been happy to get back on the road for some more real riding. We’re all looking forward to setting up our booth and attracting lots of passers-by at the Amelia Earhart Festival in Atchison, KS later this week. 

Thanks for reading and keep on following along!

Roll tires,

Sam & Team Spokes
