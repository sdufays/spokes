# Day 45: A GOATed Day

Wednesday, July 17th, 2024

Gunnison, CO 

0 miles, 0 ft elevation

  


When our days are packed as they are, it can be hard to sort through my reflections and what I've learned. But today was a little slower, and gave me space and time to take in my surroundings.

  


Like this morning when Thalia and Jeff cooked us breakfast. A warm, steamy frittata with crispy hash browns layering the bottom. A toasted, buttered bagel with cream cheese and cinammony apple butter. Coffee with honey and creamer. Food is my love language.

Or the evening spent in Thalia and Jeff’s backyard, fairy lights shining softly against the Gunnison dusk. A meal of creamy pasta sat in front of us with shreds of parmesan, chunks of sauteed chicken, and freshly buttered bread. Greens picked fresh from their garden, lightly acidic from balsamic vinegar. Bubbles from La Croix and sweet homemade mead (don't worry parents, it was just for people over 21!). We belly-laughed over dinner, going over the results of our latest Spokes quiz and sharing silly stories with our hosts.

Dinner and drinks!

I am reminded that joy and happiness can be found in smaller moments, that the greatest gift can just be appreciating the now.
