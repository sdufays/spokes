# Day 60: An Ode To How Spokes Ended Up In Vegas

Rest Day in Zion National Park (or Las Vegas) — 0 mi, 0 ft

Today real early I awoke  
To the sound of Tatiana who spoke  
It’s time to get up  
But I was fed up  
To wake up at 6 everyday is some sick joke

At least today we’re going on an epic hike  
A million degrees in Utah, it’s just not right to bike  
So we head to the Visitor Center  
With Caroline’s family as our mentors  
Who know everything about Zion, shuttles and hikes alike

We stand in line for the shuttle  
30 minutes we await, befuddled  
On what we should do  
Carmen and I need to move  
We have a long day ahead full of trouble

Recall that right now, Drew’s bike is at the bottom of a cliff  
Carmen and I volunteer to road trip to find him a new whip  
So we must finish the hike by ten  
Which is only an hour away by then  
It seems like Angel’s Landing will be summitted another day, RIP

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06140.jpg?w=768) ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06143.jpg?w=768)

We decide to still hike as much as possible  
Ascending up Walter’s Wiggles*, the scenery seems impossible  
How can red rocks be so pretty  
To have to turn back is such a pity  
So we instead decide to do something irresponsible

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06151.jpg?w=1024)The wiggles ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06141.jpg?w=1024)look like ice cream

Carmen and I race up, rock scrambling the ridge to the summit  
Dodging slow families left and right, to our death we hope we won’t plummet  
We take jumping photos at the top  
Then we run down, no stops  
By 11 we’re back on the bus, glad we didn’t kick the bucket

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06158.jpg?w=1024) ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06172.jpg?w=1024) ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06157.jpg?w=1024) ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06169.jpg?w=1024)

I drive back to the Airbnb to get Drew  
And call lots of bike shops, hoping one will pull through  
With a bike ready for pick up today  
So Drew can get back on the road without delay  
Finally Trek in Las Vegas calls me back, “WE GOTCHU!!”

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06176.jpg?w=1024) ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06177.jpg?w=1024)

So this turns into a story about how MIT Spokes goes to Vegas  
A coffee, an iced tea, a 44 oz Pepsi, and three hours it takes us  
After a pit stop at In-and-Out  
We show up and check out  
The pink bike Chadd at Trek has all ready to go for us

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06181.jpg?w=1024)Meal of ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06183.jpg?w=1024)kings and ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/dsc06191.jpg?w=1024)queens

Drew takes it for a joyride, his first order of business  
Around the parking lot he goes, full of impulsiveness  
Grinning ear to ear,  
Forgetting yesterday’s fear,  
Drew looks like a little kid opening his presents on Christmas

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/img_0167.jpeg?w=768) ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/img_0170.jpeg?w=768)

With the mission accomplished, we then decide to do  
What anyone who comes to Vegas decides to do  
We hit the club  
Ready to pull up  
In sports shorts and flip-flops, lookin’ too fly to be true

Except then it takes us an hour just to drive down the whole strip  
And another hour to find reasonably priced parking for the whip  
Now Drew’s feeling tired  
But Carmen and I are still wired  
We promise him just one hour for our worldwide trip

At 6:58 we start in Lake Como, watching the Bellagio fountain show  
We then walk to Paris, where slot machine after slot machine is aglow  
Drew takes a souvenir off the streets  
Carmen gets called fine by a guy rappin a beat  
At 7:58 we’ve looped back to the start, on the dot because I’m just like that, yo

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/8cd25680-b107-4926-8af9-842e4a468456.jpeg?w=768) ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/8610f89a-47b9-4bca-b721-75d90fe448f0.jpeg?w=768)

All summer in a day*, all Vegas in an hour  
In the City of Sin, imagine the willpower  
No money was spent  
Our temptation zero percent  
Only pavement we pound, only climbs we devour

We make our last pit stop a photo by the famous sign  
With no parking in sight, we leave our van on the curb, misaligned  
We sprint up real quick  
Ask a stranger to take a flick  
We’re back within a minute, thank god our fender has no fine

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_2328.jpg?w=768)

To Arizona’s Favorite Mexican Food we go, Filiberto’s  
Drive-thru burritos so hyped up by Carmen that we must go  
Didn’t eat anything on the strip  
In promise of delayed gratification, but RIP  
The only Filiberto’s in Nevada is temporarily closed.

I wish I was home for the feast Caroline’s grandpa had cooked up instead  
Instead of picking yet another fast food place on Google Maps, I pled  
Curry Pizza House, I said  
Let’s go there instead, but  
Drew still bought fried chicken and bread.

Finally it was time to head home sweet home  
Carmen drove this time; I was coming down with some syndrome  
Suddenly ill in the backseat  
I needed to take a couple beats  
And when I wake up, it was 1 am, we were home.

Biked a century yesterday, then hiked Angel’s Landing today  
Road tripped to Vegas, got a bike, walked the strip, pretty eventful, I’ll say  
In four hours we’d be up again  
Hiking the river of the Zion Canyon  
It’s hard to believe so much can happen every day,  
But that’s the story of how Spokes went to Vegas today.
