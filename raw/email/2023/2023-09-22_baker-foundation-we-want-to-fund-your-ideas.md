TL;DR: Have a project to bring MIT undergrads together? Apply for funding through the Baker Foundation now!

Hi everyone!

Hope you all have had a great start to the Fall semester.

The Baker Foundation is accepting applications for any student-led projects for this school year. We would love to hear all your ideas to bring our community together and improve student life. Just a quick reminder that we prioritize new ideas/projects that haven't happened in the past! For context, some of the projects we funded last year were a Mince fine dining experience, Infinite Magazine's MIT Gala, MIT Spokes' cross-country bike ride, and Stand-Up CoMITy's comedy night.

Please check out our website (http://bakerfoundation.mit.edu/) for more information about our mission, and this check out this link<http://bakerfoundation.mit.edu/#apply> to apply!

Looking forward to hearing from you all,

Baker Foundation
