Do you love biking? Crave adventure? Want to see more of the US? Like sleeping on the floor? Looking for some awesome friends? Need a new fun fact? Don’t know what to do with your life?

If you answered yes to any of the questions, Spokes 2026 might be the perfect opportunity for you. 🫵

Hmm, I’m feeling seen… tell me more?

Yay!! Spokes is a nonprofit, student-run initiative dedicated to spreading opportunities for STEM education to students in rural and underserved communities. The catch? We do it all by bike. Across about ten and a half weeks, we bike over 4,000 miles from DC to San Francisco, teaching STEM workshops all along our route. Students (you!) plan the route, coordinate with learning festival partners, and, of course, do lots and lots (and lots...) of biking!

Ooh that sounds fun! How does the whole teaching thing work?

Every member of Spokes designs their own workshops to teach at our day-long STEM learning festivals for middle school students. We teach about once a week at schools, libraries, and correctional facilities along our route. Our workshops this year included 3D printing, bottle rockets, cryptography, machine learning, and phases of matter… but the sky’s the limit on curriculum design!

This all sounds super cool but I’m not really a biker… do you think I’m capable of biking across the country?

If you’re considering Spokes, you definitely need to be up for adventure, ready to push yourself physically, and have an appreciation for Type 2 fun. However, you don’t need to have previous biking experience. Our team had a wide range of experience levels with biking (and athletics in general). I truly believe anyone who’s excited about the idea of Spokes is capable of meeting the physical demands.

Signing up for Spokes was one of the best decisions many of us made at MIT, and we can’t wait to pass the torch to the next team! If you fill out this application<https://forms.gle/azCBcQdNYmN2EzeF7> by Sunday, September 28 at 11:59pm, that could be you. 🥳

To learn more, check out our website at https://mitspokes.com/. You can see our route, curriculum, and daily blogs from throughout this past summer. We will also have an information session on Wednesday, September 24th at 4pm in 1-132.

And if you have any more questions about Spokes, feel free to respond to this email or reach out to us at spokes-2025@mit.edu<mailto:spokes-2025@mit.edu>. We can’t wait to read your application!
