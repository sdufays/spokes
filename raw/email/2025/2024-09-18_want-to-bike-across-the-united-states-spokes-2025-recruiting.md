TL;DR Apply<https://forms.gle/MRxX1xAdq8ZxrFWi7> to join MIT Spokes by Sunday 9/29 and learn more about the trip at our Zoom info session<https://mit.zoom.us/j/93949783378> on Monday 9/23 from 7:30-8:30pm!

Every year, MIT Spokes sends a team of students on a cross-country cycling adventure from D.C. to San Francisco. Over 75 days, the team bikes over 4000 miles, stays in over 50 different towns, and hosts hands-on Learning Festivals that promote STEM to middle-school students across the country.

We’re looking for a team of bold, open-minded, and ambitious individuals for the Spokes 2025 team. You don’t even have to be an experienced cyclist to join! By joining Spokes, you have the freedom and responsibility to plan next year's adventure from start to finish as the initiative is all student-run, and then...you get to do it! Seems demanding, but take it from us, it is one of the most impactful and meaningful ways to spend your summer.

We will host an online info session about the trip on Monday, September 23rd from 7:30-8:30pm. Feel free to join at this link: https://mit.zoom.us/j/93949783378 to ask questions or just get more information about MIT Spokes!

For more information on this year’s MIT Spokes team, visit www.mitspokes.com<http://www.mitspokes.com>. To learn more about our day-to-day endeavors, read our blogs, which can also be found on our website!
