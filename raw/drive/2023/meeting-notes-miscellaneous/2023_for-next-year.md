# For next year (2023 team advice)

- Get the **ProCard and travel card ASAP**
  - Requires an application; may take ~2 weeks to arrive. Application is on the MIT website (just Google it).
  - Email Sandi and Marvelin (Edgerton Center) the application; bump/go into their office if you don't hear back.
  - Put ALL expenses on these cards so **no reimbursement / out-of-pocket is necessary**. (A few grants/funds may only reimburse: student is reimbursed, then transfers money into the Spokes account through giving.mit.edu and the cost object.)
  - To document a ProCard purchase, just email Marvelin a PDF of the receipt named `[cardholder last name] mm-dd-yyyy $00.00 [Name of vendor].pdf` (instead of filing a report in the system).
  - How things should be paid for:
    - Sandi / Edgerton Center directly: giant orders (e.g. the one-time ~$13k bike purchase) - they get invoiced and transfer funds directly.
    - **ProCard**: everything non-travel ("field trip" related) that is not on the MIT "Non-Allowed Items for ProCard" list (https://vpf.mit.edu/non-allowed-items-for-procard-purchases).
    - **Travel card**: everything else.
- It takes **90 minutes (no less) from alarm to get everyone on bikes**. Plan accordingly.
- Joy recommends trying to **reroute Kansas further north** and maybe swap the Newton learning festival for **Manhattan (KS)** so the team can stay with MIT alums instead of Warm Showers and churches.
