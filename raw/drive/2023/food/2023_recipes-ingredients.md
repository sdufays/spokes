NOTES:
Buy premade sauces/ flavorings (i.e. curry pastes that only require water)
Buy precooked meat (i.e rotisserie chicken)
Buy canned beans and canned fish
Buy protein powder for Protein Shakes to maintain muscle and aid in recovery
Buy minute rice for faster cooking
Buy multivitamins to ensure we are meeting basic nutrition needs
Can keep dried goods handy like pasta/ noodles
Eggs, yogurt, cheese spreads, plant-based milk, tofu, onions, chicken goes in the cooler.
Buy Garlic and ginger in self stable jars
Buy spices (salt, pepper, turmeric, garam masla, garlic and onion powder, shah’s spice mixes)
