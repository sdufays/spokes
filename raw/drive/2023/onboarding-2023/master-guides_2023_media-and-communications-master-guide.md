Spokes Media and Communications Master Guide

Website
We now use Wordpress for our website now. Wix was used in the past, and hosts some old team websites.

We also use the Wordpress blog format to easily post our blogs that have comment features and image capacity, pretty intuitive to use and post.

We own the “mitspokes.com” domain, which is currently pointed at the previous team’s domain. You should migrate that over to your own year’s website. This was a bit complicated and not super well documented how to do it online. I would recommend just talking to customer support if you are stuck as they were able to get the domains transferred for me. Please make sure you preserve the entire past site!

Instagram
The login information for the spokes Instagram is:
Username: mitspokes
Password: [redacted]

We tried to post semi-regularly before and during the trip, it was nice for hosts/family/friends to be able to see what we were doing! We posted introductions at the beginning of the spring semester to introduce each person on the team, and then during the semester we would post semi-frequent content (training rides, meetings, planning).

In 2022, we got featured on mitstudents during the semester and also during the actual trip which was great, mitpics reposted a couple of our posts which got us a lot of publicity. Generally Instagram is a great way for people to learn about the trip, so posting regularly during the trip is a good move. Stanford spokes posted daily last year which was really great on their end.

In 2026, we did a couple of collaboration posts with the mitedgerton Instagram which was great for visibility. We posted a couple posts related to fundraising progress. The most important thing is to make sure everyone on the team is reposting these types of posts on their individual instagram’s stories so that it can get more visibility. During the summer we posted a weekly dump with photos and a short caption which was good for our supporters to get a visual glimpse of our day to day and also a great way to remember the summer. Another thing we did was we made a post for every learning festival we did so make sure to take a lot of pictures and videos during the event. Finally, we would often ask restaurants to sponsor us a meal and would thank them by posting about it on our Instagram.

2026 TLDR advice:
Individual team member intros
Collaboration posts with the mitedgerton Instagram for fundraising and marketing
Fundraising progress and thank you posts - make sure to repost on individual Instagrams!
During the summer, weekly recap posts
One post for every learning festival
One post for every restaurant that sponsors us a meal

Pamphlets
Maxwell Yun (Spokes 2022) designed a snazzy-looking 12-page pamphlet in InDesign that boils down the essence of the trip into an easy-to-read pamphlet. Great for finding sponsors and looking professional – Maxwell is pretty sure that the pamphlet is what got us the Lightforce sponsorship. Also, Maxwell is writing this section.

The pamphlet prints really easily and inexpensively – CopyTech charges ~$5 to print one copy of the booklet. The technical term for the way it’s printed is “saddle-stitched” – the booklet prints on 11x17 paper (two pages wide) and staples in the middle. Very professional look.

The 2022 pamphlet is in “Social Media, Blog, Website, Media” / “Pamphlets” in the Spokes 2022 Google Drive. The InDesign files and resources for the pamphlet are in the “pamphlet_resources_2022” folder.

Fonts are “Proxima Nova” and “Overpass”. They’re in the “Resources” folder.

Thank You Card
We gave each host a personally handwritten thank-you card (except the one unpleasant one). Maxwell also graphic-designed a thank-you card visually similar to the pamphlet.

The thank-you card is in the “Social Media, Blog, Website, Media” folder in the Spokes 2022 Google Drive. It is ready to print and does not include a year, so any year Spokes can use it. Design files are in the same location as the pamphlets’ files.

The card prints double-sided on 8.5x11” paper and folds in the middle. For extra “wow” factor, print on a cardstock and ask for the card to be machine-folded, which MIT Copytech can do. FedEx Kinko’s can print on cardstock, but cannot machine-fold.

 After the trip, we also made thank you cards and mailed them to our hosts. You can print these on nice paper at Copytech for pretty cheap ($30 for around 40). We tried to make them into postcards but I think we must have used the wrong type of paper for it :(

Posters (for the Infinite)
We don’t have a spot on the Infinite right now, but you can totally apply to one if you want to!

Maxwell created two posters in Photoshop, an 8.5x11” and an 11x17” version, for different postering needs. You can easily edit information in the poster in Photoshop.

You’ll need to install the “Overpass” font to edit the document.

There’s also an “event poster” variant that has dates/times for an event (in our case, the bike repair/safety workshop). Feel free to tweak/adjust that poster for your own needs.

Design files are in the “Social Media, Blog, Website, Media” / “Pamphlets” / “pamphlet_resources” folder in the Spokes 2022 Google Drive.

Spokes 2022 postered this along the Infinite and seemed to get some eyeballs – great for recruitment or donations.

Mailing Lists

All mailing list operations are done in WebMoira (https://groups.mit.edu/webmoira/list/[LIST])

spokes-20[xx]@mit.edu
This is the list containing all members of the Spokes team. Upon accepting next year’s Spokes class, you should create the next year’s Spokes mailing list, add them to the list, then transfer ownership to self-owned. For example, Spokes 2022 created the “spokes-2023@mit.edu” mailing list.

spokes-current@mit.edu
A wrapper list owned and managed by spokes-20[xx]@mit.edu. This list owns several other lists, hence its existence.

To hand off to next year’s Spokes group to manage, perform the following:
Add next year’s spokes-20[xx] list to the spokes-current
Remove yourself (spokes-20[xx-1]) from spokes-current
Change owners to next year’s spokes-20[xx] list
Assorted Spokes mailing lists
A few other lists owned by spokes-current, owned by current year spokes-20[xx]. Examples include:
