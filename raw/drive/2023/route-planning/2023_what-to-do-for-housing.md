# What to do for housing (Duha, 2023)
(Warm Showers shared account login in the original: [redacted]; account email was the team address spokes-2023@mit.edu.)
Strategy: Warm Showers + email **5 churches per city** + alumni.
1. Use Warm Showers to find hosts for each stop; if none in the town, try nearby towns (within ~10 miles).
2. Message hosts with the template: "My name is Duha and I am one of the MIT SPOKES students. We are a group of seven students biking across the US. Along our route, we host learning festivals in underserved communities to spread STEM and a love for education. We are stopping in ___ and were wondering if you would host us around ___. We will have a support vehicle to carry our teaching equipment. I know seven people is quite a large number but we are happy to camp in a backyard! (website mitspokes.com)"
3. Record host contact info in the route sheet, "Cities/Stops" tab.
4. If staying in a campground/park, highlight it and have Lucy book it.
5. If there's a learning festival, ask the LF host (after housing is confirmed) whether they know anyone who can house the team.
6. If no hosts, check prior years' lists and re-contact old hosts: 2022 Route Plan https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1KxtpM3YeC8zaMZJ8Tnt0Y_PLeut23lLlKPYiTx3w5Jw ; 2019 https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1sKeN98AI68h8YIaUNc7oRk24z4JXFHNmNjAtLXVesYg and .../1N0nORkCieYe1zldEQLBfxTrVOabEy2rHBspkR08XDds ; 2018 https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1t2rcDZ0FMIpyMKqpSBLPsGQnvz2FZbaVwVCBoxtDKb0
7. If all else fails, cheap hotel/motel.
