# Baker Foundation application, Spokes 2023 (submitted Feb 23, 2023)
- Submit to baker-foundation@mit.edu. **Accepts proposals Sept 1 - May 1**, rolling review; submit >= 3 weeks before event/needed-by date. Funds NEW projects or substantially changed events; no annual funding. Criteria: realistic goals, long-term impact/vision, sustainability, number of people affected, creativity, other funding sources. Named for Everett Moore Baker, MIT Dean of Students 1947-50. They interview the requesting org.
- Form data: Org MIT Spokes; Spring funding period; contact Katherine Guo kathe@mit.edu; MIT main account number 2530032; total budget $30,000; **requested $3,000**.
- Organizers: Katherine Guo kathe@mit.edu, Audrey Gatta agatta@mit.edu, Lucy Nester lnester@mit.edu, Joy Ma joym@mit.edu, Duha Syar duhasyar@mit.edu, Andrew Doan doan1218@mit.edu.
- Motives: target underserved/rural areas; Teach for America helped identify communities previously; networking dinners after LFs to answer college questions; ~10 LF locations.
- **History/lessons stated:** ten-year program; 2014 each member ran own workshop - less impactful; 2015 did fewer, longer workshops -> more hands-on, take-home items loved by kids; 2022 team had spring planning issues because members didn't prioritize it - 2023 team committed up front; no trips in 2020/2021 (COVID); added arts/design topics in 2022 with positive feedback.
- Philosophy fit: equitable education; school funding tied to community wealth; biking breaks "academics vs athletics" stereotypes. MIT impact: shaping future MIT demographics.
- **Estimated budget (based on prior years): total $33,634** - bikes & accessories $9,120; repairs $917; tactical clothing $980; transportation $470; food $5,690; emergency $547; education materials $7,500; misc $1,740.
