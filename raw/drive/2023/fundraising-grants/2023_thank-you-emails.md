Dear [Donor's Name],

I am writing to express my sincerest gratitude for your generous donation to the MIT Spokes program. Your support will enable us to continue our mission of biking across the country while teaching and organizing learning festivals in rural communities throughout the USA.

We are thrilled to have your support as we work to bridge the educational gap in the United States and bring knowledge and learning opportunities to those who may not have access to them otherwise. Your contribution is critical to the success of our program.

With your donation, we can provide resources and materials for our learning festivals, cover travel expenses, and reach more communities in need. Your support will help us inspire and engage students in STEM fields.

Once again, thank you... If you have not, consider staying in touch with us by signing up on our website, www.mitspokes.com, as we will be blogging throughout our journey this summer.

Sincerely, [Your name], MIT Spokes 2023
