# Mad Money Grant application (Joy Ma, 2023)
Statement: bike DC -> SF in summer 2023; LFs with STEM workshops for middle schoolers "from physics to philosophy"; seven MIT students; 80 days, 3,900 miles; nine summers since 2013.
**Workshops:** Electronics (breadboard circuits); Chemical Engineering (berry-juice solar cells, extracting leaf pigments); 3D Printing (watch a printer build a part); Art and Science (philosophy of paradoxes, math of music/fine art, design a board game).
**Planned LF locations (Feb 2023 draft):** Washington (DC), Louisa County Library (VA), Challenger Learning Center (KY), Bowling Green, Ferguson Middle School, Newton Public Library (KS), Lamar City Library (CO), White Pine County Library (NV), Fallon Youth Club (NV).
**Budget $33,600** - same breakdown as SHASS application (bikes $12,000; tools $1,500; gear $2,000; travel $8,000; camping $500; consumables $6,000; workshops $2,000; emergency $1,500; covid $100).
