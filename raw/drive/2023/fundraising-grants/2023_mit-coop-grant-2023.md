Please write a brief description of your group's work. Why and for whom does this group exist? (150 words max)

MIT Spokes is a completely student-led initiative that has been running for nine years. Each summer, the group embarks on a cross-country bike ride to provide STEAM workshops to students in rural communities. These exploratory, hands-on workshops are inspired by their experiences at MIT and cover topics like using berry juice to create solar cells, exploring the mathematics of music, and creating art with artificial intelligence. In addition to positively impacting the communities they visit, the program helps students develop important skills such as leadership, perseverance, and teamwork, while fostering lasting friendships with the communities they meet. The educators in these communities eagerly anticipate MIT Spokes' return, and with COVID having limited in-person opportunities to give back to their community, the project's focus on sharing STEAM with kids has become even more critical.

Please write a brief description of the project to be funded by the COOP (100 words max).

We are seeking funding to cover food and living expenses during our 80-day trip. While we plan to stay at campsites or with generous hosts to rest and refresh, we may need to use emergency funds to stay at budget-friendly hotels during the trip. In 2022, the COOP generously granted MIT Spokes $1,500, and we are hopeful for comparable support this year. Your contribution would ensure that our team members have adequate nourishment and a safe place to stay, allowing us to focus on our goal of inspiring and educating students in rural communities across the USA.

What is the total annual budget for your organization? 30,000

Please elaborate on how funding will be used. How much funding will be allocated to each expense item in your budget? Please provide any additional detail that the COOP should know in making a funding decision.

$1000 will go towards partially covering our food (our total food budget need is around 4000)
$500 will go towards covering emergency living expenses

Please explain how you will measure your impact. Describe the way you currently do, or plan to, evaluate your program's impact on the community, on the clients, and on the volunteers, including any pre-/post-test, mechanisms to gather feedback or satisfaction surveys. Feedback on your event is a required part of The Coop Grants program. The feedback form can be accessed at https://store.thecoop.com/coop-gives-form-feedback-2018/.

At MIT Spokes, we value feedback and are committed to ensuring the success of our workshops. To achieve this, we will keep a tally of the students who participate in our various workshops and collect feedback from both participants and learning festival hosts. As we plan our logistics and workshop content for the upcoming trip, we will take into account the feedback we received from last year's hosts. We believe in maintaining strong relationships with our hosts, and are often invited back annually to continue our partnership. This year, we plan to gather feedback through Google surveys to gain insight into what participants and hosts liked best about our workshops, and what we can improve on. This information will help us refine and improve our workshops, and ultimately enhance our outreach efforts.
