(Modified 2024-12-21; year-2025 version.) Remember to cc spokes2025 on emails.

**General email:** Dear [contact name], I'm [name], a current [grade] in Courses [course]. I am writing to the [department] to request sponsorship of MIT Spokes 2025. The Spokes team, made up of 8 MIT students, will be biking from Washington D.C to San Francisco this summer. Along our route, we will stop at schools, libraries, and camps throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions. A goal of Spokes is to introduce science and design in exciting ways to students who may not have access to such opportunities. We are starting the fundraising process... would [department] be willing to help fund this program by donating. [Link website mitspokes.com], would love to meet, share more about Spokes 2025, and discuss possible sponsorship.

**Example (2023 MechE):** Personalize with the student's major, mention the lesson they will host in that discipline (e.g., 3D printing for MechE), and name last year's donation ("as it did last year by donating $750").
