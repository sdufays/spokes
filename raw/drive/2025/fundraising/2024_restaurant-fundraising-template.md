Ideally go in person or phone. 1) Introduce yourself and say your team frequents the location. 2) Briefly describe Spokes (8 MIT students biking DC to SF, free Learning Festivals) and the ask; 2024 goal $40,330 for the 74-day trip. 3) Ask about either/both: a sponsorship/donation (they go on the website in exchange) or an event day where part of proceeds go to Spokes. Candidate list for 2025 is in the fundraising tracker (Restaurants/Coffee Shops tab) - none were contacted.
