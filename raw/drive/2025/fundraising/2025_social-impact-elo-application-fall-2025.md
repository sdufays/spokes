**Applied Sept 2025 for Spokes 2026; ELO offered $5,000 (see fundraising tracker).** Sponsor: MIT Edgerton Center. Contact: Chris Mayer (Edgerton) or Ruth Shiferaw. Funding periods: IAP 2026, Spring 2026, Fall 2026 (not summer). Category: Education. ELO funding must go to student participant expenses (stipends, travel, supplies); not staff. Criteria: explicit social/environmental impact, near-term application. Requested $5,000; supports 1 undergraduate (as stated).

## Pitch / impact framing (reusable)
- Only 22% of rural adults 25-44 hold a bachelor's vs 40% urban (USDA); STEM workers earn 47% more (NSF); each $1 on prison secondary education saves $2.25 (Mackinac Center); formerly incarcerated people with education 7.6% more likely to be employed. Aspiration + preparation drives STEM enrollment (Blau & Duncan 1967); best programs are summer camps, internships, distance learning (Harris & Hodges 2018).
- Spokes founded 2013; 8 students bike across the US; 8-10 festivals in schools, libraries, youth clubs, correctional facilities; 11-week expedition. 2025 stops named: Washington DC; Hazard KY; Bowling Green KY; Kansas City MO; Denver CO; Fallon NV, among others. Returning partners: Challenger Center (Hazard KY), Newton Public Library (Kansas), Fallon Youth Club (Nevada).
- Feedback: QR-coded feedback forms to parents/participants after each festival. Edgerton staff Chris Mayer and Marvelin Higgenbottom mentor logistics/financial planning.
- Website https://mitspokes.com/ ; article on Spokes 2025: https://wkuherald.com/85487/news/mit-students-host-steam-learning-festival/
