# Spokes 2025 Info Session: "Spreading STEM Coast-to-coast!" (given fall 2024)

**Who:** presenters Jess ('23 MEng '24, 6-3), Sophia ('24, 16-ENG), Varsha ('23 MEng '24, 6-9), Rebecca ('24, 3A+11), JD ('27, 18+6-2), Amulya ('23 MEng '24, 6-7), Clelia ('27, 18), Hank ('26, 6-3). (These are the 2024 trip riders who recruited the 2025 team.) Slides give: Amulya (fall count during Spokes: 2; favorite ride: entering Kansas), Rebecca (fall count 2.5; favorite ride: Continental Divide), Hank (fall count 1; favorite ride: South Lake Tahoe to Placerville).

**Main components:** Bike (Washington DC to San Francisco, 4000 miles, 170,000 ft of climbing); Teach (design and conduct STEAM learning festivals at libraries, jails, youth centers, and more); Plan the whole thing ourselves (fundraise, route, develop LFs; all decisions up to the team).

**What is it like:** average daily mileage 73; average climbing 3,000 ft/day; started the trip with lower-mileage days to get used to daily biking; lots of bike repair knowledge gained.

**LF examples shown:** Physics of Sound, Strawberry DNA, Krispie Koncrete, 3D Printing, DC Jail and DC Youth Services Center. Impact: design your own activity from scratch; deliberately hosted festivals in rural/underserved areas.

**Trip highlights:** Arches National Park rest day; highest point of the trip 12,000 ft; the one road across Nevada; forests of Colorado; cows in Virginia; Utah; Nevada sunset.

**School year:** fundraise, develop/schedule LFs, plan route, train; commitment ~4 hours/week; bike blender at Edgerton Center Expo; Midnight Marathon with the team.

**FAQ:**
- Biking experience: no touring experience required; if you're comfortable on a bike and think you'd enjoy it. Boston is a great place to improve.
- Cost: Spokes fundraises during the school year to cover the whole trip: bikes, food ("lots of it"), any lodging, bike gear. Typical lunch: bagel sandwich. Support minivan nicknamed "Chrissy".
- Typical day: wake 6-7 am; bike until afternoon with lunch stop (often gas stations/small towns); group grocery runs and cooking for 8; stay with MIT alumni/hosts from the biking community or camp. Blog at mitspokes.com/blog.
- Applications at tinyurl.com/spokes2025, deadline 9/29.
