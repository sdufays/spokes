# Dormspam recruiting email (text used in the fall 2024 cycle for the Spokes 2025 team)

To: spokes-2024@mit.edu and dorms (bcc'd to dorms, "bike grease stain black" for bc-talk)
Subject: Want to bike across the United States?
Attachment: Amulya's poster

TL;DR Apply to join MIT Spokes by **Sunday 9/29** and learn more about the trip at our Zoom info session on **Monday 9/23 from 7:30-8:30pm**.

Every year, MIT Spokes sends a team of students on a cross-country cycling adventure from **D.C. to San Francisco**. Over **75 days**, the team bikes over **4000 miles**, stays in over **50 different towns**, and hosts hands-on **Learning Festivals** that promote STEM to middle-school students across the country.

We're looking for a team of bold, open-minded, and ambitious individuals for the Spokes 2025 team. You don't even have to be an experienced cyclist to join! By joining Spokes, you have the freedom and responsibility to plan next year's adventure from start to finish as the initiative is all student-run, and then... you get to do it! ...one of the most impactful and meaningful ways to spend your summer.

More info: www.mitspokes.com (blogs on the site). Questions? Email spokes-2024@mit.edu. "Hope to see you on the road! MIT Spokes 2024"
