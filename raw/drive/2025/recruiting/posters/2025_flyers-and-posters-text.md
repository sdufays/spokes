## Recruiting poster, fall 2025 (for the Spokes 2026 team): "GAIN LORE THIS SUMMER"
Main flyer: "Want to bike across and explore the country? Bring STEM to kids in underserved rural and small towns? Make your new best friends? Just want the COOLEST summer lore? LEARN ABOUT SPOKES! Stop by our info session! September 24, 4PM, 1-132. Got questions? Email spokes-2025@mit.edu, website mitspokes.com, Instagram @mitspokes."
"Extra" poster set (6 PNG panels + PDF), tongue-in-cheek lines: "Want future parent lore?"; "Your new FDOC fun fact: 'I biked across the country'"; "See the states in a whole new way! (from a bike)"; "The solution to your quarter-life crisis"; "This is better than any summer internship"; "Want gains? Hit leg day for 80 days straight."
Files: posters folder (PDFs main and extra), subfolders with PNG exports (main flyer-1.png; extra-1..6.png).

## Crowdfunding flyer (spokes crowdfund flyer.png, April 2025)
"SUMMER 2025. 8 students. 8 bikes. 80 days. 4000 miles. A quest to rethink STEM education. MIT Edgerton Center presents... SPOKES 2025. DONATE TODAY! Support Spokes as we bring fun, interactive STEM learning festivals to K-12 students in underserved communities! Questions? Contact us! spokes-2025@mit.edu, @mitspokes." (copies in Advertising and Fundraising folders)

## Learning-festival flyer (spokes lf flyer.png, April 2025)
"Summer 2025. MIT is coming to your city! Come do fun, interactive, and educational STEM activities with MIT Spokes at your local learning festival!" Activities listed: The Power of Machine Learning (how computers learn from data); The Science of Secrets (hiding and encrypting information); What's the Matter? (temperature, viscosity, molecular interactions and states of matter); Blast Off! DIY Bottle Rockets (chemistry and physics); From 2D Design to 3D Printing (design and make small creations).
