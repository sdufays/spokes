# Group interview format (fall 2025 recruiting for Spokes 2026)

- :05 roundtable introductions (name, pronouns, major and year, fun fact)
- :10 split into two groups; intro interview structure and 1st prompt. No right or wrong answers; looking at how they work in a team and for creative, robust answers.

**Prompt 1:** design a learning festival schedule with activities for a group of 40 students in Newton, Kansas at the public library.
- LF time 10-3, lunch break included; 20 elementary kids + 20 high schoolers; team of 6.
- Activities can target one age group but all kids engaged at all times.
- 10 minutes to work, 2 minutes to present; trade one person between groups.

**Prompt 2: Bad day on the Spokes trip** (made-up day, not an actual one). Map shared via a Google My Maps link (with a "Super Secret Layer" of points A-D).
- 106 miles; driver in a support car carrying supplies and most larger gear; riders carry ~1.5 L water, need more every ~2 hours; 90+ F; start 7:30 am.
- Part A (6 min): where to put lunch and water stops.
- Part B: water stop at point A; the car breaks down on the way. What should team/driver do? (Good answer: a pre-made protocol: bikers do X, driver does Y.)
- Part C: a biker leaves the interstate shoulder because of debris, gets a flat at point B; another flat at point C; driver must reach both and still make the water stop at D. How to proceed?
- Last 5 min: questions.
