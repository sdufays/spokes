**Logistics:** interviews 30-45 min in 1-hour blocks (last 15 min to discuss + make a slide on the candidate for the debrief). Calendly books slots onto a shared Spokes calendar. Review the candidate's application responses first. Use when2meet for interviewer and debrief scheduling.

**What to look for:** motivation and commitment above all: motivation to plan and fundraise in the spring; fundraising experience; creativity/engagement in teaching via mock lesson; adaptability and problem-solving; teamwork and maturity. Must ask about summer conflicts flagged in the application (commencement, jobs, grad school, early move-in): cannot participate with a conflict.

**Questions** (bold = must ask):
- Intro: name, pronouns, year, hometown, course; interviewers share favorite Spokes memory; interviewee icebreaker.
- Motivation: **Why do you want to do Spokes?** What do you hope to gain?
- Logistics: **What does your IAP and spring schedule look like?** Summer commitments?
- Planning: organized a big event?; **How do you want to contribute to the planning phase** (fundraising, route planning, learning festivals, bikes/gear, physical training, website/media)?; **"For the trip, the team must fundraise around $40K. What are some ideas you have for fundraising?"**; **biking experience / fitness routine** (fine to have little); **how would you prepare physically**; bike maintenance experience?
- Trip/Team: roles of interest (route, finance, housing, LFs, media, bike maintenance); **a time you worked in a team and hit a challenge**; **role you expect to play and expectations of others**; **challenges you foresee on/off the bike**; best synergy; camping/backpacking experience.
- Teaching: why teach; previous experience; **teach a 3-5 minute lesson on any middle-school subject with 3 minutes to prepare**; difficulties at LFs; experience with kids.
- Questions for us?
