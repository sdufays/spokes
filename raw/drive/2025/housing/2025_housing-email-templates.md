**Template A (new host, e.g. Roanoke VA, June 11):** "Dear Steve, I'm Tian, an alum who graduated in 2024 from MIT (EECS and Theater Arts), part of the MIT Spokes 2025 team. We are a group of eight MIT students biking from Washington D.C. to San Francisco. Along our route we host learning festivals in underserved communities to spread STEM and a love for education. We are stopping in Roanoke, VA and wondered if you would host us on June 11. We will have a support vehicle to carry our equipment. Eight people is a large number, but we are happy to camp in a backyard or sleep on the floor! We'd appreciate a place to cook and maybe do laundry, but it isn't necessary. See https://www.mitspokes.com/. Thank you, Tian & Spokes 2025"

**Template B (reconnecting):** Subject "[Reconnecting] MIT SPOKES 2025 Housing Search in Afton, VA". "Hello Pam, ... You might remember our team from 2022! They had a great time with you and recommended we reach out this year..." same body, asking for June 8-9; happy to camp in a backyard; may want to cook, shower, laundry.

Key pattern: lead with who you are, group size (8) and that a support vehicle exists, flexibility (floor/backyard), exact dates, website link.
