## Planning the route
For many years Spokes has gone DC -> San Francisco; recommended since lots of documentation exists and relationships with LF hosts/hosts exist. Route follows **US Bike Route 76 (TransAmerica)** from DC to Colorado (Adventure Cycling USBRS maps). The 2025/2026 teams detoured for national parks/cities, which raised mileage. Keep Warm Showers host map, Google Maps and USBRS maps open together: one team planned route first and had to change it for lack of hosts. At least 2 planners (3 ideal): one contacts Warm Showers, one books campsites.

Route spreadsheets (in Drive): 2023 master file, 2024 Route, 2025 Route (stays/notes in comments), 2026 Route. RideWithGPS daily collections: 2025 https://ridewithgps.com/collections/4376129 ; 2026 https://ridewithgps.com/collections/9887125. 2024 daily routes on Strava (Hank, Varsha, Jess).

## Day to day navigation
Stops every 1.5-2 hrs (~15 mi) to refill water, usually at gas stations (soda fountain cold water, snacks, bathroom); car meets riders where no gas stations. 2026: route pre-planned before summer, route team adjusted the night before; read previous years' blogs for next-day notes. Tools: RideWithGPS (free; export GPX to bike computer, charge every few days), Strava premium (shows paved/unpaved), Google Street View (shoulders), Google Maps offline for reroutes.

## Finding stays
2022 stays by frequency: Warm Showers, campsites, learning festivals, friends, MIT connections, churches, motels. Ask LFs for housing. 
- **Warm Showers:** new account, mention support car, start contacting in February; only hosts who can take 2+ (2026: hosts able to take 4+, which meant nicer houses); get phone number and address as the app is buggy; confirm a week ahead, driver confirms day-of.
- **Campsites:** book from February (national parks fill up); showers if possible.
- **MIT alumni clubs:** emailed all clubs in states passed through; only 3 responded but one donated and Sam Ford (KY) came from this.
- **Churches:** large kitchens, bathrooms, sometimes feed you; call AND email every one (usually no response).

Colorado: Silverthorne CO, Roberta Flack (WS): negative, uncomfortable remarks and a harsh WS review; do not return.
Utah: national-park heavy. Don't bike into Arches from Thompson Springs (little shoulder, high traffic; 2022 shuttled). Bryce Canyon to Cedar City is one of the most beautiful rides (mostly downhill).
Nevada: Loneliest Road (Baker to Carson City): stock up in Ely; Eureka-Austin-Fallon has no services; collect stamps at gas stations for a pin; Middlegate burger stop; look for messages from past Spokes.
California: stay a day at Lake Tahoe; US-50 to Sacramento becomes freeway (use side roads); gas stations north of SF before Sausalito often lack water/bathrooms, use grocery stores like Whole Foods.
