## How to use
Compiled from multiple years. After your trip, update with your year and delete outdated info.

## How to start planning
1. Get to know each other. 2. Figure out what needs doing: Money (grant applications, earlier the better; crowdfunding must be through MIT, not GoFundMe; talk to Marvelin Higginbottom marvelin@mit.edu, Sandra J Lipnoski slipnosk@mit.edu, Chris Mayer mayerc@mit.edu); Bikes/Gear (matching gravel tubeless bikes; bike fits with Brendan Connor via CycleLifePT who comes on campus for fits + PT); Route (stops, Warmshowers); Curriculum (school/library contacts, set LF dates, design workshops). 3. Assign who does what (fundraising EARLY). 4. Regular meetings with a note taker and moderator. 5. Learn the Drive. 6. Ask past teams (spokes-2026@mit.edu). 7. Create a timeline. 8. Use Edgerton contacts (Sandy, Chris). 9. Don't reinvent the wheel; email templates in the Drive.

## Timeline (roughly what 2026 did / should have done)
- **November:** first meeting; read the master guides; meet before Thanksgiving and assign roles; start fundraising ASAP (top to-do every month); introduce yourselves to Edgerton.
- **December:** pick rough start/end dates; set up crowdfunding page and website; email departments/labs/centers for sponsorship (they often have leftover funding in December); meet Edgerton (IAP ok); start training.
- **January:** meet during IAP; start route; shared GCal; finalize trip dates; decide 1-way vs 2-way rental; very rough budget for fundraising; apply for 2 pro + 2 travel cards; start reaching out to LF locations.
- **February:** weekly meetings; finish website; LF ideas; start car rental once dates final (background checks for drivers); bike purchase around end of month; visit the Bike Lab.
- **March:** apply to J-WEL Education Innovation Grant (where Spokes 2024 got most money); blog post introducing members; MIT 24 Challenge crowdfunding; order biking gear; 2nd budget round.
- **April:** finalize gear list/ordering; Midnight Marathon (fundraise with a bingo card of dares); LF materials list; finalize LFs EARLY; design/buy Spokes shirts and bike jerseys; J-WEL answer end of April; Edgerton Center Showcase; start housing for first states.
- **May:** finalize all housing; finalize budget; get tetanus shot; assign trip roles; train; check in with Edgerton (photo release forms); optional local LF.
- **June (first week):** get all drivers added to rental in person at the rental agency; organize car, plastic boxes; shared toiletries/sunscreen; set up bike computers; bike repair crash course (and car maintenance).
- **During trip:** check-in meetings about logistics/team function.

## Food and nutrition
Carbs in the morning and while biking, protein at dinner, electrolytes while riding.
- Breakfast: hosts usually provide; camping: bagels + cream cheese/PB/Nutella, yogurt + granola, overnight oats in ziplocks, apples + PB, protein pancakes (too slow), Spokes 24 classic = powdered milk + instant coffee + cereal + powdered PB, cottage cheese.
- Lunch: 2022: sardines + tortillas, rotisserie chicken + Hawaiian bread, deli sandwiches, hummus + veggies. 2024: bagel sandwiches ~74 days in a row, later cheap Walmart French bread (deli meat, hummus "very important", tomato, cream cheese, cheese, pickles, chips). 2026: found snacks everyone liked and repeated them; sometimes liquid diet or fruit and snacks.
- Snacks: dried dates (Costco; "carried the 2022 team"), dried fruit, Clif/Lara bars (Nature's Bakery brownie bars 2024 favorite), nuts, electrolytes (Nuun, Liquid IV, Propel, Gatorade; tub of Gatorade powder cheapest).
- Dinner: 2026 never cooked (called places for free meals when host couldn't feed them). If cooking, 2-pot meals: jambalaya, feijoada, chicken salad (canned), tacos, chicken and rice, spring rolls (no stove), one-pot pasta.
- Protein: sardines, textured vegetable protein, tofu, canned chicken, unflavored protein powder, fresh meat, rotisserie chicken, deli meat, jerky (Costco), soymilk, peanut butter.
- Tricks: Minute Rice; Costco and Walmart (will miss Walmart in remote areas); baby wipes; paper towels as plates; pocket stove (boiled 9 eggs at once); driver buying cold drinks/iced tea on hot days.

## Packing guide
Pack light; laundry nearly daily. 2-3 biking outfits, 2 non-biking outfits (incl. Spokes shirt), cold-weather clothes (50s in the Rockies), quick-dry synthetic/wool. Carry-on suitcase works better than backpacking bag. Sample (Franklin, 2022): carry-on with bottom layer camping gear/warm clothes + top layer daily outfits; small backpack (laptop, toiletries, Crocs/shoes, frisbee); handlebar bag/fanny pack and saddle bag (snacks, phone, power bank, bottles, pump/tools, first aid, air horn and mace for dogs; chased by dogs a lot in Kentucky). 2024 gear recs: Pearl Izumi fingerless gloves, merino wool bike socks, clip-on shades for glasses wearers, jersey WITH POCKETS, padded shorts (2023 skeptical, all found essential), communal chamois butter. Varsha's 2024 blog "Day 37: junk in our trunk: what I packed for Spokes" on mitspokes.com.
