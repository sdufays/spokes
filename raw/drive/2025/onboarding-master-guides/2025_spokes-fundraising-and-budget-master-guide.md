## Fundraising
Resources: 2026, 2024 and 2022 fundraising tracking spreadsheets (see Drive Fundraising folders). Start early; need more than one person; by spring much corporate excess budget (Texas Instruments, Microsoft, Dell...) is gone.
- MIT departments: cold-call emails to department heads/administrators; template emails in the Fundraising folder.
- Grants inside MIT: TBP funding ($2000) after application; Baker Foundation ($2000) for a hosted "bike repair and safety workshop"; Mad Money Grant gave 2019 a few thousand for a trip pamphlet.
- Alumni groups: small donations but good connections and housing in some states.
- Bake sale + raffle for bike tune-ups made ~$2,000 total.
- **2024: most money ($45,330) from the J-WEL Education Grant** (https://www.jwel.mit.edu/grants); applications open February, decisions April; pursue other sources meanwhile.
- Outside MIT: corporate/nonprofit grants (see 2022 tracker); St. Anthony Educational Foundation ($4000).
- Companies: Lightforce and Desktop Metal (3D-printing) helped with the 3D-printing workshop; Lightforce sponsored $5000 in 2022. Companies must register with MIT to donate (conflict-of-interest check); talk to Sandi.
- Crowdsource: family, friends, coworkers; everyone participates.
- Tips: emphasize education; offer sponsor benefits (website/T-shirt recognition, in-person visits); cast a wide net; use Word mail merge.

## Budget
Budget dictates fundraising target. See the "Expenses + Budget" sheet (2026). Itemize as much as possible (e.g. list each bike gear item at local bike shop prices). Guessed transport and food costs; consult 2026 finances.

## Finances: travel card and pro card
- Travel card: expenses during the trip (food, gas, lodging). ProCard: gear and LF supplies; anything that made sense to buy at MIT before the trip. "Health and beauty" items (shampoo, body wash, sunscreen) cannot go on either card. To get either: online training + application; 2026 had 2 pro-card holders and 2 different travel-card holders.
- Travel card: holders make a Concur report at the end (tedious; upload receipts during the trip). Receipts required for purchases >$75 and any food. Catalog all purchases (gas can look like groceries). Lost receipt >$75: attach a missing-receipt affidavit and mark Non-Recoverable (Sandi Lipnoski). J-WEL funds are a sponsored account (extra approval); Spokes account is a departmental account (non-sponsored).
- Pro card: send receipts to Marvelin within two weeks (procard@mit.edu emails pending transactions); may be audited, respond promptly; avoid buying food, gas, or health/beauty on the procard; a lost receipt on audit can get the card deactivated.
- Purchases are tax exempt in MA but not elsewhere.
