## Email templates

**Follow-up to a confirmed host (Challenger Learning Center, Hazard KY, 6/14/2026, K-12, 9:45-3:15):** share registration form + graphic, workshop descriptions, schedule, ask space details (that site: 3 college classrooms with projector/HDMI, plus a 20x30 carpeted briefing room), student capacity and when to close registration, lunch for students and team plus dinner/breakfast needs, photo release for parents, housing (Harmony House B&B nights of 13th and 14th).

## Typical schedule
Setup 9-9:45; students arrive 9:45; workshop periods 10-11, 11-12; lunch 12-1; periods 1-2, 2-3; wrap-up 3-3:15; cleanup 3:15-4. 4 workshops in parallel, 4 sessions; build in breaks and snacks.

## 2019 reflections (summary)
Middle/early high school is where impact felt greatest; LFs were clustered at start and end with a dry middle; many small towns in KS/CO/UT would fit the target demographic; continuity matters (Hazard KY). Track LF status in a spreadsheet; call if hosts are bad at email; start class prep far earlier (finish before Spark/ESP in the fall) because funding struggles into April delayed prep. Hosts: Challenger Hazard KY (local HS "interns" helped; small first LF was good practice); Lab: Revolution, Farmington MO; St. Louis Homeschool Connection; aSTEAM Village Kansas City (80 kids, behavior hard, thin lunch; william@asteamvillage.org); Newton Public Library (very helpful, ~30 kids, meals provided); Boys and Girls Club Denver (good, many little kids, no lunch); Christa McAuliffe Space Center, Lindon UT (smart kids, lunch provided); White Pine County Library Ely NV (conference room split in two); Churchill County Library Fallon NV; Boys and Girls Club Carson City NV (less organized).

## Workshops
Teach in pairs, adaptable to ages, expect to rework after 1-2 runs. 2026 slide decks in a shared Drive folder. 2022: Electronics (breadboard LED circuit; paper speaker), Physics of Music, Chromatography and Color, 3D Printing (hands-on real printed parts), Solar Cell (dye-sensitized with berry juice; more for high school). 2019: Design Test Repeat (rapid prototyping), Decoding DNA (strawberry DNA, gel electrophoresis), Intelligence: Brain to Self-Driving Cars (neural net demo), Fun with Chemistry (whoosh bottle, elephant toothpaste).
