**Team norms (agreed June 6, 2025)**
- Receipts filed within 2 days (day of or day after), handled by purchaser.
- Pro + travel card basics: NEVER buy gas on the procard. Travel = flights, airbnbs, campground reservations, restaurants. Pro = groceries. Neither card is tax exempt outside MA (tax comes from Spokes funds).
- Personal gear refunds (from extra gear/camp gear/misc budgets): case by case; new norm: small group -> large group discussion on money matters depending on how many people it affects.
- Core responsibilities: daily cooler, laundry etc., be flexible; driver checks tomorrow's route the night before (elevation, tricky navigation); share expertise across teams; be concise.
- Smaller: offer to help hosts; do NOT buy glass for groceries; consolidate belongings (shoe bag); clean up after yourselves. Fenders: break up in pairs?
**Routes etc.:** when RideWithGPS won't let a route finish, write out directions (route person or, probably, the driver). 4 walkie talkies + buddy system. Driver may help with directions between stops and deploy the car for rescue in bad storms (esp. thunder) or darkness. Pump tires daily (recommended 40-50 psi, ~45).
