Route 1 around Zion: https://ridewithgps.com/routes/50172756 - Watchman Campground, Hurricane UT (day 1), Milford UT (day 2).
Route 2 through Zion: https://ridewithgps.com/routes/50172801 - Day 1 Zion to Lava Point Campground (Kolob Campground rejected as too expensive); Day 2 Lava Point to Milford. Lava Point: free, good climate, great views, must reserve 2 weeks ahead, only 6 sites (NPS page). Plan B: Lambs Knoll Designated Dispersed Camping (Recreation.gov), closer to Zion but less nice.
