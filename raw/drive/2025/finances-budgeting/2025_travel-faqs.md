# Edgerton Center Travel FAQ (received 2025)

**Contacts:** Chris Mayer (mayerc@mit.edu), Edgerton Center point of contact for travel registration and all insurance matters; Marvelin Higginbottom (marvelin@mit.edu), Edgerton Center admin for travel cards, rentals, bookings, letters; Sarah Brown (se_brown@mit.edu, 617-324-5031), MIT Insurance Office for automobile claims.

- **Reporting travel:** local travel needs no registration. Travel outside Massachusetts: email Chris Mayer (Kerberos IDs of all travelers, dates, destination) at least 2 weeks ahead to register Group Travel in Atlas; each traveler then enters flight info and emergency contact. International: at least 3 weeks ahead (MIT Travel Registry).
- **Missing classes:** Edgerton can provide a letter on letterhead via Marvelin; the professor decides whether to excuse.
- **Buying airfare, fees, hotels:** a team member can apply for a travel card (vpf.mit.edu travel-card application); send to Marvelin and allow 2-3 weeks. Students can be reimbursed out of pocket if no card. Edgerton can book flights/fees/hotels with a department travel card.
- **Renting passenger vehicles:** drivers must first pass an MVR (Motor Vehicle Registration) background check via MIT Emergency Management; send full name, DOB, license state to Marvelin. MIT corporate agreements: Avis/Budget and Enterprise/National waive the age-25 minimum (minimum age 18; Enterprise 18-20 only in MA, NH, ME) and limit/waive damage responsibility. Corporate codes: Avis A122600; Budget T240202; Enterprise XZ10MTY (18-20 in MA/NH/ME) or XZ10MIT (all others). Do NOT buy extra damage/liability insurance (not reimbursed). Marvelin sets up Direct Bill reservations. Only MIT-community members may drive unless approved by MIT Insurance with an MVR check; extra drivers can be added only at the rental counter in person with license. Tickets/violations are the driver's responsibility; never put them on a travel card.
- **12/15-passenger vans:** insurance office restricts rentals larger than 7 passengers.
- **Trucks:** Budget Truck special rate account number provided by MIT (insurance not included but business-rental insurance IS reimbursed); outside companies (e.g., Herc Rental) can be paid by travel card.
- **Accidents:** report immediately to Edgerton (Chris Mayer) and MIT Insurance; get a police report; submit rental agreement (both sides), accident report, approved travel expense report, rental agency correspondence, incident report (Origami Risk form), and the rental company's accident form.
- **Personal vehicles:** strongly discouraged; gasoline not reimbursable but mileage is.
- **Allowable expenses:** registration fees, hotels, airfare, rental car and gasoline, ground transport, food (depending on team budget, full-detail receipts). **Unallowable:** personal items, insurance of any kind, entertainment, alcohol.
- **Travel card vs ProCard:** travel card only for travel expenses; procurement card (ProCard) for materials/supplies even when traveling. If no ProCard, submit receipts for personal reimbursement through Atlas. MIT's sales-tax exemption (ST-5) is valid in some states but not for travel expenses.
- **After trip:** trip reports within 30 days (Concur 2.0 guide). Receipts required for purchases over $75 except food; food receipts must show full detail; if food includes non-MIT people it is a business meeting. Missing receipt: fill out missing-receipt affidavit, mark Non-Recoverable. Credit card statements are not proof of payment.
- **Reimbursement:** open a travel report in Atlas; non-travel items via Request for Payment (RFP) with receipts. Personal mileage reimbursable; gas only for rental vehicles.
- Personal rentals: Avis/Budget leisure codes give discounts but no insurance.
