# Edgerton meeting, Feb 20, 2025

**Cards:** 2 ProCards + 2 travel cards. ProCard holders: Ruth (+1). Travel card holders: Sarah, Joseph. Better to spread cards so more people know how to use them. ProCard needs extensive training; travel card just needs a form (Edgerton FAQ). **NEVER buy gas on the ProCard.** While waiting for cards use Buy2Pay (B2P); purchases over $500 need approval.
- Tax: MIT is tax-exempt only in Massachusetts (Home Depot has a different tax form). Outside MA just pay the tax; if you use a personal card outside MA the tax is subtracted from the RFP, so DON'T use a personal card; the auditor may return tax money.
- Finance lead will see the Spokes account after Marvelin adds them.

**Bike purchasing:** need a quote for bikes, send to Marvelin. Quote won't be accurate until May because of a late-joining rider (Tian); get a quote for 7 via B2P, the 8th later on ProCard/B2P (if under $1,500?). J-WEL has a separate cost object/account; leftover J-WEL money from the previous year to be checked with Sandi (expiry unknown). Can also reimburse from personal credit card.

**Lessons/curriculum:** K-12 education contact Diane Brancazio for student feedback; ask Edgerton for build lesson ideas.

**Vehicles:** drivers MVR-checked; be specific about pickup/dropoff time and date; send Marvelin names and Kerberos IDs of drivers; extra drivers can be added only in person at the rental counter. **All extra drivers need to be at the car rental, otherwise Avis won't send help if the car breaks down. Next time ask how many drivers the rental company can add; MIT covers all 7 but if the rental company does not, towing will be a problem.** Cannot rent anything bigger than a 7-seater. Suggestion: rent just 1 car for the trip to DC. "Enterprise over Avis?" (the 2025 team ultimately used Enterprise for the main rental, ~$6,003.82).

**Misc:** "Santa tracker" on the van on the website; ask Marvelin if Lobby 10 reservation needed; share photos with Edgerton (Jamie Shallel is Edgerton social media chair); talk to the Spokes 2024 team about the J-WEL account; bump crowdfund email, cc Chris and Marvelin.
