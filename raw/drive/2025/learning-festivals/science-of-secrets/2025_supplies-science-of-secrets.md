**First purchase (~$99):** hole punchers (3 pack) $9; scissors (32 pack) $20; compasses (32 pack) $29; metal paper fasteners (3 x 100 pack, 10mm head) $15; markers (100 pack) $16; pencils (50 pack) $8.
**Estimates:** ~$40-50 one-time purchases then ~$1-2 per student. One-time needed (~$9): hole puncher. Optional (~$15): correction tape/marker, paper tape. Per person ours (<$30): scissors, markers/pens, pencils; maybe rulers. Take-home (a couple $): 1-2 page worksheet, one sheet of thicker paper printed with the cipher wheel, one paper fastener (~$0.03).
