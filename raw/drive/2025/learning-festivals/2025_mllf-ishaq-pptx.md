Goal: introduce ML (classification) and train/test a simple model. Outline: What is ML? (self-driving cars, Alexa/Siri, social media algorithms) -> How do machines learn (patterns from many examples) -> Activity at https://machinelearningforkids.co.uk : (1) each student draws a happy or sad face, (2) upload images to train, (3) test on held-out data -> Discussion: was the computer always right? how to help it learn? what would you create? -> Takeaway: more examples make smarter machines.
