**Robotic arm** (straws, cardboard, string): supplies - disposable straws, upholstery thread or dental floss, rubber washers, polymer clay, rubber bands, ping pong balls, Elmer's glue; tools - X-acto, ruler, fine marker, scissors, sewing needles. Ref: https://www.sciencebuddies.org/stem-activities/build-a-robot-hand . Question: control one finger or all with one string? improve to lift heavier things?

**Hovercraft** (balloon, CD, bottle cap): CDs, water bottle spouts, sandpaper, round balloons, hot glue gun, dot stickers; scissors, balloon pumps. Ref: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/edu/resources/project/make-a-hovercraft/

**3D printing lesson plan:** supplies - printer, laptops with TinkerCAD, filament, 3D pens. Plan: show printer printing (previous workshop's designs / a benchy); explain filament; demo printed overhang/tolerance sheets/rulers for design constraints; introduce TinkerCAD; walk through a simple build (sketch->extrude or boxes/cylinders); freeform design with constraints; print designs after the workshop while the next one runs.
