# First Week Food (June 2025)
- 06/04: dinner with Greta's family (DC area).
- 06/05: breakfast at Airbnb: yogurt (1), granola (1 bag), strawberries (1 box), blueberries (1 box), oatmeal (1), peanut butter (1), bananas (1 bunch). Dinner: takeout or dinner out in DC.
- 06/06: quick breakfast with Greta's family. Lunch on the road: turkey + cheese + hummus + avocado sandwiches (hummus + pesto + avocado for the vegan rider), apples, pretzels. Dinner with Warmshowers host.
- 06/07: breakfast with WS host. Same road lunch. Dinner at campground.
- 06/08: breakfast at campground (a parent possibly bringing it). Same road lunch. Dinner with a Warmshowers host; breakfast at WS host the next morning.
- "GOING GROCERY SHOPPING" reminder at end.
Tactic: hosts (Warmshowers, family) cover many dinners/breakfasts early; standard repeatable lunch kit.
