(Written by the Spokes 2024 team, left for Spokes 2025. Facts below are from the 2024 trip unless stated.)

## Admin/fundraising timing
- Tax-free forms expire 10/2024 so re-apply.
- Apply for 24-challenge micro challenges in October if wanted. Discretionary funding cycles generally end in Fall, so email people early.
- Annual RFP cycle supporting social impact experiential learning: https://elo.mit.edu/social-impact-elo-initiative/ (closes in fall; questions to ktrimble@mit.edu).
- Move website off Wix (got much more expensive). Reach out to Newton Free Library for a local LF.

## Route warnings (2024)
- Don't take 285 out of Denver (nightmare, unsafe). Don't take US-50 out of Tahoe (highway-like, little shoulder).

## Learning festivals
- TEJI @ MIT (Educational Justice Initiative), contact lperlman@mit.edu: discussed in Spring 2024 holding LFs in Boston correctional facilities before Spokes began, but too close to start; also useful for finding correctional facilities other than DC along the route.
- Hosts that expressed interest: Keely Pettijohn, Hermann MO, +1 970-234-5336 (nonprofit Youth Center, wants a STEM program); Alexander Stoll, St. Louis MO, 573-406-5474 (Excel Center adult high school, https://excel.mersgoodwill.org/).

## Housing
- Churches are hard to reach; call early, not 2-3 days before.
- Local library or city hall can redirect you to hosts. County fairgrounds are another good place to stay.

## Fundraising
- Go to Edgerton for advice: finance person Peggy will sit down and brainstorm fundraising.
- In-kind sponsorships are easier than money; request larger materials for specific LFs. Other Edgerton teams have corporate fundraising experience. 2024 team wasn't very successful.
- Don't do boba sales (too much labor, little profit). Spring Fest is fun but don't expect much. Set up crowdfunding website early.

## Day-to-day logistics (2024)
- ~90 min to get ready each morning; driver does bulk of packing; driver does laundry the night before their driving day and writes the host thank-you card the morning of. Lunch break daily (2023 had none).
- Charge front and back lights and keep them on the bike. Chamois butter, even preemptively.

## Finances
- 2 travel cards + 2 pro cards (driver and bike crew each have one of each).
- Have a specific budget BEFORE the trip.
- Card holders need every receipt; set up a collection system (2022: expense form; 2023: folder per day; 2024: text receipt to card holder). Physically collect too; the end-of-trip report asked for clearer images. Everyone must know what goes on travel vs pro card.

## Bikes
- 2024 had three bike types (Specialized Diverge E5, Specialized Allez, Liv Avail AR4), annoying for repair parts. Many flats on tubed tires (Hank had 12), frequent from Utah on.

## Training
- Midnight Marathon together (done as fundraiser in 2024). Practice hills, braking/turning downhill, one-handed riding, eating/drinking on bike.
- MIT Bike Lab teaches free bike repair weekly.
- If your bike sounds weird, stop and check (Rebecca broke a spoke unknowingly). Host David in Denver: "things start breaking once you start to replace them." Replace chain early (new chains in Moab; earlier is better, worn chain wears cassette). Safety checks in Denver (halfway point).

## Car
- Avis/Budget is poor but the only MIT-agreement company covering drivers under 21. Issues: talk to Edgerton and Jessica Tam (Office of VP for Finance), who got a discount after rental troubles.
- When renting: ask when last serviced, walk around and photograph scratches, check engine and tires, look up recalls for that model.
- Buy sturdy plastic storage boxes at Walmart on day 1; get a decent cooler (soft cooler stank); top-of-car carrier is a must (soft one ripped; hard-sided better).

## Shipping / end of trip
- Buy boxes at U-Haul (FedEx/UPS pricier). Bike shipping was a pain and expensive; used bikeflights.com. Box SIZE matters more than weight; $20 REI bike boxes were huge so shipping cost a lot.
- 2023 and 2024 did one-way rentals; in hindsight maybe not the best (more expensive plus shipping). Idea: check bikes on planes.

## Social media / website
- Fell behind on blogs; designate one blog lead. Post a pre-trip blog (people offer help).

## Misc
- Gets easier; by the end a 60-mile day was chill. Wash sleeping bags before leaving. Read past blogs. Reach out to past teams. Ship online orders to a future host.
