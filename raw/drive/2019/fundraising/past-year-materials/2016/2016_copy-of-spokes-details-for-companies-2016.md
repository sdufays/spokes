Spokes America 2016
Inspiring students to learn what they love: Spokes America is a bike trip across the United States designed to make STEM and project-based learning accessible to students everywhere! We are picking up where Spokes 2015, 2014, and 2013 left off, each of which were hugely successful.

Learn more at www.spokesamerica.com
   Spokes America: Who and Why 
Across the United States, students in middle schools and high schools aren’t getting the access they need to programs in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math (STEM). This summer, a group of 10 STEM students from Harvard and MIT will  bike across the country from Washington, D.C. to San Francisco, inspiring students to discover new passions and learn what they love. The Spokes America team members come from a range of backgrounds—engineering, computer science, biology, and physics—but we share a common passion: getting the next generation excited about learning STEM. 
Along our bike route, we will run 10-15 Learning Festivals, where students will have the opportunity to touch, feel, design, build, and learn. They will discover the joy of designing and creating something new, and they’ll leave our workshops excited to learn more. We’re just getting them started.
By biking, we want to show students that scientists can be athletic and that science and engineering are glamorous; biking will allow us to break down negative stereotypes.
As a SPONSOR of Spokes, you can help us make a lasting impact on the future of the United States. The U.S. will need more talented scientists, engineers, and programmers, and America’s youth will become those people—or they won’t. You can help us make the difference! 
   What We Need & What You Get 
To bike across the country, we’ll need: bikes and biking accessories to carry all of our gear and make our trip safe; proper biking and outdoor apparel; a support van: your donation will allow us to rent and insure a vehicle; food, lots and lots of food.
To make our workshops awesome, we’ll need: supplies, like laptops, computer parts, circuits, power supplies, various materials for engineering design kits, and other similar items necessary for us to effectively bring STEM education into classrooms.

   The Impact 
Spokes America means a lot to a lot of people. Students across the U.S. will be exposed to hands-on projects that show them that engineering is fun. Our workshops will also build off of what students see in their everyday lives by peeling back layers and showing them the science underneath the way they drive to school, use their computers, or even climb a tree.
Spokes will also help science and engineering teachers make a difference by exciting students in a new way by creating hands-on seminars.
As for us, we’re all college students. Travelling across the United States, meeting all kinds of new people, and teaching what we’ve come to love will be a formative experience for all of us.
Your sponsorship will help make this trip possible. 
Spokes America 2016 is picking up the successful thread of the 2015, 2014 and 2013 Spokes America teams. Check out how last year’s trip went on our website, http://spokesamerica15.tumblr.com/.
