Subject/greeting: "Hello from Spokes America!" Pitch: group of MIT and Harvard science/engineering students; make STEM and project-based learning accessible; 10 members biking DC to San Francisco; "We are asking you, YOU, to join our cause by sponsoring our journey." Problem paragraph: American education system struggling to produce enough scientists, engineers, software engineers "at companies like [YOUR ORGANIZATION]"; hosting 10-15 Learning Festivals where kids touch, feel, design, build; cycling challenges stereotype that STEM kids cannot be athletic. Sponsors enable creative problem-solving, connect teachers to resources, build economic foundation. Personalize: company mission (don't copy-paste), specific reason they'd care, relevant past actions, how audience matches theirs, 1-2 benefits. Attach sponsor-benefits document and expected budget; link spokesamerica.com and two promotional YouTube videos. Sign "[YOUR NAME] and the Spokes America teams".
