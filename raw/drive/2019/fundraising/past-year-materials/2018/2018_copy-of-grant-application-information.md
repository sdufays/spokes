Spokes America, MIT Edgerton Center, 77 Massachusetts Ave Rm 4-408, Cambridge MA 02139. MIT tax ID 04-2103594. MIT Giving page giving.mit.edu (search Spokes), account number 2530032. Mission: six-year program of MIT students cycling across the country; learning festivals at schools, libraries, museums, camps; defy stereotypes. 2018: smallest but most dedicated team; mentors from past teams. History: since 2013, 8-10 students to towns like Hazard KY and Newton KS. Safety: no member of any team has been injured on the road; tight schedule to avoid arriving after dark; helmets, reflective vests, lights; van as safety valve; extensive previous route notes. Grant purpose: educational supplies and journey necessities (food, bikes, camping equipment).
