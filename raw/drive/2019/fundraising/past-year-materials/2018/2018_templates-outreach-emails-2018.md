## An's Sponsorship Email (REI)

Hello from Spokes America! 

We are a team of 8 science and engineering students from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) in Cambridge, MA. This summer, we’re embarking on a huge adventure: a 3,500 mile bike ride from D.C. to San Francisco over the course of 75 days to host over 10 Learning Festivals in small towns along the route. You must be thinking: “You’re going to do…what?!"

Spokes America is a non-profit, student-run organization in collaboration with edX and Teach for America as part of an effort to rethink STEM education. Our team is comprised of 5 freshmen, 2 juniors, and a recent graduate from MIT, and while we initially came to MIT to pursue our passions in math and science, we believe that we now have a responsibility to share our knowledge to inspire future generations.

Learning Festival (n): an intensive, 8-hour day with 4 STEM-focused workshops that complement each school’s curriculum and highlight MIT’s hands-on approach to education. Our workshops range from Computer Science to Chemistry to Robotics to Design, and we aim to target schools and libraries in the middle of the U.S. where the STEM curriculum may not be as well-structured. 

Simply put, we need help. To accomplish this educational and physical feat, we are asking you, REI, to join our cause by sponsoring our journey. Your sponsorship is more than just a donation - it is an opportunity to get the REI name out to communities, small towns, and other bikers we’ll interact with along the way. More importantly, your sponsorship is the catalyst for STEM education, inspiration, and to prove that yes: engineers and scientists can be athletic too! As 8 broke college students with a powerful idea to rethink STEM education, we think your sponsorship is just what we need. 

We ask for a grant of $10,000, though we would appreciate any amount of money, equipment, or gear (ranging from clothes to helmets to shoes to camping gear). This money will help us acquire the necessities for our journey, improve the quality of our programming for students, and enable us to spread the passion for STEM to students from all over the country. We are excited to embark on this journey, and we hope that REI will join us for the ride.

Please reach out to me if you have any questions or concerns or if I can provide more information. Here is the link to our MIT Spokes website in-the-making: https://mitspokes.wixsite.com/mitspokes and our Spokes America website: http://www.spokesamerica.com/. Thank you for taking the time to read this email, and I look forward to hearing back from you. 

Respectfully,
An Jimenez and the MIT Spokes team 
MIT Class of 2021 | Mechanical Engineering

## Boston Globe pitch

Hello. My name is Kate, and I’m an undergraduate student at MIT. I’m reaching out on behalf of the MIT Spokes America 2018 team. This summer, we will be biking across the US from DC to San Francisco, teaching STEM workshops along the way. The mission is to spread science and engineering to rural areas of the country that would regularly lack this exposure. Also, the biking serves to break stereotypes about scientists/engineers, proving that we are athletic, strong, and adventurous. You can read more on our website here.

I am reaching out to you to see if the Boston Globe can provide any sort of publicity for the Spokes team. We are a 501c3 non-profit, and we need to raise $25,000. If you think that the Boston Globe or any of its affiliates might be able to help, please do let me know. We are happy to provide more specific information on route, curriculum, goals, etc.
