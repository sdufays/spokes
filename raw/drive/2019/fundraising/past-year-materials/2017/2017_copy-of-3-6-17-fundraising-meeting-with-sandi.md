- Sandi has last year's transaction list; account had $8,500 (note says $8,000 elsewhere); ~$21,000 spent last year (food, rental van, bikes); must raise **$25,000 or more**; send Edgerton Center a proposal; funds can transfer internally; can spend before money arrives, even go a little over. Maureen Moran (moranm@mit.edu) at MIT Giving set up the account number.
- Bikes: worked with Cambridge Bicycle two years in a row; they give a quote and Edgerton issues a purchase order (too large for a credit card); "very supportive".
- Two card types: **pro card** (materials/supplies; no travel; keep receipts, use tax-exempt forms) and **travel card** (water, food, gasoline, hotel, tolls). Not allowed: sunscreen, shampoo, soap, personal items. Each rider can have a travel card; pro card form from Sandra Lipnoski, 4-408.
- Departments: ~2 weeks to receive money after promising; follow up if nothing in 2 weeks; departments can transfer funds internally.
- Vehicles: Budget and Avis have MIT relationships; students under 25 can rent; they will try to sell liability waivers but MIT is self-insured; use the MIT corporate code; walk to the local Budget office for rates; book via Concur/MIT travel; just drive the car back; definitely an SUV (2019 later recommended minivan); switch drivers every 4-5 hours, no one drives alone; collect emergency contacts; medical is personal (use own insurance). Harvard and MIT sign forms; Stanford under Stanford.
