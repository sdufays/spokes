Key content (2019):
- Mission: 7-year-old program; MIT students cycle DC to SF, teach STEM workshops in rural communities; new in 2019: networking dinners about the college application process, paying for college.
- Expected impact: 50-75 students per LF = 500-750 students; networking dinners 20-40 students each = 200-400. LF list/dates: Challenger Learning Center KY Jun 10-11; Lab: Revolution MO Jun 23; St. Louis Homeschool Connection MO; aSTEAM Village MO Jul 1; Newton Public Library KS Jul 3; Boys and Girls Club of Denver Jul 11; Fort Collins Library Jul 13; Christa McAuliffe Space Center UT Jul 20; White Pine County Library NV Jul 25; Churchill County Library NV Jul 29; Boys and Girls Club Carson City Aug 1. Trip May 26 to Aug 12.
- Funds use: **rental vehicle**. Past years rented two vehicles to get to DC then used one DC to SF and back to Boston; one-way/leaving a car in SF is far costlier, so the car is driven back to Boston (this is also why the Boston-DC extra rental that stays in DC is costly). In 2019 a parent's car was going to be donated but insurance rules prohibited a personal vehicle, creating an urgent van-rental need. Van carries gear and teaching materials, gives injured/resting riders a role, and shuttles riders where biking is unsafe.
- Request: $4,000 to cover half of ~$6,000 food plus some lodging; grants announced in June, after the program starts, so pre-trip costs (bikes, cars) need other funding.
- Evaluation: surveys to community partners and students after each LF; debrief at MIT; applications for next team follow.
- 501(c)(3) status: yes (via MIT). Sponsor recognition: on website and social media before departure.
