- Abstract: eight MIT students bike across America teaching STEM workshops; program since 2013; new networking dinners.
- Plan: >3,500 miles over 79 days, ten LFs; workshops 1.5 hr each; typical LF = three 1.5-hr blocks with all four workshops running, lunch in middle, students choose 3 of 4. Survey community coordinators after each LF.
- Logistics: camping or Warmshowers hosts; safety via prior teams and MIT Student Activities Office; no biking at night or July 4.
- Timeline: team formed Dec 2018; squads fundraising/route/gear/education. At application time had met half of the $20,000 budget; 8 of 10 LFs confirmed; bikes and gear purchased; interview with NBC Boston Apr 27.
- Qualifications (teaching/tutoring/camp experience of each rider) and personal statements from all 8 riders (rural backgrounds, desire to challenge stereotypes). Includes full route table with LF sites bolded (same as "formatted route").
