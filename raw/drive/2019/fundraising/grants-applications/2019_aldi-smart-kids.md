Project (60 words): Spokes bikes DC to San Francisco teaching STEM workshops emphasizing MIT-style hands-on, real-world problem solving; ALDI funds would partially cover educational materials.
Organization: Since 2013 every summer Spokes bikes across the US to rethink STEM education; inspire students toward STEM and show scientists and engineers can be athletic and work in local communities. (ALDI stores are also on the route: St. Louis and Roanoke hosts mention ALDI for shopping.)
