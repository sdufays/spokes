### Notes carried over from the first pass
Planning stages Dec 2018 - May 2019:
- Research (12/18-2/19; $0): grants and deadlines; company sponsors; design/publish website; publishing materials; equipment research; develop workshops.
- Fundraising (2/19-4/19; $0): apply to grants; publish crowdfunding site; email alumni; email companies; contact MIT departments; reach out to news orgs.
- Route and LF research (2/19-4/19; $0): plan route; contact LF locations; confirm LFs and publicize; confirm hosts.
- Purchase gear and equipment (3/19-5/19; **$22,300 needed**): buy bikes early for practice; develop curriculum and purchase materials; rent a long-term minivan and a car for the DC trip.
- Trip (5/19-8/19; **$11,700 needed**): bike DC to SF; teach LFs.
Draft route (superseded by final): includes Roanoke Public Library Jun 4, Gardner KS, Emporia KS, Carson City Public Library Jul 30, Strawberry UT, end Aug 12 San Francisco, Aug 13 return home (flight or begin trip back).
