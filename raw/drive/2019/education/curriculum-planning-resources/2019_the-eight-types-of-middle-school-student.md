| Student | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Distracted Danny | Keep it short; no lecture over 3 min without a question ("raise your hand if..."). |
| Zoned-out Zoe | Short break and talk privately; don't call her out; ask a general question ("Do you agree with what Danny said?"). |
| Active Andy | Activities out of chairs; make group formation move (count off to corners); ask him to hand out materials. |
| Excited Emily | Save tangents for the end; "I know you know, but let's hear from someone else." |
| Shy Shawn | Assign groups; don't cold-call; respond enthusiastically when he volunteers. |
| Bystander Bernie | All-play questions (everyone answers at once; show 1-5 with hands). |
| Intimidated Irene | Pre-plan vocabulary; glossary on board; "have you heard this term?"; 1-5 understanding checks. |
| Chatty Cathy | Split friend groups; open-ended, specific discussion questions. |

Overall: Encourage them (thank risk-taking), relate to them (don't act like one), respect them (make them feel smart).
