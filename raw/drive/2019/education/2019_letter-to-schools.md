## Template: repeating Learning Festivals
Good afternoon [ ],

My name is Maile Jim, and I am a student at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in Cambridge, MA. I am part of a program called Spokes which sends a team of 8 MIT students on a 75-day biking trip across the United States in collaboration with edX and Teach for America as part of an effort to rethink STEM education. As we go, we will stop at schools, libraries, and camps throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions in STEM.

Last summer, our Spokes 2018 team stopped at [school name] around [date] to hold a Learning Festival, and our team would love to add this school to our route again! We predict that we'll be in the area around [date]. Let us know if you would be interested in having us this upcoming summer. We can provide more information about our curriculum and the specific dates as we approach the summer. Please don't hesitate to reach out with questions!

Best, Maile

Best, Maile
