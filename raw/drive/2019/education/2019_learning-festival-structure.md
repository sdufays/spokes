## Structures
- 2019: 9-10:30, 10:30-12, Lunch, 1-2:30; plus optional 6-8 evening block OR 2:30-4 social. Tweak per LF.
- 2018: 9-11, 11-1, Lunch, 2-4; or 8:30-10:30, snack, 11-1, lunch, 2-4; or with an inserted 1-hr other activity.
- For big groups add a 2nd day (e.g. 9-11, 11-1 or full repeat).
- Other activities: night blocks (7-8, 8-9; stargazing ~10 PM), teach 2 workshops instead of 3 and use the other block for a fun activity (stargazing, salsa dancing), unstructured bonding/Q&A time (lunch is good for this).
- Age groups: middle school, adaptable to high school.
- Opening/closing ceremony: little value unless a whole-group activity; could be short talk on why STEM interests us.
- Space: 4 rooms for 4 workshops.
- Ideas: "Firestorm" (rapid 5-min classes).
- Open questions: resources hosts should provide; whether kids do a different workshop each block; what happened at lunch last year.
