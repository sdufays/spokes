- Intro (10 min): What is chemistry? Why important?
- Whoosh Bottle (10): 5-gal water jug, methanol, matches, splints, beaker.
- Hands-on color change (15): 3 half-filled tubes + food coloring; add bleach / vinegar / hydrogen peroxide by dropper; kids decide chemical vs physical change (bleach changes color).
- Yeast + hydrogen peroxide heat (25): 1 tsp yeast, 1/4 cup H2O2, thermometer, bowl; record temps before/after; predict chemical vs physical.
- Elephant toothpaste (10): Erlenmeyer flask, H2O2, yeast, dish soap, food coloring.
- Slime (25): 3/4 cup warm water + 1 cup glue; separate 1/2 cup warm water + 2 tsp borax; mix; bags to take home.
