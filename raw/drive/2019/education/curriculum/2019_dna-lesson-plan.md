- Intro (10 min): what is DNA; genome as a book (chromosomes = chapters, genes = sentences, bases = words).
- Gel electrophoresis setup (30): DNA is polar; smaller pieces move faster ("fat cat, skinny cat"); food dye stands in for DNA. Protocol: gel in blue gel box, cover with TAE buffer, pipet 6 colors (red, yellow, green, blue, mystery 1, mystery 2), cover, plug in, start. Emphasize pipette technique (don't push all the way down).
- Strawberry DNA extraction (20): strawberry + 20 mL solution in ziplock, mush, cool 30 s, repeat; filter through cheesecloth/funnel into tube; add 2 mL ethanol down the side; white material is DNA (strawberries have 8 genome copies).
- Gel cont. (20): stop gels, read results (use flashlight if room is dark); challenge: identify mystery samples (1 simple, 2 ambiguous).
- Conclusion (10): 3 takeaways (DNA builds who you are; how scientists analyze DNA; DNA in health). Extras: CRISPR, bioethics. Ladder in well 1; wells 2-12 can spell a word.
