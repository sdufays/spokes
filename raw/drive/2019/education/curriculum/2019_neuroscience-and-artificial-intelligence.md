Neuroscience goals: synapses are the basis of brain function; brain structures serve functions; nervous system evolved for survival (e.g. color vision); how we record brain activity; amputee/prosthetic applications; interactive ideas: videos, Rube Goldberg builds, electrode demos.

AI segment: transition -> explanation (5 min, genre-guessing game, neural network) -> activity (10 min, train a face to react to kind vs mean things) -> applications (10 min) -> look at how models break (5 min, pair teams) -> free play (20 min).
Demo links: ml4a, deepart.io, what-dog.net, thispersondoesnotexist.com, teachablemachine.withgoogle.com, machinelearningforkids.co.uk, dalelane.co.uk. Applications: assistants, Netflix recs, grammar/sentiment, Watson/AlphaGo, self-driving vision, art, fashion, fake news, AR, adversarial examples.
