# Highlights: Spokes 2019 folder (most valuable facts for a 2027 team)

- Total 2019 funding was $33,251 (incl. $8,500 carried over from 2018) and ~$30,092 was actually spent: ~$18,689 on the trip, ~$11,403 on bikes and cars. Use as the calibration for a 2027 budget (2019, source: Categorized Budget with notes for 2020).
- 2019 funding sources: TBP Fellowship $3,500 (asked $5,000), Mad Money Grant $5,000, Edgerton Center $3,000, Texas Instruments $4,000, MathWorks $1,500, MIT Course 8 $2,500, Course 18 $500, Course 9 $250, SWE $400, crowdfunding ~$4,100+ (2019, source: Tracking Spreadsheet).
- Most large corporate cold asks fail: North Face and Gore-Tex said no; Boeing/Microsoft are invite-only; SpaceX, Shell, Sony, GoPro, Nikon went nowhere; Jamis needed 6 months' notice (2019, source: Tracking Spreadsheet; 04/22/19; Copy of Potential Sponsors 2018). What works: TI, MathWorks, MIT departments, Edgerton, MIT grants/fellowships.
- Fundraise early: MIT departments finalize budgets early and by spring excess corporate money is gone; apply to as many grants as possible and run an MIT crowdfunding campaign (2018-2019, source: How to Plan; Advice for Spokes 2020!).
- Food ran about $94.50/day for 8 riders (~$7,560 total, ~$12/person/day); lodging only ~$1,165 for 21 paid nights because Warmshowers hosts, churches and campsites carried most nights; bike repairs cost $2,815 (Fort Collins shop alone $1,379) (2019, source: Categorized Budget with notes for 2020).
- Bike repair lesson: change chains every ~1,000 miles and clean/oil weekly; learn to adjust derailleurs and brakes before leaving to avoid ~$2,800 in shop costs (2019, source: Categorized Budget with notes for 2020).
- Bikes: 8 Jamis Renegade Exile at ~$879 each bought via Cambridge Bicycle with a purchase order (too large for a credit card), ~10-15% discount; order bikes early and train on them (2019, sources: Budget - 2019; 3/11/19; Advice for Spokes 2020!).
- Do not rely on a donated personal vehicle: in 2019 insurance rules killed the family-van plan late and a $3,180-$3,847 rental had to be funded; rent a minivan; renting one-way and leaving it in SF costs far more than driving it back to Boston (2019, source: STEM Action Grant Application Form; Budget v1.1.xlsx).
- MIT admin rules: two cards (pro card for materials; travel card for food/gas/lodging/tolls), no personal items (sunscreen, shampoo) on Spokes funds, save/photo every receipt, Massachusetts purchases tax-exempt, TBP fellowship money is reimbursement-only (2017-2019, source: Agenda for Meeting with 2018 $$Peeps; 3/6/17 meeting; 3/11/19).
- A folder of receipts (plus national park pass and bike lock keys) was lost mid-trip; photograph receipts same day (2019, source: Lost Flyer; Missing Receipts).
- Learning Festivals: 12 hosts in 2019 (Challenger Learning Center Hazard KY, Lab: Revolution Farmington MO, St. Louis Homeschool Connection, aSTEAM Village KC, Newton Public Library KS, Boys and Girls Clubs in Denver and Carson City, Christa McAuliffe Space Center UT, libraries in Ely and Fallon NV); libraries and Boys and Girls Clubs were easiest, aSTEAM Village (80 kids) was hardest for classroom control (2019, source: Spokes 2019 Learning Festivals).
- Do not hold LFs on Sundays/major holidays; confirm LF dates before booking hosts; do a practice LF before departure; ask hosts whether lunch is provided; use a status spreadsheet and call hosts who are slow on email (2018-2019, sources: How to Plan; Advice for Spokes 2020!; Spokes 2019 Learning Festivals).
- LF prep slipped because funding was unresolved until April and riders were too busy in May; use the Workshop Planning Timeline (takeaways Apr 22, materials Apr 29, full workshop May 6, handouts May 13, practice May 20) (2019, source: Spokes 2019 Learning Festivals; Workshop Planning Timeline).
- Housing: Warmshowers hosts are free and usually feed you (standouts: Francine Johnston Waynesboro VA, Sherry Spradlin Harmony House Hazard KY, Orrisons Natural Bridge VA, Brad McCormick IL); where none exist, email 30+ churches (one reply: Union Church in Berea); also fire stations and police departments; write thank-you notes (2019, sources: 2019 Route Details: for 2020; churches; Advice for Spokes 2020!).
- Route hazards/tips: stay on Route 80 near Lebanon VA (Google Maps led onto downed trees); flooding closed Mississippi bridges in 2019 (Chester IL); book the right Camp Bethel; Nevada has no groceries/propane between Ely and Fallon, stock up in Ely; Utah stores may not take MIT Visa travel cards; gravel days in MO (2019, sources: 2019 Route Details: for 2020; Advice for Spokes 2020!).
- Team 2019 chose San Francisco (via Yosemite/Tahoe/Nevada) over Portland (Yellowstone/Tetons, 2 more days); trip dates May 26 to Aug 12 (~79 days, 3,500 miles) with Evan joining at Wise VA on Jun 8-9 after graduation (2019, sources: Ideas; formatted route; 02/11/19).
- Website cost: Wix Unlimited plan $168/year plus domain mitspokes.com renewal $14.95 each April; fronted by one rider and reimbursed (2019, source: Website Info).
- 2017 benchmark: TI gave $10,000 in 2017 (vs $4,000 in 2019) and Course 8 $2,500 both years; 2017 crowdfunding raised $4,380 from ~47 individual donors with a tiered perk ladder; 2019 perks had to be fulfilled after the trip (2017/2019, sources: Copy of OFFICIAL DONOR LIST; Crowdfunding Perks).
- Teaching middle schoolers: no lecture over 3 minutes ("pop song rule"), assign groups, get kids moving, be encouraging; grade-level prereq tables are in Curriculum Planning Resources (2019, source: Teaching Middle Schoolers - Cheat Sheet).
- Past 2019 workshops: Rapid prototyping/carnival games, DNA extraction + gel electrophoresis, Brain & AI (nice/mean neural net on machinelearningforkids.co.uk), Chemistry (whoosh bottle, elephant toothpaste); each workshop budgeted ~$75-$400 and lab supplies came partly from MIT Course 20/BioBuilder (2019, source: Spokes 2019 Review; 04/22/19; 5/13/19).
- Alumni directory 2013-2017 with mentor flags exists; use alumni and MIT clubs (Austin, Chicago, NJ etc.) as donors and housing/route contacts; ESG alumni coordinators in SF, DC, Boston, NYC were emailed in 2019 (2019, sources: Copy of Spokes Alumni; Tracking Spreadsheet).
