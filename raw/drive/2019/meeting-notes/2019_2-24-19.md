Questions for Delia and Kate and their answers: LFs - Louisa County Public Library, Challenger KY, Lab: Revolution; acceptable to skip Louisa County; MUST return to Challenger; enjoyed Newton Public Library, Loveland Public Library (go to Fort Collins), Space Center (simulator); iffy on aSTEAM Village (hard to teach, adult loves having them, kids hard to handle, small physical area, don't double up on workshops, but good for the kids). Memorable host: "Nefi house". Workshops: Scratch good for many ages but open-ended, limited laptops; building challenges fun but little theory; the car project very work-intensive/expensive for little kids. Delia has resources at MacGregor (sleeping bags, pads, bike stuff, cooking, bike rack). Kids come to LFs largely via daytime camps; night sessions hard. 2 full days at Challenger, others 1 day.
Dates: 5/24 last finals; 6/7 graduation; 5/26 drive to DC; 5/27 prep day DC; 5/28 first biking day; 6/9 Evan flies to meet group; 6/10 first LF. Top workshop choices: rapid prototyping, DNA extraction, fun with chemistry, catapults, probability game. Plan 3 x 1.5-hr sessions.
