We create a sub-team that forms under Bike n Build
We play by their rules - what does that mean?
They focus on affordable housing and youth development
We focus on youth education and empowerment
When we are part of their grant pool, what does that mean?
They use a part of their funding to provide small grants to the communities that they visit.
We could potentially dictate where some of that funding goes, or help to direct it towards educational endeavors.
Can we apply for our own grant money?
Yes! Grants would go through Bike & Build, so there would likely be overhead charges that would get paid to Bike & Build
Could we access their resources, like outside legal counsel, to help us write waivers etc that cover our specific needs?
At least initially, we would have to fit completely to their structure to ensure that their legal and insurance policies continue to apply to our riders.
If they wanted to support us in some new activity that did require a new waiver, they would be able to provide that support (but convincing them of this might be difficult)
Would we run our own trips, or combine trips with theirs? I.e. build a house and teach a workshop?
Either way! The important criteria is that our trips conform to all of their standards
2 van requirements
X number of riders
X training miles ridden before trip
Etc.
Do we need a new waiver specifically for the teaching component?
Would we hire part/full time staff as well?
Can we hire someone to support our work, and collaborate with Bike n Build?
Would they want input on who we hired? How would that look?
Can we join in on their insurance payments for our officers/directors etc?
We would not have our own officers and directors
How could we then become protected if we are providing support to Spokes riders?
EG What if our trips do a unique route? If we are the ones recommending that route how are we protected based on that?
Are there any concerns of, for example, us giving advice on teaching a rocket class and then a student burning their hand? How are we protected from that?
Would we form an independent board or have a representative on their board?
Either representative on theirs or no board presence.
We may have the option of creating a Advisory Council for education, that is not a formal Board
