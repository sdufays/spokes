Spout Workshop Plan

Introduce ourselves
Show the students the robot they will be building (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s6X6d-RXNps)
Ask the students how they think it works.
Write a list of design specifications using the students’ answers
The right motor must change direction if the the left antenna is pressed
The left motor must change direction if the right antenna is pressed
Tell the students that the workshop will be divided in several challenges that they will have to overcome by working in groups of 2
Make sure everyone knows how to use the tools at their disposal (wire stripper, pliers, hot glue gun)
Challenge 1: make the motor spin
After 5 minutes, ask someone to explain how he made the motor spin.
Ask the students questions
What happens when you switch the terminals?
Draw a circuit diagram of what they built (introduce symbols for batteries and motors)
Give the students a brief introduction about how LEDs work.
Explain that in order to produce light, the positive end of the LED must be connected to the higher voltage and the negative end of the LED must be connected to the lower voltage.
Explain that you need to add resistors in series to avoid burning the LED.
Challenge 2: make the LEDs shine.
After 5 minutes, ask someone to explain how they solved the problem
Draw a circuit diagram of what they built (introduce symbols of resistors and LEDs)
Give the students a brief introduction about how on/off switches work
Explain what terminals they need to use (terminals 2 and 3)
Explain that the wires cannot touch.
Challenge 3: make the LED turn on and off when you move the switch.
After 15 minutes, ask someone to explain how they solved the problem.
Draw a circuit diagram of what they built (introduce symbol of switches)
Give the students a brief introduction about how micro switches work
Show them the diagram of micro switch terminals (https://www.micromark.com/html_pages/instructions/82611i/82611microswitches.htm)
Challenge 4: Connect two LEDs to the micro switch. When the lever is not pressed, LED 1 should be on and LED 2 should be off. When the lever is pressed, LED 1 should be off and LED 1 should be on.
After 15 minutes, ask someone to explain how they solved the problem
Draw a circuit diagram of what they built (introduce symbol of micro switches)
Give the students a brief introduction about how batteries work
Explain that voltages add if batteries are in series
Show the students that the red wire is at a 3V potential, the black wire is at a 0V potential, and the back of the battery is at a 1.5V potential
Challenge 5: Design a circuit that meets the specifications defined at the beginning
The right motor must change direction if the the left antenna is pressed
The left motor must change direction if the right antenna is pressed
After 30 minutes, ask someone to share their design
If there are problems with the circuit, explain what the problems are.
Show them the circuit that we used in our robot
Build the robot!
