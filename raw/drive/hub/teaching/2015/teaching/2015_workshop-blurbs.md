Intro to Computer Science:

	In the world today, programming has become almost as vital a skill as reading and writing. Companies these days look for all of their future employees to have a basic knowledge of computer languages. But to learn a computer language you need a certain way of thinking. In this workshop students will learn the important skills of how to think procedurally, which is the first step to becoming a computer programmer. Once they’ve had a chance to explore this new concept, they will implement it with the online programming tool Scratch. After the workshop students will have the basic skills necessary to learn any programming language they wish to pursue.

Introduction to Electrical Engineering

Electrical engineering is one of the most famous engineering fields that attracts lots of students each year. In this workshop, students will learn about basics of electrical circuits: how to arrange a circuit and the different components of a circuit. After an introduction to these concepts, students will work on building a Spout robot with their knowledge of circuits and circuit elements. Students are encouraged to think about personalizing their robots in the future by adding circuit elements or design variations.
