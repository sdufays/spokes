Hello Paul,
Spokes is a group of students from MIT and UC Berkeley who love learning. We have all found passions –
mechanical design, music, film, neuroscience - and spend time pursuing them simply for the joy they
bring us. We think everyone should have the opportunity to pursue a passion of their own. This summer,
we are testing what we hope will become a national mentorship program.
Starting on June 11th, our team of 8 MIT and UC Berkeley students is cycling 4,000 miles across the US,
stopping in 10 towns over the course of 80 days. In each town we will offer a “Learning Festival” to
expose high school students to powerful new ideas, help them outline projects of their own, and
connect them with fantastic mentors to make their ideas a reality.

We were going to have our bikes spon sored by a Scottish supplier, however they have fallen through at
this critical stage . We are in need of 7  touring bikes, however we don’t have the funds necessary to
purchase them. We hope you would be willing to  provide us with $8,000 to purchase the bicycles we
need to complete this trip. An additional $2,000 beyond that will help us rent a support car.  We
understand if you are not able to make such a donation  at this time, and  appreciate any support that
you are able to provide.
The founder of Spokes , Turner Bohlen, has contacted a 501(c)3, Startlabs, that  is willing to accept
donation’s on our behalf. This organization  now runs Startup Bootcamp, which I believe you  have
participated in before. Their leadership has assured us that any donation intended for our initiative will
be used, in full, to support Spokes.
We are excited about the impact that we are about to have, and we hope that you will become a part of
it.  If you would like to support our project, please shoot me an email confirming your sponsorship. From
there, we will arrange a money transfer to Startlabs.
Sincerely,
The Spokes Team

P.S. We begin our journey on June 10th with 7 flights to San Francisco. We would like to have confirmed
any additional funds by June1st so that we have time to effect any final changes on our budget.
