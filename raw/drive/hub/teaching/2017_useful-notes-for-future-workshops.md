Spokes Workshop Teaching Interview Questions

Because the workshops are meant to be creative and incredibly varied, I think the most helpful information to us and to new members is about what the Spokes Member did for their workshop and how the students reacted to the workshop.

General Idea - Help any new member creating a workshop obtain a better sense of the basic aspects of an effective and efficient workshop.

Workshop Content
What workshop/subject did you teach the students?
What important changes/adjustments did you make during the trip?
What were the most valuable lessons that the students got from your workshop?
What were some common problems that you found with your workshop?
I.e, coweye dissection had a lot of trouble teaching young kids how to use a scalpel

Workshop Cost
How much did your workshop cost?
Any supplies that were essential/not needed?
Where did you obtain the majority of your materials?
How were your materials transported?
How much space did your materials take?

Student Reaction
What was the optimal classroom size that you taught?
What age group of students (K-5, 6-8, 9-12) worked best with your workshop? (Were the most attentive, learned the most, had the most fun, whatever you felt like “clicked”)

Teaching Festival Location
What location/teaching situation was ideal for your workshop?
I.e. library, school, summer camp and classroom, computer lab, a few tables, gymnasium, etc.
How did the teaching location react? How was publicity done? What was the connection and communication like between Spokes and the learning festival location?
I.e. Did they respond to emails? Were they flexible with dates? etc.
