Lean Manufacturing Simulation

Overarching Objectives:

Hands-on teaching: Teach in an engaging, hands-on way - give students an appreciation for learning through exploring and hacking things in their own lives.

Lifelong curiosity: Try to inspire kids to plan projects, think creatively, explore, and tinker after we leave / throughout the year.

Introduce students to engineering: Give students a better understanding of what engineers do.

Technical Objectives:

Learn to experiment with real-world simulations: The students would work in teams to try to optimize their “manufacturing lines.” With each round, they would have the opportunity to change the way they run the line, and see the impact of their changes in real time.

Understand how to think like an engineer/scientist: We will encourage students to ask “how will changing this variable change the output?” which is the basis of the scientific method.

Encourage curiosity: We want to introduce students to basic concepts of lean manufacturing by allowing them to ask questions about it and then finding the answers out themselves. We want them to apply the simulation method we used here in answering other questions they might have (say about road traffic patterns, weather patterns, anything modeled mathematically)

Introduce students to manufacturing: Manufacturing is everywhere, but we hardly ever think about it. By running simulations ourselves, we’ll dive headfirst into understanding how manufacturing processes work.
