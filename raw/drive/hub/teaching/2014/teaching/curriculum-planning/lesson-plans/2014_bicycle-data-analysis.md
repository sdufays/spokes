Bicycle Data Analysis

Overarching Objectives:

Hands-on teaching: Teach in an engaging, hands-on way - give students an appreciation for learning through exploring and hacking things in their own lives.

Lifelong curiosity: Try to inspire kids to plan projects, think creatively, explore, and tinker after we leave / throughout the year.

Learn with bikes: Get kids excited about bicycles, building, and being creative.  Share the impact bicycles and seemingly simple engineering have and can have on the world.

Technical Objectives:

Teach how engineering modeling works: We’ll ask basic questions like: How much force do you apply to a bicycle wheel? and then collect data in real time to show that their estimates are correct.
