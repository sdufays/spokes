Field work for Education

Purpose
Our workshops gives high school students
- a glimpse at many dozen areas of human endeavor they’ve never seen before exposure
- the motivation to follow their curiosity
- one practice run of stubbornness

Portable splash?
- splash is better because of it size and breadth
- does splash work?
- LETS TALK TO SPLASH

Can we just go in and suggest a few dozen different ridiculous things people are doing in the world and let the students latch on to one of them.

Quantitfication?
- count the number of people working on ideas/projects/anything in the field you taught 2 months later
- grades/teacher impressions in the field you taught
- student surveys
- parent surveys
- college admissions/jobs (LONG TERM)
- our version of “test”
- sign ups if we do it again
- referrals
- engagement

Hypothesis
Is there a forum we can get people using? Or do they just engage somewhere?
