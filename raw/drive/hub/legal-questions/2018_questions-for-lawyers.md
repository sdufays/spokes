Questions for Lawyers:

What, if any, is the legal liability to officers/members associated with non-profit? (Considering that Spokes involves cross-country bicycle trips with a non-zero risk of accidents etc)
How to deal with liability of supporting bicycle trips? Should we be purchasing liability insurance for individuals/the nonprofit organization?
Can you help us draft waivers for participants (students and cyclists)?
Should we setup a corporation before we have fully determined all of these issues?
Can you help us with filing as a non-profit and filing as 501(c)3?
Minimum things we need to do to stay active as a non-profit (in Delaware)? Costs associated with that?
Is Delaware a good place to incorporate or should we look elsewhere?
Are our personal reputations and networks at risk by leading an organization like this (one that has the possibility of physical harm)? What can we do to minimize those risks? Are there other examples out there of organizations that have successfully (or unsuccessfully) navigated similar situations?
