“How do we run an effective team?”
“How do we represent the Spokes vision?”
Centralize past resources (organize and compile the following from past years in one easily accessible platform):
Curriculums
Old files
Step-by-step instructions on workshops (what works and what doesn’t)
Results from past surveys/questionnaires/student feedback forms
List of resources/contacts for teaching mentorship
List of schools/practice opportunities (testing workshops out with Spark classes, libraries, local schools, etc. before the trip)
Contact information (obtain from previous team)
Past hosts
Past educators
MIT contacts (Sandy, helpful professors, etc)
Fundraising information
Past sponsors with amounts and notes
Easy to use/adapt standardized sponsorship letter/email templates
Easy to use, standardized crowdfunding templates
More broad logistical information
“Spokes calendar”--laying out a step-by-step template for when to get things done by, what to prioritize, etc. (emphasizing that the calendar is a guideline and not a rule)
Support vehicle advice/renting
Financial information (instructions on how to spend money/get reimbursed on the trip)
Route planning (past routes with exact schedules and notes)
General tips/safety advice
Resources to learn first aid
Bike maintenance
Road safety
General advice! (Categories can be formed depending on the advice that comes in)
Archive of past years’ blogs/social media
Point contact person (“Call me anytime”)
Previous teams that are still in school/easily accessible on campus = on retainer for information/mentorship (or just the previous year, and anything past that = opt-in)
Spokes employee to handle questions while on the trip/check in with team?
Spokes discussion forums (questions and answers archived)
Mentorship “groups” of past members who all had the same “role” (education coordinator can reach out to past education coordinators, etc)
Route planning, equipment purchase
Past routes with durations and notes
Tips for equipment purchase
Policies, how to communicate with groups you meet
Spokes vision and purpose
Protocols with respect to behavior (establish acceptable methods of dealing with people in a non-patronizing manner)
Protocols with respect to learning festivals (do we want to have a set Spokes Learning Festival format?) i.e. reminding teams to reach out afterwards and take action to build longer lasting relationships, etc.

1 year goals
1) Have information listed above collected, compiled, organized in a reasonable, easy to use manner
Wiki with basic components--workshop resources, fundraising templates, PR kit, policies, “Spokes calendar,” contact info, general safety tips
2) Have mentorship framework of past Spokes members in place (establish who is interested, how they wish to contribute, in what capacity, have a communication system in place)
Perhaps adjust the framework of Spokes as a club? Delegate specific committees for certain tasks (mainly fundraising) that would benefit from continuity

Tasks: By Sept 26
Set up some MVP of a wiki
Gather small team of Spokes members who are willing to collect the necessary information from past teams
Email members who would potentially be interested in doing so
Potentially meet to introduce Hub, nail down exactly what a Spokes wiki should contain, how to establish mentorship framework
Figure out what information we need to request from past teams, what’s already on Drive, etc.
