Spokes Tanzania Email

Hello!

I have some very exciting Spokes news :) It turns out we have the opportunity to run a trip in Tanzania next year with the help of some local NGO organizers! We’re looking to put together a 3000 KM trip around the country with participants from Tanzania and abroad, and deliver very low cost science and engineering education to schools across the country. Access to science and engineering education is incredibly lacking in Tanzania.

I thought of you when I heard of the trip and thought this might be something you’d want to be involved in! Would you like to join the organizing team for the trip?

You don’t have to go on the trip itself, but you would help define what the trip is, its goals, and its team members. Our first meeting is going to be in 2 weeks.

What do you think?

Spokes Central Email

Hi There!

I just got some big Spokes news. After running for 5 years, Claire, Turner, and the more recent team leads have decided that its time for Spokes to become its own non-profit and provide a bit more support to the teams doing trips.

As I was talking with everyone about this project, I thought you might be excited to join in on the effort! I so appreciated working with you during our trip. It would be awesome to have an excuse to hang out again!

We’re going to meet for the first time in two weeks. We’ll be working on establishing an advisory board, becoming a registered 501(c)3, and defining better ways of fundraising and supporting teams around the US. We’ll also be thinking a bit about how to bring all the spokes alums together for some get togethers.

What do you think? Want to join the project?
