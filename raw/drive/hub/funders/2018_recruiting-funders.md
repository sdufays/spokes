Recruiting Funders
Search for possible funders
Linkedin search keywords: “nonprofit”, “education”, “angel investing”, “funding”, “grant”, “foundation”
Add to central spreadsheet
Email funders to gauge interest
Set up phone call
Review Spokes Pitch Deck
Gather feedback, and share with Drew, Turner, Emma
At end of call, if it seems like a possibility, ask, “Is this something you might like to help use kick start with a small amount of funding?”
If yes, great! Connect with Drew, Turner, or Emma to talk through details
If no but interested in staying involved, note in doc
If hard no, say thanks and head out.
Logistics:
Set up the entity (LLC?)
Open a bank account

Email:

Hi _____ -

I’m part of Spokes America, a 5-year-old organization that helps middle and high school students develop their creativity and critical thinking by sharing project-based STEM workshops from the backs of our bicycles.

We’ve grown organically so far, and now work with Harvard, MIT, and Stanford, and help to support a UK government-sponsored trip.

We’re looking to raise a small amount of money - $20 to $30K - to kickstart the orgs next step. I wondered if this might be something you would be interested in partnering on & helping to fund.

Would you be willing to talk a bit more about the possiblities here?

Thank you so much for your time. I’ve attached a one pager to give you a bit more context.
