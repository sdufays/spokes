Spokes helps middle and high school students develop their creativity and critical thinking by sharing project-based STEM workshops from the backs of our bicycles.

Sharing Creative STEM Learning
Spokes opens K-12 students to new possibilities in two ways:
We show up by bicycle. You can get to any goal under your own power.
We teach workshops that help develop creative thinking. Each workshop 1) introduces students to a new and exciting topic and 2) gives them a framework for pursuing an original idea. Every workshop includes dedicated time for students to explore their own questions with the support of the workshop instructor.

Developing Tomorrow’s Educational Leaders
Spokes is organized around local teams. In groups of 6 to 8, college students:
Design and iterate new project-based workshops
Plan a supported bicycle trip to underserved communities
Deliver workshops to 200 to 400 K-12 students
Share their love of the topic they teach.
Gain first-hand understanding of the breadth of US educational experiences

Spokes So Far
We’re a 5-year old organization that has grown organically thanks to the hard work of individual students at MIT, Harvard, and Stanford. Each year, teams of 6 to 8 college students bicycle 4,000 miles across the US to offer original workshops to educationally underserved communities.

So far, 44 college students have taught approximately 1,900 students in 31 communities.

Next Steps
Spokes is expanding. In 2017, we started The Spokes Hub, a central group of Spokes alumni to increase the impact of new Spokes teams organized by universities across the US. The Hub will
Measure impact and pass down best-practices learned in prior years
Mentor team officers in trip logistics, financial sponsorship, and workshop development
Coordinate workshop locations and publicity between all university teams

Spokes Vision
Spokes is building a self-sustaining movement that values self-directed learning styles and empowers all individuals to start their own project and pursue their own ideas. Learning should be tangible, accessible, and community focused.

OTHER NOTES

We all believe in the power of self-directed, hands-on learning as a tool to build/nurture/cultivate a go-and-do-it mindset in educationally underrepresented communities. Through our learning festivals, we teach our younger peers to become self-directed learners through hands-on projects, without the pressure of academic success.

Provide them on-demand support
Demonstrate how to work on a new project

Spokes workshops provide this self-driven education experience to your local community at no cost and high impact value.

Spokes workshop has three parts:
Structured learning of a new, exciting idea
Free space for students to explore that idea through original projects
An instructor that is truly passionate about what they are teaching
