Info to maybe put on wiki?

Logistics documentation from previous years (e.g. previous routes, equipment, tools, budgets, etc.) JL ECB MH CY
Financial structure (i.e. reimbursements, credit cards, etc) ECB TB CY MH JL
Resources or training in hands-on education; some sort of direction on how to design your own lessons, what works well, etc.  TB MH
Educational notes from previous years: workshops taught, materials needed, and feedback on the lesson CY JL
Personal mentorship from older team members TB CY
Developing ways to survey the outreach effects (say through surveys or feedback forms that can be looked at offsite by the Hub while the team is traveling) ECB JL MH CY

What do we share as a vision for Spokes?

Way for kids to connect with students who are slightly older, next step in life
Way for young adults to discover the country
Experimentation in education - try different teaching techniques
Spread technical literacy through broad topics
Make kids unafraid to be hands on by taking apart things like bicycles
Teaching kids they can do amazing things like bike across the country
The people who organize it learn incredible leadership/management skills
A way for kids to teach kids
Influence education policy in the US?
Study the state of education across the country
Pay it forward system for students who love STEM
Build a national dialogue around the value of STEM education (vs things like sports which get a lot more funding in most schools)
Way of improving american STEAM education
Equal opportunity for all - focus on serving underserved communities as well as ones that are more engaged
Why do children from underserved communities not get into STEM as much?
Lack of resources
Lack of positive relationships with mentors who can help them through the process
What if Spokes focused much more on the personal relationship over the long term?
Lots to ask from college students who think they’re only doing a summer trip
Do we change the philosophy so that they’re like a “big brother/sister” and making a buddy on the trip who they’re going to commit to helping over the longer term?
We reach a lot more students than we have cyclists…
Do we have a buddy cyclist for each community that we visit, and anyone from that community can ask the cyclist questions for the next year, until the next team rides through the same community?
Do we want to talk about the effect of the trip on the riders - this is a way for building a community of young adults who have seen more of the US than many people will in their lifetime
Its a way of making people more open-minded, able to understand people from different backgrounds within one country
Democratizing effects of Spokes
Importance of developing young leaders who have seen the whole country (remember Sam Green’s campaign for president? :))
This already has a lot of grassroots strength, which means a lot of people think it is worthwhile and valuable. What if someday kids who meet Spokes riders became Spokes riders themselves? It would be like a self-growing, completely open fellowship of people who care about education and perpetuate it in some kind of amazing virtuous cycle!
Its a way of connecting “coastal kids” (i.e. the largely liberal young people who attend universities on the coasts of the US) with the rest of america
If Spokes affected everyone positively in the country, and Spokes participants now had personal relationships with all kinds of people with all kinds of socioeconomic/political backgrounds, how would that affect national politics? Would we be more connected? Less out of touch with the “other”? More willing to have a conversation than be partisan? Able to unite over common interests like improving education?
