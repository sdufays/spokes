Spokes helps middle and high school students develop their creativity and critical thinking by sharing project-based STEM workshops from the backs of our bicycles.

Sharing Creative STEM Learning
Spokes opens K-12 students to new possibilities in two ways:
We show up by bicycle. You can get to your goal under your own power.
We teach workshops that 1) introduce students to a new and exciting topic and 2) give them a framework for pursuing an original idea. Every workshop includes time for students to explore their own questions with the support of the instructor.

Developing Tomorrow’s Educational Leaders
Spokes is organized around college teams. In groups of 6 to 8, college students:
Design and iterate new project-based workshops
Deliver workshops to 200 to 400 K-12 students
Share their love of the topic they teach
Gain first-hand understanding of the breadth of US educational experiences

Growing Spokes
For 5 years, Spokes has grown organically thanks to the hard work of students at MIT, Harvard, and Stanford. So far, 44 college students have taught approximately 1,900 students in 31 communities.

In 2017, we began conversations with our third, fourth, and fifth university partners. We launched Spokes Hub, a central group of Spokes alumni that will
Measure and implement best-practices to increase impact
Mentor team officers in trip logistics and workshop development
Coordinate workshop locations and publicity between all university teams

Spokes Vision
Spokes is building a self-sustaining movement that values self-directed learning styles and empowers all individuals to start their own project and pursue their own ideas. Learning should be tangible, accessible, and community focused.
