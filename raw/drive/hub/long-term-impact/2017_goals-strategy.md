Goals:
(Many of these things can happen in parallel!)

Stage 1: Information Gathering
Get a picture of the current “education ecosystem” in the US, as well as in specific past or potential teaching locations (surveys, interviews, research)
Gather feedback from stakeholders on what could help improve Spokes’ long-term impact (surveys, interviews)
Gather feedback on the long-term impact of past Spokes trips (surveys, interviews)
Research “prior art” and its impact: educational initiatives, summer teaching programs, outreach, educational theory, etc. (research)
Research program assessment methods (research, find expertise)
Stage 2: Define Program Goals
Define strategy and goals for improving Spokes’ long-term impact
Brainstorm ideas for improving engagement with students and teachers
Develop evaluation plan for assessing impact of different initiatives
Stage 3: Test and implement!
Prioritize and test initiatives with existing teaching locations
Iterate / improve!

First step: put together a realistic timeline that will let us start collecting information ASAP, and hopefully start testing some new initiatives in time for this summer’s Spokes trips. (Maddie: TO DO for two weeks from now).

Questions:

Categories:
Current education “ecosystem” / “landscape”
Educational / content
Logistics / participation
Long-term support
Outreach and involvement

Specifics:
Current education ecosystem:
Trying to get a good picture of the current education system, what works, what needs improvement, etc.  What do students feel is missing?  Teachers?
Describe current system
What works well?  What is missing?
Do students feel like school prepared them for work and life?  Were classes engaging?  What classes and teachers do they remember?  What learning experiences made a long-term impact?
What opportunities do students have during and after school?  Are they prepared for work / life?
Are teachers supported?  What do they need or feel is missing?
Educational / content
What sort of content do folks want to see from Spokes?  What did they like / use from previous Spokes trips, if applicable?
What sorts of workshops do you think would be useful?  Fun / engaging?
If you participated in previous Spokes trips, what workshops did you attend?  Did you find the workshop helpful or inspiring?
What are you interested in learning and/or teaching?
Logistics / participation
Is it easy for students to participate in Spokes over the summer?  What would enable more students to participate, or make participation easier?  As a teacher / program director, what would enable Spokes to interface more effectively?
Could you (or your students, children, etc.) participate in a summer workshop?  How long of a workshop(s) / event could you participate in? What length would you prefer?
If not, what would allow you (or your child / student / etc.) to participate?  (e.g. child care, meals, coordinating with a summer program or school, etc.)

Long-term support
What sorts of long-term connections should Spokes be pursuing, and what might be useful?
What did the students remember most about Spokes trips after they happened? What did they want to do more of?
Did any students stay in touch with Spokes members over the long term?
Would long term contact with Spokes mentors (i.e. college age students) be useful to students?
If students had access to challenges or puzzles from Spokes, would they participate?
Would students be interested in reaching other students across the country?
Outreach and involvement
How can we reach more students / educators?  Who is not benefiting from Spokes, and how can we reach out to those people?
