Components:
1. Rides: mid distance, interval training, long distance, climbing/descents. Outdoor road rides (endurance, skill); indoor stationary (endurance); outdoor gravel (skill, fun): Wompatuck gravel ride (ridewithgps.com/routes/49424278), Middlesex Fells rides (Trailforks).
2. Strength: upper body (back, lower back, shoulders, arms, grip - stay upright, downhill taxing on forearms); core (balance, shock absorption); lower body (quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves, single-leg; use clip-ins to engage all lower-body muscles).
- Maybe ride with MIT cycling club for group practice. Midnight Marathon (team bonding + training; April 19, 2026).
