## Reinvent the Learning Festival
Create a new LF model (target: rural middle schoolers). How to ensure lasting community impact? What does an LF look like for a student? How would your team divide roles?
## Broke-Bike Mountain
Team arrives at dusk at Breaks Interstate Park; tomorrow to Hazard, KY; route not finalized; one bike will not shift (unrideable); camped last night so everything must be repacked; a Warmshowers host in Hazard offers dinner at 6 PM. Plan the day: wake-up/departure, food/water/breaks, broken bike, differing speeds, safe and on-time arrival. (Link: tinyurl.com/spokesinterviews)
