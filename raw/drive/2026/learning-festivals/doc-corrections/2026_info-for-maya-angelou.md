# Info for Maya Angelou / DC Dept. of Corrections (6/1/2026)

## Department of Corrections (DoC) session
- Bring ID, wear baggy clothes.
- Workshops: Monster, 3D (Dream Home), Iodine.
- Arrive 8:00 am; meet at 7:45 am at Le Pain Quotidien (Capitol Hill, https://www.lepainquotidien.com/us/en/stores/307). Start 8:30, end 10:30.
- About 25 students, 2 groups (another note: 19 students, 8 per group); 2 learning festivals; 2+ people go to the adult DC jail. Write down who is going and everyone's names (needed for entry).

## Youth Services Center (YSC) / Maya Angelou Academy (DYRS)
- Address: 1000 Mount Olivet Road NE, Washington, DC 20002.
- Arrive 7:15 am (team arrives 7:20-7:30; 8 am start). Lunch at 11:30 at YSC (pizza provided by Zia). School ends 3:30 pm.
- All five workshops, 40-minute workshops; measure out materials for them in advance. 5 people at the juvenile center, then all 7 come and do the intro. 2-3 learning festivals per classroom; about 120-130 kids.

## Rules and lessons from the host
- Cannot keep anything (nothing left behind!).
- Most successful format is hands-on. Edible things are OK.
- Bottle rocket works at the youth center but not at the older center.
- Send host a list of needed items with hyperlinks (Amazon links); floppy rulers noted.
- Do NOT bring: glass, sharp metal, scissors.
- 1 or 2 team members usually go in beforehand.
