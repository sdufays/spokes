# SPOKES 2026 LEARNING FESTIVAL WORKSHOPS (flyer text)
- The Invisible Force: Science of Electromagnetism - build and test working solenoid motors.
- Design Your Dream Home: From Idea to Model - 2D sketches to 3D paper models.
- Now You See Me, Now You Don't - chemistry: colorless liquid turns dark blue; limiting reagents.
- The Monster Genome Project - Punnett squares determine a creature's traits; bring monsters to life with craft supplies.
- Turn it On! Bringing Electronics to Life - build simple circuits: LED, current, wires, resistors, motors.
