- Memorable (Sarah Schmitt, Spokes '25): Day 1 energy; Loveland Pass 11,990 ft (go at your own pace); all five Utah national parks (Capitol Reef = International Dark Sky Park); US Hwy 50 "Loneliest Road" in Nevada.
- 2026 team looks forward to: teaching (some LF sites have hosted Spokes for up to 10 years), Utah national parks, arriving in SF (several teammates from there), Warmshowers hosts in rural/suburban/urban places.
- Route choice: route subteam balances safety and housing (towns with hosts vs sensible mileage); uses Google searches, Satellite View, past Spokes members; avoid gravel downhills. 11 prior MIT teams.
- Motivation (Tatiana Vassiliev): teaching STEM since middle school, first half-marathon, wanted to do something seriously hard.
- Impact: Stanford + MIT Spokes brought STEM to thousands of kids in small-town/rural America.
- Challenges (Sarah Schmitt, 2025): anaphylaxis 60+ miles from nearest hospital in rural northern Nevada (Epi-Pen; van coordination while supporting riders); headwinds, rain, long climbs, mud.
- Pitch to donors: spokes is free for every student; money pays for the van, bikes, housing and LF materials.
- Basic info block: 7 MIT students (names/courses) and mission paragraph (reusable in grant/press materials).
