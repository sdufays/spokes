Rest Day in South Lake Tahoe — 0 mi. Author: Nate (2026).

- Late start (hot tub, avocado toast, Five Guys burger). Drew's rear tire blew out two days earlier; he believed there was a Trek store in North Lake Tahoe, but the nearest was in Reno (~1 hour each way). Plan: beach, Drew drops team and goes to Reno shop, returns ~5 pm. Lesson: verify bike shop locations/hours before the rest day.
- Beach (Sand Harbor-area state park): a boat ramp area said "No Drop Off"; rangers interrogated Caroline. Team members without phones got separated from the group; the team packed up and left them, unaware. A bear walked past and checked a dumpster; ranger shouted at beachgoers to leave. Lessons: keep phones and know a teammate's number; keep food away from bears.
- Dinner cooked at the Airbnb (burgers, baked potatoes, fruit, corn salad, mac and cheese). Drew splashed hot oil, second-degree burns on arms and legs; grocery run to replace burnt patties. Only one of two meals team cooked all summer.
- Bloggers were behind on blogs (Tatiana "double lapping" - blog from 2 weeks before). Aarushi recorded 7 hours of audio memos for her 200-mile blog.
