Murphysboro, IL to St. Louis, MO: 101.1 mi, 2678 ft (first century). Author: Tatiana. A list post; practical items extracted below.

Century day:
- Host Jessica (Murphysboro) made eggs and pancakes; dog Echo. Left by 7am, a team record. First tailwind of the trip. Candy salad (Aarushi) fueled the ride.
- First rest stop: met members of St Luke's Lutheran Church (photos for their monthly newsletter). Second rest stop: Dairy Queen cookie dough blizzard and a local coffee shop. Third rest stop: chamois butter application. Rode the paved, scenic Metro Bike Link Trail into St. Louis; Gateway Arch; crossed the main bridge into St. Louis near cars; Drew's dad met them at the Arch; Pride festivities. Host Alexander (and wife) gave lasagna and cookies; a bed to share.
- MIT admin: "Experiencing the support of our awesome MIT Edgerton Center contact, Marvelin Higginbottom" and "the anti-support of ... MIT auditors who have to review our transactions" (friction on budget/receipts review).

General items with logistics value:
- Postcards from every state; stickers on bikes from every state; grateful circles at dinner; laundry loops (efficient laundry system); new Pactimo bib (shipped by MIT friend Emma Hopkins) praised; sun sleeves prevent burning; fill bottles with ice; snack evolution (mangos replaced by fig bars and gummy worms); Vox "Explained" and games (country game) pass time; Strava posts show progress; hosts who read the blog; sharing a food journal (Caroline).
- Support: Greta and Charles got on a call to discuss Tatiana's Kentucky dog fears; Sarah and Tian (2025 alumni) support the blog; all Spokes alumni helped with the dog situation.
- Fazoli's employees thanked the team for teaching at a correctional facility; LF students at the correctional facility got a leadership badge; a student promoted her FIRST Tech Challenge team.
- Urgent cares across the country used often.
