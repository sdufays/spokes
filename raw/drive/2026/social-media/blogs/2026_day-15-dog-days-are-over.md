Hazard, KY to London, KY, ~80 mi (Day 15). Author: Nunu (2026).

- Preceding week: Aarushi crashed into a tree, Caroline was bitten by a Great Pyrenees ("Max"; 6 rabies shots, fever), poor sleep at an overcrowded campsite. Recovered 2 nights at **Harmony House Bed & Breakfast**. Spokes 2025 alumni Charles and Greta advised Tatiana.
- **Decisions**: bikers Drew, Tatiana, Nate; Nunu, Aarushi, Carmen, Caroline drove. A second rental car is needed for SAG capacity (Caroline can't drive). Online pickup truck reservation was not honored by the rental shop (website doesn't update availability); got a Chevy Trailblazer and exchanged later. Lesson: confirm vehicle class by phone.
- **Dog protocol**: a "follow car" trails bikers at ~10 ft at 10 mph, horn to scare dogs, windows down, walkie-talkies, bikers in V formation, Drew and Nate carry mace (Drew maced a small dog); Great Dane hit a mailbox; Great Pyrenees chased group; house with cage of 20 dogs. Splash pad rest stop.
- Hosts: **Charles** (host and LF coordinator for the **Challenger Center**) in Hazard; **Lucy and Steve** (London KY; pool, bonfire, bolognese dinner, dog Milo). Tatiana sprained ankle on porch at night; urgent care X-ray next morning. Grocery restock at Walmart.
- Tools: sandwiches with hummus, salami, bruschetta; water, speaker.
