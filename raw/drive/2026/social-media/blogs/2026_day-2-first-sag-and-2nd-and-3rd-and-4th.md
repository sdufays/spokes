Washington D.C. to Linden, VA: 88.6 mi, 4947 ft (5833 ft with the wrong turn). Author: likely Nate (route/bike).

Definitions: SAG (noun) = Support And Gear, the minivan with gear/food. SAG (verb) = to get picked up by the car during a ride.

- Sleep: two nights before the team had max 4 hours, then 2 hours before teaching 7 hours at the juvenile center. Decided to wake 7am instead of 5am. Left 8:40am, hit DC rush hour, reached Lincoln Memorial 10am. Technical troubles: red lights, falling walkie talkies, a twisting ramp caused first fall, wrong turns.
- Thanks: Aarushi's uncle and aunt Nikunj and Deepika hosted and shuttled team to DC; Tatiana's mom Olga returned the cargo van.
- Group split into faster/slower groups within 2 miles despite plan to ride 20 miles together. Drafting (Drew and Carmen) held 15-17 mph. Drew's driver license was found in a laundry machine at the host house; Tatiana drove back to fetch it. Team keeps a count of things Drew left behind (4 in 3 days).
- Route: Washington & Old Dominion (W&OD) trail until ~mile 40 (separate from road, green, shade). Rest stops at mile 20, ~35 (lunch sandwiches), ~55. In 2 days the team ate two 1kg bags of dried mangos.
- Warmshowers host Jen (and husband Jim/Tim), Linden VA: wanted the team at the house before 9pm quiet hours (2-year-old baby). Author biked ahead and followed a rerouted bike computer onto a parallel road: 1 mile 500 ft climb, 4-lane 60 mph highway with 18-wheelers, then 1000 ft climb at 5 mph, ended off route. Option chosen: Tatiana SAGed him ~6 miles along the highway back to route, then he rode the last ~20 miles hard (170-180 bpm) and passed Carmen and Drew. Aarushi, Nunu, Caroline were SAGed at 76 miles by Jim (red pickup; one parked truck had a "violators will be shot" sign).
- Jen cooked ground beef tacos; shower line, laundry, team meeting, blog. Jen warned that next day's planned route (60 mi, 8000 ft, notorious among Spokes teams every year) has sharp low-visibility turns; the team swapped to a 30 mi, 2000 ft ride to a different campground. Jen said it was the longest day 1 ride she had seen. Footnote: Warmshowers is used heavily by Spokes for 12 years; Jen has hosted four Spokes teams.
- Lessons: do not split off alone with a bike computer that reroutes; check the planned route on the computer when leaving rest stops; plan an easier day 2 after sleep deprivation; get to Warmshowers hosts before their quiet hours.
