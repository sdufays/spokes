Milford, UT to Baker, NV — 84.9 mi, 4,539 ft. Author: Nunu (2026). Video: "Day 63: Romanticizing the Mundane.mp4" exists separately.

- Driver of the day: Bacon (Carmen). Support car nicknamed **Mr. K** (bright orange camper top). Straight desert roads, headwinds and crosswinds (team drafted sideways to minimise).
- Snacks: crunchy green grapes, Cheez-Its, Nilla Wafers, Wheat Thins, Nature Valley bars, Pop-Tarts, soda, and **peach rings** (6 peach rings = enough carbs per rest stop - macro hack). Macaroni and potato salad tubs gifted by Nate's family. Nate bonked; a week-old melted gummy worm chunk. Lesson: eat even when not hungry.
- All squeezed into the AC car while waiting for group photo at the Nevada border sign.
- Lodging in Baker: **a small church** (slept among the pews). Dinner served by **Stargazer Inn & Bristlecone General Store** (cafe-inn next to Great Basin NP; sign welcoming the team; sandwiches, blueberries, chips, juice, cookies). Staff recommended a short hike to a lake. Showers and laundry at the local gas station.
- Evening: hike (birch forest) + very cold lake swim ~30 min drive from church (Nunu, Nate, Tatiana). Nate cut foot on a rock; Nunu cleaned with gallon of sterile water, alcohol pad, nonstick gauze and medical tape. Lesson: bring real shoes, not flip flops, and keep first aid kit plus gallon of sterile water in the car.
- Stargazing lecture at the Great Basin visitor center at 8 pm (telescopes). Sky pink and hazy (smoke foreshadowing next day). Drew sick; given NyQuil; planned a rest day as passenger.
