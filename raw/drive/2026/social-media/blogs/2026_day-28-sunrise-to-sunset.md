Marshall, MO to Kansas City, MO: 86.2 mi, 4,953 ft. Author: Nunu.
- Forecast: hottest and near windiest day so far ("feels like 107", 30 mph crosswind), and the route surprisingly hilly (5,000 ft; planned the night before by Carmen and Aarushi with RideWithGPS). Slept 11pm, woke 4am; breakfast by hosts Shandra and Dave; started 5:30am, a team record early departure.
- Double drafting line (Nate and Drew leading); the formation broke down with hills; Tatiana cried at the second rest stop because the pace was too fast; Drew rode slowly with her. Cooling towels did not work. Pink Hill Rd was long ups and downs.
- Midday heat: stopped at Wendy's around 2pm to cool off while the hotel check-in details were pending. Carmen drove.
- Lodging: hotel in the middle of Kansas City (4 rooms; luggage cart for 7 duffels and 7 backpacks); BBQ delivered to the lobby at 8pm; boba. Two rest days follow in Kansas City.
- Lessons: check hour-by-hour heat/wind; early start (5am) still insufficient with wind and hills; hold a hot-day plan for SAG and water; do not rely on cooling towels.
