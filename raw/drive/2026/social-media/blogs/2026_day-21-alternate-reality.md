Bowling Green to Madisonville, KY, ~87 miles (Sunday June 21, Father's Day). Author: Nunu.
- Chaos morning: slow starts (Tatiana, the wake-up caller, was at a hotel with her boyfriend), lost bike computer found in backpack; left at 9am; 4 of the team stayed up until 2am. Warmshowers hosts always comment that the team is not morning people.
- Group rode 24.2 miles to first rest stop together; lunch at mile 43.1 at a river park (everyone napped for ~2 hours). Left Appalachia: many PUDs (pointless ups and downs). Road closure at 6:30pm pushed through. Dusk riding.
- Dinner donated: Fazoli's (thanks to Morgan and Eddie). Final miles to Life Christian Center church in Madisonville, KY: Pastor Jon greeted team, slept in the church gym; shower line.
- Camping gear (sleeping bags, pillows, mats) smelled like old socks / garbage after being stored in the roof bag; the cause was not known yet.
- Reflection on the "alternate reality" routine, 54 days left.
