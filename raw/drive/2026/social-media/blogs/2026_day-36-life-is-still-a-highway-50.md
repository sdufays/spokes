Author: Drew (2026).

- US-50 pros: massive shoulder; cons: trucks at 70 mph, feedlot smell, demoralising. Took an alternate gravel/sandy route one stretch. Wind turbine factory and farm. Tailwind noted as rare for east-to-west riders.
- Aarushi's flats: tubed tires (Nate says part of running tubes).
- Pool rest stop outside Garden City (public pool, concession stand).
- Host: **Victory Baptist Church**, Lakin KS: homemade sloppy joes, homemade ice cream, and the church paid for rooms at the local inn.
