Macksville, KS to Dodge City, KS: 62.6 mi, 957 ft. Author: Nunu (written three nights later).
- July 4: ate from a Mexican food truck and played ultimate frisbee in a local park; mosquito bites despite repellent (Nunu 25, Caroline 30+, Nate hundreds; Tatiana none).
- Slept in the church basement of Macksville Christian Church; Pastor Russell hosted a community breakfast (fruits, pastries, biscuits and gravy); team had to be out by 9:30am; Drew played piano.
- Rode flat US-50; halfway point sign/museum between New York and San Francisco; hundreds of windmills (700 in the county) gave tailwinds; closed pool as third rest stop.
- Inventory of food eaten from one grocery trip: 30-pack Capri Sun, 11 oz Nilla wafers, 90 pepperoni slices, 3.5 lb grapefruit, 2.25 lb gummy worms, 24 pack GoGo squeeze, 2 trays of watermelon. Lunch from 4pm to 6pm; bike-to-rest ratio 1:2.
- Hosts in Dodge City: Lindy and Warren (lasagna, iced tea; history of "Wickedest Little City in America"). Showers before dinner; tire pumping and chain oiling. Team popcorn-reads blogs together.
