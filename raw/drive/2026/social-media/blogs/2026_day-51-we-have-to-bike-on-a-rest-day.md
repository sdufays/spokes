Hittle Bottom Campground, UT to Moab, UT — 32.14 mi, 1703 ft. Author: Nate (2026).

- Bikes locked overnight at Hittle Bottom campground; unsupported 30 mi ride 9:00-11:30 am in heat (wind helped). Airbnb in Moab (donated by the owners) 8 mi uphill past town.
- Lunch at Milt's burgers; cafe work session; Carlos (Nunu's boyfriend) visited; Moab t-shirt shop.
- **Arches Thai** (downtown Moab) donated dinner at 5 pm.
- Canyonlands NP at 6 pm under flash flood warnings; almost empty (visitor center closed), Mesa Arch, double rainbow at Green River Overlook; Caroline, Drew, Carmen's hair rose (static) so they ran to the car. Rented Jeep (Carlos) plus minivan.
- Lesson: finishing the unfinished ride on rest day preserved route continuity; hot weather = 7 am start.
