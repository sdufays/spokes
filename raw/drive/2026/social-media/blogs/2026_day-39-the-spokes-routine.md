Lamar, CO to Fowler, CO — 86.3 mi, 1566 ft. Author: Aarushi (2026).

**Morning (~2 h)**: wake within 5 min of Tatiana's alarm; pack sleeping bag/pad/pillow; chamois butter; bib, jersey, sun sleeves; pack personal bags; **write thank-you card for host**; communal tasks (re-pack overhead orange camping bag; toiletries bin with team face wash, body wash, shampoo, conditioner, detergent, extension cords; re-pack cooler with ice/food stored in host fridge; fill six team water jugs; sweep host place; attach bike rack and mount driver's bike); breakfast from "pantry" box (bars, GoGo squeeZ, Cheez-Its) unless Warmshowers host cooks; load route onto bike computer (Garmin); sunscreen; say goodbye to driver.
**Biking (5-7 h)**: clip in; rotate hand positions; draft when windy; music, podcasts, audiobooks (Heroes of Olympus), Spotify Jams.
**Rest stops (~equal time)**: planned the night before by Carmen and Aarushi every ~20 miles (rounded down). Early summer: churches were the typical rest stop (parking lot, bench). Lately more creative.
**Example day**: Valley Grocery (21.1 mi; Arizona Iced Teas); Tony's Fresh Fruit Stand and Dairy Queen in Las Animas (37.2 mi; fruit with chamoy and tajin; met a retiree cross-country biker); La Junta swimming pool (57.4 mi; diving board, slides, lifeguards photo); flat tire plus storm shelter at McDonald's/Wendy's (58-60 mi); Knapps Farm Market in Rocky Ford (69.7 mi; melons; owner stayed past closing).
**Night**: host **Pastor Josephson**, **Trinity Lutheran Church** (taco bar, corn, pudding, drinks bought by him); showers at the local high school locker room (his friend unlocked it); charge devices with one USB-C cable; Carmen and Aarushi make next day's route (2 hours; sleep 1 am); blog (many hours of sleep lost). Tatiana's grandparents Brenda and Tom visiting.
**Lesson**: faster routine = fewer meetings; keep thank-you notes; plan rest stops at pools, fruit stands; bring more than one charger.
