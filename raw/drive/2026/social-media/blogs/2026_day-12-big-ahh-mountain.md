Tazewell, VA to Breaks Interstate Park, VA: 72.59 mi, 5,103 ft. Author: Caroline.
- Slow start; the church house (Main Street Methodist Church) meant stuff spread everywhere. Too much food for the cooler; ate it at breakfast. Goodbye to Teresa. Drew and Tatiana mailed postcards.
- Route issues: a last-minute route change by Aarushi the night before (to cut gravel and elevation) led to a "Private Property: No Trespassing" gate and a huge downed tree on a gravel trail; Drew scouted and the team passed (state route). Extremely bumpy gravel. Lesson: do not change routes at night without checking road status (private/gravel).
- Many dogs on side roads, sketchy neighborhoods; group call during ride lasted only 5 min (no service). Water bottles left at a rest stop. Hand strain.
- Lunch at a church; Big A Mountain (1500 ft in 6 miles; 1st gear); scary blind downhills; dangerous drivers on narrow two-lane highways passing on blind corners. Heavy rain at a convenience store, Spokes 2025 hit rain at the same point.
- Camp: Breaks Interstate Park campsite (bare bones; later blogs say no outlets or drinking water). Aarushi and Nunu set up tents.
- Dinner donated: Mi Rancho Mexican Bar and Grill, Elkhorn City KY; owner Taylor offered a free meal (chicken fajitas, lemonade).
