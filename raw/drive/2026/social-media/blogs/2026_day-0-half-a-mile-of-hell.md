Author: Tatiana. Departure day, MIT to DC (driving, minivan + rental cargo van carrying bikes).

- 7am alarm; mom (Olga) and grandfather (Victor) helped drive to DC. Planned 8am leave slipped: "Spokes Standard Time" (+/- 30-60 min), a term coined by 2025 Spokie Sarah. Send-off party: 2025 Spokie Ishaq, Dunkin from Sarah, Bagelsaurus from Aarushi's family.
- Half a mile in: loud banging and dashboard alarm; roof-storage straps slamming windows, and car alarm triggered by heavy bags on the unbuckled middle seat. Fixed in 5 min at a gas station (buckle seatbelt, secure straps).
- Mom drove first leg; first rest stop right before NYC where she laid out a packed feast (veg, chicken, rice, potatoes, pasta). Gas stop in New Jersey (team refused to let attendants pump for the cargo van). Nate and Nunu drove the huge cargo van with no incidents. Arrived DC ~7:30pm.
- Hosts in DC: Carmen, Drew, Caroline (teammates) and Aarushi's family friends Deepika and Nikunj, who prepared three rooms with mattresses, snacks, drinks and home-cooked Indian dinner.
- 10pm "lock-in" for next day's learning festival: the Department of Corrections / Youth Services Center does not allow scissors, glass or sharp objects, so ALL felt, cardboard and paper had to be precut for 120 students. Bed 2:30am for 5:30am wake up (about 3 hours sleep). Nunu organized LF supplies; Caroline, Aarushi, Tatiana cut cardboard/felt; Carmen and Nate measured liquids.
- Lesson: LF prep was only "50-60% done" at 1am; do prep before departure day; check facility restrictions (sharps) early.
