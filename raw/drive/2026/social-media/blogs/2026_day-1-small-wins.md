Author: (driver/weatherman blogger, likely Drew). 

- Team left on time on day 1 (a first and, jokingly, last).
- LF at the DC Youth Services Center (rehabilitation facility for incarcerated youth). Seven renditions of the festival through the day: students assemble an electric motor from legos. Only a minority succeeded in the first session. The YSC science teacher: "Small wins are what we're after." One whole class of boys built working motors, driven by competition with one another and focused the whole hour.
- Thanks to Judge Zia Fariqui, who created the opportunity and lent his entire staff of law interns to help run the festivals (essential for managing a class).
- Food: Aarushi and the author went to Costco to stock up for two days using her uncle's Costco card; the store now strictly enforces card owner presence; got through with a story. Dried mango is the "most important necessity".
- Lesson: set expectations; a handful of inspired kids is a win. Do not rely on borrowed membership cards.
