Liberty, KY to Mammoth Cave, KY: 88.8 mi, 5427 ft. Author: Tatiana (driving day with sprained ankle).
- Hosts in Liberty: Pastor Roger Allen's fellowship hall (Liberty Assembly of God), farm-fresh eggs. Lesson: dried milk clumps in eggs, do not use.
- Vehicles: minivan plus a "5.8 L V8 GMC Sierra" rental pickup. Team drives every ~6 days anyway.
- Rest stops: playground with sports equipment; Harden Coffee (praised by Spokes 2025 as best rest stop; cute stationery store and $2 paperback book shop); Brush Creek Baptist Church with a river and waterfall; Farmwald's Dutch Bakery and Deli (Amish; donated first dinner: pulled pork sandwiches, burgers, cheesecake); free cantaloupes from Mike and Nancy Tucker at Milby's Produce.
- Donations: Tatiana's role is calling businesses to ask for donations; "it takes at most three calls per city to get our team a meal". One call to La Cabana Mexican Bar and Grill near Mammoth Cave got a donated dinner; Louis and Nolasco hosted (birria tacos).
- Wind made a downhill feel uphill; 10 miles to go with 45 min to sunset; Drew followed riders as safety. Last 2 mile climb to Mammoth Cave campground.
- Camping: Mammoth Cave campground with coin showers (6 quarters for 4 minutes per person; bring quarters). Tents set up by drivers.
- Lesson: sunblock and mayo bottles look alike. Spokes SAGs out the whole team only when required.
