Idaho Springs, CO to Breckenridge, CO — 56.0 mi, 5,927 ft. Author: Tatiana (2026).

- Lodging the night before: donated Airbnb in Idaho Springs (steep gravel driveway; walked bikes down). Breckenridge host: **Alison** (husband Phil on a bike tour): taco/burrito bar dinner, comfortable bed.
- Driver: Carmen; moved a rest stop closer (coffee shop with art gallery/book nook, dam detour). Carmen left car at the top of Loveland Pass and rode down and up to join. Loveland Pass climb: 4 mi / 1,500 ft final push at altitude; blankets at top (wind). Met bikers **HDWND (Headwind)**: three riders crossing the US in 20 days (finished in 22), ~200 mi/day.
- Drew had altitude sickness at the pass. 10-mile descent at 35 mph.
- Keystone: Ashley Fisher (friend) and her family drove from Denver; Ashley rode with the team on Carmen's bike for 2 miles; lightning forced a gas-station shelter; Tatiana and Aarushi were SAGed in the Fishers' SUV at 8 pm (bike in the car, front wheel off); Caroline, Nunu, Nate rode in the dark/storm. Drew rode on alone (lightning).
- **Food log (a day of eating)**: Breakfast - leftover vodka pasta with chicken sausage, Gogo squeeze, brownie, salad, dried mango; Drew - donut sticks and holes, Gatorade, sesame chicken takeout. Snacks - bars, Arnold Palmer; lunch at the pass - turkey/pepperoni/guac sandwich, pickles, sweet tea; Keystone crepes and Arizona sweet tea; dinner burrito. Estimated 3,400 kcal (Tatiana) and 4,300 kcal (Drew) vs 4,600 burned per Garmin: team likely still under-eating. (Claude declined to estimate and expressed concern; ChatGPT gave numbers.)
- Lesson: expect ~4,000+ kcal burn/day; carbs and snack sharing; expect afternoon thunderstorms in the Rockies - start early.
