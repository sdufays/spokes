Rest day in Afton, VA. Author: Caroline.
- Slept 8+ hours; host Pam's breakfast casserole and OJ.
- Shannon Farm: intentional community on 500+ acres of Virginia woods; community gardens, shared skills; many members are cyclists (one just back from a Scotland tour; another rode Boston to SF). Pam's home has no WiFi: Tatiana and Aarushi went with Pam to the community center to blog and hold meetings; later a group went back for WiFi to make Airbnb arrangements.
- Ticks and mosquitoes ruined outdoor picnic; played card games (Stab Your Friends from Spokes 2025, Durak, ERS).
- Lake on Shannon: used the 7-person floatie purchased in Boston (the pump failed; Nunu and Aarushi blew it up by mouth). Met JMU students (two grew up at Shannon) who lent a paddle.
- Pam hosted a potluck for the team: mac and cheese, peach cobbler, eggplant dish, watermelon with mint; met Shannon members. Volleyball afterwards; basketball/volleyball rivalry caused small tension.
- Prep for the next day's LF (second of the trip). A spider in the boys' cabin bed meant some bed-sharing.
