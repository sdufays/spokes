Harrisburg, IL to Murphysboro, IL: 52.5 mi, 1406 ft. Author: Nate.
- Morning routine drifted from 6am wake/8am leave to 7am wake/9am departure. Hosts Sherie and Brad (Harrisburg; they have done cross-country rides): blueberry french toast casserole.
- Side quests: Finding Forever Animal Rescue and Thrift Store (Marion IL; kittens), BBQ shop (rack of ribs $19.99; pork steaks $7.99/lb), Little Egypt Art Association (owner Meliah, bathroom and photo), a cafe (lavender lemonade), an antique store (1 hr); McDonald's bathroom stop. Team cut the planned lunch stop. A friendly Great Pyrenees named Bart. Avg 18 mph draft line for last 20 miles.
- Warmshowers host Jessica in Murphysboro (dog Echo; former mayor). Watermelon.
- Team meeting: blogs were 6 days behind; need discipline on morning routine; plan for the century ride (100 mi). "Candy salad" bowl.
- Lesson: very slow days are affordable only on short rides; blogs should not stack up.
