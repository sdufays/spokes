Capitol Reef National Park, UT to Escalante, UT — 68 mi, 6,031 ft. Author: Carmen (2026). (Includes her bullet notes; condensed here.)

- Last morning camping (campground in Capitol Reef; tents). Tatiana and boyfriend Noah brought breakfast burritos from their hotel. Strangers repeatedly asked the team for bike tools/tire help (3-4 times in 48 h): the 7 bikes locked at the campsite act as "bat signal".
- Torrey: first rest stop at a farm-store/pie shop (four pies, two ice creams; strawberry rhubarb recommended; horchata latte). Memories of Subway, a Mexican restaurant, Tatiana's hotel, farmers market, flyer for BIFF.
- Weather: 15 mph east winds on a gravel climb, then stinging rain/hail. Nunu pitched a tent at a rest stop as shelter from wind and rain (Mr. K support car); team waited ~1.5 hours then left (1000 ft in 10 miles of gravel next). Cold at 9,500 ft: blankets at lunch; rain jackets debated (Carmen/Caroline went without). Climbed Dixie National Forest to 10,049 ft; steep gravel downhill (Drew's brakes failed from overuse; chain came off). Cows and "experimental farms" on the road.
- Rest stop at a lake with docks and kayaks (Drew paddled out); bald eagle, ducks. Aarushi and Tatiana arrived over an hour later (slow, no service): safety worry. Past accidents cited: 2025 team slid out on gravel and was seriously hurt; a UT Austin team rider was blown off a cliff and airlifted. A black pickup followed Carmen for miles (turned out harmless).
- Lodging: **Pastor Richards** and hosts in Escalante (dogs Rocky and Mocha), sloppy joes, own beds.
- **Route decision**: Pastor Richards suggested a gravel route north of Highway 12. Considered: no street view, RideWithGPS elevation may be inaccurate, gravel slower, fewer rest stops, same total elevation vs semi traffic on Hwy 12. After an hour's debate the team took Highway 12 (last year's route) because Nate's parents could meet at rest stops.
- Lesson: one hour of lost sleep over route debates; default to last year's vetted route when no strong reason.
