Author: Drew. 41-42 mile, ~2000 ft day.
- LF Saturday: younger kids got a drawing activity as electromagnetism content was too advanced for them (lesson: have a low-grade-level fallback).
- Host Pam made pancakes; borrowed a truck to shuttle bikes from Shannon Farm to the route start. 
- Aarushi had ear infection symptoms; Aarushi and Tatiana biked to a local urgent care in Waynesboro: over an hour of waits, antibiotics prescribed, the CVS prescription was missing a signature (lots of delays), Tatiana locked her sunglasses in a CVS bathroom. Lesson: rural urgent care takes hours; budget time; check pharmacy transfer.
- Others rode 42 miles at 15 mph average. Fixed Tatiana's derailleur at the midway stop.
- Host Dirk (and wife) shuttled team to the river: paddleboards and inflatable one-person kayaks; offroading in an old jeep, sunset hill; best dinner so far, s'mores. Dirk and Krissie later gave a carton of fresh eggs.
- Lesson/context: the previous blog had a joke at Nate's expense that Nate's parents found "not cool"; note to be careful what the blog says about teammates.
- Truck stop with Popeyes/Papa John's/Subway used for lunch.
