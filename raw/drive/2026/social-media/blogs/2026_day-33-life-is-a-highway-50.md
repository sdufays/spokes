Emporia, KS to Newton, KS: 75.4 mi, 1737 ft. Author: Caroline.
- Snooze-alarm morning; left 9:40am. US Highway 50 (will rejoin it in Nevada, "the loneliest road in America"): massive shoulder, drivers move over, wind from semis; wind is the biggest factor; ride at your own pace.
- First rest stop: HeBrews CoffeeHouse in Cottonwood Falls (cafe and place of worship): met Lola (competitive rodeo, won $4,000 night before) and barista Vonda who donated a box of pastries.
- Minivan oil pan part came loose; a local woman directed them to Dieker Oil, which fixed it for free.
- Lunch at an old mill/abandoned town. Aarushi picked up a giant nail through tire and rim: swapped Nate's tire, later used gorilla tape and a tube (tires are tubeless). Last rest stop Dollar General.
- Hosts (Warmshowers): Janet and Orvin (dog Enzo, cat Phoebe): corn on the cob, mulberry-rhubarb pie made with local produce. Tatiana's grandparents Brenda and Tom visiting in Colorado.
