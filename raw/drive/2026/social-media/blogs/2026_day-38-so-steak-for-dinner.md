Learning Festival in Lamar, CO — Author: Tatiana (2026), LF coordinator for the summer. This was the team's 7th LF.

**How LFs are planned**: Months ahead, email directors of libraries, jails and community centers with rough dates; once hosts confirm, coordinate with the route team to lock arrival dates. It is hard to keep the same locations year to year (weekends, holidays, route, reading periods): 2026 had two new locations. Per host: infrastructure, food for students, marketing support, registration forms, photo release (hundreds of emails). Lamar library was on reading period (previous LF years ago), so the Community Building hosted.
**Lamar LF**: Director **Ethan Settles** (Lamar Community Building; set up tables and chairs; pizza lunch for kids; gym for basketball). Registrations only appeared two days before; 30 students (ages 4-15). Two rooms, two workshops each: Aarushi and Carmen chem/bio (Iodine Clock, Monster Genome) with Tatiana's electronics (copper tape magic circuit with tilt switch, buzzer for older kids); Nate and Drew electromagnetism with Nunu and Caroline design (cardboard). Gear in two grey bins organized by Nunu. Opening game: Rock, Paper, Scissors, Rockstar. Puzzles brought back from IIT Gandhinagar. Parents explained 4-day school weeks in SE Colorado.
**Teaching notes**: for young kids use challenges (e.g. light an LED) rather than lecturing; kids "trick" mentors by flipping the circuit.
**Local help**: local steakhouse gave $20 credit per person for dinner (Tatiana's call; Nate's joke about donated steak); fire department refused to help remove a bike cable from Aarushi's wheel due to liability; **JJ at the local Tractor Supply** lent wire cutters. Zumba class at the Community Building. Church hosted (sleeping pad circle). Car without rack/camper top.
