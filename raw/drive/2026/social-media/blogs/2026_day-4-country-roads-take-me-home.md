Luray, VA to Waynesboro, VA: 62.5 mi, 3,900 ft. Author: Aarushi (Aarus).
- Woke to everything wet from dew despite no rain forecast; belongings (bike shoes, fresh laundry, tents) left outside were soaked. Lesson: stow shoes, laundry and tents/gear at night even if no rain forecast. Left campsite 9am (3 hours to get going).
- Rode along Shenandoah River/country roads; long picnic lunch with group nap (7-person floatie brought but unused). Calling friends/family while biking is a "hack" of long-distance riding.
- Ride ended in Waynesboro; next host Pam picked the team up in her truck over the mountain; riding resumes from Waynesboro to keep a contiguous route.
- Host Pam lives at Shannon Farm (hippie intentional community founded 1973, member since 1979); fed team BBQ pulled pork sandwiches, potato salad, baked beans, corn on the cob. Pam: nurse, Vietnam-war activist, chairman of a primate sanctuary in North Carolina, met Jane Goodall.
- Reflection: first real team; feelings of inadequacy (slower biker, slowest to get ready); goals: become a "Caller" and complain less. Mom's advice: eat lots of protein, be more shameless asking for donations.
