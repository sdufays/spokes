Hermann, MO toward Columbia, MO (Katy Trail). Author: Caroline (elbow issue; took day off).
- Airbnb with an 11-person capacity (in Hermann, MO area). Car stuck in wet dirt/gravel driveway; pushed free.
- Driver-day tradition: local coffee shop first (Stomp'n Grounds Espresso Bar in Hermann).
- Nunu skipped biking (chafing; urgent care in Jefferson City). Three bikes on the rack hitch tight.
- Drew's tire slashed on the Katy Trail in rain; sealant failed. Solution: take the wheel off an unused spare bike on the rack, install it on Drew's bike, and take the damaged wheel to a bike shop in the next city that had the exact tire; picked up by Drew the next day (his driver day). Lesson: carry a spare wheel/tire; check that the next town's bike shop stocks the size.
- Walmart restock ("best haul"); snack box was depleted. Mud-covered riders needed deep cleaning.
- Warmshowers host Vince (Columbia MO): lasagna, local ice cream (recommended by another cross-country cyclist). Nate went to a local rock concert in Columbia with a friend from the MIT pistol team. World Cup watching (Cape Verde).
- Caroline keeps a traveler's notebook with a food journal.
