London, KY to Liberty, KY: 64.8 mi, 4060 ft. Author: Nate.
- Prior night (hosts Lucy and Steve in London KY): five slept in hammocks in the backyard pavilion; brought hammocks were missing parts and had to be "frankensteined"; cold (50F), dew-wet blanket; bad sleep. Hosts provided donuts, eclairs, pigs in blankets, fruit, juice, eggs for breakfast. The previous day had an 80-mile ride with dog chases.
- Injuries: Tatiana sprained her ankle (found after going to three hospitals because the first had no x-ray machine); Caroline's bite bruising; Caroline got her second rabies dose at a hospital.
- Dog strategy used by riders who biked: yelling plus mace; a Great Dane chasing at full speed made riders swerve into oncoming lane. Everyone chose individually; Drew gave his driver day to Nunu to bike; Carmen rode.
- Vehicle: team rented a small SUV for extra SAG space, exchanged for a large GMC Sierra pickup (50 min drive). The team has two MIT corporate cards for renting cars (Nate and Caroline hold them; Caroline has no driver's license).
- Ride: 20 miles to first rest stop uneventful, long lunch/nap at second rest stop (3.5 h), Rummikub, HORSE. Last leg: 6-8 dogs chased (Rottweiler, two retriever/pyrenees mixes, a Great Pyrenees); neighbor warned about the dog.
- Host in Liberty: Brother Rodger at Liberty Assembly of God gave fried chicken and lots of groceries. Nunu used a local laundromat. Caroline's dad Forest and two siblings visited (dad did 1000 miles in 10 days on a single-speed).
- Plan: next day 90-mile ride; Nate gave up his driver day to Drew so the others could bike; "avoid MIT Rims 2026 (everyone driving)".
