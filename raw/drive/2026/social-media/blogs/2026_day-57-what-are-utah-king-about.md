Escalante, UT to Bryce Canyon National Park, UT — 47.9 mi, 3961 ft. Author: Drew (2026).

- Host in Escalante: **Pastor Richards** (dogs Rocky and Mocha); breakfast casserole with eggs and sausage. Protein-heavy breakfast meant riders forgot carbs: Nate exhausted at the top of an 18-mile climb; Drew shuttled food by bike to climbers (Nature Valley wafer bars). Lesson: high-protein breakfasts need carb follow-up.
- Nunu sick; driver bought medicine on roadside.
- Last rest stop: coffee shop next to a convenience store selling cheeseburgers (gas station bacon cheeseburger ~$7).
- Met Nate's mother Claudia Mustafa and family at the gas station. Airbnb at Bryce generously provided by Nate's family; Nate's dad cooked dinner (meat in tortillas).
