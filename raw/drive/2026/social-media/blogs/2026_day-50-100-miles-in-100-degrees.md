Author: Drew (2026). Day: Tatiana was driver.

- No ice at any of the 5 gas stations within 30 miles; riders ran out of water in 100+ F; plastic "insulated" bottles gave hot water. Bike path/gravel detour next to I-70 near the Utah sign; hundreds of cars (detour around an accident) coated riders in dust for half an hour. Rental Toyota Sienna has only 3 inches of ground clearance (Tatiana drove it up a bike trail).
- Some riders took the interstate shoulder to avoid extra mileage. Rest stop under a bridge for ~45 min recovering from mild heat exhaustion. Swam in the Colorado River at a second-to-last stop to wait out peak heat (planned to bike in the dark on a low-traffic road).
- Storm and wind forced the team back; riders locked bikes at a campground (Hittle Bottom Campground) and finished the next morning on the rest day (see Day 51). Wendy's food run in Moab.
- Safety: "the most likely thing to kill us is drivers"; pickup drivers intentionally trying to run cyclists off the road.
- Lessons: plan ice and water supply early in desert days; have shade/storm rules; chain of command for shuttle plans.
