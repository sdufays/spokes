Kansas City LF day. Author: Nate.
- Hotel provided by Steam Village (program hosting the LF). Breakfast burritos from a nearby cafe. LF had only 15 students (usual 60-80); fewer students means more one-on-one, conversational workshops.
- Format: Tatiana's introduction circle and RPS tournament (Caroline unbeaten). Nate and Drew teach brushless motor from electromagnet, circuit board, magnets and legos (magnets, Earth's field, Lenz's law, solenoids). Tatiana's circuits workshop. A student showed a homemade Iron Man helmet (exposed lithium battery). Mini-LF for a talented student who missed the LF (chemistry from Aarushi and Carmen plus the motor workshop); the student's parents treated the team to sushi.
- Laundromat: three machines ($8.50 per machine) for clothes plus sleeping bags and tents; the owner warned tents would melt in a dryer (they dried at "No Heat" then accidentally on medium and the tents survived). Lesson: wash stinky camping gear at a laundromat on a rest/LF day; tents survive medium heat.
- Dinner at Joe's BBQ in the Kansas City Power and Light District during the Mexico vs Ecuador World Cup watch party (KC the only host city on route). Packed minivan by 10:10pm for a 4am wakeup. Nate cleaned and lubed his chain and fixed a front brake.
