Green River State Park, UT to Capitol Reef National Park, UT — 94.3 mi, 4398 ft. Author: Caroline (driver day, 2026).

- Camped at Green River State Park (2-person tents). Broke camp 5 am, riders left 6:30 am for heat. Driver tasks: ice from a gas station, snacks, drinks; legs lengthened to ~20 mi early in the cool morning.
- **Tamarisk** restaurant in Green River (dinner the night before; coffee shop/hotel attached) opens at 7 am; chai latte, big burritos and cinnamon rolls.
- Route used **I-70 for ~13 miles** with decent shoulder (speed limit 80). Remote gravel-pullout rest stops, black sand dunes, only one town with a cafe (WiFi). Drivers waited long because riders were staggered by heat. Pepsis handed out. Colorful rock formations "Mars".
- Wind and a threatening storm on the final climb; low morale for Tatiana and Aarushi. Tatiana turned 95 mi into a century by looping the campground (with Drew and Noah-related plan).
- Torrey: Subway dinner, shower line at Tatiana and Noah's hotel (campsite had no showers/laundry), 11 pm back to campsite.
- Lessons: with heat, make early legs longer, stagger stops, plan ice; carry cold drinks.
