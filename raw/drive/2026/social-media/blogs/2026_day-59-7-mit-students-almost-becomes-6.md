Bryce Canyon City, UT to Zion NP, UT — 100.3 mi, 4,402 ft. Author: Tatiana (2026).

**Context**: Nate's family (Mustafa family) hosted in Bryce earlier; pepper spray incident the night before (Tatiana's bag washed twice). Wake time moved to 7 am; Drew missed the memo and left early. Cars 2 movie night.
**Ride**: Fantastic downhill; rest stops: shop with free coffee, gas station, and "Rock Stop" (rocks, trinkets, donuts, coffee, goats, birria tacos). Thunder and lightning; team sheltered under a bush. Annual National Park pass requires holder in the car: Nate and Nunu had to pay another $25 entry. Tatiana nauseous/ill; Pepto-Bismol.
**What happened to Drew**: Aarushi and Carmen rerouted onto a trail to avoid shuttling through the closed-to-bikes Zion tunnel (the 2025 team used the main road and shuttled by car; the 2025 blog for that day was missing). Drew's trail turned into an unrideable hiking trail, then a "Trail Closed, No Trespassing, $5000 fine" section; the switchbacks collapsed and his bike plunged off the cliff in a rockslide. He descended by "butt scootch", reached a shuttle stop, took a shuttle to Zion Lodge. Nate and Nunu got the gate code from a security guard for the restricted road and found him at the lodge eating a cheeseburger. Local outdoor guides at a lodge on the route said the trail was dangerous and closed.
**Rescue/hosting**: Caroline called her father Forest, who had a bike rack on a minivan and shuttled four riders through the tunnel. Caroline's family (brother Brady, grandfather) had an Airbnb in Zion; grandfather cooked Chinese dinner (egg and tomato stir fry, hua juan, beef and potato stew, bone soup, cucumber salad, string beans).
**Aftermath**: plan to buy a replacement bicycle and gear (Carson City, SLC, or Las Vegas considered; ended up Las Vegas); a fundraising effort to cover unexpected costs.
**Lessons**: vet every off-road route (check closures, ask local guides, have service), always carry shuttle plan for tunnels (2025 team shuttled), keep teammates within comms, share gear/Garmin counts (the team lost a Garmin).
