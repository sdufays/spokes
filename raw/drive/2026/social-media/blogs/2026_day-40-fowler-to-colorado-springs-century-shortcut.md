Fowler, CO to Colorado Springs, CO — 87.4 mi, 3,174 ft. Author: Caroline (2026).

- Church in Fowler provided breakfast (Arizona iced teas become favourite). Century wake-up routine from first century in Missouri: up at 6, leave by 7; route shortened from 100 to 87 mi by Aarushi (route queen) late at night.
- Rest stop 24 mi in at a closed produce stand (picnic blanket by car); Grandpa Tom and Grandma Brenda (Tatiana's? family side - hosts/visitors) brought giant cinnamon rolls and coffee.
- **Pueblo Weisbrod Aircraft Museum** detour (B-29, MiG-17, A-10, F-86; space suit history lesson); small museum run by a small team.
- Gravel to I-25 shoulder (80 mph traffic felt unsafe); closed rest area; used a parallel trail. Colorado Sno-Balls snow cone shop ($2). In-N-Out in Colorado Springs (Drew's first). Rain, first rainbow, headwind on creek path.
- Aarushi's family (flew into Denver) brought fruit; Airbnb shared with them for two days (Indian dinner). Highs and lows discussion.
- Lessons: facilities are sparse east of Colorado Springs; plan rest stops on blanket by car; route shortcuts can save energy.
