Linden, VA to Luray, VA - 31.8 mi, 2303 ft. Author: Tatiana.
- Breakfast oatmeal by host Jen; her husband drove team down the gravel mountain.
- At a gas-pump rest stop with no shade, a man (Jimmy Baker) asked about the trip and donated $1,000 and offered to buy food later on the route.
- Campground (Luray area) with pool, clean bathrooms, jumping pad (blown-up tarp), volleyball, pool table.
- Pizza: author phoned a nearby town the day before and a pizza place donated several pizzas for dinner. Carmen and Aarushi bought Lofthouse cookies. Group shopping run for groceries, pizza, medicine.
- Roles ("GOAT"): Tatiana morning person (wakes team at 6am); Nunu organization; Nate car and gear (bike rack, top storage); Drew groceries; Caroline laundry; Aarushi housing (found new campground and free Airbnbs); Carmen route and bicycles.
- Tents: 4-person (Aarushi, Nunu, Tatiana); 2-person (Carmen, Caroline); 2-person (Nate, Drew). Noah gave Tatiana 75 notes to open daily.
- Lesson: calling ahead to local restaurants for donations works; strangers are generous.
