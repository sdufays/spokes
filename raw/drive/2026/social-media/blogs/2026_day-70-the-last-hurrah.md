Fallon, NV to South Lake Tahoe, CA — 101.4 mi, 5,750 ft. Author: Nunu (video script), 2026. Note: author says the Tahoe Airbnb was donated by "my awesome parents" and that she persuaded the team to turn one rest day into two.

- Second overnight ride in days (earlier ~200 mi overnight ride, very hard). Woke 1 AM at Fallon Youth Club (FYC), left 2:15 AM. Breakfast burritos warmed in FYC's industrial kitchen; water bottles filled from the industrial ice machine. No cars at night; sunrise seen on the road. Night-mode sunglass lenses.
- Mr. K (support car) upgraded with Caroline's elephant and Drew's "Max" on the dashboard (from design workshop).
- Liquid diet: Aarushi bought 4-5 gallons of Arizona iced tea plus sodas; easier carbs than Cheez-Its and gummy worms.
- 63 mi in at about 8 AM: coffee shop in Carson City (still closed shops so no distractions), brief visit to the Nevada State Capitol, Wendy's (Drew waiting) with Homestyle seasoned potatoes.
- Drew's tire punctured before the climb (maybe the 100th puncture of trip), plug repair at a park; sandwiches given by FYC eaten there. 2,000 ft climb Carson City to Tahoe; Aarushi drove back and forth giving makeshift mid-climb rest stop. No bike computer for author (Aarushi's broken).
- Crossed California border on US-50 (author's home state; grew up visiting Tahoe). Jamba Juice meeting spot. Beach needed an entrance fee. Group photos at border sign (fancier sign on closed trail).
- Last 8 mi to Airbnb, Chipotle pickup at 10 PM.
- Lessons: night riding avoids heat/wind but ruins sleep; plan an easy next day; carry plug kit for tires.
