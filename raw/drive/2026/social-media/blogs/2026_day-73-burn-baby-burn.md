South Lake Tahoe, CA to Folsom, CA — 100.5 mi, 6580 ft. Author: Tatiana Vassiliev (Day 73, 2026).

**Seasons of Spokes** (75 days becomes "life"). Airbnb and Family Season: in the last month team families provided lodging as they visited: Aarushi's family (Airbnb, Colorado Springs), Tatiana's family (hotel, Parachute CO), Nate's family (Airbnb, Bryce Canyon), Caroline's family (Airbnb, Zion), Carmen's family (Airbnb, Cedar City), Nunu's family (Airbnb, South Lake Tahoe). Earlier seasons: Sleeping on the Side of a Ditch, Biking at 2 AM to Escape the Heat, Begging Churches to Sleep on Their Floor, Camper Top Fell Off (lost camping equipment, stuff smelled, a tent broke), Liquid Diet Season (only sugary drinks for carbs), Barren Land with Dust Devils, Dog Apocalypse, Fires on our Path, National Parks Everywhere, California Gorgeous Forests.

**The ride**: immediate climb out of Tahoe, then an ~8,000 ft descent into Folsom. Two one-way construction traffic stops slowed Tatiana. Drivers Carmen and Caroline brought a snack haul (Fudge Stripes favourite) and went back to Tahoe for coffee. The descent passed through large burned forest (fire aftermath, small fire crews working). Then very aggressive California drivers (yelling, honking, running cyclists off road). Tatiana bonked from under-eating; final rest stop at a Jamba Juice; rode on alone early. Forecast: "California Ultra Mega Awful Wind Season."

**Folsom**: met by Aarushi's family friends (hosts) and former Spokes member **Rebecca Lizarde**, who joined for dinner. Earlier in Colorado the team met alum Kate. Alumni meetups make the trip feel multi-generational.

Thanks: hosts, kids at Learning Festivals, alumni, family. Lessons: eat enough on 100-mi days; accept family-provided lodging late in trip; California drivers were the worst of the trip.
