Learning Festival in Ely, NV — 0 mi (or 36.44 mi, 2254 ft). Author: Nate (2026; turned 23 at midnight).

- Slept on the floor of the **Ely high school library** (host), ~5 mi from a 0% contained wildfire (doubled from 4k to 8k acres, wind shifting toward the route road). Previous day's ride stopped partly because of smoke/air quality and fear of road closure separating the team from Drew in Ely. Locals showed no concern ("There's nothing we can do about it, so why worry" - HS football coach). Bikes were left at a random campsite and shuttled.
- LF at the **Ely library** actually ran 11:00 am to 5:00 pm (team assumed 9-3); library staff had said "see you tomorrow morning... or I guess afternoon." Lesson: re-confirm LF times in writing with each host. Also the team didn't know which building was the library (walked into the courthouse).
- ~15 students; ran workshops in 3 groups of 5 (electric motors etc.). Library staff helped supervise; library cooked dinner for team. Lunch burgers for 7 at a local burger place. Tatiana and Drew led Pictionary. Kids went to bouncy castles at a festival thrown by the city's police department across the street; massages offered "for the firefighters" to the team.
- Nate then rode the missed 36 mi from 7:30 pm with Nunu as support driver ("Rider in White"), 2000 ft/8 mi climb in the dark, peach rings (20 g carbs per 3 rings; 90 g per bag) handed without stopping; finished 9:30 pm; stars and shooting stars.
- Food: McDonald's drinks runs; taco bowls.
- Next day 80 mi hot ride; team watched movie late anyway.
