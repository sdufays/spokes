- Slept on real beds, woke 8:20am; Tatiana's wake-up duty.
- LF at the Challenger Learning Center, Hazard KY. Assistant director Charles: drove 4 hours to transport team after the dog bite, coordinated a very structured LF with talented kids, provided meals (Applebees dinner) and sleeping place. Passionate about STEM for rural Kentucky kids.
- Teaching technique (Nate and Drew, motors): start with "What is a magnet?" to calibrate depth by age; with strong kids went into solenoid magnetic fields, square wave generator frequency and rotor spin. Student Connor discussed aerospace.
- Dairy Queen blizzards after LF.
- Team decision ("Spokes politics", like Oregon Trail): each member chooses whether to bike through the dog-heavy Kentucky stretch; plan to SAG any biker out; rent a truck able to carry five bikers and bikes (the minivan carries 2, 3 max).
