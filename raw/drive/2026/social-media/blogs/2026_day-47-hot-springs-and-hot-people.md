Learning Festival in Glenwood Springs, CO — 0 mi. Author: Caroline (2026).

- Hosts: Glenwood Springs **Public Library** (one block from a coffee shop, "Wild Coffee"; big room, library provided lunch from a local restaurant: fish and chips). Team stayed at **Greg's house** in Glenwood (host). Aarushi and Tatiana stayed in a hotel with Tatiana's visiting family (grandpa Victor).
- LF: Tatiana opened with Simon Says; four groups sorted by age; Nunu and Caroline's cardboard design workshop (houses, animals, cacti, elephant); a kid pivoted dog into bird (lesson on adaptation). Workshop template: papers, rulers, markers, colored pencils, tape.
- **Stanford Spokes** (Paisley, Ian, Freya, Jack, Logan) drove 2.5 h to meet for dinner at the same restaurant as lunch; their route is parallel and south of MIT's. Their fancy cargo van; they planned a warm showers host later. Toilet tag and fence volleyball in a park. Caroline notes the first time the groups met in a couple of years.
- Hot springs: skipped paid private springs (~$30 for 1 hour); went to a free natural public hot spring 10 min away at 9:30 pm until 12:40 am. McDonald's closed at 1 am; rushed.
- Lesson: look for free hot springs; schedule LF days with later wake-ups (8 am is a treat).
