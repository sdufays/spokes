Written by a male rider (likely Nate/Drew) after the trip (2026).

- Woke very sick; first non-mandatory SAG. A wildfire pushed smoke into the valleys; air quality rose to "very unhealthy," so the team called off the day's route and shuttled bikers (Nunu drove) to Ely. Rider keeps a record of "no non-entire-team sags".
- Ely host: a **high school** hosted them; a Carl's Jr in the gas station across the street where dozens of firefighters ate lunch.
- Burger ranking: 5. In-N-Out (CO Springs; reliable 6/10), 4. Dairy Queens in Virginia, 3. Freddy's Colorado Springs (shoestring fries, jalapeno fry sauce), 2. TJ's Mainstreet Grill, Overbrook, Kansas (double bacon cheeseburger $7, two 1/3 lb patties), 1. Milt's in Moab, Utah (white shack; storm delay beforehand; lunch from the Moab Airbnb).
- Lesson: have a smoke/air quality decision rule (monitor AQI; call off ride when very unhealthy).
