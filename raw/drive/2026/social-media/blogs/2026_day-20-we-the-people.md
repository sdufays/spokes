Learning Festival in Bowling Green, KY. Author: Carmen.
- LF at the WKU Innovation Campus (entrepreneurship hub). Icebreaker: tournament rock-paper-scissors. Nearly 100 kids in groups of ~25, grades 3 to high school; helped by interns; photo with WKU hosts and Sam. Older kids asked better genetics questions (dominant vs recessive); inspired to rework the genetics workshop for younger kids. Team philosophy: kids having fun over deep conceptual grasp.
- Kentucky kids "advanced for grade level"; some families drove hours or stayed in hotels.
- Howard's bike shop re-measured and realigned Aarushi's bike (fit thrown off after crash) free of charge.
- Caroline's rabies series: another shot at urgent care ("third (technically eighth)"). Evening in downtown Bowling Green: coffee shop patio, cockatoo on a leash, fountain; Zaxby's vs Cane's taste test; blueberry brownies; stayed up until 2:06am before an 87 mile day.
- Lesson: talk to people; the town's quirky people make for memorable content.
