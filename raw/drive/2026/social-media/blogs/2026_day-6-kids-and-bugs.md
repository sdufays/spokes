Learning Festival in Fluvanna, VA: 0 mi. Author: Carmen ("Bacon Carmen").
- Morning: laid out mildewy tents and sleeping pads to dry on the forest floor at Pam's: bugs, spiders and ants moved in (lesson: dry gear on tarps/in sun, not on forest floor). Host Pam's breakfast: biscuits and grits.
- LF at Fluvanna County Public Library, first time there; arranged via Tatiana. Taught 70 kids from around the county. The library lacks state funding for educational events; nonprofit "Friends of Fluvanna" raises money for them. Pizza lunch for kids.
- Workshops: Carmen and Aarushi chemistry (middle/high schoolers) and genetics "Monster Genome" felt monsters (K-4); Tatiana circuits; Caroline and Nunu paper 3D spheres; Drew and Nate LEGO electromagnetic motors. Kids always ask to blow up chemicals and eat them.
- Post-LF: ice cream (Spokes-funded) and a team meeting; Drew and Carmen shopped at Wegmans (first time) for two days of food plus gummy worms/bears. Tent put-away with bug spray; Carmen fixed bikes in Pam's greenhouse. Aarushi and Caroline briefly stranded ~1 mile away at the community center and picked up.
- Wake-up 6am to finish rides before heat.
