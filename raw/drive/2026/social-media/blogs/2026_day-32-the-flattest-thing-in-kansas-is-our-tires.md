Overbrook, KS to Emporia, KS: 62.6 mi, 1608 ft. Author: Aarushi. Answers to the 15 lies in Day 25 included at the top (the blog was unreliable; real behaviors include communal ice bag/hands in food hygiene concerns).
- Breakfast with host Scott at the retirement community he owns in Overbrook; waffles with residents (Linda).
- Flats: Caroline's repaired tire (third flat in 24 hours), Carmen's, Aarushi's own sealant bubbling, another biker's, Carmen's second: seven flats counted. Drew's spare wheel swap took minutes vs half an hour for the team the day before (lesson: practice wheel/axle removal). Tires need to keep spinning for sealant to seal. Drew's bike ended up with no wheels.
- Met Shaun, founder of Helmet Flair (helmet horns and ears), who gave free decorations on the Flint Hills Trail.
- Osage City: downtown closed; grocery store fruit splurge (oranges, grapes, watermelon, grapefruit cups, Naked smoothies) after days of Cheez-Its, fig bars, gummy worms. Headwind. Started afternoon with 60% left at 4pm.
- Housing in Emporia: Vickie at the Didde Catholic Campus Center helped find housing; Pastor Brett hosted at 12th Avenue Baptist Church of Emporia; Diane and Elaine from Didde cooked a meal.
