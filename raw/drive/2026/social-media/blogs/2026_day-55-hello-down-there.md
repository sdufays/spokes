Rest Day, Capitol Reef NP / Torrey, UT. Author: Nunu (2026).

- Camped in Capitol Reef (no phone data; no showers or laundry at the campsite). Used Tatiana and Noah's Torrey hotel for showers, pool, hot tub, AC and power.
- Hike: 5-mile Grand Wash Trail (Caroline has visited 36 national parks). Carmen, Aarushi and Drew put rain covers on gear when it drizzled.
- Campground pie shop makes fresh pies daily (peach, apple, cherry, mixed berry, strawberry rhubarb) and homemade ice cream.
- Flash flood meter: "not expected" to "possible" to "probable"; a ranger turned cars back from the scenic drive.
- Lunch at a Mexican restaurant; Torrey farmers market (lemonade, zucchini and banana bread, live band).
- **BIFF** (Bicknell International Film Festival): annual "bad film" night, raffle (won goldfish crackers), film "Hello Down There" (1969). Afterparty at bar; under-21s couldn't join.
