Author: Nate (2026).

- Rafting booked the night before: Class 2 ("more for scenery") vs Class 5 ("extremely strong swimming ability"). Company needed all 7 of the team to go (minimum 7 people to run a trip). Chose Class 2 with two double kayaks; steering inflatable kayaks is hard (paddle backward). Cliff jump 30 ft. Bus 20 min to river, 40 min back.
- Dinner: food hall too expensive (burgers $20-25, empanadas ~$6); picked Chinese takeout (big portions).
- **Finance**: Nate and Nunu spent 2-3 hours on receipts and expenses to update the "badly out of date" budget tracking spreadsheet. Lesson: update the budget regularly rather than weekly batches.
- Aarushi's friend Margarita visited; Airbnb has sauna and hot tub.
