Zion National Park, UT to Cedar City, UT — 45.6 mi, 3737 ft. Author: Caroline (2026).

- Caroline's family (drove 12 h from California; 11-person party incl. grandpa, dad, brother Brady) visited for the Zion rest day and stayed an extra day; they helped as chauffeurs while the team split to get Drew a new bike (Aarushi and Carmen went to Las Vegas).
- Hiked the Narrows, 5 am wakeup, shuttle line at 6:20 am; rental boots + pole $30 each from outfitters (family wore sneakers and found sticks - not needed). Complicated plan to meet with the minivan at Zion Lodge failed because the gate code for the car-restricted road was unknown; Lodge food overpriced. Lesson: no phone service in Zion - set meeting points in advance.
- Caroline left early with her family; poke and boba in Cedar City; free **Southern Utah Museum of Art**. Met Carmen's mom (Tara) and sister (Simone).
- Cedar City Airbnb donated by Carmen's family; dinner of homemade butternut squash pasta, chicken, brownies.
- Ride (3 pm to 10 pm): parallel side roads to minimise time on I-15; extreme heat (100+), concerns Aarushi might pass out; gas stations as AC oases; gravel hill. Start delayed to 3 pm to wait out heat.
