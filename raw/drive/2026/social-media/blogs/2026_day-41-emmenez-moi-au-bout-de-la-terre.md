Rest day, Colorado Springs, CO — 0 mi (optional 9.6 mi, 1117 ft). Author: Carmen (2026).

- Home-cooked breakfast and dinner by Aarushi's family (the Mehrotras: omelets, poha, mango smoothie, Indo-Chinese cauliflower). Tatiana's grandparents also present.
- Pikes Peak: noon entry, ~3 hours up and down; removed minivan's camper top so it would not blow off. Switchbacks without guardrails. Souvenir stickers.
- Mountain biking (Drew and Carmen) on gravel bikes at Red Rock Canyon Open Space (across from Garden of the Gods); Garmin cannot navigate trail junctions; 5 ft drop walked down. Tip: gravel bikes can handle some MTB trails but walk big features.
- Freddy's in Colorado Springs: burgers overhyped; In-N-Out considered better. World Cup quarterfinal Argentina vs Switzerland watched at Airbnb.
