Roanoke, VA to Blacksburg, VA: 40.5 mi, 3,294 ft (about 48.5 mi, 4,794 ft including hiking). Author: Tatiana.
- First day anyone cooked: Nate made breakfast with a carton of eggs from Dirk and Krissie in Steve and Debbie's kitchen.
- Host Steve (cycling grandpa jersey) rode 20 miles with the team to the McAfee Knob trailhead (most photographed spot on the Appalachian Trail). Team hiked 4 miles up and 4 down, met AT hikers ("Coach" and "Detail"), then picnic lunch in the parking lot.
- Final 750 ft climb in 3 miles to host Ann's house in Blacksburg; Ann told of her own trip across Illinois at age 14; dinner with her; mini fruit jelly cups; gummy worms every half mile helped. Nunu's chain fell twice and Nate rescued her.
- Reflection on wanderers and hope. Routine: chores, showers, route planning, bed.
