Kansas City, MO to Overbrook, KS: 75.5 mi, 3771 ft. Author: Tatiana. New state (Kansas).
- 4am wake due to heat forecast. Cooler condiment spill stank; scrubbed in the hotel shower ("we need a new cooler").
- Dutch Bros as first rest stop; "fish scale clouds". Road closure solved with the Public Land Survey System grid (all roads 1x1 mile squares): ride one more mile and take the next turn; Garmin kept trying to reroute back. Gravel Edgerton Rd.
- Vinland Valley Nursery (family-owned, pick-your-own flowers, ceramics) side quest with free iced drinks.
- Two flats on gravel (Tatiana's tire popped; Caroline's sealed itself). Fixed with spare tire taken off Nunu's bike. Nunu went to Lawrence, KS where a Trek bike shop could fix the tire. Tubeless sealant worked for some punctures. Tatiana bonked (low energy) because lunch was skipped; emergency bars from Carmen helped.
- Host Scott (Overbrook; also owns a retirement community): took team to a local tavern famous for burgers, pickleball at the courts ("be more fun than last year's team"), water tower "Don't Overlook Overbrook", jacuzzi.
- Blog crossword contest: $5 Venmo prize to the first correct emailer at spokes-2026@mit.edu (team email address).
- Lesson: pre-plan lunch food; check tire repair options on gravel; Kansas grid detours are easy.
