Author: Drew (2026).

- Golden, CO: rest stop moved to Woody's Pizza (all-you-can-eat buffet $18.99); the original coffee shop charged $8 for a lemonade.
- Clear Creek: team floated light rapids on their inflatable "floating dock".
- Largest climb so far: 2,000 ft over 10 miles. Rockies climbs are long uphill followed by long downhill, unlike repetitive Appalachian climbs, but altitude makes people winded/nauseated.
- Construction detour roads handled by chill workers. Lodging in Idaho Springs: donated Airbnb with hot tub; Aarushi and Nunu cooked pasta. Drew felt sick (altitude).
