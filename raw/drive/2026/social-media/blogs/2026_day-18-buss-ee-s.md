Mammoth Cave, KY to Bowling Green, KY: 35.6 mi, 1393 ft. Author: Aarushi (driver).
- Reflection on highs and lows: tree crash, dog bite, sprained ankle, numb hands, bad sleep, but also waterfall, hammocks, many free meals from strangers.
- Booked Mammoth Cave Historical Tour 11:45am. Late start (9am wake). Everyone rode for the first time in a week.
- Mishap: Nunu and Aarushi drove the extra rental F-150 to Bowling Green (Enterprise) and the minivan; the nearly-empty roof storage bag unzipped on I-65 and lost two camping pillows, a sleeping pad, a camping chair, a hammock and a blanket. Enterprise refunded $100 for returning a day early; they then got rain and a flood warning. Lesson: remove or strap the roof bag when not full; check zippers.
- Mammoth Cave facts (tour): found 1797 by John Houchin; saltpeter mining 1810-1815; longest cave system in the world; artifacts to 5000 BCE. Visitor center cafe is overpriced. Mailed postcards from Mammoth Cave post office (team sends a postcard from each state).
- Rest stop: Buc-ee's (snack haul, brisket sandwiches); team tax: share snacks.
- Lodging in Bowling Green: two neighboring townhouses (3 bedrooms each, laundry), donated; Sam is the Bowling Green LF coordinator; Adam and Lindsey hosted for free.
- Dinner: Thai Thai of Bowling Green donated a meal. Movie night: We're The Millers. Tatiana's boyfriend Noah visiting for the weekend.
