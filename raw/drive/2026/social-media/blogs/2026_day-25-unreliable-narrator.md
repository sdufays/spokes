St. Louis, MO to Hermann, MO: 74.4 mi, 2428 ft. Author: Aarushi. The blog contains 15 intentional "lies" (unreliable narrator format), so treat narrative details with caution; logistics below are the plausible ones.
- One third of the trip. Host Alex (St. Louis, second apartment; left a pasta shell bake); wore cycling lederhosen outfit; took team through Forest Park (1904 World's Fair/Olympics history), Kaldi's Coffee, Wash U campus. Greta (Tatiana's St. Louis acquaintance) met in Forest Park. Slept on floor in sleeping bags the night before.
- First rest stop: Sophia M. Sachs Butterfly House (butterfly farm, snow cone truck, carousel). Lunch from a sausage donated by Ann (Virginia) and plastic cutlery gifted by Theresa (Virginia).
- Caroline SAGed with elbow/pinky numbness nerve pain. Katy Trail (240-mile rail trail across Missouri) to a Missouri River pier rest stop.
- Thunderstorm: Nunu and Tatiana ran 1.5 miles to the Augusta, MO trailhead (ghost-town storefronts); waited until after 7pm, then Nate SAGed everyone.
- Dinner donated by Philly's Pizza in Marthasville; Airbnb along the Katy Trail donated by Kate Engelmann. Team named the car.
- Lesson: lightning plan (get off bikes, shelter); Katy Trail is good for riding but with limited services.
