Author: a 2026 rider (graduated MIT Fri May 29; in Washington DC June 1 to start). Rode with roommate/best friend Aarushi. Longest ride before trip: ~70 miles once; initially hated biking, learned to love it.

Goals: push herself, adventure and new friends, learn/be inspired.

Team: **Tatiana** ring leader, **Aarushi** comedy, **Drew** skeptic/optimal-path, **Carmen** backbone, **Nate** muse, **Caroline** voice of wisdom (youngest).

Mishaps in week 2 (2026): Aarushi crashed head first into a tree, Caroline was bitten by a dog, Tatiana sprained her ankle. These brought the team closer.

Takeaways: strangers were extremely kind (nightly hosts cooked elaborate meals and prepared beds, strangers on the street donated cash, family-owned restaurants gave great food). Stayed in Denver with a Spokes alum who said her lesson was that you can go on such adventures on your own. Barely used phone/laptop on trip.
