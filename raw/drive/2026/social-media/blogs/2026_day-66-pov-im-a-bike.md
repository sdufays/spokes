Ely, NV to Eureka, NV — 79.2 mi, 4543 ft. Written by "Tatianabike" (2026).

- Bike condition: chains unlubed for days, axles coming loose, sticky brake, gears not shifting, worn grips. "Drewbike" (lost earlier) replaced by Drewbike2 with hydraulic brakes, pink.
- Lodging in Ely: slept in the **White Pine County Library** (not a shed); a library staff woman brought a tray of breakfast burritos and cinnamon rolls. Team did a puzzle, watched a movie until 1:30 am.
- Nate's birthday: Drew drove ahead to buy cake and snacks (Nilla Wafers, peach rings, Cheez-its, oranges); card, party hat, kazoos; Spotify playlist of the summer.
- Nate's tire popped 5 mi in; Drew drove back to Ely for a new tire.
- Heat, flies swarming food, Fly swatter (Nunu's) used by Nate. Tatiana and Caroline ill (following Aarushi and Drew). 1,500 ft climb in 9 miles; Aarushi retold Percy Jackson to distract Tatiana.
- Ended at a gas station in Eureka; the host **church was 18 mi north of the route**, so team was shuttled by car; Drew bought pizzas. Showers at church. Some cowboy camped outside.
- Lesson: lube chains and check bikes more regularly in desert stretches; sick days are rough in 100 degree Nevada.
