Columbia, MO to Marshall, MO: 67 mi, 2,432 ft. Author: Carmen.
- Team has a spectrum from "speed demon" to "extreme sidequester" that became divisive. Conclusion: balance, "efficient dilly dallying", consider drivers and hosts (arrive before dinner/quiet hours).
- Host Vince made biscuits, gravy, watermelon; team deep-cleaned muddy bikes in his yard (mud and sand in derailleurs/brakes causing squeaks).
- Katy Trail dried overnight. Sidequests: biker cafe, roadside peaches, art gallery (90+ min off bike), Boonville festival (quilting circle, vintage car show), a casino (Tatiana won 19 cents of $5). Lunch sandwiches with sun-dried tomatoes, pesto, pickled onions; ice cream shop (shared flavors).
- Nate and Nunu collided near the end (his Garmin and phone screen protectors cracked).
- Hosts Dave and Shandra with dog Percy: iced tea; Dave had read all blogs. They agreed to serve breakfast at 5am to beat the 95F heat.
- Lesson: when a hot forecast looms, start at 5am and have the host breakfast ready.
