Learning Festival in Fallon, NV — 0 mi. Author: Carmen (2026). Includes her bullet notes and the final blog.

**Night before**: team woke in a ditch on Highway 50, then slept at the **Fallon Youth Club (FYC)** Learning Center (ottomans, floor, communal blankets). Motion-sensor lights kept turning on at night.

**Hosts/food**: FYC hand-delivered breakfast takeout boxes (pancakes, bacon, eggs, hash browns), later spaghetti dinner and burritos for next morning breakfast. Pre-LF tradition: coffee at a log cabin cafe (32 oz iced latte $8.50 split vs ~$10 for smaller fancy drinks).

**LF**: it was mandatory school-supply pickup day, so almost all registered kids cancelled; one kid first (taught chemistry by Tatiana, Aarushi, Caroline, Nunu), more kids at 2 pm for Monster Genome (4 kids made monster siblings) and electromagnetic motors (Drew and Nate). Last time the chemistry workshop was run.

**FYC**: nonprofit, affordable aftercare, grants plus a "Friends of FYC" donor group, 10 employees, waiting list; foster kids automatically enrolled and prioritised; mostly single-parent families near poverty level.

**Other**: Caroline and Aarushi spoke for the team on a Zoom event hosted by Tatiana's friend (turned out to be ~400 people). Laundry done by hand at a laundromat by Drew and Nate after days without. Leftover LF crafting, chemistry and electronics supplies donated to FYC rather than storing them in "milkdrop."

**Plan**: team meeting decided to wake at 1 am to beat heat/wind; bibs ready (Nate slept in his); Aarushi driver next day, route made at night, Walmart run for sodas and Arizona iced tea (the "liquid diet").

Lessons: check local school calendars when scheduling LFs (supply pickup killed turnout); donate leftover supplies to host.
