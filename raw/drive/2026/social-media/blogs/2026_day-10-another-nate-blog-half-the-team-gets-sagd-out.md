Blacksburg, VA to Tazewell, VA: 83.38 mi, 4744 ft. Author: Nate.
- 6am wake, 1h45m to get out the door (team record). Host Ann made oatmeal and eggs. Nunu's chain fell off twice previous day; diagnosed "misaligned derailleur". Caroline cannot drive (unofficial driver day for her).
- Aarushi hit a pothole, rode off road and into a tree: scraped, dent in helmet, bent handlebars. Tatiana SAGed her back to Blacksburg: the Trek Bicycle shop (Spokes bikes are Trek) honored the 1-year helmet warranty (new helmet) and fixed handlebars; derailleur barrel adjuster broken and part would take a week; next door "Hokie Spokes" bike shop fully repaired the bike. Two passersby donated $41 (Bruce) and $20 (Wendy) after hearing the Spokes story.
- No bike shop in destination town (Tazewell): do repairs in the start town. Lesson: minivan carries only 2 people/bikes, so SAG logistics split the team; reorganized minivan and bike rack to hold 3 people and 3 bikes.
- Dairy Queen at mile 30. Thunderstorms forecast; took cover under a church porch in Narrows, VA; four riders napped on concrete until 1:45pm. All 7 converged at rest stop mile 44 at 2:30pm for the usual sandwich lunch.
- Carmen SAGed 13 miles to a gas station for a restroom (no bathrooms along route). Nunu had sharp knee pain and was SAGed at mile 65. Guidance from PT Brendan at Cycle Life PT (who did the bike fittings): sharp, repetitive pain means stop riding. Team notes SAG out is emotionally hard. Road named "Scenic Highway".
- Host in Tazewell: Teresa of Tazewell United Methodist Church (Main Street Methodist) hosted in a house-turned-office with pizza, fruit, juice, sodas and a fridge stocked for breakfast and dinner; also LF site next day. Slept 9 hours.
