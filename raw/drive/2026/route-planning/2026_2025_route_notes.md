## Summary of changes recommended
1. Stay somewhere else day-3 night to avoid the insane Skyline climb followed by an immediate descent day 4. 2. Avoid Breaks Interstate Park campground (rained on); maybe avoid Big A mountain. 3. Follow USBR 76 more closely to save mountains in VA. 4. Roanoke -> Blacksburg VA. 5. Day 39: get off Interstate 25 (75 mph limit). 6. Day 56: back to 2024's route along the main highway. 7. Day 62: no gravel descent.
## Route team notes from Joseph (2025 alum)
Day 41 CO Springs -> Denver: sharp turn section of Hwy 85 is high speed with no shoulder. Day 45 Black Hawk -> Frisco: bad gravel descents. Day 49 only option even on I-70. Grand Junction lacks water. Route 128 = majestic. Moab housing is hard; Wingate Campground sucks; way out of Moab (Rt 191) is high-traffic with no shoulder. Day 56 gravel descent was very bad (someone broke an arm). Zion housing hard, consider campground; book Moab and Zion campgrounds early.
## 2025 Days 1-25
- D1 DC LF (host via Aarushi's dad's friend). D2 DC -> Linden VA 82 mi / 5,361 ft, "hellish hills, diabolic descent", host MIT alum Jen and Tim.
- D3 Linden -> Big Meadows Campground 55.8 mi / 6,555 ft; Skyline Dr 10-mile 3,000 ft climb. D4 -> Afton 42.7 mi / 3,045 ft; skipped Skyline descent due to rain/fog (drove). Host Pam Mendosa - STRONG RECOMMEND. D5 Afton rest.
- D6 Afton -> Lexington 47 mi / 4,354 ft; 3,000 ft climb, avg 10%, max 17% grade; hosts Dirk and Chrissy. D7 -> Roanoke 59.6 mi / 4,160 ft; hosts Lisa and Rob (good reviews). D8 -> Radford 52.4 mi / 4,265 ft; hosts Sharon and Keith. D9 -> Tazewell 68.2 mi / 4,380 ft; Theresa at Main Street UMC. D10 Tazewell LF (K-12).
- D11 Tazewell -> Breaks Interstate Park 61.9 mi / 6,827 ft (campground rainy, wouldn't recommend). D12 -> Hazard KY 84.4 mi / 4,671 ft, host Sherry; super rural. D13 Hazard LF at Challenger Learning Center (nearest bike shop 2 hrs away in Lexington KY; late nights 3D printing for the workshop; K-9).
- D14 Hazard -> London KY 70.7 mi / 5,293 ft; road flooded to Sizerock, rerouted via Hell for Certain Rd (puddles/potholes). D15 -> Liberty KY 78 mi / 4,710 ft; Liberty Assembly of God Church, spokies cooked. D16 -> Mammoth Cave NP 88.6 mi / 5,465 ft; NP campground. D17 -> Bowling Green 32.7 mi / 1,342 ft; did Mammoth Cave tour; Buc-ee's a must; "best airbnb ever" from LF partner. D18 rest. D19 Bowling Green LF (biggest yet, 4 groups of 15-20).
- D20 -> Madisonville KY 84.8 mi / 4,439 ft; very hot, heat exhaustion, start early; Life Christian Center (Pastor Jon, Isaac, Steve) warm meal. D21 -> Harrisburg IL 90.3 mi / 2,533 ft; host Sherry (hosted many Spokes generations). D22 -> Murphysboro IL 51.6 mi; a fall on a gravel turn. D23 -> St. Louis 101.4 mi (first century), hosts Nora and Alex; last-minute extra day in St. Louis. D24 rest. D25 St. Louis -> Jefferson City 125 mi (or 74.1 mi on alt route) - 5 am wakeup; Katy Trail 260 mi fine gravel; host Sarah.
