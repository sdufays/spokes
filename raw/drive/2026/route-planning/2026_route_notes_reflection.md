- Day 2 Lincoln Memorial -> Linden VA: do NOT do what 2026 did. Most years start from the host's house (closer to Linden) or take an extra day to stay with Jen. To do it in one day, take the straight route on Hwy 55 along Route 66; ignore Google Maps and Spokes 2025 on the W&OD trail (too far out of the way). Beautiful second half but too long for day 1; most got SAGged.
- Day 3 Linden -> Luray VA instead of Big Meadows (after a 90-mile day they were cooked; booked a campground in VA, took Rt 340; hosts shuttled them down the gravel hills; 2,400 ft climbing).
- Hazard -> London KY: "Hell for Certain Road" - just avoid the whole day maybe.
- Jen's house (Linden): a bit awkward, baby woken, messy. Leaving Breaks: beware of Max (dog).
- Don't stay with Janet: nice but a lot for them to host 7 people, weird place to sleep, smells.
