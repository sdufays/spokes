- Project title: MIT Spokes 2026. Cost object: 2530032. Proposed crowdfunding goal: **$15,000** (later raised to $20K; final ~$19-21K). Summary (100 chars): "Support Spokes as we bike across the country, rethinking STEM education for underserved communities!"
- Group: mission to share knowledge/passion for math, science, tech, engineering and humanities with young students across the US while biking 4,000 miles in 75 days; 8 MIT students (list: Aarushi Mehrotra '26 Course 20/6-3; Angelica (Nunu) Zhuang '26 Course 4; Carmen McCay '29 10B; Caroline Huang '29 11-6; Drew Dowdy '29 16; Nathan Mustafa '25 '26 6-3/18; Tatiana Vassiliev '26 6-2/15-3).
- Purpose: bridge the gap in STEM education/empowerment for rural, low-income and underserved communities and incarcerated youth (juvenile detention students get significantly less instruction time); show engineers/scientists can be athletic.
- Impact: LFs / all-day hands-on STEM workshops at schools, churches, libraries, juvenile detention centers, summer camps; curricula complement host K-12 schools; build connections with communities, students and host families.
