## Innovations Fund (OACES/IACME, oaces.mit.edu/resources/innovations-fund)
Small grants avg $100-$500 for individual/group seed-money projects by MIT undergrads (focus on OMESAC groups); must submit a 1-2 page outcome summary within two weeks of project completion. Spokes requested **$500** for workshop materials ($200 workshop materials, $150 educational supplies, $150 portable demo materials). Concept: modular interdisciplinary workshop - rotating stations (design/fabrication, electronics/circuits, aerodynamics/mechanics, computer science with micro:bit) feeding a shared final collaborative project.
Overall 50K budget stated: bike equipment 15K; transportation 15K; lodging 5K; food 10K; workshop supplies & marketing 5K. Projected revenue: individual donations 15K; university and external grants pending; Innovations Fund $500.
## Focused PKG Fellowship - Tatiana (draft)
Fields: project title (50 words), abstract (200), community challenge (500), community partner(s), timeframe, work plan (1500), skills (500), motivation (500), safety and cultural impact (500), letter of commitment from community partner (planned: Marvelin, Edgerton Center), letter of recommendation from MIT staff (planned: Bruno), optional extra recommendation ("Learning Festival guy"). Only the abstract was written: Tatiana's role = outreach to schools/libraries/orgs to coordinate LFs, then designing/teaching rotating-station workshops (engineering design, electronics, coding, sustainability). PKG Fellowships are awarded to individuals, not teams (each applicant submits their own).
