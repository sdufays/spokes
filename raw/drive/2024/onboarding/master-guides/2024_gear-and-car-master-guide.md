**Bikes:** Trek Cambridge (Alewife) gives 20% on gear - negotiate for big purchases; Cambridge Bicycle wholesale but small selection (also called overpriced); Landry's gave gear discounts (not bikes). Recommendations: go tubeless (fewer flats); everyone the same bike model (swap parts); gravel bikes, disc brakes, drop bars, 35 mm+ tires; shop around. 2026: Trek Checkpoint ALR3 gen3 (1x10 drivetrain, wide clearance; Trek warranties saved several hundred dollars); bike fits with Brendan Connor at CycleLifePT. 2023: Giant Revolt 2 / Liv Devote. Gear storage: Milkdrop N52 (coordinate with Edgerton).
**Must-have gear:** metal water bottles (2026 lesson: plastic doesn't stay cool; Camelbak Podium); 2024 started with 48 oz water capacity and upgraded to 72 oz in Utah; frame/saddle bags; first aid kit; spare tubes matching tires; multitool with allen keys; frame pump + floor pump with gauge; portable charger; front and rear lights; clip-in shoes; helmet; gloves; padded shorts; sunglasses; tire plugs for tubeless; seat upgrade optional (Terry Liberator used 2019/2022; 2024 and 2026 didn't). Nice-to-have: sun sleeves/legs, chamois butter, handlebar bag (fanny pack works), open-ear headphones (Shokz; helpful in Kansas/E. Colorado), fenders optional. Car kit: chain cleaning tools, allen keys, floor pump, extra sealant, tires/tubes, bike rack, locks. Maintenance: clean/lube weekly; everyone able to fix flats, tune derailleur, adjust brakes, lube chain.
**Car rental:** MIT discount with Budget and Avis (only companies covering drivers under 21). Contact Sandi slipnosk@mit.edu and Chris mayerc@mit.edu; in 2024 Chris was main contact. Reserve as soon as dates known. Minivan recommended (Budget Central Square, 350 Mass Ave). 2023 and 2024: 2022 Chrysler Pacifica; 2024 does not recommend the bike rack (bent and fell). 2022: Ford Explorer (6 riders), rack Saris Bones EX. 2023, 2024 and 2026 also rented a truck for first days to carry bikes/supplies/people (2024: Toyota Tacoma holding 3 bikes, returned at Dulles; 2026: cargo van). Minivan seats 7 for 8 riders; SUV costs more and is harder to drive; nothing smaller than a minivan; Stanford 2024 had an 11-seater. **One-way (dropped at SFO in 2023/2024) costs a few thousand dollars more and forces shipping bikes/gear back (2 days, very expensive); two-way means some East Coasters drive back.** **Avis/Budget was bad in 2024; contact Jessica Tam (VP for Finance office) - she got a discount after the trouble.** Background checks for drivers via Edgerton (start early). **Add 4 drivers at an Avis/Budget service desk (only 5 allowed total) so they can request roadside assistance; the named renter was biking once and they were refused.** Edgerton books the car and pays directly; travel card only for emergencies. Insurance info: https://insurance.mit.edu/services/insurance-coverage-claims/automobile/rentals/. At pickup: ask last service, photograph scratches, check engine and tires, check recalls. Car troubles: rental has no repair tools; call the roadside number; Avis switches the whole car (loaded with gear); accidents: call Edgerton, don't give personal insurance, carry written proof of MIT insurance. Day to day: hand vacuum, hard plastic boxes (Walmart), everyone learn to inflate tires, an overloaded van rides low (2024 got stuck on a bump, blog Day 52). 2026 note: pushback with MIT admin may be needed.
