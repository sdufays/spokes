**How to start planning:** get to know each other; money (grants early, crowdfunding must be through MIT not GoFundMe; talk to Marvelin Higginbottom marvelin@mit.edu, Sandra J Lipnoski slipnosk@mit.edu, Chris Mayer mayerc@mit.edu); bikes/gear (matching gravel tubeless bikes; bike fits with Brendan Connor through CycleLifePT - comes to campus); route and Warmshowers; curriculum; roles; weekly meetings with note taker + moderator; create a timeline; no need to reinvent the wheel.
**Timeline (2026 recommendation):** Nov: first meeting, read master guides, assign roles before Thanksgiving, start fundraising ASAP, introduce yourselves to Edgerton. Dec: rough start/end dates, crowdfunding page and website, reach out to departments (leftover funding in Dec), meet Edgerton, start training. Jan (IAP): plan route, shared GCal, finalize dates and 1-way vs 2-way rental, rough budget, apply for 2 pro cards and 2 travel cards, start LF outreach. Feb: weekly meetings, website, LF ideas, car rental + background checks, bike purchasing end of month, bike lab. Mar: **apply to J-WEL Education Innovation Grant (Spokes 2024 got most of its money there)**, intro blog, MIT 24 Challenge crowdfunding, order bike gear, second budget round. Apr: gear list, Midnight Marathon (bingo with dares), LF materials list, finalize LFs early, design/buy shirts and Jakroo jerseys, J-WEL result end of April, Edgerton Center Showcase, housing for first states. May: finalize housing and budget, tetanus shot, trip roles, train, photo release forms from Edgerton, optional local LF. June (first week): add all drivers to rental in person, organize car with plastic boxes, shared toiletries/sunscreen, set up bike computers, bike repair crash course. During trip: check-in meetings, learn to hold bike overhead.
**Roles (2026, recommended count):** Fundraising [2] (tracker, timeline, events like bike raffle/bake sale, email drafts, grant deadlines, crowdfunding site); Finances & Budget [2-4] (cards, tax exemption forms, receipts, itemized budget, spending report); Edgerton Contact [1]; Car Rental [1]; Route Planning [3]; Learning Festivals [2]; Housing [3]; Social Media [2] (graphics, contact Sonny Oram, Edgerton Communications Officer, shirts/jerseys); Gear [2] (holds pro card); Website [1]; Moderator/Notetaker [1]; Training [1]. Trip roles: Driver (alternates), Laundry, Thank-you cards, Dinner lead (2026 cooked 0 meals - one person called places nightly asking for free food, "extremely successful"), Blog lead (rotating daily writer: drivers, passengers, bikers), Documentation.

**Food and nutrition:** Carbs in the morning and while biking; protein at dinner; electrolytes (Nunn, Liquid IV, Propel, Gatorade; bulk Gatorade powder cheapest). Breakfast: bagels + cream cheese/PB/nutella, yogurt + granola, overnight oats in ziplocks, apples + PB, "Spokes 24 classic": powdered milk + instant coffee + cereal + powdered PB. Lunch 2022: sardines + tortillas, rotisserie chicken + Hawaiian bread, deli sandwiches, hummus + veggies. **Spokes 2024: bagel sandwiches ~74 days in a row** (deli meat/tofurky, hummus "very important", tomato, cream cheese, cheese, pickles, chips); discovered cheap Walmart French bread late. Spokes 2026: team found a snack Venn diagram and spammed it. Snacks: dried dates (Costco; carried 2022), dried fruit, Clif/Lara bars, Nature's Bakery brownie bars (2024 best), nuts. Dinner ideas (2 pots): jambalaya, feijoada, chicken salad, tacos, chicken and rice, spring rolls, one-pot pasta. Protein sources: sardines, TVP, shelf-stable tofu, canned chicken, protein powder, rotisserie chicken, deli meat, jerky, soymilk, PB. Tricks: Minute Rice, Costco and Walmart (you'll miss Walmart in remote areas), baby wipes, paper towels as plates, pocket stove (9 eggs at once, 2022), cold drinks from driver on hot days.
**Packing:** 2-3 bike outfits max, 2 non-bike outfits incl. Spokes shirt; laundry nearly daily; cold clothes for Rockies (50s F); synthetic/wool; small carry-on suitcase beats backpacking bag. Franklin's 2022 list: suitcase (2 bike outfits, sun sleeves, 2 casual, thermal leggings, pants, wool socks, puffy), backpack (toiletries, laptop, frisbee, Crocs), handlebar + saddle bags (snacks, phone, power bank, water bottles, pump, tools, first aid, air horn and mace for Kentucky dogs). 2024 bike gear recs: Pearl Izumi fingerless gloves, merino socks, clip-on shades for glasses wearers, jerseys with pockets, padded shorts, communal chamois butter.
