**Email templates (2026):** Past-location outreach to Judge Faruqui and DC Youth/Corrections: Spokes = 7 students, 80 days; offer June 1 festival. Follow-up template to Charles at Challenger Learning Center (Hazard KY, 6/14/2026, 1 Community College Dr, Hazard KY 41701): registration form/graphic, workshop descriptions, schedule (4 of 5 workshops in parallel per session), space needs (3 classrooms with projectors/outlets + 20x30 briefing room), expected student count and when to close registration, food (lunch for students and team; dinner/breakfast days around), photo release forms for parents, housing at Harmony House.
**Typical festival schedule:** 9-9:45 setup; 9:45 students arrive; 10-11 and 11-12 workshop periods; 12-1 lunch; 1-2 and 2-3 workshops; 3-3:15 wrap-up; 3:15-4 cleanup. 4 workshops in parallel x 4 sessions; build in breaks/snacks.

**2019 reflections:** best impact middle/early high school; many elementary kids needing more attention; disliked Farmington/St. Louis LFs, some disliked Kansas City; LFs clustered at start/end leaving dry middle; target small towns in Kansas/Colorado/Utah; continuity matters (Hazard). Use a status spreadsheet; call rather than email if hosts slow. Class prep was too late and funding struggles lasted until April - finish before SPARK. Hosts 2019: Challenger Hazard (first LF, fewer students was helpful - do a smaller practice one before Hazard), Lab: Revolution Farmington MO (Ann; paid hotel when hostel full), St. Louis Homeschool Connection, aSTEAM Village Kansas City (80 kids, behavior difficulty, poor lunch, camp costs money), Newton KS (Marianne), Boys and Girls Club Denver, Christa McAuliffe Space Center Lindon UT (space sim; ~20 kids), White Pine County Library Ely, Churchill County Library Fallon, Boys and Girls Club Carson City.
