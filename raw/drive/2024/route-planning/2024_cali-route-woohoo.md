# Cali route (Aug 2024)
- **Aug 8: Fallon NV to South Lake Tahoe NV.** Per Strava, all paved, approx 96 mi and 5,200 ft elevation; passes Spooner Summit (as "Dave" said). (Actual ridden: 109 mi, 5,712 ft.)
- **Aug 12: South Lake Tahoe to Placerville CA** (contact number in doc: 510 303 0071).
  - Rough calc: 21.17 mi (1,878 ft up, 1,062 ft down) to the 88/89 junction; 67.4 mi (4,448 ft up, 9,722 ft down) to Diamond Springs.
  - Route: https://bestrides.org/carson-pass-plus/ ; goes through Luther Pass and Carson Pass along CA-88 and CA-89; RideWithGPS https://ridewithgps.com/routes/37797133 (very high reviews).
  - Diamond Springs is about 5 miles from Placerville. Messaged Warm Showers host in Pleasant Valley (few miles east of Diamond Springs). Ideally stay in Diamond Springs or Placerville; need to call churches in both. (Outcome: First Lutheran Church of Placerville hosted, see 2024 Route.)
- **Aug 13: Placerville to Sacramento** along bike trail: https://ridewithgps.com/routes/44947123 . Messaged Sacramento Warm Showers host (outcome: Robert Mandelson).
- **Aug 14: Sacramento to Vallejo (ended Benicia)**: https://ridewithgps.com/routes/37467871 "seems good".
