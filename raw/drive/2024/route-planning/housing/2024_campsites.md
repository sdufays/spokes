# Campsites (Utah leg, Spokes 2024, notes from Mar 2024)
- **Dead Horse Point State Park / Wingate Campground (7/21-7/22):** reserve on ReserveAmerica (dead-horse-point-state-park/UT/344161).
- **Green River State Park:** ReserveAmerica (green-river-state-park/UT/344341); all sites unavailable at time, "hopefully reservations not open yet".
- **Capitol Reef NP / Fruita Campground:** recreation.gov campground 272245; available.
- **Bryce Canyon / Ruby's Inn Campground:** available - book the tent ("T00n") campsite.
- **Zion NP:** Watchman Campground.
- **Lake Tahoe:** likely find Warm Showers hosts; unsure camping is worth it.
