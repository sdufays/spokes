## Route ideas
- Try to stay on **US Bike Route 76** to Colorado. USBR 76 is divided into 12 sections, section 1 in OR and 12 in VA; we start at section 12 and count backwards.
- **If the Utah national parks route:** Arches, Canyonlands, Capitol Reef, Bryce and Zion during July. Cedar City library could host a learning festival after Zion (Director Steven Decker, dsteve@cedarcity.org, contacted 2/4/24); only about 180 miles from Ely NV (White Pine LF).
- Other LF options: Lamar Public Library, Gunnison Public Library.
- **Flint Hills Trail State Park (KS):** rails-to-trails, very bike friendly (camping, towns, likely Warm Showers hosts). Last year they went through Emporia (shorter) but this is safer; could do all 118 miles in one day.

## Campgrounds (from 2023 route)
Lewis Mountain Campground VA; Breaks Interstate Park VA; Mammoth Cave Campground Site 103; Council Bluff Recreation Area Site 27; Wingate Campground Site 28 (Dead Horse State Park); Green River State Park Campground; Fruita Campground Sites 57, 58 (Capitol Reef NP); Ruby's Inn Campground (Bryce Canyon).

## America the Beautiful pass
Pass covers entrance/standard amenity day-use fees for the owner and accompanying passengers in a private vehicle; for per-person fee areas it admits the passholder plus three adults (16+). So 2 passes may cover all 8 riders. https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/passes.htm
