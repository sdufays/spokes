# 1/21/24 Route Planning (Spokes 2024)
## Actionables
- Start confirming learning festivals first (cannot fully plan route until these are in place) - marked done.
- Find campgrounds (need to book by February for some national parks).

## Planning method / resources
- Advice from Spokes 2023 doc; 2023 route sheet; 2022 route sheet; "Spokes Route Planning and Housing Master Guide" (Google Doc 1U67diTUgAo0_ITKXkeBuOrD7iFSWNXaekpFSDA2-vLc).
- **Find housing before choosing towns** (Warm Showers, alums, hotels/campgrounds).
- Use Strava premium for day-to-day route; use Komoot for planning and Google Maps to navigate.
- 2022 teams recommend a route similar to theirs; keep building relationships with hosts and learning festivals.
- Followed US Bike Route 76 (TransAmerica) from DC to Colorado. Adventure Cycling has USBR info.
- Keep Warm Showers map, Google Maps and USBRS maps open at once.
- Day-to-day navigation: the driver plans next day's route (including all stops) and leads the group.
- Everyone adds cool stops to the shared Google Map in a layer named after them.
- National parks: check on timed entry tickets.
