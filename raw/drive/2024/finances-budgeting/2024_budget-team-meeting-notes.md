## Notes from the call with Sandi (6/4/24)
- $12k from bikes: J-WEL will backdate the grant to cover expenses from March; money goes back to Spokes. All charges go to J-WEL first (credit Spokes account first).
- Within the week charges should be moved over so the balance is visible.
- ProCard and Travel Card can go wherever. Travel card charges are not allocated until the end of the trip (account number assigned at the end). ProCard charges are posted to J-WEL with the appropriate account number by the ProCard poster (Marvelin).
- Team has no access to the J-WEL cost object; Sandi may need to ask J-WEL (Maria) for viewing rights. Travel charges won't post until the trip ends (like a loan until the report is filed); Travel card visible in Atlas profile.
- ProCard: you should receive emails from the ProCard administrator acknowledging charges; send receipts to Marvelin.
- Sandi to send: SAP GUI app screenshots, breakdown of how much was raised/spent, statements.
- Travel card: upload receipts to your travel profile but cannot submit until the summer (edgertoncenter.mit.edu clubs & teams FAQ has a travel report section). Post all receipts for food and anything over $75.
- Reimbursements: travel charges (gas, food, flights, hotels) not reimbursed until trip ends (report with travel card). Anything bought for the project can be submitted via RFP directly through the account. Massachusetts purchases: deduct the tax amount (you pay it); if tax is too high, put separately as a meal etc. Other states: all taxes are reimbursed (tax exemption not valid there).
- Reading balance: "Commitments" = unfulfilled purchase order (still to be paid). Transaction list and account balance totals will not match, and that is okay.
