- Systems: https://mit.coupahost.com/ ; guide https://vpf.mit.edu/buy-to-pay-b2p
- New cost object (2024): 2530508
- Do not buy items considered personal, except food. Maximum transaction limit: not set in stone.
- Ship-to address can be personal. Example used: JD Hagood, 189 Vassar St, Cambridge, MA 02139.
- Commodities: M&S (420226), Raw Materials (420310) or Miscellaneous Materials (420245) are most appropriate. Do not use the recreational GL.
