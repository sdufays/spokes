People Venmo you to do a dare; you cross it off the bingo grid and post proof. Duration 1 week. Example grid: I'll make you food; crack an egg on my head $5; wear my helmet around campus for a day $10; viral TikTok dance $2; control the music I listen to for a day $2; dancing down the Infinite $8. Put Venmo handle at the bottom; flier by social media team.
