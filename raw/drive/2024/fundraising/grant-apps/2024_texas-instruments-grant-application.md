Mission (<200 words): MIT Spokes, started 2013, group of 8 students bikes DC to SF; learning festivals; some students from rural communities; planning a festival at DC Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services and working with a San Francisco judge to set up one in the criminal-justice system.

Impact numbers used: 10 teachers, 500 students; 50% male / 50% female; 15% Hispanic/Latino, 15% Black, 70% White; 70% low/moderate-income. **Requested amount: $6,000.**
