Idea: students design a city transportation system under budget and carbon constraints; stakeholder roles (city government, climate activist, oil tycoon, grid owner, wealthy resident, low-income resident); cards moved on a map. Learning goals: carbon emissions from transport; tradeoffs (emissions, capital/operating cost, land use).
Structure: Intro on emissions and Sankey diagrams with toy vehicles as props; Activity 1: groups get fake money and carbon credits (carbon tax if exceeded), city size/population and info sheet (cost, emissions, land use, people served); buy options from module organizer; part 2 adds world events (recession, hotter planet, oil price rise, cheaper solar electricity); Activity 2: map with neighborhood populations and traffic patterns; place roads, rail, bike lanes, subway; Conclusion: groups present reasoning. Adaptation: local examples (rail-rich cities vs rural roadway design); simplify info sheet for middle school; add maintenance costs for high school. Teacher PD: practice runs. Materials: cardstock, toy car/truck/bus. Team: Jess Xu (M.Eng CS), Hank Stennes (CS, Chicago), Cleo (math/AI, Paris) - none available in summer. Learned of grant via dormspam. Contact spokes-2024@mit.edu.
