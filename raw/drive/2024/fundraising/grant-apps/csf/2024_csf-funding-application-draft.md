Link: https://csf.mit.edu/get_involved/apply_for_funding (csf-staff@mit.edu). Due March 31. **Framing**: CSF funds service that improves lives and builds bridges between MIT and Massachusetts communities; serve underserved populations; started by/engaging MIT community; no CSF funds for volunteer goods (e.g., t-shirts); seed/short-term funding to become self-sustaining; funding limited to 3 application cycles. Spokes' angle: "short-term need" = establish permanent Boston ties (DYS correctional facilities, libraries, schools), new for Spokes.
Project: "Learning Festivals at Local Massachusetts Schools and Correctional Facilities - MIT Spokes". 8 MIT volunteers; references: Amulya Aluru aaluru@mit.edu, Rebecca Lizarde rlizarde@mit.edu, Clelia clelia27@mit.edu, Hank Stennes hpstennes@mit.edu, Jessica Xu jyxu@mit.edu, Sophia Wang wangsj@mit.edu.
Partners: TEJI (The Educational Justice Institute) to contact DYS facilities for April-May festivals; Morse Institute Library and Newton Free Library; later Splash and dynaMIT. Each member ~5 hrs prep + 5 hrs teaching.
Other funding mentioned: J-WEL education innovation grant, MIT Open Learning pK-12 grant, MIT COOP grant, Baker Foundation; alumni clubs; crowdfunding; Edgerton Center.
Workplan: Spring 2024 local festivals (funds needed bike equipment $15,000, workshop supplies $2,500); Summer 2024 cross-country (food & lodging $8,000, transport $12,000, educational supplies $500); Fall 2024 data analysis/toolkits; IAP 2025 + Spring 2025 handoff to Spokes 2025; model repeated 2025-26, 2026-27. Funding sources by category: bike equipment from crowdfunding/departments/alumni clubs; workshop supplies CSF; transport partly CSF, crowdfunding, sponsors, J-WEL; food & lodging partly CSF, Edgerton Center.
