Submitted 3/1/24 via https://www.mathworks.com/company/aboutus/soc_mission/grant_application.html . Requested $3,000. Outcome: $1,000 (per fundraising tracker).
Budget total $40,330 (bikes $16,000; transport incl. support car rental, gas, flights $12,500; lodging $2,030; food $6,000; workshop supplies $3,000; website/marketing $500; safety $300). Reach: festivals draw ~30-80 attendees; ~10 festivals x ~50 = ~500 students.
Previous MathWorks funding: 2022 $1000; 2019 $1500.
**Gifts received by 3/1/2024 (as listed in app):** $500 individual (11/20/23); $500 MIT Climate and Sustainability Consortium (12/21/23); $25 + $25 individuals (12/31/23); $500 MIT History (1/2/24); $500 MIT Music (1/2/24); $500 MIT Urban Science & Planning (1/2/24); $500 MIT Aero/Astro (1/3/24); $100 individual (1/3/24); $500 MIT Materials Science (1/5/24); $2,500 MIT Libraries (1/12/24); $50 individual (2/13/24); $1,000 MIT Museum (2/14/24); $200 Bike Safe Boston (2/23/24); $250 MIT Club of Southern California (2/26/24); $750 Kars4Kids (awaiting check).
Org structure: 8 students supported by the Edgerton Center (logistics and some funding); tax-exempt through MIT. Sponsors recognized on website and on learning-festival t-shirts.
Alignment angle: first and last festivals in DC and SF near MathWorks offices (Chevy Chase MD, Santa Clara CA); possible Boston-area festival near Natick HQ.
