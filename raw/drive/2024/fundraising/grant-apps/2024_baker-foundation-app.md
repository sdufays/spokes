Starting date June 3, 2024. Student organizers (MIT emails): Rebecca Lizarde rlizarde@mit.edu; JD Hagood jdhagood@mit.edu; Sophia Wang wangsj@mit.edu; Jessica Xu jyxu@mit.edu; Clelia Lacarriere clelia27@mit.edu; Amulya Aluru aaluru@mit.edu; Varsha Sandadi vsandadi@mit.edu; Hank Stennes hstennes@mit.edu.

**Project**: team of 8 bikes D.C. to San Francisco, learning festivals at schools, libraries, youth correctional facilities; elementary to high school; workshops incl. intro physics, programming, biology, sustainable design; careful documentation to publish for other educators.
**Motives**: 3500 miles and 75 days, ~10 learning festivals; route favors underserved/rural areas; Teach for America helped identify communities in the past; networking dinners after festivals to answer college questions.
**History**: 10th year; learned that the most successful festivals are hands-on and avoid heavy lecture. 2024 changes: (1) detailed documentation of learning festivals so they can be reused; (2) host a local Boston-area festival to beta test before the road.
**Baker fit**: equitable education opportunities, rural funding inequity (school funding tied to community wealth), biking to show academics and athletics combine.
**MIT community impact**: inspire students who might not consider STEAM/MIT; broaden MIT demographics; inspire next Spokes cohort.
**Budget** (based on last year's expenses normalized for 8): Bikes & Gear $16,000; Transport $12,500; Lodging $2,030; Food $6,000; Educational Supplies $3,000; Marketing $500; Safety Gear $300; Total $40,330.
