Form asked daily availability (hourly Mon-Fri 11am-5pm) and weekly availability (Mar 4 - May 24). Respondents: Hank, Rebecca, JD, Jess, Amulya, Varsha, Sophia Wang (2/27-3/4/2024). Most were available all weeks; Varsha only Mar 18-22 and May 13-24; Amulya only Mar 6-8, 11-12, 20-22; Jess not available 3/15 or 3/18. Individual hourly detail omitted; the resulting schedule is in the boba fundraising sheet. Lesson: collect availability with a form 2 months ahead.
