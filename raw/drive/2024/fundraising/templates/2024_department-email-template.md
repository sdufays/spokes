**Remember to cc spokes-2024 on your emails!**

**General email**
Dear [contact name],

I'm [your name], a current [grade level] in Courses [course number]. I am writing to the [department name] to request sponsorship of MIT Spokes 2024. The Spokes team, made up of 8 MIT students, will be biking from Washington D.C to San Francisco this summer.

Along our route, we will stop at schools, libraries, and camps throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions. A goal of Spokes is to introduce science and design in exciting ways to students who may not have access to such opportunities.

We are starting the fundraising process for this summer's program, so I wanted to reach out to ask if [department name] would be willing to help fund this program and make our trip a reality by donating[, as it did last year!]. I've linked our website showcasing last year's success (https://www.mitspokes.com/), but I would love to get an opportunity to meet with you, share more about Spokes 2024, and discuss possible sponsorship to make the project possible!

Thank you,
[your name] & Spokes 2024

**Example email (2023 team, MechE)**: "Hello Colleen, My name is Andrew, and I am a current sophomore in Course 2. I am writing to the MechE department to request sponsorship of MIT Spokes 2023. Along with 6 other students... As a MechE student, I plan on hosting a 3D printing activity so they are able to learn about rapid prototyping and design... ask if our department would be willing to help fund this program and make our trip a reality as it did last year by donating $750."
(Lesson: personalize with your major/activity and name last year's gift amount.)
