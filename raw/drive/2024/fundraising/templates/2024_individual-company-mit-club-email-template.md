**Remember to cc spokes2024 on your emails!**

Key links used: Donation link http://giving.mit.edu/search/node/spokes ; MIT corporate matching https://giving.mit.edu/matching-gifts ; Website https://www.mitspokes.com/

### Individuals (professors, previous employers, etc.)
Dear (contact name), (insert personal connection here). This summer, I will be biking from Washington D.C. to San Francisco with MIT Spokes, a team of 8 MIT students hoping to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers. Along our route we stop at schools, libraries, and camps to hold free Learning Festivals with hands-on, project-based workshops... We are actively fundraising... Your support would help us pay for bike gear, camping fees, food, gas, and educational equipment. We're aiming for $40,330 to fund the 74-day trip but are only part of the way there. [donation link, corporate matching, website]. If you are unable to donate, please share our website and donation page; you can also sign up for updates when we start our journey. Thank you, [name] & Spokes 2024.

### MIT Club email
Dear [contact], My name is [name], a current MIT student studying [major and where you are from if relevant to the club]. I am writing to [club] to request sponsorship of MIT Spokes 2024... [we will be biking through your state (if relevant)] ... ask if [club] would be willing to help fund this program by donating. [For clubs:] We were also hoping you could advertise our crowdfunding link and MIT's corporate matching program to your members in events and newsletters. ... would love to meet, share about Spokes 2024 and discuss sponsorship.

### Company email
Same structure; includes "[insert your connection to company / how you would incorporate that into Spokes]". Ask the company to donate; link to website; offer to meet.

### ELOx (MIT Office of Experiential Learning) email (sent by Sophia Wang, Aerospace Eng.)
"Dear Office of Experiential Learning, ... I am writing to learn more about the ELO Teaching and Learning program on behalf of the Spokes team. Spokes is an MIT-based, non-profit, student-run initiative as part of an effort to rethink STEAM education. This year will be the 10th iteration... I know the ELO Office has funding opportunities for programs that make a difference in PK12 or higher education. I wanted to begin a conversation on how we could share our unique perspective as students at MIT to teach and impact a diverse set of local communities... let me know if you have any availability this week or the next."

### Cycling club email (two versions; second by Sophia, senior in aerospace)
Ask for sponsorship AND support: "We will be biking through your state, [state], and we're looking for enthusiastic cyclists to stay with and bike alongside." Include crowdfunding link and website.
