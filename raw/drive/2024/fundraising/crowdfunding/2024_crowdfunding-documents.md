**Requirements (MIT crowdfunding platform):** (1) dynamic campaign image 1-10 MB; (2) project title, 50 characters max; (3) campaign summary / call to action, 100 chars max; (4) project story with description and purpose; (5) recommended: additional images/videos (videos as links), infographics, GIFs. Last year's page for reference: https://crowdfund.mit.edu/story/Spokesamerica . 2024 page: https://crowdfund.mit.edu/story/Mit-Spokes-2024 .

Campaign image: IMG_1191.JPG (group photo, see Crowdfunding folder). Title: "MIT Spokes". Summary: "Support Spokes as we rethink STEM education for K-12 students in underserved communities!"

**Story text:** This summer, MIT Spokes is embarking on a cross-country bike ride to teach STEM workshops to underserved K-12 communities. Over 75 days, our team will bike over 4,100 miles from Washington, DC to San Francisco and host learning festivals, all-day events with hands-on STEM workshops taught by Spokes members. A central goal is bridging the gap in STEM education among rural, low-income and underserved communities; rural schools lack funding (property-tax funding exacerbates inequality). Spokes also addresses lack of engaging educational opportunities for incarcerated youth (students in juvenile detention receive less instructional time).

WHO WE ARE - 2024 team: Amulya Aluru '23/'24 (CS & Molecular Biology); JD Hagood '27 (Math, Physics); Clelia Lacarriere '27 (Math, AI & Decision Making); Rebecca Lizarde '24 (Materials Sci & Eng, Urban Science and Planning); Varsha Sandadi '23/'24 (Computation and Cognition); Hank Stennes '26 (CS&E); Sophia Wang '24 (Aerospace Eng); Jessica Xu '23/'24 (CS&E).
WHAT WE DO: create STEM curricula complementing host schools; teach at schools, libraries, summer camps; build connections with communities and host families; document; bike the whole 4,100 miles.
WHY BIKE: show engineers and scientists can be athletic; break stereotypes; step out of comfort zones.
WE NEED YOUR HELP: in addition to corporate sponsorships, crowdsource $10,000 (2024 goal; earlier drafts said $15,000).
