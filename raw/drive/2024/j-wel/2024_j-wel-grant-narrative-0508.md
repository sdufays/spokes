J-WEL Education Innovation Grants - Grant Narrative. Project title: *Bridging STEM Education Gaps: Fostering Aspirations through Learning Festivals and Deployable Learning Toolkits in Underserved American Communities*. Project lead: Prof. Kim Vandiver, Edgerton Center. Narrative is shared with J-WEL members, MIT faculty and stakeholders.

**8 students, 8 bikes, 80 days, 4000 miles. And a quest to rethink STEAM education.**

- **Need**: rural communities lag in education quality (school funding by property taxes); correctional facilities offer limited instruction. For over a decade Spokes teams have biked cross-country delivering STEAM workshops in schools, libraries and correctional facilities, raising aspirations (many students have not heard of MIT).
- **Activities**: learning kits as centerpiece of one-day learning festivals hosted by community partners built up over years; tailored to each community; post-tour review of partner comments and student interest surveys.
- **Team**: 3 graduate + 5 undergraduate students ("near peer role models").
- **By June 2025**: festivals delivered, accessible learning kits and instructional materials, expert-designed surveys, partnership with MIT education-outreach groups to disseminate.
- **Call agenda item**: discuss structure of reports given to J-WEL.
