April 23, 2024. To: Prof. Kim Vandiver (Edgerton Center). From: John Brisson (Faculty Chair, J-WEL Higher Education Advisory; Prof. Mech. Eng.) and Dr. Anjali Sastry (Faculty Director, J-WEL). Copy: Maria R. Segala (Data, Reporting, and Grants Administrator, J-WEL). J-WEL = MIT Jameel World Education Lab, MIT Office of Open Learning, 600 Technology Square, NE49-2nd Floor, Cambridge MA 02139.

Proposal: "Bridging STEM Education Gaps through Learning Festivals and Toolkits" for J-WEL 2023-2024 Grants in Education Innovation.

## Award
- **$45,330 allocated** for **July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025** (accepted "with a caveat", based on submitted budget).
- Student participants will each provide J-WEL a **photo essay** of activities throughout the project.
- J-WEL recommends a long-term tracking plan to assess participant outcomes.

## Requirements / obligations
1. **Before June 5, 2024**: send a brief one-page **Grant Narrative** (public: shared with MIT faculty, J-WEL members, website). Funding is released when it is received.
2. Consent to share the narrative.
3. Join at least one J-WEL event/forum to share insights (panel, talk, or recorded presentation) as feasible.
4. Respond to an informal **mid-year update meeting**.
5. Submit **final report within two months of project close, no later than Aug 31, 2025**, with outcomes vs. project metrics.
6. Respond to a 3-5 question interview (video or written) for the Grantee Spotlight Series.
- Subject to MIT IP/revenue-sharing policy. **If you obtain funding from other sources for any activities J-WEL was asked to support, you must tell J-WEL so they can reallocate funds.**
- Schedule a 20-minute intro meeting with Maria R. Segala in June (Calendly link in letter: calendly.com/j-welmsegala/20min).
- After the narrative is in, J-WEL assigns a Financial Analyst to process paperwork/fund transfer.
