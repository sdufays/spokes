Idema, J. and Patrick, P. G. (2019). International Journal of Science Education, Part B 9(3), 214-232. doi:10.1080/21548455.2019.1614238.

Abstract (only the first part of the 85k-character PDF was read): examined lasting impacts of a science festival on family members; 175 visitors completed a questionnaire on the day, and five families (7 children, 6 adults) did a drawing, drawing description and interview 3 months later. Children describe themselves as active participants while parents describe themselves as onlookers; families attend as a unit but experiences differ. Paper ends with seven characteristics of successful festival activities. Cited by Spokes 2024 for pre/post/follow-up survey design.
