## Prep
Print in color, double-sided (8.5x11): Atom Key/Layout Mat x4; What Is Air Made of x3; Burning Fuel Complete Combustion x4; Incomplete Combustion x4; Air Chemistry and Pollution x3; CO2 graph x1 (single-sided). Buy 8 plastic page stands. Print 2 copies of the Station Layout Cards, tape inside small takeout boxes, fill with the bricks (8 boxes, 2 per station). Print volunteer instruction sheets; brief and practice with volunteers before the event. Two participant chairs per station (2 people at a time).

## Stations
1. **Learn about air**: guess what air is made of (mostly nitrogen); build a model with bricks; 350 ppm CO2 as the climate-balance level; check current CO2 at co2.earth. Good for youngest kids.
2. **Burning fuel produces CO2**: build propane/octane combustion with oxygen into H2O + CO2; "clap for the spark".
3. **Burning fuel harms health**: incomplete combustion yields soot or carbon monoxide; discuss solutions (walk, bike, bus, EVs, no idling).
4. **Air pollutants**: build NO2, SO2, ozone; coal vs gas; good (stratospheric) vs bad (ground-level) ozone; smog; EPA Air Quality Index. Color side for younger kids, white-outline side for older.

Tip from the guide: ask visitors' names and responses first to gauge their level; invite shy kids to build with bricks.
