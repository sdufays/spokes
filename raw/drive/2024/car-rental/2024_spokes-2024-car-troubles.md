Email from Rebecca Lizarde (rlizarde@mit.edu) to Amulya Aluru and Jessica Tam (MIT Travel and Hospitality), cc spokes-2024@mit.edu, Mon 6/10/2024. Avis rental agreement # 757754911 (Chrysler Pacifica picked up at Logan airport Fri 5/31/2024).

## What went wrong (2024)
1. **Tue 6/4 night**: front tires found completely bald. Avis agreed to a tow truck but ETA was after 11:30pm; cancelled and rebooked 7am next morning.
2. Tow driver dropped two team members at Springfield VA (not Dulles Airport as texted), stranding them 45 min from Dulles; Avis first refused an Uber voucher, eventually gave it after arguing.
3. First replacement car at Dulles had a plastic wheel cover dragging; had to go back. Replacement: hybrid 2024 Pacifica with <7,000 miles.
4. Avis offered to refund only **2 days** of rental (policy usually 1) although the car was dangerous for 5.
5. **Thu 6/5, Shenandoah National Park**: the replacement Pacifica showed "Stop safely. Vehicle will turn off soon" and stalled mid-road; many years of hybrid Pacifica are recalled for electrical issues. A mechanic got it running but no one felt safe driving it.
6. **Avis roadside assistance refused to tow** because no one present was an authorized driver on the contract (the authorized driver was biking in the park). Nobody had been told that authorized drivers must be added in person. Eventually Avis accepted consent from the Edgerton employee who made the reservation.
7. Team rented a separate **Enterprise truck** to bring food and camping gear to riders in Shenandoah (extra cost); scrambled for housing in the DC area; new rental agreement for the replacement cost **$2,000 more** than the original.
8. Next day, at Avis, they were told company rentals do not need everyone authorized, yet only 4 additional authorized drivers could be added: of 8 team members only 5 can easily call roadside assistance.

## Lessons for 2027
- **Add every driver as an authorized driver at pickup**, in person; confirm roadside assistance rules.
- Inspect tires and condition at pickup; avoid hybrid Pacificas with open recalls (check recalls); consider requesting a different vehicle class.
- Know the contact at MIT Travel and Hospitality and escalate early; consider Enterprise.

## MIT rental policy (June 7, 2024, from Jessica Tam, Senior Strategic Sourcing Analyst, Travel and Hospitality, MIT VP for Finance, NE49-4100, 600 Technology Square, Cambridge MA 02139; 617-253-2756; jtam@mit.edu)
- MIT negotiated Young Renter account number **XZ10MTY**.
- Young renter fee waived for eligible renters 21-24 renting for business; renters must be 25+ for luxury cars and SUVs.
- Fee waived for ages 18-20 for business use **only in MA, NH and ME**, insurance included; 18-20 year-olds cannot rent outside those three states (book via Avis or Budget instead).
- Applies to all renters and all drivers; non-MIT renters/drivers cannot use the MIT negotiated rate.
- Amulya's question (3 drivers aged 20 or under, can they drive if a 21+ person rents?) led to this answer.
