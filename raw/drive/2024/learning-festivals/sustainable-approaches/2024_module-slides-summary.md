**"Spokes Learning Festival" deck (3D printing LF, 2024-07-08):** trivia - Tecla WASP house in Italy took ~200 hours (~8 days) and ~$1k in materials. Roadmap: what is 3D printing; how to generate paths (infill, supports); 3D-pen activity. Concrete home printing (discussion: limiting factor of scale?), glass printers, 3D printed rockets, 3D printing chocolate, peanut-butter graham cracker pie (snack). Additive vs subtractive (Hurco 5-axis), steps CAD -> STL/3MF -> slicing -> print; bonus Prusa printers have many printed parts.

**Module 1 (2024-05-29):** same 3D printing content plus deeper slicing: uniform, adaptive, conical, curved (CurviSlicer), collaborative slicing; supports; support-free printing (Rapid Liquid Printing, NIR direct ink writing); infill patterns (direction parallel/zig-zag, contour parallel, Hilbert curve, Fermat spiral). Activity 1: 3D pens tallest structure in groups of 3-4. Activity 2: choose an infill pattern, research pros/cons, 1-minute summary. Step 5 bonus: post-processing.

**Module 2 - Sustainable Materials (pptx, from Patricia Stathatou, Georgia Tech, open-source MIT MAS.863):** plastic production 500k tons/yr (1950s) -> 500M (2020) -> 1B (2050); ~1B tons CO2-eq/yr; ~1M tons waste/day; 150M tons in oceans. Commercial options: PLA (biobased, industrially compostable; recycled: Prusament, 3D Jake, Reflow), PHA (colorFabb), recycled PETG/PET/TPU, wood PLA (Filamentive 40% wood fibers/60% PLA). Activity: fact sheets for wood PLA (walnut, oak, cherry, teak). Molding/casting: glycerol/gelatin, latex, alginate (Smooth-On Alja-Safe); sustainable epoxies (Entropy, GreenPoxy, Ecopoxy). CNC: eucalypt laserply, recycled acrylic. DIY: wet lay-up biocomposites, SuperWood, lignocellulosic bioplastics, kombucha SCOBY.

**Module 3 - Intro to CAD (2024-06-21):** create Onshape account; courses Navigating Onshape, Intro to Sketching, Part Design Using Part Studios; Design Challenge (additive-only piece; lattice, ball in cube; top 3 printed); Prusa self-replicating printer bonus.

**Module 4 - Frontier of New Materials (pptx):** large-format printing (concrete homes, rockets, glass); Materiom recipes (eggshell biocomposite, oyster alginate), open-access biomaterials database and AI; examples (banana fiber, apple leather, etc.); activity: explore commons.materiom.org, create account, choose 2-3 locally producible materials.
