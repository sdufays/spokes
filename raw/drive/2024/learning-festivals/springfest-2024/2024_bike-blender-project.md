Instructions: Instructables "How to Create a Smoothie Making Human Powered Bike Blender for Less Than $25".
Materials / cost: personal blender (Hamilton Beach, Walmart) $20; old bike inner tube (free); 1x8 board $10 (Home Depot); 3x4 metal plate $15; 7 cm M4-.70 bolts + 2 coupling nuts $5; two 1.5in wooden wheels $10 (Amazon); four 2in bolts + nuts, two 1in wood screws, bike rear rack (free); M4-.70 tee nuts (2) $5; tools (wood saw, hacksaw/vise, wood glue, drill, tape measure, hammer, screwdriver, file, clamp, pliers, 2 1/4 in hole saw).
**Budget total ~$100:** $20 blender + $50 other supplies + $30 transportation of the assembled blender and large materials.
