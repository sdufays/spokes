**Krispie Koncrete:** Rice Krispies cereal; marshmallows; butter; white vinegar; calcium oxide; baking soda; distilled water; modeling clay; bending straws; plastic dish; plastic Tupperware container; plastic mixing bowls; measuring cups; trays.

**Scratch:** 14 small laptops; 2 power strips; requires internet connection.

**Physics of Sound:** magnet wire; small magnets; paper plates; paper bowls; 9V batteries; 3D-printed speaker stands; hot glue + gun; small prebuilt Bluetooth audio amplifier circuits (powered by 9V); cardstock; super glue; stickers.
Demos: vacuum chamber (vacuum pump, glass jar, marshmallows, alarm clock); Chladni plate (sand, mechanical oscillator, function generator); conservation of angular momentum (bike wheel, rotating stand); resonance (2 tuning forks, ping-pong ball on string, ring stand); Newton's cradle.

**Strawberry DNA:** strawberries; plastic bag; dish detergent; salt; water; coffee filter; plastic cups; rubbing alcohol; coffee stirrer or plastic fork.

**3D printing:** 2 3D printers; plastic filament; small plastic 3D-printed parts; finely ground eggshells; air-dry modeling clay; glucose syrup; unflavored gelatin packs; oyster shells; mold-making kit; xanthan gum powder; electric scale.
