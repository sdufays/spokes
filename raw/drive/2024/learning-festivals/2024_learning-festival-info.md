MIT Spokes Learning Festival (2024 pitch text)

Curriculum = 4 activities, typically an hour each (adaptable to larger/smaller slots). Each supports ~15 students; all can run simultaneously; typically a few class periods with students rotating between activities.

Adaptable from elementary through high school. Great success with inmates in their early 20s at the learning festival at a correctional facility in Washington, DC. Ideal audience: students in youth correctional facilities (particularly impactful), but open to any group in the age range.

Activities:
- **3D Printing** - technology behind 3D printing, types/applications, basics of CAD, design own 3D models; printed on Spokes' printer at end of lesson.
- **Physics of Sound** - how/why sounds are made; physics demos and building a (Bluetooth) speaker.
- **Krispy Koncrete** - science of concrete; mix quick-dry concrete; rice krispies treats as analogy.
- **Strawberry DNA** - extract DNA from strawberries with household items; intro genetics.
