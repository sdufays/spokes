Maya Angelou Academy at Youth Services Center, 1000 Mount Olivet Road NE, Washington, DC 20002. Office (202) 987-7007 (also how to reach you in emergencies: they page you). www.seeforever.org. Parking extremely limited; use Corcoran Street NE street parking if full.

- **Must bring:** government-issued photo ID. Bag check and screening on entry; possible extra pat-down by DYRS staff.
- **Permitted:** snacks in clear bag (max 2 sealed individual snacks); asthma inhalers/epi-pens (daily dose; other meds in locker on nonsecure side); blue/black coats with no logos.
- **Not allowed on secure side:** non-clear bags, money, aerosols, hazardous materials, nail polish/files, perfume, drugs, tobacco, lighters, electronics (MP3, smart watches, phones, cameras, bluetooth), eating utensils, open beverage containers/water bottles, outside food (without Superintendent approval), can openers, aluminum foil, beverage bottles, weapons, explicit material. Leave in car or lockers (107 and 109 reserved for MAA visitors).
- **Dress code (not allowed):** halter/tank/midriff/tube/cami/sleeveless/muscle/fishnet/strapless tops, sweatpants, gym clothes/shorts, skirts above knee, low-rise jeans, fleece joggers, latex/spandex, stretch/skinny jeans, profane/illicit clothing, spikes, open-toed shoes/flip-flops, large hoop earrings (bigger than a quarter), torn clothing showing skin.
