Author: Varsha. Camped at Big Meadows (Shenandoah NP), fast downhill out of the park, lunch at Lil Gus's in Grottoes, VA; host Francine (Warmshowers). Only 4 additional drivers could be added to the Avis rental at the counter (limit), so be strategic. Team made up a mnemonic story from street names to remember route turns (only a couple bike computers were set up). Rebecca drove food to riders.
