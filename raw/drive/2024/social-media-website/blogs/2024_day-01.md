Author: Rebecca. Day -1 (Sat): JD, Hank, Varsha drove the packed minivan Cambridge to DC (camping gear on top, 4 bikes on back); stayed with hosts Heather and Maurice. Day 0 (Sun): Rebecca, Sophia, Amulya drove 3 more bikes in a 2024 Toyota Tacoma rental (<1000 miles); JD did a 26-mile ride in Virginia; Jess and Cleo arrived from NJ and Paris. 
Day 1: learning festivals at the DC Jail (students over 18; Rebecca "krispie koncrete" - chemistry of concrete via rice krispies, concrete = 8% of global CO2; Amulya strawberry DNA) and DC Youth Services Center (Sophia/JD 3D printer demo printing a little boat; Hank/Varsha intro to Scratch). Activities built into students' school days; coordinated by Judge Faruqui. Small class sizes (mostly under 16) let teachers learn names. Evening debrief over Loren's vegan gluten-free cookies. Lesson: don't underestimate students; festival aims to spark curiosity.
