Rental had bald front tires. JD and Rebecca took a tow truck at 7am toward Dulles but ended up in Springfield VA (45 min away); an hour on the phone with Avis then an Uber to the airport. Second Chrysler Pacifica dragged something (ketchup packet found in hood) and was swapped; third was a hybrid Pacifica with 7000 miles. Avis staff do not allow inspection of the engine in the lot (they checked at a nearby mall instead). Rest of team: bikes checked and fitted in Winchester VA, groceries; Sophia cooked Mediterranean dinner. Lessons: video walk-around of the rental, check tires, expect Avis/Budget hassle (see Gear and Car Master Guide).
