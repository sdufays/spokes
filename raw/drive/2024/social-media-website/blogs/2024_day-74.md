Hosts: Tony in Benicia. Duha (Spokes 2023) drove the van on the last day. Hank's 12th flat. Bridges: Alfred Zampa, Richmond-San Rafael (bike path on upper... one of the bridge's levels), Golden Gate (many pedestrians). 14% climb on Sausalito St (max 24.9%). Friends and family greeted them; Spokes 2022 alumni Maxwell Yun and Simone Lassar met them at the SF REI for ice cream. **Trip totals: 74 days, 4,038 miles, 171,691 ft elevation, 8 learning festivals, 55 biking days, 9 states.** Packing/shipping bikes at SF REI took far longer than expected (REI let them store bikes overnight). Lesson: budget a full day for packing/shipping bikes; apartment in SF used for staging.
