Kenosha Campground to Buena Vista, CO (65.3 mi, 2064 ft). Previous day's US-285 descent near Conifer had trucks with trailers passing within inches. Option routes: Elkhorn Road dirt (Google Street View showed packed dirt but it was loose gravel - not rideable), CO-9, or US-285. Hail near Fairplay, flat tire (hand pump only 40 psi - bring floor pump). Last stretch where US-24 merges with US-285 was scary (Cleo grazed multiple times). Team debated shuttling; decided to ride slowly. Author: "I'm never biking this portion of US-285 again." Lesson: when in doubt shuttle with the van; do not trust Street View for road surface; check Adventure Cycling forums.
