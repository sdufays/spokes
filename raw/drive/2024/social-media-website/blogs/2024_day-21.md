Author: Varsha(?). Every ~8 days riders trade bikes for driving duty. Driver duties: on call for emergencies, groceries (Kroger), lunch setup, contact hosts, organize the van, fix issues (JD fixed Amulya's chain). Camera needed protection from a thunderstorm. Crossed into Illinois. Hosts Sherie and Brad (Harrisburg IL), who bikepacked across America and shared tips. Lesson: store the toolbox where the driver can reach it; keep a floor pump and tools in the van.
