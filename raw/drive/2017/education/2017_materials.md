For Static Electricity Detector:
15x:
• 3x bc547 or 2n2222 or 2n3904 transistors
• 1x 220 ohm resistor
• 1x 100K resistor
• 1x 1M resistor
• 1x LED

For 2-bit Adder

2x 74LS86 (Quad two-input XOR gate)
2x 74LS08 (Quad two-input AND gate)
1x 74LS32 (Quad two-input OR gate)
2x 4-position DIP switch
5x LEDs
1x Solderless breadboard
22 gauge wire
5v Batter (or 3 1.5 V)
