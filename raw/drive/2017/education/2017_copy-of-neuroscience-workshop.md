Psychology
1) Explain purpose behind Intro - Trust Game
- Trust game is to highlight how complicated brain must be to do simple tasks

2) Selective Attention Test
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJG698U2Mvo
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IGQmdoK_ZfY
- Play first video, ask if they notice Gorilla
- Play second video, ask if they notice change in curtain
- Explain how brains take in so much info, they must filter out and "selectively attend" to information
- Part of Selective Attention, I change shirt midway through lecture (go to bathroom) and see if people notice
- Can also do Cell Phone Experiment, where you ask them to remember the color of the bottom right icon on their phone, then ask them to if they noticed what time it was

3) Left or Right Sided Brain? (Group Activity / Worksheet)
- http://www.sciencebuddies.org/science-fair-projects/project_ideas/HumBio_p007.shtml

4) Measuring Speed of Neurons (Group Activity / Worksheet)
- https://backyardbrains.com/experiments/reactiontime
- OR Experiment I heard from friend where you sit in circle and tap feet and measure body length and stuff to find neuron speed but I can't find write-up online

5) Random Number Survey
- https://www.education.com/science-fair/article/seventeen-favotite-random-number/
- Teaches how our brain has tendencies even when we try to be random
- Tie in how social engineers can use this to crack passwords easily
- teach them to create unique passwords!
