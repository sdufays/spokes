Target Grade Levels: Middle school-High school
What prior knowledge do you expect your students to have coming in?
Middle schoolers likely will not know any physics concepts
	-Good idea to not get into details with middle schoolers
High Schooler’s maybe starting to get into E&M

Learning Objectives:
What do you want the students be able to do at the end of the lesson?
To be able to explain the basics of how these devices work, if nothing else to be fascinated and not be discouraged by these complex topics
What do you want the students to know at the end of the lesson?
Basic intuition that electricity and magnetism are linked in a way that we can use
Go into the history a little bit with industrial revolution and say some key names-michael farday, James clerk Maxwell, Heinrich Hertz
How do you want your lesson to affect students?
Excitement and wonder about E&M, ability to look up how things work
What do you want your students to tell their parents and friends when they go home?
They built a cool speaker, they have the ability to do these DIY things by themselves! All they need to do is go on the interweb and take initiative.
