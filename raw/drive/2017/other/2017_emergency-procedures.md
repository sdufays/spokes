Team Member Critical Injury
1)	At least one team member should be with injured person; they should first check to see if injured teammate is in critical condition.
2)	If in critical condition, call 911 and ask for ambulance.
3)	Conduct first aid/CPR as necessary.
4)	Contact team leader (or alternate) when possible; team leader will call MIT faculty supervisor with situation update.
5)	Team leader will send driver back to help teammate and transport to hospital if necessary.

Team Member Non-Critical Injury
1)	At least one team member should be with injured person; they should first check to see if injured teammate is in critical condition. If stable, call team leader (or alternate).
2)	Continue to provide first aid as necessary.
3)	Team leader (or alternate) will:
a)    Assess situation and determine if teammate needs to go to hospital
b)    Send vehicle back to injured teammate with support as needed.
4)	Team leader will call MIT faculty supervisor with situation update

Bicycle Malfunction
1)	Rider will stop and assess situation with teammate to determine if it is:
a)    Safe to continue riding
b)    A problem that can be fixed with repair tools on bike
c)    A problem that can be fixed with tools in car
d)    Severe and needs to be addressed by bike repair shop
2)	Rider will contact driver:
a)    With update if delayed
b)    For transportation if necessary
3)	If the malfunction is severe, plans will be made for rider to become driver or join driver in car as allowed by space in van.

Team Members Are Lost
1)    If team members realize they are lost, they should call team leader and notify of situation.
a)    Team leader will help them try to regain route by phone.
b)    If in 15 minutes team members cannot find the correct route, they will stop where they are and wait for team leader to send vehicle to get them.
c)    Team leader will communicate team members’ last known position to driver who will remain in contact (on hands-free device) with lost teammates while driving to them.
2)    If team members cannot contact team leader, they should call alternate.
3)    If team members cannot contact any teammate, they should contact MIT faculty supervisor and notify of situation.
4)    If team members cannot contact teammates or faculty supervisor, they should contact MIT Dean on Call and notify of situation.
5)    Team members should stop and wait where they are if they are lost and cannot reach any team members. The driver will be sent out at 30 minutes past the scheduled stop time and it will help the driver to find team members more quickly if they do not continue to go the wrong way when they realize they’re lost.

Sudden Weather Changes
1)    If not severe (i.e. not a thunderstorm or hurricane/tornado) take appropriate action to protect yourself (i.e. get out rain gear) and continue to next stop point.
a)    At next stop point, team will all gather together and decide what to do next:
i)      Wait
ii)    Stop for the day
iii)   Drive on in car if possible.
2)    If lightning is seen, stop immediately and take cover wherever possible.
a)    If a serious thunderstorm, wildfire or hurricane/tornado, also stop and take cover wherever possible.
b)    Do not stray from set route.
3)    Call driver and coordinate getting picked up.
4)    Driver should call team leader with situation report and together they should select a gathering point to wait for weather to subside.
5)    Driver should collect all team members and assemble team in chosen gathering point.
6)    Team will decide what to do next:
a)    Wait
b)    Stop
c)    Drive together to next rest point if safe.
