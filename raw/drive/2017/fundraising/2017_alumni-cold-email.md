Alumni Cold Email

Dear ,

My name is Catherine Looby and I am a Course 8 at MIT. I found your contact information through the MIT Alumni Directory. I am part of a team of 8 MIT and Harvard students called Spokes who will be biking from Washington D.C. to San Francisco in order to get students across the country excited about STEM! Along the trip, we stop at local schools and libraries to hold learning festivals where we show students our passion for STEM through hands-on project-based workshops. We add the extra challenge of biking across the country to show everyone that STEM and athletics can go hand-in-hand.

**Relevance

I was really hoping that the you would be able to help us reach our fundraising goal of $20,000. This money will be going towards bikes, teaching materials, travel supplies, etc. If you or anyone you know is interested in donating, please visit our CrowdFund Page. In addition, please let me know if you have any questions about Spokes/MIT in general or would like to share any fond memories about your time at MIT. I look forward to hearing from you!
Sincerely,
Catherine Looby
~Spokes 2017
