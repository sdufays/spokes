We appreciate your interest in sponsoring our Spokes 2017 team. We have developed several levels of corporate sponsorship, which are associated with various promotional benefits:

Platinum Sponsor ($10,000):
Includes all Gold Sponsor benefits
Inclusion in Spokes promotional video
Distribution of your promotional materials (stickers, pens, bags, etc…) at schools and camps that we teach at
Receive a pack of TEN Spokes t-shirts

Gold Sponsor ($5,000):
Includes all Silver Sponsor benefits
Having your logo placed on the FRONT of the t-shirt complete with company logo and slogan
Have your company mentioned as a sponsor at the start of each learning festival
Have your company logo placed on the side of our support van
Receive a pack of FIVE Spokes t-shirts

Silver Sponsor ($2,500):
Have your name and logo placement under “Sponsors” on the Spokes website with a link to your company website
Have a “shout-out” to your company posted on our blog in a location of your choosing
Have your name placed on the back of the t-shirt under “Sponsors” to proudly display your support of STEM education
Have lunch with a team member and talk about our experience on bike and teaching STEM OR have a Skype chat with a team member live from one of our learning festival or bike stops
Receive an end-of-trip update about the program and future plans for Spokes America
Receive a pack of THREE Spokes t-shirts
