Internal Revenue Service, Department of the Treasury, P.O. Box 2508, Cincinnati, OH 45201. Dated July 9, 2001, addressed to Massachusetts Institute of Technology, 77 Massachusetts Avenue E19 510, Cambridge, MA 02139. Federal Identification Number: 04-2103594.

Content: affirms that MIT is exempt from federal income tax under section 501(c)(3) (recognized October 1926) and is not a private foundation (sections 509(a)(1) and 170(b)(1)(A)(ii)). Donors may deduct contributions under section 170. Signed John E. Ricketts, Director, TE/GE Customer Account Services.

Practical use for Spokes: gifts to Spokes routed through MIT/Edgerton are tax-deductible; give companies this letter plus the MIT W-9 and EIN 04-2103594.
