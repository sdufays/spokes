Adventure Cycling Association

Hello from Spokes America!

We are a group of MIT, Harvard, and Columbia University science and engineering students, with a mission to incite a passion for learning science and engineering in students while encouraging an active lifestyle. Our science and engineering outreach program will consist of a cross country cycling trip from San Francisco to Washington D.C., following the Western Express and TransAmerica Trail. We are asking you, Adventure Cycling Association, to join our cause by sponsoring our journey.
We will be cycling in order to challenge the stereotype that students interested in STEM cannot also be athletic and active. We also hope to support cycling as a recreational activity and a healthy, fun and convenient method of travel.
Sponsors of the Spokes America 2014 trip are enabling young people across the country to better realize their own creative problem-solving potential and to get outside and hop on a bike. We believe that Spokes’ goals align both with ACA’s role in the developing American cycling routes and inspiring travel by bicycle.
 I look forward to discussing Spokes in more detail with you. Please feel free to contact me or visit our website at www.spokesamerica.org/, and you can watch some footage about Spokes and last year’s successful trip at here and here.

We are excited to embark on this journey, and we hope that ACA will join us along the way.

Sincerely,
Caleb Fujimori and the Spokes America team
