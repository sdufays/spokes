Room 35-311
77 Massachusetts Avenue
Cambridge, MA 02139

To whom it may concern,

As Director of Communications and Outreach for the MIT-Gordon Engineering Leadership Program (GEL), I have worked with Emma Benjaminson for the past two years as a mentor and supervisor. Emma joined the GEL program in September 2012 and is now a GEL Year 2 student helping to lead the program. I work with Emma on a weekly basis in our Engineering Leadership Labs, and as a mentor to the GEL Admissions team, where Emma worked as the team leader for this year’s admissions season.

Emma has grown over her two years in the GEL program. This year she has been leading small teams of GEL Year 1 students; she has taken responsibility for their development by meeting regularly with them individually to discuss their progress in the program, by organizing regular team bonding events, and by taking the initiative to run bi-weekly Movie Nights for the entire program to watch movies on leadership and to discuss their key lessons.

As part of the GEL program Emma has taken a project management class, ESD.052, where she learned the basics of risk analysis and mitigation. I have seen Emma’s emergency planning documents for Spokes and, while I do believe it is important for her to continue to refine these plans with MIT staff and to ensure she and her team receive adequate emergency training, I believe that Emma is capable of leading this trip safely and successfully.

For the past 3 months I have worked closely with Emma on the GEL admissions process, where she has demonstrated her ability to build and lead a long-term project with a successful outcome - this year we set a new record for number of applicants to the GEL program. As team leader, Emma applied creative problem-solving to navigating a number of challenges throughout the admissions process. She recruited experts and helped to lead an effort (along with the GEL Student Leader) to create a new branding campaign for the GEL program to effectively communicate its appeal to students; the campaign included postcards, posters and television displays. Early in the program, a subcommittee leader left the team due to unforeseen circumstances, and Emma stepped in to recruit a new leader and an additional team member to strengthen the team. That subcommittee continued on to organize two successful information sessions. As the recruiting process progressed, Emma took input from her teammates and concerns from the GEL staff and increased the face-to-face contact between GEL students and potential applicants by coaching her teammates to give brief pitches about the GEL program to engineering classes during the final week of the admissions season.

Outside of the GEL program, Emma has maintained a 4.8 GPA as a mechanical engineering student, with a minor in African and African diaspora studies. She has worked on multiple project teams for various classes; for example, in 2.009 Emma was one of two systems integrators for the Pink Team, which had 18 engineers and a budget of $6500 which they used to make a bicycle-pulled vegetable cart for an underserved community in Boston. Outside of class, she has served as treasurer to the Black Students’ Union for this year, and in the past served as logistics co-captain for the women’s frisbee team. She volunteers for MIT’s Habitat for Humanity chapter and participates in the MIT Outing Club’s fall circuses and winter school trips in New Hampshire.

Emma has demonstrated dedication to helping her teammates develop their leadership skills both in and out of the classroom. She has also shown a concern for promoting diversity on campus as a member of the Black Students’ Union. I know that next year, as an engineer with the Boeing commercial division, Emma will continue to pursue interests in building workplace diversity and outreach to the community. She is well-equipped to pursue those same interests as the team leader for Spokes this summer.

Please contact me with any further questions or concerns about Emma’s ability to lead the Spokes team during this summer’s trip.

Sincerely,
Bruce Mendelsohn
Director of Communications and Outreach
MIT-Gordon Engineering Leadership Program
