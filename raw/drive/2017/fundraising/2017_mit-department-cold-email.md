Subject: Spokes America 2017 Fundraising

Dear [their],

My name is [name]. I’m a [year] here at [MIT/Harvard], and I’m excited to be a member of Spokes America. This summer, I will be biking across the country with a team of eight MIT and Harvard students in an initiative to get kids excited about STEM. We will be stopping in towns along the way to lead workshops for underprivileged kids, sharing our enthusiasm for STEM.

My workshop focuses on [specific subject]. Students will [specific activity description]. I hope that this workshop will provide a new, compelling perspective on [general subject].

As [their position] in [their subject department], would you be willing to help fund this program and make our trip a reality?

STEM can often seem intimidating or boring to youth who are not given sufficient opportunities to explore. They may not even consider themselves capable of appreciating or participating in scientific fields. These kids — children who may not otherwise be exposed to engineering ideas— will be given the chance to explore science in an interesting and accessible way. Spokes hopes to inspire these students, so that they may believe in their own potential to do STEM.

Please contact us at Spokes-2017@mit.edu if you have any additional questions about who we are or what we do! Linked here is our website to learn more about the program, along with two articles about Spokes in previous years here and here. Thank you for taking the time to read this email.

Best,

[name]
