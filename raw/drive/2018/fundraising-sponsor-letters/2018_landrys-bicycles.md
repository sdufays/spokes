Letter from Delia Stephens and the Spokes America team to Landry's Bicycles: as a community-oriented Boston shop it could leverage expertise and resources to help; blog, Facebook and Instagram exposure, sponsor listing on website; links to promo videos. Outcome: Landry's quoted Specialized Diverge A1 at $720 and Trek CrossRip 1 at $900 (see Possible Shops sheet), but the team bought from Cambridge Bicycle.
