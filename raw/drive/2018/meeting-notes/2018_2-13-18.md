**Teaching:** hands-on beats lectures; half-lecture/half-hands-on; something kids can take home; aim middle/high school but got more elementary/middle; 2017 taught 11 days (about once a week); 25-30 laptops available (No. 6) so coding workshop is possible (not done in 2017); reuse supplies. Existing stuff: 1 tent, 1.5 semifunctional tents, stove, pots/pans, cooler, 10 cow eyeballs, sleeping bags, bike maintenance stuff, school supplies, a folder of info and cold-email examples.

**Division of labor:** 1-1.5 people on schools; 2 on fundraising (everyone helps); 2 on route (where to bike/stay); social media (Stanford team is better, beat them); equipment/budget person. Talk to Sandy (Edgerton) ASAP for money help.

**Money:** STEM companies: TI, MathWorks. Crowdfunding through MIT (cannot do Indiegogo); org is a 501(c)(3), tax exempt; MIT will not pay for taxes. Budget will let under-25 riders rent vehicles with no fees and a small discount (also gets all stuff home). Impulse buys/souvenirs on you, otherwise nothing out of pocket. Travel card for trip purchases; procurement card for stuff at home.

**Bikes:** Ace Wheelworks (Porter Square) and Cambridge Bicycles (commuter store); ~$1,000/bike; get fitted so you can adjust yourself; cycling shoes and bright cycling clothing; phones ok for navigation, maybe 2 cycling computers; split into two groups; bike in twos to fours.
**Safety:** always wear helmets; bright clothes even if dorky; front/back lights (strobing preferable) for rain and night; get comfortable on the bike early; know signals and road rules; close calls with big vehicles; never overreach; do tire/brake/chain checks (drop bike two inches to check for loose parts, flip upside down).

**Route:** Warmshowers.org = couchsurfing for bikers; start as early as possible; 2017 was about 60/40 Warmshowers/camping; 2017 route (third year, same route) recommended for minimal work; west-to-east vs east-to-west debated (Appalachians short but steep, Rockies long but gradual).

**Communication:** 2017 used Slack; maybe just text/email; GroupMe adopted.

**Food:** tossed a lot of food, buy less than you think; many Warmshowers hosts cook dinner/breakfast; van driver often runs food; bean salad (corn, garbanzo/salted beans, peas, tomatoes, cheese, seasoning, roll into tortilla; canned foods are fast); oatmeal; almond milk good; do not eat PB and tuna sandwiches; do not buy potatoes. Tell Warmshowers hosts about dietary restrictions and allergies.
**Packing:** pack half as much as you think you need and carry twice as much money; do not need that many clothes.
