Recurring meeting Tuesdays 9pm; want more people. **Education:** reached out to schools, waiting on 1; they want firm dates; some schools said 90 kids (1-day or 2-day LF?). **Fundraising:** draft email needs workshop titles; tell companies we show off their products; crowdfunding; register as a nonprofit. **Route:** stay with last year's route (change schools if Carson City fails); Washington to SF; June 2 to Aug 17/18; 2-3 weeks max for Warmshowers. **Bikes:** $800-$1,100; wider treadier tires; maybe cyclocross.
**Workshop ideas discussed:** Polaroid pinhole camera (film expensive, physics complicated); design/building (egg drop, bottle rocket); pinhole with real film; earth science (rocks/minerals, gem kits); intro circuits (motor with battery and magnet, magnetic train); calculus; basic coding (hard to monitor levels); logic puzzles; real-world math; biology. Consensus: design and building. **Format: 4 two-hour workshops, kids pick 3 but we provide all four**: 1 build the thing; 2 electronics/computers; 3 science.
**Before spring break:** fundraising (X companies, crowdfunding set up, bikes funded?), education (titles, descriptions, lesson plans, dates, start ordering), budget (pick bike shop), route (Warmshowers set). Misc: practice workshops on Cambridge-area kids; reach out to SWE.
