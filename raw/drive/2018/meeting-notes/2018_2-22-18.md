Meeting time: Thursdays 8-9. Dates June 2 to Aug 14. Emailed 8 schools from last year and 7 of 8 responded.
**Needs:** flyer, brochure, description for schools. **Website:** make blog into a real website; need a domain (mitspokes.com) to pass to next class, same for Instagram/Gmail. **Fundraising:** grant applications each have their own format; idea to reach out to Stanford Spokes team; Austin to Anchorage (4,000 mi) team; T-shirt sales?
**Route:** need not end in SF (could go through LA and stop at national parks); East coast harder to change so stick to TransAmerica route with secure contacts; Salt Lake City cool; Kansas can get hot.
**Schools (2017 recap):** Carson City only 11 people (saying no is fine; "nobody came"); Challenger good, less self-selecting, mind teaching level; Lab: Revolution good (makerspace, great organizers); St. Louis Homeschool good (varied backgrounds, smart but did not know exponents); aSTEAM Village responded eagerly, young kids "super crazy", tiny two-room space; Newton Public Library small, chill, well behaved, push for more attendees; Loveland rich area, maybe 15 kids, nice contacts, need better advertising; Space Center 100 high schoolers. Get more contacts, advertise proactively, add at least one new place; aim for 9 schools. Lessons: look at Spark and Splash.
**Other:** send photos to MathWorks (email Margalit "about that photo thing"); Ace Wheelworks for bikes; look into donated bikes (Specialized).
