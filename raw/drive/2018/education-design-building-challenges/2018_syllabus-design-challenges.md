**Instructors:** Bethany Cates and An Jimenez. Inspired by MIT hands-on learning: egg capsules, paper boats, bridges holding textbooks, with household materials.

**Total time:** 2 hours (5-minute intro, 5-minute close).
- Warm-up Book Balance: 8 sheets of paper, structure 6 in off the ground holding most books; 20 min (10 build, 10 test); computer paper, books.
- Egg Drop: capsule that prevents a raw egg cracking from heights; 50 min (25 build, 25 test); paper, straws, rubber bands, tape, toothpicks, raw eggs.
- DOTS Tower: tallest standing structure from DOTS and toothpicks; 40 min (30 build, 10 measure).

**Materials master list:** books, paper, toothpicks, straws, rubber bands, tape, raw eggs, DOTS, measuring tape.

**Special accommodations:** lots of tables/space; egg drop must happen outside. (Budget lists build-workshop supplies at $145.10.)
