**Mission:** Spokes America brings MIT students cycling across the country, teaching students in middle America to engage with the scientific world through Learning Festivals at schools, libraries, museums and summer camps, while defying stereotypes about scientists (athletic, community-engaged).

**Organization overview (2018):** smallest but most dedicated team to date; students from MIT and Harvard; several on fundraising, a couple on school outreach, two on route and lodging, one on social media; mentorship from past teams.

**History:** 2018 is the sixth year; since 2013, 8-10 MIT and Harvard students per year to rural towns such as Hazard, Kentucky and Newton, Kansas. All past years completed the journey.

**Grant purpose:** inspire kids to ask questions, challenge the notion that STEM students are not athletic; funds buy educational supplies, food, bikes, camping equipment.

**Safety First (reusable text):** no Spokes team member has ever been injured on the road; keep a tight schedule to avoid arriving after dark; scout locations; purchase all safety gear (helmets, reflective vests, lighting); use the van for extra safety, stop if uncomfortable riding; previous teams compiled route difficulty/condition info.
