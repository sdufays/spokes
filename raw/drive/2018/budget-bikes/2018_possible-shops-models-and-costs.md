Responses: **Wheelworks** recommended the 2018 Diverge Sport A1 (MSRP $1,150) at $1,000/bike plus 15% off gear (rider annoyed 2018 costs $250 more for the same parts). **Cambridge Bicycle** (emailed 4/9): staff visited and friendly, recommended the Renegade line and promised a full quote by 4/17 covering all gear based on the team's budget, "so much more thoughtful" than Wheelworks. Final order: Cambridge Bicycle, 8 Renegade Exile, see final quote ($7,185.53).
