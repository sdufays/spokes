DIY camera obscura. Students learn what light is, how humans process light, how cameras work; at the end take an instant-camera photo and learn the chemistry of instant photography.

Materials: cardboard box, scissors, 4 popsicle sticks, tape (share with a buddy), magnifying glass, laminated tracing paper.
Steps: 1 mark the center of each small side of the box with a ruler; 2 cut a larger hole (for your eyes) at one end and a smaller one at the other (for the magnifying glass); 3 tape the magnifying glass over the small hole; 4 build a frame for the laminated paper from popsicle sticks; 5 slide the tracing paper back and forth until an inverted image is clear. Adapted from pinholephotography.org.
