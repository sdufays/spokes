**Early group ideas:** Delia and Alex: pinhole camera (physics and chem); An and Bethany: building/design challenge; Annie and Kate: comp sci.

**Potential topics (* = involves building):** discrete math; environmental science; intro to Python; intro to CAD; product design; public speaking; *circuit building; *building challenges (rocket, catapult); logic puzzles; DNA and genetics; math and physics of music; real-world math; professional world (interviews, resumes, college admissions); *origami math; chemistry experiments; food science; *architecture (DOTS tower, bridges); 3D printing; psychology/decision making; *Rube Goldberg machines; chemistry photography; intro to finance; soapmaking; *pinhole cameras; *cardboard airplanes; *bottle/model rocket.

**Questions to settle:** hours/days per festival; instructors per lesson (1-3); supplies; age group; how to publicize; resources at the location (projectors, whiteboards); adaptable to ages; beyond school content?

**Festival organization ideas:** one day vs two; opening/closing ceremonies; 9-11, 11-1, lunch, 2-4, closing; larger groups a second day (9-11, 11-1); Google Form feedback after. 2 days for Challenger Learning Center.

**Pre-spring-break goals:** cement workshops (groups, titles, descriptions, lesson plans); reach out to schools for firm dates; practice workshops with nearby kids.
