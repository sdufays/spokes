Bolded in original = completed. One blog writer per day, rotating through the 8 riders (An, Bethany, Kate, Delia, Alex, Annie, Matt, Lisa); a separate "Email Update" writer assigned roughly every 7th day.

Day 1 = Sun May 27 (An writes blog; An also email update). Days 1-9, 11-15 and 19 were completed by the snapshot (June 14). Rotation continued through ~Day 72 (rider order repeating: Alex, Annie, Lisa, Matt, An, Bethany, Kate, Delia). Email-update writers in order: An (d1), Bethany (d8), Kate (d15), Delia (d22), Alex (d29), Annie (d36), Lisa (d43), Matt (d50), An (d57), Bethany (d64), Kate (d72).

Lesson: several days slipped (Day 10, 16-18 not marked done by mid-June); fixed rotation made it easy to see who owed a post.
