Subject pattern: Hello from Spokes. 8 students, 3,500 mile ride D.C. to San Francisco over 75 days, 9 to 10 Learning Festivals. Defines Learning Festival (8 hours, 4 workshops). Ask: "we are asking you, REI, to join our cause by sponsoring" with a grant of $10,000 or any money, equipment or gear (clothes, helmets, shoes, camping gear). Sponsorship offers brand exposure to small towns and cyclists. Sent as An Jimenez with the MIT Spokes team; links to mitspokes.wixsite.com/mitspokes and spokesamerica.com. Same body was sent on 4/6 to Camelbak, Arc'Teryx, Heartbreak Hill Running Co., Bicycle World.

Tip from a related draft ("Local News"): claims of edX/Teach for America collaboration appear in the Email to Schools and in this draft; verify before reuse.
