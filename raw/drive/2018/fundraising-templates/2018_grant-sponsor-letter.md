We ask you, the Koch Foundation, to sponsor our journey, which will be documented on Facebook, Instagram and our website. We ask for a grant of $5,000 though we would appreciate any amount of money or equipment, to acquire journey necessities, improve programming quality and spread STEM to students nationwide. Respectfully, Delia Stephens and the Spokes America team.
