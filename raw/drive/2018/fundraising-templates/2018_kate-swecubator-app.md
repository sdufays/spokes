Questions and Kate's answers (April 2018):
- Goal: bike DC to San Francisco, STEM workshops of own design at ~10 Learning Festivals; spread hands-on STEM and show engineers can be athletic and adventurous. Kate teaches a computer science workshop.
- When/where: May 27 to about Aug 8 across the country (VA, KY, KS, UT, MO, CO, NV and more).
- SWE alignment: exposing young girls to STEM, improving the image of engineers; team of 8 (6 women, 2 men) of varied majors, ethnicities, backgrounds; workshops include a mini lesson on STEM careers.
- Requested: $2,000, to cover gear purchase/rental and teaching materials (tents, bikes, power strips, tape, breadboards, etc.).
- Extra: each LF is a one-day, 7-hour event with four workshops; target ages 10-15 in rural communities; route planned day by day on a spreadsheet with mileages and elevations; need to reach the $25,000 goal.
(Application submitted 4/30 per tracker.)
