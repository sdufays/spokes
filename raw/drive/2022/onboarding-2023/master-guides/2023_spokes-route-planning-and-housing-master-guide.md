# Route Planning and Housing Master Guide (guide updated 2026; host reviews are from Spokes 2022)

## Planning the route
- For many years MIT Spokes has started in Washington DC and headed west to San Francisco. Recommend a similar route: lots of documentation done and strong relationships with learning festivals/hosts. 2026 route sheet: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1FZTiJBeVcn6Pf-vUAqcwMFPduEfY1yFDthliL9qTybc/edit
- Follows US Bike Route 76 (TransAmerican Bike Route) from DC to Colorado. Adventure Cycling USBRS maps: https://www.adventurecycling.org/routes-and-maps/us-bicycle-route-system/maps-and-route-resources/?route=virginia76
- Team detoured through national parks/cities, increasing mileage vs earlier years; decide your detours.
- Mistake to avoid: plan the route with the Warm Showers host map, Google Maps and USBRS maps all open at once. 2022 planned the route before checking for Warm Showers hosts and had to change it several times.
- Need at least 2 route/housing planners (3 is good): one contacts Warm Showers, one books campsites.

## Day-to-day navigation
- Stops every 1.5-2 hrs (~15 mi) to refill water. Gas stations have soda fountains for cold water, snacks, bathrooms. Meet the car where there are no gas stations.
- 2026: route pre-planned before summer, tweaked the night before. Read previous years' blogs for next-day notes.
- Tools: Ride With GPS (free route planning, export GPX to bike computer; charge computers every few days), Strava premium (shows paved/unpaved), Google Maps street view (shoulder size), Google Maps offline maps for reroutes.
- 2024 daily routes: Strava pages of Hank, Varsha (https://www.strava.com/athletes/69682060), Jess (https://www.strava.com/athletes/77959178). 2026 Ride with GPS collection https://ridewithgps.com/collections/9887125?privacy_code=AeJqH2wbeCqRhCKUSOWwa9z9ftOzTzU6 ; 2025 collection https://ridewithgps.com/collections/4376129

## Finding stays
2022 stays by frequency: Warm Showers, campsites, learning festivals, friends, MIT connections, churches, motels/hotels. In 2022 stayed with many of Sophia's church members. A learning festival and many stays came via an alumni connection (Sam Ford, Kentucky).
- Warm Showers: new account; say you have a support car. Start contacting in February. Be flexible/appreciative (we can sleep on the floor). Contact hosts who can take 2+ people (2026: hosts who take 4+ people, which gave surprisingly nice houses, e.g. basement guest rooms). Get host phone number and address (app is buggy); give address to route planner the night before. Confirm a week before; driver calls day-of.
- Campsites: start booking in February (national parks fill fast). Prefer showers.
- Learning festivals: ask LF hosts to find housing, esp. where no Warm Showers.
- MIT alumni clubs: emailed all clubs in states passed through; only 3 responded but worthwhile (one donated; met Sam Ford of Kentucky).
- Churches: large kitchen, many bathrooms, sometimes feed you. Call AND email every church (usually no response).

## Specific hosts (Spokes 2022)
**Virginia**
- Afton, VA: Pam Mendosa (friend of WS host Melissa), commune, off-grid, solar; 484 Seymour Lane, Afton VA; 434-327-9270; hosted 2022. Favorite.
- Abingdon, VA: Julia Wright (WS); 366 Bradley Street SW, Abingdon VA 24210; hosted 2022. Favorite: had covid but invested in team, sent cross-country cyclist pins, made cookies with Spokes logo, responds to every Instagram story.
**Kentucky** (great hospitality; many stays via Sam Ford; Sam also organized the Bowling Green KY learning festival). Coal-mining history, poor towns. DOGS notorious for chasing cyclists: a 2022 rider crashed on the first KY day while chased by a dog; carry air horns.
- Wayland, KY: Jerry and King Fultz (Jerry was mayor of a town of 400); (606) 226-2012; 46 Kentucky Rt 1086; hosted 2022. Favorite.
- Liberty, KY: Don Cravens (cousin of Tom Cravens, Hazard learning festival); 1673 E State Hwy 70, Liberty KY 42539; hosted 2022. Cooked feast and chocolate gravy, cow farm tour.
- Henderson, KY: Karen and Roy Brunner (friends of Sam Ford); cabin in Audubon State Park (4 beds, big kitchen); 904 Kelly Court, Henderson KY 42420; phone listed 27086008542 (as in doc); hosted 2022. Stocked snacks for weeks.
**Missouri**
- Rolla, MO: Kathryn Northcut (WS); 705 Lariat Ln, Rolla MO 65401; NEGATIVE: weird vibes, seemed not to like support vehicle; camped in backyard; another WS cyclist there.
**Illinois**
- Harrisburg, IL: Brad McCormick and Sherry (WS); 2235 Walnut Grove Road, Harrisburg IL; positive (sourdough BLTs, kombucha, rode with team next day).
**Kansas**
- Farlington, KS: Nathan Hughes (WS), youngest host, lake house with boats/kayaks; 620-238-3222; 16 East Lake Road; positive.
- Eureka, KS: Jefferson Baptist Church (Pastor John, wife Joann); 300 South Jefferson St; 620-583-9129; pulled pork dinner; helped in medical emergency (a member needed hospital); positive.
- Great Bend, KS: Adam Demuth and Kassie (WS); hosted 2022 and 2019; chef, lasagna; positive.
- Dodge City, KS: Lindy and Warren Durree (WS); 503 Sierra Ct, Dodge City KS 67801; 620-255-4909; hosted MIT 2022 and Stanford 2022; positive (one of them drove out to return forgotten bottles).
- Newton, KS: Janet Voth (WS); 303 W Broadway St; 316-284-4269; hosted 2022, 2019, Stanford 2022; NEGATIVE weird vibes; recommend a different host (2-day stay due to teaching in Newton).
**Colorado**
- Silverthorne, CO: Roberta Flack and Randy (WS); NEGATIVE: friends made uncomfortable racist/sexist remarks; left harsh WS review. Do not stay again.
**Utah**: many national parks. Do NOT bike into Arches from Thompson Springs (little shoulder, heavy traffic; 2022 had to shuttle). Bryce Canyon to Cedar City is a beautiful mostly-downhill ride.
**Nevada**: much desert, hundreds of miles with no services. Baker NV to Carson City = "Loneliest Road in America"; stock up in Ely; Eureka to Austin to Fallon has no services. Collect stamps in a booklet at gas stations, trade for a pin. Middlegate NV famous burger stop (look for previous Spokes messages).
**California**: stay a day at Lake Tahoe (camping nice; hard to find a Welcome to California sign near S. Lake Tahoe). Lake Tahoe to Sacramento: Hwy 50 becomes freeway with no-cyclist signs; use side roads. Many gas stations north of SF (before Sausalito) lack water/bathrooms; use grocery stores (e.g., Whole Foods).
