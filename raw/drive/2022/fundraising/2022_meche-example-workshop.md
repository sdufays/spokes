## Slide 7
Introduction to Leaf Chromatography 
We will go collect leaves and separate out the pigments in them by doing chromatography

Chromatography is separating components of a mixture by putting them through a substance in which the components move at different rates.

## Slide 9
Leaf Chromatography Instructions
Crush up your leaves in separate containers 
Transfer the leaves to a cup and pour rubbing alcohol over the leaves 
Place a strip of coffee filter, paper, or paper towel in the mixture
Don’t be afraid to experiment with type or shape of your paper! See which you like best, have fun with this!
Observe the leaf pigments travel up the paper and separate out 
Make some beautiful art!
