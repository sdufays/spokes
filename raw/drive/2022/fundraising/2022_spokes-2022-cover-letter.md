Keaten Clarno, Sophia Fang, Robert Henning, Simone Lassar,
Mariela Perez, Maxwell Yun, Franklin Zhang

Mission Statement
Spokes MIT brings STEM education to kids across the US. We offer exciting, hands-on STEM workshops to rural areas and underrepresented communities across the country to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers. As we bike from DC to San Francisco, we aspire to promote outdoor adventure, embrace spontaneity, and lead community-focused endeavors.  

Mission
Spokes MIT is a completely student-run program that is entering its 8th summer of operation after a 2-year pause due to COVID. Along the way, we will host more than 10 learning festivals at schools, libraries, and museums, encouraging children across the country to engage with the scientific world around them. The Spokes 2022 team is made up of 7 experienced enthusiastic MIT students from Materials Science, Biology, Physics, Environmental Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Computer Science. Several of us have been MIT Teaching Assistants, Educational Studies Program teachers, and CovEd mentors for middle-schoolers. We have collectively helped teach classes in math, physics, electrical engineering, chemistry, computer science and philosophy. We are also long-distance runners, varsity rowers, mountain bikers, and backpackers. The 7 of us are grateful for this opportunity to impact the lives of over 500 students across rural and low-income America this summer. We can’t wait to bike the entire 3,500 miles and inspire the next generation of STEM students!

Grant Purpose
The purpose of the grant would serve to further our dream of encouraging children to follow theirs. By biking from coast to coast and sharing our passion for science and technology, we hope to inspire the next generation of students to ask questions and improve their creative problem-solving in an encouraging, welcoming environment. By challenging the stereotype that students interested in STEM are neither athletic nor active, we hope to shape well-rounded, engaged, and creative leaders and innovators. Our dream is to empower young people across the country to better realize their own creative problem-solving potential and connect teachers to new teaching resources for science and technology. Funds will be spent on purchasing educational supplies, acquiring the necessities for our journey like food, bikes, and camping equipment. In helping us to complete our journey, the grant will enable us to spread our passion for STEM to students from all over the country.

History
2022 marks the eighth year of the Spokes America program. Since its inception in 2013, Spokes America has sent 8-10 MIT and Harvard students to small rural towns like Hazard, Kentucky, and Newton, Kansas to share their love of science, technology, engineering, and math along the way. Past years have all successfully completed their journeys across the country, and this year’s team is just as ambitious as the last. Along the way, we will continue a tradition of documenting our experience in small towns and big cities alike. Spokes did not run in 2020 and 2021 due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

Safety First
Our primary safety concern is with the logistics of biking across the country; long-distance biking is never completely safe. To mitigate safety risks, we will keep a tight schedule to avoid arriving at any locations after dark. This will enable us to scout locations thoroughly before we spend the night and ensure that we don’t have to cycle in the dark either. We will also purchase and use all the biking safety gear, including helmets, reflective vests, and lighting. Along the way, we will use the van to ensure additional safety: if we are uncomfortable with riding in particular conditions, we will stop to minimize risk. Previous teams have compiled extensive information about the route, including difficulty, locations, and conditions along the way. As a result, we believe that the MIT Spokes trip will be relatively safe considering the length and difficulty of prolonged strenuous activity.
