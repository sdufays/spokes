Bike Repair Workshop
The bike repair workshop aims to train the MIT community to be self-sufficient with their basic bike maintenance and repair, allowing them to save time and money compared to a bike shop. We’ve heard a lot of MIT students want to learn bike repair skills, so we at MIT Spokes were inspired to hold a bike repair workshop, letting people learn on their own bikes. The event would help the broader MIT community, as well as increase awareness and raise money for the MIT Spokes cross-country bike trip. We’re planning for Saturday, April 23 from 12pm - 2pm.

Our objective is to teach basic bike maintenance, including inner tube patching / replacement, tire replacement, and chain lubrication. We can cover progressively advanced topics, such as shifter tuning, brake adjustment, and cable replacement, depending on participant interest. We would do live demonstrations on our own bikes, then proceed to helping participants with their own bikes. We’d also like to raffle off a nice bike helmet (MIPS certified) to improve bike safety, as well as other small giveaways like tire lever/patch kit.

The workshop could be a 2-3 hour event at the steps of the Student Center. Our ideal event size is 20-30 participants, as there’s seven members of MIT Spokes who can run the workshop. We would like to get free food and poster around campus to encourage people to come to our event. We would have all tools, which we’d purchase, available for people to borrow to fix their own bikes. The event is free, although we will have a suggested donation amount ($20, to be determined) to raise money for our cross-country bike trip.

Bike Safety Workshop
Alongside the bike repair workshop, we will also be holding a smaller but just as important bike safety workshop. A lot of MIT students commute by bike, but biking in the city can be dangerous. Knowing some basics about bike safety can go a long way to keeping our community safe. As part of the safety workshop, we will be showcasing bicycle safety gear such as helmets, lights, bells, mirrors, reflective vests, etc.

This will be a free event that is happening alongside the bike repair workshop on the steps of the student center with a tentative date of Saturday, April 23 from 12pm - 2pm. We are expecting people to stay here 5 minutes at a time and can accommodate up to 10 people at once. Some topics we will likely cover are how to choose and size a helmet, visibility, locking bikes, safety tips while riding in the city, and some general biking tips specific to the MIT area.
