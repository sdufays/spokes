## Slide 7
The Three Essentials: #3 Lights

Front Light is White
Rear Light is Red
Massachusetts law requires white light 30 mins after sunset and 30 minutes before sunrise
Also requires rear reflectors or lights

## Slide 13
Biking Laws
Bike = vehicle
Stop at stop lights and stop signs
Yield to pedestrians on roads and paths
Ride on right side of the road, dont go wrong way down one-ways
Sidewalk Biking Banned in Parts of the Following Areas
Central Square Business District 
Harvard Square Business District 
Huron Village
Inman Square 
Massachusetts Ave - Russell Street - Wendell Street 
## Slide 14
Multi-Modal Biking
Most MBTA buses have bike racks (but only 2 spaces)
Bikes are allowed on subways and commuter rail except the green line. Bikes not allowed during commuter hours (7-10 am and 4-7 pm)
