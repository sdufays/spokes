Maxwell Yun
Statement:
I really enjoy sharing my passion for STEM and electrical engineering. I’ve been lucky enough to attend MIT and be surrounded by like-minded people, but lots of students aren’t aware of the possibilities and excitement of the STEM field. Spokes is the perfect opportunity for me to combine my passions for biking and STEM, as we adventure across the country on bike and inspire students to pursue STEM.
Qualifications:
I’m the mountain biking chair of the MIT Outing Club – I’m responsible for maintaining our fleet of ten bikes and planning club bike trips. I’ve also led multi-day backpacking trips before, and I’m Wilderness First Aid certified. In the classroom, I’m a fourth-year MIT student studying electrical engineering. I’m taking CMS.587 (Evaluating Education) this semester, and enjoy teaching informally to friends. Ask me about any topic I’m enthusiastic about, and I’d be more than happy to explain.

Mariela
Statement:
I frequently find myself searching for new ways to explore the outdoors and to share my knowledge of STEM with young people. At MIT, I do this by rowing with my team every morning and admiring the sunrise over the Charles River behind the Boston skyline. I am thrilled by the opportunity to experience a new sunrise every day this summer as I bike across America with the Spokes team. More importantly, I am eager for the chance to serve as a role model and mentor for young students who, like me at that age, might not see themselves as the kind of person who can go to MIT or succeed in STEM. My hope is that our team’s interaction with these underrepresented groups of kids will spark in them a desire for the pursuit of knowledge and adventure. 

Qualifications:
Global Teaching Labs: I prepared content for and taught physics, circuits, computer science, programming, biology, and time management for 50 8th-11th grade students in Russia both independently and with a team of 1-2 others. 
Experimental Study Group Physics/Calculus TA: I held office hours to provide clarity and answer student questions on topics in electricity and magnetism and single/multivariable calculus.
Gordon Engineering Leadership Program: Within a challenging and supportive leadership development environment, I engage in leadership practice opportunities with peers, staff, and leaders in industry in order to become a highly effective leader of engineering teams.
Rowing team: I have rowed on the Varsity Women’s Openweight rowing team at MIT for 3.5 years now so I have experience in engaging in extended durations of physical activity as well as doing so in a cooperative team environment.

Keaten
Statement:
Two of my favorite things include traveling (which I spent last summer doing in my Dodge Camper Van with my dog!) and helping underrepresented students in my community fight adversity. As a low-income student myself and a leader of the FLI@MIT (First-Generation and/or Low Income) organization at MIT, I make it my task daily to attempt to create a level playing field for all students to experience. Because Spokes encapsulates both of these hobbies of mine – by means of BIKING across the US and spreading STEM education to rural, low-income, and underrepresented students, it feels like the perfect project for me. I look forward to extending my passions with Spokes and furthering my goals as a person, adventurer, and low-income student. 
Qualifications:
Physics TA for ESG: I have regularly helped MIT students in my ESG physics courses with homework and digestion of material for two years. FLI@MIT/Questbridge: I have held leadership positions in this organization for two years and am now the Co-Executive Director; I help erase the gap in experience between low-income students and the rest of MIT’s student body. SEDS: I teach and extend a variety of Space Knowledge through this club by being an executive member – something which I will continue doing in Spokes.
