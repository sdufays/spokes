Costco

Summarize the mission/purpose of the organization:

MIT Spokes will bike across the US in Summer 2022, hosting educational STEM workshops along the way for middle school students. In its seventh year running, Spokes is a group of eight MIT students eager to share our enthusiasm for science, technology, engineering, and math (STEM). Over 3000 miles and 70 days, we will stop at various schools across the US and lead hands-on, interactive workshops to get kids excited about STEM -- in particular, electronics, computer science, chemistry, and physics. We hope to inspire the next generation of students to pursue STEM, as well as encourage them to stay athletic and active from coast to coast.

To make this trip possible, we would greatly appreciate Costco's support, and would be happy to provide advertising opportunities on our trip.

Brief summary of the organization's primary programs? (1-2 Paragraphs)
The Spokes team will be biking from Washington D.C to San Francisco to engage with students in rural communities this summer. We will stop at schools, libraries, and camps as we travel throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions in STEM. Students range from elementary to middle-school students, and workshops have included introductory electronics, software, and chemistry, among other topics. We hope to inspire students to pursue STEM and higher education, as well as encourage them to stay athletic and active from coast to coast.

Briefly describe how the requested funding will be used? (1 -2 Paragraphs)
Funding would be used to purchase educational supplies for our workshops, bikes and bike supplies for our ride, and food and supplies for our trip.

How will the organization determine it has accomplished the intended purpose of the program/project/event (other than satisfaction surveys)?

Our program intends to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers who may otherwise have never considered exploring these fields. We consider ourselves successful if we’ve inspired students to further pursue STEM, and maximized our engagement with people along our trip. Spokes has been running for seven years, but we hope we can continue this program well into the future.
