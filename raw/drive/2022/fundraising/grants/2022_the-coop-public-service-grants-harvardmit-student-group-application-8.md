MIT Spokes is an 8-year old completely student-run
group. Biking across America, we seek to inspire
students in rural communities through our STEM
workshops. Our exploratory hands-on workshops
spring from our own MIT experiences. Sample
activities include making solar cells from berry juice,
designing board games, playing with the mathematics
of music, and using artificial intelligence to make art!
MIT Spokes has also powerfully impacted the
dedicated students who put together the whole trip.
Through perseverance and leadership, the students
become a cohesive team while forming long-lasting
friendships with communities they meet. As Spokes
often returns to the same communities, educators
across the country are excited to have us, often
promising large numbers of attendees. Furthermore, 4
of the MIT students are seniors who lost several in-
person opportunities to give back to the community
due to COVID, so this project of sharing STEM with
kids is incredibly important.
