Letter of intent

Describe the project(s) for which you seek funding *

The Spokes team will be biking from Washington D.C to San Francisco to engage with students in rural communities this summer. We will stop at schools, libraries, and camps as we travel throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions in STEM. Students range from elementary to middle-school students, and workshops have included introductory electronics, software, and chemistry, among other topics. We hope to inspire students to pursue STEM and higher education, as well as encourage them to stay athletic and active from coast to coast.

State the organization's mission *
MIT Spokes will host educational STEM workshops for middle school students, while biking across the US in Summer 2022. In its eighth year running, Spokes is a group of eight MIT students eager to share our enthusiasm for science, technology, engineering, and math (STEM). Over 3000 miles and 70 days, we will stop at various schools across the US and lead hands-on, interactive workshops to get kids excited about STEM -- in particular, electronics, computer science, chemistry, and physics. We hope to inspire the next generation of students to pursue STEM, as well as encourage them to stay athletic and active from coast to coast.

Project Name: MIT Spokes
Requested Amt: $3000
Area of focus: Education
Grant will cover: 1/2022 - 1/2023

Provide a summary of project or grant request *
Funding from this grant would cover educational supplies and travel expenses from bicycling cross-country. We will gladly recognize the New Earth Foundation in our media/promotional material

Describe the innovative, pioneering, ingenious aspects. *

MIT Spokes not only teaches STEM to students, but also encourages them to stay fit and physically active by bicycling across the country. The eight MIT students in our group have a wide array of expertise, from STEM to teaching to outdoor experience. We aim to be role models representing MIT when meeting people across the country -- in the past, many people have expressed surprise and gratitude that MIT students would visit them, and some have written testimonials for our group.

How are those served involved in the planning and/or implementation? *

MIT Spokes reaches out to local schools and community centers to schedule Learning Festivals and plan our route. We primarily target underserved, rural areas for our Learning Festivals. While coordinating, we also discuss particular topics of interest and shape our curriculum based on the target audience. We also solicit feedback and aim to improve with each Learning Festival.

Is your project limited to a specific population? Explain. *

Our project attempts to reach as many students as possible along our trip, but we can only host a limited number of Learning Festivals at limited locations. We try to design our route to favor hosting Learning Festivals in underserved areas, where STEM may not be as widespread.

What are the practical steps and the goal? *

We will need to design a route, coordinate with local schools along the route, plan a curriculum, and plan bicycling logistics, all while training for the bicycling itself. We not only aim to spread the word about STEM, but also encourage kids to exercise and stay fit. We hope we can successfully and safely bicycle across the country, while running well-planned workshops along the way. 

Workshops include electronics, chemistry, and 3D-printing, among others, and are mainly targeted at a middle school level. We aim to produce enough content to cover a wide range of fields, ages, and background levels.

By what means do you plan to replicate your project? *

We aim to pass down our knowledge to next year's Spokes group. Spokes has been running for eight years continuously since 2012, excluding 2020 and 2021. We hope this program continues into the future.
MIT Spokes has helped inspire the Stanford Spokes team to bike across the country with a similar mission. We would love to help start other Spokes clubs at other universities.
