What is brand ambassadorship to you?
We believe brand ambassadors should showcase a company’s product and embody a company’s values. MIT Spokes will push ourselves and our gear to the limits as we bike 3000 mi across the US in Summer 2022, requiring durability and endurance just like Darn Tough’s socks. We will also host educational STEM (science, tech, eng., math) workshops along the way to encourage middle-school students to pursue STEM and college, leveraging our MIT education to make a positive impact along our route. We’d be excited to do all that, while wearing Darn Tough socks.

Why you would be a good fit for Darn Tough? How could you help tell the Darn Tough story?
Durable socks are especially important for MIT Spokes’ cross-country bicycle trip as we bike 50-100 miles per day, for 75 days. When we’re not riding, MIT Spokes also strives to make a positive impact through our 10 STEM workshops along the way, reaching between 30-100 students each workshop. We’d be proud to showcase Darn Tough as the official sock of MIT Spokes by wearing these hardworking socks as we teach and ride during our trip. We will happily recognize Darn Tough as a corporate sponsor on our website/media/apparel.
