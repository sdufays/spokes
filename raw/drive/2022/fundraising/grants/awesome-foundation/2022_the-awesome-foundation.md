* (required) Tell us about your awesome project!
MIT Spokes is an 8-year old completely student-run group. Biking across the 
United States, we seek to inspire students in rural communities through our 
STEM-based workshops. Our exploratory mens-et-manus-inspired workshops 
spring from our own experiences at MIT. Sample workshops at our learning 
festivals include making solar cells from berry juice, puzzling through the great 
paradoxes of philosophy, building circuits, designing board games, playing with 
the mathematics of music, and using artificial intelligence to make art! The MIT 
Spokes project also powerfully impacts the dedicated students who collectively 
put together the trip, workshops, and learning festivals. Through perseverance 
and leadership, the students become a cohesive team while forming long-
lasting friendships with communities they meet. Because MIT Spokes often 
returns to the same rural and underrepresented communities, educators across 
the country are excited to have us, often promising large numbers of attendees. 
The MIT Spokes project ultimately hopes to connect to students across the US in 
an adventurous, educational, and exciting nature.
