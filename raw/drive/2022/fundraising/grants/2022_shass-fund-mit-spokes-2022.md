A paragraph describing the project: 
MIT Spokes is an 8-year old completely student-run group. Biking across the United States, we seek to inspire students in rural communities through our Arts, Philosophy, and STEM-based workshops. Our exploratory mens-et-manus-inspired workshops spring from our own experiences at MIT. Sample workshops at our learning festivals include making solar cells from berry juice, puzzling through the great paradoxes of philosophy, building circuits, designing board games, playing with the mathematics of music, and using artificial intelligence to make art! The MIT Spokes project also powerfully impacts the dedicated students who collectively put together the trip, workshops, and learning festivals. Through perseverance and leadership, the students become a cohesive team while forming long-lasting friendships with communities they meet. Because MIT Spokes often returns to the same rural and underrepresented communities, educators across the country are excited to have us, often promising large numbers of attendees. The MIT Spokes project ultimately hopes to connect to students across the US in an adventurous, educational, and exciting nature. 

A paragraph describing how the funds would be used: 
The funds requested by MIT Spokes would be used to purchase supplies for our Arts, Philosophy, and STEM-based workshops so that we can put on the best possible activities at our learning festivals. Primarily, we would purchase arts supplies for crafting and making; different softwares for music technology, AI, and coding; and various teaching supplies – such as pencils, notebooks, and coloring materials – for delivering our Arts, Philosophy, and STEM-based workshops. MIT Spokes would use the SHASS Funds to enhance our workshops that serve rural and underrepresented communities of students across the United States.
