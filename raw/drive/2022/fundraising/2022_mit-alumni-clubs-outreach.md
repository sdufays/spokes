Dear [insert name],

My name is Sophia Fang, and I am a current senior in Course 3 and 7 at MIT. I saw your name as [insert position] at the MIT Club of [insert location] and I found your contact info through the MIT Alumni Infinite Connection. I’m reaching out to ask for your help with a cool project I’m doing in [insert location]!

This summer I will be biking across America with the MIT Spokes team to teach STEM workshops in rural communities in Virginia, Kentucky, Missouri, Kansas, Colorado, Utah, Nevada and the San Francisco area. We will start in Washington D.C. late May and end in San Francisco mid-August! We are excited for this unique opportunity, but we need support for this trip to work. We are aiming to raise $32,000 and we are half-way there!

Your support for our trip would help us buy bike gear, educational equipment for our workshops, food, gas, and camping fees. Your support will carry us over 3,500 miles from Washington DC to San Francisco. But mostly, your support will help us inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers. Every little bit helps.

This trip is an extraordinary feat, and it wouldn’t be possible without your help. 

Donation page: giving.mit.edu/search/node/spokes
