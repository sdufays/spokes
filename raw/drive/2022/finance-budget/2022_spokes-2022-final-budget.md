# Spokes 2022 Final Budget (complete, from local zip)

Key takeaways for 2027: gear total about $34.4k for 7 riders (bikes $9.8k = 7 Giant Revolt 2 at $1,400); car rental was budgeted $6,103 (vs ~$3,500 in past years; actual estimate on the receipt form was $8,634 plus $1,249 for four new tires after a flat); gas budgeted $1,800 (assumed 20 mpg, 8k miles, $4.50/gal); food about $70/day x 75 days = $5,250; Education Programming $2,791.39 (linked from the Educational Supply tab). Notes in the sheet record what to re-buy or avoid (front lights dim with bad battery, frame pumps broke, mirrors good, tubeless tires meant only about 10 flats in 80 days).
