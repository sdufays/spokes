Bike maintenance schedule

Every day:
Feel tire pressure + pump up tires they feel low

Every week:
Degrease + clean + lube chain
Tune shifters if poor shifting

Near Denver / halfway point:
Top off tubeless tire sealant, if still running tubeless
Replace chain (NOTE: not needed for us)
Replace brake pads (should be >1mm thick) (NOTE: not needed)
