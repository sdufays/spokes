## Sheet: bikes
Note: There was a bicycle shortage when we were purchasing our bikes (Feb 2022), and prices may have risen by > $300 over normal.
Landry's said that the Giant Revolt 2 was the only bike they had plenty of in stock, so we went with the Revolt 2
Great bike (after the trip), but try getting something closer to the $1000 mark
Landry's Bike Shop has been very helpful with our trip. The store workers are pretty nice.
Max's required features: drop handlebars, disc brakes, >=32C tires (wider than 32mm), at least 2x8 shifting
