Reaching Out Email to Past Locations

Good afternoon [ ],

My name is Franklin Zhang, and I am a student at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in Cambridge, MA. I am part of a program called Spokes which sends a team of 7 MIT students on a 80-day biking trip across the United States as part of an effort to rethink STEM education. As we go, we will stop at schools, libraries, and camps throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions in STEM. 

A couple summers ago, our Spokes 2019 team stopped at [school name] around [date] to hold a Learning Festival, and our team would love to add this school to our route again! We predict that we’ll be in the area around [date]. Let us know if you would be interested in having us this upcoming summer. We can provide more information about our curriculum and the specific dates as we approach the summer. And of course, please don’t hesitate to reach out to me if you have any questions!

In the meantime, feel free to check out our website at mitspokes.com.

Best,
Franklin, Simone, Keaten, Maxwell, Sophia, Robert, and Mariela

Following Up with Learning Festivals

Hello {Learning Festival Name},

Thanks for getting back to us. It’s great to have {Learning Festival Name} as part of our journey!

I just wanted to give a little more detail of what our plan looks like and we also have some questions on our end.

Workshops
We are planning to run four workshops this year. We are really excited about our workshop ideas and just wanted to share some of the topics.

3D printing
chemistry workshop with solar panels
circuits and electronics
Leaf chromatography

Schedule
Our learning festival is a one day event and we are aiming to run 3 sessions. During each session, 3 of the 4 workshops will be run simultaneously. We are proposing a schedule that looks something like this. 
9 - 10:30 Session 1
11 - 12:30 Session 2
12:30 - 1:30 Lunch
1:30 - 3:00 Session 3
We have finalized the dates and we are planning to arrive []. If that changes, we will let you know as soon as possible.

The Space
We just wanted to double check how much space we will have at the venue. We are wondering if we would be able to run 3 workshops at the same time. If not, we can run 2 workshops instead.

Students
Our target audience for the workshop is middle school students and a total of 45 students is a good number for us. We are flexible with the number of students and the age level. Would you please let us know how many students we should expect and the demographics?

Accommodations
We are arriving the day before the learning festival and we are looking for a place to stay. There are 7 of us and we have a car as well. Would you be able to help us with accommodation? Previous years we had an amazing stay with {Name of Stay}. We were wondering if that is also available this year?

Publicity and Media
Would you be able to reach out to students in the area and publicize this learning festival for us? We are hoping to reach out to more students who don’t usually have access to STEM education or come from underrepresented communities. We have included our logo in this email, so feel free to use that if you would like. 
We also attached a photoshop file. Feel free to use that for making fliers for publicity.
More more information about our group, please visit mitspokes.com  

COVID
We understand that covid has been making planning difficult and plans can change at the last minute, so we will try to be as flexible as possible. During the trip we will be monitoring our team for covid symptoms and are bringing with us thermometers, antigen tests (for testing symptomatic members), and symptom management kits. During the learning festival we will plan to wear masks indoors when we are not actively eating or drinking. Let us know if there are other covid protocols we should follow while we are at the learning festival.

Food
During our learning festival, we have a scheduled lunch break. Many times in the past, our hosts have generously provided lunch to our team and the students. There have also been times when our team drove into town for our lunch break. We were wondering what the plans for lunch would look like and if {Learning Festival Name} would be open to providing lunch to the students and to our team.

We are looking forward to meeting everyone at the {Learning Festival Name} this summer!
Please reach out to us if you have any questions.

Thanks,
Franklin, Simone, Keaten, Maxwell, Sophia, Robert, and Mariela
