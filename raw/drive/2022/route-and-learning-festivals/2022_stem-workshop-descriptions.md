Workshop Descriptions 

1. 3-D Printing: introduction to 3-D printing. How does it work? What can 3-D printing be used for? Students will then have the opportunity to design and print an object of their choice. We will bring a 3-D printer and 3-D printed objects for this workshop. 
 

2. Leaf Chromatography: Students will learn about the pigments present in leaves that perform photosynthesis. Then through simple techniques they will learn about chromatography (the separation of compounds by sizes). We will create artwork with the paper used to perform chromatography! We will also explore the pigments present in different art supplies like water colors, markers, and pens!
 
3. Solar Cell: Students will build their own solar cells in this workshop (aka a homemade solar panel)! We will learn about what renewable energy is, why it's important, and then each student will learn about the components in solar cells. We will build solar cells together using household ingredients like raspberries and graphite pencils. 
 

4. Philosophy Paradox: In this workshop we will learn about a few famous paradoxes in philosophy and then see them demonstrated in person. These mysteries have been puzzling humans for years! Learn about why, and how we can explain them in very simple ways!

 
5. Circuits: Students will be able to build their own circuit to turn electricity into light! We will learn about how electricity works, different components of a circuit and the fundamentals of laws of electricity.
