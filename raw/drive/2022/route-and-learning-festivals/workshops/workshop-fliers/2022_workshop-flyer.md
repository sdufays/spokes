Spokes Workshops 2022
3D Printing
In this workshop, students learn about real-world applications of 3d-printing, as well as the various 3d-printing technologies that make them possible. Students will have hands-on exposure to cutting-edge, modern 3d-printed parts, as well as watch a 3d-printer print a part in real time.

Final product: Students can expect to have their very own 3d-printed part by the end of this workshop. 

Featured here are some of the 3d printed parts we will be bringing in! From left to right we have a 3d printed wrench (made from metal!), a 3d printed metal bracelet, and a printed bike seat!

Solar Cell
Have you ever wanted to make your own electricity? Using the power of plants? Well now you can! In this workshop, students will build their own solar cells (aka a homemade solar panel), using raspberry and blackberry juice! We will learn about how the berry juice is actually able to interact with light to create electricity (spoiler alert: it is very cool). Featured here are pictures of Sophia and Simone testing out our homemade berry solar cells. 

Final product: Students will actually be able to build a homemade solar cell! 

Circuits
Electricity is everywhere! We use it to power everything, including our phones, laptops, television and lights. But have you ever wondered how electricity works? Do you learn how to harness the power of electricity? Students will be able to build their own circuit to turn electricity into light! We will learn about how electricity works, different components of a circuit and the fundamentals of laws of electricity.
  
Final product: Students will build circuits on a breadboard using various electronic components!
Color and Chromatography

What is color? How does it get there? We will explore some deceivingly complex questions in this workshop through simple experiments! Learn about the structural color that makes peacocks so bright, or the pigments present in leaves that are hidden from sight. All your questions about pigments, dyes, paints, and color explored in this colorful workshop!

Final product: Students will create a chromatography and color booklet with the results of their experiments!
Physics of Music
From workout playlists to love songs, music is an experience that shapes our emotions. It surrounds us in everyday life, and yet rarely are we shown the underlying decisions that make it so emotionally powerful. 

In this workshop, learn about music through melody, harmony, timbre, and rhythm with a focus on the physics that underlies these fundamentals.

Final product: Students learn how to make some music with simple instruments.
