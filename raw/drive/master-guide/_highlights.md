# Highlights: The Spokes Master Guide

- Start fundraising in November and treat it as the top to-do every month; crowdfunding MUST go through MIT (not GoFundMe etc.). (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- J-WEL Education Innovation Grant: apply in March, hear back end of April; this is where Spokes 2024 got most of its money. (2024/2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- December: contact departments, labs and centers for sponsorship; they often have leftover funding then. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Other fundraising hooks: MIT 24 Challenge crowdfunding (March), Midnight Marathon bingo-card-with-dares (April), bike raffle, bake sale, Edgerton Center Showcase (April). (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Edgerton admin contacts: Marvelin Higginbottom, Sandra J Lipnoski, Chris Mayer (mayerc@mit.edu); Sonny Oram is Edgerton Communications Officer for social media. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Get 2 pro cards and 2 travel cards in January; the 4 card holders effectively are the finance team; keep tax-exemption forms current and all receipts. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Strongly recommended: matching gravel tubeless bikes for the whole team; start bike purchasing end of February. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Bike fits with Brendan Connor (CycleLifePT) who comes on campus for fits + PT: "it will save your life". (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Decide 1-way vs 2-way car rental in January; start rental and driver background checks in February; get ALL drivers listed on the rental in person at the agency in the first week of the trip. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Dinner: Spokes 2026 cooked zero meals; one person called places every night asking for free food, "extremely successful". Hosts usually provide breakfast. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Lunch: 2024 ate bagel sandwiches ~74 days in a row (French bread from Walmart late in trip); 2022 ate sardines/tortillas, rotisserie chicken; 2026 spammed the snack "Venn diagram" everyone liked. (2022/2024/2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Snacks/electrolytes: Costco dates (carried 2022), Clif/Lara bars, Nature's Bakery brownie bars (2024 favorite); tub of Gatorade powder is cheapest electrolytes. (2022/2024, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Dogs: chased a lot in Kentucky; carry an air horn (and mace) in an easy-to-reach spot. (2022, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Packing: max 3 biking outfits + 2 non-biking; carry-on suitcase beats backpacking bag; laundry daily/every other day; Rockies can drop to the 50s. Padded shorts and jersey with pockets are essential. (2022/2024, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Housing: alumni, Warmshowers hosts, churches; start housing for first states in April, finalize ALL housing by May; write each host a thank-you card. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Meetings: have a moderator and notetaker; weekly meetings from February; hold regular team check-ins during the trip. Consider a Documentation role (2026 lacked one). (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Get tetanus shot and photo release forms from Edgerton in May; finalize LFs early (April) so supplies can be bought. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Blog: rotate daily writer across drivers/passengers/bikers; a Blog Lead nudges people. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
- Five linked sub-guides (Route/Housing, Gear/Car, LF/Workshops, Media/Comms, Fundraising/Budget) hold the detailed content; fetch them separately. (2026, source: The Spokes Master Guide)
