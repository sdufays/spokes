# Highlights (Master Guide sub-guides)

- J-WEL Education Grant gave $45,330 of Spokes 2024's money; applications open in February, decisions only come in April, so keep fundraising elsewhere meanwhile. (2024, source: Spokes Fundraising and Budget Master Guide)
- 2024 raised $63,764 against a $40,330 goal (158%), left ~$7,000 for 2025; the 2026 tracker stood at $37,115 of a $50,000 goal (74%) with crowdfunding $21,115 the largest single item. (2024/2026, source: Spokes 2024 / 2026 Fundraising)
- 2026 department gifts: Edgerton $5,000, School of Engineering $2,000, Nano $2,000, Music & Theater Arts $500, Aero Astro $500, Course 4 $150; plus ELO grant $5,000 (applied Sept 2025 for 2026). (2026, source: Spokes 2026 Fundraising)
- Fundraise early: by spring much corporate excess budget (TI, Microsoft, Dell) is gone; December is when departments have leftover funds, and department asks need "bump" follow-ups (2024 sent them Dec 8-21). (2024/2026, source: Fundraising Master Guide; Spokes 2024 Fundraising)
- Past sponsors/grants that said yes: Lightforce $5,000 (2022; pamphlet credited), St. Anthony Educational Foundation $4,000 (2022), TBP $2,000, Baker Foundation ~$2,000 (needs a bike repair/safety workshop), MIT Coop $1,500, MathWorks $1,000 (2022, 2024; $1,500 in 2019), Jane Street employees $1,200 (2024), ESG $7,500 (2022), Costco $500 gift card (2022). (2022-2024, source: 2022 and 2024 Fundraising trackers)
- Bake sale + bike tune-up raffle made about $2,000 (earlier years); 2026 bake sale/raffle $400; Midnight Marathon bingo-dare fundraiser raised $701.50 in 2024. (2024/2026, source: Fundraising Master Guide; 2024 Fundraising)
- Corporate donors must register with MIT before donating; talk to Sandi at Edgerton as soon as a company says yes. (2022, source: Fundraising Master Guide)
- 2026 budget: $41,290 budgeted, $35,164 spent, $6,126 left. Bikes $9,500 (7 Trek Checkpoints), one-way car ~$5,000-5,900, food $80/person-day, bike repair/marketing/first-aid ran over budget. (2026, source: Expenses + Budget)
- Money rules: travel card = on-trip food/gas, upload every receipt >$75 and all food receipts during the trip (Concur report is painful); procard = gear/LF supplies, send receipts to Marvelin within 2 weeks; health & beauty (even sunscreen), food and gas can't go on the procard; missing receipt >$75 = affidavit + "Non-Recoverable". (2026, source: Fundraising and Budget Master Guide)
- Route: start DC, follow US Bike Route 76 / TransAm; check Warm Showers host availability BEFORE locking the route; two people on route (one WS, one campsites); start contacting Warm Showers hosts (4+ capacity worked best) and booking campsites in February. (2026, source: Route Planning and Housing Master Guide)
- Housing mix (2022): Warm Showers, campsites, LFs, friends, MIT alumni, churches, motels; call AND email churches; emailing all MIT alumni clubs got only 3 replies but yielded a donation and Sam Ford (KY). Reliable repeat hosts: Jen Strackbein (Linden VA), Pam Mendosa (Afton VA), Dirk van Assendelft (Lexington VA). (2022-2026, source: Route guide; 2026 Route)
- Hazards: Kentucky dogs (a rider crashed 2022; bring air horns), Highway 50 into Sacramento becomes a freeway, don't bike into Arches from Thompson Springs, Nevada's Loneliest Road has no services for hundreds of miles, no cell signal in Shenandoah. (2022/2025, source: Route guide; 2025 Route)
- Bikes: matching tubeless gravel bikes (Trek Checkpoint ALR3 gen3, 1x10, 35 mm+ tires); Trek Cambridge gives 20% off; bike fits with Brendan Connor/CycleLifePT; carry metal water bottles (plastic doesn't stay cool). (2026, source: Gear and Car Master Guide)
- Rental car: Avis/Budget via MIT is the only option covering drivers under 21 (18-20 only in MA/NH/ME for Enterprise); get background checks via Edgerton early; **add 4 drivers at an Avis desk or roadside assistance will be refused** (2024 team got stranded in Shenandoah); photograph the car at pickup and check recalls. (2024/2026, source: Gear and Car Master Guide; Spokes 2024 Car Troubles)
- One-way rental costs a few thousand dollars more plus a 2-day expensive gear/bike shipping job back to Boston; 2026 paid ~$5k one-way plus a ~$590 cargo van to DC. (2023-2026, source: Gear and Car Master Guide; Expenses + Budget)
- Learning festivals: repeat hosts with strong ties are Challenger Center (Hazard KY, Tom Cravens), AccelerateKY (Bowling Green, Sam Ford), DC Youth Services Center (Judge Faruqui, day before the ride), Newton Public Library, Tazewell UMC; 2026 held 12+ LFs with 30-115 students each. (2018-2026, source: LF Master Guide; 2026 Route)
- LF process lessons: track every LF in a status spreadsheet, call hosts who are slow on email, send host a registration graphic + photo release + schedule + food/housing asks, aim for 4 parallel workshops x 4 periods, finish workshop design before spring break (2019 prepped too late), and publicize (Lamar got 4 students; Fallon on a Sunday ~20). (2019/2022/2026, source: LF Master Guide)
- Media: WordPress site, preserve the whole past mitspokes.com; Instagram: team intros, collabs with mitedgerton, fundraising progress, weekly summer recap, one post per LF and per sponsoring restaurant; Jakroo jerseys (one-time license since not an MIT vendor), 4imprint T-shirts, handwritten thank-you cards for every host; pamphlet printed at CopyTech ~$5 each. (2022/2026, source: Media and Communications Master Guide)
- Hand off WebMoira lists each year (spokes-20xx -> spokes-current) so ownership transfers to the new team. (2026, source: Media and Communications Master Guide)
