# Spokes 2024 Car Troubles (email thread, June 2024)

## Rebecca Lizarde to Jessica Tam (MIT VPF Travel), cc spokes-2024, Mon 6/10/2024
(Rental agreement no. 757754911; attachment: original rental PDF + photos.)
- Team (housed under the Edgerton Center) picked up a Chrysler Pacifica minivan at **Logan airport Fri 5/31**. Tuesday night they noticed the **front tires were completely bald**. Avis agreed to send a tow but ETA was past 11:30pm, so they cancelled and called again at 7am.
- The tow driver dropped two team members at Springfield VA (45 min from Dulles) instead of Dulles; Avis initially refused an Uber voucher (granted after arguing). First replacement car at Dulles had a plastic wheel cover scraping the floor and had to be swapped; final replacement: hybrid 2024 Pacifica (<7,000 miles).
- Avis refunded only 2 days of rental (policy is usually 1), though the team drove the unsafe car for 5.
- **Thu 6/5 in Shenandoah NP:** the hybrid Pacifica showed "Stop safely. Vehicle will turn off soon" and stopped in the road; online search showed many hybrid Pacifica years were **recalled for electrical issues**. A local mechanic got it running but the team did not feel safe.
- **Avis roadside assistance refused to tow** because none of the people with the car were Avis-authorized drivers (the authorized driver was biking in the park). Two days earlier a tow had been sent after simply saying they were part of the team. Eventually Avis accepted consent from the Edgerton employee who made the reservation. The team also rented a separate **Enterprise truck** to bring food and camping gear to the riders (extra cost), and had to find DC-area housing for the night.
- A **new rental agreement cost ~$2,000 more** than the original because the authorized driver wasn't present.
- Next day the team went to Avis to add drivers: confusion, finally only 4 additional authorized drivers could be added, so only 5 of 8 team members can call roadside assistance even though MIT lets all background-checked students drive.
- Request: accountability from Avis and clarity on who may call roadside assistance; considering switching to Enterprise.

## MIT young renter policy (Jessica Tam, Senior Strategic Sourcing Analyst, Travel and Hospitality, MIT Office of the VP for Finance, jtam@mit.edu, 617-253-2756; reply to Amulya Aluru, 6/7/2024)
- **Young Renter account number XZ10MTY** (Enterprise/MIT account as quoted).
- Young renter fee is waived for eligible renters **21-24** renting for business; must be **25+ for luxury cars and SUVs**.
- Fee waived for **18-20** year-olds for business only in **MA, NH and ME** (insurance included). 18-20 year-olds **cannot rent outside MA, NH, ME**; book via Avis or Budget when renting elsewhere.
- Applies to all renters and all drivers; non-MIT renters/drivers cannot use the MIT negotiated rate.
- Context: three 2024 drivers were 20 or under, which is why Avis/Budget remained the only option.
