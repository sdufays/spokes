(The source doc is ~11 MB because of embedded images -- pamphlet, thank-you card, poster, etc. -- which could not be captured as text.)

## Website
Spokes now uses **WordPress** (Wix was used in the past and hosts some old team sites). The WordPress blog format makes posting easy (comments, images). The team owns the **mitspokes.com** domain, currently pointed at the previous team's site: migrate it to your own year's website. This was complicated and poorly documented; talk to customer support if stuck (they got the domain transferred). **Preserve the entire past site.**

## Instagram
Account username: mitspokes; password [redacted - ask previous team].

Posted semi-regularly before and during the trip so hosts/family/friends could follow. Posted team-member introductions at the start of spring semester, then semi-frequent content during the semester (training rides, meetings, planning).
- 2022: featured by **mitstudents** during the semester and the trip; **mitpics** reposted several posts (big publicity). Stanford Spokes posted daily in 2025 (their account) which worked well.
- 2026: collaboration posts with the **mitedgerton** Instagram (great visibility); posts on fundraising progress; **everyone reposts these to their own stories**; weekly photo-dump with short caption during summer; one post per learning festival (take lots of photos/videos at events); asked restaurants to sponsor a meal and thanked them on Instagram.

**2026 TLDR advice:**
1. Individual team member intros
2. Collaboration posts with the mitedgerton Instagram for fundraising and marketing
3. Fundraising progress and thank-you posts (everyone reposts on personal Instagrams)
4. Weekly recap posts during the summer
5. One post for every learning festival
6. One post for every restaurant that sponsors a meal

## Team T-Shirt
JD designed a team T-shirt everyone wore; it builds unity and identity. Polyester T-shirts from **4imprint** (great for LFs and PJs). Jerseys from **Jakroo** -- needs a one-time license agreement because Jakroo is not an MIT-approved vendor. "STRONGLY RECOMMEND getting jerseys." (2026 expense log: Jakroo jerseys $1,641.34 on 5/5/2026; LF shirts via CustomInk $252.61 on 5/18/2026.)

2026 advice: put your first name on the back of LF shirts; 2026 had two jerseys, one black and one white (colder vs hotter days); laundry loops!

## Media
### Logo
Spokes has an official logo (2024 team changed the colors). File in "Social Media, Blog, Website, Media" in the Spokes 2022 Google Drive.

### Pamphlets
Maxwell Yun (Spokes 2022) designed a 12-page InDesign pamphlet summarizing the trip; great for finding sponsors; Maxwell believes it won the Lightforce sponsorship ($5,000, 2022). Prints cheaply: **CopyTech ~$5 per booklet**, saddle-stitched on 11x17 paper stapled in the middle. 2022 pamphlet in "Social Media, Blog, Website, Media / Pamphlets" in the Spokes 2022 Drive; InDesign files in "pamphlet_resources_2022". Fonts: Proxima Nova and Overpass (in the Resources folder).

### Thank You Card
Each host got a personally handwritten thank-you card (except one unpleasant host). Maxwell designed a card matching the pamphlet; print-ready, no year, usable any year. Prints double-sided on 8.5x11 and folds in the middle; for extra wow print on cardstock and have **MIT CopyTech machine-fold** (FedEx Kinko's can print cardstock but not fold). After the trip, thank-you cards were mailed to hosts (nice paper at CopyTech: ~$30 for ~40; the postcard attempt used the wrong paper). 2026 card: given to every host with a photo and handwritten note.

### Posters (for the Infinite)
No current spot on the Infinite but you can apply. Maxwell made two Photoshop posters (8.5x11 and 11x17) plus an "event poster" variant (used for the bike repair/safety workshop). Need the Overpass font. 2022 postered along the Infinite: good for recruitment or donations. Files in the pamphlet_resources folder.
