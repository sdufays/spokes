# Highlights: Spokes Tanzania (2017 planning, never confirmed as run)

- Spokes Tanzania was a 2017 side project of the Spokes Hub: a partnership/exchange with Tanzanian students rather than one-way aid; core team Emma, Nicole and Megan (2017, source: Milestones Planning).
- Support vehicle is feasible: renting a car/truck with a local driver is possible, cost depends on route; Megan could compile medical services along the route (2017, source: 8_31_2017).
- Teaching model reference: HEAL trained students before summer, then a week in-country, then taught 2x/week for 6 weeks in Arusha-area schools; heavy translation needs (2017, source: August 19_20, 2017).
- Large Arusha school network (primary, secondary, government); propose directly to the Minister of Education; emerging science-heavy schools in Arusha area (2017, source: August 19_20, 2017; 8_31_2017).
- Milestone categories: funding, team building, equipment, training, teaching locations/supplies, lodging/food, health/safety, visas/permits, publicity, bike maintenance (2017, source: Milestones Planning).
- Route research sources: Exodus and Intrepid Tanzania cycle safaris, bikemap.net, ibike.org Africa guide (2017, source: Spokes Africa Planning).
- Idea: make workshops leaner (less equipment, more ingenuity), e.g. teach sorting algorithms by sorting students by height (2017, source: Spokes Africa Planning).
- Weekly meeting slot proposed Thursdays 6:30 am PST / 4:30 pm Tanzania (2017, source: 8_31_2017).
