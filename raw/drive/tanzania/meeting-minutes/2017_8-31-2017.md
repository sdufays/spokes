Rapid-fire questions:
Possible to rent a car to support? Insurance?
Definitely possible to rent a car/truck for support. The cost will depend on where we are going and we would probably need to hire a driver to know where we are going. There are definitely people who would be willing to do this :)
Access to medical care?
Depending on what our final route is, I will put together a list of all medical services in those areas. I know most of the hospitals in northern Tanzania, and as long as we have transport, getting to those places won’t be too hard. There are of course challenges with the healthcare systems in general, so if there are serious cases, we would just have to make sure the person could get to Moshi, Dar es Salaam, or Nairobi.
Educational needs?
The full range of STEM subjects will gladly be accepted. Things that are as hands on as possible would be best (robotics, etc.). Also one thing to think about would be sharing how to use technology to solve local problems. I saw this program once who went into a community and discussed the needs with the students - they came up with their own problem to solve and utilized the knowledge of science and technology that the team could provide, along with local resources, to build that solution (all within a couple of days).
