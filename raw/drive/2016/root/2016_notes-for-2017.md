Opening quote to the new team: "This is going to be really challenging... You need to learn how to ask for help because you're going to need help, and you're going to need to help each other."

**Preparing: START EARLY.**
- Fundraising (2016): sent a form to all MIT departments (quick/easy), another to companies, launched an MIT Crowdfund campaign. Got: **$5,000 UROP from Course 20 (actually Course 9 per LEAP proposal), $1,000 Course 8, $2,500 Edgerton (Sandi), $7,000 Texas Instruments, $1,000 MIT STS, $2,500 Course 8/6 (see donors sheet)**.
- Bikes/supplies: bought at **Cambridge Bicycle with 20% bulk discount** (quote in 2016 biking-supplies doc).
- **Amazon: MIT is tax-exempt; do not pay tax on items sold by Amazon LLC**, because Spokes won't reimburse tax.
- Sections left blank in the notes: renting the car, getting an MIT travel credit card for the team, school contacting/outreach (Chloe), finding Warm Showers (Yang), finding churches/unorthodox places (Noam), teaching, teamworking.

**Biking and driving:**
- Starting all together failed (different sleep/wake/prep times). What worked: each morning the last rider left within 2 hours of the first.
- Mornings: riders eat, prep, gather belongings near the car and leave; the driver of the day takes down tents, cleans trash, packs the car.
- 5 of 9 could drive: 5 drivers rotated; the 4 non-drivers handled dishes, locking bikes, night cleanup so duties were evenly spread.
- 9 riders went through **35 tubes and 5 tires**; patched tubes ~2x before replacing.

**Camping, food, supplies:** nights were campgrounds 40%, Warm Showers hosts 30%, churches 20%, other 10%. Diet: tuna/peanut butter (and Nutella) sandwiches for breakfast and lunch, protein bars and trail mix while riding, pasta and soup dinner, occasional fruit ("should have eaten more fruit and fresh food"). Hydration: water with Gatorade powder.
