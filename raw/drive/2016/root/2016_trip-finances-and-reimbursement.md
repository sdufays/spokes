**Process used (2016):** every purchase logged with who paid, date, item, store, cost, "mailed to Sandi" (Edgerton), "processed", "refund received". Purchases labelled "MIT - <name>" were paid on the MIT/team card; others were personal and reimbursed. Receipts were batch-mailed to Sandi at Edgerton (batches 6/27 from Harrisburg and 7/11 from Ottawa, KS). One receipt (Fairfax groceries $294.32) was lost; "missing last receipt" flagged for one person; items with no store listed are a hassle. **Lesson: photograph and log receipts daily; mail in batches.**

Personal-reimbursement totals by rider (as listed): Nick 182.81, Omar 211.91, Antonis 212.62, Lily 236.69, Chloe 25.77, Jean 18.51, Yang 311.02, Noam 292.63 (sheet-level total of the personal ledger: $5,084.92 including MIT-paid lines). Nick's Amazon sales tax issue: $1,871.52 - $1,795.07 = $76.45 tax paid on Amazon items (not reimbursable).

**Typical costs seen (June 5 - Aug 16, 2016):**
- Groceries: very frequent Walmart/Kroger/Dollar General runs of $10-85; Costco $187.05 (Aug 3, bulk for several days); a $91 diner meal and a $100 buffet for the whole team.
- Gas for support car: roughly $20-45 per fill, about every 1-2 days.
- Camping: Lake Anna SP $34.48; Natural Bridge/Lexington KOA $75.81; Dixie Caverns $90.96; Gatewood Park $15; Laurel Branch $17; Audubon SP $16; Maybell Park $40; Border Inn $10.90; KOA Ely $59.93; Austin RV Park $30. Inn (Relax Inn) $60.
- Tolls: MA Pike $2.90, NJ Turnpike $13.85, Kansas Turnpike $1.40-2.50.
- Bike repair en route: Blue Wheel Bicycles (Charlottesville) $20.80 + patch kit $13.68; PedalPower $50.66; Family Bicycles $43.54; Bike Source $36.22; Bicycle Shack $42.02; The Bike Smith $86.08; a wheel $81.64.
- Teaching supplies bought on the road (CVS, Walgreens, Dollar General, Walmart): ~$3-85 each.
- Misc: propane $5.25, bike rack $159.36 (Noam), thank-you cards $4.22, postcards/stamps for donors $10.80, envelopes $1.94.

**Returns (Lily's sheet):** returned 2 hitch bike racks, 2 sleeping bags, 3 neuroscience kits = $778.45 refunded. Individual rider bike purchases (example: 2016 Jamis Ventura Sport 44cm $583.20, helmet $30, rear rack $22.49, Gore-tex shorts, Giro shoes $63.75, bag $15.69, lights $7.98 = $843.09 with pedals credited back to Spokes). Lesson: hitch-mount racks do not fit rental vehicles (no hitch receiver) -- bought and returned.
