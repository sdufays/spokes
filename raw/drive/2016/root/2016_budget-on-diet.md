Version 1 ("on a diet") total **$23,924.91**; Version 2 (also used in the LEAP proposal) **$30,530.85**.

| Category | Lean (v1) | Full (v2) | Key assumptions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bike equipment | $8,850.71 | $9,096.65 | 10 bikes @ $700 = $7,000; locks, tape, lights, speedometers, racks/bags, avg $30 repair each; v1 also $70/bike shipping; spare tires 4 @ $35 |
| Sleeping/camping/clothing/group | $2,295 | $8,940 (incl. food) | pad $12, bag $30, tents 4 @ $75 (v2: 2), shoes $60, shorts $20, gloves $15, socks $10; v2 adds **food: 700 person-days @ $10 = $7,000**, propane 30 @ $3; cook set/Jetboil from last year |
| Emergency kit | $571 | $316 | first aid, dog spray $8 x10, purification tabs, bug spray |
| Car | $6,530 | $6,500 | **rental $5,000 (maybe underestimate)**, gas $700 (v1 counted $1,400), 2 racks |
| Teaching | $3,578.20 | $3,578.20 | 20 Chromebooks @ $149 = $2,980, 20 water-rocket kits @ $14.91, electronics $300 |
| Campsites/accommodation | $2,100 | $2,100 | 60 nights @ $35 |

Reality check: the team actually spent far less on lodging because ~60% of nights were free/cheap (Warm Showers, churches); see trip finances and NOTES for 2017.
