# Spokes tag vocabulary

Use ONLY these tags (lowercase, hyphenated) so everything is searchable consistently. Pick every tag that genuinely applies.

## Planning / organizing
- `route` — route choice, daily routes, maps, mileage plans, RideWithGPS/Strava
- `housing` — hosts, churches, Warmshowers, alumni hosts, schools, motels
- `camping` — campgrounds, wild camping, booking campsites
- `learning-festivals` — LF events, hosts, activities run, how they went
- `curriculum` — LF activity design, lesson plans, workshop content
- `fundraising` — campaigns, Giving Tuesday, events, crowdfunding, donor emails
- `sponsorship` — companies, in-kind donations, sponsor outreach & deliverables
- `grants` — MIT/department/external grants, deadlines, applications
- `budget-finance` — budgets, spending, travel/pro cards, receipts, reimbursement
- `mit-admin` — Edgerton Center, MIT policies, approvals, insurance, risk forms
- `recruiting` — applications, interviews, team selection
- `team-roles` — role assignments, responsibilities, meeting structure
- `timeline` — semester timelines, deadlines, checklists of what to do when
- `transport-logistics` — support car/van, rental, flights, shipping bikes, getting to start / home from end
- `communication` — emails/templates, contacting hosts/partners, internal comms
- `social-media` — instagram/tiktok/youtube, posting strategy, graphics
- `website-blog` — website, blog process
- `press-media` — news coverage, interviews, articles
- `merch` — jerseys, t-shirts, stickers

## Riding / on the trip
- `gear` — bikes, bike fitting, camping gear, clothing, what to bring
- `packing-list`
- `bike-maintenance` — flats, mechanicals, repairs, bike shops
- `food-nutrition` — meals, groceries, cooking, nutrition, food donations
- `training` — pre-trip training rides, fitness
- `safety` — traffic, dogs, road conditions, riding formation, near-misses
- `health-injury` — injuries, illness, heat, medical, first aid
- `weather` — heat, rain, wind, storms, cold
- `terrain` — climbs, elevation, gravel, desert, mountains
- `rest-day` — rest days and what was done
- `sightseeing` — attractions, side trips, national parks
- `local-people` — notable kindness, hosts, community encounters
- `team-dynamics` — group morale, conflict, bonding
- `lessons-learned` — explicit advice / "next time we should" / what went wrong or right
- `reflection` — personal reflection, emotional, not much logistics

## Places
Also add free-form place tags prefixed `place:` for US states (e.g. `place:kentucky`) and notable cities/parks (`place:zion`). Use full lowercase state names.
