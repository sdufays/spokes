Do you crave adventure? Want to see more of the US? Enjoy biking? Like sleeping on the floor? Looking for some awesome friends?

If you answered yes to any of the questions, Spokes 2027 might be the perfect opportunity for you. 🫵

Spokes is a nonprofit, student-run initiative dedicated to spreading opportunities for STEM education to students in rural and underserved communities. The catch? We do it all by bike. Across 75 days, we bike over 4,000 miles from DC to San Francisco, teaching STEM workshops at schools, libraries, and correctional facilities all along our route. Students (you!) plan the route, fundraise, create teaching material, coordinate with learning festival partners, and, of course, do lots and lots (and lots...) of biking!

Do I need biking experience?

No! If you’re considering Spokes, you definitely need to be up for adventure, ready to push yourself physically, and have an appreciation for Type 2 fun. However, you don’t need to have previous biking experience. Our team had a wide range of experience levels with biking (and athletics in general). Anyone who’s excited about Spokes is capable of meeting the physical demands!

Signing up for Spokes was one of the best decisions many of us made at MIT, and we can’t wait to pass the torch to the next team! If you fill out this application<https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdApYtANdNuWVn3uLlxFo31cpKxhU-1J8hQyHR_SyxwtD4Nuw/viewform> by Sunday, September 27 at 11:59pm, that could be you. 🥳
