### 2025-04-24 02:24 — Sarah Schmitt <sar55@mit.edu>
Hey DormSpam!

Looking for a way to enjoy the warmer weather this weekend? ☀️ Stretch your legs and take a break from STEM?  🧪💻

Spokes 2025<https://mitspokes.com/> is collaborating with the List Visual Arts Center to host a FREE biking tour of MIT's public art collection! 🎨  Register here<https://listart.mit.edu/calendar/pedaling-public-art>, and meet at the BlueBikes station on the corner of Ames and Main Street at 2pm on Sunday, April 27th. You can either bring your own bike or rent a BlueBike, but a helmet is required either way.

"Ok, but what is Spokes?" you may ask? Spokes is a student-run club under the Edgerton Center that sends 8 MIT students every summer to bike from the Lincoln Memorial in Washington, D.C. 🇺🇸 to the Golden Gate Bridge in San Francisco, CA  🌉  and host STEAM learning festivals for rural, underserved communities along the way. We are currently in the final stages of planning and fundraising<https://crowdfund.mit.edu/story/Spokes-S25> for this summer's program, and we need your help to meet our goal 💸 You can donate directly, share the link with your roommates, classmates, teammates, friends and family, spread the news on social media, or stay tuned for our Instagram bingo challenge later this week!

Hope to see you on the road this weekend!

Warm wishes,
Sarah Schmitt '28 and Spokes 2025 🚲
