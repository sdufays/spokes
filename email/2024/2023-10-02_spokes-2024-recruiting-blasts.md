## Key points
- Recruiting for Spokes 2024 ran Oct 2 to Oct 15, 2023; applications via Google Form, deadline Oct 15, 2023 (2024 team recruiting).
- 2024 pitch numbers: 80 days, 3,800+ miles, 70+ towns, middle-school STEM/STEAM Learning Festivals (2024).
- Sent to dorm lists via Sponge-talk with a TL;DR first line; questions routed to the team alias spokes-2023@mit.edu (the outgoing team runs recruiting).
- Sent by Katherine Q Guo (Oct 2) and Joy J Ma (Oct 9, Oct 15 due-today bump).

### 2023-10-02 12:13 — Katherine Q Guo <kathe@mit.edu>
Hi all,

tldr: apply <https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc5bJhJ6GDi-g92bh5A9Wy1a9-2zMrlcwZtcE6FKVlnGE8O0A/viewform?fbclid=IwAR1vRCJEPVHApN2vHY0D11Et1K8z10Y9yKxwhNDlKBpXb14NjdeWqFTE4ks> to be a part of Spokes 2024 - bike across the USA over the summer while teaching students in rural America and earn a cool fun fact to use as an ice breaker for the rest of your life.

Every year, MIT Spokes sends a team of students on a cross-country cycling adventure from D.C. to San Francisco. Over the course of 80 days, the team bikes over 3,800 miles, stays in over 70 different towns, and hosts hands-on Learning Festivals that promote STEAM to middle-school students across the country.

We’re looking for a team of bold, open-minded, and ambitious individuals for the Spokes 2024 team. You don’t even have to be an experienced cyclist to join (most of us don't)! By joining Spokes, you have the freedom and responsibility to plan next year's adventure from start to finish as the initiative is all student-run, and then...you get to do it! Seems demanding, but take it from us, it is one of the most impactful and meaningful ways to spend your summer.

For more information on this year’s MIT Spokes team, visit www.mitspokes.com<http://www.mitspokes.com/> or check out our Instagram @mitspokes. To learn more about our day-to-day endeavors, read our blogs, which can also be found at our website!

Apply here: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc5bJhJ6GDi-g92bh5A9Wy1a9-2zMrlcwZtcE6FKVlnGE8O0A/viewform?fbclid=IwAR1vRCJEPVHApN2vHY0D11Et1K8z10Y9yKxwhNDlKBpXb14NjdeWqFTE4ks (deadline October 15st). Questions? Email spokes-2023@mit.edu<mailto:spokes-2023@mit.edu><mailto:spokes-2022@mit.edu>.
