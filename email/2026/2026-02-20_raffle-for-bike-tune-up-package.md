## Key points
- Raffle structure (2026): 1 ticket $5, 3 for $12, 10 for $30; sold via Google Form; closes Mar 6 then extended to Mar 13, 2026 11:59pm, winners Mar 14.
- Prizes (2026): Trek Cambridge Level 2 service ($189.99) and Level 1 ($99.99); three Cambridge Bicycle services ($150 each) donated Mar 6; $750 total value.
- Result: raffle raised about $1,000 (2026, per Nov 2026 thank-you emails).
- 2026 fundraising goal: $50K for equipment and workshop materials; crowdfunding page crowdfund.mit.edu/story/Spokes-S26 (2026).
- 2026 team of 7: Aarushi Mehrotra, Carmen McCay, Caroline Huang, Drew Dowdy, Nathan Mustafa, Angelica (Nunu) Zhuang, Tatiana Vassiliev; this was the 12th year of Spokes (2026).
- Pattern: a bump email every ~1 week plus a "new prizes" announcement drove sales; a ticket pool worth less than prizes was used as a hook (2026).
