# Highlights, Spokes 2016 folder (and handbook with multi-year entries)

- 2016 funding that actually arrived: TI $7,000; MIT Course 9/20 UROP ~$5,000-5,280; Edgerton $500-4,000 (via Sandi); Physics $2,500; Course 6 $2,500; edX $1,750; STS $1,000; Aero/Astro (Course 16) $1,000; unspent prior-year fund ~$7,000 (2016, source: NOTES for 2017 / Donors / LEAP Grant Proposal)
- Budget: full plan $30,531 (10 people, 70 days), lean version $23,925; biggest lines are bikes $7k, food $7k ($10/person/day), car rental $5k, laptops $3k (2016, source: Budget on diet)
- Cold-emailing ~100 corporations produced almost nothing; money came from MIT departments, a past sponsor (TI), edX, and personal donors/MIT Crowdfund. Each department email tied the subject the member would teach to kids (2016, source: Fundraising timeline / departments-cold-email)
- Handbook advice: grants didn't work in 2015; each member personally emailing 10 people worked (2015, source: Spokes Handbook)
- Corporate sponsor tactics: send two emails (personal + formal PDF), own your email chain, ask 5-10k from giants, benchmark later asks on earlier sponsors, don't over-promise deliverables (2014, source: Spokes Handbook)
- Sponsor tiers $10k/$5k/$2.5k with defined perks are ready to reuse (2016, source: Donor Levels / Details for Companies)
- Bikes: bought 11 Jamis bikes + helmets, shorts, shoes, locks via Cambridge Bicycle with 20-25% bulk discount for $9,938.92, tax-exempt (2016, source: Cambridge Bicycle quote I-97886)
- Amazon: MIT is tax-exempt; don't pay tax on Amazon LLC items (not reimbursable); hitch bike racks don't fit rental cars (returned) (2016, source: NOTES for 2017 / Biking accessories)
- Reimbursement workflow: log every receipt (who/date/store/cost), mail batches to Edgerton (Sandi), track "processed/refund received"; photograph receipts, one was lost (2016, source: 2016 trip finances)
- Recommended team size 5-8; 9-10 in 2016 meant 5 drivers and rotating chores (2016, source: Spokes Handbook / NOTES for 2017)
- Mornings: staggered departure (last rider leaves within 2 hours of first) beat everyone leaving together (2016, source: NOTES for 2017)
- 9 riders used 35 tubes and 5 tires; patch twice before replacing (2016, source: NOTES for 2017)
- Lodging mix 2016: campgrounds 40%, Warm Showers 30%, churches 20%, other 10%; Warm Showers hosts hosted BBQs and were the best experiences, avoid ones that add 30 off-route miles (Wichita) (2016, source: NOTES for 2017 / tentative Itinerary)
- Food: tuna/peanut butter sandwiches, bars, pasta/soup; wished for more fruit and fresh food; Gatorade powder for hydration; Costco bulk run $187 (2016, source: NOTES for 2017 / trip finances)
- Reusable route: 2015/2016 DC-to-SF route with known festival hosts: Challenger Learning Center (Hazard KY), LASCLEX (Lexington), Lab: Revolution (Farmington MO), St. Louis Home School Network, Newton Public Library, Loveland Public Library, The Leonardo (SLC), Carson City library; re-contact past hosts first (2016, source: Contacting Schools / tentative Itinerary)
- Pre-trip calendar: Indiegogo set up spring break, half of fundraising done by 4/25, bikes bought by 5/8, curriculum done by 5/15, trial-teach by 5/29, depart 6/6 (2016, source: Calendar-By Week)
- Teaching: stick to 2-3 hands-on workshops of 3-6 hours; 2019 team ran 4 workshops (rapid prototyping, DNA, neuroscience/AI, chemistry) at 10 festivals with 30-50 students (2015/2019, source: Spokes Handbook)
- Reputation is Spokes' lifeblood; one rude interaction in a small town travelled back to a host (2015, source: Spokes Handbook)
- Close-out checklist: thank-you postcards and t-shirts to donors and hosts, Warm Showers reviews, donor summaries/videos (Physics dept wanted 2-page summary + video) (2016, source: the last to-do list / Donors)
