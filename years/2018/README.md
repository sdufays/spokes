## Highlights (from Drive 2018)

# Highlights: Spokes 2018 folder

- Total 2018 cost estimate was about $20,217 (bikes/gear $7,488, van $4,547 incl. $3,847 rental via Edgerton + $700 gas, food $6,090 at $10/person/day, emergency kit $478, teaching $1,000), excluding Airbnbs; grant forms quoted a $23,000 to $25,000 total. (2018, source: Shareable Budget; Statement of Purpose; CRW Grant Application)
- Confirmed funding reached about $36,060 by June: $7,786 already in account, Edgerton supplemental $3,000, TBP Fellowship $5,000, MIT Course 8 $2,500, Course 6 $2,000, Course 18 $750, Course 2 $750, Course 9 $250, SAO $1,000, Cynthia Barnhart $2,000, MIT Club of Chicago $500, crowdfund $9,330 (goal $10,000). Actual spend by early June was $13,483. (2018, source: Projected Costs)
- The Tau Beta Pi Summer Fellowship gave $5,000 after a full written application (abstract, motivation, project plan, timeline, rider qualifications, personal statements); resumes and "why I joined" blurbs were collected from every rider. (2018, source: TBP Grant Formatted; 5-1-18)
- MIT department asks: Course 8 (Peter Fisher) "WE GOT MONEY"; Course 2, 6, 18, 9 also gave; Course 16 gave $0; cold emails to alumni, Sony, TI, Nikon, GoPro, Garmin, Ford and Dell produced no recorded money. Corporate cold outreach had a very low yield; warm MIT-internal and personal-network asks worked. (2018, source: Potential Sponsors; Projected Costs; 4-24-2018)
- Check eligibility before writing: Sony was "not accepting donations", Nikon needs 6 months advance notice, GoPro only sponsors athletes, Ford's portal wouldn't answer until July. (2018, source: Potential Sponsors)
- The MIT crowdfund (crowdfund.mit.edu/project/10264) raised about $9,330 from about 50 donors (the $20+ list has 49 emails); the $50+ tier got handwritten letters (each rider wrote to donors they knew), gifts of $500 came from STR, James Stephens, Cathy Glover and the MIT Club of Chicago; most donors were family and friends. A usable donor list with emails is in the folder. (2018, source: $50+ Letter Recipients; $20+ Email List)
- Alumni club emails: Kate's MIT Club of Chicago email (personal local connection) produced $500; clubs without a personal tie (Austin/San Antonio, WNY, Northern NJ) did not respond. (2018, source: Kate's Alum Club Email; Potential Sponsors)
- Bikes: eight Jamis Renegade Exile bikes plus gloves, pedals, helmets, cages and tubes from Cambridge Bicycle for $7,185.53 (10% discount, no tax). Cambridge Bicycle won because staff visited, gave a thoughtful full-package quote; Wheelworks quoted $1,000/bike + 15% gear discount. Ordered only in May, after funding clarity: order earlier. (2018, source: final_quote_for_spokes.pdf; Possible Shops, Models, and Costs)
- Money mechanics from 2017 advice: MIT crowdfunding (not Indiegogo), 501(c)(3) tax exempt, travel card for trip purchases and procurement card for stuff at home; riders paid out of pocket and were still owed $1,908 in reimbursements (Bethany $1,283, Matt $362, Delia $263) in June. Fix reimbursement process early. (2018, source: 2-13-18; Projected Costs)
- Learning Festival format that worked: 8:30 setup, three 2-hour sessions (9-11, 11-1, 2-4), lunch 1-2, four rooms (Intro to CS, Design Challenge, Picture This/camera obscura, Circuit Building); students pick 3 of 4; Challenger Learning Center gets two days. Target age 11-15 (middle school). (2018, source: Learning Festival Breakdown; 3-6-2018; General Notes)
- 2018 host/school list and 2017 attendance: Challenger KY (strong, whole-school groups, young and tough), Lab: Revolution MO (makerspace), St. Louis Home School (varied math levels), aSTEAM Village KC (chaotic, tiny space), Newton KS library (25 kids, chill; advertise more), Loveland CO (15 kids), Space Center UT (100 HS kids), Carson City NV (only 11; "nobody came"; do not go back). Advertise early and add one new place. (2018, source: Potential Schools; 2-22-18)
- Virginia Warmshowers hosts hosted the team repeatedly: Bruce and Vikki White (Fredericksburg), Francine Johnston (Waynesboro), John and Patty Brenner (Abingdon, 2015 to 2017). Hosts give dinner and sometimes breakfast; a few ask for chores. (2018 and earlier, source: 2018 Route)
- Route lesson: start with a rest day in DC to learn bikes before riding; tough days were Waynesboro to Natural Bridge (3,031 ft) and Abingdon to Wise 2017 (4,764 ft, everyone shuttled); rain days in Virginia led to shuttling in 2017; Google Maps routes were unreliable, stay on Rt 1. (2018, source: 2018 Route)
- Eat and drink more than you think you should on day one; set up navigator groups A and B, a van driver rotation (Matt, Delia, Annie, Lisa, Kate, An, Alex), and a group essentials kit (multitool, tire levers, patch kit, tube, frame pump, first aid, water and snacks). (2018, source: 5-28-2018)
- Food advice from 2017 alumni: buy less than you think, canned bean salad in tortillas is fast, oatmeal and almond milk good, avoid PB-and-tuna sandwiches and potatoes, tell Warmshowers hosts about restrictions; budget $10 per person per day. Team food likes: bars, PB, fruit, quinoa salad, tortillas, trail mix. (2018, source: 2-13-18; Food Preferences; Shareable Budget)
- Safety rules passed on: always helmets, bright clothing, front/back lights (strobing), get fitted and get bikes early, tire/brake/chain checks, never overreach; pepper spray for dogs (2017 advice). Team claim: no Spokes rider ever injured on the road. (2018, source: 2-13-18; 5-8-18; Grant Application Information)
- Timeline reality check: team formed Feb 2018 and weekly deadlines slipped (bikes ordered May, gear purchases May 22, departure May 27); aim to finish route, hosts and curriculum by end of April and hold a pre-departure packing session. (2018, source: Planning Timeline; 5-22-2018)
- Sponsorship ask pattern: grants of $5,000 (Sony, Garmin, Koch) or $10,000 (REI) with offers of website listing, social media posts and special recognition at $5,000+. In-kind wins: COOLA sunscreen samples, MakeCrate electronics kits, donated jerseys/shorts per budget. (2018, source: letters; Potential Sponsors; Shareable Budget)
- Return trip: the team drove home Aug 6-16, 2018 through Zion/Arches/Rocky Mountain, Denver, Chicago, Buffalo, Boston (about 3,900 miles, 8-hour days) with the van; plan car return (Matthew reserved the car for Aug 17) and budget gas and lodging. (2018, source: 2018 Return; 4-17-2018)
- Curriculum assets with materials lists and costs are reusable: egg drop/DOTS tower/book balance design challenges ($145), camera obscura box camera ($125), Arduino/photoresistor circuits ($404), and Scratch coding with a resource handout. (2018, source: Syllabus files; Projected Costs)
