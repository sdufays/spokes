# Highlights: Spokes 2024 (top 25; full lists in _parts/*_highlights.md)

- 2024 total spend was $34,510.29 against an original Edgerton-funding plan of $34,404.96; bikes were the largest line: budget $10,448.38 for 8 bikes (~$1,306 each) vs. $946.87 originally planned; bike-equipment category ended at $17,421.59 (102.8% of $16,948.38 budget, includes camping gear). (2024, source: 2024 BUDGET; Spokes 2024 Budget for Edgerton Funding)
- Revised 2024 category budgets: Bike equipment $16,948; Transport $11,000 (car one-way $6,000, Boston-DC $500, gas $1,500, flights $3,000; $8,678.54 spent); Lodging $2,030 ($543 spent); Food $10,000 (only $781 booked as Food); Workshop supplies/education $6,000 ($5,402.50 spent); Marketing $2,000 ($1,411 spent). (2024, source: 2024 BUDGET)
- J-WEL grant covered the bikes: ~$12k of bike costs; J-WEL backdated the grant to March and all charges are credited to J-WEL first; ProCard charges are posted to the J-WEL account by the ProCard posting staff. (2024, source: Budget Team Meeting Notes)
- Travel Card charges do not post until the end of the trip (like a loan) and the account number is assigned at the end; upload receipts to your travel profile in the meantime; receipts needed for anything over $75 and all food. (2024, source: Budget Team Meeting Notes; MIT Travel Card Application)
- Mistake to avoid: groceries and personal-ish items were charged to ProCard "mistakenly" and Amazon items had tax; keep Buy-2-Pay (Coupa) for Amazon/online items, cost object 2530508; do not use recreational GL; use commodity codes 420226, 420310 or 420245. (2024, source: Buy-2-Pay How-To; Jess Procard Expenses)
- 2024 route ran DC (June 1-2 start) to San Francisco (Aug 16), roughly 4,038 actual miles over about 55 riding days with 8 riders, 1 support vehicle, and rotating driver/blogger roles (2024, source: 2024 Route)
- Housing tactic that worked: confirm learning festivals first, then find housing; mix of MIT alumni, Warm Showers hosts, churches (pastors responded to last-minute asks), and pre-booked campgrounds (2024, source: 1/21/24 Route Planning; What to do for housing)
- Learning festivals 2024: DC DYRS (confirmed), Hazard KY Challenger Center, AccelerateKY Bowling Green, Newton KS library, Gunnison CO library, Ely NV library, Fallon NV youth club, Tahoe STEM Academy; SF detention LF never happened - 2023 note recommends trying again (2024, source: Learning Festivals; 2024 Route)
- J-WEL Education Innovation Grant awarded **$45,330** for July 1, 2024 - June 30, 2025 (application due 3/15/2024; decision letter 4/23/2024). (2024, source: Vandiver J-WEL Decision Letter.pdf)
- J-WEL obligations: one-page public Grant Narrative **before June 5, 2024** (funds released upon receipt), a photo essay from every student, a J-WEL event/talk, mid-year update meeting, Grantee Spotlight interview, and **final report by Aug 31, 2025**. (2024, source: Vandiver J-WEL Decision Letter.pdf)
- 2024 budget request $40,330: bikes/gear $16,000; one-way van DC to SF $6,000; lodging and food $6,000; flights $4,000; LF supplies $3,000; food $2,030; gas $2,000; rental Boston to DC $500; marketing $500; safety $300. (2024, source: jwel_budget_template.xlsx)
- Fundraising status at application: $19,872 raised of $40,330 (carryover from 2023 $6,372; MIT departments $6,500; outside $2,000; expected $5,000); remaining need $20,458. (2024, source: jwel_budget_template.xlsx)
- Other 2024 funding reported to J-WEL: MathWorks $1,000; Kars4Kids $750; MechE $200; pk-12 Open Learning $6,293; MIT Alumni Club of Colorado $500. (2024, source: J-WEL Grant Narrative 0508.docx)
- Edgerton: teams try to **leave ~$10k in the bank for next year**; $6,700 was raised in Dec-Jan; travel-card purchases are not expensed until the end of the trip; sunscreen/health items are personal; list department money and grants (not small crowdfunding) in grant reports. (2024, source: 0430 Edgerton Meeting)
- Rental car was ~$8k for two sedans; Avis ~$6k, two SUVs ~$9k; a 7-seat SUV (Chevy Traverse or similar) at minivan price picked up at Logan was favored; reserve ASAP. (2024, source: 0430 Edgerton Meeting)
- **Avis disaster (2024)**: bald tires on the support van, stranded riders, recalled hybrid Pacifica stalled in Shenandoah, replacement contract cost $2,000 more, Enterprise truck rented to haul gear; Avis refunded only 2 days. (2024, source: Spokes 2024 Car Troubles.pdf)
- **Add every driver as an authorized driver in person at pickup** - Avis refused roadside assistance/tow because nobody present was authorized; only 4 extra drivers could be added (5 of 8 team members). Inspect tires and check recalls at pickup. (2024, source: Spokes 2024 Car Troubles.pdf)
- 2024 LF curriculum was 4 one-hour activities (3D Printing, Physics of Sound, Krispie Koncrete, Strawberry DNA), ~15 students each; all run simultaneously with 3-4 groups rotating through rooms (2024, source: Learning Festival Info; Newton/Fallon/Bowling Green schedules).
- Standard LF day template: check-in 8:30/9:00, four 1-hour rotations, lunch 12-1, pickup 2:30; groups color-coded Red/Green/Blue (Orange added at Bowling Green) (2024, source: Newton Schedule, Fallon Schedule, Bowling Green Schedule).
- MIT department asks worked: $500 each from Courses 16, 3, 11, 21H, 21M and MCSC (MIT Climate & Sustainability Consortium, because of climate content); MIT Libraries $2,500; MIT Museum $1,000. Many departments "bumped" the request; remember to cc spokes-2024 on every email. (2024, source: Spokes 2024 Fundraising)
- Company cold outreach was "lowkey not successful" in 2024 (Tern no, Jane Street company no, Trek/Garmin/Decathlon never confirmed); only MathWorks said yes ($1,000; gave $1,000 in 2022, $1,500 in 2019). Personal asks to employees/alumni worked better (Jane Street employees gave $100, $100, $1,000). (2024, source: Spokes 2024 Fundraising; Mathworks Grant Application)
- Crowdfunding must be via MIT (crowdfund.mit.edu): title max 50 chars, summary 100 chars, image 1-10MB; 2024 goal $10,000; page dropped Mar 23, "quiet phase" Apr 10, ended May 29; MIT 24 Hour Challenge was Mar 14. (2024, source: Crowdfunding Documents; Fundraising Timeline)
- Trip results: 74 days, 4,038 miles, 171,691 ft climbing, 8 learning festivals, 55 biking days, 9 states (ended Aug 15, 2024 at Golden Gate). (2024, source: Day 74 blog)
- Route/housing: stick to US Bike Route 76 (TransAm) to Colorado; find hosts (Warmshowers, churches, alumni) BEFORE choosing stopping towns; aim for 75+ days (2023 did 71 and had too few rest days); Kentucky dogs (carry air horns). (2023/2024, source: Advice from Spokes 2023!; Route Planning and Housing Master Guide)
- Timeline model: start fundraising in November, crowdfunding page + dept asks in December, grants Feb-Mar, Midnight Marathon in April (CPW), crowdfunding closes late May; finalize housing in May. (2024/2026, source: Fundraising Timeline; The Spokes Master Guide)
