# Highlights from Spokes 2023 (Drive folder) for a 2027 team

- **Get the ProCard and travel card ASAP (about 2 weeks to arrive); put ALL expenses on them so nothing is out-of-pocket.** Big orders (e.g. the ~$13k bike purchase) go directly through Sandi/Edgerton; ProCard for non-travel; travel card for the rest; email Marvelin each receipt as `LASTNAME mm-dd-yyyy $amount vendor.pdf`. (2023, source: For next year)
- **2023 budget planned ~$37.9k, spent ~$31.6k (83%).** Biggest lines: bikes+gear $13.8k (99% used), transport $8.3k (rental car ~$5.8k, gas ~$6.5k used incl. other), food budget $10k but only $2.8k used at snapshot, lodging $2.0k. (2023, source: FINANCES)
- **Fundraising total 2023:** ~$10,020 carried over + ~$14,400 from MIT departments/offices: MIT Libraries $2,500, Course 8 $2,500, Edgerton $4,000, Course 10 $1,000, MIT Energy Initiative $1,500, Course 3/4/OME/SHASS $500 each, 21G $350, Course 11 $300, Course 12 $250; plus MIT Museum $1,000 and alumni/crowdfund gifts. (2023, source: SPOKES 2023 FUNDRAISING)
- **Status on Mar 3: $9,900 raised by the team with ~$10,000 still to go;** the team asked departments every week and tailored each email to the department's story (e.g. CEE: "re-route due to floods"). (2023, source: Meeting notes)
- **Alumni Association leader calls (Apr 12, 40-50 attendees) were the most effective alum outreach:** donations came in during the call plus housing leads. Most route-state alumni clubs were never contacted (only 12 states). (2023, source: Meeting notes / Alumni Clubs Contact)
- **Grant calendar:** SHASS undergrad fund 3/1; TBP 3/10; Coop 3/31 ($1,500 in 2022); Kelly-Douglas 4/5-4/7; Baker accepts Sept 1-May 1 and wants a new/changed project (rolling, 3 weeks ahead); Mad Money rolling ($5,000 in 2019). Joy's Kelly-Douglas (~$1,500) was jeopardized by a professor's reference letter arriving a month late. (2023, source: Grants to apply to / Meeting notes)
- **Late planning cost campsites: Arches, Canyonlands, Bryce and Zion were fully booked by April 21; booked Wingate ($128), Ruby's Inn, Mammoth Cave, Mark Twain NF, Capitol Reef, Breaks.** Book national-park camping the moment dates are fixed. (2023, source: Meeting notes / FINANCES)
- **Car:** 2023 rental ~$5,810 (2022 Chrysler Pacifica, one-way to SFO); one-way adds a few thousand dollars and shipping bikes/gear home took 2 days and was costly; only the renter plus 4 added drivers can call roadside assistance - add drivers in person at Avis/Budget. (2023-2024, source: Gear and Car Master Guide)
- **Bikes/gear:** 7 Giant Revolt 2 gravel bikes from Landry's ($1,377.50 each; $11.6k order incl. gear) + ~$1.5k Amazon extras; keep the same bike model for part swapping; the MSW frame pumps and 100-lumen lights were weak ("GET ANOTHER ONE"); Sprintech mirrors "quite nice"; sunscreen used a lot. (2023, source: SPOKES 2023 GEAR / FINANCES)
- **Reimbursement process:** Concur report fields: Trip Purpose = Field Trip, Cost Object 2530032 SPOKES, Non-Sponsored Activity; set up direct deposit in Atlas; submit after trip end. Learning supplies reimbursements via atlas RFP to Marvelin. (2023, source: Concur reimbursements)
- **Housing process that worked:** Warm Showers (message template, 7 people, backyard camping ok), email ~5 churches per city, MIT alumni, ask LF hosts, reuse prior hosts; first 9 nights were hosts (Heather Cogdell, Jen Strackbein, Francine Johnston, Dirk van Assendelft, Steve Andes, Jennifer and Curtis Brown, a church, Breaks Interstate Park campsite A-52). (2023, source: What to do for housing / New route master file)
- **Joy's route advice:** reroute Kansas further north and replace Newton LF with Manhattan (KS) to stay with MIT alums rather than Warm Showers and churches. (2023, source: For next year)
- **Trip timing realities:** it takes 90 minutes from alarm to everyone on bikes; set lunch at 12pm regardless of mileage; regroup at the apex of climbs; no dropping people; stop riding at ~160 AQI for smoke (Canadian wildfire smoke on Jun 6 near Linden, VA). (2023, source: For next year / Meeting notes)
- **Social media: reels/TikTok beat blogs.** A 33-sec video got 1k IG views; sports-nutrition sponsor said they would only consider Spokes with much more daily video presence ("craft a story through daily videos"). (2023, source: Meeting notes / Video ideas)
- **Learning festivals 2023 (11 confirmed or attempted):** DC juvenile corrections (Jun 5, Judge Faruqui), Hazard KY Challenger Center (Tom Cravens, ~70 students), Arnold MO, Newton KS Public Library, Lamar CO, Denver, Gunnison, Ely NV, Fallon NV, Lake Tahoe STEM Academy; Lexington VA and Lamar did not confirm; SF did not happen. Repeat sites are reliable: re-email last year's hosts with last year's date. (2023, source: New route master file / Learning Festivals Emails)
- **Spark (MIT ESP, March 19) as a dry run:** each member taught a 15-min rotation (Fibonacci in nature, balloon car/rocket, camera obscura, two-slit diffraction, molecular gastronomy); lesson: balloon car was the hit; manage timing; finish workshop design before May. (2023, source: Meeting notes)
- **Costs per item to expect:** campsites $30-128 each; motel ~$120 for 4 people/1 night; Wix domain $17 + plan $178/yr; checked bike on flights $35-75; bike box ~$20. (2023, source: FINANCES / receipt screenshot)
- **Costco donation form must be taken in person to a warehouse 4-6 weeks ahead; gave $500 in 2022;** Edgerton EIN and contact details on form. (2023, source: Costco Spokes grant)
- **Cross-year Master Guides in this folder (edited through Nov 2026) hold the best advice:** metal water bottles, tubeless gravel bikes, bike fits via CycleLifePT, JWEL grant ($45k in 2024), no cooking in 2026 (restaurants donated dinner), 2-pot meals, hard-sided boxes in the car. (2023-2026, source: Master Guides)
