## Highlights (from Drive 2017)

# Highlights: Spokes 2017 folder (plus 2013-2016 carry-over material)

- 2017 money in: Texas Instruments $10,000 (Platinum), Edgerton Center $3,000, MIT Course 8 $2,500, Mathworks $1,000, Course 9 $250, plus 46 personal donors totalling $4,380 via the MIT crowdfund (started May 10, 2017). (2017, source: OFFICIAL DONOR LIST; Spokes America_DonorInfo_8-14.csv)
- Budget scale: team budget/fundraising goal ~$30,000; ~$26,000 spent the year before; Edgerton started the 2017 semester with $8,500 on hand and told the team to raise $25,000+. By May 7 the team had enough for the trip but wanted ~$10,000 more. (2017, source: Grant Application Info; 3_6_17 Fundraising Meeting with Sandi; 5/7/17 Meeting Notes)
- 2016 funding sources, in the 2016 team's own words: UROP from Course 20 $5,000, Course 8 $1,000 + $2,500, Edgerton $2,500, TI $7,000, STS $1,000. Biggest lever = emailing every MIT department (a form letter) and companies. (2016, source: NOTES for 2017)
- MIT card rules: pro card (supplies/materials in MA, no travel) vs travel card (food, gas, hotels, tolls, water; NOT sunscreen/personal items); keep every receipt; MIT is tax-exempt so do not pay tax on Amazon (Amazon LLC) purchases or you will not be reimbursed; departments take ~2 weeks to transfer funds, follow up if no money after 2 weeks. (2017, source: 3_6_17 Fundraising Meeting with Sandi; NOTES for 2017)
- Van rental: Budget and Avis have MIT corporate rates; under-25 students may rent; MIT is self-insured so decline the waivers; use an SUV; swap drivers every 4-5 hours, never drive alone; est. $5,000-$6,500 (2016-2017 budgets). (2017, source: 3_6_17 Fundraising Meeting with Sandi; Budget on diet)
- Bikes: Cambridge Bicycle gave a 20% bulk discount on a purchase order (two years running); in 2017 the team compared Belmont Bikes (wider selection, same discount, ~$100 fitting) and Ace Wheelworks assembled/fit them; bikes were bought by April-May 2017, shoes/helmets were the slow part. (2017, source: 3/5, 3/19, 4/9 Meeting Notes; Final Blog Post)
- Reward and sponsor tier templates ready to reuse: personal $1-$1,000 (sticker, video, postcard, shirt at $75, drawstring bag $100, we-come-teach at $750/$1,000) and corporate Silver $2,500 / Gold $5,000 / Platinum $10,000. (2017, source: Personal Sponsor Levels; Corporate Sponsor Levels)
- Sponsor outreach tracker lists ~140 companies and ~40 MIT departments/labs with a point person each; applications noted: Garmin ($5,000 ask), North Face ($5,000, 4 weeks), Walmart ($2,500, 60 days), GM (only if $10,000 + support vehicle). Many (HP, Patagonia, REI, L.L.Bean) only fund environment/local causes. (2017, source: Fundraising Companies 2017)
- Grants history (2014 team): applied to ~20 grants; PSC Fellowship and Davis Projects for Peace were rejected; received ~$26k total from Harvard SEAS $5,000, MITFCU $4,000, Edgerton $3,500, DUE $1,000, GEL $300, CAMIT $488, IDEAS partial $250. PSC workshop tips: safety section matters, show community partner, no unsustainable materials. (2014, source: Funding Scheduling; Grant Deadlines; Notes from PSC Workshop)
- 2017 route: DC (June 11) to San Francisco (Aug 19) = 3,578 miles, ~110,000 ft climbing, 54 biking days, 10 weeks, 9 states, 8 learning festivals; nine LFs were planned and Hazard KY was booked first (June 21-22). (2017, source: 2017 Route; Final Blog Post)
- Housing mix 2017: Warmshowers hosts for most nights, plus churches (Colby, Burlington, Ely, Fallon), a hostel (Al's, Farmington MO), campgrounds ($15-$30), a B&B in Hazard KY (Sherry Spradlin charged nothing). The 2016 team's split was ~40% campground / 30% Warmshowers / 20% church / 10% other. (2017/2016, source: 2017 Route; NOTES for 2017)
- Hosts for LFs double as housing: Beth Gudmestad (Loveland CO library) and Ashlea Appelbaum (St Louis Homeschool, 4 nights, meals on teaching days) hosted AND organized festivals. Best-rated LF hosts (past ratings 5/5): Challenger Learning Center KY, Lab: Revolution MO, University Academy KC; lower: Leonardo SLC, Carson City, Exploration Place. (2016-2017, source: School Feedback; Final Blog Post)
- Host outreach tactic: Caity split the route into quarters, used Warmshowers templates (one for hosts taking >=5 people, one for smaller), asked at 2+ hosts per night, picked backup churches/campgrounds, and sent a reconfirmation email about two weeks before. She mis-typed a date twice (check dates!). (2017, source: Tentative Contacts; 3/5/17 Meeting Notes)
- Hard days in 2017: Abingdon-Wise VA (4,764 ft) forced shuttling; rain days in VA; Great Bend to WaKeeney 103 mi; Austin to Fallon NV 110 mi (US-50 "loneliest road") was doable and "rewarding"; Chambers Lake and Steamboat days 4,000+ ft. A tropical storm hit June 22. Google Maps route suggestions were sometimes bad, so use driving directions or local advice. (2017, source: 2017 Route; Direction Advice)
- Daily rhythm lesson: starting all together failed; staggering so the last rider leaves within 2 hours of the first worked; driver of the day takes down tents and cleans/packs; 5 of 9 could drive so non-drivers took dish/lock duties. June 14 tweaks: 6-7am starts, shorter checkpoints, blog/Insta 3x a week, split into 2 groups of 3 for navigation. (2016-2017, source: NOTES for 2017; 6/14 Little Powow Meeting Notes)
- Flats: 2016 used 35 tubes and 5 tires for 9 people (patch twice before replacing); 2017 had 30 flats. Bike maintenance classes ($100-$200) and CPR training were done before leaving. (2016-2017, source: NOTES for 2017; Final Blog Post; Bike Maintenance)
- Food: 2016 plan was sandwiches (tuna/chicken + cucumber), couscous/pasta/rice-beans dinners, ~$10/person/day budget and Gatorade powder; the team said it should have eaten more fruit and fresh food. Hosts' recipes became the "2017 Cookbook". (2016-2017, source: Final List of groceries; Budget on diet; NOTES for 2017)
- Curriculum: four paired workshops in 2017 (catapult/speaker/motor physics; circuits and binary adder; math games; neuroscience/sports science with cow eye or sheep brain dissection). Lesson-plan worksheet, materials lists and the middle-school "four acts" format are reusable; confirm dissection with schools first. (2017, source: Workshop Curriculums (All); Circuit Lesson; Neuroscience Workshop; 5/21/17 Meeting Notes)
- Teach for America endorsed Spokes in 2014 and connected schools; Stanford ran a sister team in 2017 (route shared, funds kept separate; Stanford administration did not want fundraising under their name). (2014/2017, source: SPOKES_TFArecommendation; 3/12/17 Meeting Notes)
- Safety/admin: Emergency Procedures V1.0 gives step-by-step protocols (injury, bike failure, lost riders: wait after 15-30 min, weather) and the handbook contents list (two emergency contacts per rider, insurance/waivers). Liability forms go through Edgerton. (2017, source: Emergency Procedures; 5/21/17 Meeting Notes)
- Alumni network: a 2013-2024 alumni list (mentor-willing flags) feeds the spokes-alumni@mit.edu mailing list and a May alumni BBQ is a cheap way to collect advice. (2022/2017, source: Spokes Alumni 2022; Spokes Reunion BBQ)
- Credentials found in sheets/notes (an Instagram login and a corporate-giving portal password) were NOT copied; the Usernames_Passwords doc was discarded. (2017, source: Usernames_Passwords.docx)
