# Highlights, Spokes 2013 folder (the first Spokes team: SF to DC, MIT + UC Berkeley)

- 2013 raised about $15,650: Pressman $6,000, Fidelity $5,000, Turner's parents $1,500, Draper $1,000, Baker $1,000, Ace Metal Craft $750, plus small gifts; budget $22.6k itemized, $10k extra reserve for car/gas/food/campgrounds (2013, source: Complete Budget)
- Fronted-money log totalled $17,671; van first payment $1,000 came from Fidelity money; fiscal host StartLabs received $13,200 so donations could be tax-deductible (2013, source: Complete Budget / EIN docs)
- Team got an IRS EIN (partnership, banking purposes) 5/28/2013: EIN 46-2852868; not tax exempt (2013, source: Spokes EIN.pdf)
- Mojo/Freeflow bikes: Jamis Aurora ~$950 with 17% discount; Missing Link supplied cycling clothing; a tiny shop quoted $775/bike but was poorly run (2013, source: Remy_s Equipment List / meetings)
- Van decisions: Thrifty rental ~$1,600 + $550 gas = $2,150 for the summer; rental needs insurance per driver; a team member's family van was preferred (2013, source: Meetings 5_6, 5_29)
- Food: 5,000 kcal and 7 L water capacity per person per day, ~$7/person/day in bulk; carry 2 days of food max on Utah/Nevada stretches (2013, source: Food Info / pitch deck)
- Trader Joe's and Whole Foods food requests worked best as a faxed formal letter stating purpose, dates and needs; keep a written agreement (2013, source: Cities and Food / meetings)
- Kickstarter plan: reward tiers $5 to $5,000; advice: huge first day, email 40 people each, strong video, set goal to your network (2013, source: Kickstarter docs / Kevin Rustagi)
- Festival structure: 3 parallel classes, then "Pursuing your passion" project workshop; project workshop did NOT land; team decided on 7/25/2013 to prioritize exposure over mentoring (2013, source: Meetings 7_10, 7_25)
- Confirm every school with a real person by phone/video and a signed schedule with room numbers; get parent consent forms a week ahead (2013, source: Meetings 5_31 / Project-Box notes)
- Classroom management script: explain rules as comedy skits, warning then room change; ask host for a spare room (2013, source: Class Rules and Procedures)
- TFA connections were inconsistent: strong in Oakland/Sacramento/Denver, nothing in Kentucky/Kansas City (2013, source: TFA 5_10_2013)
- Edgerton Center (Peggy) advice: connect with MIT alumni clubs in SF and DC for meals and showers (2013, source: Peggy from Edgerton 5_2_2013)
- Publicity: Forbes, Boston magazine, BostInno, VentureFizz, Huffington Post, MIT Admissions blog covered the 2013 team; blogger pitches work best with three tailored reasons (2013, source: Blogs and Campaigns to Pub Us)
- Rules of the road from prep meeting: staggered pace, daily group stretch, bikes locked together under a tarp, 4 L min water, purify always (2013, source: Prep Meeting 6_9_2013)
- Training logging was patchy; require 3 workouts/week and WFR + bike-repair classes up front (2013, source: Meetings 1_23_2013 / Expectations)
- Grant list with amounts: Eloranta $6,000, UROP direct $4,625, TBP $2-5k, Kelly-Douglas $1,000, DUE (got $270) -- verify current deadlines (2013, source: Funding Sources)
- Utah/Nevada/Colorado day-by-day plan names campgrounds and costs; Kansas/Missouri city parks often allow free camping after a call to police (2013, source: Day to day route)
- Dutch teammate raised gear in NL: Gazelle donated a bike, university departments $800 each; free-bike asks worked only with some brands (2013, source: Titiaan Log Outreach)
- Note: OrganizedContacts.xlsx (Phillip/GeneralDocuments) is password-protected and unreadable; ask the 2013 team for the password (2013, source: file check)
