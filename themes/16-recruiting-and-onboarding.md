_Last built: 2026-11-24. Sources: 31 docs, 2 blog posts, 14 emails. Recent years first; every fact has its year._

Naming note: "Spokes 2027" = the team that rides in summer 2027. It was recruited in fall 2026 by the Spokes 2026 team. The 2027 team will recruit **Spokes 2028 in fall 2027**.

## TL;DR for 2027
- **You run the next recruiting cycle in fall 2027, after the trip.** The outgoing team always recruits the incoming one: 2026 recruited 2027, 2025 recruited 2026, 2024 recruited 2025, 2023 recruited 2024. The Crash Course ends with "Step 13: Recruit Spokes 2028" and "Step 14: Adjust Email Lists" (2026).
- **Calendar to copy (the fall 2026 cycle that produced your team):** blast Sep 8-9, info sessions Sep 11 (in person, 1-132) and Sep 12 (Zoom), application due Sun Sep 27, Round 1 Zoom interviews Oct 5-10, Round 2 in-person group interviews Oct 24-25, offers Oct 31, reply due Nov 8, waitlist answer Nov 10, transition meeting Nov 15 (2026).
- **The pitch:** "75 days, 4,000+ miles, DC to SF, STEM learning festivals at schools, libraries and correctional facilities." The only requirement is being an MIT student with a sense of adventure; **no biking experience is needed** (2026, 2025, 2024, 2023). Recruit with a dorm-list blast (Sponge-talk) that opens with a TL;DR line, plus bumps, info sessions, posters and Instagram (2023-2026).
- **Interview for motivation and commitment above all.** Ask about summer conflicts (commencement, jobs, grad school, Camp Kesem); a candidate with a conflict "cannot participate" (2025). Every interview asks "the team must fundraise around $40K — what ideas do you have?" (2026, 2025).
- **Two rounds:** a 30-minute individual Zoom interview that includes a 1-minute mock lesson (3 min prep), then an in-person group case interview built from real trip problems ("Reinvent the Learning Festival", "Broke-Bike Mountain") (2026, 2025).
- **Team size is 7-8 and has been for years** (8 in 2027, 2025, 2024; 7 riders in 2026). Count licenses: MIT caps registered drivers on a rental at 6, 2025 had 7 licensed members, and 2027 has one member without a license (2027, 2025).
- **Onboarding sequence once people accept:** offer, reply by Nov 8, transition meeting, roster to Edgerton, Atlas financial access, MVR background checks, first team meeting and role assignment, all within ~3 weeks (2026).
- **Handoff package from the outgoing team:** the Drive (planning docs back to 2013/14), the Master Guide and 5 sub-guides, a Crash Course deck, an Advice doc, a shared group chat, and an Edgerton meeting. Update the MIT mailing lists (2026).
- **Be a more active handoff team than 2026 felt it was.** The 2026 minutes say it "would help if 26 played more of an active role in informing 27", e.g. looking for department funding for the next team because most of it closes in the fall (2026).
- **Pitfall — roles:** a two-person "Finances" role in 2026 became just "whatever the four card holders do". Make two card holders the finance team on purpose (2026).

## Playbook — how to do it

### A. Recruit (fall, run by the outgoing team)
1. **Set the calendar in August.** Use the 2026 dates. The 2025 cycle slipped: a Sep 29 deadline got a Oct 4 extension, and the group-interview date was struck out and moved (2025). Fix dates before the blast and book rooms (2026 used 1-132 for info sessions, 1-134 for group interviews, 1-135 for the transition meeting).
2. **Build the application form** (Google Form). Past forms collected summer conflicts, IAP/spring schedule and a potential lesson topic (2024 roster columns). Reviewers read each candidate's responses before the interview (2025). The actual 2026 form questions are not in the Drive (only links).
3. **Send the dorm blast** from the team alias (spokes-2026@mit.edu in 2026) to Sponge-talk / bc-talk. Open with a TL;DR line. Use the Q&A format ("Do I need biking experience? No!"). Link 5-7 favorite blog posts (2026: Drew Day 7, Carmen Day 20, Tatiana Day 24, Caroline Day 26, Aarushi Day 39, Nate Day 44, Nunu Day 55). Bumps: info-session day, "happening now" with room or Zoom link, due-tomorrow (2023-2026). Post "Apply to Spokes 20XX!" on the blog and link the team's best posts (2024, 2025).
4. **Hold 1-2 info sessions.** 2026: Fri Sep 11 4pm in person and Sat Sep 12 4pm on Zoom; slides emailed Sep 16 for people who missed them. Deck structure: who (students/bikes/van), what (miles, LFs), when, how (roles: LFs, route, fundraising, housing, gear, budget), what it's like (mi/day, climbing, highlights), the school-year commitment (~4 hrs/week, bike blender at the Edgerton Expo, Midnight Marathon) and FAQ (cost is covered by fundraising; no experience needed; typical day) (2024-2026).
5. **Posters and social.** Fall 2025 posters "GAIN LORE THIS SUMMER" plus six joke panels ("Your new FDOC fun fact", "This is better than any summer internship", "Want gains? Hit leg day for 80 days straight") (2025). Poster the Infinite; apply for a spot (2022-2023 guide). In 2023 the team took over the @mitstudents Instagram for a week and "several applicants first heard of Spokes that way" (2023).
6. **Round 1 — individual interview (30 min, Zoom).** Send an acceptance email with a Calendly link feeding a shared Spokes calendar. Two interviewers per candidate. Block an hour so the last 15 minutes go to a debrief and a slide on the candidate (2025). Use when2meet for interviewers' and debrief times (2025). 2026 script:
   - Intros (3 min): name, pronouns, year, hometown, course; interviewers share a favorite Spokes memory; candidate icebreaker.
   - Motivation (3): what do you hope to gain?
   - Planning (4): roles you're excited about or want to avoid (fundraising, route/housing, LFs, bikes/gear, training, website/media, finances); "~$40K — fundraising ideas?"
   - Trip/team (10): a time you worked on a team and hit a challenge; the role you expect to play and expect from others; challenges on and off the bike.
   - Teaching (5): 1-minute middle-school lesson after 3 min prep (2025 long version: 3-5 min lesson).
   - Logistics (2): year-long commitment, a few hrs/week, ~75-day trip, lighter fall recruitment.
   - Closing (3): questions.
7. **What to look for and red flags (2025 guides).** Motivation and commitment above all (they must plan and fundraise in spring); fundraising experience; creative teaching in the mock lesson; adaptability; teamwork and maturity. Red flags: no gusto about Spokes; can't distill a topic simply; vague or conflict-avoidant team answers. Always ask about conflicts flagged in the application. Biking experience is not required; ask about fitness plans, bike maintenance and camping.
8. **Round 2 — in-person group interview.** 2025 format: 5 min round-table intros; split into two groups; first prompt; trade one person between groups for the second; "no right or wrong answers", watching teamwork and creative, robust answers; last 5 minutes for questions. 2026 prompts: **Reinvent the Learning Festival**; **Broke-Bike Mountain** (arrive at dusk at Breaks Interstate Park; tomorrow to Hazard, KY; one unrideable bike; camped last night; Warmshowers dinner at 6 PM). 2025 prompts: design a 10-3 LF for 40 kids (20 elementary + 20 high school, team of 6) at the public library in Newton, KS; a made-up 106-mile bad day (car breakdown, flats, 90+°F, ~1.5 L water per rider; a strong answer is a pre-made protocol: bikers do X, driver does Y); **The Loneliest Highway in America** (Ely to Fallon NV, ~275 miles in 3 days with no housing). Tell candidates timing early: 2026 asked for conflicts by Fri Oct 16 and sent slot details by Sun Oct 18.
9. **Decide as a team.** 2023's deliberation weighed "value add: diversity, time/commitment" (2023). Also weigh summer conflicts, driver's-license count (MIT cap of 6 drivers per rental), skill spread across roles and class-year spread so graduating seniors don't leave gaps (2025, 2023).
10. **Send decisions.** Templates in the 2026 Interview Doc: Round 1 acceptance, Round 2 acceptance, Round 2 rejection ("strong pool; follow the blog and reapply"), Team acceptance (reply by end of day Nov 8; "Passing the torch, Spokes 2026"), Team waitlist (answer by Nov 10) (2026). 2025's Round 1 rejection: "unfortunately we are unable to offer you an individual interview". The Nov 10, 2023 rejection for the 2024 team encouraged reapplying, and that applicant later made the 2027 team (2023, 2026). Keep rejections warm.
11. **Follow up with recruits.** In 2026 the team answered date questions ("approximately June 1 to Aug 15, subject to graduation, fall jobs, Camp Kesem") and texted/called a recruit who hadn't confirmed. A recruit asked for the other members' names before replying; plan to share them once offers are out (2026).

### B. Onboard the new team (November, right after acceptance)
1. **Roster sheet:** name, email, year, course, driver's license; earlier years also had gmail, phone, potential lesson, summer conflicts and positions (2024-2026).
2. **Transition meeting** (2026: Sun Nov 15, 4-6pm, room 1-135, in person; 2026 team presents the Crash Course). The deck's steps: fundraising, route, LFs, housing, bikes, training, get/pack the van, DC and first LF, first ride, general tips, end-of-trip logistics, recruit next team, adjust email lists (2026).
3. **Roster email to Edgerton** (sent Nov 15, 2026). Marvelin's welcome (Nov 19) set out the new team's process: get a bicycle quote early (Edgerton Building Clubs and Teams Showcase, **Apr 8, 2027**); travel cards and ProCards (Marvelin is travel delegate/approver; holders must be "responsible, responsive, punctual"); Atlas financial access to cost object #2530032 (granted Nov 24); MVR via MIT Background Check for anyone driving rentals (valid unexpired license or learner's permit; MVR requested Nov 24 for 7 of 8); first meeting with Justin Schmidt and Marvelin via Edgerton Bookings (week of Nov 30 or later); **pick a team safety officer** as Justin's point of contact (2026).
4. **First team meeting and role assignment** (2027: Sun Nov 22, before Giving Tuesday Dec 1). The master guide says: first meeting in November, read all master guides, assign roles before Thanksgiving if possible, pick rough dates in December, meet weekly, Zoom over IAP (2026, 2025).
5. **Hand over resources:** Google Drive; Master Guide + 5 sub-guides (route/housing, gear/car, LF/workshops, media/comms, fundraising/budget); Crash Course; "Spokes 20XX Advice" doc; shared group chat with the outgoing team; "Past Members' Information" table (2026).
6. **Set team norms early.** The 2026 team's first meeting (Dec 13, 2025) wrote: CC everyone on every email; vibe checks; reply within ~24 hrs or at least acknowledge; resolve conflict honestly and proactively; "keep the messiness within the family"; decide through discussion, not voting. They also listed non-negotiable dates (commencement May 27-29; Camp Kesem Aug 16; a job start Aug 31) before fixing trip dates (2025).
7. **Mailing lists (WebMoira):** spokes-current@mit.edu owns spokes-alum, spokes-alumni, spokes-interest, spokes-updates, spokes-present and mitspokes@. Each year add the new team and remove the old; create spokes-20XX@mit.edu, add members and make it self-owned (2022, 2023). The 2026 Crash Course repeats this for 2028: move gmails to spokes-2027-alumni, make a spokes-2028 list, make spokes-current owned by spokes-2028 (2026).

## What worked / what didn't
### 2026 (recruiting the 2027 team)
- ✅ One Q&A-style blast with real blog links, then bumps (Sep 11, 12, 16, 26) and slides sent afterward.
- ✅ Two rounds with a clear email for every step; Calendly for Round 1; Round 2 slots announced two days after conflicts were due.
- ✅ Case prompts drawn from real trip problems doubled as a preview of daily-ride logistics.
- ✅ Warm follow-up with recruits (calls/texts, a waitlist deadline) and a fast handoff: offer Oct 31, transition meeting Nov 15, Edgerton intro Nov 15, first meeting Nov 22.
- ⚠️ The team wished it had informed 27 more actively, e.g. chasing department funding; the Course 7 deadline was missed in 2026 and 2027 must restart it (2026).
- ⚠️ A safety-officer role was wanted for 27 as a direct line to Edgerton; Edgerton now requires one (2026).
- ⚠️ The license column mattered immediately: Liv can't drive MIT rentals (2026).

### 2025 (recruiting the 2026 team)
- ✅ The best-documented cycle: timeline, long and short interview guides with "must ask" items, group prompts, email drafts, info decks, posters (2025).
- ⚠️ Deadline slipped (Sep 29, extended Oct 4) and the group-interview date was struck and moved. Fix the calendar before the blast (2025).
- ⚠️ 3 members had commencement conflicts, only 7 had licenses, and MIT allows 6 registered drivers (2025).

### 2024 (recruiting the 2025 team)
- ✅ The blog post "Apply to Spokes 2025!" linked 5 popular posts as recruiting material; a single weeknight Zoom info session; a ~2-week recruiting window (Sep 17-29, 2024).

### 2023 (recruiting the 2024 team)
- ✅ Three blast emails (Oct 2, Oct 9, Oct 15 "DUE TODAY") and Calendly interviews on Zoom (2023).
- ✅ Instagram takeover of @mitstudents brought in applicants (2023).
- ⚠️ In Feb-Mar 2023 the team was still interviewing extra people for its own team; one candidate was a "No" because March was unavailable. Late recruits must have the time (2023).
