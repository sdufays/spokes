## Playbook — how to do it

### A. Accounts, access & handoff (do first)
1. **Inventory every account** and who holds it: Instagram **@mitspokes**, TikTok (created May 5, 2023 by Joy), WordPress site (shared login `spokes-current@mit.edu`), legacy Wix (`spokes-2022@mit.edu`, hosts old team sites), Warmshowers account, YouTube, Venmo handle, Google Drive "Social Media, Blog, Website, Media" folder (2022 Drive). Passwords are redacted in the repo; ask the previous team/Edgerton. (2026, 2024, 2023) The 2017 hub publicity outline wanted *one* official name/username/mailing list for all channels and a shared account spreadsheet — still a good idea. (2017)
2. **Mailing lists (WebMoira, groups.mit.edu/webmoira):** after accepting the class create `spokes-20XX@mit.edu`, add the team, make it self-owned; hand off by adding next year's list to `spokes-current@mit.edu`, removing your own, and changing owners. Also owned by spokes-current: `mitspokes@`, `spokes-updates@` (used in 2022 to email trip blog posts to subscribers), `spokes-alum@`, `spokes-alumni@`, `spokes-interest@`. (2026 master guide; 2022)
3. **Website domain mitspokes.com:** renews each April (2019: $14.95; 2023: $17.35; 2026: $13 on WordPress). In 2024 the Wix plan + domain expired **Apr 23** — renew before then or lose the domain. The domain is pointed at the previous team's site; point it at yours, move last year's site to an archive/subpath (e.g. mitspokes.com/2023), and **preserve the entire past site**. Domain moves were "complicated and poorly documented"; customer support got them done. (2026, 2024, 2023)
4. **Platform history & conflict:** 2019–2024 teams used **Wix** (2019 Premium $168/yr; 2023 $178.50/yr); the 2024 team told 2025 "move website off Wix (got much more expensive)"; 2025 notes: transfer domain off Wix, consider static site generation; 2026 uses **WordPress** (and the 2026 master guide says WordPress is "now" used). Note the master guide text carried into the 2023/2024/2025 copies already says WordPress, since it is a living doc edited through 2026. (2026, 2025, 2024)
5. **Blog author attribution:** on Wix, add every member as a "managed writer" (Dashboard → Blog → More Actions → Create new writer) so posts show the right author; on WordPress give each member an account/author tag (2026 posts are tagged by author). (2024, 2023, 2022)

### B. Pre-trip content calendar (Sept → May)
1. **Fall recruiting (Sept):** posters/flyers + info session (2025: "GAIN LORE THIS SUMMER", info session Sept 24, 4 PM, 1-132; poster set with tongue-in-cheek lines); recruiting blog post with deadline (2024: apps open mid-Sept, close Sept 29, Zoom info session Sept 23); email blasts; best-known blog posts linked as recruiting material. Instagram/@mitstudents helped: in 2023 "several applicants first heard of Spokes" from the @mitstudents takeover. (2025, 2024, 2023)
2. **Team bios & photos (Jan–Feb):** a Google Form asking each rider for a bio (name, year/course, hometown, activities, fun facts, why Spokes/what they're excited for) and **up to 10 photos, with one marked "main"** (Instagram carousel = 10 images; website needs one). Use it for Instagram intros and the website About page. Form used Jan 2025; Feb 22, 2024; 2023 prompt required 3+ photos incl. a headshot. (2025, 2024, 2023)
3. **Team photoshoot** early (2024: March, Dropbox folder; 2024 asked a friend with a camera). Group photo in front of the Great Dome (2024 plan). (2024)
4. **Post individual intros at start of spring semester**, then semi-frequent posts (training rides, meetings, planning). 2024's calendar for Feb 19–Mar 1: Feb 19 team presentation ("when you and 7 strangers decide to…"), Feb 20 start challenge, Feb 22 route presentation, Feb 24 trend, Feb 26 solo Blue Bikes tour, Feb 28 "get ready with me to go biking", Mar 1 something light; hook "Hi, we're back. Did you miss us?"; double-post to TikTok; accounts held by several people work better. (2026, 2024)
5. **Fundraising posts (spring):** donate poster + crowdfund progress graphic (2026: "our goal $50K — crowdfund.mit.edu/story/spokes-s26 — mitspokes.com"; progress graphic framed as $15K friends/family + $35K departments/corporations); raffle post and business card (2026); dare/bingo graphics (2024 Midnight Marathon Bingo with $5/$10/$25/$50 tiers; 2024 Bingo Dare Mar 18-22; 2025 bingo with $3/$5/$10 tiers); Edgerton Showcase flyer with Venmo/website/mission (2026, 2025). **Everyone reposts to their own stories.** (2026, 2025, 2024)
6. **Collab posts with @mitedgerton** (via Sonny Oram) for fundraising and marketing; 2022 also asked @mitstudents for a pt-1 (give us money) and pt-2 (on the road) feature and took over their account Apr 4-10, 2022 plus a summer week; @mitpics reposted 2022 posts. (2026, 2022)
7. **Sponsor thank-yous:** Edgerton Center + MIT ESG graphics (2022), sponsor graphics on the website (website role, 2027), restaurant-meal thank-you posts (2026), Trek/Cambridge Bicycle (2026–27).
8. **Pre-trip blog post(s):** first blog (2026: Trek built all 7 bikes, Husky Mind interview, route finalized, LF topics, crowdfund link); Day −96 intro post (2024); "Spokes is happening" restart post (Jan 20, 2022). (2026, 2024, 2022)
9. **Shirts & jerseys:** see section D.

### C. On the trip — blog/Instagram schedule
1. **Blog schedule:** 2026 rule — post two days after the day covered, consistent schedule; assign each member blog days (6/23/2026 note lists Aarushi, Caroline, Carmen, Nunu, Drew, Nate) and **block writing time** (2026 dropped two riders at a community center for 2 hours to write blogs). 2017 plan: blog/vlog + Instagram **3×/week and after learning festivals**, "first thing after showering," each person posts the day before they drive, peer-edit. (2026, 2017)
2. **Pitfall:** teams fall behind (2024 advice; 2026 had no Blog Lead — "could be good because we fell super behind"). The 2026 archive has 77 posts (Day 1 on 2026-06-01 … Day 73 on 2026-08-12), yet "Day 74: One last century of sidequesting" is dated 2026-11-17, ~3 months after the trip ended Aug 16. (2026, 2024)
3. **Instagram on the road:** weekly photo dump with short caption, plus one post per LF and per sponsoring restaurant; Stanford Spokes posted daily in 2025 and "worked well." Take lots of photos/videos at LFs. Trek offered to repost trip photos (2026). (2026, 2025)
4. **Video ideas** (2023 Joy's scripts): hook "392 spokes" (7 bikes), packing a bike for a flight, fixing a flat, a rider's 200-mile fundraiser ride, "imagine if middle school looked like this" LF montage. One 33-second reel got ~1k views/40 likes; the same video got ~2 TikTok views. 2024: weekly vlogs on camcorder; documentation gear researched (Canon R10 ~$1,000 vs Rebel T7 ~$600, drones $250–$530, SD cards, GoPros); team decision "1-2 GoPros, 1 mic, cheap tripod." 2013: Nathan carried pounds of camera gear in panniers — plan pannier weight for a media rider. (2023, 2024, 2013)
5. **Hosts:** handwritten thank-you card the morning of each host (the driver's job), and keep a running host photo/address log. (2026, 2017)
6. **Contact-form tracking:** 2019 kept a sheet of every inbound website message (name, summary, point person, status) — host offers, meetups, workshop interest; copy it. (2019)

### D. Merch: jerseys, team/LF shirts, stickers
1. **Jerseys — Jakroo** (custom, online design lab; contacts/other.md): not an MIT-approved vendor → one-time license agreement; pay by ProCard. 2026 chose two designs (black for cold, white for hot) and put rider names on; 2026 sketches were inspired by a Primal Wear jersey. 2019 advice: order a size bigger (they run small and shrink). Get sizes by Google Form (cut: men/women; fit: standard/slim; size). (2026, 2025, 2019)
2. **Sponsor logos on jerseys:** the 2026 donor package gives jersey logo placement (large at $10K+, medium $5K, small $2.5K) — design the jersey around sponsor commitments, so decide sponsors before the design is final. (2026) 2022 levels: apparel logo at 3 of 4 tiers. (2022)
3. **LF/team shirts:** 2024–2026 teams bought polyester/short-sleeve tees ("great for LFs and PJs") — 4imprint (2025, 2024), CustomInk (2026). 2022: CustomInk ProCard order was cancelled and redone at Rush Order Tees on a personal card. 2026 design: first names on back (no big number), "DC to SF, symbols of learning festival." JD designed the 2024 shirt. (2026, 2025, 2024, 2022)
4. **Question raised in 2026:** whether to remove "MIT" from LF jerseys (checked off, outcome not recorded). (2026)
5. **Stickers:** 2022 bought custom die-cut stickers from Sticker Mule after the trip ($125). (2022)
6. **Laundry loops** — everybody has two jerseys; plan wash cycles. (2026)

### F. Press & media
1. **Campus/student press:** The Tech interview (April 2026, with a 2025 alum — good press-kit language: route-choice method, anaphylaxis story, fundraising pitch); MIT News Q&A (2022, post-trip, 9 LFs); MIT Admissions blog (2013); Husky Mind podcast YouTube interview (2026). Prepare a one-page "basic info" block (team names/courses + mission paragraph). (2026, 2022, 2013)
2. **Local press along the route:** pitch each overnight town's paper/TV in advance with a one-paragraph blurb (2018 template: 8 MIT students, 3,500 miles, 75 days, 9 LFs; "even getting the word out helps"); use host/alum/LF contacts — **in 2022 an MIT alum (Sam Ford) set up the Bowling Green LF and got two TV stations to film workshops; in 2017 hosts Brad & Amy Ayer called the Henderson, KY news station**. News-tip form blast to ~10 Boston/Austin outlets (2018); tip lines again listed pending in 2026. (2022, 2018, 2017)
3. **2013 press model:** ranked outlet list with owner per contact; pitch = one-sentence connection, why you like their blog, what Spokes is, three reasons it fits, offer photos, launch date; Forbes, Boston magazine, BostInno, VentureFizz ran stories; "Spark Truck" as the hook. (2013)
4. **Copyright hygiene:** the 2026–27 site was hit with an AP image claim — use only photos you own/license (team photos, Creative Commons), and keep a record of image sources. (2027)
