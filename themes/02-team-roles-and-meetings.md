## Playbook — how to do it

### 1. Assign roles (Nov-Dec)
1. Read the role descriptions (handed down from 2026; in the Master Guide and your 11/22 notes). Use the recommended headcount as a starting point, not a rule (2026).
2. Spread people across roles; avoid one person owning 4 roles (2027 table flags this). Make sure every sub-team has someone who is *not* also the finance cardholder, so purchases get a second check (2026).
3. **Finances = the card holders.** 2026 assigned Finances to two people, but it "basically just became the responsibility of the 4 card holders" because they didn't understand how it differed from Fundraising and Budget; advice: "have the card holders automatically be the Finances team" (2026, repeated in the 2027 notes). 2026 card holders were Nate, Caroline, Carmen (travel) and Drew (pro) (2026). 2025 split: ProCard = Ruth, Ishaq; Travel = Sarah S., Joseph (2025).
4. **Budget needs 3 people**, not 2 — the role "often has to make decisions that affect the whole team about whether we can make purchases" (2026 note in minutes 12/13/25).
5. **Housing roles got shared on the trip** — the team switched who handled housing state by state (2026, minutes).
6. Add roles the Master Guide says were missing: **Blog lead** (2026: blogs fell far behind), **Documentation** (writes down advice for the next team), **Daily Route Planner** (alternate so everyone can do it) (2026 Master Guide).
7. Keep an eye on **Edgerton-facing roles**: Edgerton contact (answers Edgerton emails; can delegate), Car rental (background checks, accident reporting), Social media (liaison to Edgerton communications), Gear (communicates with Edgerton/MIT to purchase bikes) (2026).
8. **Name a safety officer** (2027 request from Edgerton). 2026 suggested ideas for safety training: dog training, first aid via MIT EMS (2026, 10/8/26 meeting).

### 2. Run weekly meetings (Nov onward)
1. **Pick one meeting time for the semester** (2026 chose Monday evening + dinner; used when2meet on 3/9/26 to find extra times) (2026).
2. **Moderator/notetaker role**: watches the clock, makes sure agenda items are discussed, sends the agenda, takes notes (Master Guide 2026; 2027 assigns Noah). 2026 originally split this in two (moderator vs notetaker) (2025/2026).
3. **Agenda format that worked (2026):** heading with date + attendees → Agenda (numbered, items struck through when done) → "Subteam updates" (Finances, Gear, Route, Fundraising, LFs, Housing, Social media/website) → Notes → **Action items as "Name: task"**, struck through when done. 2022 used: Agenda / Standup-Updates / Actionables / Last Week's Actionables (2022). 2017 used a "Team Charter" sheet with per-person weekly goals, deadlines, done flags (2017).
4. **Open each meeting with a short bonding activity** (2026: 10 min, led by Aarushi) (2026).
5. **Meetings during IAP:** 2027 plan is Zoom over IAP + a winter-break meeting + an end-of-IAP in-person planning session (2027). 2023 did daily 9am coworking on Zoom in January for email-sending sprints (2023).
6. **Side meetings:** fundraising people "meet sometimes outside of weekly meetings" (2026). 2024 ran a separate biweekly fundraising meeting (2024).
7. **Keep a shared Google Calendar** (Master Guide, January; 2026 Nunu made one on 12/13/25) (2026).
8. **Use work sessions when a task is large:** 2026 route team split the route in thirds (Aarushi days 1-25, Drew 26-50, Carmen 51-75) and reconvened; housing did a Wednesday 5pm "reach out to 5 hosts each" work session (2026). 2024's J-WEL application was divided in a task table with names + deadlines (2024).

### 3. Set team norms (by Feb; revisit on the trip)
- **2026 norms (agreed 2/2/26):**
  1. Consistently show up to group dinners and the weekly Monday meeting
  2. Communicate ahead of time if you can't make a meeting
  3. Respond promptly to emails
  4. CC everyone on every email
  5. Be present when we hang out
  6. Vibe checks and checking in with each other
  7. GAF (give a f—)
  8. Give each other the benefit of the doubt ("we are all human, up to a certain point")
  9. Encourage each other
  10. Respond to texts in a timely manner (~24 h, or acknowledge you've read it)
  11. Be honest, proactive and communicative about resolving conflicts
  12. Keep the messiness within the family
  13. Make decisions through discussion and communication, **not voting** (2026)
- **2025 trip norms (set June 6, 2025, day 3):** receipts filed within 2 days by purchaser; never buy gas on the ProCard; Travel card = flights, Airbnbs, campgrounds, restaurants; Pro = groceries; neither card is tax-exempt outside MA; personal-gear refunds case by case; money decisions go "small group → large group" depending on how many people are affected; driver checks tomorrow's route the night before; share expertise across teams; be concise; offer to help hosts; don't buy glass; consolidate belongings; clean up after yourselves; 4 walkie-talkies + buddy system; pump tires daily to ~45 psi; if RideWithGPS won't finish a route, write out directions (2025).
- **2023 group norms (June 5, 2023):** lunch consistently at 12 pm regardless of miles; no dropping riders; go as fast as you want uphill but regroup at the apex; daily debrief at 9 pm; driver shops for dinner, preps next-day lunch, and cooks dinner; repair kits in saddle bags; med kit = Andrew unless he's driving, then Lucy (2023). Plan **90 minutes from alarm to everyone on bikes** (2023).
- **2022 expectations (5/31/22):** 100% communication; "if you go somewhere, tell someone / don't go alone"; daily dinner meeting + weekly dinner meeting for meal plan; established lunch location; build in extra time ("things take longer than we think"); a "time tsar"; driver duties (set up site, replenish water, laundry, ice); a **daily stand-up** (route/schedule, meal, planned stops for tomorrow); ride in groups of 2-3 with the last 2 staying together; whoever doesn't cook cleans; cook and clean in pairs (2022).

### 6. On the trip: roles, rotations, check-ins
- **Driver (rotates daily):** groceries/shopping, water stops for riders, most van packing in the morning, laundry the night before their driving day, thank-you card the morning of, misc tasks (2025 list in the 2026 Master Guide; 2022 driver duties: set up site, water, laundry, ice). Driver can be "one rest stop ahead" of riders (2026 reflection with Manaas, 5/4). Rotation sheets: 2024 (Driving, Blog, Laundry sheet; laundry order never filled), 2014 four drivers alternate so a driving day doubles as a rider's rest day (2014), 2016 five drivers rotated (2016), 2022 roughly each person drives every ~6 days (2022).
- **Dinner lead:** 2026 cooked **zero** meals; one person called places every night asking for free food ("extremely successful") (2026 Master Guide). Past teams mostly cooked (2026).
- **Laundry** alternates; use laundry loops (2026). **Thank-you card** for every host, written by the driver that morning (2025/2026).
- **Blog lead / blog rotation:** writer rotates daily, mix drivers/passengers/bikers (2026 Master Guide). 2026 set "two days after day-of, consistent schedule" on 6/23 (2026); 2022 posted next day with "Day N: title" and the date in the body (2022); 2017: 3x/week and after learning festivals, peer-edit (2017); 2024 had a blog order sheet (2024).
- **Daily route planner:** alternate people (2024/2025 note); route for tomorrow checked the night before by the driver (2025).
- **Morning routine (2026, 6/8):** getting dressed → pack bags and put them outside → breakfast (everyone pauses when it's ready) → thank-you card → pack toiletries basket → bring laundry basket → load car → bike rack on → cooler + snack box → pump tires → fill personal + group bottles → download route → sunscreen → personal snacks. Cooler: sealed items at the bottom, permeable at the top; **driver cleans and repacks cooler** morning and night (2026).
- **What's covered (2026 "money matters", 6/1):** breakfast, lunch, dinner, group snacks, and anything bought for the whole group; not covered: whole-group leisure/luxury purposes (2026).
- **Pacing/breaks:** rest stops ~15-20 min; wait at most ~30 min at the end of the day for other groups; "try to be maximally inclusive"; eat lunch together at rest stops; dinners together (2026, 6/8 meeting).
- **Check-in meetings:** see TL;DR. 2022's week-2 meeting: morning routine, hand signals, blogs, concussion/COVID testing, sleeping arrangements (2022).

## What worked / what didn't

### 2026
- **Worked:** weekly Monday meeting + dinner; subteam update format; 13 written norms; Aarushi's bonding activities; role table in Dec with a transition crash course; asking restaurants for free dinners as a defined role; weekly meetings that doubled as team-bonding (2026).
- **Worked:** "How do we all feel" rounds (Kentucky dog decision, 6/13: each person rated physical/mental state and willingness) leading to the 6/14 decision to rent a pickup so any rider can be shuttled (2026; blog Day 14).
- **Didn't:** the Finances role was unclear and became the four card holders (2026). Blog fell far behind with no blog lead (2026). Budget role needed 3 people (2026). Early-morning routines slipped after rest days (e.g., 9am start on 6/23; a 2am night delayed 6/21 by 90 minutes) (2026 blogs).
- **Friction found:** ride pace/priorities (speed demon vs. "sidequester") needed a 3-hour meeting on day 8 (2026).
- **Edgerton feedback (10/8/26):** transition would be easier if 2026 alumni actively informed 2027; Edgerton really wants a safety officer role (2026).
- **Admin overhead:** MIT auditors reviewing team transactions during the trip added friction; keep receipts organized (2026, Day 24 blog).
