_Last built: 2026-11-24. Sources: 44 docs, 14 blog posts (plus the 78-post tag digest), 2 emails. Recent years first; every fact has its year._

## TL;DR for 2027
- **Drivers need an MVR check from MIT Background Check, and only MVR-cleared students may drive MIT rentals.** Marvelin requested MVRs on 2026-11-24 for 7 of 8 members. Liv Girand has no license and is excluded. Each person must answer the email from Background Check, and the license or learner permit must be unexpired. Chase everyone to finish. Checks took about 1–2 weeks in 2024. (2027, 2024)
- **Gloria Ma is the 2027 Edgerton contact and car-rental lead.** Car rentals and travel dates were not yet finalized as of Nov 15, 2026. Reserve as soon as the Boston departure date is known, because late booking cost "thousands of dollars" in 2023. (2027, 2023)
- **Budget about $6,000 for a one-way minivan, plus about $600 for a cargo van to DC.** Recent actuals:
  - 2026: $5,912 Avis one-way, plus $590 cargo van.
  - 2025: about $6,003–$6,226 for a two-way Enterprise.
  - 2024: about $6,000 Avis.
  - 2023: $5,810.
  (2026, 2025, 2024, 2023)
- **Rent a two-way and drive back** if you can. 2026, 2025 and 2024 all recommend it. 2025 drove the van from SF to Boston in 10 days. 2024 paid about $1,568 in BikeFlights shipping after a one-way rental, and the 2024 and 2023 teams both called one-way "maybe not the best choice." (2026, 2025, 2024, 2023)
- **Do the roadside-assistance workaround before leaving Boston.** Avis lets only the renter plus 4 added drivers call roadside help. Get those drivers added in person at the counter. In 2024 Avis refused to tow because the named renter was biking. Ask whether Enterprise/Avis currently allow more. (2024, 2025, 2026)
- **MIT will not insure or let you rent more than 7 seats.** A 7-seat minivan holds 8 people only with the luggage moved out. Plan a second vehicle or the cargo-van shuttle for the first days. (2025, 2026)
- **Inspect the rental at pickup.** Ask when it was last serviced, photograph scratches, check tires and engine, and look up recalls. 2024 got bald front tires and a recalled hybrid Pacifica. 2025's Pacifica was also recalled, and Enterprise would not renew its contract. (2024, 2025)
- **Do not use REI bike boxes or ship cross-country if you can avoid it.** Air shipping runs about $200/bike. Buy boxes at U-Haul and use BikeFlights. Box size matters more than weight. (2024, 2025)
- **Use the correct card for each expense.** Travel card: flights, gas, rentals booked on a trip. ProCard: supplies. **Never buy gas on a ProCard.** Edgerton books the main rental and pays from the account, not the travel card. (2025, 2024)
- **Register travel with Edgerton at least 2 weeks ahead for out-of-state trips.** Send Kerberos IDs, dates and destination. Do it in the spring; 2026 logged "everyone register travel" on 4/27. (2025, 2026)

## Playbook — how to do it
1. **Set up Edgerton first.** Introduce the roster and ask Marvelin for MVR checks (she sends Background Check the names and Kerberos IDs). Gloria owns this thread. (2027, 2026)
   - Done 11/24/2026 for 7 people.
   - Give Marvelin driver full name, DOB and license state. Keep the sheet of driver info as in 2024.
2. **Decide the shape of the trip logistics early, before booking anything.** (2026, 2025)
   - One-way vs two-way (see below). Dates: 2026 used about Jun 1–Aug 15, arriving in DC May 31, with commencement May 27–29.
   - Who drives the vehicle from Boston to DC. 2026 had underclassmen drive because upperclassmen had graduation. 2026's team had family drive the minivan down.
3. **Pick the vehicle.** Recommended: a 7-passenger minivan. Don't go smaller. (2024, 2023, 2022)
   - Cost: SUVs are 8 seats with more packing space, but cost more ("two SUVs ~$9k" in 2024). Inexperienced drivers raise incident risk. Sandi advised against an SUV.
   - The 2025 meeting notes said "Don't get Chrysler Pacifica/minivan." In practice 2025 got a Pacifica again, and it was recalled.
   - Cargo van: only seats 5. Edgerton might want a second vehicle, or you can "convince" them only 5 will ever ride (2026-10-08 meeting). MIT will not allow a passenger van of 8–9 seats.
   - Avoid an 11–15 seat van. Stanford Spokes 2024 used an 11-seater and called it fine, but MIT restricts rentals above 7 passengers. (2025)
4. **Company choice.** (2026, 2025, 2024)
   - MIT agreements exist with Avis/Budget and Enterprise/National. Both waive the age-25 minimum. Corporate codes are listed in the Travel FAQ (Avis A122600; Budget T240202; Enterprise XZ10MTY for ages 18–20 in MA/NH/ME, XZ10MIT for everyone else).
   - Age rules (Jessica Tam, 2024): renters 21–24 have the young-renter fee waived for business. 18–20-year-olds are waived only in MA, NH and ME and **cannot rent elsewhere**; book via Avis or Budget instead. SUVs and luxury cars need 25+.
   - Avis/Budget was the only agreement that covered under-21 drivers in 2024. The master guide calls it a "bad rental company" and tells you to check whether that has changed.
   - Do **not** buy extra damage or liability insurance. It is not reimbursed and MIT is self-insured. (2025, 2017)
   - Conflict on 2026's company: the 2026 expense sheet logs Avis; the 2026 crash course says "Rent through Enterprise and MUST coordinate with Marvelin." Ask Marvelin which applies in 2027.
5. **Get quotes and decide.** Ask Edgerton for a quote for the specific vehicle and for Boston → DC. (2025, 2024)
   - 2025's finance lead planned to ask for prices for Jun 3–Aug 19 and for a Boston → DC leg (Jun 3–5).
   - 2024's 4/30 Edgerton meeting decided: "go ahead with the rental car, reserve ASAP." The 7-seat SUV option at minivan price was "Chevy Traverse or similar," pick-up at Logan.
6. **Reserve through Edgerton.** (2025, 2026, 2024)
   - Edgerton sets up a direct-bill reservation and pays from the account. The travel card is only for an emergency rental on the trip.
   - Be specific about pickup and drop-off date and time. Only MIT-community members may drive. Avis/Enterprise add extra drivers only **in person at the counter, with a license.**
   - 2026 added a note to email Marvelin about needing everyone at the counter and whether a renter under 25 can pick up.
7. **Add drivers at the counter** before the first week. (2024, 2025, 2026)
   - Avis: renter + 4 only. 2025's Enterprise reservation included 9 additional drivers. The Nov 2025 Edgerton note says "only 6 drivers can be registered … don't let the 7th driver crash." Confirm the current number with Edgerton in writing.
   - Added drivers can call roadside assistance. MVR-cleared non-added drivers can still drive.
   - A 2024 learning: the original renter on the contract was on a bike when the car broke down, so Avis would not authorize a tow; a new rental agreement cost about $2,000 more.
8. **At pickup.** (2024, 2023)
   - Ask when it was last serviced; walk around and photograph all scratches; check engine and tires; look up recalls for the make and model; read the contract.
   - Avis staff in 2024 said renters could not open the hood.
   - Rental cars come without a tire-change kit or spare. Get a written proof of MIT insurance for the glove box if possible.
9. **Racks and storage.** (2026, 2025, 2024, 2016)
   - Hitch racks do not fit most rentals (2016 bought and returned them; 2024's non-hitch rack fell off over a speed bump; 2023 used a trunk-mounted 3-bike rack). Ask the rental company about rack compatibility before booking (2022 swapped cars twice in Denver over a trunk that wouldn't stay open with the rack). 2026 asked for a hitch "but not end of world if no hitch."
   - 2026 bought a Thule Gateway Pro 3-bike trunk rack ($293.42). 2025 bought one for about $240–$300. Test the roof bag on the actual car before packing day. The 2026 minivan had no roof rails, so straps went to the rear window. Remove or check the roof bag before highways: in 2026 it unzipped on I-65 and lost camp gear.
   - Buy hard-sided plastic boxes at Walmart, a hand vacuum, air fresheners, and a hard-sided roof carrier. 2026 packing that fit: LF supplies in 2 bins, kitchen in 1, bike gear/first aid in 1.
10. **Boston → DC.** (2026, 2025, 2024, 2023)
    - 2026: cargo van with bikes plus the minivan. 2025: two cars did not fit all bikes in one trip with two racks; count rack capacity. 2024 used the Tacoma (returned at Dulles). 2023 and 2024 and 2026 all rented a truck/cargo van for the first days.
    - Pack the day before. 2026 packing ran until 3 AM for a 6 AM departure. Flights to DC are cheap if booked early (2026: $145–$361). Rental pickup near Central Square, Cambridge: Enterprise (25 River St) in 2025.
11. **On the trip: driver rotation.** (2026, 2025, 2023, 2022, 2014)
    - One driver per day. Past rotation: ~every 6 days for each driver (2022); "each drove about once a week" (2015).
    - Driver duties: grocery shopping, water stops for riders, most of the packing in the morning, laundry the night before, writing the host a thank-you card the morning of, route checks the night before, cooler cleaning. Alternate so everyone can do it.
    - MIT rule (2017): switch drivers every 4–5 hours; nobody drives alone.
    - A driver being "one rest stop ahead" works for water. If the van says a situation is dangerous and comes back for you, you must get in (2026, 2025).
    - A driving day doubles as a rest day for the rider (2014). Per 2022 insurance quirk, someone "under the insurance policy" could only drive when no one else could; confirm each rider's status.
12. **Gas and cards.** (2025, 2026)
    - Pay gas with the travel card. Receipts within 2 days. Never gas on the ProCard. Expect about $20–$65 per fill, every 1–2 days; 2022 spent $2,569.68 on gas.
13. **Car troubles protocol.** (2024, 2026)
    - Call the 24-hour roadside number on the keys. If none of the added drivers is with the car, call Edgerton (Chris/Marvelin). Avis will usually swap the entire car, which is painful when it is loaded.
    - Keep the vehicle and camp gear with the riders, or within reach. In 2024, the team had no camp gear for the night after the van broke down.
    - Accident: call Edgerton and MIT Insurance, get a police report, fill out the rental company's accident form. Don't give personal insurance, and show the rental agreement.
14. **End of trip: choose early between drive-back and ship.** (2026, 2025, 2024)
    - **Drive-back (recommended):** 2025's 10-day northern route (Aug 21–30) cost $194 in campsites and about $1,464 in total. Have someone local drive the van on the last day so the whole team rides in together (e.g., Charles '25 in 2026 advice).
    - **Shipping:** buy boxes from U-Haul (cheaper than FedEx or UPS), use BikeFlights, allow a full day in SF (REI SF stored half-packed bikes overnight in 2024). Consider checking bikes on planes.
    - Alternatives raised in 2025–2026: ship via MIT mail services or an MIT FedEx account/platform.
    - Return flights: 2022's FAQ made members pay their own return from SFO; 2025 covered flights only up to $400 each. Decide reimbursement for flights **before** the trip (2025 and 2026 advice).
15. **Return the rental.** Fix the drop-off address to the right city well before the end (2026 changed the California drop-off on 4/27). Plan Enterprise renewals every 30 days for long rentals (2025). (2026, 2025)

## What worked / what didn't
### 2026
- **Worked:** One-way minivan plus separate cargo van (Budget Truck, 20% discount, one-way to Rockville, MD) kept costs under $9,300 for transport. 7 MVR-cleared drivers. Packing bins by category. The "van says get in" safety rule.
- **Worked:** Flights bought as one-ways on red-eyes were cheap ($145–$361).
- **Didn't:** The soft roof bag needed a different strap setup than 2025, and later unzipped on I-65. A pickup reserved online was not held at the counter (call to confirm vehicle class). A minivan with 3 people fits no more than a few riders for SAG. (2026)
- **Didn't:** Packing ran until 3 AM; the first ride day (88.6 miles) after sleepless days sent most riders into the van. (2026)
- **Unclear:** the receipts ledger had Avis "just reserved, not paid yet." Check the final amount. (2026)

### 2025
- **Worked:** Two-way Enterprise, 9 drivers included, unlimited mileage; drive-back from SF to Boston (campsites $194, about $1,464 total) replaced shipping for the car and about 5 bikes. (2025)
- **Didn't:** The 2024 Pacifica's side-airbag recall meant the Kansas City branch would not renew the 30-day contract (Day 29, 2025-07-03); Enterprise replaced it by phone with an identical silver hybrid in Denver (2025-07-17). The rental was split into several contracts, so budget for paperwork per segment. (2025)
- **Didn't:** Two cars with two racks still could not carry all bikes in one trip from Boston. In Tillamook the van with the bike rack backed into another car's tail lights. (2025)
- **Didn't:** Several members paid flights personally and were reimbursed late; Ishaq's $446.19 ticket was capped at $400. (2025)

### 2024
- **Didn't:** Avis minivan had bald front tires; a hybrid Pacifica threw an electrical fault in Shenandoah NP; Avis refused to tow because the renter was biking; team rented an Enterprise truck for food and camp gear and needed DC housing for the night; a new rental cost about $2,000 more; Avis refunded 2 days when the team drove the bad car for 5. Only 5 of 8 could call roadside help. (2024)
- **Worked:** The Tacoma pickup for the first days (3 bikes in the bed). Jessica Tam (VP Finance) helped get a discount afterward. (2024)
- **Didn't:** The non-hitch rack fell and dragged bikes over a speed bump. The loaded car got stuck on an unmaintained road (day 52). Shipping from SF: REI boxes were oversized, FedEx/UPS expensive, packing took far longer than expected. (2024)
- **Lesson stated:** Drive the rental back to Boston — cheaper, less complex. (2024)
