## Playbook — how to do it
1. **Assign roles (Gear 2 people).** The 2026 handoff says a gear person should probably hold the pro card (or pair one gear person with one finance person). The role: create the gear list, work with pro-card holders on purchases, and work with Edgerton and MIT to buy bikes. (2026 role description, in 2027 kickoff notes)
2. **Spec the bike (use the master-guide rules).** Gravel bike, disc brakes, drop bars, tires ≥35 mm (2026 used 42 mm), tubeless-ready, **same model for everyone**. (2026 master guide)
   - Why the Checkpoint ALR 3 Gen 3: 1x10 drivetrain (44T ring, 11–48 cassette) eliminates the troublesome front derailleur and gives a huge climbing gear (helped in Appalachia); wide tire clearance matters because a significant minority of time is off-road; Trek was easy to work with and the team "squeezed several hundred dollars" of warranty work out of Trek during the trip. (2026)
   - 2025's Gen 2 was a 2x9 Sora with 46/30 rings; several riders did not realize they had two sets of gears and rode the hardest setting on day 2, so **teach gearing before day 1**. (2025)
   - Compare options on a sheet (the 2026 "Bike Choice" tab compares 11 bikes; 2022's "bike research" compares ~25 with required features). Brake/shifter notes from 2025: Tektro MD-C550 mechanical is solid, Mira is not good, GRX is great; hydraulic brakes brake better but mechanical is easier to repair; Tiagra > Sora > Claris. (2026, 2025, 2022)
   - 2022 lesson: buy closer to $1,000/bike if possible. In Feb 2022 a bike shortage left Giant Revolt 2 as the only plentiful model at Landry's. (2022)
3. **Choose a shop and negotiate.** Trek Cambridge (Alewife) gives ~20% off everything, so use it for bikes and big purchases; Cambridge Bicycle sells small items at wholesale; Landry's gave gear discounts but not on bikes (15% accessories in 2022). Shop around for better prices. (2026 master guide, 2025, 2022)
4. **Size riders early.** 2025 collected height and frame sizes and tried bikes at Trek Cambridge (49/52/54/58) and Landry's (Commonwealth 52–58, Hood Park 52–56). Order ≈ 2×49, 3×52, 1×54, 2×56 for eight riders; the shop recommended per-rider tweaks (shorter stem, cutting a seatpost). Trek offers 30-day returns. (2025)
5. **Pay by purchase order, not card.** Bikes exceed the $3,000 p-card limit, so they need a PO via Edgerton/Buy2Pay (2022 guidance from Sandi; 2026 Trek order paid via Buy2Pay; 2025 invoice paid via MIT Buy2Pay). MIT is tax-exempt ($0 tax on 2016, 2022, 2025 invoices). Requisitions over $10,000 need the "Selection of Source & Price Justification" form (2024). (2022, 2026, 2025, 2024)
6. **Accessories in two batches:**
   - Trek (20% off): helmets, SPD pedals, shoes, tubeless kits, multitools, pumps, sealant, chain lube, replacement parts.
   - Cambridge Bicycle / Amazon: cages, locks, lights, bottles, seat bags, chamois cream. (2026)
7. **Buy personal clothing individually.** Bibs/shorts, jerseys, gloves, sunglasses, sun sleeves and socks are bought individually and reimbursed. 2022 policy: Spokes pays for 1 pair of gloves, glasses, shorts, 1 jersey and 1 reflective jersey per rider (buy yourself, then file an RFP). 2026 budgeted **$350/rider** ($130 × 2 bibs + $30 sleeves + $30 gloves + $30 sunglasses). (2022, 2026)
8. **Get bike fits** at CycleLifePT/Wellness in Motion with Brendan Connor (comes to campus; used 2022, 2023, 2026). 2026 paid $1,150 for 6 people ($200 each, $150 for one without PT) plus $70 for Tatiana's two fits ($35 each). Other fits: hosts in Osawatomie KS gave free fits and maintenance advice (2024), and Howard's Bike Shop re-measured a bike after a crash for free (2026). (2026, 2024)
9. **Build the spare-parts and tool kit before leaving.** See "Templates" for the lists. Minimum per the 2026 workbook:
   - sealant (2 L), injector, valve-core tool and spare cores;
   - tubeless plug kits, spare chains (7), brake pads, shift/brake cables, quick links and tool;
   - multitools and Allen keys, spare tubes (2 per rider is the repeated advice), floor pump with gauge, frame pump, tire booster, derailleur-hanger alignment tool (in Milkdrop). (2026, 2025)
10. **Learn maintenance before the trip.** The minimum from the master guide is to fix flats, tune the derailleur, adjust brakes, and clean/lube the chain. More members who can do it is better. Past options: the 2022 Baker-funded bike safety & repair workshop (Apr 23, 2022; ~50 RSVPs; pizza $200); Boston classes in 2017 (Broadway Bikes 6×2h $200; Wheelworks 2×3h $100; Bikes Not Bombs). (2026, 2022, 2017)
11. **Maintenance routine on the road** (2022 schedule, plus later advice):
    - Daily: feel tire pressure and pump up.
    - Weekly: degrease, clean and lube the chain; tune shifters.
    - Midpoint (near Denver): top off tubeless sealant; check chain and brake pads (>1 mm).
    - 2019 advice: change chains about every 1,000 miles.
    - Clean/lube chains at a host's house (2022 went from Virginia to Missouri without washing chains). (2022, 2019)
12. **Pack with a purpose.** Per-person list: one small carry-on/duffel plus a backpack, 2 bike outfits, and clothes "DON'T OVERPACK" (2026 list: 2 bike shorts, 2–3 underwear, 3–4 socks, 1–2 normal outfits, one set hiking clothes, sandals, long pants for the DOC facility). The car will be full: hard-sided plastic boxes (Walmart), with LF supplies in 2 bins, kitchen in 1, bike gear and first aid in 1. Keep food, water, first aid, pump and spare tubes accessible. Do the packing the day before if possible (2026 packing ran all day and the team slept ~3 AM before a 6 AM departure). (2026, 2024, 2019)
13. **Inventory what you inherit.** Gear is stored in **Milkdrop N52** (the 2025 sheet writes "NE52"); previous team shows you; coordinate with Edgerton for storage. (2026, 2025)
14. **Keep a thank-you loop with shops.** Tatiana's 11/16/2026 thank-you to Trek and Cambridge Bicycle offered trip photos for their social media. (2026, 2027)

## What worked / what didn't
### 2026
- **Worked:** Matching Checkpoint ALR 3 Gen 3 bikes with 1x10 gearing; wide tires handled Kansas gravel and Utah/Nevada; Trek warranties and shops everywhere (Hokie Spokes same-day repair in Blacksburg; Howard's free refit in Bowling Green; Trek honored a 1-year helmet warranty; Trek Highlands Ranch, Grand Junction, Lawrence and Henderson shops did repairs). Bike fits with a PT were useful: Brendan's rule "sharp, repetitive pain means stop riding" was used to SAG a rider at mile 65. Cambridge Bicycle + Trek donated tune-up raffle prizes ($189.99 and $99.99 packages) that raised money. Foam/self-inflating pads, hard-sided boxes, and a cargo van for the first days carried bikes to DC. (2026)
- **Didn't:** Plastic insulated bottles melted ice (bought metal in Moab for $303); about **100 punctures** and a day with **7 flats in ~24 h** near Osage City, KS (tube-equipped tires flatted repeatedly; Nate advised 2–3 spare tubes); a tire slashed (borrowed a wheel from an idle bike, found a shop before closing); Drew's brakes overheated on a steep gravel descent; Drew later bought a second bike at Trek Henderson ($1,945.63 incl. pedals, cages; reason not stated in the sources read); a blown rear tire at Eureka NV needed a replacement driven in from Ely. A derailleur was "missing gears", chain kept falling off (misaligned derailleur, fixed roadside twice). Roof storage bag fell off earlier and unzipped on I-65, losing two pillows, a pad, a camp chair, a hammock and a blanket; stored sleeping bags smelled by evening. Hammocks from home were missing parts and a cold night was poor sleep. Cooling towels did nothing in 100°F heat. Hitch rack struggled with 3 bikes; the minivan had no roof rails so the 2025 roof method didn't work. Bike Equipment ended **~$1,949 over its category plan** (planned $16,930 vs $18,878.68 as laid out in the budget sheet). Pepper spray leaked in a bike bag. (2026)
### 2025
- **Worked:** Trek 20% discount and shop fit recommendations; 30-day returns; a long in-car maintenance kit (degreaser, rags, chains, brake pads, derailleur hanger); personal-gear receipts organized per rider. (2025)
- **Didn't:** Charles's tubeless tire was punctured by a screw and the **plug burst at 45 PSI** (rode in van; a tube is the backup); a gravel descent crash badly hurt Tian; derailleur/cable failures (Greta's rear derailleur cable, Ramona's snapped cable, Ishaq's front derailleur left him in the big ring for 30 miles, Ishaq's rear derailleur broke at stop 4); Ishaq's new tire slashed by metal on Kolob Terrace Road; spokes broken (repaired in Charlottesville). Riders were unsure how to remount tubeless tires: seating one took several tries at ~150 PSI with the tire booster. Propane stove and new stove failed for days (fixed with a small outdoor-store canister; test the whole cooking setup first). Gear left on car roof (a glove) fell off the highway. Fenders and satellite phones ($400 each) were considered and not bought. (2025)
### 2024
- **Worked:** Specialized Diverge E5 at ~$975 (special order, vs MSRP $1,299.99) from Landry's; Lumina 900 front lights; per-rider gear checklist; inherited Park Tool kit from 2023; hosts in Osawatomie, KS fit bikes and taught maintenance; Winchester VA shop checked/fit bikes on a rest day. (2024)
- **Didn't:** A non-hitch bike rack fell off over a speed bump and dragged two bikes (Day 73); no pump on one ride, and a plug failed because the tire was **not actually tubeless** (Day 10); branches on the Katy Trail damaged derailleurs; a bent derailleur hanger (3×) on the Conifer climb (carry a spare); a stuck chain could not be fixed roadside because the toolbox was left behind (keep tools in the van); a hand pump only reached ~40 psi; discontinued Garmin Edge units bought online had only a Europe map; three Colorado shops gave conflicting advice on brake pads (bring the part number); a subpar shop repair left a bike unusable (ask the mechanic to test-ride); forgetting a front light before dark; shipping bikes from SF REI took a full day. Rental van tires were bald (see theme 12). (2024)
### 2023
- **Worked:** Same bike for everyone (7 Giant Revolt 2) allowed part swapping; Sprintech mirrors "quite nice". (2023)
- **Didn't:** MSW frame pumps and the 100-lumen lights were weak ("GET ANOTHER ONE"); sunscreen was used a lot. (2023)
### 2022
- **Worked:** Foam sleeping pads were the team's MVP; Baker-funded tools became the roadside toolbox; tubeless setup meant only about 10 flats across 6 bikes in ~80 days; Ted's Bicycles in Colorado Springs ($419.28: shifter, wheel true, cables); high-visibility vests worked on I-70 shoulder; Sprintech mirrors great. (2022)
- **Didn't:** Rack didn't fit the Subaru Ascent and the first rack was replaced on day 2 ($263 logged); MSW Tigermoth lights tiny and dim; MSW pumps broke; shifter fixed with an Allen key wedged in the derailleur (temporary); cracked helmet found at lunch; a deer collision destroyed a helmet (~$100 Bontrager WaveCel replacement); lost items over the trip (chargers, socks, glasses, shoes, two water bottles that fell off bikes); a pump broke on a gravel road after tires were deflated. (2022)
### 2019 and earlier
- **2019:** Order bikes ASAP and make sure they're good; waterproof lights; only 2 bike outfits; double shorts and Chamois Butt'r for sore seats; lost receipts folder plus bike lock keys mid-trip. Jamis Renegades "complained about breaking" (2018 and 2019, unsure if bike or maintenance). Repairs $2,815. (2019, 2022 note)
- **2018:** Ordered only in May after funding clarity; advice: order earlier, start with a rest day in DC to learn bikes, always helmets, lights, tire/brake/chain checks. (2018)
- **2017:** 30 flats; thorny trails (Flint Hills, "Natural Earth" section) caused 10+ punctures; free shop tune-ups along the route; bike was stolen pre-departure. (2017)
- **2016:** Bulk order with 20–25% discount; backup tires and tubes bought for 11 bikes. (2016)
- **2013:** Derailleur worked loose near Bailey CO with no tool or expertise; "chafing butter is absolutely critical"; bib shorts better; Smartwool socks; food/water rule of thumb 7 L capacity/person/day. (2013)

(2026, 2024, 2023)
