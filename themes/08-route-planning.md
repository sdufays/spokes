# Route planning — how routes are chosen, tools, each year's route & mileage, rest days, hazards
_Last built: 2026-11-24. Sources: ~45 docs, ~110 blog posts (read through `blogs/by-tag/weather.md`, which carries per-post route/hazard notes for 2013–2026), 0 emails (none of the email digests cover routes). Recent years first; every fact has its year._

## TL;DR for 2027
- **Reuse the DC → San Francisco route on US Bike Route 76 (TransAmerica) to Colorado, then the Utah national parks and US-50 across Nevada.** Teams have narrowed and de-risked it each year, and hosts, learning-festival (LF) partners and known hazards are documented. Take the Katy Trail across Missouri. Use the 2026 sheet as the base. (2026, 2025, 2024)
- **Order of planning is LFs → housing → route.** The 2026 team found route and housing kept clashing with LF dates and set that priority in March 2026. Have the *general* route done by end of IAP (late January) so LF outreach (late January) and housing outreach (Warm Showers + campgrounds from February) can start. (2026, 2025, 2024)
- **Find housing before locking towns.** Keep the Warm Showers map, Google Maps and USBRS maps open together while routing. 2026 routed first, then had to change several nights for lack of hosts. Use at least 2–3 people on route/housing (2026 had 3: one on Warm Showers, one on campsites). (2026, 2024)
- **Typical numbers:** ~4,000 miles, ~170,000 ft of climbing, 72–73 mi/day average, ~3,000–3,300 ft/day; 75 days = ~55 biking, ~10 rest, ~10 teaching. About 10 LFs is "solid". (2026, 2025)
- **Known bad days to change or avoid (details below):** Skyline Drive climb/descent (VA), Breaks Interstate Park campground and the Big A Mountain / "Hell for Certain Road" area (VA/KY), gravel descents anywhere (Utah/Colorado), US-285 out of Denver, US-50 out of Tahoe, Hwy 85 south of Denver, Rt 191 out of Moab, I-25 and I-70 sections, KS-68 out of Osawatomie. (2026, 2025, 2024)
- **Heat, storms, wind and dogs are the real hazards, more than distance.** Leave 5–6 am on hot days (100–108°F in KS/UT/MO in 2026, 2025, 2024); check radar/SPC at every rest stop; Kentucky dogs triggered a team safety debate in 2026; Nevada US-50 and Grand Junction → Moab have 40–75 mile stretches with no services or ice. (2026, 2025, 2024)
- **Plan rest days in places worth resting in** (Afton VA, Bowling Green KY, Kansas City/St. Louis, Colorado Springs, Moab, Capitol Reef, Bryce, Zion, Lake Tahoe, San Francisco). Short ride days can replace full rest days (Mammoth Cave → Bowling Green, 31–35 mi). (2026, 2025, 2024)
- **Book campgrounds/national-park sites in February** (Moab, Zion, Bryce, Capitol Reef, Big Meadows, Lava Point fill first); buy an America the Beautiful pass (2 passes can cover 8 riders for per-person fees). (2026, 2024)
- **MIT only allows 7 seats in one vehicle**, so route legs where the van must carry more than that (shuttles, 2 vehicles) need a plan; Edgerton wants a safety-officer role for 2027. (2026)
- **Give every rider the right to skip any day they feel is unsafe**, and keep the rule that if the van calls a situation dangerous you must get in. (2026)

## Playbook — how to do it

### 1. Pick the base route (Nov–Jan)
1. **Start from the 2026 route sheet** ([2026 workbook](../docs/2026/route-planning/2026_2026_route_master_sheet.md), [2026 Route to Share](../docs/2026/route-planning/2026_route_to_share.md)) and the master guide ([Route Planning and Housing Master Guide](../docs/master-guide/sub-guides/guides/2026_route-planning-and-housing-master-guide.md)). The guide explicitly recommends "a similar route" because documentation, hosts and LF relationships exist. (2026)
2. **Direction:** DC → San Francisco. Some past years went SF → DC for tailwinds, but the 2025/2026 crash courses call it more upfront logistics and don't recommend it (2026, 2025, 2024). 2022 FAQ lists north (popular, more isolated), middle (the Spokes standard), south (hot, "recommend against") (2022).
3. **Follow USBR 76 (Virginia → Colorado).** USBR 76 is divided into 12 sections, 12 in VA counting down; follow it more closely through Virginia to avoid extra mountains (2025 recommendation; 2024). Take the **Katy Trail** (≈240–260 mi rail trail across Missouri, fine gravel, no water fountains) (2026, 2025, 2024). Flint Hills Trail (118 mi, Osawatomie → Council Grove KS) is bike friendly but floods/turns muddy (2024).
4. **Choose "highlight" stops deliberately:** Utah's "Big 5" (Zion, Bryce Canyon, Capitol Reef, Canyonlands, Arches) were hit in 2026 and 2025 (and 2023–2024). Add Mammoth Cave, Great Basin (Baker NV), Lake Tahoe. 2026 added more off-route city visits and national parks, increasing mileage (2026).
5. **Compare alternatives with numbers** (miles, climb, % unpaved), as the 2025 team did: 2024 route 3,553 mi / 150k ft / 5% unpaved; DC–Columbus–St. Louis–Colorado Springs–Moab–SF 3,344 mi / 128k ft / 4%; "The North" via Cleveland–Chicago–Des Moines–Badlands–Yellowstone 3,400 mi / 127k ft / 20% unpaved ([brainstorm](../docs/2025/route-planning/2025_bike-route-brainstorming.md)) (2025). 2019 compared SF vs. Portland endpoints (SF: Yosemite/Tahoe, 2 fewer days, higher elevation; Portland: Yellowstone/Tetons, +2 days, gentler) and chose SF ([Ideas](../docs/2019/route/2019_ideas.md)) (2019).
6. **Priority order when things conflict:** 1) learning festivals, 2) housing, 3) route (2026 meeting minutes, March 2026).

### 2. Build the daily route (Jan–Mar)
1. **Tools** (2026, 2025, 2024):
   - **Ride With GPS** (2026 had a shared Pro account set up by Carmen McCay; free tier works; daily route collections: [2026](https://ridewithgps.com/collections/9887125?privacy_code=AeJqH2wbeCqRhCKUSOWwa9z9ftOzTzU6), [2025](https://ridewithgps.com/collections/4376129)). Export GPX for bike computers; charge computers every few days.
   - **Strava premium** (one person) to see paved/unpaved surface; 2023 built routes with Strava premium plus Google My Maps ([How we made route](../docs/2023/route-planning/2023_how-we-made-route.md)); 2024 used Komoot for planning and Google Maps to navigate (2024).
   - **Google Maps** (street view for road type and shoulder width; reroutes; download offline maps), **Google My Maps** (2024/2025 shared map, each person adds stops on their own layer).
   - **Adventure Cycling** USBRS maps; **Warm Showers** host map.
   - A 2025 GPX of the whole route is saved at [`media/2025/maps/spokes-2025-frfr-route.gpx`](../media/2025/maps/spokes-2025-frfr-route.gpx) (9,137 track points, 2,315 turn cues) to import as a starting track (2025).
2. **Split the analysis.** 2026: Aarushi, Carmen and Drew each reviewed a third of the route by 2/19, borrowing mostly from 2025, then met to agree changes (2026). The route team lists 2025 hazards before reusing a track — e.g., the 2025 track added 7 unnecessary miles for an ice-cream shop after the Ohio River bridge; 2026 skipped it (2026).
3. **Cross-check every day** with 2 sources. Garmin routed a 2025 rider into a building; Ride With GPS had a road near mile 33 on Colorado Springs → Denver that doesn't exist (2025); Google Maps put 2022 riders on a major highway, a one-way street, and through a train yard (2022); Google routed the 2025 van onto a closed muddy road in MN and a 2024 "avoid interstates" van setting onto boulders in Utah (2025, 2024). Check terrain/satellite view and ask locals; the gravel road recommended by an Escalante host had no Street View and unclear elevation, so 2026 and 2025 took paved Hwy 12 (2026).
4. **Day-length rules of thumb:** rest/water stops every 10–20 mi (1.5–2 hr; every 10 mi at 108°F); car meets riders where there are no gas stations; make the route team add a map of the water/lunch stops. Gas stations have soda fountains/ice/bathrooms (2026, 2024). Navigation devices often read ~6,000 ft where Ride With GPS said 4,300 ft (Radford → Tazewell, 2025): expect the higher number.
5. **Elevation sanity-check:** "Missouri is flat" is false — Marshall → Kansas City was ~5,000 ft (2026). Typical hard days: Linden → Big Meadows 6,555–7,589 ft (2023–2026), Breaks → Hazard 4,671 ft, Idaho Springs → Breckenridge 7,590 ft (planned 2026), Capitol Reef → Escalante 7,510 ft (2024–2026), Lake Tahoe → Placerville 6,113 ft (2025).
6. **Cell coverage column:** the 2025 sheet recorded coverage for the first 16 days (no signal after Front Royal mile 8; none Hazard → London; spotty on the Blue Ridge Parkway; etc.) and 2026 told riders to download routes for multiple days and set meeting points (2026, 2025).
7. **Share the route** (start/end towns and dates) with Stanford Spokes if they ask (2026 meeting minutes) and publish a Google My Maps version for the website ([2026 Route to Share](../docs/2026/route-planning/2026_route_to_share.md) has the website sheet ID).

### 3. Day-of navigation and safety (trip)
1. The **driver (or route person) preps tomorrow's route the night before**, including all stops; 2026 finalized route at ~7 pm while cleaning bikes (2026, 2024).
2. Ride in groups of 2–4; stay together on climbs and in the afternoons; the driver can be "one rest stop ahead" (2026, 2022).
3. **Agree a storm and heat plan before the trip** (2024's team spent ~30 min undecided in a Kansas storm and hosts kept asking what they'd do) — see the hazard table below (2024, 2026).
4. **Check each morning:** radar/SPC outlook, wind, air quality (2026 cancelled Baker → Ely for "very unhealthy" wildfire smoke), flash-flood meter in parks (2026), road closures (2024).
5. Set **check-in points** where there's no signal (two 2026 riders were an hour late with no way to contact them) and a "what if someone doesn't show up by X" plan (2026).

## What worked / what didn't

### 2026
- **Worked:** 99% of housing booked before the trip (Warm Showers + churches + LF hosts); 3-person route team; shared Ride With GPS account; tailwinds across Kansas/Colorado plains (Dodge City → Lamar); rest stops every ~20 mi rounding down (Valley Grocery, Las Animas Dairy Queen, La Junta public pool at ~57 mi); using trails when interstates were unsafe (I-25 near Pueblo; Sacramento → Davis).
- **Didn't:** *Day 2:* DC (Lincoln Memorial) → Linden in one day (88.6 mi / 4,947 ft); most were SAG'd. Hwy 55 along Route 66 is better than Google or the W&OD trail; most years start from the host's home near Linden or take an extra day with Jen (2026). *Day 3:* after a 90-mile day they swapped Big Meadows for Luray (Spacious Skies Campground, 2,400 ft climbing) (2026). *Day 12:* Big A Mountain (6 mi, 1,500 ft) with blind-curve descents; a last-minute reroute passed a "No trespassing" gate. *Hazard → London:* "Hell for Certain Road" — maybe avoid the whole day. *Day 28 (Marshall → KC):* 107°F "feels like" and 30 mph crosswinds with 5,000 ft; 4 am wake / 5:30 start. *Day 32:* seven flats in 24 hours on gravel/tubeless near Osage City (over-inflated tires, max 35 psi). *Day 50:* Grand Junction → Moab in 100°F, no ice at 5 gas stations, a 28-mile exposed stretch; route file said 122.5 mi, actual 87.9; team stayed overnight and rode back 32 mi unsupported on the "rest day." *Day 56:* ~6,000 ft climb to 10,049 ft, hail, steep gravel descent (brakes overheated), no cell. *Day 61:* Zion → Cedar City in 100°F+, a rider nearly passed out, arrived 10 pm. *Day 64:* wildfire smoke cancelled Baker → Ely. *Day 67–68:* Austin → Fallon ridden overnight (left 10 pm / midnight, white jerseys as capes, "CAR" calls) to dodge heat — a team-chosen plan with a viral outbreak, so treat as a last resort. *Day 74:* Folsom → Benicia 108 mi stretched to 18 hours (10 side quests, closed rest stops, cold, arrived 3:20 am).
- **Do not repeat:** the Newton KS WS host (Janet Voth) (2022, 2026 notes), the Silverthorne host (2022), Jen's Linden house when hosting 7 (2026), the Breaks Interstate Park campground in rain (2025).
- **Dogs in Kentucky:** the team debated skipping or rerouting Kentucky on 6/13 (options: car escort, route change, dog training, optional riding days); rated 3 stars in the blog; air horns recommended (2022, 2026). A "safety officer" role was proposed for 2027 (2026).

### 2025
- **Worked:** Katy Trail; Colorado Springs → Denver on trails (New Santa Fe, Plum Creek, Daniels Park); Springs/Denver MIT-alum hosts; Lava Point free campground (6 sites, reserve 2 weeks ahead, no showers/cell).
- **Didn't:** Skyline Dr (10 mi / 3,000 ft climb day 3 then fog/rain descent day 4, driven); Breaks campground (rain); Hazard → London flood reroute onto Hell for Certain Rd; heat exhaustion in KY/IL (late June); St. Louis → Jefferson City 125 mi with car shuttle; a gravel descent after Lava Point caused a serious fall (Day 62) and the Day 56 gravel descent was "very bad" (a broken arm was reported on that day's descent in the 2026 route notes); Moab exit on Rt 191 (no shoulder, high traffic); Cedar City trail was "SCARY"; Baker NV almond-flour anaphylaxis (nearest urgent care ~45 min); Davis → Benicia ridden after dark on an interstate service road (arrived 10:30 pm).
- Recommended changes list for 2026: stay somewhere else night 3 (avoid Skyline then immediate descent), follow USBR 76 closely in VA, Roanoke → Blacksburg, get off I-25 (75 mph), return to 2024's main-highway route on day 56, no gravel descent day 62 ([2025 Route Notes](../docs/2026/route-planning/2026_2025_route_notes.md)).

### 2024
- **Worked:** shorter ride instead of a rest day (Mammoth Cave → Bowling Green 32 mi); camping at Gun Creek IL; Zion tunnel shuttled by van (tunnel closed to bikes).
- **Didn't:** US-285 out of Denver (steep, shoulder-less, some quit mid-climb; gravel alternative impassable); US-50 out of Tahoe (no shoulder; construction detour +15 mi); KS-68 out of Osawatomie; 70 mph winds and lightning near Newton; groups split in a Council Grove flood ("never split up or cross flood alone"); stuck support van in Utah gravel; E. coli in the Fremont River; third Tahoe rest day was unneeded; one rider broke an arm on the steep descent into Escalante; shipping bikes home was "logistical hell".
- Takeaways: skip US-285 and US-50 out of Tahoe; French bread is a cheap lunch; carry bike computers, dry bag, hand pumps; ~4,000 cal/day for big riders; stay together ([2024 Route Notes](../docs/2025/route-planning/2024_spokes-2024-route-notes.md)).

### 2023
- Two versions of the route; the first draft went through Mt Rogers/Damascus and the Mark Twain forest, the final one used Oakton → Linden and Missouri via Council Bluff/Mountain Grove/Springfield → Pittsburg/Eureka KS → Newton. Joy's advice: reroute Kansas further north and consider Manhattan KS instead of Newton so the team can stay with MIT alums rather than Warm Showers/churches (2023). The Thompson Springs → Arches ride has no shoulder; do not bike into Arches from Thompson Springs (2022).

### 2022
- Day 1 DC → Stafford 59 mi with a late start (11 am, finished 9 pm) was "way too long for day 1"; Day 2 flash flood/lightning; Roanoke storm shuttle; the Lakin 105°F day shuttled; Loveland Pass hail; Austin → Fallon 113 mi because Middlegate has only a burger hostel; "the difficulty of a route is not just distance or elevation but also wind".
- First time using Capitol Reef gravel: "GET GRAVEL BIKES" (2022).

### 2019 and earlier
- 2019: Wise → Hazard "bike out of Hazard is steep and hard"; stock up in Ely at the latest (Eureka and Austin have next to nothing); split a 120-mile Nevada day 55/35/30; Utah/Nevada very hot and dry. (2019)
- 2018: color-coded difficulty ranking (Easy / Okay / Normal / Difficult / Too Difficult) and a "Route Notes" column per day ([2018 route](../docs/2018/route/2018_2018-route.md)). 2018 and 2017 had the same Virginia corridor (Fredericksburg, Mineral commune, Charlottesville, Waynesboro, Natural Bridge, Wytheville, Abingdon, Wise, Hazard). (2018, 2017)
- 2016: the SLC → Carson City desert alternative (548.9 mi vs 560.9 mi); Nevada Hwy 50 days of 62–110 mi with services only in towns (2016).
