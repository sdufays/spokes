**2. First Edgerton meeting (week of Nov 30 or later).**
- Use the Bookings link in Justin's Nov 24 email (2027).
- Bring a roster, rough dates, a rough budget, and the question list. A model is the Jan 2026 email set (2026) and the Oct 2025 review prep: a PowerPoint of last year, this year, and finances (2025).
- Also meet the right people: Marvelin for card holders; Jamie Chelel for crowdfunding and social media; Maria/Sandi for sponsors and fundraising (2025, 2027).

**3. Cards (apply Jan; allow 2–3 weeks, 2025).**
- *Four cards:* 2 ProCards + 2 travel cards. Spread them so more people know how to use them (2025). A ProCard holder shouldn't also hold a travel card (2022). A gear person should hold a ProCard (2026).
- *ProCard steps (2024):*
  - Fill in the application PDF in Adobe Reader; Mac Preview doesn't work.
  - The Administrative Officer/Primary Authorizer signs it and emails ProCard@mit.edu.
  - Read the User Guide, do the cardholder training, and sign the online agreement.
  - The card arrives by interoffice mail. Activate it with your MIT ID by phone.
  - This takes 7–10 business days. A department verifier who can't be the cardholder needs SAP authority.
  - Limits seen in 2024 and 2025: $3,000 per transaction, $15,000 per month. Charges go to cost object 2530032.
- *Travel card steps (2024, 2025):*
  - Complete the Atlas Learning Center course "Travel Policies for the Traveler".
  - Fill in the PDF and sign digitally. Email it to Marvelin or Sandi, who forward to travelsupport@mit.edu (617-253-8360).
  - Monthly limit is $10,000. The cost object must be non-sponsored.
  - Lost or stolen card: travelsupport@mit.edu, or Bank of America 888-449-2273 after hours. A replacement comes within 24 hours.
- *What goes where:*
  - Travel card = supporting a person on the road: food, gas, hotels, camping, flights, and the rental in an emergency (2022, 2024).
  - ProCard = supporting the program: gear, learning-festival supplies, anything purchasable at MIT before the trip (2024, 2026).
  - Never put gas or food on a ProCard. Fines and tickets never go on a travel card (2025).
- *While waiting for cards:* use Buy2Pay (Coupa, mit.coupahost.com). Purchases over $500 need approval. Commodity codes: M&S 420226, Raw Materials 420310, Misc Materials 420245. Don't use the recreational G/L (2024, 2025).

**4. Bikes (big purchase).**
- Get a quote and send it to Marvelin (2025, 2027).
- The bike order is over the ProCard limit. It goes through a PO or Edgerton pays the invoice directly (2022, 2023, 2025).
- Requisitions over $10,000 need MIT's **Selection of Source & Price Justification** (2+ vendors or a justification). Sandi thinks the threshold is ~$10K (2024, 2022).
- If a late-joining rider isn't in the quote, order 7 via B2P and the 8th later on a ProCard or B2P (2025).
- Trek Cambridge gives 20% on gear; Edgerton wants the new bikes at the Apr 8 Showcase (2026, 2027).

**5. Rental vehicle and drivers (start Feb, or earlier).**
- MVR (Motor Vehicle Record) check: Marvelin requests it through MIT Background Check. Each driver then files and passes it from MIT's email (2027).
- A valid unexpired license or learner permit is required (2027). Checks take about 1–2 weeks (2024). Send Marvelin full name, DOB and license state (2025) or name + Kerberos ID (2025, 2027).
- Edgerton books the car and bills it directly. The travel card is only for emergencies (2026). Marvelin sets up the Direct Bill reservation (2025). MIT's corporate agreements are with Avis/Budget and Enterprise/National.
- **Only 6–7 drivers can be on the rental. Max vehicle size is 7 passengers** (2025, 2026). The Oct 2026 Edgerton meeting floated a cargo van that seats 5, or a second vehicle. "Just do your research" (2026).
- **Add every driver in person at the counter**, or Avis may refuse roadside help (2024). The 2026 guide says renter + 4 added = 5 max on Avis/Budget (2026). The 2025 Enterprise rental included 9 extra drivers (2025).
- **Corporate codes (2025 Travel FAQ):** Avis A122600; Budget T240202; Enterprise XZ10MTY (ages 18–20, only MA/NH/ME) or XZ10MIT (all others). The young-renter account in the 2024 email is XZ10MTY.
- **Ages:** the fee is waived for ages 21–24 on business rentals. Renters must be 25+ for luxury cars and SUVs. 18–20-year-olds can only rent in MA, NH and ME (2024, 2025).
- **At pickup:** ask when it was serviced, walk around it, photo every scratch, check tires, and look up recalls (2024, 2026). Don't accept hybrid Pacificas with open recalls (2024).
- **Accidents:** call Edgerton (Chris Mayer) and MIT Insurance right away. Get a police report. File the rental company's accident form and the Origami Risk incident report (2025, 2026).
- **Billing:** a long rental may be split into several contracts and Concur items (2025).
- **Moving the car and gear:** the 2023 and 2024 one-way rentals to SFO cost thousands extra, and shipping gear back was a hassle (2024, 2023). Oct 2026 ideas: ship back via an MIT account on FedEx or MIT's shipping platform (2026).

**8. Money in, money out.**
- **Donations to MIT:** crowdfunding and giving.mit.edu money lands in the Spokes cost object (2024). Crowdfunding must be through MIT, not GoFundMe (master guide 2026).
- **Internal MIT sponsors:** give them cost object 2530032 and they transfer internally (2027). Sandi oversees sponsorships (2027).
- **External sponsors:** a check goes to MIT with a letter, and Sandi deposits it (2022). Companies must register with MIT (a conflict-of-interest check; done for Lightforce, 2022). They sign the sponsorship agreement. They cannot imply MIT endorsement (2022).
- **Tax paperwork for sponsors and vendors:**
  - MIT IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter (EIN 04-2103594) and MIT W-9.
  - MA ST-2 exemption certificate, effective Sept 17, 2024 and expiring Sept 16, 2034. The ST-5 is the vendor form (2025).
  - Present the ST-5 with a copy of the ST-2. Stores sometimes reject the nonprofit number on a personal card (2024).
  - Only valid in MA. Other states' taxes are reimbursed (2024). Don't use a personal card outside MA, because the tax is subtracted (2025).
- **Reimbursements:** non-travel out-of-pocket items go by Atlas RFP with receipts (send PDFs named `lastname mm-dd-yyyy $amt vendor.pdf`, 2023). Travel items go in Concur (2023). Concur fields from 2023: trip purpose "Field Trip", cost object 2530032, classification Domestic, benefit "Non Sponsored Activity". Set up direct deposit in Atlas first.
- **Lost receipts:** over $75, attach a missing-receipt affidavit and mark it Non-Recoverable. The worst case is an audit inquiry (2026, 2025). A lost ProCard receipt can get the card deactivated (2026). Credit card statements aren't proof (2025).
- **J-WEL account (2024):** J-WEL has its own cost object and a separate rule set (2025). Edgerton said to confirm leftover J-WEL expiry with Sandi (2025).
- **Allowed and not:** allowed are fees, hotels, flights, rental and gas, food (full-detail receipts). Not allowed are personal items, any insurance, entertainment, alcohol (2025 Travel FAQ). Sunscreen and "health & beauty" aren't reimbursable (2024, 2026).
- **Overhead:** J-WEL grants have no overhead. Edgerton usually transfers funds (2024).

**9. After the trip.**

- Update the master guides and delete outdated info (2026). Fill in Justin's finances spreadsheet (Oct 2026).
- Leave ~$10k and report what next year needs (2024).
