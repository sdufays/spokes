# Corporate sponsorship & in-kind donations
_Last built: 2026-11-24. Sources: 46 docs, 4 blog posts, 4 emails (plus `contacts/sponsor.md`, `contacts/sponsorship.md`). Recent years first; every fact has its year._

Scope: companies and businesses (cash, gear, food, services), sponsor tiers/agreements/deliverables, outreach templates. MIT departments, grants and crowdfunding are covered in `03-fundraising` / `04-grants`; bikes/gear purchasing in `11-gear-and-bikes`; food in `13-food`. They appear here only where they overlap with sponsorship.

## TL;DR for 2027
- **Corporate cash has been small and unreliable; in-kind and discounts from bike shops have been the real sponsor value.** 2026 sponsor value = Trek Cambridge (20% off bikes/gear, donated tune-ups, built all 7 bikes) + Cambridge Bicycle (3 donated tune-ups, wholesale gear); 2025 company cash was only $25 on the tracker. (2026, 2025)
- **Last big corporate cash: Lightforce $5,000 (2022), TI $10,000 (2017) / $7,000 (2016) / $4,000 (2019), MathWorks $1,000–1,500 (2019/2022/2024).** TI and MathWorks said no or went silent in 2018 and 2025. Don't budget on any company cash. (2022, 2019, 2017, 2025)
- **Personal asks beat corporate forms.** Jane Street said no as a company (2024) but four employees gave $100 / $100 / $1,000 + one pending after individual emails. Same pattern with Course 8/TI alumni contacts in 2017–18. Prioritize people you know inside companies. (2024)
- **2027 has already started: intro emails to Trek (Eric Stilwell) and Cambridge Bicycle (Doug) sent Nov 2026, no reply yet as of Nov 23.** Gear leads (Noah Roberts, Max Oatney, Michelle Wang) asked Trek on Nov 23 about a "bike package" and team fittings. Edgerton wants the bike quote done early for the **Apr 8, 2027 Clubs & Teams Showcase**. Follow up on both emails next week. (2027)
- **Nov 22, 2026 meeting brainstormed nutrition/food sponsors:** Honey Stinger; Clif Bar or GoMacro; Liquid IV or Nuun; also GU, Tailwind, Hammer, Red Bull, Aloha, DripDrop, a peanut-butter brand, Sun Bum sunscreen. GoMacro has a donation form. Nobody is assigned yet. (2027)
- **Start in September–October, not spring.** The 2026 master guide says by spring the excess company money (TI, Microsoft, Dell…) is already gone. Companies contacted in Dec (2024) at least answered; those hit in May (2025) mostly stayed "Awaiting." Gear/outdoor brand forms may require lead time (Nikon wanted 6 months in 2018; Costco wants 4–6 weeks). (2026, 2025, 2018)
- **Rules for company money (MIT):** companies must register with MIT (conflict-of-interest check) and sign an MIT sponsorship agreement; no MIT name/logo in the company's ads without MIT Technology Licensing Office consent; we can't make a sales pitch for sponsor products (educational use in workshops only). Sandi Lipnoski (Edgerton) oversees sponsorships. (2026, 2022, 2027)
- **Local businesses along the route donate meals reliably** if you call ahead — in 2026 ≥10 restaurants donated dinners (e.g., a Lamar, CO steakhouse gave $20/person). Ask a few days before, by phone. (2026)
- **Deliver on sponsor benefits and thank everyone:** the 2026 tier package (Summit $10k+ → Trailhead $500) promised jersey logos, social posts, website donor page; no one reached the top tiers in 2026 (only $1k and $500 donors). Collect host/sponsor mailing addresses *during* the trip (2025 thank-you card address sheet stayed empty). (2026, 2025)
- **Hard "no" list so you don't re-ask:** GoPro (athletes only, 2018), Sony (no donations, 2018), Tern (2024), Aldi (can't fund MIT, 2022), North Face ($2,500 asked 2019, no), Gore-Tex (2019), Kars4Kids (2025 no after $750 in 2024), Museum of Science (2025 no), Tootsie Roll / Nature Valley / Quaker (2025 no), Tom's (2025 no). (various)

## Playbook — how to do it
1. **Build the target list in September–October** (2026 guide; 2025 tracker shows Dec 5–21 is the realistic first-send window). Split into: (a) bike/gear partners, (b) nutrition/food, (c) local restaurants/grocers along the route, (d) STEM/education companies, (e) alumni inside companies. Use the 2025 "Companies" tab and 2022 tracker categories (Cycling / Outdoor / Education / Community) as the template. (2025, 2022)
2. **Renew existing partners first** — Trek Cambridge and Cambridge Bicycle (they asked nothing but tune-ups/discounts and want photos for their social). Meet them in person; ask for: bike package/20%+ discount, fittings, helmets, tune-ups along the route (Trek has stores nationwide), raffle prizes, jerseys. (2026, 2025, 2027)
3. **Choose the ask by company type.** Gear companies: ask for product (helmets, pedals, tubes, bottles, bags) — 2022's Trek/Bontrager ask was "helmets, bike lights, pedals, shoes, inner tubes"; Stanford Spokes gets a sponsored Trek *rental* and doesn't keep the bikes (2024 idea). Food: product or gift cards (Costco gave $500 card, 2022; Trader Joe's letter for snacks, 2025). Tech/education companies: cash $500–5,000 or workshop materials (Formlabs 3D-printed parts, 2022). (2022, 2024, 2025)
4. **Use the right door.** In order of success: (1) a personal contact/alum or employee, (2) a local store manager in person, (3) a company's own donation/sponsorship web form (Trek, Tern, Garmin, Costco, GoMacro, Shimano), (4) cold email. Corporate foundations often want a 501(c)(3) tax ID: the MIT tax packet (IRS letter, W-9, ST-2/ST-5) is in `docs/2025/finances-budgeting/`. (2025, 2024)
5. **Tailor the pitch** (2016 cold-email rule: customize why *this* company cares; don't paste their mission). Emphasize education/underserved/rural/incarcerated youth, 8 MIT students, DC→SF, ~75 days, free learning festivals, measurable (# festivals, # students). Include budget one-pager and website/crowdfund link. (2016, 2024, 2026)
6. **Offer benefits in writing.** Use the 2026 donor package (tiers below) or the 2022 MIT-agreement tiers. In-kind benefits are decided case by case. (2026, 2022)
7. **For any company cash: sign the MIT sponsorship agreement.** Steps used in 2022: get template from Edgerton development officer (Peggy) / Sandi; company signs, wires payment per MIT wire instructions (Exhibit B: Edgerton cost object 2530032, Sandi Lipnoski as admin contact), registers with MIT; logo use needs written consent via MIT Technology Licensing Office. Talk to Sandi *before* promising anything. (2022, 2026 guide)
8. **Log everything** in the fundraising sheet (Companies tab: source, angle, contacted?, point person, person contacted, email, date, ask, actual, status, notes, previous years). Cc the team address (spokes-2027) on every email — 2024 forgot and lost visibility. Update status with "Said yes / Said no / Awaiting / Bumped / Duplicate". (2025, 2024)
9. **Follow up (bump) about weekly for 2–3 rounds**; add a new hook with each bump (2026 raffle: new prizes announcement boosted sales). Mark unresponsive as "Bumped" rather than re-sending every year. (2026, 2025)
10. **Deliver the benefits during the trip:** jersey/jersey-back logos (design jerseys early; 2017 and 2022 planned logo-back wicking shirts), website donor page, social posts, thank-you posts (2026 tiers), sponsor dinners/visits where local (2025 aSTEAM Village). (2026, 2025, 2022, 2017)
11. **After the trip (Nov):** send thank-you notes/cards with photos; ask partners to continue (2026 emails to Trek/Cambridge Bicycle; 2025 thank-you card). Offer partners photos for their social media. (2026, 2025)

(2017, 2016)

## What worked / what didn't

### 2026
- **Worked:** Trek Cambridge and Cambridge Bicycle partnership; tune-up raffle ($5 / 3 for $12 / 10 for $30; weekly bump emails; adding 3 Cambridge Bicycle prizes on Mar 6 extended it to Mar 13 and made it $750 of prizes). Restaurants along the route donated meals when called ahead. Trek built all 7 bikes before pickup. Company matching gifts via parents' employers $950 total.
- **Didn't:** the Gear tab of the tracker stayed empty (7 blank rows), the local restaurant list (Kendall Sq cafés) was never contacted, and no one hit the Summit/Crosswinds/Tailwind tiers. Course 21H "fundraising $50,000" email drafted Mar 30 (late). 2027 lesson: spring is too late. Don't sell boba in the Infinite (raffle/bake sale lesson).

### 2025
- **Worked:** Trek gave ~20% off 8 Checkpoint ALR 3 bikes plus shoes at $59.97 (vs $124.99) and fit recommendations, 30-day returns. Bikes Not Bombs said yes (gear). A sponsor (aSTEAM Village's benefactor) hosted a dinner with Kansas City community leaders and set up a visit to The Children's Place (Day 28/29); when the dinner was cancelled the sponsor sent BBQ by DoorDash.
- **Didn't:** Companies tab produced $25. Many "Awaiting" with no reply (Trek form, Tern, Formlabs, Clif, Nalgene, GoPro, Strava, REI, North Face, Columbia, Red Bull). MathWorks said no (asked $2,000), Kars4Kids no, Museum of Science no, Tootsie Roll / Nature Valley / Quaker no, Tom's no. Costco form submitted 4/29 — no recorded outcome; Trader Joe's in-person — no recorded outcome. Patagonia learned local retail grants are favored over corporate (note: mission not environmental). Thank-you-card address sheet never filled.

### 2024
- **Worked:** MathWorks $1,000 (applied 3/1). Individuals at Jane Street ($100, $100, $1,000). Kars4Kids $750 (pledged; website application). Edgerton wanted a budget before giving (~$4k).
- **Didn't:** Jane Street (company) no; Tern Gives Back no; Garmin $5,000 form — nothing; Decathlon USA — nothing; Trek "very promising" but no confirmed donation recorded; forgot to cc spokes-2024 on emails. Idea: contact Stanford Spokes about a Trek sponsored rental (no outcome recorded).

### 2023
- Bike sponsorship lead via an MIT alum pro biker (Pika); Trek, Papa Wheelies, Hobby Shop emailed Feb 6 — only "will hear back if they accept." Landry's Cycles served as discount shop (Mar 5 gear list; Mar 16 order of 7 Giant Revolt 2 bikes). Costco requested (2022 had given $500). MIT Coop gave $1,500 (not a company in the usual sense); MIT Museum $1,000; Concourse $1,000.

### 2022
- **Worked:** Lightforce Orthodontics $5,000 (sponsorship agreement, 3D-printed braces shown at workshops; "pamphlet helped"); Formlabs and Desktop Metal in-kind 3D-printed parts and curriculum for a 3D-printing workshop; Costco $500 gift card (used for groceries); DYS $506 Tier-1 agreement (May 13, 2022). Peggy (Edgerton development officer, Mar 1, 2022) advised: local bike shops (~$500, e.g., Wheelworks), local foundations via MIT alumni clubs, Backroads (bike-tour company), DAPER, design jersey/T-shirt with sponsor logos on the back, have the sponsorship agreement ready.
- **Didn't:** Aldi can't fund MIT; Clif Bar Foundation doesn't accept individuals/teams; Liquid Instruments meeting only; Trek/Bontrager gear request "pending."

### 2019 and 2018
- **2019:** TI $4,000 (Leah asked $4,000, granted); MathWorks $1,500; SWE $400. North Face (asked $2,500), Gore-Tex denied; Shell, SpaceX ($5,000) no reply. Riders each took route towns to email local businesses and news outlets. Goal $20,000.
- **2018:** About 50 tech/outdoor/gear companies emailed Mar 29–Apr 21 (REI asked $10,000; Camelbak, Arc'teryx, Heartbreak Hill, Bicycle World same email on 4/6). Almost nothing came back: Sony not accepting, Nikon needs 6 months, GoPro athletes only, Kelty "reviewed quarterly," Garmin form 3/31. Wins: COOLA suncare samples, MakeCrate kits. **Lesson: mass cold emails in April don't work.**

### 2013–2017
