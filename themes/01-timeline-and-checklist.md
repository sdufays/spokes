3. **One weekly meeting, with a moderator + notetaker + action-item list**; 2026 met Mondays with a dinner after, sub-team updates every week, and norms (CC everyone, respond within ~24 hrs, decisions by discussion not voting). 2027 plan: weekly, Zoom over IAP/winter break, in-person plan at end of IAP. (2027, 2026)
4. **Run the Edgerton onboarding checklist** (below) before anything that needs money, cars or travel registration. (2027, 2025, 2024)
5. **Work in this order when things collide: LFs → housing → route** (2026, Mar 9 minutes: "1. Learning festivals 2. Housing 3. Route"). (2026)
6. **Lock sequencing for hosts vs LFs:** confirm the school/learning center date first; Sunday LFs are usually a no-go especially in religious areas; check when local schools restart (Fallon NV LF on Aug 16, 2022 had low turnout because school began the next day). (2019, 2022)
7. **Use the repeating annual goals as your checklist** (2025 yearly review "& repeat!"): 10+ LFs; route + daily schedule by IAP; raise ~$40K "ASAP, always"; housing contacts in spring; all gear bought by IAP; budget management; "really everything is ongoing". (2025)
8. **Before departure:** tetanus/Tdap shot, register group travel in Atlas, photo-release forms from Edgerton, communal gear list, personal packing list, plastic boxes, bike computers set up, tents counted, tune-up of every bike. (2026, 2022)
9. **During the trip:** schedule regular team check-ins, nightly route/housing/LF planning, blog rotation, host thank-you cards, laundry rotation; follow dangerous-weather and "van calls it, you get in" rules. (2026, 2025)
10. **After the trip:** close the budget, update Master Guides (delete outdated info), write advice docs, recruit next team, transition meeting, rotate mailing lists (below). (2026, 2016, 2022)
