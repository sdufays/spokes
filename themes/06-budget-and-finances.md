## Playbook: how to do it

### A. Set up (Nov-Jan)
1. **Assign roles** (2026): *Finances* [2-4] = the card holders (get cards, keep tax forms current, track receipts, talk to Edgerton about purchases, buy for gear/LF people). *Budget* [2-3] = itemized budget, decide whether a purchase fits, track spending on the trip, finalize the spending report. The 2026 team had two on Budget and recommends three. A gear person should probably hold a ProCard. (2026) The 2027 roles table lists "Finances / Budget (2-4): Noah, Sarah" (2027).
2. **Intro to Edgerton and get Atlas access.** Done Nov 15-24, 2026 for 2027. Marvelin granted Financial Access to cost object 2530032 on Nov 24, 2026. Read the Edgerton Center website FAQs first; Sandi always recommends that (2027). Edgerton group list: edgerton-clubs-admin@mit.edu (2027).
3. **Apply for cards in January (2 ProCards + 2 travel cards).** Don't wait; the 2023 finance lead says "ASAP", about 2 weeks to arrive, bump Sandi/Marvelin or go to their office (2023). Travel card: first complete the Atlas Learning Center course "Travel Policies for the Traveler", fill in the VPF Travel Card application, and send it to the Administrative Officer (Sandi) who forwards it to VPF Travel & Card Services; cost object must be non-sponsored; 2025 monthly limit $10,000 (2025). ProCard needs "extensive" online training and an application; 2025 limits: $3,000 per transaction, $15,000 per month (2025). Spokes 2026: two people got ProCards and two *different* people got travel cards (2026). Marvelin asks that cardholders be "the most responsible, the most responsive, and the most punctual" (2027).
4. **Rough budget for fundraising** (Jan), refined after fundraising (Mar), finalized in May (2026 timeline). Start from last year's sheet: "set rough budget by taking 2025 budget and adjusting estimated costs" (2026). Itemize wherever possible (list each gear item with local bike-shop price) and note that transportation and food were "guessed" in 2026 (2026).
5. **Tax forms**: check the MIT ST-2 certificate, ST-5 and W-9 are current (see Tax section). (2024, 2025)

### C. On the trip
11. **Which card for what** (2025 Edgerton meeting, 2026 guide): **Travel card** = anything for the traveler: flights, rental car, gas, food, campsites, Airbnbs, tolls, park passes. **ProCard** = raw materials, supplies, services (gear, LF supplies, bike repairs). **Never** gas, food or health & beauty (shampoo, body wash, sunscreen) on the ProCard (2025, 2026). Health & beauty can't go on either card (2026). Edgerton can book flights/hotels with a department card. Tickets and violations are the driver's cost, never on a card (2025).
12. **Receipt system.** Past systems: 2022 Google Form log + per-person folders; 2023 folder per day; 2024 text receipts to the cardholder; 2025 finance lead sorted receipts into folders by payment path (Ruth ProCard, Ishaq ProCard, Joseph Travel Card, Sarah Travel Card, RFPs travel / RFPs non-travel, Personal Gear, paid in cash); 2026 one shared "Expenses + Budget" sheet with who/how/receipt link/"Emailed Marvelin?"/"If RFP, reimbursed?" columns (2022-2026). Collect physical receipts too; the end-of-trip report asked for clearer images (2024). Everyone must know which card to use.
13. **File naming** (every year since 2023): `LastName MM-DD-YYYY Vendor $Amount.pdf`. PDF names must match the actual receipt total; in 2026 file names disagreed with receipts (Kroger $19 vs $89.34; ASSOS $183 vs $385.25) (2023, 2026).
14. **ProCard receipts → Marvelin within 2 weeks.** Watch procard@mit.edu pending-transaction emails; respond to audits promptly with what was bought and how it will be used. A lost receipt is worse: the card can be deactivated (2026 guide).
15. **Travel-card receipts**: upload to your travel profile in Atlas/Concur during the trip. Receipts required for >$75 and any food; food receipts need full detail; alcohol always needs a receipt; credit-card statements are not proof of payment (2025, 2026). Catalogue gas too, because it can look like groceries on the Concur report (2026).
16. **Lost receipt**: for >$75 attach a **missing-receipt affidavit** and mark **Non-Recoverable** (Sandi Lipnoski's advice); worst case is an inquiry (2025, 2026).
17. **Log daily and categorize at purchase time.** 2026: "the team spent hours reconciling the trip's receipts", with a 2-3 hour spreadsheet session on rest day 44 (Jul 14). 2022: "DO NOT KNOW" in the person column on early log entries; record the buyer's name immediately (2022, 2026).

Check Costco rules: Costco in DC required the cardholder to be present at checkout (2026).

## What worked / what didn't

### 2026
- **Worked**: one shared "Expenses + Budget" sheet with payer, receipt link and "Emailed Marvelin?" columns; the per-person gear cap; renting a cargo van ($589.66, 20% discount, no damage waiver) to move bikes from Arlington MA to Rockville MD; buying through Trek Cambridge at 20% off and repeat fitting provider discount ($650 off 6 fits); Cambridge Bicycle and Trek tune-up raffles (raised ~$600-$1,000); donated lodging (a Moab Airbnb offer of $190 for three nights beat the $550 listing).
- **Didn't**: ProCard orders placed outside the Amazon Business account got taxed; some members ordered on personal Amazon; role confusion (two assigned to Finances, but the four cardholders did the work); Budget team of 2 was too small; the food budget ($7,200) was under-logged ($2,378), so the data is unreliable; first aid went over ($493 vs $300); the plan for $1,115 home shipping wasn't used; receipts were unnamed or mis-named, and 2-3 hours of rest-day time went to reconciling; the Recreation.gov Zion lottery entries were many small charges ($6, $12); a $500 "bonus" came from an old 2024 charge that was dismissed.
