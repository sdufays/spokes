# Fundraising — crowdfunding, Giving Tuesday, MIT departments, alumni, events, donor tracking, totals per year
_Last built: 2026-11-24. Sources: ~70 docs, 5 blog posts, 7 emails. Recent years first; every fact has its year._

## TL;DR for 2027
- **Goal is $50K "over the next few months"** (stated to SoE comms Nov 23, 2026). The 2026 team had the same $50K goal and tracked **$37.1K** (not counting a $5K ELO grant). The 2027 team inherits roughly **$19K** (2026 fundraising overview). Real spend has been ~$34.5K in 2024 and ~$35K in recent years, so $50K is a stretch figure, not the budget. (2027, 2026, 2024)
- **Start now, not in spring.** Most MIT department funding windows close in the fall, and the Edgerton "Big 6" told the 2026 team on Oct 8, 2026 that this is why 2026 should hunt for 2027's department money. The 2026 team missed the Course 7 (Biology) deadline. The 2024 and 2025 teams sent the main department wave **Dec 5–22**, with a second "bump" wave Feb 12. (2026, 2025, 2024)
- **Giving Tuesday is Dec 1, 2026, aimed at alumni.** The plan is not yet written; Fundraising (Michelle, Sarah, Kevin) must make it and communicate it. No earlier year has a Giving Tuesday record, so this is new. (2027)
- **Departments are the reliable money.** 2025: about $20.5K from ~20 units (Libraries $2,500; School of Engineering $2,000; School of Science $2,000; Museum $1,000; nano $1,000; CSAIL Alliance $1,000). 2026: only about $10.2K, because many departments never replied. (2025, 2026)
- **Crowdfunding (crowdfund.mit.edu via Edgerton) yields $5K–$21K:** 2023 $4.8K, 2025 ~$9K, 2026 ~$19K (Edgerton's official record) or $21,115 (team sheet). Family, friends and social posts drive most of it. Ask alumni during the **MIT 24-Hour Challenge** (Pi Day, Mar 12–14, 2026; Mar 14, 2024), the only time the team may ask alumni for money. (2026, 2025, 2024, 2023)
- **The biggest single gifts were grants, not events.** J-WEL gave $45,330 in 2024 but denied the ~$20K ask in 2025. Other MIT grants: Kelly-Douglas $1,500 (2025, 2023), ELO $5,000 (2026), TBP $2,000–5,000, Baker $1,000–2,000, Coop $1,500. Events (raffle, boba, bake sale) net hundreds, not thousands. (2024, 2025, 2026)
- **Company money is mostly in-kind plus a few gifts.** Cold emails to ~100 companies produced almost nothing in 2016, 2024 and 2025. Warm or past sponsors work (TI $4K–$10K in 2016–2019, Lightforce $5K in 2022, MathWorks $1K–1.5K). Trek Cambridge and Cambridge Bicycle are 2026 partners and are being re-engaged. (2026, 2025, 2024, 2022, 2019)
- **Corporate donors must register with MIT** (conflict-of-interest check), and sponsors sign Edgerton's sponsorship agreement. Talk to Sandi Lipnoski before accepting any company money. (2026, 2022)
- **Always CC the team list on every outreach email** and log it in the tracker. 2024 lost track of several because they forgot to CC. (2024)
- **Overfund on purpose.** Edgerton staff say teams try to leave ~$10K in the bank for the next team. 2025 ended about $3K over its goal, and 2022 left $10K. Report department money and grants, not small crowdfund amounts, in big grant narratives. (2024, 2022, 2025)

## Playbook — how to do it
**1. Set up the plumbing first (Nov–Jan).**
- Get the Spokes cost object **#2530032** and Atlas financial access for everyone (granted Nov 24, 2026). All donations, department transfers and grants land there. In Atlas: Financials → Cumulative Account Balance Report or Transaction List shows the balance. (2027, 2026)
- Meet Sandi Lipnoski (Finances & Administration Officer, oversees sponsorships) and Marvelin Higginbottom (travel delegate and ProCard approver). Ask Jamie Chelel (Edgerton communications) for crowdfunding help. Internal MIT sponsors only need your cost object number to transfer money. (2027, 2026)
- 2026 questions to ask Edgerton in January: Venmo policy for fundraising, how to register for the 24-Hour Challenge and the Great Showcase, the seven-digit fund designation for the crowdfunding application, who creates the page, where donations from departments, individuals, companies and grants go. (2026)
- Companies that want to give must be **registered with MIT** (conflict-of-interest check; done for Lightforce in 2022). Companies cannot use the team for marketing or say they sponsor MIT Spokes; sponsors sign Edgerton's sponsorship agreement (Peggy, Edgerton's development officer, has a template). (2026, 2022)
- Crowdfunding must go through MIT, not GoFundMe. (2018)

**2. Build a tracker and assign owners (Nov–Dec).** (2026, 2024, 2022)
- Copy the 2026 tracker (tabs: Tracking, Departments, Companies, Alumni Groups, Publicity, Grants). Columns that worked: contacted?, outcome (Said yes / Said no / Bumped / Awaiting / Duplicate), amount, JV/confirmation number, point person, date of first contact, notes. Mark money "received" only once it is in the account.
- 2026 split: Tatiana and Nunu each took 30% of the department emails; the other six took 40% between them; alumni clubs were split by where each person is from. (2026)
- Include a share of the network: the 2026 team estimated what fraction of each person's network had been tapped (Mar 9). (2026)

**3. Ask MIT departments (Dec peak; keep going into spring).**
- Use the 2026 department template: subject **"MIT Spokes 2026 x [Course] Fundraising $50,000 to Support Underserved Communities"**, a brief personal tie to the department, one workshop tailored to the department (e.g. Course 4: design process and 3D printing; Course 7: protein folding with origami and AlphaFold), the ask for any amount plus referrals, links to the sponsorship package, giving.mit.edu, matching gifts and mitspokes.com. Always CC the team list. (2026)
- Contact the **Director of Administration & Finance** or the **Leadership Giving Officer**, not only the department head; many replied "bumped" (forwarded) and a bump after ~2 weeks often worked. (2025, 2026)
- Typical gift: $200–$500; larger from Libraries ($2,500), School of Engineering ($2,000), School of Science ($2,000), Museum ($1,000), nano ($1,000–2,000), CSAIL Alliance ($1,000). Only ask a department like Course 4 or 9 if a team member belongs to it. (2025, 2026)
- Some units say no outright: Anthropology 2026 ("just donate to One MIT"), Office of Sustainability 2026 (funding cuts), IPC 2025 ("don't ask next year"), MITES 2025 ("don't ask next year"), MISTI 2026 ("don't give money to stuff like this"). Read the 2026 tracker's "Notes 2025/2026" before re-emailing. (2025, 2026)
- Second wave: ask departments at fiscal year end for excess funds (cycle ends about the first week of May; send as a mass email from the spreadsheet). (2024)
- Use Word mail merge for volume. (2026)

**4. Run MIT crowdfunding (launch late March, end late May).** (2026, 2025, 2024)
- Application needs: a dynamic campaign image (1–10 MB), a project title (50 characters), a 100-character summary, and the story text. The 2026 summary line: "Support Spokes as we bike across the country, rethinking STEM education for underserved communities!" Reuse and update the 2024–2026 story text (team bios, 4,000 miles in ~75 days, rural/low-income/incarcerated-youth framing). (2026, 2024)
- Set the goal low enough to hit and raise it later: 2026 started at $15K and moved to $20K on Mar 16. 2024 used $10K. (2026, 2024)
- Plan a "quiet phase" (friends and family first), then a public launch, then pushes (24-Hour Challenge, showcase, restaurant night, raffle). Advertise beyond social media with a deadline. (2024)
- Everyone on the team posts and personally asks; family, friends, coworkers and social posts raised most of it. (2026, 2025, 2023)
- Ask the donor-relations contact for the emails of donors so thank-yous can go out; the MIT alumni platform can email alumni directly (needs each member's MIT ID); think about affinity groups by interest (education, biking) and by location (also useful for finding hosts). (2023)
- Send alumni clubs a short newsletter blurb plus QR code (see the Colorado club blurb) once the page is live. (2025, 2024)

**5. Do the MIT 24-Hour Challenge (mid-March).** It is the only time the team may ask alumni for money. Register through Edgerton, make a flyer linking to the giving page, and share it in club alumni networks (FSILGs, Infinite Connections). (2026, 2024)

**6. Apply for grants (calendar below).** Reuse the master application text (mission 200 words, budget by category, impact stats). Match the pitch to each funder: education for J-WEL/ELO/Coop; travel for Kelly-Douglas; community service for CSF; "bike repair workshop" for Baker. (2025, 2024, 2022)

**7. Alumni, clubs and cycling groups.** 2025 emailed ~30 MIT regional clubs Mar 10–12 and ~25 cycling clubs; results were mostly "linking in newsletter" or "bumped," with a few gifts (SoCal $250, Connecticut $25, Kansas "working on it") plus housing and festival help. Treat alumni outreach as connection-building first. Ask clubs to share the crowdfund link only after it exists. (2025, 2023, 2022)

**8. Companies and in-kind.** Keep it targeted: bike shops (Cambridge Bicycle, Trek Cambridge), outdoor/nutrition brands (2027 list: Honey Stinger, Clif Bar or GoMacro, Liquid IV or Nuun, GU, Tailwind, Hammer, DripDrop, Sun Bum), and corporate foundations with open forms (Garmin, Trek, Tern, MathWorks, Texas Instruments, Costco, Trader Joe's). Costco: submit in person at a warehouse 4–6 weeks ahead. Ask for **employee matching gifts** (giving.mit.edu/matching-gifts); 2026 got $950 this way. (2027, 2026, 2025)

**9. Events (small but good for engagement).** Boba sale, raffle, bake sale, restaurant night, dare fundraisers. See "What worked / what didn't." (2026, 2024)

**10. Thank and report.** Thank-you email template (2023); donor tiers and recognition (2026); handwritten letters for $50+ donors (2018); perks tracker (2019); a final write-up for funders (2019 Review, J-WEL narrative). Send photos to Trek/Cambridge Bicycle (offered Nov 2026). (2026, 2023, 2019, 2018)

**11. Hand off.** Leave ~$10K for the next team, update the tracker's "Notes" column for next year's asks, and in the fall introduce the next team to department contacts (as Tatiana did with Dooyon Cho, Nov 18, 2026). (2026, 2024)

## What worked / what didn't
**2026**
- ✅ Crowdfunding reached ~$19–21K, mainly from social posts and family/friends; goal raised from $15K to $20K mid-March. (2026)
- ✅ Edgerton $5,000, School of Engineering $2,000, nano $2,000; ELO $5,000 (applied Sept 2025). Parents' employer matches gave $950. (2026)
- ✅ Raffle with prizes donated by Trek Cambridge and Cambridge Bicycle: $5 tickets (3 for $12, 10 for $30), Level 2 tune-up ($189.99) and Level 1 ($99.99); closed Mar 6. Sponsors want to continue. (2026)
- ✅ Donor tier package (Summit $10K+, Crosswinds $5K, Tailwind $2.5K, First Mile $1K, Trailhead $500) with jersey-logo benefits; recognized Brenda and Tom Sanzone ($1,000) and PrettyGoodAI ($500). (2026)
- ✅ Phoning a pizza place ahead got meals donated; a stranger gave $1,000 at a gas-stop rest stop near Luray, VA (Day 3). (2026)
- ❌ Many departments never replied; Course 7 deadline missed; several said "give to One MIT." 2026 department total (~$10K) was half of 2025. (2026)
- ❌ Boba in the Infinite: don't. Bake sale + raffle net was only $400. (2026)
- ❌ Alumni Group and Company email templates were left as empty stubs. (2026)

**2025**
- ✅ Early start: first emails Dec 5, 2024; ~20 departments paid, ~$20.5K. Edgerton $4,000 arrived Oct 7, 2024, the earliest. (2025)
- ✅ Kelly-Douglas $1,500; ended ~$3K over goal. (2025)
- ❌ J-WEL denied (~$20K ask); CSF ($2,000), MindHandHeart ($5,000) and MathWorks ($2,000) all declined. Companies produced $25; 12+ outdoor/sports brands were still "awaiting" in May. (2025)
- ❌ Fall 2025 "preemptive grant list" shows several deadlines already closed (Coop Sept 17, PKG IDEAS Nov 25). (2025)

**2024**
- ✅ J-WEL $45,330 — the largest single source ever; Edgerton advised not to list small crowdfund gifts in the narrative. (2024)
- ✅ Jane Street said no as a company, but four employees gave (e.g. $1,000, $100, $100). MathWorks $1,000. (2024)
- ✅ Event menu: 24-Hour Challenge, social-media bingo dare (Mar 18–22), Midnight Marathon dares ($701.50 across 7 people), bike-a-thon ($5/mile, Apr 23–27), boba (below), DIG restaurant night May 1. (2024)
- ⚠️ Boba sale (Lobby 10, Apr 22–26, noon–5pm): sold $1,045, cost $640, **profit $405**; sales fell after Monday (21–23 unsold daily). Lesson: order less, one treasurer, track Venmo vs cash. (2024)
- ❌ Company outreach "lowkey not successful" (Apr 10); remaining need then $17K. (2024)
- ❌ Several department emails forgot to CC spokes-2024. (2024)

**2023**
- ✅ Departments plus Edgerton, Coop, Baker, Kelly-Douglas totaled $29.5K confirmed; Course 8 $2,500, Libraries $2,500. (2023)
- ❌ Crowdfunding only $4.8K against a $15K goal. (2023)

**2022**
- ✅ Over goal: $38.7K in account vs $30K target; Lightforce $5,000 (sponsor agreement); St. Anthony $4,000; Baker $1,758.52 for a bike repair/safety workshop (Apr 23, 2022); Formlabs and Desktop Metal in-kind 3D-printed parts. (2022)
- ❌ Many foundation applications rejected (Alpkit, Dell, Entergy, New Earth, PKG). (2022)

**2016–2019 lessons**
- ✅ Past sponsors and warm leads beat cold emails (TI repeat gifts 2016–2019). (2016)
- ✅ New department email (2018) personalized with the rider's own workshop landed MIT course money. (2018)
- ❌ Boston Globe and TV news outreach got little response. (2019, 2018)
- ❌ 2015 grants "didn't work"; small MIT sources (UROP, TBP, LEAP, departments) did pay. (2016)
