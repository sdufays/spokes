# Top lessons learned & advice from every past team
_Last built: 2026-11-24. Sources: 29 docs, ~95 blog-post lesson entries (from `blogs/by-tag/lessons-learned.md`), 2 emails. Recent years first; every fact has its year._

How to read this: lessons are **ranked by how many different teams repeated them** (the "Seen in" column counts years that said it independently, not copies of the same slide deck). Handoff decks are partly copied forward (2024 → 2025 → 2026), so a lesson that appears in the 2024, 2025 and 2026 decks counts as one strong signal, not three. Lessons that only came from a blog post (a one-off incident) are in the second half.

## TL;DR for 2027
- **Money first, and start in the fall.** Every team from 2016 to 2026 says "fundraise earlier." Department funding deadlines close in the fall and corporate money is gone by spring; 2026 started in Jan and ended at ~$37K tracked against a $50K goal. Send department emails Sept–Oct and set up the MIT crowdfund by December (2026, 2025, 2024, 2019, 2018, 2016).
- **You inherit ~$19K and need ~$40K** (2026 handoff). Past handoffs: ~$23K (2024→2025), ~$16K (2025→2026, incl. a $5K ELO grant) (2026, 2025).
- **Do everything you can before the trip; the trip itself is too busy.** Book ~99% of housing, finalize LFs, settle reimbursement rules, buy gear, and do a practice LF *before* leaving (2026, 2025, 2019, 2022).
- **Everyone gets the same gravel bike** (2026 and 2025 Trek Checkpoint ALR 3, 20% off; 2024 mixed bikes caused repair-parts trouble; 2022 and 2018 also gravel/tubeless). Buy by spring break, get fitted, practice with clip-ins, and **learn how your bike works** (2026, 2025, 2024, 2019, 2018).
- **Add every driver to the rental *in person* at pickup and get MVR checks early.** In 2024 Avis refused roadside assistance because nobody present was an authorized driver; 2027 MVR requests were issued Nov 24, 2026 (2024, 2025, 2026).
- **Never ride DC → Linden in one day from the Lincoln Memorial** (2026: 88.6 mi, most of the team SAG'd; 2025: 82 mi / 5,361 ft). Start from the host's house or add a day (2026, 2025).
- **Avoid known-bad roads:** US-285 out of Denver, US-50 out of Tahoe, gravel descents, Skyline Drive climb, Hell for Certain Rd, Breaks Interstate Park campground (2024, 2025, 2026).
- **Have a dog protocol and a storm protocol before day 1.** Kentucky dogs injured a rider (2026-06-13 rabies shots), and in 2024 the team lost 30 minutes to indecision in a Kansas storm (2026, 2025, 2024).
- **Receipts within 2 days; never gas on the ProCard; Amazon only through the Spokes business account (tax-exempt in MA only)** (2025, 2026, 2023, 2019).
- **Take the ~2-way rental** if you want to drive the van home: it avoids bike shipping (REI boxes ≈ $200/bike to ship; 2026 budgeted $1,115 shipping, 2026 one-way rental $5,912) (2026, 2025, 2024).
- **Edgerton showcase Apr 8, 2027:** Marvelin wants the new bikes there, so get the bike quote done early (2027 email, 2026-11-19).

## Playbook — ranked lessons (strongest, most repeated first)

### Tier 1 — said by 4+ different teams

1. **Start fundraising earlier than feels necessary.** Fall for departments (budgets close; "by spring corporate money is gone"), December for crowdfund, March for J-WEL. 2026 team: "start fundraising ASAP"; 2025 same; 2024 "discretionary funding cycles end in the fall"; 2019 "many MIT departments finalize budgets early"; 2018 "by spring, excess corporate money is already gone"; 2016 "START EARLY." *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2019, 2018, 2016)*
2. **More than one person on fundraising; everyone emails their own department/network.** 2018: need more than one person; "go crazy." 2026 split the department emails (two leads took 30% each, the other six 40% total) and tracked "% of network tapped" per person. Social-media posts and family/friends drove most crowdfund donations in 2026 and 2018. *(2026, 2018, 2015)*
3. **Do as much as possible before the trip.** 2026 and 2025: "don't leave things you can do before the trip to the start; it will be very busy." Examples: housing 99% planned before departure ("made our lives 1000x easier"; 2026, 2025) — though 2019 said "plan well but not perfectly; places can be found on short notice," and 2024 improvised some; do a pre-trip bike tour of ≥2 nights (2025); do a practice LF before leaving (2019, 2025 Framingham test run, 2022, 2015 Vermont). *(2026, 2025, 2022, 2019, 2015)*
4. **Same bike for everyone, gravel, bought by spring break, fitted.** 2026/2025: Trek gravel, same model = swappable wheels/parts, group discount, free tune-ups at Trek shops en route; 2024: three different bikes (Specialized Diverge E5, Allez, Liv Avail AR4) made parts a pain; 2019: bikes arrived ~1 month before departure, one rider added clips the day before; 2018: ~$1,000/bike, get fitted; 2016: Cambridge Bicycle 20% bulk discount. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2019, 2018, 2016)*
5. **Learn how your bike works — before day 1.** 2025: riders spent day 1 climbing 15% grades in the hardest gear, unaware of a second set of lower gears; derailleur problems recurred in 2025 (stuck in big ring for 30 mi) and 2026 (chain fell off, missing gears). Nearly all repairs are DIY after reading the manual (sealant top-up, tube swap, derailleur/brake adjust, cables); a shop charges ~$30 to turn a few screws. Go to a shop for wheel truing, first cable snaps, tight schedules. Use the MIT Bike Lab (free weekly repair class). Carry tiny screwdriver, pliers, spare hanger. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2018)*
6. **Housing: Warmshowers and churches are the best hosts; use LF hosts, alumni and campgrounds as backup; fire stations/police/city hall/fairgrounds as emergency.** "Warmshowers: 10/10" (2019), "the BEST ppl ever" (2026, 2025); churches: "single most helpful" (2026 alum advice), 2019 church log shows 30+ churches contacted for one town; call churches early, not 2–3 days before (2024); message 5 hosts each, start early March (2026). *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2019, 2018, 2016, 2015)*
7. **Do not overload day 1/2 and respect the opening ride.** 2026 left DC at 8:40 AM, reached Lincoln Memorial at 10 AM, rode 88.6 mi on 2–4 hours of sleep: most of the team SAG'd. 2025: 82 mi / 5,361 ft "hellish hills." 2022: first day was a ~58-mi plan with cramps. 2026 reflection: most years start from the host's house or take an extra day; "first 2 weeks will be rough no matter what." *(2026, 2025, 2022)*
8. **Keep your team's reputation clean.** "Spokes' lifeblood is its reputation" (2015 handbook entry); 2026 rural-etiquette advice: respect private property, be religiously conscious at churches (no profanity), don't debate politics; thank-you card for every host (2024, 2019, 2017, 2026). 2025 incident: peering into a glassblowing shop window got a deputy called. *(2026, 2025, 2019, 2017, 2015)*
9. **Plan the van carefully: add all drivers in person, check tires, check recalls, photograph the car, use a hitch rack.** 2024: bald front tires, a recalled hybrid Pacifica that stalled in Shenandoah, roadside assistance refused (non-authorized drivers), replacement rental ≈ +$2,000, Avis refunded only 2 days; 2025: Edgerton meeting — extra drivers can only be added in person, max 7 seats; 2024 and 2026: roof bag fell/unzipped (2026 lost pillows, a pad, a chair, hammock on I-65); 2024 non-hitch rack fell off over a speed bump. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2016, 2015)*
10. **Eat, drink and carry more than you think; ice and electrolytes; stop for heat.** 2018: "eat and drink more than you think" (and "pack half as much, carry twice the money"); 2022: Nuun ended cramps on day 1; 2024: ~4,000 cal/day for big riders; 2026: rest stops every 10 mi at 108°F, "ICE" (alum advice), heat exhaustion in 2025 and 2026 (Kansas/KS-MO heat; leave by ~5 AM; cooling towels didn't help). *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2022, 2018)*
11. **Pack light: "Minimize Random Bullshit."** One duffel/backpack each (2025 from 2024 JD, 2026); 2 bike outfits, 2 non-bike outfits (2019, 2025 master guide); van space is the limit. 2026 also found that wet camping gear stored in the roof bag mildewed/smelled — air gear daily. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2019, 2018)*
12. **Everyone must know the day's route, and have an offline copy plus a plan for no cell service.** Download routes (bike computers help), set meeting points in advance, check-ins, a plan if someone doesn't show; cell dies at Capitol Reef/Escalante, Bryce→Zion, Breaks, US-50 (2026, 2025, 2022, 2024). Walkie-talkies worked when cell failed (2026-06-13, 2025 buddy-system norm). *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2022)*
13. **Start fundraising deliverables and records from day 1: receipts.** Photograph receipts immediately (2019); folder per day / text to cardholder (2022–2024); within 2 days (2025 norm); the 2019 team lost a receipt folder (June 2019: also lost park pass and bike lock keys); 2026 had Amazon orders placed on personal accounts so "tax is fucked" — order through the Spokes Amazon Business account. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2023, 2019, 2016)*
14. **Get the ProCard and travel card ASAP (~2 weeks to arrive).** 2023: put everything on the cards so no reimbursement is needed; name PDFs `[last name] mm-dd-yyyy $amount [vendor].pdf`; never buy gas on the ProCard (2025); tax exempt only in MA (2025); 4 cardholders share the finance role (2026). *(2026, 2025, 2023, 2018)*
15. **Don't sell boba (or little else) in Lobby 10 / the Infinite.** "DO NOT sell boba in lobby 10" (2025 from JD); "too much labor, little profit" (2024); same in 2026. Edgerton showcase and Spring Fest = exposure, not money (2024, 2025). What did work: crowdfunding, department emails, J-WEL (2024), bike tune-up raffle (2026), 24-hour challenge. *(2026, 2025, 2024)*
16. **Sleep, bedtimes and start times are underestimated.** It takes 90 minutes (no less) from alarm to everyone on bikes (2023, 2024 same figure); 2016: starting all together failed — last rider leaves within 2 hours of the first; 2026: a 2 AM night delayed the next start by 90 min; 2022: planned 3 AM wake never happened. *(2026, 2024, 2023, 2022, 2016)*
17. **Never split up in storms; have a storm protocol.** 2024: Kansas Flint Hills thunderstorm, flooded road crossed alone, 30 minutes of indecision; "hosts asked every week what the team would do in a storm"; 2026 advice: get down, away from the road, stay with someone; if the van deems it dangerous and comes back, you HAVE to get in (2025, 2026). *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2022)*

### Tier 2 — said by 2–3 teams

18. **Pick LF hosts that already know you; ~10 LFs per trip is solid; repeat Hazard KY (Challenger), DC correctional facility, Fallon Youth Club, Tazewell, Bowling Green.** 2026/2025 crash courses, 2019 reflections ("continuity matters — Hazard shows how a strong connection can mean a lot"), 2018 school feedback (Challenger 5/5). Do more correctional-facility LFs (2026). Confirm date with the school *before* arranging anything else; no Sundays (2018, 2019) — 2022 Fallon Sunday LF drew only ~20. *(2026, 2025, 2022, 2019, 2018)*
19. **LF design rules.** Hands-on over lectures (2018, 2016); teach in pairs and revise after the first or second teaching (2018, 2019); cut content to a third for middle schoolers (2017); design by age — juvenile: no razor cutters, pre-cut everything (DC bans scissors/glass/sharp objects; 2026 team precut felt/cardboard/paper the night before); elementary: help with cutting; high school: goal-driven; turnover 8–10 min (2026); 3D printing for 40+ kids is the hardest to run (2025); bottle rocket demos need goggles and a checked bottle (2025). Finish class prep before the spring crunch (2019: "class prep done way too late"). *(2026, 2025, 2019, 2018, 2017)*
20. **Learning-festival host emails: some coordinators are bad at email — just call; keep a status-tracking spreadsheet** (2019). Follow-up email checklist: registration graphic with edit access to the form, workshop descriptions, space/capacity, food, photo release (all parents must sign), housing (2026 master guide). *(2026, 2019, 2017)*
21. **Do a scavenger hunt / side quests only on short days.** 2025 Placerville: first rest stop took 5 hours; 2026 Folsom→Benicia 108 mi with ~10 side quests: arrived 3:20 AM, 18 hours; freezing, van left leading riders unsupported ~2 hours. Set time limits; protect the sleep window; don't drink caffeine late. *(2026, 2025)*
22. **Don't reroute onto unfamiliar backcountry trails; check Street View for shoulders.** 2026 near Zion: a closed trail with a $5,000 fine; Drew's bike went off a cliff in a rockslide; 2026 Day 2: a rest-stop right turn sent a rider onto a four-lane highway; 2025/2024: US-285, Elkhorn Road, I-70, Hwy 85 no-shoulder; "ask locals before committing to a road" (2025). Compare old route files for detours before reusing them (2026 cut a 7-mile ice-cream detour inherited from 2025). *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2022)*
23. **Check in with and protect riders with injuries/sore spots; sharp repetitive pain means stop.** 2026: PT (Brendan, Cycle Life PT) rule used to SAG a rider at mile 65; anything beyond 1–2 weeks of aches is "get that checked out"; 2024 broken arm on steep Byway-12 descent (rider skipped brakes); 2025 gravel crash (Tian) and a Kentucky fall; 2026 helmet crash with 1-year helmet warranty replacement; hit your head → ER (2026 alum advice). Every recent team had ≥1 injury (2024). *(2026, 2025, 2024)*
24. **Share the daily chores; the driver has the heaviest day.** Driver: groceries, water stops, bulk of van packing, laundry the night before, thank-you card in the morning (2026, 2024, 2019); 2025: a driver packing a campsite alone took ~2 hours; build a shopping list to avoid four supermarket stops; 2016: non-drivers handled dishes/locks/night cleanup. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2019, 2016)*
25. **Bonding is a real project.** 2026: weekly Monday meeting + dinner, "bonding activity EXTREMELY IMPORTANT," Midnight Marathon = bonding + training; 2025: weekly meetings became 2–3-hour "yap sessions"; JD (2024): dinner together every night, NYT 36 questions; 2015: Hot Seat, Moth stories. Team norms: show up, CC everyone on every email, decide by discussion not vote, give benefit of the doubt (2026-02-02 norms); 2025 norms set only on trip day 3 (receipts, cards, walkie-talkies, daily tire pressure) — set them *before* departure. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2015)*
26. **Two-way rental if someone wants a road trip home.** 2026/2025: cheaper and saves "the fuck-ass logistics" of shipping bikes; 2025 drove SF→Boston via northern national parks (10 days, $194 campsites; book Glacier/Tetons early; propane stove failure cost 2+ hours in Helena MT); 2024 shipped bikes — "logistical hell"; 2023 and 2024 did one-way: "in hindsight maybe not the best." Counter-note: 2018 also drove home (Aug 6–16). *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2018)*
27. **Ask restaurants for free food; ask hosts for the next day's roads.** 2026: one person called places each night; "extremely successful," alum Manaas: "just sell your cause"; 2026 Jen (Linden host) warned about sharp low-visibility turns on the planned 60-mi/~8,000-ft day and the team swapped to 30 mi/2,000 ft. *(2026, 2024)*
28. **Dogs.** 2026: Day 13 dog bite at Breaks (rabies shots, 2 counties argued over jurisdiction, ~1 hour for rescue squad); follow-car tactic 10 ft behind at 10 mph worked; mace used on a pack; 6–8 dogs on Day 16; 2024: air horns not enough; sprinting/walking the bike/barking back worked; 2022 master guide: air horn and mace for dogs; 2026-06-13 team vote: everyone has the right not to ride any day they feel unsafe. Decide a protocol before the day; photograph owner plates. *(2026, 2024, 2022)*
29. **Keep a floor pump / backup pump and tire pressure discipline.** 2026: tubeless over-inflated (max 35 psi) caused "flat after flat in Kansas"; 2025 norm: pump daily to 40–50 psi — **conflict**: 2025 norm applied to a tubeless team that also reported micro-leaks; resolve by checking the actual tire's rated pressure and testing leaks (should lose only a few psi in days); 2024 hand pump only reached ~40 psi; 2022 pump broke (SAG brought replacement). Carry spare tubes even with tubeless. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2022)*
30. **Know the weather/wind direction logic:** West→east is "lame" because of headwinds (2026, 2025 crash courses) but 2015 argued the westward prevailing wind is "largely a myth" and finishing in California was prettier; 2017 debated Appalachians (short/steep) vs Rockies (long/gradual). DC → SF has been the direction every year since 2015. *(2026, 2025, 2015)*

### Tier 3 — one-off but high-impact (single team, concrete incident)
- **Allergy / anaphylaxis 60+ mi from a hospital** (2025, Baker NV): almond flour in a cafe muffin; EpiPen from a teammate; nearest urgent care 45 min away. Ask about ingredients; tell Warmshowers hosts about dietary restrictions (2018) and allergies. Carry EpiPens; know nearest urgent care. *(2025, 2018)*
- **Bike shop deserts:** Hazard KY nearest bike shop 2 hours away (2025); Green River UT has none (2025); Utah/Nevada gear-replacement scramble on 2026-07-29 (calls to SLC, Las Vegas, Cedar City, Trek Checkpoint ALR 3 size L); US-285/Moab problems. Carry spare parts and ask Trek about replacements. *(2026, 2025)*
- **Confirm host address before riding:** 2022 host lived 27 mi past the destination; a 70-mi day became 97. *(2022)*
- **Check the support vehicle's road:** 2024 Capitol Reef — "avoid interstates" routing sent the van onto boulder gravel; riders collected 16 bottles from strangers. *(2024)*
- **Confirm the campsite by phone**, not a verbal "OK from town": Austin NV park banned overnight camping (2022). *(2022)*
- **Don't deep-clean bikes the night before a big day** — brakes loosened (2025); check brakes before long descents — Drew's overheated brakes (2026). *(2026, 2025)*
- **Mechanics:** ask the mechanic to test-ride and listen for the noise (2024); replace chains early (2024: new chains in Moab; "things start breaking once you start to replace them" — host David, Denver); carry spare derailleur hanger (2024). *(2024)*
- **Drafting too close caused a crash** (2024); double draft lines break on climbs (2026). *(2026, 2024)*
- **Hammocks:** the two hammocks brought from home were missing parts and 50°F nights made them poor; bring tents. *(2026)*
- **Costco requires the cardholder at checkout** (2026 DC); some Utah stores don't accept Visa travel cards (2019). *(2026, 2019)*
- **Nevada resupply:** stock up on food and propane in Ely at the latest; Eureka/Austin have next to nothing (2019); no services Grand Junction→Moab for 40–50 mi (2025). *(2025, 2019)*
- **Water:** carry extra jugs in the car for Utah/Nevada (2019); no support at first stop on a 100-mi desert day (2024).
- **Late arrivals:** carry working bike lights (2026, 2025, 2024, 2019: waterproof lights); 1,000+ lumen front for rural roads (2025/2026 advice); finish before dark when possible. *(2026, 2025, 2024, 2019)*

## What worked / what didn't

### 2026
- **Worked:** weekly meetings + weekly team dinner and bonding nights; 99% of housing booked before the start; first-run LF at DC Youth Services Center/DC jail (small wins, interns from the judge's office helped); 4 rotating workshops; raffle of donated Trek/Cambridge Bicycle tune-ups; calling places each night for free food; one-way rental with a separate cargo van to DC for bikes; PT bike fit (two one-hour sessions covered by insurance, "highly recommends").
- **Didn't:** Day 2 (see above); Day 4 wet gear mildewed; Day 18 roof bag lost camping items on I-65; Kentucky dogs (Day 13 bite); Hazard→London "Hell for Certain Road" (avoid); Zion backcountry reroute (Day 59); Day 74 side quests to 3:20 AM; Kansas flats from over-inflated tubeless; Day 28 KC heat: "not flat; ~5,000 ft climbing"; **Blog fell behind** (no blog lead: "super behind"); no Finance role clarity (it became the 4 cardholders); budget: food recorded only $2.4K of $7.2K, bikes/gear overspent by $1,949 (11.5%); 2026 team cooked 0 meals.
- **Their "should have" list:** tiny screwdriver and various pliers; decide flight/personal-gear reimbursement rules before the trip; document gear distribution; try gear early so returns are easy; a Blog Lead and a Documentation role.

### 2025
- **Worked:** Trek gravel bikes, tubeless (far fewer flats than 2024), 99% housing planned (only northern NV was hard), practice LF in Framingham 4 weeks before departure, 10 LFs / ~400 students, team norms (receipts, walkie-talkie buddy system), 2-way rental + scenic drive home via Pacific NW/Glacier/Yellowstone/Badlands.
- **Didn't:** day 1 gear confusion; micro-leaks in tubeless by Colorado; stove/propane failure on the drive home; Breaks campground rain; Skyline Drive; Hell for Certain Rd; stop-sign near-miss; July 4 reroute errors; heat dizziness (Tian); gravel crashes.
- **Their summary advice (2025 → 2026):** pre-trip ≥2-night bike tour; lights ≥1,000 lumens; same bike; wax-based chain lube; practice clip-ins; derailleur-side up; 24-hour challenge on Pi Day; decide reimbursements before trip.

### 2024
- **Worked:** J-WEL grant ($45,330 — "research grant application"; apply March, answer late April; requires photo essays, learning toolkits, follow-up surveys, tighter reporting); Midnight Marathon as fundraiser; 2 travel + 2 pro cards (driver and bike crew each); lunch break daily (2023 had none).
- **Didn't:** Avis van chaos (see #9); three different bike types; many flats on tubes; fell behind on blogs ("designate one blog lead; post a pre-trip blog — people offer help"); shipping bikes ("logistical hell"; REI boxes oversized; use Bike Flights); US-285 and US-50 out of Tahoe; Kansas storm indecision; third rest day in Tahoe unneeded; Moab 106-mi day with 6 flats; broken arm on Byway 12; E. coli swim at Capitol Reef.
- **Their advice (2024 → 2025):** tax-free forms expire 10/2024 → re-apply; apply for the 24-challenge micro-challenges in October; Edgerton finance person will sit down and brainstorm fundraising; in-kind is easier than money; move website off Wix; ship online orders to a future host; wash sleeping bags before leaving; "it gets easier — by the end a 60-mile day was chill."

### 2023
- Get cards ASAP; email receipt PDFs to Edgerton instead of filing in the system (2023 finance lead Joy); 90 minutes from alarm to bikes; reroute Kansas further north and consider Manhattan KS for MIT-alum housing instead of Newton/Warmshowers/churches. Video ideas (reels/TikTok beat blog posts for reach, 2023). Food spreadsheet/inventory tracked use-by dates (2023). One-way rental later judged not great (2024 note).

### 2022
- 6 riders (7 planned), tubeless tires meant ~10 flats over 80 days; MIT News Q&A: LFs in juvenile-justice facilities taught in DC & MA; hosts often found lodging and press (Sam Ford's outreach got 2 TV stations at Bowling Green); "rest days were never really rest days" (hiking, caves); accidentally deleted a spreadsheet row and lost July 7 → an unplanned rest day; first-day expectations meeting (5/31): always tell someone where you're going; time tsar; "build in extra time, things take longer than we think"; driver duties (site, water, laundry, ice); cook in pairs and "whoever doesn't cook, cleans"; Warmshowers at rural spots (Mayor Jerry of Wayland KY).

### 2019
- Bikes arrived ~1 month before departure; jerseys run small and shrink (order a size up); only two bike outfits; motels can fit 8 in one room; fire stations/police departments as a last resort; 120-mile Nevada day split 55/35/30; budget-spreadsheet and receipt discipline matter (lost folder); LF prep too late; practice LF before leaving Boston; don't schedule LFs on major holidays or Sundays.

### 2018
- Sequence host vs LF wrongly and you must rearrange: confirm LF date first (2018). "Always a little more adventure than we expect." In-kind bike kit "sucked, you probably want to invest." TI said no (2018) while 2016/2017 got $7,000/$10,000 — corporate giving is inconsistent. Day-2 DC debrief: eat and drink more than you think; group A/B navigator system; essentials kit.

### 2017 and earlier
- 2017: ~140 companies and ~40 MIT departments tracked on one sheet; 30 flats in 3,579 mi; "Little Powow" on-trip tweaks: earlier starts, shorter checkpoints, blog/Insta 3×/week, thank-you cards for hosts, navigation in two groups of 3.
- 2016: lodging mix ≈ 40% campground / 30% Warmshowers / 20% church / 10% other; 5 of 9 could drive; "learn how to ask for help." Amazon tip: MIT is tax-exempt, don't pay tax on items sold by Amazon LLC.
- 2015: 7 members, DC→SF, a 2004 Honda Odyssey support van ("10/10 every team should have one"), grants "didn't work" (corporate + Indiegogo + personal donors); 2014: send two emails (personal intro + formal PDF pitch), one owner per email chain, don't over-promise sponsor deliverables.
- 2014/2013: crowdfunding, fellowship workshops (PSC fellowship: community partner, safety, budget, sustainability; $1,500–$4,000 per person).
