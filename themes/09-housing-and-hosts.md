# Housing & hosts — churches, Warmshowers, alumni, schools, campgrounds, motels
_Last built: 2026-11-24. Sources: ~45 docs, 195 blog posts (via `blogs/by-tag/housing.md`; camping/local-people tags skimmed), 0 emails (none cover housing). Recent years first; every fact has its year._

## TL;DR for 2027
- **Plan ~99% of housing before departure.** 2026 and 2025 both did and call it "1000x easier"; the only real trouble spot was northern Nevada (2026, 2025). Finalize all housing in May at the latest (2026, 2024; 2024 goal was housing through 6/21 set by 5/21).
- **Pick towns after checking hosts, not before.** 2026 drew the route first, then had to change several stops for lack of Warmshowers hosts. Keep the Warmshowers map, Google Maps and USBRS maps open together (2026, 2024).
- **Start in February:** Warmshowers outreach, church calls and emails, and campsite bookings (national parks fill fast) (2026, 2025). In 2025 the Utah/Colorado/Virginia/Kentucky campsites were booked Mar–Apr 2025; 2026 booked Apr–May 2026.
- **Stay mix to expect:** Warmshowers (the "BEST ppl ever") + churches + learning-festival (LF) hosts + alumni/friends/family + campgrounds. 2025 sheet: 15 Warmshowers, 9 churches, 3 "Spokie fam" (27 non-campground nights, plus campgrounds); 2022 by frequency: Warmshowers, campsites, LFs, friends, MIT connections, churches, motels (2025, 2022). 2016 split: campgrounds 40%, Warmshowers 30%, churches 20%, other 10% (2016).
- **Put 2–3 people on housing** (2026 had 3: one on Warmshowers, one on campsites). 2027 housing/route team per first meeting: Liv, Neelu, Noah, Max (2027, 2026).
- **Warmshowers rules:** only message hosts who take 2+ people (2026 used 4+, which got "surprisingly nice houses"); say you have a support car; offer floor/backyard; get phone + address once confirmed; re-confirm a week out (2026).
- **Churches: call AND email, early, and many of them** — 5 per city (2023, 2024). Many never respond; calling 2–3 days before fails (2024, 2019). Churches were still ~9 of 27 stays in 2025.
- **Ask every LF host for lodging** — "most of the time a learning festival = free housing" (2025, 2026). Ask *after* the date is confirmed (2024, 2023).
- **Be religiously conscious and non-political around church/rural hosts**; write a thank-you card for every host (driver does it the morning of) (2026, 2024, 2019).
- **Last resort = cheap motel/hotel** (2024, 2023, 2019) — but 2024 "took an L" on the Ely Motel 6 (2024), so line up Nevada earlier.

## Playbook — how to do it
1. **Choose the base route** (US Bike Route 76 / TransAm DC → Colorado, then Utah/Nevada/California) and reuse prior years' stops because hosts and LF partners already exist there (2026 master guide, 2016). *Source: [2026 route/housing master guide](../docs/master-guide/sub-guides/guides/2026_route-planning-and-housing-master-guide.md).*
2. **Open the three maps at once** (Warmshowers host map, Google Maps, USBRS) and fix each night's town only after confirming a realistic host/campsite/church exists within ~10 miles; "find housing before choosing towns" (2026, 2024).
3. **Build the housing sheet** (copy the 2026 "Housing" tab): columns Date, Point B, Possible Host, Type (WS/Church/LF/Alum/Friend-Fam/Airbnb/Camping), Host contact, Spokie in contact, Contacted?, Confirmed?, Address, Meals, Laundry, Shower, Notes (2026). One owner per state (2024: Sophia = VA, Amulya = KY).
4. **Re-use known hosts first.** Check the 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023 route sheets, 2022 Route Plan, 2019 "Route Details", 2018 route sheet; use the "if we stayed there in…" email variant (2022, 2025). Strongest repeat hosts are listed under Key contacts.
5. **Warmshowers:** make an account (the 2026 team made a new one; 2024 reused the shared 2023 account and chose not to pay for a new one — conflict, see below). State you have a support car. Message hosts who take 2+ (ideally 4+) people. Template in "Templates". Ask for phone and address once confirmed; re-confirm 1 week before; driver coordinates day-of (2026).
6. **Churches:** for each town without a confirmed host, email 5 churches and phone them (voicemail is normal). Mention you only need a floor or yard. Ask about kitchen, showers, laundry, and whether they can feed you (2026 follow-up template). Churches have "big kitchens, many bathrooms, sometimes snacks/meals" (2026); but often no showers (2019).
7. **Alumni:** (a) email MIT alumni clubs along the route (2026: only 3 replied, but met Sam Ford of Kentucky, one donation; 2025 sent the state-by-state "Housing Alumni Request" list; 2023 tracked who contacted each state club); (b) search alum.mit.edu/directory (needs Infinite Connection login) by course + town (2024); (c) past Spokes alumni (2022 outreach email); (d) friends/family of every team member (2022 "potential hosts" sheet, 2026 family-booked Airbnbs).
8. **Learning-festival hosts:** once the LF date is confirmed, ask the LF contact if they can lodge you or know someone (2026, 2024, 2023). Examples: Hazard KY Challenger Center → Harmony House B&B (2017–2026); Fallon Youth Club (2024, 2026); White Pine High School Ely (2026, 2025); Bowling Green Accelerate KY partners donated Airbnbs (2026, 2025, 2024).
9. **Campgrounds:** book in Feb–Apr (Recreation.gov, ReserveAmerica/Utah State Parks, Colorado Parks, Breaks via guest portal). Prefer sites with showers. Buy America the Beautiful pass(es) (1 per 4 people) (2024). See campground table below.
10. **Fallbacks:** fire stations / police / city hall / library / rec dept → free park or fairground (2019, 2024; Eureka NV fairground 2024); cheap motel/hotel (8 in one room possible in 2019, "be sneaky"); donated Airbnbs from family (2026).
11. **Food & diet with hosts:** send a dietary-restrictions form (2024 Google Form; 2025 constraints: 1 vegan, 2 no pork, 1 nut allergy) and ask hosts "dinner/breakfast or kitchen?" (2026, 2025). 2026 cooked zero meals: a "dinner lead" phoned places nightly for free food — very successful (2026).
12. **During the trip:** driver reconfirms; thank-you card each host (driver writes it the morning of) (2024); offer to help cook/clean, clean up after yourselves, consolidate belongings (2025 team norms); quiet hours as requested (2026 Linden: 9pm–6am; 2025: 8pm–6:30am). Ask hosts about tomorrow's roads (2026 Linden host redirected the route). Leave a review on Warmshowers.
13. **After:** send thank-you postcards/t-shirts to hosts (2016 postcard list), review hosts honestly in the route sheet notes (2022, 2026).

## What worked / what didn't
**2026**
- Worked: 99% housing set before leaving; 3-person route/housing team; contacting only hosts who take 4+; family-donated Airbnbs/hotels (Moab, Bryce, Cedar City, Tahoe, Parachute, Colorado Springs, Bowling Green) cut cost; churches carried the rural stretches (Tazewell VA, Liberty KY, Madisonville KY, Emporia KS, Macksville KS, Lakin KS, Lamar CO, Fowler CO, Milford UT, Baker NV, Eureka NV); phoning pizza shops/restaurants for donated dinners; many hosts read the blog (Marshall MO).
- Didn't: route drawn before checking hosts → rerouting; Linden VA (Jen & Jim) "a bit awkward, baby woken, messy" on a 88-mi first day; Janet (VA, 2026 reflection) "a lot for them to host 7, weird place to sleep, smells"; Colorado Springs and Idaho Springs Airbnbs fine but pricey (Idaho Springs $304); Liberty KY church shower "sketchy"; church hosts sometimes ask for early departure (Milford UT 7:30am, Sunday service); Baker NV church had no kitchen / clogged bathroom (2025).
- Lesson: avoid hosts too small for the group; one night in a place "for rest" works better than back-to-back long days (2026 reflection).
**2025**
- Worked: church gyms (Madisonville KY), churches with showers + dinner (Macksville KS), Katy Trail Airbnbs, alumni hosts in Denver (Maud & Dave), Didde Catholic Center in Emporia, LF-partner Airbnbs in Bowling Green; Bowling Green LF partner "best airbnb ever."
- Didn't: Breaks Interstate Park campground "rained on, wouldn't recommend"; Wingate Campground "sucks"; Moab and Zion housing hard — book campgrounds early; Placerville church had no laundry/shower ("find somewhere better").
**2024**
- Worked: alumni hosts (Heather Cogdell, Oakton VA; Dr. Sherman, Roanoke), Warmshowers winery host (Draper VA), Lamar Community College converted-motel dorms, Eureka NV county fairground, Fallon Youth Club.
- Didn't: Ely Motel 6 ("We took an L"); churches hard to reach at short notice — call early (2024 handoff); Warmshowers new-account fee led to reusing shared account.
**2023**
- Worked: Warmshowers + "5 churches per city" + alumni; recurring hosts (Dirk, Francine, Heather, Jennifer & Curtis, Steve Andes). Joy's advice: reroute Kansas north / swap Newton LF for Manhattan KS to stay with alumni instead of Warmshowers/churches.
**2022**
- Worked: many stays via Sophia's church members; MIT alumni network in Kentucky (Sam Ford); a mini-network of favorite hosts (see below). Negative reviews recorded: Rolla MO (Kathryn Northcut: dislikes support vehicle), Newton KS (Janet Voth: weird vibes, Stanford Spokes agreed), Silverthorne CO (Roberta Flack: racist/sexist comments, harsh review, do not stay again).
**2019**
- Churches: Leah's log (Front Royal VA, Berea KY, St. Paul VA, Elizabethtown KY, Fordsville KY, Limon CO, Colby KS, Burlington KS): ~66 church rows; mostly no response; church may email its congregation to find home hosts; cast a wide net (2019).
- Advice: Warmshowers "10/10"; camping fun but costs money and rain makes packing miserable; motels anywhere; fire stations/police may suggest parks to pitch tents; plan well in advance but not perfectly (2019).
**2018 / 2017 / 2016**
- 2018: sequence hosts and LFs carefully — LFs on Sundays are a no-go in religious areas; confirm school date first (2018). Confirmations for Warmshowers by April 15 (2018).
- 2017: Caity's outreach log: two Warmshowers templates (>5 people vs <5 people) + confirmation message; wrote the wrong date twice ("JUNE 14TH MAKE SURE") — double-check dates; Nevada plan A/B/C of campgrounds/churches/RV parks; Hazard B&B $50/person (half price for bikers) (2017).
- 2016: "Spokes' lifeblood is its reputation": 2015 rude behavior in a small town reached a makerspace host (2016 handbook). Local parks via city hall/fire/rec dept (2015 per 2016 handbook).
