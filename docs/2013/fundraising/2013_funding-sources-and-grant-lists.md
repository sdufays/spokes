## What the 2013 team actually pursued (Funding Sources.docx)
- **Peter J. Eloranta Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship** -- $6,000; due Apr 1 (extended Apr 5); SUBMITTED (budget, recommendation, 2,000-word summary).
- **UROP Direct Funding** -- all applying; due Apr 18; $4,625 at $9.75/h; hear back May 2; needs faculty enthusiasm/department coordinator support; special funds list (Class of 1992 fund = specifically K-12 education; Class of 1948/1972/1982 funds etc.).
- **Kelly-Douglas Fund** (shass.mit.edu) -- up to $1,000, due Apr 16 noon; not awarded if substantial external funding; one recommendation, cover sheet, 2-page summary.
- **Tau Beta Pi Fellowship** (web.mit.edu/tbp) -- submitted; $2,000-5,000 typical, due mid-April (the 2016 team applied again).
- **Pressman Award** (poli sci only; 3-5 page proposal, 2 recs, resume; $6,000, due Apr 9) -- listed as funding received ($6,000) in budget.
- **Baker Foundation** -- emailed; fellowship applications closed; pitching for special separate funding; dinner May 7; $1,000 received.
- **DUE Grants for Student Ideas** -- rolling, hundreds to thousands (got $270). Edgerton -- visited, "cannot fund" in 2013 (later years Edgerton gave $500-4,000).
- Others on list: Awesome Foundation ($1,000 rolling), Do Something Awards (due Apr 15; $500 seed grants), Shuttleworth, DonorsChoose (needs precise supply list), Kairos, Draper Richard Kaplan ($300K, for later), Katerva (too slow), Legatum (deadlines passed), Gates/Radix Labs, Lemelson, ASME, Bikes Not Bombs, STE@M, amicus; corporations: Trader Joe's, REI, Amgen, Raspberry Pi Foundation, Upward Bound, Sal Khan/Lifelong Kindergarten.

Internal: Lemelson-MIT Student Prize $30,000; $100K D-track; CAMIT arts grants (Director's grant up to $500 rolling); Grants for Student Ideas (DUE); Kelly-Douglas travelling fellowships $1,000; Eloranta $6,000; TBP $2-5k; UROP direct funding semester $1,300 / summer $4,400; UAAP Class of 2010 summer housing grant $1,000; IDI Tech Dissemination; Legatum; PON summer fellowships up to $3,500; Carroll Wilson up to $7,000 (grad). External: Awesome Foundation $1,000; Do Something $100,000 grand prize/$500 seed; Draper Richards Kaplan $100K/yr x3; Echoing Green $60-90K; McNulty Prize $100K; Shuttleworth; Skoll; Acumen; Aspen; Fulbright; Unreasonable Institute (Boulder, June 12-Jul 24 2013). Most are post-grad social-entrepreneur programs -- low fit for a summer team.
