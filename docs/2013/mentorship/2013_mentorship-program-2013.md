**Design:** weekly one-hour video chats for 3-6 months (Spokes site HQ team in Cambridge: Josh Haimson, Seve Esparrago, Anna Leonard, Shivani Kaushal, Aakanksha Sarda, Tyler Willette designing). Phases: 0 pre-meeting prep (why do you do what you do; inspirational videos) -> 1 build excitement and curiosity ("career-day for online education"; leading questions about home, hobbies, lottery, hero, favorite teacher) -> 2 explore specific interests / starter projects (Instructables) -> 3 ideate and outline original project (PM spreadsheet, budget up to ~$200 per project) -> 4 create (weekly goals, 10 min relationship + 10 min scrum "what done/what next/what blocked" + 5 min actions) -> 5 show and tell (final presentation, Timeline.js). Mentee nicknames: "Padawan"/"young grasshopper"; "scavenger hunt" question list for mentees to interview mentors. Post-meeting Google form.
**Recruiting/vetting:** Google-form application (areas of interest, who are you, prior experience, "a topic you were taught but don't understand", mentor ideals); interview; mentors 18+; **background checks: national sex-offender registry (nsopw.gov) + state criminal record (MA CORI)**, cost ~$25, mentor pays then reimbursed; 5 mentor invites per person via Facebook ("messages on Facebook work well"); mentor response template (contact between May 20-27). Candidates: Kevin Rustagi (MIT alum, entrepreneur), Maggie Lloyd (MIT '10 ChemE, DC science policy), Catherine Johnson (U Minnesota civil eng. grad student), Kuljot Anand, Patra Virasathienpornkul, Missy Showers, Morrisa Brenner, Rene Peters, Draper Lab staff list (Abramson, Avila, Carlton-Foss, Chaparala, Fiering, Johnson, Kwok, Mueller, Stroud @draper.com).
**Partner orgs considered:** Moneythink, Mowgli Foundation, Ashoka Youth Ventures, TechStars, NFTE (Steve Mariotti), Science from Scientists. International: Skoltech (Irina Zhelavskaya).
**Meeting thoughts (4/11):** the hard part is relationships continue; match from both sides, hand-pick, "think of the site as a dating site"; start narrow. Ted Gonder's three elements: relationship/trust, a reason for students to stay in touch (10-week program with weekly deliverables), local partner (futurefounders.com, youthbuild.org).
**Outcome:** by 7/25/2013 team decided exposure over mentoring; HQ site/mentor matching never became central. Whiteboard photo of Phase 0/1 planning saved in media (2013/other).
