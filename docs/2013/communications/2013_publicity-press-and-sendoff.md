## Templates
Blog pitch: one-sentence connection, why you like their blog, what Spokes is, **three reasons it fits that blog**, offer photos, launch date. Launch-day emails: influential people, friends who don't know / do know Spokes, acquaintances/lists; pre-made FB/Twitter posts; Kickstarter profile picture. Funding email (Titiaan): ask for the final 20% of $25k; offer a custom short film, your name on gear, a workshop at your office. Press pitch (end of summer): "What do you get when you mix MIT, hands-on learning for high-school students and bicycles?" with Q&A attached, links to Forbes and MIT blog + start/end videos. Dutch funding email (see Titiaan doc).
