## Safety/medical (Medical Supplies List ~$90 total, ~$17/person): acetaminophen, ibuprofen, bacitracin, hydrocortisone, benzalkonium wipes, toe covers, fingertip/knuckle/winged bandages, butterfly strips, nonstick pads, triangular bandages, 2nd Skin burn pads, self-sticking gauze, tweezers, scissors, blister pads, gloves, medical tape. Two first-aid kits; pepper spray; satellite phone rental considered (Iridium 9555 $299.57; later dropped to cut budget); walkie-talkies with throat mics. Each member also filled a "Health Info" sheet (allergies, insurance, emergency contact) kept in first-aid pack -- individual details omitted here.
## Camera/video kit (for documentary): Canon 60D $699, Sigma 18-200 $349, Manfrotto fluid head/tripod, Zoom H4n $270, shock mount, SD cards 3x32 GB, battery chargers; total ~$1,852-1,941; GoPro bought instead; San Jose list pared version.
## Personal packing list: waterproof clothes set, cycle shorts, 3 jerseys/t-shirts, 2 warm sweaters, 2 jeans, teaching outfit (t-shirt and jeans), cycling shoes, slippers, swimwear, towel, sleeping mat, toiletries, socks/underwear; only a small duffle each (gas cost: smallest car possible). Movies, books, laptops, phones/charging, car charger, earplugs.
## Food rules of thumb (Food Info): ~5,000 kcal/person/day (1.5-2x normal), ~2 lb food and **7 L water capacity per person** per day (drink 4-6 L); cost **~$7/person/day** ($1 breakfast, $1 snacks, $2 cold lunch, $3 hot dinner) buying in bulk. Breakfast: ziploc-bag station of oatmeal, muesli, powdered milk, hot chocolate, raisins; snacks: bagels, fig rolls, trail mix, jerky, cheese; lunch: sandwiches/tortillas/tuna/hummus mix; dinner: potatoes, soup mixes, couscous/quinoa, pasta, beans+rice. Always purify water unless known-safe tap. Cooking in rotation of two.
