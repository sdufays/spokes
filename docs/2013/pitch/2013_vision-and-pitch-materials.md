**Context:** 2013 was the first Spokes: 9 MIT + UC Berkeley students bike SF to DC (3,681.5 mi in ~71 days) running "Learning Festivals" and pairing students with remote mentors (see mentorship doc). Tagline: "Inspiring students to learn what they love"; also "Dedicated to revealing the boundless nature of learning" and "Learn what you love". Later years dropped mentorship and focused on STEM workshops.

**30-second pitch:** 9 MIT/UC Berkeley students riding ~3,860 miles SF to DC, 10-15 stops, project-based workshops, plus using data gathered to devise a new way of quantifying achievement (not test scores).
**One-pager ("Spokes in a Page"):** long-term vision = every US student works with a mentor on a project of their own choosing; summer = test the system; 8 bike 4,000 miles giving workshops (build sun-tracking mechanisms, grow vegetables, program a computer to draw plants, map the brain), then match each student with a mentor for weekly video chat; sites spokesamerica.org / blog.spokesamerica.org.
**Two goals (Goal doc):** (1) engage communities lacking academic culture and follow up for 6 months so Spokes isn't "a one-day circus"; (2) devise a better measure of academic achievement.
**Educational purpose notes:** make learning active/exploratory; project-based; mentors beyond the trip; "Take what kids like. Make learning like that"; Ethan's L-system blog essay ("from How does... to What if...").
**Impressions to make:** US invented much of what surrounds us; "we went to the moon because someone said let's do this"; learning for its own sake.
**Ethan's proposal (letter):** nine college students, schools across the nation, courses on outlandish subjects (procedural plant generation, neuroscience, management); will launch Spokes as non-profit after summer; budgeted **$30,000** for the whole trip and 100+ classes.
**Slide deck facts:** team of 9 (Turner Bohlen '14, Jeff Prouty '14, Claire O'Connell '14, Bruno Faviero '15, Phillip Daniels '13, Ethan Sherbondy '14, Manny Singh '16, Nathan Kennedy UCB '13, Natasha Balwit '16); route 60 mi/day, 1 rest day in 10; training 3 group rides/week, Sunday long rides max 100 mi; food/water 6 L water and 5,000 kcal per person per day, longest stretch without resupply 2 days (Hanksville-Blanding UT), <15 lb food/person, $200/person; safety: 2 first-aid kits, required training, pepper spray; gear: Jamis Aurora and Trek 520, $2,750/person, ~40 lb/person; classes 60 minutes, requested online; 10-20 destinations via TFA, schools, libraries, FIRST/robotics, summer programs; interviews with public-school teachers.
**Quotes used:** Rita Pierson "Teaching and learning should bring joy"; Ken Robinson "tyranny of common sense"; Lincoln "dogmas of the quiet past".
