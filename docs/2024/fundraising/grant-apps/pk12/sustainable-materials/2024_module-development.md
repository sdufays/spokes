See "Sustainable Materials: Reimagining the Manufacturing of the Future" for the full proposal. Session 1 (2.5h): discussion of materials through history, plastic age lecture, environmental impact (>2.75 bn t CO2 by 2050 estimate; ~450 years to degrade), renewable biomass/circular systems/digital fabrication; Activity 1 3D printing with biofilaments (recycled PLA/PETG, PHA, wood PLA, carbon-fiber PETG; groups argue material choice, slice test file, print). Session 2: Activity 2 sustainable mold making (glucose-gelatin-glycerol into clay/silicone molds, chill); Activity 3 biocomposites (eggshell + xanthan paste printed with retrofitted printer; explore materiom.org). Adaptations: use local materials (Boston oyster shells, Maine maple syrup, forests/wood densification, bamboo); middle vs high school worksheet depth. Teacher PD: 1-hr in-person training on loading filaments/pastes. Budget narrative: Prusa/Elegoo printers, bio extruder, filaments, molding materials.
