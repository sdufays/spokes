Ideally go in person or call the restaurant/cafe.
1. Introduce yourself and say you and your team frequent the location.
2. Describe Spokes briefly: biking D.C. to San Francisco with MIT Spokes, 8 MIT students; free Learning Festivals at schools, libraries, camps; funds pay for bike gear, camping fees, food, gas, educational equipment; aiming for $40,330 for the 74-day trip; share donation link and MIT corporate matching info.
3. Ask about either/both: (a) sponsorship/donation (we put them on our website in exchange); (b) an event/day where part of proceeds go to Spokes.
