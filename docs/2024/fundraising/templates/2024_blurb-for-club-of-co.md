Spokes is an MIT-based, non-profit, student-run initiative as part of an effort to rethink STEAM education. This year will be the 10th iteration of this program at MIT. This summer, Spokes is sending a team of 8 MIT students on a cross-country bike ride starting on the steps of the Lincoln Memorial in Washington, D.C. and ending at the Golden Gate Bridge in San Francisco. Along our route, we will stop at schools, libraries, correctional facilities, and camps throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions. A goal of Spokes is to introduce science and design in exciting ways to students who may not have access to such opportunities.

We are currently crowdfunding for our trip this summer, and would love to have your support! Crowdfunding link: https://crowdfund.mit.edu/story/Mit-Spokes-2024 . Check whether your company matches donations: https://giving.mit.edu/matching-gifts . Website https://www.mitspokes.com/ , email spokes-2024@mit.edu.
