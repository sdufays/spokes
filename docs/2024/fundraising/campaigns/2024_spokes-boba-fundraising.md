Booth: Lobby 10, Booth 8 (Apr 22-26, 2024, 12-5pm). Note: other student groups sold show tickets a few booths over, so try to get people to buy boba after buying a show ticket.
**Shift schedule (1-hr slots; one person per slot)**: Mon 4/22 Cleo, Sophia, Jess, Amulya, JD; Tue 4/23 JD, Jess, Sophia, Amulya, Rebecca; Wed 4/24 Varsha, Cleo, Varsha, Hank, Sophia; Thu 4/25 Cleo, Jess, Amulya, Amulya, Hank; Fri 4/26 Rebecca, Rebecca, JD, Varsha, Hank. About 3-4 hours per person.
**Pricing**: cost ~$4/boba; sold at $5, $5.50, $6 (10.50 for 2). Payments by Venmo, cash, Zelle (confirm payer's Venmo name).
**Daily results**: Mon: started with 80 boba, sold ~76 (flavors matcha 8, taro 14, mango 18, milk tea 6), profit $105.50. Tue: 80 boba (mango green tea, thai tea, taro, first love [red guava, passion fruit, aloe]), sold 42+17, 21 unsold. Wed: 45 boba, sold ~22, 23 unsold, "profit" $117.
**Deposit tab**: Total collected $1,045 (Amulya $853 via Venmo plus cash; Hank $40, Rebecca, Varsha $11, Cleo $33...) minus boba cost $640 = **profit $405**, all forwarded to Jess for the crowdfunding page. 
Lessons: sales dropped after Monday (unsold 21-23 per day); order less boba per day and track Venmo vs cash carefully; money should go to one treasurer. (Personal phone numbers/payment handles in the sheet omitted.)
