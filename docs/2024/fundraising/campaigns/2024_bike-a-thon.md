Objective: people pay you $5/mile. Post a flier online with the Spokes (or personal) Venmo handle and a progress bar of miles pledged; repost each time someone donates; at the end of the week bike that number of miles. Duration: 1 week, ideally biking right at the end. Fliers designed by social media people.
