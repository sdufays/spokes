Objective: like social media bingo; people pay you to do certain things during the Midnight Marathon; encourage others to show up (wear donation boards). Post a flier online with Spokes Venmo handle (or personal) with a checklist of dares; post what you do. Duration: 1 week before the marathon. Fliers designed by the social media people; post all dares on Spokes IG, tag the payer; (*) = dare can be repeated.
- BRONZE $5: pour water on your head*, choose helmet/bike decorations, sing while biking*, choose a song to blast from speaker*, TikTok dance break*
- SILVER $10: choose my bike outfit, bike with a sign with a message of your choosing, pour juice/crack an egg on head, drink a can of Red Bull while riding, wear a helmet all day
- GOLD $25: bike one-handed for a mile*, lowest gear for a mile*, highest gear for a mile*, make a friend willing to be posted on spokes IG*
- PLATINUM $50: jog with your bike for a mile*, ultramarathon (52 miles)
Result (from tracker): $701.50 raised across 7 members.
