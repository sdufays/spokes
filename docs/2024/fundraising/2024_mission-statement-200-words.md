**Draft (2024 team, lightly edited from 2023; still contains a placeholder and says "Spokes 2023 team")**
Our mission is to bring STEM education to rural and low-income communities across the United States while inspiring kids to be active. Biking from Washington, D.C. to San Francisco, we will be teaching 4 workshops in Physics, Chemistry, Mechanical Engineering, and Art and Technology in more than 10 self-organized learning festivals. These hands-on, interactive workshops are based on our experiences at MIT, revolving around our motto of "Mens et Manus."

Spokes is a completely student-run program that is entering its 10th summer of operation. The team is made up of eight experienced and enthusiastic MIT students from Materials Science, Biology, Physics, Urban Studies and Planning, Aerospace Engineering, Mathematics, and Computer Science. Several of us have been Teaching Assistants, Global Teaching Labs instructors and [INSERT]. We have collectively helped teach classes in math, physics, chemistry, computer science and philosophy. We are also long-distance runners, varsity rowers, mountain bikers, and backpackers!

We're grateful for this opportunity to impact the lives of over 500 students across America this summer. We can't wait to bike the entire 3,500 miles, represent MIT's hand and mind spirit, and inspire the next generation of STEM students!

**2023 version** is identical except "9th summer", team of experienced MIT students from Materials Science, Art and Design, Economics, Biology, Physics, Mechanical Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Computer Science; TAs, Educational Studies Program teachers, and CovEd mentors.
