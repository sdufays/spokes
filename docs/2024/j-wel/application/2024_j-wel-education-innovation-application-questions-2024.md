Jameel World Education Lab Innovation Grants Application (2024 cycle; project end June 2025).

Sections and limits:
- Overview: title (30 words), short title (300 chars), abstract (75 words); pick a focus area: (1) equipping transitional learners, (2) curriculum-linked real-world projects, (3) embedding evidence-based learning science.
- Context: define need (50 words on form; 125 used), evidence (50/100), prior research (50/100), prior work (50/75), prior J-WEL funding and other funding (incl. pending), whether it extends an existing project.
- Output and outcomes: deliverables by June 2025; how it improves learning outcomes; data to be collected; who benefits; how findings reach users (how J-WEL can help).
- Workplan, team, budget: four-page Excel template (phase-by-phase workplan with risks, team, budget); project budget narrative justifying travel, personnel time, equipment.
- **Required: letter of recommendation from department chair** (a second letter of support in some cases).
- Pointer to blank budget template: Google Sheet linked in the PDF.
