Draft of the application (see *2024_finalized-mit-spokes-j-wel-eig-application.md* for the full text, which is nearly identical). Extra brainstorm notes in the draft:
- Project pitch ideas: bridge STEM education gap in rural/small towns through one-off learning festivals, provide toolkits for further exploration, quantify via surveys that carry through multiple months.
- Historically teams only heard second-hand anecdotes (e.g., DC jail employees) or from previous members about how festivals went; the proposal centers on directly asking students about their experience.
- Info to collect when customizing each LF: age groups, etc. Deliverables include guidelines for customization.
- Reference links noted: Sage journals 10.3102/0013189X211027528; ERIC EJ1269639; NCSES NSF 23-315 (STEM workforce growth 2011-2021 and median wages).
- Survey plan: before, immediately after, many months after; survey LF coordinators too; survey toolkit users.
