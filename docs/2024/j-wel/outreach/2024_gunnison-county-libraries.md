Contact: Keelin MacCarthy, keelin@gunnisoncountylibraries.org (Gunnison County Libraries, Colorado). LF planned July 17, 2024. Point person: Rebecca. Call date May 2024.

- Note: "Spokes 2023 just didn't work out" (2023 visit did not happen).
- **Community**: Gunnison ("Gunny"), largest city ~8k, home of Western Colorado University, lots of ranchland, near Crested Butte, 7,700 ft elevation, 8 months of winter, rarely above 90F. Large Spanish-speaking community, many bilingual households and young families. Sustainability is a big theme (sustainable ag is hard at altitude, home gardening; they ran a sustainability seminar series).
- **They like strawberry DNA** (strawberries may be in season); 3D printers at the college and the game store, both open to the public.
- **Audience/timing**: families with smaller kids in the daytime (10 and younger), older kids later after camps release (many camps at the college until 2pm); mornings mostly preschool/in-home daycares.
- **Space**: large community room inside the library (capacity 150) and great covered patio outside; can connect indoor/outdoor. Main weather risk is 20-minute afternoon thunderstorms. Attendance trickles in: about 60 people over 2 hours is a good turnout.
- **Spanish**: provide as many written resources in Spanish as possible; they sometimes hire a translator; younger kids often bilingual, many parents are English language learners.
- **Marketing**: they would like marketing materials/flyers and social media content; LF is more like a conference schedule, so a little schedule flyer addition helps.
- **Route warning**: Highway 50 west of town has a bridge closure/construction (CDOT page); detour via Lake City cutoff south of the reservoir on a dirt road, or the one-car-wide dirt-road pass (name garbled in notes, "cold cbuler pass"). "We are used to it."
