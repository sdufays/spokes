Contact: scepeda@newtonplks.org (Newton Public Library, Newton, KS). LF planned July 3, 2024. Point person: Rebecca. A "normal host in Newton that we've had for a few years".

- Usually **30-40 kids sign up; cap about 40**. Split into 3-4 groups (up to 5 possible).
- Rooms: 2 good rooms for 20-25 each, one small room (reserved); larger rooms can hold 2 groups. Projector and whiteboard in all 3 rooms. Outdoor space good for concrete activity.
- Anything STEM works. **Market to ages 10-18; ~90% are 10-13/14.**
- Have materials ready ASAP.
- Asked about specific community needs: none at the moment.
