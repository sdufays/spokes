## Email template
Subject: MIT Spokes Request to Meet

Hi [NAME],

I'm [NAME], a member of MIT Spokes. We're looking forward to coming to [LOCATION] this summer and holding a learning festival in partnership with [ORG]. As we prepare the content for these learning festivals, we would love to meet with you to discuss the learning festival and how it can best fit the needs of your community. If you are interested, feel free to send me dates and times you are free for a 15-30 minute Zoom or phone call. Thank you again!

Best, [NAME]

## Meeting agenda (flexible)
- Frame: we want to customize LFs as much as possible to your community's needs.
- Ask: expected primary audience (middle school, little kids...)?
- Overview of the 2024 LF activities: concrete/materials science and sustainability innovations; 3D printing and biomaterials for sustainable manufacturing; strawberry DNA extraction; physics of sound; Scratch tutorial.
- How to tailor activities to community needs? Specific needs LFs could address? Foreseeable challenges? Anything else we should know?
