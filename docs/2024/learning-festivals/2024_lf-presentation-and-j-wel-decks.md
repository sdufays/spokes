Spokes 2024: "Spreading STEAM Coast-2-Coast."
Team: Jessica Xu, Sophia Wang, Varsha Sandadi, Rebecca Lizarde, JD Hagood, Amulya Aluru, Clelia Lacarriere, Hank Stennes.
Pitch: Bike - 8 people, 8 bikes, 4000 miles, 75 days of biking. Teach - learning festivals in 9 cities (schools, libraries, jails, youth centers, and more). Break stereotypes - STEAM shouldn't be geographically or economically restricted; engineers and scientists can be athletic.
Longer J-WEL version sections: How did we teach? (Physics of Sound, Strawberry DNA, Krispie Koncrete, 3D Printing), DC Jail and DC Youth Services Center, Surveys. **What's next:** recruitment of next year's Spokes team; continuing longitudinal surveys; sending activity kits to the Learning Festival sites.
