1. Pull off any green leaves on the strawberry.
2. Put strawberries in a plastic bag, seal, gently smash ~2 minutes.
3. Mix 2 tsp detergent, 1 tsp salt, 1/2 cup water in a plastic cup (extraction liquid).
4. Pour extraction liquid into the bag (quart-sized works well).
5. Reseal, gently smash another minute; avoid too many bubbles.
6. Place coffee filter inside another plastic cup.
7. Pour strawberry liquid into the filter; twist and squeeze remaining liquid into cup.
8. Pour an equal amount of rubbing alcohol down the side of the cup. Do not mix.
9. Within seconds a white cloudy substance (DNA) appears in the top layer.
10. Tilt the cup and pick up the DNA with a fork.
