Session 1: Module 1 Intro to Additive Manufacturing (what is 3D printing, process, slicing, supports - Activity 1: 3D pens tallest structure; infill - Activity 2: research a pattern). Module 2 Sustainable Materials (Stathatou slides; Activity: Wood PLA fact sheets; molding/casting; CNC; DIY).
Session 2: Module 3 CAD (Onshape tutorials; design challenge; bonus self-replicating printers). Module 4 Frontier of New Materials (large-format printers, Materiom; Activity: choose 2-3 locally producible materials).
