References: Krispie Concrete Teacher Guide and Teacher Guide Template (pk12 grants), MIT Edgerton Center "Understanding Air" presentation.

Teacher guide contents:
- Brief description: learning objectives, key concepts
- Appropriate grade level
- Schedule of activities with timing
- Lesson guide: (optional) why it works; materials needed (buy links for hard-to-find items); how to prepare materials; suggested room setup (images); step breakdown (slides with presenter notes as rough script; activity instructions)
- How to adjust for different age groups (e.g. under ten)
- Miscellaneous tips
- Optional additional resources for teacher understanding
