# What to do for housing (Spokes 2024)
Shared Warm Showers account existed (email spokes-2024@mit.edu; password [redacted]). On 3/6/24 the team decided NOT to create a new Warm Showers account (costs money).

Process:
1. Use Warm Showers to find hosts for each city/town; if none, look at nearby cities (within ~10 miles).
2. Message hosts with the template below.
3. Update the 2024 Route sheet (Cities/Stops tab) with the host's contact info.
4. If staying in a campground/park, highlight it and book it (or tell Lucy to book).
5. If there is a learning festival, ask the LF host (after housing is confirmed) whether they know anyone who can host.
6. If no hosts, look at last year's and earlier lists and contact old hosts: 2023 "New route (master file)", 2022 Route Plan and Spokes Route Planning and Housing Master Guide, 2019 "Route Details: for Planning"/"for 2020", 2018 Route sheet.
7. If all else fails, find a cheap hotel/motel.
Also: email 5 churches per city; contact alumni.

Message template: "My name is ___ and I am one of the MIT SPOKES students. We are a group of seven students biking across the US. Along our route, we host learning festivals in underserved communities to spread STEM and a love for education. We are stopping in ___ on our route and were wondering if you would host us around ___. We will have a support vehicle to carry our teaching equipment. I know seven people is quite a large number but we are happy to camp in a backyard! You can visit our website at https://www.mitspokes.com/ to learn more about us and our mission!"
