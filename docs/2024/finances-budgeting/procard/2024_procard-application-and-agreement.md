Steps: (1) applicant fills the application PDF in Adobe Reader (Mac Preview not compatible); (2) send to Administrative Officer/Primary Authorizer who signs and emails to ProCard@mit.edu; (3) read the Procurement Card User Guide; (4) complete the ProCard cardholder training; (5) complete the online Cardholder Agreement; (6) card arrives by interoffice mail; verify and activate with MIT ID by phone. Average 7-10 business days.

2024 values: cardholder Jessica Y. Xu (Computer Science and Engineering dept on agreement; Edgerton Center mailing 4-408), jyxu@mit.edu, single-transaction limit $3,000 (max), monthly limit $15,000 (max), enrolled in Amazon Business, primary cost object 2530032, G/L 420226; secondary cost object must be open-ended general/discretionary Institute funded; unprocessed charges go to non-reimbursable G/L 420255. A department verifier (cannot be the cardholder) must have SAP "spend & commit" authority. Application dated 01/29/2024; agreement signed 01/29/2024. [MIT ID omitted]

Agreement: unauthorized purchases may be deducted from salary and lead to discipline; card is MIT property; use only for authorized purchases per the ProCard User's Guide. Questions: procard@mit.edu.
