MIT requires documented vendor and price competition for requisitions exceeding $10,000 (attach to the Atlas requisition number). Written quotes must be attached whenever available, when the description is not precise, and when the price exceeds $50,000.
- Section 1: list at least 2 vendors considered with prices (vendor selected, considered vendors, total price).
- Section 2 - reason for choice: 2.1 MIT preferred (SmartBuy) vendor; 2.2 adequate competition (2+ quotes), lowest bidder; 2.3 2+ quotes but not lowest (justify higher price); 2.4 non-competitive procurement (unique product, continuity, critical features, repairs only from manufacturer, compatibility, emergency, sponsor-required vendor, inadequate competition) requiring explanation A, justification B, and price-reasonableness C (market research, screenshots, catalog prices, peer-institution prices).
- Requisitioner's signature required.
