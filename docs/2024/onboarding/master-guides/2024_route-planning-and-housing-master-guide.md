**Route:** For many years DC to SF; recommended to reuse the existing route (lots of documentation and relationships). Follows **US Bike Route 76 (TransAmerican Bike Route)** from DC to Colorado (Adventure Cycling USBRS maps). Planning tip: have Warmshowers host map, Google Maps and USBRS maps open together; check hosts BEFORE choosing towns. 2-3 people on route planning (one for Warmshowers, one for campsites). Route spreadsheets: 2023 master (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1imhRSZyuWZihWP__9ljCspxKy4_lJcRn2XELbLmZ004), 2024 (https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1PnepaXnbzFOyzaRXGoqNvND4MNnkiFRsor63XCN5-tk), 2025 (...1MLkJIYMAmKvNSBm9iUi61X9-dF-Fe5-oP0_GzwjW5bc), 2026 (...1FZTiJBeVcn6Pf-vUAqcwMFPduEfY1yFDthliL9qTybc); 2024 daily routes on Strava (Hank, Varsha, Jess). Stays and notes for 2023-2026 are in comments of those sheets. 2025 and 2026 Ride with GPS collections exist.
**Day to day:** stops every 1.5-2 hr (~15 mi); gas stations for water (soda fountain) and bathrooms; car meets if no gas stations. Tools: Ride With GPS (export GPX to bike computer), Strava premium (shows surface type), Google Street View (shoulder), offline maps. Read previous years' blogs for next-day notes. 
**Finding stays (2022 order by frequency):** Warmshowers, campsites, learning festivals, friends, MIT connections, churches, motels. Warmshowers: new account, mention support car, start contacting in February, only hosts who accept 2+ (2026: 4+ gave nicer places), get phone and address early, confirm a week before. Campsites: book in February (national parks fill fast), showers preferred. LFs often help find lodging. MIT alumni clubs: emailed all clubs in route states, only 3 responded but one donated and Sam Ford (KY) connected them. Churches: kitchen, bathrooms, sometimes feed you; call and email every church.

**State notes:** Utah - many national parks; don't bike into Arches from Thompson Springs (no shoulder, heavy traffic; 2022 shuttled); Bryce Canyon to Cedar City beautiful downhill. Nevada - Baker NV to Carson City is the "Loneliest Road in America" (stock up in Ely; Eureka-Austin-Fallon has no services; collect stamps for a pin; Middlegate burger stop; messages from previous Spokes). California - stay a day at Lake Tahoe; US-50 from Tahoe toward Sacramento becomes a freeway with no-bikes signs - use side roads; gas stations north of SF often lack water/bathrooms, use Whole Foods.
