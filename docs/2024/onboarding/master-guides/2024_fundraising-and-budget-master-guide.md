**Fundraising advice:** Start early; need more than one fundraiser person. By spring much of companies' excess money (Texas Instruments, Microsoft, Dell) is gone. Start with MIT departments: cold-call emails to heads/administrators (templates in the Fundraising folder). Grants inside MIT: TBP funding ($2000 historically, after application); Baker Foundation ($2000 - planned and hosted a "bike repair and safety workshop"); "Mad Money Grant" gave 2019 a few thousand for submitting a trip pamphlet. Alumni groups wouldn't donate much but made connections and housing in some states. Bake sale + raffle for bike tune-ups made ~$2,000 total (year not stated). **In 2024, most money ($45,330) came from the J-WEL Education Grant (https://www.jwel.mit.edu/grants); applications open in February, decisions in April, so also pursue other sources.** Outside MIT: corporate/nonprofit grants - St. Anthony Educational Foundation ($4000, 2022 tracker). Companies: Lightforce and Desktop Metal (3D printing companies) helped with the 3D printing workshop with professionally printed parts; **Lightforce sponsored $5000 in 2022** (the 2022 12-page pamphlet is believed to have won it). Companies must register with MIT to donate (conflict-of-interest check) - talk to Sandi. Crowdsource: everyone participates. Tips: emphasize education; offer sponsor benefits (website, t-shirt); visit local sponsors; cast a wide net; Word mail merge for mass emails.
**Budget:** budget dictates fundraising need; itemize (list each bike gear item at local bike shop prices); consult the prior trip finances.
**Finances:** Two cards: Travel card (food, gas, during trip) and Purchasing card/ProCard (gear, LF supplies; anything sensible to buy at MIT before the trip). "Health and beauty" products (shampoo, sunscreen) cannot be bought on either card. Getting cards requires online training + application; 2026 had 2 procard and 2 travel card holders. Travel card: Concur report at end of trip (tedious - upload receipts during trip); receipts needed for purchases >$75 and any food; missing receipt >$75: missing receipt affidavit + mark Non-Recoverable (Sandi Lipnoski quotes). J-WEL account is a sponsored account (possibly more approvals); Spokes account is a department account so travel charged to SPOKES = non-sponsored. ProCard: send receipts to Marvelin within two weeks (emails from procard@mit.edu list pending); audits possible - answer promptly; avoid food, gas, health & beauty on procard; lost receipt can lead to deactivation. Purchases tax exempt in MA but nowhere else (text truncated).
