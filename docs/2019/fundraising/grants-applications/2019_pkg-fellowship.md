- Title: MIT Spokes 2019. Weeks: 12 (leave Boston May 26; finish San Francisco ~Aug 11-12).
- Community challenge: rural students graduate and score as well as urban, yet only <30% of rural 18-24 year olds are enrolled in higher ed vs ~45% in cities/suburbs (NCES).
- Partners: Thomas Cravens, Director of the Challenger Learning Center of Kentucky (main partner); other confirmed LFs: Roanoke Public Library (VA), Lab: Revolution (Farmington MO), aSTEAM Village (Kansas City), Newton Public Library; Teach for America contact Jessica Usery, a Boston-area recruitment manager, who connects the team to regional managers.
- Goals: (1) bike DC to SF; (2) teach STEM workshops; (3) networking dinners on college applications.
- Team roles (2019): Leah Yost (class of 2021; funding, crowdfunding, website design, TFA liaison); Bill Kuhl (2022; funding + equipment); Caralyn Cutlip (2017 alum; funding organization/tracking); Maile Jim (2022; route + social media; LF scheduling); Erin Leydon (2022; route + social media; hosts/Warmshowers); Devin Seyler (2022; gear subteam, costs, bikes); Evan Tey (2019; LF + website); Edgardo Letona (2022; LF structure, dinners, logistics). Squads: fundraising, route, gear, education.
- Workshops: Rapid Prototyping (Leah, Caralyn), Intelligence: Brain to Self-Driving Cars (Evan, Edgardo), Fun with Chemistry (Devin, Erin), Decoding DNA (Maile, Bill).
- Safety: avoid dangerous roads and July 4; use Adventure Cycling TransAmerica resources and prior teams' notes; bike in pairs/threes; lights, reflective gear; van to shuttle; medical kit; past minor injuries (e.g. sprained ankle) meant a couple days to weeks off; teach in pairs so an injury doesn't break a LF.
- Budget lines: bikes $7,032; helmets $360; locks $400; shoes $800; spare tubes $80; lights $127.92; emergency kit $478; car rental $3,846.79; gas $700; teaching equipment $1,200; food $6,000 (~$9/person/day for 12 weeks); accommodation $1,000 (9 nights camping + 9 nights hostels/inns/cabins/Airbnb from last year's costs).
- Funds secured: prior Spokes team leftover $8,297.74. Pending: PKG Fellowship $6,000; crowdfunding $800; MIT departments $6,000; Community Service Fund $500; Edgerton Center.
