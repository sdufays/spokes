200-word answer: Spokes America is a seven-year-old program; eight MIT students cycle DC to San Francisco teaching STEM workshops in rural communities through Learning Festivals; workshops cover mechanical engineering, computer science, neuroscience, biology, chemistry; each team member has teaching experience (tutoring, TA); applicant cites MIT TA work, high-school peer tutoring, and teaching physics at a high school through an MIT program. Result: TI granted $4,000 (Tracking Spreadsheet).
