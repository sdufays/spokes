Pitch: team is half female; shows female students that STEM is possible; expects ~half of students to be female; networking dinners encourage higher education. Budget ~ $25,000 total. If approved SWE funds buy emergency equipment (first aid kit, emergency water, pepper/dog/bear spray, water purification; ~$550 estimate from past teams) and part of bike repair gear (tire lever, chain lube, spare tubes, patch kit; ~$240). Partial funding still helpful. Outcome: SWE gave $400 (reimbursement).
