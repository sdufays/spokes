## Statement of Purpose and Project Description

Applicant Name: Kate Pearce
Email: kpearce@mit.edu 
Phone: 224-567-2524
Project Title: MIT Spokes America 2018

Statement of Purpose
Spokes America brings STEM education to kids across the US. We offer exciting, hands-on STEM workshops to rural areas across the country to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers. As we bike from DC to San Francisco, our cross country trip also serves to prove that engineers are athletic, adventurous, and community-focused.  

Project Description
8 students. 8 bikes. 3,500 miles. 75 days. An effort to inspire the next generation of STEM students and professionals. The Spokes team will be biking from DC to San Francisco, teaching at 9 schools and community centers along the way, listed below.

Louisa County Library, VA | June 1st
Challenger Learning Center, KY | June 13-14th
Lab: Revolution, MO | June 23rd
St. Louis Homeschool Connection, MO | June 26th
aSTEAM Village, MO | July 2nd
Newton Public Library, KS | July 5th
Loveland Public Library, CO | July 14th
Christa McAuliffe Space Center, UT | July 21st
Carson City Public Library, NV | July 31st

At each location, we will be teaching four workshops. These include “3… 2...1… Build!”, “Circuits Galore!”, “Playing with Python”, and “Picture This: The Science of Photography.” These workshops cover topics ranging from engineering design to physics to computer science, and will be adaptable to fit the needs of elementary through high school aged students. Our website (https://www.mitspokes.com) has lots more information as well.

Team
This year’s Spokes America team includes actors, athletes, scientists, mathematicians, and engineers. We are all intensely passionate about spreading our love for science, technology, engineering, and math to students across the country, and we look forward to embarking on such a journey! Though we are a small team of college students, our dedication to our goal means that we are well-coordinated in our efforts, with several students handling fundraising, a couple reaching out to schools, two handling the route and lodging, and one maintaining our social media presence. However, we are all engaged with all jobs, as organizing something so ambitious is no small task. We are lucky to receive mentorship from students from past teams (this is the 6th year) who are similarly passionate about spreading a love for science across the globe. 
History
2018 marks the sixth year of the Spokes America program. Since its inception in 2013, Spokes America has sent 8-10 MIT and Harvard students to small rural towns like Hazard, Kentucky, and Newton, Kansas to spread their passion for science, technology, engineering, and math along the way. Past years have all successfully completed their journeys across the country, and this year’s team is just as ambitious as the last: along the way, we hope to continue a tradition of documenting our experience in small towns and big cities alike, sharing the experience of what it means to be an athletic and active engineer.

## SWEcubator application (2018)

If you are an MIT SWE member, how have you been involved with SWE in the past? (100 words max) *

I have volunteered at WiSE throughout this year. Also, I participated in the Big/Little program. Earlier this year, I enjoyed going to the Meet the Professors dinner as well. 

Describe the goal of your project/initiative. (100 words max) *

I am part of the MIT Spokes America 2018 team. We are biking across the country from DC to San Francisco, teaching STEM workshops of our own design at about 10 "Learning Festivals" along the way. The goal is to spread exciting, hands-on STEM education to areas that otherwise wouldn't have it. Also, by biking there it also proves that engineers can be athletic and adventurous, not just nerdy :) I'll be teaching a computer science workshop.

When and where will the initiative take place? (50 words max)  *

This will take place over the course of the summer (May 27th through ~August 8th) across the country, from DC to San Francisco. We are stopping at schools in VA, KY, KS, UT, MO, CO, NV, and more. 

How does this initiative align with the SWE mission statement (100 words)? *

This aligns with the SWE mission to encourage women to pursue careers as engineers and leaders as we will be exposing young girls to STEM. Also, this works to improve the image of the engineering profession as we prove that engineers aren't just nerds and that engineers can have a big impact on the world. Our workshops will be hands-on activities as well as include a mini lesson about what types of careers and impacts the STEM disciplines can have. Lastly, since our team has 8 people (6 girls, 2 boys) of a variety of majors, ethnicities, and backgrounds, it is also a demonstration of the power of diversity.
