## Dell (MSDF grant form answers)

Problem Statement:*
Define the problem or issue you are working to address. This should primarily be data or numeric evidence documenting the problem in your geographic area and target population. Please limit your summary to 500 characters (about 100 words).
It is no secret that STEM education in rural America is lacking; according to a study by the Carnegie Science Report, rural students are rarely aware of the opportunities in the STEM world despite teachers’ optimistic efforts to increase access to STEM education in rural communities. Indeed, the 2014 Condition of Education revealed that rural students are less likely to attend college, complete a high school science sequence of classes, or have qualified teachers.

0 of 500 max characters
Project Description:*
Provide a summary of your grant request and the overall project this grant will be funding. Include how the project addresses the above Problem Statement. Please limit your summary to 1500 characters (about 300 words).
Spokes America is a six-year-old program that brings MIT students cycling across the country, teaching students in middle America to engage with the scientific world around them. Along the way, the Spokes team hosts learning festivals at schools, libraries, museums, and summer camps, encouraging children from across the country to engage with the scientific world around them. Our trip will defy stereotypes about what it means to be a scientist, teaching kids that scientists and engineers can be athletic and engaged with their communities. 
We hope to form a lasting partnership between these rural communities and our sponsors, encouraging children to pursue their passions for STEM and giving them role models to look up to. Our experiential learning program is just one part of a larger movement to get kids in rural communities interested in STEM; by providing them with a scientific, yet creative, outlet, we hope to encourage them to be engaged with the world around them. Introducing children in rural communities to the world of science and engineering will help to close STEM opportunity gaps for rural students.
UItimately, the MSDF grant will help us share our passion for science, technology, engineering, and math, encouraging children to be creative and engaged with the scientific world around them.

Request Summary:*
Provide a brief description of how the funding requested will be used. Be specific about what the solicited MSDF grant will fund. Also include a summary of the overall project finances. Please limit your summary to 500 characters (about 100 words).
Currently, we have roughly $9000 of our $25,000 goal. The money from the MSDF fund will go directly to improving quality of programming, enabling us to purchase educational materials and supplies to maximize the hands-on component of the workshops. While our largest cost will likely be transportation for the team (8 bikes and a rental van for supplies), we hope to use the money from this grant to increase the effectiveness of  the classroom portion of the journey.

Success Factors:*
Summarize the goals of your program and how you will measure the program’s success. This should primarily be data, numeric evidence and metrics directly related to the above Problem Statement. Please limit your summary to 500 characters (about 100 words).
Because later teams return to the same locations and connect with the same educators and students as prior teams, we hope to get a subjective evaluation of past year's programs, and we hope that subsequent years will do the same. In addition, we will send a two-part survey to educators to evaluate the short-and-long-term effectiveness of the program. We hope to see a long-term boost in interest in STEM, and we hope that future programs will continue to evaluate our team’s success.

## Ford Grant Application

Please provide a brief description of this request.
We ask for a grant of $6,000 to help us with the costs of embarking upon this ambitious journey. This money is for the transit van that we will be taking along with us on the trip to store gear, educational supplies, personal items, and food. 

Please provide a detailed description of this request
All of the grant will go towards providing our team with a reliable vehicle rented through the Budget Rent A Car service; we estimate that this vehicle will cost us roughly $6,000 for a long-term rental. This vehicle is pivotal to our mission, as it will contain the educational supplies and outdoor equipment we will use on the trip; additionally, it will serve as our headquarters. One member of the team will drive ahead, scouting locations to eat or cooking breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the team as we journey across the country. We need a larger van in order to house all of the educational supplies we will bring along with us: each workshop has a construction component, and we need a place to store the materials that will impact hundreds of students across the country.
 Please describe how the request fits within the guidelines of Ford Motor Company
Our mission of improving underrepresented populations’ access to education in science, technology, engineering, and math aligns perfectly with Ford Motor Company’s commitment to developing tomorrow’s innovators. By teaching kids that scientists and engineers can be active, engaged, and passionate, we hope to inspire the next generation of leaders to be committed to the pursuit of science. 

Please list and describe the current objectives
With the Spokes America 2018 program, we hope to:
Share our enthusiasm for STEM subjects with middle school students
Empower these students to be self-driven, creative, and curious learners.
Change the way scientists and engineers are perceived in society, so that they are seen as athletic, active, and adventurous. 

To achieve this mission, we intend to impact as many lives as possible, focusing on the underserved communities of middle America. Based on the experience of past years, we believe that many of these kids haven’t even considered a career in science and engineering; by making STEM fun, interesting, and engaging, we hope to encourage them to pursue their curious sides.

We are excited to embark on this journey, and we hope that Garmin will join us along the way. 

Sincerely, 
Delia Stephens and the Spokes America team

Hello from Spokes America!

Since its inception in 2013, Spokes America has sent 8-10 MIT and Harvard students to small rural towns like Hazard, Kentucky, and Newton, Kansas to spread their passion for science, technology, engineering, and math. Sony, with its vested interest in improving access to STEM education for the nation’s future innovators, could leverage its significant resources to impact the lives of hundreds of students and educators across the country. 

We ask for a grant of $5,000, though we would appreciate any amount of money or equipment. This money will help us acquire the necessities for our journey, improve the quality of our programming for students, and enable us to spread the passion for STEM to students from all over the country.

We are excited to embark on this journey, and we hope that Sony will join us!

Respectfully, 
Delia Stephens and the 2018 Spokes America team
