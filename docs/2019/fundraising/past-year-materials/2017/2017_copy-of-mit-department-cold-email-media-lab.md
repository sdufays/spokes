Dear MIT Media Lab,

My name is Thi Bui. I’m a Freshman here at MIT, and I’m excited to be a member of Spokes America. This summer, I will be biking across the country with a team of eight MIT and Harvard students in an initiative to get kids excited about STEM. We will be stopping in towns along the way to lead workshops for underprivileged kids, sharing our enthusiasm for STEM.

My workshop focuses on Psychology and Sports Science. Students will learn about the neuroscience behind how small aspects such as diet and mental attitude have on the neurobiology of an athlete. I hope that this workshop will provide a new, compelling perspective on neuroscience. There will also be other workshops on math, electrical engineering, and physics. 

As the MIT Media Lab interested in education outreach, would you be willing to help fund this program and make our trip a reality?

STEM can often seem intimidating or boring to youth who are not given sufficient opportunities to explore. They may not even consider themselves capable of appreciating or participating in scientific fields. These kids — children who may not otherwise be exposed to engineering ideas— will be given the chance to explore science in an interesting and accessible way. Spokes hopes to inspire these students, so that they may believe in their own potential to do STEM.

Please contact us at Spokes-2017@mit.edu if you have any additional questions about who we are or what we do! We also have a website at SpokesAmerica.com Thank you for taking the time to read this email.

Best,

Thi Bui
