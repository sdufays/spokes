Sources: MIT Department Cold Email (1Qq7cRFp6kUDc8ZTw-bENPV0PfetoKAdLkfZXFHP8iRg), Alumni Cold Email (1fhy5J5TRRxQ3Foppgz9fykEUHyYnAcxlEPyMSYpK9DI), STEM company Cold Email (14EeV9RhCvn3-gywlHYt4QIzEeHsMbmbjaJ02bWd-etE).
- **Department email:** subject "Spokes America 2017 Fundraising"; intro with name/year, "my workshop focuses on [subject]; students will [activity]", ask the department head "would you be willing to help fund this program and make our trip a reality?"; STEM can seem intimidating or boring to under-served youth; links to website and two news articles (BetaBoston 2015, Cyclocamping). Old version emphasized a Boston Globe article.
- **Alumni email (by Catherine Looby):** found via MIT Alumni Directory; goal $20,000 for bikes, teaching materials, travel; donation page crowdfund.mit.edu/project/6618. Alumni targets: Frank Chan (1995, San Francisco Bicycle Coalition), Mark Cote (2007, Specialized), Gary Klein (1974, Klein Bikes; responded), Slava Menn (2011, Fortified Bikes), Adam Vollmer (2005, Faraday Bikes), Dr. Frank Jamerson (1948, Course 8), Lynore Abbott (1991, STEM).
- **Company letter:** same structure as 2016 template; 9 learning festivals; goal $30,000. 2017 workshops: Build your own speaker and catapult (physics), How computers work: building circuits (binary, logic gates, breadboards), Games and math strategy (SET, probability), Sports science seminar (biomechanics, robots "playing sports").
