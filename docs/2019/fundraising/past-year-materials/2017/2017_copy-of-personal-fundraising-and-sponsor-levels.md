**Campaign page plan:** video under 5 minutes answering What/Who/Why/Where/When (use 2016 footage); text sections (history/mission, team bios of 2+ paragraphs, mission, route map and daily mileage).
**Draft reward chart v1:** $1 thanks; $5 name on blog + email; $15 postcard; $30 two postcards + video; $50 weekly postcards for a month + 2 videos; $100 weekly postcards all program + 4 videos; $200 2-hr meal on the route; $400 official sponsor (logo on blogs/site/speeches); $600 team visits to speak (limit 5); $1,000 team teaches half a day at your school (limit 3); $5,000 "eternal gratitude" (limit 1).
**Final reward chart v2 (used):** $1 donor listed on website; $5 friend + end-of-trip email; $10 sticker + thank-you note; $20 video message; $30 science fact of the week; $50 personal postcard; $75 Spokes T-shirt; $100 drawstring bag; $250 two T-shirts; $500 recognized as official sponsor; $750 team speaks at your school (limit 5); $1,000 team teaches half a day (limit 3).
**Corporate tiers (2017):** Silver $2,500 (logo and link on website, blog shout-out, name on back of team t-shirt, lunch or Skype with a rider, end-of-trip update, pack of 3 shirts); Gold $5,000 (silver + logo on FRONT of t-shirt with slogan, mentioned at start of each LF, logo on van, pack of 5 shirts); Platinum $10,000 (gold + promo video inclusion, distribution of promo materials at schools/camps, pack of 10 shirts). Lesson from 2019: perk fulfilment tracking is needed (see 2019 Crowdfunding Perks).
