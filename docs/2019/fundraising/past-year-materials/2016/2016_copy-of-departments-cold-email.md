Subject: Fundraising for Spokes America

Dear [their name],

My name is [your name], and I'm a [year] at [MIT/Harvard]. I am part of a team of 8 MIT and Harvard students called Spokes America  will be biking across the country to teach STEM. The program is called Spokes (click here to read a Boston Globe article about Spokes 2015), and I am a member of one such team. Everyone on the team devises a curriculum, and I am planning on teaching kids about [subject].

As head of the [subject] department, would you be willing to help fund this program and make our trip a reality?

I hope to cover [subject you're teaching]. By showing kids that research and science can pertain directly to their daily lives, [reason/thing that pertains to kids’ lives], I hope to clear away the idea that STEM is inaccessible to them.

STEM can often seem intimidating or boring to youths who are not given sufficient opportunities to explore. They may not even consider themselves capable of appreciating or participating in scientific fields. These kids--children who may not otherwise be exposed to engineering ideas--will be given the chance to explore science in an interesting and accessible way. Spokes hopes to inspire these students, so that they may believe in their own potential to do STEM.

Best,

[name]
