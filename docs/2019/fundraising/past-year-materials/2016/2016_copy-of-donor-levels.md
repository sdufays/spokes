Platinum ($10,000): Gold benefits + inclusion in future video promotional materials + logo at Spokes promotional events on MIT/Harvard campuses. Gold ($5,000): Silver benefits + logo on team t-shirts worn during the trip and on the support vehicle. Silver ($2,500): logo on Spokes website; lunch with team or Skype chat live from a learning festival; pack of three Spokes t-shirts labelled "Sponsor"; three souvenirs from three locations on the route; listed as educational resource on website and social media; distribute promotional materials (stickers, pens, bags) at schools they teach in.
