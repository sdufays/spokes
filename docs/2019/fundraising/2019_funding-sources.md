- **PKG Center** (studentlife.mit.edu/pkgcenter): public service programs and funding; office W20 5th floor.
- **MIT Community Service Fund** (csf.mit.edu): for students/staff/faculty/retirees; public service initiatives; good fit, due March 31 (2019: applied 3/25 for $1,500).
- **Coop Student Group Grants / Coop Public Service Grants** (store.thecoop.com Coop Gives): not likely, must benefit Boston/Cambridge.
- **Arthur C. Smith Student Life Grant**: ideas that create a sense of community within MIT.
- **Student Group Collaboration Grant** (innovation.mit.edu): seed grant for innovation/entrepreneurship student groups working together.
- **MindHandHeart Innovation Fund** (mental health/wellbeing): not likely. **Sandbox Innovation Fund** (sandbox.mit.edu; startups, non-profits welcome): not likely.
- More: studentlife.mit.edu/sao/finance-resources/funding-opportunities
