"Hello, I'm a recent MIT alum and an excited member of an otherwise undergraduate team that will be biking across the country this summer in an initiative to get kids excited about STEM. We will be stopping in towns along the way to lead workshops for middle schoolers... Our four workshops are on engineering prototyping, brain- and computer-intelligence, chemistry, and genetics. ... we will be cycling in order to challenge the stereotype that students interested in STEM cannot also be athletic and active. As a company at the forefront of scientific and engineering development, would you be willing to help fund this program and make our trip a reality? ... visit www.mitspokes.com. **Our goal this year is to raise $20,000 for bikes, teaching supplies, etc.** Sincerely, Caralyn Cutlip, Spokes Fundraising Squad"
