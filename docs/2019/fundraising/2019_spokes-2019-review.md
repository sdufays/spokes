**MIT Spokes America 2019** team: Leah Yost, Maile Jim, William Kuhl, Caralyn Cutlip, Evan Tey, Edgardo Letona, Devin Seyler, Erin Leydon.

Goals met: (1) bike DC to San Francisco; (2) inspire students across the U.S. via workshops; (3) networking-style dinners to talk about college applications and next steps.

**Ten Learning Festivals:** Challenger Learning Center (Hazard KY), Lab: Revolution (Farmington MO), St. Louis Homeschool Connection, aSTEAM Village (Kansas City MO), Newton Public Library (KS), Boys and Girls Club of Denver, Christa McAuliffe Space Center (Pleasant Grove/Lindon UT), Ely Public Library (NV), Churchill County Library (Fallon NV), Boys and Girls Club Carson City. Approx 30-50 students at each, K through high school, mostly grades 3-7.

**Four workshops**
1. Design, Test, Repeat! (Leah Yost, Caralyn Cutlip): rapid prototyping with cardboard/foamboard/paper/straws; challenges: keep a ping-pong ball moving as long as possible; prototype a carnival game others test; discussion of design stages.
2. Decoding DNA (Maile Jim, William Kuhl): strawberry DNA extraction and gel electrophoresis; analyze mystery solutions ("science is not always certain").
3. Intelligence: from the Brain to Self-Driving Cars! (Evan Tey, Edgardo Letona): brain, AI; demo of reading nerve signals (Evan controlling Edgardo's arm via electronic device); students train a nice/mean neural network.
4. Fun with Chemistry (Devin Seyler, Erin Leydon): Whoosh Bottle, Elephant Toothpaste etc.; younger students wrote hypotheses.

Social connections via dinner/social time or lunch: talked about STEM interests, high school and college experience, bike-trip logistics. Biking: whole route biked except some flooded/too dangerous sections; riding was an easy way to start conversations about the mission.
