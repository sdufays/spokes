"My name is Leah Yost and I am a current sophomore at MIT... part of a student group called Spokes. We are a group of MIT science and engineering students with a mission to encourage school children across the US to be self-driven, creative and curious in their approach to science and engineering. In collaboration with Teach for America, the Spokes team this summer will be biking from Washington D.C. to San Francisco to engage with students in rural communities... Our 3-month program confronts stereotypes about scientists and engineers... I would love to talk more about Spokes's impact, and how ____ can help us spread the word." Include email and phone.
