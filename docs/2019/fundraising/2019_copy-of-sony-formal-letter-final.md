Header: Spokes America, MIT Edgerton Center, 77 Massachusetts Avenue Rm. 4-408, Cambridge, MA 02139.

"We are a group of MIT science and engineering students with a mission to encourage school children across the US to be self-driven, creative and curious... Our 3-month program confronts stereotypes about scientists and engineers as we embark on a cycling trip across America... We are asking you, Sony, to join our cause by sponsoring our journey. 2018 marks the sixth year of the Spokes America program. Since its inception in 2013, Spokes America has sent 8-10 MIT and Harvard students to small rural towns like Hazard, Kentucky, and Newton, Kansas... Sponsors... are enabling young people across the country to better realize their own creative problem-solving potential, connect teachers to new teaching resources for science and technology, and challenge stereotypes about what it means to be an engineer... We ask for a grant of $5,000, though we would appreciate any amount of money or equipment." Signed Delia Stephens and the 2018 Spokes America team.
