- Parent fund (ATI): no role. Purchasing: talk to Sandy. **Two MIT credit cards**: Travel (over 21; trip expenses: food, gas, campground fees, bike tools; can have more than one) and Procurement (pre-trip: bikes, teaching equipment). SAVE RECEIPTS, order by date. Can't buy personal items with Spokes money (sunscreen, insect spray, toiletries). If a personal card is used for something eligible, receipt needs name, kerberos, MIT ID. Bank statements through Atlas. TBP Fellowship only reimburses.
- Advice: get budget done early; can buy stuff (bikes) early.
- Transport: Cambridge rental; two SUVs Boston to DC (recommend minivan; get a good packing system); SF back to Boston: Delia drove back (need two licensed drivers; took 10 days with side trips, 5 would be reasonable); much more costly to drop the car in SF; shipped half the bikes home, rest in car. Luggage: small backpack or duffel, sleeping bag and pad.
- Workshops: kids 6-18, avg 11-12; 1.5 hr (longer makes them wiggly); focus on fun not technical. Scratch coding worked but laptops limited and too open-ended; building challenges fun (competition) but little theory and kids may have done it; simpler materials better. Idea: informal night hangout/dinner (hard recruitment since day camps came to workshops).
- Reusable from last year: cooking equipment, workshop materials, bike rack, sleeping gear. Create a warmshowers.com account.
- Most money came from: crowdfunding, MIT departments, PKG fellowships (deadline March 19).
