**Checkpoints:** DC; Shenandoah NP; Challenger LF (Hazard KY); Mammoth Cave NP; Kansas City; Denver; Grand Teton; Yellowstone; Portland.

**Endpoint options**
| San Francisco | Portland |
|---|---|
| Yosemite and Lake Tahoe; 2 fewer days; route from last year (2018); Nevada desert / Loneliest Road; higher elevation; road down to SF | Yellowstone and Grand Teton; 2 more days; TransAmerica trail route; gentler climate and elevation |
(2019 chose San Francisco.)

**Notes:** bike out of Hazard is steep and hard; 2 full days at Challenger; Newton public library; Loveland public library (go to Fort Collins); "soft confirm" schools by asking for dates around the time; minivan instead of SUV.

**National parks:** Great Smoky Mountains (add ~1 day, would skip Challenger LF); Mammoth Cave and Rocky Mountains on the 2018 route; Grand Teton, Yellowstone, Yosemite. Three routes after the Rockies: previous (2018) 1400 mi; Yellowstone route to SF 1600 mi (~70 mi/day, 4 more days); TransAmerica (Denver to Yellowstone to Oregon) 1500 mi. Possible added LF towns on the Yellowstone route: Idaho Falls, Pocatello (Idaho State Univ.), Twin Falls, Elko, Carlin, Battle Mountain. LF hosts with strong connections to revisit: Louisa County Public Library, Challenger KY, Lab: Revolution.

**DC to Shenandoah (from prior bike-route writeup):** Day 1: DC to Bear's Den Hiker/Biker Hostel (~$20/night, catering to AT hikers) via C&O towpath to White's Ferry ($2), south on Rt 15 into Leesburg, W&OD trail to Purcell, Silcott Springs Rd, Paxson Rd to Snickersville Turnpike, Rt 7 (worst road of trip, half mile), Rt 601 to hostel. Day 2: 601 to Rt 50 west 2.5 mi (busiest road; descends 600 ft), Howellsville Rd (638/603), Morgan Ford Rd/Happy Creek Rd into Front Royal (last non-park resupply) and the park by mid-day.
