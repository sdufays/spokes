**Materials:** hot glue guns (5-8), pipe cleaners, fabric scraps, cardboard tubes, cardboard, foamboard (needs razors, maybe skip), scissors, tape, Legos.

**Plan (~2 hr):**
- Setup (20 min): cut fabric, plug in glue guns, cover tables with brown paper (quotes, shapes exercise, workshop name).
- Intro: 1 min introductions, 1 min intro to rapid prototyping, 4 min "prototype yourself", 10 min introductions.
- Brainstorming warm-ups (20 min): shapes puzzle ("look for the next right answer"), Roman numerals ("look from multiple viewpoints"), timed "ideal home aspect" ("defer judgement", Pauling quote), squares ("challenge assumptions"); brainstorming types: categories (carnival games), reverse (most boring carnival game), active (walk around table/stairs), alphabet, timed, keywords (swap random words, "new favorite snack").
- Main event: carnival game. 1 min intro (interactive, scoring/winning, players); 2 min groups; 4 min timed + reverse brainstorm; 3 min vote; 7 min first prototype ("works like"); 3 min user testing; 10 min share; 20 min second prototype.
- Optional: perspective drawing exercise (straightedge, paper, pens).
- Resource: MIT 2.009 lectures (web.mit.edu/2.009/www/schedule/LectureList.html).
