PPE: lab coats, safety goggles (2x anti-fog), gloves, newspaper. Glassware/tools: 5-gal water jug, 4 beakers (+1 dedicated to sugar/sulfuric acid demo), bowl, Erlenmeyer flask, matches, splints, stir stick, thermometer, droppers, test tubes with lids (3 per group per workshop), sandwich ziplocks. Chemicals: food coloring, 1500 mL methanol (3x 500 mL ACS grade), 1 L bleach, 1 L vinegar, yeast, 3 L hydrogen peroxide (3x 500 mL 20-volume), sugar, dish soap, ~1 L 18 M sulfuric acid, borax, glue. (Amazon product links in source.)
