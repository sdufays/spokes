## Fundraising
Resources (tracking spreadsheets, saved in linked/):

**Start early.** After being accepted, hit the ground running. You need more than one person on fundraising. By spring, much of the excess money companies might donate (Texas Instruments, Microsoft, Dell, etc.) is already gone.

- **MIT departments** are a good place to start: cold-email department heads/administrators. Templates are in the "Fundraising" folder in the "Spokes 2026" Drive.
- **Grants** inside and outside MIT. Inside MIT: historically **TBP funding ($2,000)** after an application; **Baker Foundation $2,000** (Spokes planned and hosted a "bike repair and safety workshop"); the **Mad Money Grant** gave 2019 a few thousand for submitting a pamphlet with trip information.
- **Alumni groups:** wouldn't donate much but great connections; helped with housing in some states.
- **Bake sale + raffle for bike tune-ups** made ~$2,000 total.
- **2024:** most money ($45,330) came from the **J-WEL Education Grant** (https://www.jwel.mit.edu/grants). Applications open in February; highly recommended to apply again; decisions don't come until April so pursue other sources too.
- **Outside MIT:** many corporate/nonprofit grants; see the 2022 tracking sheet for received grants and more to apply to. Funded by the **St. Anthony Educational Foundation ($4,000)**.
- **Companies:** Lightforce and Desktop Metal (3D printing) helped with the 3D-printing workshop (real professionally printed parts); **Lightforce also sponsored $5,000 in 2022**.
- **Warning:** companies must register with MIT to donate to MIT (conflict-of-interest check). Done for Lightforce. Talk to Sandi if you get a corporate donation.
- **Crowdsource:** family, friends, coworkers, Facebook friends; everyone in the group participates.

Tips:
- Emphasize the education portion of the trip; that is the attraction for sponsors.
- Offer sponsor benefits (recognition on website / T-shirt; in-person visit if local).
- Cast a wide net; worst case they say no.
- Use mail merge in Word to send many emails at once.

## Budget
The budget dictates how much to fundraise, so make a good one. 2026 trip budget: "Expenses + Budget" sheet https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10qw-YwBw6pqD9v3xrn6hnOZSJoMLIgIy77G7KqC9vIM/edit (saved in linked/).
- Itemize as much as possible (e.g. list each bike-gear item and its local bike-shop cost, sum it).
- When in doubt consult the Spokes 2026 trip finances (transportation and food were guessed in budgeting).

## Finances
Two card types: the **travel card** (expenses during the trip: food, gas) and the **purchasing card (procard)** (gear, LF supplies; anything that made sense to buy at MIT before the trip). "Health and beauty" items (shampoo, body wash, sunscreen) cannot be bought on either card. Getting a card requires online training and an application. Spokes 2026: two people got pro cards and two different people got travel cards.

### Travel Card
- Holders must make a **Concur report** at the end of the trip ("stupid tedious and complicated"): upload receipts during the trip, not after.
- Receipts required for purchases >$75 and any food purchase. Catalogue other purchases too (gas can look like groceries on the Concur report).
- Lost receipt (quotes from Sandi Lipnoski): for charges >$75 attach a **missing receipt affidavit** and mark as **Non-Recoverable**; Non-Recoverable alerts auditors; worst case is an inquiry. The JWEL account would be a sponsored account (another department sponsoring, possibly extra approval); the Spokes account is a department account; since travel is charged to the SPOKES account, the trip is non-sponsored activity.

### Pro/Purchasing Card
- Send receipts to Marvelin within **two weeks** of purchase; you get emails from procard@mit.edu listing pending transactions.
- You may be audited: respond promptly explaining what was bought and how it will be used. Minimize audits by not buying food, gas or health & beauty on the procard. Prompt responders get compliments from Marvelin.
- A lost receipt is worse: if audited and you can't send it, they may deactivate the card.

Misc: purchases are tax exempt in MA but nowhere else.
