# MIT Spokes 2023 pamphlet (text)
**About:** bike DC -> San Francisco summer 2023; educational STEM workshops for middle schoolers, "physics to philosophy"; target rural and underserved areas; six/seven MIT students; 80-day, 3,900-mile; 9 summers since 2013 (not 2020-21).
**Workshops:** Chemical Engineering (berry-juice solar cells, leaf pigments); Electronics (breadboard circuits); 3D Printing (layer-by-layer demo); Art and Science (paradoxes, math of music/art, design a board game).
**Planned LF cities:** Washington DC, Louisa County VA, Hazard KY, Bowling Green KY, St. Louis MO, Newton KS, Lamar CO, Denver CO, Ely NV, Fallon NV. Website spokes-2023.wixsite.com/spokes2023; contact spokes-2023@mit.edu.
**People:** Katherine Caol Guo '23 (Materials Science, Art & Design), Audrey Gatta '23 (Art & Design, Econ), Duha Syar '23 (ChemE), Joy Ma '24 (Physics & Theater), Lucy Nester '24 (Nuclear Sci & Eng), Andrew Doan '25 (MechE).
**Testimonials:** Ophelia Payne (Louisa County Public Library): "great time with the 8 MIT Spokes students... hope they return." James Porter: "fantastic... 3 different sessions rotating." Janet Voth (Newton KS): hosted the team, July 4 fireworks, presented at Newton Public Library. **Tom Cravens, Challenger Learning Center of Kentucky (Hazard):** 4th Spokes group hosted; ~70 students from across rural eastern Kentucky over a two-day festival with four workshops.
**Sponsors/donation:** tax-deductible; link giving.mit.edu/search/node/spokes; email spokes-2023@mit.edu. Includes 2019 photos; handles @MITSPOKES, mitspokes.com.
