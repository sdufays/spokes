Hello,

My name is Duha Syar, and I am a student at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in Cambridge, MA. I am part of a program called Spokes which sends a team of 7 MIT students on a 80-day biking trip across the United States as part of an effort to rethink STEM education. As we go, we will stop at schools, libraries, and camps throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions in STEM.

Last year, our Spokes 2022 team stopped at the Department of Youth and Rehabilitation around May 31st, 2022 to hold a Learning Festival, and our team would love to add this spot to our route again! We predict that we’ll be in the area around June 5th. Let us know if you would be interested in having us this upcoming summer. We can provide more information about our curriculum and the specific dates as we approach the summer. And of course, please don’t hesitate to reach out to me if you have any questions!

In the meantime, feel free to check out our website at mitspokes.com.

On behalf of Spokes 2023,
Duha
