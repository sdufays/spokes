# OME application (Jan 2023; OME gave $500)
Argument: summer programs best provoke STEM interest, but outreach disparity favors "gifted" students over rural; STEM median wage $89,780 vs $40,020 non-STEM; employment growth 10.5% vs 7.5% (2020-30, BLS). Aligns with OME mission of academic excellence for underrepresented students. Citation: Harris & Hodges 2018, National Youth-At-Risk Journal.
**Line-item budget ($29,293):** Bikes & accessories $11,195; bike parts/tools $918; personal bike gear $1,490; travel $7,800; camping gear $240; consumables $4,150; educational supplies $2,000; emergency $1,500. No revenue generated; excess rolls over to next year.
