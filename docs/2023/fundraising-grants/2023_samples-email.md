(Thanks Audrey for sending a great email <3)

Dear MISTI,

I hope this email finds you well!

My name is Audrey Gatta, and I am a current senior at MIT double majoring in 14-2 and 4B and minoring in Spanish. During the summer of 2023, I will be a part of a student group called MIT Spokes. The Spokes 2023 team – a group composed of 8 MIT students – will be biking from Washington D.C to San Francisco to engage with students in rural communities this summer. As we go, we will stop at schools, libraries, and camps throughout the country to hold free learning festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops in rural towns along our route to inspire kids to pursue opportunities in STEM and in higher education.

Several members of our team have had MISTI, GTL, and study abroad experiences that inspired us to do Spokes this summer. Personally, participating in GTL in Andorra and Norway led me to discover my passion for teaching, and I am looking forward to carrying the lessons I learned from my GTL students to the learning festivals this summer. Katherine Guo also participated in GTL Brazil, and Joy Ma did a MISTI internship in the Netherlands; these experiences have taught us to connect across languages, cultures, and communities. We hope to apply this mission to our Spokes journey this summer by connecting with students in rural communities who may otherwise have limited access to STEM opportunities and higher education.

We are excited to start our journey this summer. Since we are in the middle of our fundraising process, we wanted to reach out in hopes that the MISTI would be willing to help fund this program and make our trip a reality. I and the rest of the Spokes 2023 team would love to get an opportunity to meet with you, share more about Spokes, and discuss possible sponsorship from MISTI to make the project possible!

Thank you so much! We look forward to hearing from you.

Warmly,
Audrey
