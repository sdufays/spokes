# SHASS Undergraduate Fund application (Joy Ma, 2023)
Project: MIT Spokes bikes DC -> San Francisco, summer 2023; educational workshops for middle schoolers; 80 days, ~3,900 miles; cross-country nine summers since 2013 (not 2020-21).

**Projected budget ($33,600):** Bikes $12,000 (gravel bikes); bike tools/parts $1,500; personal bike gear $2,000 (helmets, shorts, glasses, shoes); travel $8,000 (car, flights, gas); camping gear $500; consumables $6,000 (sunscreen, chamois cream, food); workshop materials $2,000; emergency $1,500 (hotels, bike repair); covid safety $100.
Applicant: Joy Ma, Theater Arts (21M-2) and Physics. Requires faculty letter of recommendation (LOR) - the 2023 LOR was late for the related Kelly-Douglas grant.
