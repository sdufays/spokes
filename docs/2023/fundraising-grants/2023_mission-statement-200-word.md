Mission Statement (200 word version)

	Our mission is to bring STEM education to rural and low-income communities across the United States while inspiring kids to be active. Biking from Washington, D.C. to San Francisco, we will be teaching 4 workshops in Physics, Chemistry, Mechanical Engineering, and Art and Technology in more than 10 self-organized learning festivals. These hands-on, interactive workshops are based on our experiences at MIT, revolving around our motto of “Mens et Manus.”
Spokes is a completely student-run program that is entering its 9th summer of operation. The Spokes 2023 team is made up of experienced enthusiastic MIT students from Materials Science, Art and Design, Economics, Biology, Physics, Mechanical Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Computer Science. Several of us have been Teaching Assistants, Educational Studies Program teachers, and CovEd mentors. We have collectively helped teach classes in math, physics, electrical engineering, chemistry, computer science and philosophy. We are also long-distance runners, varsity rowers, mountain bikers, and backpackers!
We’re grateful for this opportunity to impact the lives of over 500 students across America this summer. We can’t wait to bike the entire 3,500 miles, represent MIT’s hand and mind spirit, and inspire the next generation of STEM students!

Statement of Purpose (from 2018)

Spokes America brings STEM education to kids across the US. We offer exciting hands-on workshops to rural areas and underrepresented communities. As we bike from DC to San Francisco, we seek to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers, while encouraging communities to embrace spontaneity and outdoor adventure.
