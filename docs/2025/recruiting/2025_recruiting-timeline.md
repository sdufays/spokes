# Recruiting Timeline (run in fall 2025 to recruit the Spokes 2026 team)

| Date | Item |
|---|---|
| Sept 16 | Applications are out |
| Sept 29 | Application deadline |
| Oct 4 | Application deadline extension |
| Sept 29 - Oct 1 | Review applications |
| Oct 1 | Interview invites sent |
| Oct 2 - 15 | Interviews (45 min each) |
| Oct 16 | Interview debrief; send first batch of acceptances; send invites for group interviews |
| Oct 22, 23 | Group interviews (first decision deadline struck out) |
| Oct 24 - 25 | Final debrief |
| Oct 25 | Acceptances sent out (and waitlists/rejections) |
| Nov 1 | Decision deadline |
| Nov 2 | Waitlist decisions sent |
| Nov 8 | Final decision deadline |
| Nov 10 | Team intro email sent |
