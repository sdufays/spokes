Categories with timing:
- **Passion and Motivation (5 min):** What excites you most about Spokes? (red flag if no gusto). Which planning roles excite you or would you avoid (route planning, housing, fundraising, budgeting, website, gear, LF coordination)? Questions about planning? (note which roles they ask about).
- **Teaching (5 min):** target demographic is upper elementary to middle school; take 3 min to make a 1-min lesson on any topic. Red flag if they can't distill something simply.
- **Teamwork and group dynamics (7 min):** How do you contribute to community and morale? (look for balance of personal/group needs, emotional intelligence, inclusivity, humor; watch for vague or conflict-avoidant answers.) Describe a time you helped resolve a team conflict.
- **Personality/Fit (5 min):** what do you hope to get / contribute.
- **Logistical (2 min):** Spokes is a year-long commitment: planning in winter/spring (a few hours per week), summer trip from early June to mid-August (~75 days), lighter fall recruitment. Any conflicts?
- **Closing (2 min).**
