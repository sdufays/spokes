# Spokes 2026 Group Interview (fall 2025)
1. **Reinvent the Learning Festival:** create a new model for LFs (target: rural middle schoolers). How to ensure lasting community impact, what an LF looks like for a student, how to divide roles.
2. **Broke-Bike Mountain:** team arrives at dusk at Breaks Interstate Park; tomorrow to Hazard, KY; route not finalized; one bike won't shift (unrideable); camped the night before (must pack up); Warmshowers host in Hazard offers dinner at 6 PM. Plan the day: wake time, food/water from the car, breaks, lunch together or separate, bathrooms, broken bike, different speeds.
3. **The Loneliest Highway in America:** LF in Ely, Nevada on August 8 and next LF in Fallon, NV on August 12; ~275 miles over 3 days (Aug 9-11) with no housing and no Warmshowers hosts. How many miles a day, who to contact about housing, is camping an option? (Use Google Maps.)

Note: scenarios appear modeled on the real 2025 route (Breaks Interstate Park to Hazard KY; Ely NV to Fallon NV); the slide gives only month/day, so the year is inferred.
