# Spokes 2025 -> 2026 advice + notes

## Overall tips
- Don't leave things you can do before the trip to the start of the trip; it will be very busy.
- Do a bike tour (at least 2 nights) before the trip starts.

## Fundraising
- MIT 24-hour challenge on Pi Day for fundraising.
- Start fundraising ASAP.
- Make a concrete decision on how to allocate flight and personal-gear reimbursements BEFORE the trip.

## Bikes and maintenance
- Lights: they give timing flexibility but need to be bright (1000+ lumens front) to be useful in rural areas; consider rechargeable and battery life.
- Document gear distribution to team members; have people try things on/out early so returns are easy.
- Same bike for everyone = part compatibility, cheaper, can swap parts (e.g. a wheel).
- Get gravel bikes (wider tires, better geometry, usually lower gearing). Putting gravel tires on road bikes is not recommended (Ishaq).
- Bikes bought from Trek with 20% off everything. Ask about free maintenance, accessibility, bike-fit discounts. See "bikes bikes bikes" sheet for selection criteria (gear range, decent brakes and shifters most important; tubeless-ready a plus).
- Check availability of replacement parts; brake pads are bike-specific and cheap but hard to find in stores, so buy in advance.
- Tubeless: team had minimal flats, but a handful of tires had micro leaks (old setup or poor sealant). Check for leaks (shouldn't lose more than a few psi in a couple days). Still carry spare tubes; tubes are easy to replace.
- Almost every issue was fixable ourselves once we learned: sealant top-up, tubeless setup, tube replacement, derailleur adjustment, derailleur cable replacement, brake adjustment. A bike shop charges ~$30 for turning a few screws, so read the manual and try first. Wash and lube drivetrain (wax-based lube best).
- Go to a shop for: wheel truing, first time derailleur cables snapped, tight schedules.
- Should have bought: a tiny screwdriver, various pliers.
- Clip-ins: more efficient but a learning curve, practice early.
- Put bikes down derailleur-side up.
- Tinker with bike fit (seat height/angle, handlebar angle) since no professional fit.
- Ramona's checklist: check tires from shop if tubeless; know how to clean/lube drivetrain, fix a flat/plug tubeless/top up sealant, adjust derailleurs and brakes; tools on hand can set up tubeless, fix flats, replace inner derailleur/brake cable; small propane canisters (keep 2 instead of one big one) if camping up to ~2x as much as us.
