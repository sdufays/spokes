## Slide 7
Step 3: Route Planning
Look at previous routes
Some years go from SF - > DC. Tail winds are better but more upfront logistics
Assign 2-3 people to do this
We used the My Maps feature of google maps to do this
Try to have the basic shape of the route done by the end of IAP
Tried to follow Bike Route 76
Take Katy Trial in Missouri
Plan in National Parks Stops
## Slide 8
Step 4: Plan Learning Festival Locations
As you are route planning reach out to schools, libraries, youth camps, or whatever else you can think of along the way and ask to host learning festivals there (make an email template or bot to do this)
Start sending these emails by the end of January
Look at where we have been in the past for likely yesses, but try to add some more!
10 learning festivals is a really solid number to shoot for (but you can do more if you want. Most of the time a learning festival = free housing)
The Fallon Youth Center is really amazing go there
Do the learning festival at the DC correctional facility with Judge Faruqi 
## Slide 9
Step 5: Buy Bikes
We bought our bikes from Landry’s Bike Shop, who normally gives Spokes a 10% discount
Last year there was a 25% off sale for Specialized bikes, so we took advantage
I recommend a gravel bike. You will be a little slower than if you had a road bike, but you will encounter some gravel roads and other unexpected terrain so I think it’s worth it (it’s about completion, not speed)
EVERYONE SHOULD GET THE SAME BIKE** (this makes maintenance so much easier)
Try to buy the bikes no later than spring break, but earlier is better because you will more likely all be able to get the same bike
Get them fitted before the trip! Will save y’all from a lot of back pain!
** if you’ve got shorties like Jess, this may not hold
## Slide 10
Step 6: Rent and Pack Van
Use the MRB paradigm: Minimize Random Bullshit
Pack only what you absolutely need! Random bullshit will be very annoying to carry with you and cost the team countless hours organizing and losing shit in the sea of random stuff. 
Try to pack everything you own in one duffel bag (refer to this blog)
Buy a rooftop cargo carrier for the top of the van
Buy bike racks for the back of the van
Make sure when you pick up the van you aren’t buying a lemon
