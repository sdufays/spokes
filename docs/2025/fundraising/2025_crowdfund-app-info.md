Project title: MIT Spokes 2025. Summary: "Support Spokes as we rethink STEM education for K-12 students in underserved communities!"
Team 2025: Charles Ge '25 (2A-6), Greta Lawler '27 (3), Ishaq Balogun '25 (6-7), Joseph Alvarez '28 (16), Ramona Poreitere '28 (18 or 6-3), Ruth Shiferaw '25 (2), Sarah Schmitt '28 (7), Tian Lin '24/'25 (21M-2 and 6-2).
Purpose: share STEM with rural/underserved/incarcerated youth and dismantle stereotypes ("engineers and scientists can be athletic, too"); ~4,000 miles in ~80 days DC to SF. Also notes school funding by property tax worsens inequality; juvenile detention students get less instructional time. Workshops complement K-12 host curricula; connections with host families.
Crowdfund 2025 raised ~$9,000 (per ELO app). Last year's page: crowdfund.mit.edu/story/Mit-Spokes-2024; crowdfunding ran late March - late May.
