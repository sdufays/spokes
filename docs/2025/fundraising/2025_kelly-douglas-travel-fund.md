Budget assignment used: bike equipment, workshop supplies, website, safety, misc from Spokes 2024 carryover ($23,096); food from MIT departments ($11,700 raised); lodging from Kelly Douglas; transport still needed fundraising.
Essay: 2025 lessons - intro to machine learning, secrets and ciphers, phases of matter, bottle rockets, hovercraft design. ~4,000 miles over ten weeks.
