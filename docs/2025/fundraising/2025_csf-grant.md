Elevator pitch: 8 students, learning festivals at 14 schools/correctional facilities; topics machine learning, 3D printing, cryptology. Dates: Jun 5 - Aug 19, 2025. Members commit 5 hr/week Dec-May, 40+ hr/week summer.
Funding status at time: $21,475 raised since December from MIT departments, alumni clubs, individuals; $23,096 left from Spokes 2024. Also applied: J-WEL $20,000 (min), MindHandHeart $5,000, Mathworks $2,000.
Sustainable funding model: no recurring sources except Edgerton's annual donation; start fundraising as soon as team selected; MIT grants, departments, crowdfunding, in-person events, corporate grants/sponsors.
Team background: tutoring K-12/college; international education work in Germany, India, Mauritius, South Korea, Chile, Nepal.
