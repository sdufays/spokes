(Written for Spokes 2024; always cc the team email.) 2024 goal: $40,330 for a 74-day trip; links to giving.mit.edu/search/node/spokes (crowdfund) and https://giving.mit.edu/matching-gifts (corporate matching).
- **Individuals (professors, previous employers):** personal connection line; ask for donation to pay for bike gear, camping fees, food, gas, educational equipment; if they can't donate, ask to share website/donation page and sign up for updates.
- **MIT Club:** state your major/home; ask the club to donate and advertise the crowdfund link and matching-gift program in newsletters/events; mention if route passes through their state.
- **Company:** explain your connection to the company/how it fits Spokes; ask to sponsor; offer a meeting.
- **ELOx (Office of Experiential Learning):** ask about the ELO Teaching and Learning program; start from "Spokes is an MIT-based, non-profit, student-run initiative as part of an effort to rethink STEAM education."; route starts at Lincoln Memorial, ends at Golden Gate Bridge.
- **Cycling club:** note you will bike through their state; look for cyclists to stay with and ride alongside.
