Total estimated budget $48,119 (narrative also says $48,911). $23,096 left from Spokes 2024. $5,750 raised from MIT departments at the time. Remaining need just under $20,000; ~$7,000 more possible from pending proposals/crowdfunding. J-WEL has no overhead.
Budget narrative: bikes/gear for 8 ~ $16,500; van rental ~$7,000; gas ~$1,500; flights back ~$2,000; lodging ~$2,250 (Warmshowers subscription etc.); festival equipment $4,750. 15-60 students per festival; ~70-day trip.

## Key content
- Spokes 2024 findings: 8 festivals across 6 states, groups of 2-15; modules adapted to local context (e.g., sustainability modules adjusted where students were skeptical of climate change). Collaborated with MIT Open Learning pK-12 who ran extended versions of "Sustainable Approaches and Materials to Manufacturing" and "Chemistry and Sustainability of Concrete Production" for Boston middle schoolers.
- Lesson learned: Google Form exit surveys don't produce quality feedback from middle schoolers; plan was to switch to in-person testimonials/short interviews with partners and drop most free-text survey questions; keep numeric ratings plus "would you attend another / virtual festival?" questions.
- 2025 prep: registered to teach at MIT Splash; working with The Educational Justice Institute (TEJI) for a youth correctional LF in Boston in late spring. Call with Judge Faruqui (DC) before the DC LF: hands-on/tactile lessons work best at juvenile detention centers (all items screened before admission).
- Past activities: "Krispie Koncrete" (concrete with Rice Krispies), "Chromatography and Color".
- Deliverables: publish lessons/toolkits on mitspokes.com; coordinate with Stanford Spokes to pool lessons; onboarding/training for Spokes 2026.
- Draft notes: Meeting with Rebecca (J-WEL): build on Spokes 24 connections; deployable toolkits; same schools/build connections; pass knowledge between teams; Hermann MO and St. Louis adult teaching school ideas; rural + incarcerated; "cognizant that this is a large grant".
