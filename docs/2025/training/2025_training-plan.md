**Goal:** build endurance, strength, speed, recovery for consecutive long rides; 16 weeks (e.g. spring semester); aimed at limited-time students preparing for the first weeks, not full 50-70 mile rides. Components: endurance, intervals, hill work, strength, recovery. Reference: liftmanual.com.

**Phase 1 Base (wks 1-4):** 2-3 indoor rides/wk (start 20-30 min on mountain/hill setting or 1 hr steady, 60-80% max HR, progress weekly); mobility warm-up (elephant walk, world's greatest stretch, deep squat hold, couch stretch with hip circles, plank); strength 1-2x/wk low volume (goblet squat, split squat, Romanian deadlift, plank, pullups/lat pulldowns, pushups/bench); 1+ rest day.
**Phase 2 Build (wks 5-9; spring break week off):** 1-2 indoor rides (HIIT + hills + tempo 20-90 min) + 1 outdoor ride/wk (start 10+ mi, goal pace 18-22 mph; Saturday group rides organized by Ishaq); leg strength 1x/wk (back squat, single-leg press, deadlift, hip thrusts; avoid before rides), optional upper body.
**Phase 3 Peak (wks 10-13):** 3-4 rides/wk: endurance 1+ hr/20+ mi, indoor hill climbing, HIIT (4-6 x 3-5 min max with equal rest), tempo, progressively longer and consecutive-day outdoor rides.
**Phase 4 Taper (wks 14-16):** 3 rides/wk, endurance easy or HIIT 3-5 x 2-4 min; reduce strength.
**Tracking:** distance/duration (Strava/Apple Watch), heart rate (220 - age), speed, power (watts), cadence (80-100 rpm); strength via Hevy app.
**Glossary:** endurance 60-80% max HR; HIIT zone 4-5; tempo 76-90%; recovery <60%.
