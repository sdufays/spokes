Written Jan 2025 (Joseph Alvarez) summarizing the 2024 blog. Day: miles, elevation ft, notes.
- D1 DC festival. D2 Oakton to Linden 64.1 mi/3,881 (W&OD Trail). D3 rest to get new rental. D4 Linden to Big Meadows 60.9/7,569: highway to Shenandoah has no shoulder; brutal climb; car-rental trouble nearly stranded group w/o food - consider satellite phones.
- D5 Big Meadows to Waynesboro 61.5/2,358 (Loft Mountain Wayside only food on Skyline). D6 to Lexington 43.8. D7 to Roanoke area 58.6 (nav problems). D8 Roanoke-Draper 60.9. D9 Draper-Tazewell 69.1/4,701. D10 Tazewell to Breaks Interstate Park 70.9/5,851. D11 rest at Breaks. D12 Breaks to Hazard KY 77.9/4,255: KY dogs run loose; bought air horns but not enough; scary last stretch of KY-15, detour north at Sassafras (+2 mi). D13 Hazard LF (Challenger Learning Center). D14 Hazard to Koomer Ridge 70.8. D15 Red River Gorge to Lexington KY 64.3 (distillery tour). D16 Lexington to Liberty KY 69.3. D17 Liberty to Mammoth Cave 83.1 (gravel, cave tour). D18 Mammoth Cave to Bowling Green 32.4 (short ride instead of rest day = good idea). D19 Bowling Green LF. D20 to Madisonville KY 85.4. D21 to Harrisburg IL 82.3. D22 Gun Creek IL 43.3. D23 to St Louis 107 mi (first century; heat; someone ran out of water - ride in pairs). D24 rest St Louis (Gateway Arch). D25 Hermann MO 82.9 (Katy Trail). D26 Jefferson City 51.2. D27 Sedalia 62.9. D28 Osawatomie KS 118.9 (Katy + Rock Island trails). D29 Council Grove KS 100.5: storms, group split, flooded road crossed alone - never split up or cross flood alone. D30 to Newton KS 73: 70 mph winds, lightning; check SPC outlook daily. D31 Newton LF. D32 Strafford KS 82.6 (stealth camp at rest stop, July 4). D33 Dodge City 75.8. D34 Lakin 78.6. D35 Lamar CO 78.4. D36 Lamar LF. D37 Fowler CO 86. D38 Colorado Springs 99.8 (13 mi dirt road at Boone). D39 Denver 60.7 (alumni). D40 rest Denver. D41-42: US-285 out of Denver is very dangerous, some quit mid-climb; gravel alternative impassable; Kenosha Campground, Buena Vista. D43 rest Buena Vista (met Stanford Spokes; stake tents). D44 Gunnison 68.6 (Continental Divide). D45 Gunnison LF. D46 Paonia 76.7 (gravel descent; orchard stay). D47 Grand Junction 68.8. D48 Moab 106 mi (6 flats; I-70 shoulders). D49 rest Moab. D50 Wingate CG. D51 Green River State Park UT (8 showers). D52 Capitol Reef 96.1 (driver had no-interstates off; stuck on dirt; E. coli in Fremont River). D53 rest. D54 Escalante 78.4/7,365 (Kiva Koffeehouse; a rider broke an arm on steep descent). D55 Bryce Canyon 47.1. D56 rest Bryce (cold nights). D57 Zion 79.5 (tunnel closed to bikes; van shuttle). D58 rest Zion (Angel's Landing). D59 Hurricane UT 22.4 (Narrows). D60 Milford UT 100.3. D61 Baker NV 83.4. D62 Ely 61.5. D63 Eureka 83.7. D64 Austin NV 67.7. D65 Fallon NV 112 (US-50 "loneliest road"). D66 Fallon LF. D67 South Lake Tahoe 109/5,722 (construction detour +15 mi). D68 Boys & Girls Club LF Lake Tahoe. D69-70 rest days (third unneeded; one rider hit head on a curb). D71 Placerville 90.2. D72 Sacramento 61.4. D73 Benicia 72.3 (water bladder, roof bag, bike rack broke; illegal Interstate for 1.5 mi). D74 SF 42.3. D75 logistics: shipping bikes back was "logistical hell"; dinner with Spokes founder/original members.
Takeaways: do not take US-285 out of Denver; do not take US-50 out of Tahoe (no shoulder); French bread is a cheap calorie-dense lunch; carry bike computers and dry bag for camera; hand pumps; ~4,000 calories/day for big riders; stick together; storms - start early/late; Spokes stickers on Colorado sign; shorter ride days can replace full rest days.
