Same five blurbs as 'Summary of Workshops' (ML, Science of Secrets, What's the Matter?, Bottle Rockets, 3D Printing) plus:
- **Holy Hovercrafts!** Air pressure and friction; build mini hovercrafts with balloons and CDs; air cushions reduce friction; real-world hovercraft applications.
- **Lend a Helping Hand: Robotic Arms.** Design cardboard-and-string robotic arms to pick up ping pong balls; biomechanics, tension, control systems; how prosthetics are developed.
