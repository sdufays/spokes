- Intro (~5 min): introduce Spokes; rockets motivation; icebreakers (would you go to Mars? name your rocket and its gadget; furthest from home). Falcon Heavy = 3.13M lb ~ 20,000 people ~ 750 cars ~ 8 loaded 747s.
- Physics (~8 min): impart force; equal/opposite; overcome gravity; momentum p = mv - expel light things very fast; payloads are tiny vs rocket mass (~$1500-2000/lb to orbit). Demo: rolly chair throwing basketball slowly vs tennis ball fast.
- Chemistry (~7 min): NaHCO3 + CH3COOH -> NaC2H3O2 + H2O + CO2; balancing equations (kids guess products); extra gas = pressure; balloon demo; cork demo; real rockets use hot expanding gas.
- Launch (~30 min): overview; challenge kids to get bottle standing upright on its own with provided materials; split into two groups each supervised by a Spokes member. **Safety: do not let kids launch without warning.**
- Conclusion (~5 min): momentum transfer, expulsion of matter; careers (mechanical, software, astronautical, materials, chemists, biologists, structural, industrial engineers); current events (NASA moon, booster catching, Mars rovers, Dragonfly).
- Cleanup (~3 min).
