- Intro (5 min): names, grade at MIT, majors, career aspirations; explain activity.
- Part 1 Physics (15 min): forces - thrust(+), gravity(-), drag(-); Newton's 3rd law; rockets weigh ~750 cars; p = mv: expel heavy things quickly; mostly fuel. DEMO: cork in water bottle (chemical -> kinetic energy).
- Part 2 Chemistry (15 min): combustion in real rockets; ours makes lots of CO2 gas in closed system -> pressure. CH3COOH (vinegar) + NaHCO3 (baking soda) -> NaC2H3O2 + H2O + CO2. Conservation of mass. DEMO: balloon over bottle shows CO2.
- Bottle rockets (15 min): do one yourself; kids set up; run 1-2 more; experiment with baking-soda containment, platform design; competition for highest.
- Wrap up (10 min): questions; many careers involved in rockets; college questions; clean-up.
