Source: https://www.steampoweredfamily.com/bottle-rockets/
Educational angle: launch pad (legos, craft sticks) = engineering; baking soda + vinegar = chemistry (acetic acid + sodium bicarbonate -> sodium acetate, water, CO2; trapped CO2 builds pressure until cork pops); propulsion = physics (thrust up; gravity and drag bring it down).
Materials per rocket: empty soda bottle, 1 tbsp baking soda, 1-2 cups vinegar, 4-inch square paper towel, cork, launchpad materials.
Instructions: build launchpad; pour vinegar into bottle; wrap baking soda in paper towel, place in mouth of bottle; insert cork; flip onto launch pad; RUN.
