**The Power of Machine Learning.** Students explore how computers learn from data without explicit directions; AI training simulation; collectively train a model; applications in computer vision, speech recognition, recommendation systems.

**The Science of Secrets.** Historic anecdotes, a little math, algorithmic thinking; hiding and encrypting information; create Caesar ciphers and apply cryptanalysis against basic attacks.

**What's the Matter?** How temperature, viscosity and molecular interactions affect states of matter via the "Ice Cube Game" and oobleck; non-Newtonian liquids; how models explain them.

**Blast Off! DIY Bottle Rockets.** Chemistry (vinegar + baking soda), physics (thrust, gravity, drag), engineering (build launch platforms).

**From 2D Design to 3D Printing.** Design and create small objects; digital modeling, slicing, material properties; how 3D printing transforms industries from healthcare to aerospace.
