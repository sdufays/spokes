Learning objectives: describe properties of solids/liquids; how temperature affects particle motion; what makes non-Newtonian fluids (oobleck) behave differently; practice asking questions, forming hypotheses, interpreting evidence.
Slides also cover bonding types (metallic, ionic, covalent, intermolecular forces: strong vs weak).
Interactive: PhET States of Matter Basics sim https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/states-of-matter-basics/latest/states-of-matter-basics_en.html
Discussion prompts: properties of solids vs liquids; counter-examples to categories; "How do you think oobleck works? Is it a solid or liquid?"
