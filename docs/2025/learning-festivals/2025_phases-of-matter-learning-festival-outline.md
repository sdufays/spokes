**Learning objectives:** how temperature affects particle movement in states of matter; viscosity vs particle interactions; what makes non-Newtonian fluids like oobleck different; asking questions/hypotheses; computational modeling in modern science.

**Warm up - Ice Cube Race:** 1 ice cube + 1 paper plate per kid; melt it as fast as possible; discuss strategies, where heat comes from, how heat moves.

**Main block**
1. Brain dump: solid vs liquid? what do particles do? what does "flow" mean at molecular level?
2. Marshmallow modelling: mini marshmallows + toothpicks (solid), + string/tape (liquid), loose (gas); bag and shake = add energy; which keeps shape?
3. "Be the Molecules" game: solid at low temp -> vibrating -> liquid (slide past) -> faster -> gas (bounce around room).
4. Mixing solids and liquids: guess water + sugar / salt / dirt / sand / cornstarch; dissolving changes properties.
5. Oobleck: groups make a batch, guess its state, play, form hypotheses; explain non-Newtonian fluids; science method and computational models (MIT News 2019 oobleck/cornstarch article https://news.mit.edu/2019/oobleck-behavior-predict-cornstarch-1006).

**Cooldown:** YouTube https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JJfppydyGHw and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sl0BHueSjvA ; weird forms of matter; quiz-style; "science is all about the exceptions".

**Supplies:** cornstarch, water, food dye, ice, container for ice (plus marshmallows, toothpicks, string, tape, bags).
Note from reviewer: more on mixing, fluids, viscosity; non-Newtonian = non-linear relation between force and flow.
