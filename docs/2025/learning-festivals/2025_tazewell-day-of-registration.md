# Tazewell VA, Sat June 14, 2025 - festival-day registration sheet (aggregate only)

Source: `Tazwell Registration.numbers.zip` (an Apple Numbers file the Drive connector could not open); read from its embedded preview image. Columns: child's name, grade in school (just finished), age, parent's name, phone, email, alternate contact + phone, "Photo release signed?" checkbox, "Present at event" checkbox.

- 31 child rows (many siblings and cousins sharing a parent/guardian), grades K through 9, ages 5 to 15 (mostly 6-11).
- Roughly 40% had the photo-release box ticked on the sheet at the time; the signed paper releases are in `Photo Releases/Tazewell VA` (not copied: contain minors' info).
- "Present at event" column was still blank in the preview; the sheet was used as a sign-in/contact roster.
- Lesson for 2027: collect parent phone/email and photo-release status in one sheet before the day (2026 used pre-registration Google Forms: see learning-festivals/registration/2026_registration-form-responses-aggregate.md).
