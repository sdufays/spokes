**Part 1 - crafts and stories** (per student: compass, scissors, construction paper template, metal fastener, marker, pencil; hole puncher): 10 min craft walkthrough; 1 min "how do you hide a secret?"; 2 min Greeks and Persians (steganography); 2 min Julius Caesar (cryptography, substitution).
**Part 2 - code-making:** encrypt message together (4), decrypt together (3), encrypt to partner (5), decrypt partner's (5). Concepts: algorithm and key.
**Part 3 - code-breaking:** how safe is it (2); brute force (25/26 shifts) (3); can we do it faster? frequency analysis - double letters (2), letter counting (3); dictionary attack/guessing words (3); spaces; safety recap (2).
**Part 4 - better ciphers:** mixed alphabet (2), key phrase (3), symbols (2), Vigenere, others (4).
**Part 5 - free time** to hide messages.
Printouts also include extra readings, online course, more cipher names, common questions.
