Process: a Google Form "social media bios" (Jan 2025) asked each rider for a bio and "upload a few pictures! you can do up to 10 (for insta formatting). For the website and other platforms with only 1 pic, indicate which one you would like to be your 'main' picture." Responses (photos named "N - Name") went to a Drive responses folder (~100 photos for 8 riders, files like "1main - Ramona Poreitere.jpg").

Example bio structure (first person, casual, 3 paragraphs): name + year + hometown; major/clubs/activities; hobbies; why they joined Spokes; ends with call to support via the crowdfunding link in bio and follow along.
- Sarah Schmitt (freshman, Las Vegas NV, course 5-7, UROP in the Laub lab, pole vaulter on track team, student art guide at the List Visual Arts Center): joined "to share my love of the life sciences in a creative and engaging way, see the United States in a whole new, unique way, and truly challenge myself both physically and mentally."
- Greta Lawler (sophomore, Arlington VA, Course 3 Materials Science + Urban Studies and Planning concentration, minor in Chinese; Energy and Climate Club, Science Policy Initiative, French House, Running Club): wants to expand access to clean energy; running, cooking, reading, travel photography; "sweaty mid-ride ice cream stops"; Strava.
