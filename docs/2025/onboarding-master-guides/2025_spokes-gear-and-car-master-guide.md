## Choosing and buying bikes
Trek Cambridge gives 20% on all gear bought through them (use for big purchases); Cambridge Bicycle sells at wholesale but small selection (small purchases). Recs: tubeless (fewer flats, simpler repairs), same bike for everyone (swap parts), gravel bikes, disc brakes, drop bars, 35 mm tires or wider. Shop around. Recommended: Cambridge Trek in Alewife (negotiate 20%); Landry's (discount on gear, not bikes); avoid Cambridge Bicycle (overpriced).
Spokes 2026: Trek Checkpoint ALR3 gen 3 for everyone + bike fits with Brendan Connor at CycleLifePT. Chosen for the 1x10 gearing (no front derailleur, huge rear cog for Appalachia climbs), wide tire clearance, and Trek warranties worth several hundred dollars.
Leftover team gear (bike gear, camping supplies, LF supplies) is stored in Milkdrop N52; coordinate with Edgerton.

## Bike gear
Things to keep on you: water bottles + cages (get METAL bottles; 2026 found plastic doesn't keep water cool; Camelbak Podium metal), frame/saddle bags, small first aid kit, optional Swiss Army knife, spare tubes matching tire size, multitool with allen keys, frame pump, portable charger.
Must-have: front/rear lights, clip-in shoes/pedals, helmet, first aid, allen keys, bottles (2024 began with 48 oz, upgraded to 72 oz in Utah), floor pump with gauge + frame pump, snacks, seat (2019/2022 Terry Liberator; 2024 and 2026 did not upgrade), sunglasses, gloves, padded shorts, tire repair kit (tubes, levers, patch; plugs if tubeless).
Nice to have: sun legs/sleeves, chamois butter (used less than expected), fenders (2026 skipped; one muddy incident), handlebar bag (convert a fanny pack), battery pack, open-ear headphones (Shokz; helps in Kansas/E. Colorado).
In the car: chain cleaning tools, allen keys, floor pump, extra sealant, spare tires/tubes, bike rack (check compatibility), locks.
Maintenance: clean and lube weekly; everyone should know how to fix flats, tune derailleur, adjust brakes, clean/lube chain.

## Car rental
- MIT discount with Budget and Avis; talk to Sandi (slipnosk@mit.edu) and Chris (mayerc@mit.edu). Reserve as soon as you know departure date. Recommend a minivan; preferred Budget location Central Square, 350 Mass Ave. 2022 used a Ford Explorer (only 6 people). Bike rack used 2022: Saris Bones EX. 2023 and 2024 used a 2022 Chrysler Pacifica; 2024 does not recommend its bike rack (bent and fell). 2025 also had a Pacifica (recall).
- Avis/Budget is poor but the only company covering under-21 drivers; issues: talk to Edgerton and Jessica Tam (VP Finance office), who got a discount.
- Spokes 2023, 2024, 2026 also rented a truck/cargo van for the first days to carry bikes and people down (booked via Edgerton). 2024: Toyota Tacoma fit 3 bikes, returned at Dulles (~50 min from DC, near Oakton where trip started). 2026: cargo van.
- Size: with everything packed only driver and passenger seats were usable; minivan has 7 seats for 8 people. SUV = 8 seats and more room but costlier and harder for new drivers (Sandi advised against). Don't go smaller than a minivan. Stanford 2024 had an 11-seater and liked it.
- One-way vs two-way: 2023/2024 one-way to SFO, a few thousand dollars more, then shipping bikes/gear took 2 days and was very expensive. Two-way with East Coast members driving back is an option; people not going east still need to move bikes (check bike on plane).
- Background checks: start early via Edgerton (emailed forms); cannot drive without one. Edgerton books the car and charges the account directly (not the travel card; travel card can book a car in an emergency). Insurance/discount: insurance.mit.edu rentals page.
- Only 5 drivers (renter + 4) can be added at the rental desk for roadside assistance; have 4 drivers go to the Avis/Budget desk (Boston or Dulles) before the trip. You can still drive if background-checked.
- At pickup: ask last service date, walk around and photograph scratches, check engine and tires, look up recalls.
- Car troubles (2019, 2023, 2024 all had some): rental cars have no tire-change kit; call 24-hr roadside number on the keys; if the named renter is away call Edgerton; Avis replaced the whole car when repairs were needed. 2026 notes: don't speed in Vail CO; had to rip off a dragging oil-pan cover in Kansas.
- Accidents: call Edgerton (Chris); MIT has no physical insurance card, don't give personal insurance, show rental agreement and say company car insured through MIT; get written proof of insurance for the glovebox; fill out Avis/Budget incident form ASAP.
- Day to day: car gets gross (hand vacuum, air freshener); hard-sided plastic boxes from Walmart; everyone should know how to inflate car tires; overloaded car rides low (2024 got stuck on a bumpy road). 2026 note: "Sandi will be on your ass all the time... a little respectful pushback may be necessary."
