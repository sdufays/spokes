## Website
Now WordPress (Wix was used in earlier years and hosts old team sites). WordPress blog format supports comments and images. The team owns the mitspokes.com domain, currently pointed at the previous team's site; migrate it to your year's site (poorly documented; customer support helped) and preserve the entire past site. Login email is the spokes-current@mit.edu list (password in original, removed).

## Instagram (@mitspokes)
Post semi-regularly before and during the trip. 2022: featured by mitstudents; mitpics reposted. Stanford Spokes posted daily. 2026 TLDR: (1) individual team member intros, (2) collab posts with the mitedgerton Instagram for fundraising/marketing, (3) fundraising progress and thank-you posts (everyone reposts to personal stories), (4) weekly recap "dump" during summer, (5) a post for every learning festival (take lots of photos/video), (6) a post for every restaurant that sponsors a meal.

## Team t-shirt, jerseys
JD designed the team t-shirt (2024). Polyester t-shirts from 4imprint (good for LFs and PJs). Jerseys from Jakroo: needed a one-time license agreement because it's not an MIT-approved vendor; strongly recommended. 2026: first name on back of LF shirts; two jerseys (black and white) for cold/hot days; laundry loops.

## Media
Official logo file in "Social Media, Blog, Website, Media" in Spokes 2022 Drive. 12-page InDesign pamphlet by Maxwell Yun (2022; likely won the Lightforce sponsorship), printed at CopyTech ~$5/copy saddle-stitched on 11x17; fonts Proxima Nova and Overpass. Thank-you card (double-sided 8.5x11, folded; cardstock with machine fold at MIT CopyTech); post-trip photo thank-you cards ~$30 for ~40. Posters (8.5x11 and 11x17; used on the Infinite for recruitment/donations); Instagram ad template in Photoshop.
