## Sponsorship levels (2016 "Spokes Details for Companies")
- **Platinum $10,000:** all gold benefits + inclusion in video promotional materials + logo at all Spokes promo events on MIT/Harvard campuses.
- **Gold $5,000:** silver benefits + logo on team t-shirts worn throughout the trip and on the support vehicle.
- **Silver $2,500:** logo on website; lunch or Skype chat with team members; pack of three "Sponsor" Spokes t-shirts; three souvenirs from three route locations of the sponsor's choice; listing as educational resource on website/social; distribution of sponsor promo items (stickers, pens, bags) at schools taught.
One-pager structure: tagline "Inspiring students to learn what they love" / who and why / what we need (bikes, apparel, van rental+insurance, food; laptops, circuits, power supplies, kits) / levels / impact / team list / contact (Antonis Michael, 857-272-6993, antonism@mit.edu, spokes-2016@mit.edu). Website www.spokesamerica.com, last year's blog spokesamerica15.tumblr.com. The 2015 version was a near-copy.

## Cold email -- general (non-gear companies) skeleton
Greeting "Hello from Spokes America!", mission (10 members biking DC to SF, 10-15 Learning Festivals), problem (pipeline of scientists/engineers "at companies like [ORG]"), what sponsors enable, **customize: why this company cares (don't paste their mission), their past local-community actions, audience match, 1-2 benefits**; link to site + promo videos + attached budget; signed by name + school email + phone.

## MIT department cold email (departments-cold-email)
Subject "Fundraising for Spokes America". Personal intro (year, MIT/Harvard), team of 8, Boston Globe article about Spokes 2015, "As head of the [subject] department, would you help fund..."; tie the subject the member teaches to kids' daily lives; STEM seems intimidating to youths without exposure. This produced the department money in 2016.
