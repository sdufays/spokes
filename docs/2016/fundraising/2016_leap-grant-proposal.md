**Applicant:** Yang Dai; group: Kacie Bawiec, Noam Ben-Tzur, Jean Chow, Kiana Jodeiry, Antonis Michael, Nhat Nguyen, Chloe Yang (MIT IDs omitted). Category: Materials -- "educational materials to teach STEM workshops in underserved communities". Trip June 6 - Aug 15, 2016.

**Confirmed workshops at time of writing (7):** Challenger Learning Center of Kentucky, Hazard (Jun 16-17); LASCLEX, Lexington KY (Jun 20-21); Liberty Public Schools (Jun 23-24, later cancelled); Lab: Revolution, Farmington MO (Jul 2-3); Newton Public Library KS (Jul 15); Loveland Public Library CO (Jul 25-26); The Leonardo, SLC (Aug 2-3). Expected ~10; also a practice workshop in Cambridge.

**Abstract (reusable framing):** US education isn't producing enough scientists/engineers; 10 Learning Festivals where students touch, feel, design, build; build off everyday life ("the science underneath the way they drive to school, use their computers, or climb a tree"); connect teachers to resources; biking challenges the stereotype that STEM kids aren't athletic; team 8 MIT + 2 Harvard, 60% female.

**Plan details:** workshops: Scratch coding (needs laptops), neuroscience (toy EEG beanie with NeuroSky chip -- cost not budgeted), astronomy (borrow mobile planetarium, else constellation books), robotics (6.01-style robots, $300 electronics), water rockets (recycled soda bottle). Teams of 5, each workshop led by two experts; 2-day stays run all 5, 1-day stays pick 2. Post-workshop surveys; share edX/Coursera/Scratch/Khan/Codecademy resources. Lodging: campsites, MIT/Harvard alums, Warm Showers. Safety: required training, support van; rocket safety rules. 

**Funding status in proposal:** secured $5,280 Course 9 UROP + $1,000 Aero/Astro; anticipated $4,000 Edgerton; unspent prior-year Spokes fund $7,000; total $17,820; need $30,531 - 17,820 = **$12,711**, to be raised via MIT Crowdfund. **Requested $3,500 from LEAP** (teaching materials only: 20 laptops $2,980 + rockets $298 + electronics $300 = $3,578). Full budget identical to "Budget on diet" v2 ($30,530.85).
