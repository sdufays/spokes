**Title:** Spokes America: Interactive STEM Workshops for Middle and High School Students in Underserved Communities. Applicants (11): Jean Chow, Yang Dai, Omar Embaby (Harvard), Nhat Nguyen, Chloe Yang, Ann Hughes, Kacie Bawiec, Noam Ben-Tzur, Antonis Michael, Annie Wei (Harvard), Kiana Jodeiry. (At the TBP stage the plan was two teams of 5 and 6, one riding each direction.)

**Timeline proposed:** Apr-May 2016 fundraising, contacting schools (half previously visited), curriculum prep (astronomy, neuroscience, programming, mechanical engineering) tested on local middle schoolers; each member commits 5 hrs/week; bike-repair, CPR and physical training sessions. June-Aug 10 weeks of biking ~60 mi/day, 1-2 days per school, 10-15 schools, feedback collected. Sept 2016 reflection and recruiting for Spokes 2017. Previous Spokes members have become mentors to interested students.

**Teaching background of the team (summary):** chess coach, SAT tutors, tutors in math/physics, taught sailing, church-music teaching, Israeli military instructor, debate-conference chairs, nature-center docent/summer-camp counselor, Girls Inc. and Blind Children's Learning Center volunteers. Personal statements stress adventure, seeing the country, teaching what they love, physical challenge.
