## Email to schools (templates, signed Chloe Yang, cjwyang@mit.edu, (585) 880-4677)
**Cold:** Subject "Harvard and MIT engineering students want to visit ___school___!" We are a team cycling across the country teaching engineering at schools/camps; stopping in ___city___ this ___month___; would love a 1-2 day Learning Festival; working with MIT and Teach for America; 7 undergrads teaching environmentally conscious engineering, team engineering/design, neuroscience, astronomy, CS.
**Revisit (past hosts):** "Last year a team ... stopped by ___city___ on ___date___ ... thank you for hosting us! ... host again?" (Also a 3/28 calendar item: mention Khan School in the email.)

## Workshop menu (curriculum-deets form: subject + activities)
- **Aerospace engineering:** build and launch water rockets; ideal-gas physics; derive water-rocket thrust, numerical performance prediction, compare model vs launch.
- **Neuroscience:** cellular basis of brain; measure electrical activity in own muscles (Backyard Brains Muscle Spikerbox); perception/"the dress" illusion; music/harmony as social construct; LED sticker + copper tape neuron; EEG beanie (instructables "Build an EEG hat"). Extra ideas: sheep brain dissection (virtual/actual, biologycorner.com), virtual neuron simulator (brainu.org), Tinkertrons artificial neurons.
- **Math:** real-life probability (Deal or No Deal, PIG dice game, sports strategy, fractions), basic graph theory/algorithms, mental-math competition.
- **Computer science:** Scratch (scratch.mit.edu) interactive games/animations; Code.org Hour of Code (Flappy Bird, Angry Birds, Plants vs Zombies).
- **Astrophysics:** scale of time/space, origins of universe, black holes, celestial sphere, constellations; NASA education materials (nasa.gov/education/materials).
