Spokes enables K-12 students to develop their
creativity and critical thinking through project-
based STEM workshops led by passionate
college students biking across the US
A Budding Organization
For 5 years, Spokes has grown organically as a student-led initiative at MIT, Harvard,
and Stanford. So far, 44 college students have taught approximately 1,900 students in
31 communities — all while biking from Washington, DC to San Francisco. Our work
has been highlighted by everyone from politicians to the Associated Press.
Teaching STEM through Innovative Learning Festivals
Spokes opens K-12 students to new possibilities in two ways:
1.We show up by bicycle as peers, not teachers. We live in the communities we visit.
We demonstrate how you can achieve goals under your own power.
2.We teach hands-on workshops at schools, libraries, and camps that introduce
students to a new, exciting topic and give them a framework for pursuing an
original idea. Past workshops include building model rockets, designing robots,
and dissecting cow eyeballs.
Developing Tomorrow’s Educational Leaders
Spokes is organized around college teams.In groups of 6 to 8, college students:
-Design and iterate new project-based workshops about a topic they love
-Deliver workshops to 200 to 400 K-12 students over the duration of a summer
-Gain ﬁrst-hand understanding of the breadth of US educational experiences
Growing Spokes
After 5 years of organic growth, we are ready to turn Spokes into a world-class non-
proﬁt. We are in the process of forming an advisory board of leaders in education,
biking, and non-proﬁts to help us grow.
Spokes Vision is to build a self-sustaining movement that values self-directed learning
styles and empowers all individuals to start their own project and pursue their own
ideas. Learning should be tangible, accessible, and community focused.
