Instructions for Recruiting Spokes Advisors & Funders

Purpose:
Advisors
We need a set of advisors to help turn Spokes into a world-class non-profit.
Everyone Spokes Hub member has connections and the ability to reach out. As such, we need everyone’s help to secure those set of advisors.
Funders
We are hoping to have a small number of individuals give Spokes ~$1,000. In total, we’d like to raise $10 to $20K.
We’ll use this to hire a grant writer and kick-start the Spokes non-profit
These people will be getting huge leverage - $1 they invest might equate to $10 in funding.

Goals:
Everyone identifies at least 4 possible advisors and funders (Estimated time: 20 mins)
Everyone reaches out to 2 potential advisors & 2 potential funders
(Estimated time: 30 mins)

Instructions:
In this spreadsheet, add a list of names and contact info of people you know who are one of the following:

A) Education leaders — professors, makerspace leaders, Boy/Girl Scout leaders, past Spokes principals, school board members, Google Education managers, etc.
B) Non-profit leaders — other non-profit board members, volunteers at museums or community centers, MIT Public Service Center recommendations, pro-bono lawyers, etc.
C) Biking enthusiasts — Bike & Build alums, cross-country bikers, outdoors educators, managers at Cannondale or previous bike sponsors, etc.
D) Donors (esp. donors to education or biking groups)
E) Members of relevant non-profit foundations

Tips:
Think of who you want advising Spokes and what value they would bring
Use Facebook + LinkedIn to search through people you know
To get LinkedIn email addresses, export your connections
Some linkedin search terms to use - “Foundation”, “bicycling”, “bike”, “education”, “learning”, “non-profit”, “donor”, “advisor”, “community service”
Preferably 10+ years of experience and veterans in their field
Maybe try looking for MIT/Harvard/Stanford alums in particular (easier sell)
Diversity — age, race, perspective, etc.

If you can’t think of any for Step 1, instead come up with the names of a few well-connected people who can then put us in touch with potential advisors. List those individuals in the sheet as well

If you think of someone that another spokes member has already put into the spreadsheet, but you feel you have a strong connection to, leave a comment in the comments column. This will help us identify our strongest connection to each person.

Stop here! We’ll all meet together to choose who to contact first

After meeting: Use the below email templates to reach out. Keep note of your progress in the “Status” column.

When you receive a reply, use the below email template to respond. Drew, Turner, and Emma will “onboard” the advisor right away by scheduling a call with them to explain what Spokes is all about. You’re of course more than welcome to join!
