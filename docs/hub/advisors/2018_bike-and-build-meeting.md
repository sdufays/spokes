Topics to cover
Safety
Always emphasized as number one priority
A couple different ways that this happens
They do safety courses online. Read about safe cycling and are given two sets of quizzes that take a couple hours of their time - Can we use this?
All riders fundraise for bikes. They all have to finish the safety quiz before they get their bike
Require all riders ride 500 training miles to get comfortable on a road and with a road bike
At least one 65 mile ride
A ride in the rain to get used to different traction
Do at least one ride with a group
Have an orientation
Safety presentation to review everything - Can we get this??
Hand signals
Communication with cars and other riders
Practice with everyone in a parking lot
Do a “Shake down” ride for 30 miles to get used to working with the team, riders are broken up into small groups
Safety equipment
Safety triangle that must be worn at all times
Reflective ankle snap bracelets on left leg
Bontrager did research about how to be most safe on the road
Movement attracts someone’s eye, so the ankle bracelet works well.
Front and back bike lights for everyone
Helmet
Required to
Wear helmet
Lights on all the time
Wear their safety triangle
Another new thing that they do - They have two vans for every trip instead of one. Have one person who is a “safety navigator” who can navigate, handle calls/texts
They almost never allow solo riding. Every once in a while this would happen, but generally require groups of 2 to 6 at a time.
Times are around 30 and they tend to split up over the route each day.
2 to 4 is the sweet spot
Liability Waiver

Funding
$5000 per person required to raise
95% of revenue comes from this
$50K a year or so come from alumni donations
Part of the funds that are raised are granted out to affordable housing orgs a year
