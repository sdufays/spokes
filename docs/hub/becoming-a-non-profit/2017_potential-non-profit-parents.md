Hello _____,

I’m reaching out because I’m part of a group called Spokes that has been organizing cross-country biking-and-teaching trips every year since 2013. I participated myself in _____! It’s an educational initiative spearheaded by students and alums of MIT, Harvard, and Stanford. We’ve been growing every year as students at new universities decide to join us by creating their own trips based on our model.

A group of Spokes alumni, including myself, are working to create an organization that can provide support to all of these trips. Our vision would be to provide advice and guidance to each team, to maintain long-term relationships with the schools and organizations that would be learning with Spokes, and to assist with fundraising and logistics for the teams.

We are currently looking for a parent non-profit organization that would be willing to add our group as a “child” non-profit. We think that establishing a collaboration with _____ would be advantageous for both your organization and ours. We have a proven track record of success with Spokes and your organization would be able to take part in those successes and participate in our future work.

If you and your organization are interested in a non-profit “parent-child” collaboration with Spokes, my team and I would be excited to talk to you about it in more detail. And if you don’t think _____  is interested in this kind of proposal, any suggestions of other non-profits that might be a better fit would also be very helpful.

Many thanks,
_____
