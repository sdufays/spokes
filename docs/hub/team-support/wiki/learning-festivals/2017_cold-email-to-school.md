Subject: Harvard and MIT engineering students want to visit ___school___!

Dear ___name___,

We are a team of Harvard and MIT students cycling across the country while teaching engineering at schools and camps along the way, and we are stopping by ___city___ this ___month___! The project is called Spokes America, and we would love to host a 1-2 day Learning Festival at ___school____.

Working with MIT and Teach for America, we have designed three hands-on workshops in science and engineering. We are 7 undergraduate students that will be teaching introductions to mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, and computer science. Students will leave our workshops with everything from model rockets to robots that they built themselves.

Does this sound interesting to you? If so, please let us know and we’d love to work out the details with you. You can reach me at ___your email___ or call my cell at ___your number___. Look forward to hearing from you!

Best,
___your name___
___your school, major, etc.___
