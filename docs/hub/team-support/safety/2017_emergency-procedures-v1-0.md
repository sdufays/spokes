Emergencies Traveling on Road

Team Member Critical Injury
At least one team member should be with injured person; they should first check to see if injured teammate is in critical condition.
If in critical condition, call 911 and ask for ambulance.
Conduct first aid/CPR as necessary.
Contact team leader (or alternate) when possible; team leader will call SCHOOL faculty supervisor with situation update.
Team leader will send driver back to help teammate and transport to hospital if necessary.

Team Member Non-Critical Injury
At least one team member should be with injured person; they should first check to see if injured teammate is in critical condition. If stable, call team leader (or alternate).
Continue to provide first aid as necessary.
Team leader (or alternate) will:
Assess situation and determine if teammate needs to go to hospital
Send vehicle back to injured teammate with support as needed.
Team leader will call SCHOOL faculty supervisor with situation update

Vehicle Collision
Driver will first call emergency responders if needed.
Driver will then call team leader (or alternate) to advise of situation.
Team leader will direct team to join with driver at scene of accident.
Team leader will contact faculty supervisor with situation report.
Driver and team leader will discuss accident and fault with other party and relevant authorities.
The driver and team leader with take necessary course of action to pay for damages if at fault and will regardless of fault collect insurance information of other driver.
Team leader will contact the car rental company to inform them of the situation.
If vehicle is still operational and safe, driver will take it to nearest mechanic for a check if approved by rental company.
If vehicle is not safe to drive, team leader will call towing service and work with rental company to get an alternate vehicle.

Vehicle Malfunction
Driver will stop and assess situation, then contact team leader.
If driver is capable of repairing problem, they should discuss with team leader how they will fix it.
If another team member is capable of repairing car, they should bike directly to the vehicle location and conduct repairs.
If problem is severe enough that it cannot be easily fixed, team leader will call rental car company and alert them of situation.
Based on rental company’s advice, team leader will call tow service for car and arrange to rent an alternate vehicle.
Team leader will then contact MIT faculty member and advise of situation.

Bicycle Malfunction
Rider will stop and assess situation with teammate to determine if it is:
Safe to continue riding
a problem that can be fixed with repair tools on bike
a problem that can be fixed with tools in car
Severe and needs to be addressed by bike repair shop
Rider will contact driver:
with update if delayed
for transportation if necessary
If the malfunction is severe, plans will be made for rider to become driver or join driver in car as allowed by space in van.

Team Members Are Lost
If team members realize they are lost, they should call team leader and notify of situation.
Team leader will help them try to regain route by phone.
If in 15 minutes team members cannot find the correct route, they will stop where they are and wait for team leader to send vehicle to get them.
Team leader will communicate team members’ last known position to driver who will remain in contact (on hands-free device) with lost teammates while driving to them.
If team members cannot contact team leader, they should call alternate.
If team members cannot contact any teammate, they should contact MIT faculty supervisor and notify of situation.
If team members cannot contact teammates or faculty supervisor, they should contact MIT Dean on Call and notify of situation.
Team members should stop and wait where they are if they are lost and cannot reach any team members. The driver will be sent out at 30 minutes past the scheduled stop time and it will help the driver to find team members more quickly if they do not continue to go the wrong way when they realize they’re lost.

Sudden Weather Changes
If not severe (i.e. not a thunderstorm or hurricane/tornado) take appropriate action to protect yourself (i.e get out rain gear) and continue to next stop point.
At next stop point, team will all gather together and decide what to do next:
Wait
Stop for the day
Drive on in car if possible.
If lightning is seen, stop immediately and take cover wherever possible.
If a serious thunderstorm, wildfire or hurricane/tornado, also stop and take cover wherever possible.
Do not stray from set route.
Call driver and coordinate getting picked up.
Driver should call team leader with situation report and together they should select a gathering point to wait for weather to subside.
Driver should collect all team members and assemble team in chosen gathering point.
Team will decide what to do next:
Wait
Stop
Drive together to next rest point if safe.

Aggressive Animals
Team members should avoid making direct eye contact with any aggressive animals.
Team members should bike away and avoid confrontation if possible.
If team members are carrying pepper spray, they should use it. Alternatively, team member should slowly dismount from bicycle and hold it in front of them as a barrier.
If animals persist, team members should call vehicle for emergency pickup.

Emergencies in Campsite/Rest Stops

Confrontation with Stranger
Team members should avoid engaging in confrontation with stranger at all cost. They should walk away if stranger is persistent.
If on the road, team members should bike away or call for driver to pick them up.
In extreme cases team leader should call local authorities and alert MIT faculty supervisor.

Theft
If equipment is non-essential, team should write a list of replacement equipment necessary and driver for the day will be responsible for acquiring that material as funds allow.
If a bike is stolen, rider should sit in van and drive to nearest bike shop to purchase a replacement as funds allow.
Team leader should notify SCHOOL faculty supervisor.
