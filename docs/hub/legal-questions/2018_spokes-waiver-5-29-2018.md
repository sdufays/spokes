“Members of Past Spokes America Teams” shall be defined as including Caity Looby, Thi Bui, Margaret Shen, Chloe Yang, Francesca Childs, Drew Bent, Emma Benjaminson, Maddie Hickman, Jamin Liu, and Turner Bohlen.

I understand that bicycle touring is an inherently dangerous activity, which may result in serious bodily injury, including permanent disability, paralysis, and death. Knowing the risks involved in a bicycle touring event, I nevertheless fully assume all risks associated with my participation, and I hereby waive and give up all my rights to sue or make any claim or demand against any Members of Past Spokes America Teams for damages arising from my participation in a Spokes America event, whether caused by negligence or otherwise. I understand that Members of Past Spokes America Teams are not and cannot be construed as experts in bicycle touring. They cannot and have not condoned or approved the design or implementation of any bicycle touring event I have chosen to participate in during 2018. I have chosen to plan and participate in any bicycle touring trips in the year 2018 on my own and without the advice or encouragement of any Members of Past Spokes America Teams. I am solely responsible for evaluating the design, implementation, and risks associated with a bicycle touring trip, and my choice to participate in any such trip.

I have read this agreement, fully understand its terms, understand that I have given up substantial rights by agreeing to it and have agreed to it freely and without any inducement or assurance of any nature and intend it to be a complete and unconditional release of all liability to the greatest extent allowed by law and agree that if any portion of this agreement is held to be invalid the balance, notwithstanding, shall continue in full force and effect.
