Meeting on Vision

Emma’s thoughts
Emma’s brainstorming thoughts: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1unLM3cyheIiUwwCfF1NzhxXeF55X9yiqcXXFt80aKeI/edit
Are we going to focus on STEM or maybe STEAM (maybe both, maybe neither)
Relationship building is crucial
Mentors throughout the ages, from helping with independent projects to SAT prep to college applications
Build a connection through the ages, where a participant of a learning festival someday becomes a SPOKESperson (hahaha) themselves
Get people to buy into the program, feeding this energy into a huge fellowship of connected, interested people
Get to a point where we start writing whitepapers and influencing policy
Make sure that individuals and students involved have someone to talk to and learn skills on how to maintain such relationships
Turner: An important skill to learn for students: We should not just provide this relationship, but teach people how to build and maintain themselves
How to cross the aisle of the political divide
Students have opportunities to interact with people very different from themselves, allowing them to learn to empathize
Francesca: Perhaps past students who’ve done our workshops in the past could either co-teach/assist with workshops or lead their own -- this helps maintain relationships with communities and strengthen them. And kids can see someone from their own background teaching and excelling. This helps bridge the connection.
20-year vision: Be at a point where Spokes is self-sufficient/self-running, be able to influence policy. Foster a natural connection rather than a forced interaction, full of people who buy into this goal and vision for non-traditional education.

Drew’s thoughts
Spokes 2015 vision: http://spokesamerica15.tumblr.com/post/119730790054/the-spokes-vision
What ties Spokes together was not the STEM part, but the way learning occurs: through a non-traditional, self-directed method without textbooks
We need to flip how we think about education:
It's about the teaching method and the learning method
Students are encouraged to direct their own learning
College students are getting this experience with different communities
20-year vision: Cultures from community to community. Make it common to see that engineering occurs in many different ways (view engineering in different lights). Encourage self-directed learning. Provide not just resources, but also vision and ideas on what constitutes this pedagogy. We want to change the way people think about engineering education.

Turner’s thoughts
"Spokes is dedicated to building a world where everyone takes the initiative to learn. We work with underserved communities to build a pathway to success. Spokes projects are unique because of their dedication to this principle: Respect one another and allow people to learn what they love." (paraphrased)

Students are smart and an important participant of this experience
Ideas are hand-delivered by a community to a community
Individual students can be inspired to do something and learn on their own
College students are able to learn more about the world
Would like to see a balance for the future of Spokes
Have a system in place where anyone can pick up and start a project, shared through a bike trip
Make sure that the learning experience is democratized
Publicity/raising awareness/focus on community inspiration
We are able to attract such attention with our actions: parades, local newspapers, word of mouth
This is important because then people pay attention
We should work directly with everyone to give them skills to learn about things they want to.
This can be done through a close connection with the community to hand-deliver such a message
Flagship teams: Invite others to join the community and teach in their own local areas (ex. Bike 40 miles) to help maintain contact
An important publicity approach to help out the long distance trips that happens more rarely
Drive more local community growth, develop deeper connections and build relationships between different groups or communities that all are interested in Spokes
Accessible learning is a unique and important aspect of Spokes
Ex. Tanzania wants to purchase full lab equipments to have science and engineering labs (whatever that means). This is unnecessary.
We want to reiterate that all you need is a cool idea and a desire to try out new things. The reach can be much broader than simply high cost activities.
Should not be STEM focused as it singles out some ideas
Teach without topic
Focus on skills
People can be great engineers by taking inspiration from other fields as well, does not need to be STEM ONLY
20-year vision: Many locally grown community exchanges, where communities can go and share inspiration. Flagship projects to help with the self-directed learning process.
Uniqueness: Biking is probably not the main focus, but rather resilience (or maybe grit?). Ex. we can bike and reach the same number of people and use the teaching methodologies. Don’t downplay biking, but not the main focus.
Emma: Metaphor for community (ex. bikes, not cars = no walls, find a way to get out there and meet people).
