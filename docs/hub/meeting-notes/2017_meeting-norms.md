Things to Think About For Each Meeting
Feel free to critique and comment as you see fit. This is simply a starting point that Turner pulled from the Beagle Learning norms.
Preparation
Agenda written and shared at least 12 hours ahead of time
Agenda highlights the key purpose of the meeting
Method of communication (default: appear.in/turnerbohlen) shared 12 hours before meeting.
Ensure this meeting is the best way to solve the problem at hand.
Get the right people in the room. Ensure everyone is there for a reason.
All attendees read the agenda, identified ways they could be well-prepared (these might be explicitly communicated by the organizer, or may not be) and are prepared.
If you are invited to a meeting that you think does not meet one of these requirements, say so!
Meeting
Arrive a few minutes before the start time, even if you can only be in listen-only mode
Organizer specifies roles at the beginning of the meeting. Important ones:
Leader
Time keeper
Scribe
No withholding! Share discomforts, confusions, critiques relevant to the conversation.
No negativity about critique. We are open to learning from our mistakes always.
If you are confused, it is your responsibility to ask a question!
Leader should pay attention to the personalities in the room. Find ways of getting everyone involved (eg invite a shy person for input)
Define clear action items for post-meeting.
Every meeting ends with a meeting evaluation: each members has 20 seconds to share one thing to keep doing in future meetings & one thing to improve.
End every meeting on time.
