Games, Games, and More Games: Math and Strategy

In this workshop, students will play various games and puzzles to explore mathematical concepts such as probability and combinatorics. Students will probe at questions of probability and combinatorics through play with tilings and colorings. Also, physical games will be used as tools for exploration, conjecture, and as a means to facilitate engagement of different age levels in the classroom. Concepts will included pattern puzzles, building blocks, and the game SET as part of our workshop.

Overarching question:
When is something equal?

Introductions: Say name, ____ number in circle

Activity 1: Human Knot
        Is it possible with any number of people?
                -yes, but there are also impossible knots. Break them into groups of 3 and have them make a knot that is impossible to untangle:
        “Put three people at the three corners of the triangle, and grab each others' hands in the way shown. You will have constructed the trefoil knot, the simplest knot that is not equivalent to the unknot (a circle). This is part of knot theory, and you will find that it will be literally impossible to solve this human knot, short of letting go of each others' hands.”

Activity 3- the game SET. Have kids play the game and then do a worksheet that will help them improve their strategy. This worksheet will guide them through the concept of combinations and permutations (for the older students) and for the younger students it will be more of a counting worksheet. The end result will be 1) a better understanding of the game 2) for the older kids, a more sophisticated way to play  by looking for a certain type of set
