Brainstorming Document for Twitter
How will we access girls and women and how will we document that?

Accessing Girls/Women
Offer one workshop that is girls-only
Have a special girls-only page on website
Offer a special girls challenge every month along with the regular one
Do a short presentation on women in STEM (not a full workshop, but maybe a 20 minute historical overview with fun vidoes or something?)
Visit a girls school <-
Mention that our team is 4/7 women
offer mentorship program to girls - connect with women at MIT/Harvard/Columbia? partner with SWE?
Columbia Women in CS hg2287@barnard.edu
partner with an MIT program - there’s a ton of stuff that DUE is doing
partner with local universities in the area and invite some of the female faculty to come to the schools to talk about opportunities for women in STEM at those colleges
or find women working at an engineering firm in the area

Documentation
Website documentation of challenges and submissions
Do a survey that is girls-only asking questions to see if they’re more interested now than before
take pictures of our activities
do short interviews with girls after we finish each visit to see our impact on them
