Informal Education Brainstorming

Please add any ideas you have for “informal education” activities, spontaneous bicycle events, or any other fun Educational Stuff you’d like to see outside of the planned teaching in schools.

Bike-fixing parties in town squares
advertise beforehand for people to bring their bikes in for tune-ups or to learn how to do basic maintenance on their own
Bicycle technology demos
show off bike blenders, D-Lab technologies, or other cool bicycle-based machines, and get people excited about making things from bicycles.  Feed people smoothies!  (Will it Blend?)
We should get Jamba Juice to sponsor us!!!
also instructables.com
Science in the Park!
Have outdoor science demos and activities - kite building, wind/weather, etc. - and also raise awareness about Spokes and the hands-on STEM education mission
Conversations with Spokes-Folks (hehe)
Advertise in advance that we’re coming through a place, and set up in a park / center in case anyone wants to hear about our trip, ask questions about STEM/education, etc.
Collect data from the bike:
altimeter
GPS
forces on struts
average velocity
continuously plot all data in real time on website
use the data as a way to teach data analysis to kids in workshops
Impromptu physics estimation
how much force must that bridge withstand?
what is the force of wind on that sign post?
Design challenges
how could we make [insert current location here, i.e. park] better?
Setting up large paper & sharpies with questions: what’s something you would want to build?  What’s an adventure you want to go on?
get people to interact with us, hear about our projects, get excited about their own projects and adventures!
when people propose their ideas, are there any ways we can help them or point them to resources/ideas to pursue it?
excitement about the project, doing stuff - spontaneous, engaging, non-prescribed learning and conversations as a community
