Bicycle History and Engineering

Technical Objectives:

Share some of the history and evolution of bicycle engineering
(it’s super-cool! and informed a whole bunch of engineering that you wouldn’t expect, like ball bearings, pneumatic tires, thin-walled steel tubing, and the push for good paved roads!  And women’s suffrage!)

Teach / help kids discover how the systems of a bicycle work, by taking them apart and putting them back together again

Explore how bicycle power is and can be used in interesting new ways
(e.g. fun pedal-powered contraptions, international development / “appropriate technology” applications, etc.)

Brainstorm fun and/or useful bike-related projects and modifications!

Build a bicycle project!
(this could be a collaborative group project - like a bike blender and smoothie party - or help designing and prototyping other projects)

Overarching Objectives:

Get kids excited about bicycles, building, and being creative.  Share the impact bicycles and seemingly simple engineering have and can have on the world.

Teach in an engaging, hands-on way - give students an appreciation for learning through exploring and hacking things in their own lives.
(Learning/discovering is fun!  Figuring out things work is super-cool!)

Help build confidence with and excitement about tinkering, by providing easy and low-risk take-apart activities, building up to a larger design project.

Introduce the design process by getting students to work together to brainstorm projects and get excited about designing and prototyping their own ideas.

Try to inspire kids to plan projects, think creatively, explore, and tinker after we leave / throughout the year.
