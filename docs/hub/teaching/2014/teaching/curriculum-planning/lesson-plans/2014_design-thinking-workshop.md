Design Thinking Workshop

i
i. “How Might We Design Rediscover the Fun We Had In Kindergarten Now That We Are in Middle School?”
ii. “How Might We Create a Way To Help People Learn About the Participants in the Special Olympics?
...

Technical Objectives:

Introduce students to design thinking!

Teach / help kids discover how many variables designers have to consider when making something that people need and will use.

Incorporate some history about the leading design firms and design thinking experts (Apple)

Have students go through the design thinking process:
Start with people and finish with a prototype.

Overarching Objectives:

Get kids excited about making stuff for the world around them.

Teach in an engaging, hands-on way - give students an appreciation for learning through exploring and hacking things in their own lives.

Help build confidence and excitement by providing easy and low-risk take-apart activities, building up to a larger design project. Have students understand the importance of empathy and refraining from saying ‘no’.

Introduce the design process by getting students to work together to brainstorm projects and get excited about designing and prototyping their own ideas.

Try to inspire kids to plan projects, think creatively, explore, and tinker after we leave / throughout the year.
