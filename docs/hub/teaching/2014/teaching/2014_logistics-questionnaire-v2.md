Logistics Questionnaire V2

For sending out to schools in our planning stages.  Please let me know if there’s anything else you think should be added to this list, or anything that seems unnecessary.

Numbers:
Number of students
Age range of students
Number of teachers/parents

Time:
How much time will the school give to us to spend with students? (i.e. two-day learning festival vs. two-hour workshops?)

Space:
How much space will we have?
What does the space look like?  (Classrooms, auditorium, power/wifi, etc.?)
What resources will we have access to?
What do we need to bring?  (Include lists for specific classes; space requirements; etc.)

Curriculum:
Do the students have any particular interests?  Can we poll them beforehand (intro survey)?  I think this is a great question to ask, and once we get some answers we will need to sit down and figure out how we can make our workshops flexible enough to address those specific interests.
Of our listed classes, which might be the most exciting to teachers/students? If the teachers give us a rated list of our workshops back to us, what will we do with that information?
Do we want to run multiple classes, one long series, or several in parallel?
Potentially rephrase for teachers: do we have the space and the numbers to run several workshops in parallel, or should we select some workshops to run in series
Is there interest in informal exploration, bicycle workshops, etc.? Is this an appropriate question for the schools, or more something that we discuss on our own for informal settings?
What concepts have the students recently covered in science and math?
Is there interest in long-term connections to resources, other students, or to the project in general?
Let’s make this question more specific and ask:
Can we invite students to participate in our online challenges before we visit the school?
It may be too early to ask teachers about the possibility of building an afterschool club that does Spokes challenges, but we could have a conversation about it when we visit.

Safety:
Can we solder, etc. with the students?  Do we need them to sign a release form before participating in any of the proposed classes?
Let’s make time to discuss the classes in more detail on Sunday so we know what questions to ask related to this.
