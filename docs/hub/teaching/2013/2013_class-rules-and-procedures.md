Order of Events
Introduce Spokes, what we’re doing, the team. This is about investment.
Afterwards, talk about the rules. We want them invested before we talk about them.
Breakdown of the day.
Release to rooms/activities.
Have the teachers repeat the rules in their classrooms!
Give expectations for the next action every time you do something new.

Three Types of Activities
Mini-lecture
No notes
Sitting
Listening
Looking at the teacher/board
Working with the teacher. Answering questions

Discussion and workshops

Spokes Classroom Rules
We should call it something like “How to have Fun with Spokes” or “Exploration Tactics”

Introducing the Rules
Gonna be like a little comedy routine!
“So what is it going to look like to have fun with spokes today?”
“First of all, when a Spokesman is speaking at a workshop, everyone should be following along.”
	- Explain why
	- Always have fun with it! Keep in non-traditional. Just have clear expectations
	- model it right away. “Let’s get a quick example of what this looks like”
	- “Right now I’m teaching. Notice how everyone is paying attention”
	- Have Claire and another spokesman get into the group and show what it looks like to misbehave
“The second way to have fun with Spokes is to talk! We want to you to talk and have fun, but when you have something to say or are answering a question, raise your hand.”
	- Positive example. ??
	- Negative example. Turner asks a question, all the Spokesmen yell out different answers at the same time.
“Because we’re going to be working in groups today on cool projects, there are going to be times when we want you all to be speaking to each other and moving around the room. However, you should only be speaking about Spokes, and you should be moving around like normal human beings.”
	- Have spokesmen do cartwheels across stage.
Breath of Fresh Air
“Because this is a fun day for you to think about something new and different, you will not need to take notes, you will not have homework, you will ….”
Consequences
“Because we want you to have the opportunity to explore (or simple “have fun”) today, we’re gonna keep it really simple. The first time you’re disrupting the activity (or “breaking one of the rules”) we’ll give you a warning. The second time we’re going to ask you to go to room xxx.”
