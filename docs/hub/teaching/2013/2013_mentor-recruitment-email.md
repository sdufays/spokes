Spokes is a group of students from MIT and UC Berkeley who love learning. We each have found passions that we spend time pursuing simply because of the joy it bring us, and we think that everybody should have the opportunity to do the same.

This summer, we will cycle from San Francisco, California to Washington, DC to teach hands on classes and inspire students to learn what they love as they grow into the next generation of thinkers. We will stop in 10 cities for two days each, but want to have an impact that lasts long after we leave. To do this, we need your help.

Are you interested in helping high school students across the US with a project they're pumped about? Join us as a mentor, and change somebody’s life. It only takes one cool person to change the trajectory of a young person’s life for the better.

Once we have confirmed you as a mentor, and prior to June 9th, we will discuss the details of the mentoring process and get to know one another. Overall, we expect that you will speak with your mentee at least one hour per week for the six months following the Spokes bike trip.

If you have any questions that you would like answered before filling out the mentorship application, please feel free to email us at spokes@mit.edu. Otherwise, tell us a little about yourself here:
