Kansas City?
St. Louis fine.

Learning festivals can have classes offered in parallel if there are lots of students or to compress the day.

Learning Festival Schedule (assuming 1pm start time)

We intend to offer 3 classes in parallel so kids can choose what sounds most interesting to them, and even with a group of 100, we can maintain small class sizes (10-30 students is ideal).

We only need 3 classrooms at one time. Access to a computer lab (10-20 computers) with a projector and Internet connectivity would be great. One large space (auditorium/cafeteria) for the beginning and end would be ideal for snack time and the Pursuing Your Passion workshop. If the weather is particularly nice, we may even do the workshop outdoors.

After each class, we want to give students a chance to write down what they thought about the content and any ideas that the course stimulated. For each class, another team member will learn the material with great depth so that they can serve as a substitute teacher in case there’s an emergency.

If it turns out that we need to condense the schedule for whatever reason, we can offer all 6 classes simultaneously, shortening the festival from 4 hours to 2.5 hours. We can also each teach two rounds of a class if there are more than 100 students present.

During the breaks between classes and after the festival, we are interested in conducting short video interviews with any students who are comfortable (and with parental consent) being on camera. We would be asking questions generally on the subjects of: education, what fascinates them, and what their thoughts are on mentorship.
