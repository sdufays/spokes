Spokes is a group of students from MIT and UC Berkeley who love learning. We each have passions that we spend time pursuing simply because of the joy they bring us, and we think that everybody should have the opportunity to do the same.

This summer, we will bike from San Francisco, California to Washington, DC to teach hands on classes and inspire students to do what they love, as they grow into the next generation of thinkers. We will stop in 10 cities, but we want to have an impact that lasts long after we leave the cities we teach in. To do this though, we need your help.
Are you interested in helping high school students across the US with a project they're pumped about? Join us as a mentor, and change somebody’s life. It only takes one cool person to change the trajectory of a young person’s life for the better, and we think you could be that person.
Prior to June 9th, once we have confirmed you as an appropriate mentor, we will discuss the details of the mentoring process and get to know one another. The structure of your mentorship is largely up to you and what your schedule allows, however we expect that you will speak with your mentee at least one hour per week for the six months following the Spokes bike trip.
If you have any questions that you would like answered before filling out the mentorship application, please feel free to email us at spokes@mit.edu. Otherwise, head here and tell us a little about yourself… https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1wr8j-tCvf4RuJnw16GR3GEj1v6E-LgpGB2d2Tz_f4N8/viewform
