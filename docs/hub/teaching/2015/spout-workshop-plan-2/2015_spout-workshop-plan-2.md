Spout Workshop Plan
Introduce ourselves
Introduce the workshop
Electrical Engineering
Building a robot
Show the robot (video)
Ask students how they think the robot works
Ask students what parts are involved
Complete the parts list
Explain why we need each part
Introduce tools
Safety and usage
Explain how batteries work
Positive, negative
Current flows in one direction
Make the motors spin
Students will design a simple circuit
Check their work and show final result
Explain how LEDs work
Positive, negative leg
Connection to battery
Need resistors to avoid LEDs from burning out
Students will mount LED circuit
Check their work and show final result
Start building the robot
Explain on/off switches
Connect to battery holder
Wiring for the switches
Explain that Wires shouldn’t be touching
Let students mount the motor
Paper clips (explain why)
Mount the motors
Check their work
Motor wiring
Explain micro switches and terminals
Explain what we want from the wiring
Let students figure out the wiring
Check their work and explain final result
LED eyes
Explain where it should be connected
Check their work and show final result
Antennae and tail
Explain what we need antennae and tail to do
Check their work and show final result
