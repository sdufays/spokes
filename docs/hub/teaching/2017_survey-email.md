Hey wonderful Spokes team!

A number of Spokes alums are working to put together the “Spokes Hub” - a central non profit to help support spokes teams going forward and transform spokes from a once-per-year visit into a longer terms partner to the communities we visit. (If any of you want to help out with this just let me know! We’d love to have anyone and everyone!)

We thought it would be so awesome to collect all the workshops that have been taught during Spokes into one place. This would include syllabi, materials, and teacher instructions that we could open source online. We’d also want to share lessons learned with future Spokes team members.

With that in mind, I would so appreciate it if you could find 15 minutes to fill out this survey about your workshop!

Thanks a ton in advance for taking the time.
