INJURY CONTINGENCY PLANS

Current safety contingency plans:
https://sites.google.com/site/thespokeshub/safety/road-safety

What do we do if a member cannot continue the trip and must go home?
Should Spokes cover their hospital fees? Flight home?
Does the rest of the team continue the trip?
What do we do in the worst case scenario where a member either has to stay in the hospital for multiple weeks or passes away?
Does the rest of the team continue the trip?
If team members understandably would not like to continue, does Spokes pay for their transportation back?
What do we do if a member has to stay in the hospital for more than a few days?
What is the max acceptable # of days the team should wait for an injured member before possibly proceeding with the trip?
Is it ever acceptable to leave the injured member in the hospital? Do we wait until their emergency contact can be with them?
What do we do if a member has any kind of personal/family issues and must leave the trip early?
Does Spokes have to cover their transportation back home?
Is there a minimum number of members below which it is unsafe to continue the trip?
If a member cannot continue biking due to injuries but would like to continue the trip in the car, is that okay?
