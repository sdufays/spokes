Roles that Spokes 2017 had:
Fundraising
Route (Reached out to Warmshowers/Found Campgrounds)
Equipment (Gathered Equipment for us i.e. bikes, car rental)
Education (Reached out to Schools)
Webmaster (worked on designing the website and keeping it up to date)

We recommend roles in Spokes 218 divided as such:
Fundraising (2 people)
Organizes contact with companies to get money
Budget (1 person)
Organizes how money is spent
Route (2 people)
Contacts warmshowers hosts and organizing route
Equipment (1 person)
Purchasing bikes, supplies, etc.
Education (1 person)
Organizing learning festivals
Social Media (1 person)
Keeping up facebook, instagram, twitter, etc.

Therefore, there should be 5-6  mentors from Spokes Hub for each of these roles
