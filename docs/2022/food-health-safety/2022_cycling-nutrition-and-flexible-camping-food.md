Cycling Nutrition and Flexible Camping Food

Harvard Chan School of Public Health Healthy Living Guide
Provides useful general guidelines for balancing a meal
Gives tips on how to reduce inflammatory foods
Intended for general use

Nutrition + What I Eat in a Day 🥖 🥪 Pro Cyclist Training Camp
An example of what a professional cyclist eats in a day
Shows just how much food we need to take in
Not a great example of foods that are especially great for the gut, but the carb intake and time spent on the road limits his ability to eat well

Dr. Greger's Daily Dozen Checklist
A lists of foods to include by one of the most respected Nutritional Scientists and Doctors
This list includes foods like Fiber-rich seeds for Omega-3’s, and antioxidant-rich berries

https://www.theyummylife.com/Instant_Meals_On_The_Go	
A collection of recipes for healthy meals that can be cooked with just hot water
Fewer oils or extra dishes to clean!
These foods are flexible so that each person can tailor their meal towards their tastes
Having these options on hand helps for when a group meal goes awry or otherwise doesn’t suit an individual’s interest
