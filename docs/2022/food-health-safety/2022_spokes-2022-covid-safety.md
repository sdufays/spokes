Mask-wearing

Members will not wear masks while biking, or while eating/drinking with each other.
Members will wear masks during Learning Festivals and in indoor public spaces while not actively eating or drinking.

Testing

MIT provides kits.
Members will get tested biweekly. (4 weeks * 7 members → need 28 test kits)
FDA-authorized kit here ($18 for 2-pack → total cost for everyone for whole trip would be $252)

If someone tests positive

If asymptomatic, the positive member will drive the van with a mask on and social distance from other members for the recommended period of 5 days. The team will notify any schools/hosts who recently interacted with the positive member.
