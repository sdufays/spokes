About MIT Spokes
MIT Spokes will bike from Washington DC to San Francisco 
in Summer 2022.
 
We will host educational STEM workshops for middle school  
students along our route, with workshop topics ranging  
from physics to philosophy. We aim to inspire the next 
generation of scientists and engineers, especially in rural  
and underserved areas.
We are seven MIT students eager to share our enthusiasm  
for STEM. We look forward to representing MIT and  
embodying the “mens et manus” philosophy during our  
80-day, 3900-mile journey.
MIT Spokes has been biking cross-country for eight  
summers since 2013, excluding 2020 and 2021 due to the 
COVID pandemic.

Workshops
Electronics
Students learn about the basics of 
electronics then prototype their own 
circuits on breadboard, in a hands-on 
introduction to electronics.
Chemical Engineering
Dive into chemical engineering by 
building functional solar cells using 
berry juice and extracting the color 
pigments from leaves.
3D Printing
Learn about 3D printing, its various 
technologies, and its uses in the real 
world. Observe a 3D printer produce a 
part, layer by layer, in real time!
Art and Science
Explore the philosophy of paradoxes,  
dissect the math behind catchy music 
tracks and fine art, and design your 
own fun board game.

Testimonials
“We had a great time with the 8 MIT Spokes students at the Louisa  
County Public Library. The students did an amazing job teaching our young  
folks about STEM. We hope they will decide to return again next year!!!”
- Ophelia Payne
“Our experience with MIT Spokes has been fantastic. They were prepared with 
great hands on activities and an enthusiasm for the content. Working hard all 
day to offer 3 different sessions and allow participants to rotate through gave 
our students a good breadth of experiences. This is a fantastic program that we 
hope continues for many years.“
- James Porter
“We hosted the MIT Spokes group. After their arrival and a meal, we went to 
the local July 4 fireworks display. They presented and interacted with young 
people at the Newton Public Library. I heard positive reports from parents of 
attendees and also positive reports from Spokes about the attendees. We had a 
wonderful time with these energetic, bright young people.”
- Janet Voth
“The Challenger Learning Center of Kentucky in Hazard was once again 
thrilled to host the MIT Spokes Learning Festival on June 13 & 14 here at our 
Center. The Spokes team provided four excellent workshops during the two day 
festival and we had approximately 70 students attend from throughout our  
region. This made the fourth Spokes group that we have hosted here at our  
Center and I always think it provides such a great opportunity for our young 
people in rural eastern Kentucky to interact with students who attend the one 
of the most prestigious Universities in the WORLD!! It proves to our students 
that they are also smart and talented and have the potential of advancing their 
education and accomplishing great things.” 
- Tom Cravens
