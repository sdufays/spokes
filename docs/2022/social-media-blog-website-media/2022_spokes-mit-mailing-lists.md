# Spokes MIT Mailing Lists as of 2022

spokes-current@mit.edu: The masterlist that owns all our mailing lists. Current member is spokes-2022, but future years should add next year’s group + remove themselves, and so on.

Spokes-current owns all of the below lists:

spokes-alum@mit.edu: List containing past Spokes groups + individual alum emails
spokes-alumni@mit.edu: Mirror copy of spokes-alum
spokes-interest@mit.edu: A list for interested/prospective members of Spokes
spokes-updates@mit.edu: List for us to send email updates about our trip to subscribers!
spokes-present@mit.edu: a copy of spokes-current
mitspokes@mit.edu: alternate mailing list containing spokes-current

All lists are WebMoira lists accessible at https://groups.mit.edu/webmoira/list/*
