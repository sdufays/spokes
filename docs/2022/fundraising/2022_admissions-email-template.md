Subject: Cross Country Bike Trip Spreads STEM 

Dear MIT Admissions

Hello! We are MIT Spokes, a group of 7 MIT Students biking across the country this summer to spread STEM education by leading science and engineering workshops! 

You may have seen us featured on MIT Daily this Monday, or on the @mitpics Instagram account testing out one of our STEM workshops where we make a solar cell using juice from berries! 

I am emailing you for two reasons: 1. We are still fundraising to make this trip possible this summer and 2. We wanted to discuss potentially reaching out to admitted students to find places to stay across the country. 

To address funding first. We are around $5000 away from our goal of $35000 to make this trip possible. Fundraising was quite a bit harder than usual this year because of how much more expensive our bikes, materials, and rental cars are this year. We are spending almost 5x the amount on renting a van to drive across the country as previous years have. This is why we need your help, any small donation makes this trip possible. Myself and my teammates hope to be able to go on this trip without incurring the financial strain on ourselves. Truly any small contributions make a huge difference. Would MIT Admissions be able to offer any financial support to our team? 

Secondly, we are still looking for housing in several towns across the country this summer. Ed Moriarty recommended reaching out to Admissions to ask if we could be put in contact with incoming MIT students for housing. We have a list of towns we don’t have housing in yet, and our team thought it would be great if we could reach out to incoming MIT students to ask for housing. We could even show them some of our STEM workshops and tell them about our MIT experience! Let me know if this is something that would be feasible. 

Thank you very much for reading this, and please let me know if I can provide any additional information. 

Best, 
Simone Lassar
