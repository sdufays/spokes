Mission Statement (200 word version)

Our mission is to bring STEM education to rural and low-income communities across the United States while inspiring students to be active. While we bike across America from Washington, D.C. to San Francisco, we will be teaching (fill in number) workshops in (fill in number) self-organized learning festivals. These hands-on, interactive workshops are based on our experiences at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, revolving around our motto of “Mens et Manus.”
Spokes is a completely student-run program that is entering its 8th summer of operation after a 2-year pause due to COVID. The Spokes 2022 team is made up of experienced enthusiastic MIT students from Materials Science, Biology, Physics, Environmental Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Computer Science. Several of us have been MIT Teaching Assistants, Educational Studies Program teachers, and CovEd mentors for middle-schoolers. We have collectively helped teach classes in math, physics, electrical engineering, chemistry, computer science and philosophy. We are also long-distance runners, varsity rowers, mountain bikers, and backpackers.
We’re grateful for this opportunity to impact the lives of over 500 students across rural and low-income America this summer. We can’t wait to bike the entire (fill in number) miles, represent MIT’s hand and mind spirit, and inspire the next generation of STEM students!

Statement of Purpose (from 2018)
Spokes America brings STEM education to kids across the US. We offer exciting, hands-on STEM workshops to rural areas and underrepresented communities across the country to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers. As we bike from DC to San Francisco, we aspire to promote outdoor adventure, embrace spontaneity, and lead community-focused endeavors.

Please briefly explain what the requested funds will support in your student group:
MIT Spokes is a group of 7 MIT students who will be biking from Washington D.C to San Francisco this summer. Along our route we will stop at schools, libraries, and camps throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions in STEM. The goal of Spokes is to introduce science and engineering in exciting ways to students in rural and underserved areas who may not have had access to STEM opportunities. 
For one of the activities at our Learning Festivals this year, we will be leading a mechanical engineering themed workshop: how to build a mousetrap car and the physics behind it! For this activity we will teach students about the physics behind rolling wheels, friction, as well as spring energy. Students will then be able to build and compete with their own mousetrap cars. Our other workshops incorporate all different kinds of STEM topics such as leaf chromatography, the mathematics of music, and building a solar cell. 
	The funds will help us purchase bikes, gear (such as helmets, lights, a rental van, etc.), food along the way, and materials for our Learning Festival science activities. In particular, this money from Course 2 will likely go towards purchasing the 7 bikes we need, which will cost our group around $12,000. This is one of the largest expenses of the trip, so any level of support is greatly appreciated. 
The total budget for the trip is $30,000, and the generous $5000 donation from Course 2 in 2018 is what made this project possible for them. We would love to provide a more detailed budget if needed.
