Hello past Spokes participants! 

The Spokes 2022 cohort is here to introduce ourselves and ask for your help! This year we have a team of 7 members composed of 1 junior, 4 seniors, and 2 graduates. You can read more about us on our website here! 

We are reaching out because as you all know we have entered the exciting fundraising part of our trip. MIT departments, various companies, and grants have generously donated money to support us this summer, but we also heavily rely on crowdsourcing donations. You can find our donation link here, and really every and any contribution helps so much. 

Additionally, another way you can support our team is by helping to connect us with hosts across the country! If you/friend/relative/contact would be willing to host an enthusiastic and sweaty group of 7 bikers this summer, we are always looking for places to stay especially along towns in: Virginia, Kentucky, Illinois, Missouri, Kansas, Colorado, Utah, Nevada, and California. 

Very warmly from the Spokes 2022 crew :) 

Simone, Franklin, Keaten, Mariela, Maxwell, Robert, and Sophia
