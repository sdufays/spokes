Project Description 
MIT Spokes is an 8-year old completely student-run group. Biking across the United States, we seek to inspire students in rural communities through our STEM-based workshops. Our exploratory mens-et-manus-inspired workshops spring from our own experiences at MIT. Sample workshops at our learning festivals include making solar cells from berry juice, puzzling through the great paradoxes of philosophy, building circuits, designing board games, playing with the mathematics of music, and using artificial intelligence to make art! The MIT Spokes project also powerfully impacts the dedicated students who collectively put together the trip, workshops, and learning festivals. Through perseverance and leadership, the students become a cohesive team while forming long-lasting friendships with communities they meet. Because MIT Spokes often returns to the same rural and underrepresented communities, educators across the country are excited to have us, often promising large numbers of attendees. The MIT Spokes project ultimately hopes to connect to students across the US in an adventurous, educational, and exciting nature. 

Why we are applying for funding from the OME
We are applying for funding from the OME in order to purchase supplies for our STEM-based workshops at our learning festivals across the country. We have selected learning festival locations such that our workshops serve rural and underrepresented students across the United States. Funds from the OME would enable us to provide the highest quality educational experience for these students, giving them exposure and access to STEM education that they may not have otherwise had. Specifically, we would be able to use these funds to provide all students with something to take home with them from the workshop.
