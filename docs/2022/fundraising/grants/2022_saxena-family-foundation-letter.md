Dear Saxena Family Foundation,

My name is Sophia Fang and I am a current senior at MIT in Materials Science and Engineering. I’m part of Spokes, a student-run team of eight MIT students biking across America to bring STEM education to children in rural, low income, and underserved communities. This will be the eighth summer of our program after a 2-year pause due to COVID.

As a completely student-run group, our 80-day adventure of teaching and biking is made possible by generous supporters and sponsors. As a nonprofit organization, we are in the middle of raising funds for workshop materials, biking gear, and living expenses. We sense that our mission fits well with the Saxena Family Foundation’s goal of developing youth STEM education and are reaching out for your support!

At MIT, we have gained valuable experiences of hands-on learning, and we hope to bring a taste of this to kids across the US. This year, our 4 workshops will cover Physics, Chemical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Art and Technology. A sample of potential activities include making solar cells from berry juice, learning about 3D printing, designing board games,
building cameras and developing film, playing with the mathematics of music, designing mouse-trap car races, and using artificial intelligence to make art! By biking all 3,500 miles from coast to coast, we also hope to inspire youths and their communities to be more active, embrace the outdoors, and explore the world around them.

If you connect with our mission and want to help reach out to kids across America, please consider supporting us. In the meantime, please visit our website to learn more about our team and our mission: https://mitspokes.wixsite.com/2022.
 
Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing your thoughts!

Sophia Fang
