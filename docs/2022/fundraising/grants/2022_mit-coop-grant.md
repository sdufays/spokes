Please write a brief description of your group's work. Why and for whom does this group exist? (150 words max) 

MIT Spokes is an 8-year old completely student-run group. Biking across America, we seek to inspire students in rural communities through our STEM workshops. Our exploratory hands-on workshops spring from our own MIT experiences. Sample activities include making solar cells from berry juice, designing board games, playing with the mathematics of music, and using artificial intelligence to make art! MIT Spokes has also powerfully impacted the dedicated students who put together the whole trip. Through perseverance and leadership, the students become a cohesive team while forming long-lasting friendships with communities they meet. As Spokes often returns to the same communities, educators across the country are excited to have us, often promising large numbers of attendees. Furthermore, 4 of the MIT students are seniors who lost several in-person opportunities to give back to the community due to COVID, so this project of sharing STEM with kids is incredibly important. 

Please write a brief description of the project to be funded by the COOP (100 words max).

We are requesting funding for food and living expenses. In regards to living expenses, we are currently planning where to stay each night so that all the team members can properly rest and wash up. Although we plan to mainly stay at campsites or with generous hosts, we may need some emergency funding to stay at a cheap hotel four or five times during our 80-day trip. Since the COOP granted $1,300 to MIT Spokes in 2018, we are hoping for similar support this year.
