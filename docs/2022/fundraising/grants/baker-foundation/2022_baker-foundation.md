Brief Description of Project

The Spokes team will be biking from Washington D.C to San Francisco to engage with students in rural communities this summer. We will stop at schools, libraries, and camps as we travel throughout the country to hold free Learning Festivals where we will teach hands-on, project-based workshops inspired by our passions in STEAM. Students range from elementary to middle-school students, and workshops have included introductory electronics, software, and chemistry, among other topics. We hope to inspire students to pursue STEAM and higher education, as well as encourage them to stay athletic and active from coast to coast.

Why do you (or your organization) want to do this project (i.e. your motives)?
Spokes is a group of eight MIT students eager to share our enthusiasm for science, technology, engineering, arts and math (STEAM). Over 3000 miles and 70 days, we will stop at various schools across the US and lead Learning Festivals, which are hands-on, interactive workshops to get kids excited about STEAM. We try to design our route to favor hosting Learning Festivals in underserved areas, where STEAM may not be as widespread. With the help of Teach For America, we have identified and formed relations with some of these communities in the past, a lot of them being more rural parts of the country. Rural students are just as capable as urban students, but the percentage that moves on to higher education is lower. Many current and past members of our team are from small towns and we know how influential just one inspiring interaction can be! Networking dinners we will hold after the learning festivals will allow us to continue to connect personally, and answer questions students have about preparing for college and pursuing their passions. We will also have an impact on the communities as a whole, as we collaborate with schools and libraries in our ten learning festival locations. Our mission is to show the students that no matter how far-off college and places like MIT may seem, it’s possible for them! We hope to inspire the next generation of students to pursue STEAM, as well as encourage them to stay athletic and active from coast to coast.

History of the project- Has this project been done before? If so, tell us about its past success/ failure and if/how you are changing the project
Spokes is a ten year old program and the coast to coast journey has been done almost every summer.  Because of its long history, every year Spokes gets to learn from the mistakes made from previous years. One of the failures that a previous year’s Spokes class dealt with was having too many workshops. In 2014, each member hosted their own workshop, but found that these workshops weren’t as impactful. Learning from this experience, the 2015 cohort hosted a much smaller number of longer workshops and found that they were able to get to know the students better and teach them more significant things. The workshops ended up being less lecturing and students had more hands-on time. They found that the students loved being able to take home something tangible to share with their family. There are many other lessons learned from previous years and we hope to learn from those and make Spokes 2022.
Although Spokes has been around for some time, more recently Spokes has been on a hiatus and there haven’t been trips in 2020 and 2021 due to the pandemic. We hope to revive Spokes this year with a new focus on art. In previous years Spokes has focused on hosting STEM focused Learning Festivals. With the recommendation from previous years, we hope to incorporate art into the curriculum with the hopes that the workshops will be more engaging. The curriculum is being redesigned and the Learning Festivals this year will look very different from the years before. 

How your project will relate to the philosophy of the Baker Foundation
The Spokes program intends to inspire the next generation of scientists and engineers who may otherwise have never considered exploring these fields. We primarily target underserved, rural areas for our Learning Festivals because we believe that all students across the nation should have access to high quality education. By doing this trip on bike, we hope to encourage students to not only pursue their academic interests, but also to stay active and athletic. These following aspects of our project resonate with the Baker Foundation’s philosophy on equitable education opportunities and dynamic extra-curricular programs.

How will your project impact the MIT community
By inspiring students who either may have not considered STEAM or heard of MIT, we are shaping the future faces of the MIT community and its colleagues. We hope that our project can shape the demographics of the MIT community to more accurately represent the demographics of the nation.
