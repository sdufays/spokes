* Project title MIT Spokes 2022 
* Grant purpose The purpose of the grant is to fund learning festivals, operations, and gear for the 2022
MIT Spokes team. Specifically, our team will need resources to teach the
aforementioned workshops to 50-100 rural and/or low-income elementary/middle
school at each of the learning festivals. Our workshops are geared towards exploration
and hands-on learning, infused with elements of theory and lab practice at MIT, outside
of the traditional school curriculum. A few possible activities in the workshops include
making solar cells from berry juice, learning about 3d printing, designing board games,
building cameras and developing film, playing with the mathematics of music, and using
artificial intelligence to make art! With such a rich spectrum of hands-on classes, we
require significant funding for materials and transportation in our van. Any additional
funding leftover will go to funding bikes, gear, repair, and living expenses. The MIT
Spokes team has historically requested and received funding from TI for previous
iterations of the yearly trip, dating back to year one of the program. We are excited to
restart the program after a two-year pause, and would greatly appreciate the support
from TI in our mission to educate and inspire students across America.
