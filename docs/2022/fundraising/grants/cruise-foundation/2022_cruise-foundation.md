A statement summarizing the purpose and activities of the organization

MIT Spokes will host educational STEM workshops for middle school students while bicycling across the US in Summer 2022. In its seventh year running, Spokes is a group of eight MIT students eager to share our enthusiasm for science, technology, engineering, and math (STEM). Over 3000 miles and 70 days, we will stop at various schools across the US and lead hands-on, interactive workshops to get kids excited about STEM -- in particular, electronics, computer science, chemistry, and physics. We hope to inspire the next generation of students to pursue STEM, as well as encourage them to stay athletic and active from coast to coast.
