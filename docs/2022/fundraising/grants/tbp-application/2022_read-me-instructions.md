Example Personal Statements
“I love pretty much all things outdoors, and have gotten really into biking since college; I've been contemplating a cross-country bike trip for a while now. When I heard about Spokes, I got really excited: I could complete the cross-country trip and extend STEM to students in rural communities? Coming from a low-income background myself, I'm acutely aware of the stark disparities in exposure and resources between schools that regularly produce students that go to MIT, and those that, well, don't; it's really important to me to try and erase those disparities where I can, so Spokes was kind of a perfect fit.”
“Spokes combines STEM education with adventure. In rural areas all over the U.S., STEM curriculums are not as extensive or well-structured as those in larger cities or suburbs. Spokes is all about the unpredictable, but more importantly, it's about putting a face to an MIT engineer. It's about humanizing STEM subjects. It's about inspiring younger generations.”
“Teaching kids about what I enjoy--science, art, and mathematics--is honestly a dream come true. Being a young woman in STEM, I understand the value of a role model who looks and thinks like you; I hope to become just that role model for young women (and men) across America.”
