Dear TBP Selection Committee,
 
I am Lee Perlman, Founder and Co-Director of The Educational Justice Institute at MIT (TEJI), and lecturer in the Experimental Study Group (ESG) since 1984.  TEJI provides college education in prison environments, focusing on ‘inside/out’ classes in which MIT students take class alongside incarcerated students.

I am familiar with the Spokes 2022 team primarily through one of their team members, Simone Lassar. Simone has been my student and a TA for my inside/out classes, Authenticity and Philosophy of Love. Simone is a terrific TA, who is engaged intellectually in the classes and shows leadership, organization and planning skills.  She is extremely reliable and proactive. Additionally, three other members of the Spokes team this year have been members of ESG (Keaten, Mariela, and Sophia).  ESG is an undergraduate learning community in which students take responsibility for their education and the life of the community.  The community attracts and selects for students who are self-starters and collaborative learners.

The Spokes mission of bringing STEM education to parts of the country and populations that are underserved is one I strongly support, as much of my work revolves around supporting the education of incarcerated people; a group which has historically lacked access to quality STEM education.  Not only will Spokes bring STEM education to rural and low-income communities across the United States, but this year they will be working with TEJI to bring their workshops to incarcerated youthful offenders in states along their route.

From the work I’ve seen from these students, I am confident they will be able to lead high quality and engaging workshops on topics ranging from Physics, Chemistry, Mechanical Engineering, and Art/Technology.  Meeting with them to discuss bringing their work to incarcerated populations, it’s become evident to me that they are very well organized and industrious in budgeting, fundraising, and planning for their summer trip. They have raised nearly half of the funds required to go, and ESG is supporting their hard work by donating a significant amount to the effort. 

As a lover of biking myself I also support the impressive task of biking across the country. It is always exciting to see MIT students embarking on adventures outside the world of science and engineering, building community among themselves and in the world. I think that Spokes is a wonderful program to push the personal growth of these individuals, a bonding experience that they will remember for the rest of their lives, and a beautiful way for them to represent MIT in the world. 

The Spokes 2022 team has my complete support. I hope that you will fund this very worthwhile endeavor, and I’d be happy to answer any questions about the collaboration between Spokes and TEJI.  

Lee David Perlman, Ph.D.
Founder and Co-Director, The Educational Justice Institute at MIT
Lecturer, Experimental Study Group MIT
