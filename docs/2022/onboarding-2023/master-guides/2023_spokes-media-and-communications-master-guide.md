# Media and Communications Master Guide (updated 2026)
## Website
- Now WordPress (blog with comments and images); Wix used by past teams and hosts old team sites. Spokes owns domain mitspokes.com (points at previous team's site; migrate to your year; domain transfer was fiddly, ask customer support; preserve the entire past site). WordPress login email: spokes-current@mit.edu; Wix login account spokes-2022@mit.edu. Passwords: [redacted] (present in source doc; ask Edgerton/past team).
- Prior site: https://mitspokes.com
## Instagram (@mitspokes; password [redacted])
- Post semi-regularly before and during trip. Intros at start of spring semester, then training rides/meetings/planning. 2022 was featured by mitstudents and mitpics reposted posts. Stanford Spokes posted daily in 2025 (recommended).
- 2026 TL;DR: (1) individual member intros; (2) collaboration posts with the mitedgerton Instagram for fundraising and marketing; (3) fundraising progress and thank-you posts, and everyone reposts on their own stories; (4) weekly recap/photo dump during summer; (5) one post per learning festival (take lots of photos/video); (6) one post for every restaurant that sponsors a meal.
## Team T-shirt and jerseys
- JD designed a team T-shirt. Polyester T-shirts from 4imprint (good for LFs and PJs). Bike jerseys from Jakroo (not an MIT-approved vendor: needed a one-time license agreement); strongly recommended. 2026: first name on back of LF shirts; two jerseys (black and white for cold/hot days); laundry loops.
## Media assets (all in Drive "Spokes 2022 / Social Media, Blog, Website, Media")
- Logo: official logo (2024 changed colors).
- Pamphlet: Maxwell Yun designed a 12-page InDesign pamphlet; he believes it won the Lightforce sponsorship. Printing: MIT CopyTech about $5 per copy, saddle-stitched on 11x17. 2022 pamphlet in "Pamphlets"; design files in "pamphlet_resources_2022". Fonts Proxima Nova and Overpass.
- Thank-you card: handwritten card for every host (except one unpleasant one) plus designed card (no year, reusable); prints double-sided on 8.5x11 folded; use cardstock and ask MIT CopyTech to machine-fold (Kinko's cannot). Post-trip mailed cards printed on nice paper at CopyTech ($30 for ~40). 2026 gave each host a card with photo and handwritten note.
- Posters for the Infinite (MIT hallway): 8.5x11 and 11x17 Photoshop files, plus event-poster variant (bike repair/safety workshop). Postering got eyeballs for recruitment/donations; apply for an Infinite spot.
- Instagram ad template: Photoshop file (Proxima Nova).
