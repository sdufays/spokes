# The Spokes Master Guide (updated 2026)
Update the master guides after your trip: add your year's knowledge, delete outdated info.

## How to start planning
1. Get to know each other. 2. Figure out what needs doing: Money (grant applications early; crowdfunding must be through MIT, not GoFundMe; talk to Marvelin Higginbottom marvelin@mit.edu, Sandra J Lipnoski slipnosk@mit.edu, Chris Mayer mayerc@mit.edu); Bikes/Gear (strongly recommend matching gravel tubeless bikes; bike fits with Brendan Connor via CycleLifePT, who comes on campus for fits + PT); Route (stops, Warmshowers); Curriculum (school/library contacts, set LF dates, design workshops, get materials). 3. Assign who does what (fundraising start EARLY). 4. Regular meetings with note taker and moderator; track action items. 5. Learn the Drive. 6. Ask past members: spokes-2026@mit.edu. 7. Create a timeline (IMPORTANT). 8. Use Edgerton contacts. 9. Templates exist; no need to reinvent.

## Timeline (roughly what 2026 did / should have done)
- November: first meeting; read master guides; meet before Thanksgiving, assign roles; start fundraising ASAP (top to-do every month); introduce yourselves to Edgerton.
- December: rough start/end dates; set up crowdfunding page and website; reach out to departments/labs/centers (often leftover funding in Dec); meet Edgerton (can be IAP); start training.
- January: meet during IAP; start planning route; shared GCal; finalize trip dates (decide 1-way vs 2-way car rental); very rough budget for fundraising; apply for 2 pro cards and 2 travel cards; start contacting LF locations.
- February: weekly meetings; finish website updates; start LF ideas; once dates final start car rental process and driver background checks; bike purchasing near end of month; go to bike lab.
- March: apply to J-WEL Education Innovation Grant (Spokes 2024 got most money here); post blog introducing members; participate in MIT 24 Challenge crowdfunding; order biking gear; second-round budget.
- April: finalize gear list and order; Midnight Marathon (fundraise with dare bingo card); LF materials list; finalize LFs EARLY; design/buy Spokes shirts for LFs and jerseys; J-WEL decision end of April; Edgerton Center Showcase; start securing housing for first states.
- May: finalize all housing; finalize budget; tetanus shot; assign trip roles; train; check in with Edgerton (photo release forms); optional local LF.
- June (first week of trip): list all drivers on rental in person at the agency; organize car, buy plastic storage boxes; buy shared toiletries/sunscreen; set up bike computers; crash courses on bike repair and car maintenance.
- During trip: regular check-in meetings on logistics and team functioning.

## Food and nutrition
Carbs in the morning and while biking; protein at dinner for recovery; electrolytes while biking (Nunn, Liquid IV, Propel, Gatorade; a tub of Gatorade powder is cheapest).
- Breakfast: hosts often provide. Camping: bagels + cream cheese/PB/Nutella, yogurt + granola, overnight oats in ziplock bags, apples + PB, protein pancakes (too slow), "Spokes 24 classic" powdered milk + instant coffee + cereal + powdered PB, cottage cheese.
- Lunch: 2022: sardines + tortillas, rotisserie chicken + Hawaiian bread, deli sandwiches, hummus + veggies, apples + PB; paper towels as plates. 2024: bagel sandwiches ~74 days in a row (deli meat, hummus is key, lettuce/tomato, cream cheese, cheese, pickles, chips), later cheap Walmart French bread. 2026: found the snacks most of the team liked and repeated them (sandwiches, sometimes liquid diet, fruit and snacks).
- Snacks: dried dates (Costco; "carried the 2022 team"), dried mango/figs/apricots, Clif and Lara bars (avoid melting chocolate), Nature's Bakery brownie bar (2024 best), nuts/trail mix.
- Dinner: 2026 never cooked; called places each night for free meals if host could not feed them. Two-pot ideas: jambalaya, feijoada, chicken salad (hotel stays), tacos, chicken and rice, spring rolls (no stove), one-pot pasta.
- Protein: canned sardines (Costco), textured vegetable protein, shelf-stable tofu, canned chicken, unflavored protein powder, fresh meat (needs fridge), rotisserie chicken, deli meat, jerky (Costco), soymilk, peanut butter.
- Tricks: Minute Rice; Costco and Walmart (miss them in remote areas); baby wipes for hands and pre-wiping dishes; paper towel plates; pocket stove (2022 boiled up to 9 eggs); driver buying cold soft drinks/iced tea on hot days (ice is a luxury).

## Packing guide
Pack light (little car room). Laundry nearly daily so 2-3 biking outfits and 2 non-biking outfits (incl. Spokes T-shirt). Bring cold weather clothes (50s F in the Rockies); synthetic/wool dries fast. A small carry-on suitcase stacks better than backpacking bags.
Sample (Franklin, 2022): carry-on suitcase with 2x biking outfits (socks, jersey, padded shorts, sun sleeves/legs), 2x non-biking outfits, thermal leggings, long pants, wool socks, down puffy; small backpack (toiletries, laptop, frisbee, Crocs + shoes); handlebar/fanny bag and saddle bag (snacks, phone, power bank, water bottles, pump + tools, first aid kit, air horn and mace for dogs - chased by dogs a lot in Kentucky).
2024 recs: Pearl Izumi fingerless gloves, merino wool biking socks, clip-on shades for glasses wearers, biking jersey WITH POCKETS, padded shorts (Spokes 23 disliked padding but all found it important), chamois butter (communal).
