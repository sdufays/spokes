Arboretum or Fells: 6.5mi one-way
Revere Beach: 9mi one-way
Kelly’s Roast Beef has good… roast beef
Blue Hills: 12mi one-way. No public transportation
Salem: 18mi one-way. Has commuter rail. Rockport is 3mi away.
Nahant: 20mi one-way to a nice beach / peninsula. Mostly on bike paths but need to cross the Alford St bridge into Everett. Maxwell has a GPX file from the Stud.
Walden Pond: 21mi one-way. No public transportation

Providence, RI: 50mi one-way. Has commuter rail

Dover Loop – 44 miles Round Trip
The Dover Loop is a favorite of many amateur and pro racers in Boston. The rolling hills make for an excellent workout!
https://www.strava.com/routes/22998070
https://www.urbanadventours.com/boston-road-ride-maps-and-routes/
Great Brook Farm – 60 Miles (Round Trip)
Get out of the city and explore the rolling hills and farms of Concord and Carlisle. We also strongly recommend making a stop for ice cream at Great Brook Farm!
https://www.strava.com/routes/22989160
https://www.urbanadventours.com/boston-road-ride-maps-and-routes/
Wachusett Mountain – 119 Miles
Starting at sea level, this century-ride follows winding country roads west to Wachusett Mountain. You can see Boston from the 2000′ summit!
https://www.strava.com/routes/23002344
