MIT Spokes DYS Workshop Descriptions 
1. 3d-Printing: In this workshop, students learn about real-world applications of 3d-printing, as well as the various 3d-printing technologies that make them possible. Students will have hands-on exposure to cutting-edge, modern 3d-printed parts, as well as watch a 3d-printer print a part in real time.

Final product: Students can expect to have their very own 3d-printed part by the end of this workshop. 

Featured here are some of the 3d printed parts we will be bringing in! From left to right we have a 3d printed wrench (made from metal!), a 3d printed metal bracelet, and a printed bike seat!

3. Solar Cell: Have you ever wanted to make your own electricity? Using the power of plants? Well now you can! In this workshop, students will build their own solar cells (aka a homemade solar panel), using raspberry and blackberry juice! We will learn about how the berry juice is actually able to interact with light to create electricity (spoiler alert: it is very cool). Featured here are pictures of Sophia and Simone testing out our homemade berry solar cells. 

Final product: Students will actually be able to keep their very own homemade solar cells!
