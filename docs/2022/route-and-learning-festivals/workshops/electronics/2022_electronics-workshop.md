Electronics Workshop
Summary
Students will learn the basics of electricity, working with breadboards, LEDs, potentiometers, batteries, and multimeters. 
Schedule
0:00 (10 minutes) Introduction to Electricity. Electricity is everywhere in our lives but how does it actually work? Mechanical systems like bikes are easier for people to understand because we can see how it works. But electrical systems are more difficult because the components usually hidden inside the metal cases of your phone and electricity itself is not something we see everyday with our eyes
0:30 (30 minutes) Understanding Electricity Using an Analogy: Either using water or marching men. 
Marching Men: 
Each man is an electron
Current is the number of marching men on the trail
Resistance is mountains
Voltage is in the form of food (pizza) that the men consume in order for them to march
A battery is the campsite where the marching men come back to refuel their pizza
Walking through the mountains (more resistance) and thus requires them to consume more pizza
The marching men are lazy and go down the path of least resistance. (parallel circuits)
The marching men are lazy and many decide not to go on the journey if it is too difficult. (higher resistance less current). Providing more food can convince more of them to go (high voltage difference, more current)
Too much current creates heat. (analogy: too many people creates to much body heat)
Closed loop and open loop
Ask some check yourself questions
What happens when you add two batteries in series? In parallel?
What happens when you add two resistors in series?
Relate analogy to circuits, Resistors, Batteries, LEDs, wires. Buttons (like gate that opens or closes the road)
Symbol
What it looks like irl
Polarity
0:40 (10 minutes) Ohms Law: The marching men are pretty temperamental and we want to understand how they work. Ohm gave a simple equation to describe the behavior of electricity (the marching men).
V = IR
V = pizzas
I = number of marching men
R = resistance, or difficulty of the journey.
0:50 (10 minutes) How does a breadboard work?
Learn how to make closed circuits using a breadboard worksheet
1:10 (20 minutes) Assembling your first circuit 
Simple Battery, LED, Resistor Circuit
1:30 (10 minutes) Adding a Potentiometers
(10 minutes) measuring voltages and resistances.
