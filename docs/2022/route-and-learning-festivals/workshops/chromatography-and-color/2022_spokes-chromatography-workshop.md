## Slide 43
What can we ask about waves?
Frequency: How frequently is this wave passing by me? Ex. 1 wave/sec 

Wavelength: What is the distance between waves? 
Ex. 2 meters
If we know frequency and wavelength, we can find the speed of the wave: Speed = Frequency * Wavelength 
## Slide 44
Frequency and Wavelength determine color 
Speed of Light: 186,282 miles per second
Frequency of Visible Light: ~ 6.5×10¹⁴ cycles per second (Hertz, Hz)

Wavelength of light? 
186,282 mps /  6.5×10¹⁴ Hz = 0.00000000028658 miles 
or ~450 nanometers
