CES education contract with DYS (Department of Youth Services)

Collaborative Educational Services (CES) operates 34 (?) programs at residential treatment centers across the US
Regional leadership might come over. Lisa Strois and Wendy Taylor will be there that morning

Training notes
DYS maintains physical custody of youth. CES runs educational programs for the youth.
DYS language
Adjudication, not incarceration
Direct Care Staff, not guards
Youth committed to DYS until age 18 or 21
‘Continuum of care’
Continuum of care
Detention, Assessment, Treatment, Community, Revocation
We might be working with kids who are not committed yet but are in the process of assessment before treatment
The setting we are going to is hardware secure (?)
Stakeholders: student, family, case management team, regional/program stakeholders, education stakeholders
Education program features
Sending school district awards credits
Provided transcript/report card back to school
Youth are typically catching up and earn high school diploma while they are in a treatment program or finishing after they go back
Some youth already earned high school diploma so they can take college (e.g. Bunker Hill Community College online class) or vocational classes
Preparing for high school equivalency exam (GED)
Any student can take a class to explore
Positive Youth Development
Meeting youth where they are at
Prepares young people to meet challenges of adolescence - support socially, morally, emotionally, physically
Giving youth a voice, nurturing caring and respectful relationships
Culturally Responsive Practices
Empower students to a point where they can examine critically educational content and process and ask about its role for creating democratic and multicultural society
Tips for Success
Appreciate growth with praise
Skill, not will is usually the problem
Experience and needs of youth
Fear, worry, confusion, sadness, guilt, isolation, embarrassment, stigma, anger
Some have experienced trauma -> may be displayed by easily giving up if something isn’t going well
Staff will be in the room to support
Staff Expectations
A lot of staff will be in the classroom
We are not responsible for behavior management
Entering building
Bring photo id, sign in at every visit, entrance through security
Making sure supplies are collected at the end
Youth will be patted down to make sure they don’t have anything they’re not supposed to have
Meeting
Meeting at 7:30
Programming starting at 8:10
Lunch in cafeteria
Don’t bring
Food, beverages, belongings, gifts, cell phones, computers, outside correspondence, anything with staples, paperclips or wire binding, contraband
Don’t bring anything not needed for the workshop
Attire (professional casual)
No hats, torn clothing, revealing attire, no tank tops, gang attire
No shirts with messaging of any kind.
Plain shirts/t-shirts preferred
Keep tattoos covered
Don’t bring jewelry that could hurt you
Stay masked!
Never be alone with youth, don’t touch youth outside handshake
Photos cannot identify the program or include the youth
Protect the youth’s DYS involvement, keep that secret or any information youth might share with you
Youth should never be talking about their charges
You can share info about yourself but not personal info like address, last name, cell phone number
Great to learn about what the youth is interested in and sharing about your interests
Students are currently in quarantine and will have to see if cases are bad on Friday
Fingers crossed that the event should happen
Quarantine should be lifted by now

Check for understanding every now and then
Wide range of students’ backgrounds
Can ask students about fun facts about themselves
