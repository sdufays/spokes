# Spokes 2022 Receipts Ledger (consolidated)

This single doc replaces the ~230 receipt/photo files in Finance_Budget (Receipt Photos, Receipts for OME, Purchasing/Receipt Form Responses, dys invoice). Amounts come from the Receipt Form (Responses) sheet (the team's running log, 259 entries) cross-checked with file names/receipt text. Card numbers removed.

## How purchasing and reimbursement worked in 2022
- Payment methods used: MIT Purchase Order (bikes, gear: placed via Amazon punchout/e-procurement or vendor PO, e.g. Landry's Bicycles Boston, invoice paid on account); Keaten's purchasing card (ProCard, used by the finance lead for bike repairs, ice, printing); Maxwell's MIT travel card (road expenses: groceries, gas, lodging, restaurants, rental car); and personal cards (riders bought gear individually, then submitted receipts to be reimbursed).
- Every purchase was logged in the Google Form (date for backdating, amount, description, category, person, payment method, filing notes) and the receipt photo was filed in a per-person folder named like `[$amount] [initials] description` (prefix `[TBP]` / `[Baker]` / `[OME]` marks which grant or fund the receipt is billed to; `[PAID]`, `(Done)`, `(submitted)`, `CANCELLED`, `[DISPUTED]` mark filing status). Unfiled receipts were sorted by card (ProCard / Travel Card) and moved into `Filed` when submitted.
- Lessons: many early entries say "DO NOT KNOW" for person (log purchases with the buyer's name right away); the rental car was logged as an ESTIMATE of $8,634.06 (to be subsidized by Edgerton Center per Sandi); a flat tire on the all-wheel-drive rental forced a new set of 4 tires ($1,248.91), which the team planned to dispute with Budget; Maxwell's team shirts order from CustomInk ($333.38, ProCard) was cancelled and redone at Rush Order Tees ($320.34, personal card).
- Forms on hand: MA Form ST-5C Contractor's Sales Tax Exempt Purchase Certificate (MIT exempt number E 042-103-594, signed by MIT Director of Financial Operations) and MIT's W-9 (MIT is a tax-exempt entity under IRS 501(c)(3), EIN 04-2103594; MIT address 77 Massachusetts Avenue, Cambridge MA 02139). Use these to avoid sales tax with vendors (Landry's invoice shows $0 tax).
