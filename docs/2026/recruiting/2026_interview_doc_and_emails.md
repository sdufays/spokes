## Interview script (30 min)
- Intros (3 min): name, pronouns, year, hometown, course; interviewers share favorite Spokes memory; interviewee icebreaker.
- Motivation (3): what do you hope to gain?
- Planning & Preparation (4): how will you contribute / roles excited about or to avoid (fundraising, route/housing, LFs, bikes/gear, training, website/media, finances). "The team must fundraise around $40K. What ideas do you have?"
- Trip/Team (10): a time you worked on a team and hit a challenge; interpersonal role you expect to play and expectations of others; challenges on and off the bike and how to solve.
- Teaching (5): teach a 1-minute middle-school lesson, 3 minutes to prepare.
- Logistics (2): year-long commitment; few hrs/week planning; trip ~75 days May 31 - Aug 14; fall recruitment lighter.
- Closing (3): anything else / questions.

## Email templates (from Tatiana Vassiliev & Spokes 2026)
- Round 1 acceptance: invitation to schedule a 30-min Zoom interview via Calendly, interviews Mon Oct 5 - Sat Oct 10.
- Round 2 acceptance: in-person group interviews Sat Oct 24 / Sun Oct 25 on campus; report conflicts by Fri Oct 16; slot details by Sun Oct 18.
- Round 2 rejection: thanks, strong pool, invite to follow the blog and reapply.
- Team acceptance: "invite to join the Spokes 2027 team... reply by end of day Sunday, November 8th... trying to finalize positions and schedule a transition meeting." Signed "Passing the torch, Spokes 2026".
- Team waitlist: placed on waitlist; will hear by November 10th.
