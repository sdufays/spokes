# Spokes 2026 Advice (written by the 2026 team for 2027)

## Fundraising
- Talk to Maria Walsh about making a crowdfunding page.
- Have a bake sale, but do not sell boba in the Infinite (Corridor).
- Cambridge Bicycle and Trek donate bike tune-ups; raffle them for money. Trek gives 20% off all gear; Cambridge Bicycle gives wholesale gear.
- MIT 24-hour challenge on Pi Day is a fundraising window. Start fundraising ASAP.
- Decide concretely, before the trip starts, how flight and personal-gear reimbursements are allocated.
- Overall: do everything you can before the trip; the trip itself is very busy.

## Personal gear
Set a per-person gear budget. Buy bibs, not shorts (shorts ride down). Quality is not strongly tied to price, so research. Pactimo bibs were loved; some got Rafa; Trek bibs are expensive/stylish but do not hold up. Get gloves with soft padding.

## Rural America / hosts
- People are protective of private property; do not wander into yards.
- Be religiously conscious: you will spend a lot of time at churches, which are generous hosts. Avoid profanity, "God" outside religious context, explicit jokes.
- Do not get into political debates with hosts; just listen.

## Bikes and maintenance
- Get bike lights, but try to finish before dark; need 1000+ lumen front for rural roads; consider rechargeable/battery life.
- Document gear distribution; try things early so returns are easy.
- Same bike model for everyone = part compatibility/swappable wheels.
- Get gravel bikes (wider tires, better geometry, lower stock gearing). Road bikes with gravel tires not recommended (Ishaq).
- Selection criteria: gear range, adequate brakes and shifters; tubeless-ready is a plus. See "bikes bikes bikes" spreadsheet.
- Check availability of replacement parts (brake pads are bike-specific; buy in advance).
- Bought from Trek with 20% off everything; ask about free maintenance and discounted bike fitting.
- Tubeless: all had tubeless, minimal flats, but a handful had micro-leaks (old setup or poor sealant). Check for leaks (should not lose more than a few psi in a couple days). Carry spare tubes.
- Nearly all repairs can be done yourselves after reading the manual: sealant top-up, tubeless setup, tube replacement, derailleur adjust/cable replace, brake adjust. A shop charges ~$30 to turn a few screws. Wax-based chain lube is best.
- Go to a shop for wheel truing, first derailleur cable snaps, and when on a tight schedule.
- Should have bought: tiny screwdriver, various pliers.
- Clip-in pedals: practice with them. Lay bikes down derailleur side up. Experiment with bike fit.
- Ramona's checklist: check shop tires if tubeless; know how to clean/lube drivetrain, fix a flat/plug tubeless/top up sealant, adjust derailleurs and brakes; tools carried can set up tubeless, replace inner derailleur/brake cables. Small propane canisters (keep 2) if camping not much more than ~2x.
