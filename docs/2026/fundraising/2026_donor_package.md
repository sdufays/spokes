- **Tailwind Donor, Silver - $2,500**: small jersey logo; sponsor a full day of STEM instruction; individual social post; website donor page.
- **First Mile Donor, Bronze - $1,000**: sponsor a specific workshop/lesson module; group social post; website donor page.
- **Trailhead Donor, Community - $500**: inclusion in donor thank-you post; website donor page.
