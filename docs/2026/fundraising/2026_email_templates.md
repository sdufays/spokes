## Department email
Subject: "MIT Spokes 2026 x [Course Name] Fundraising $50,000 to Support Underserved Communities". Body: Dear [contact], hope IAP was restful. I'm a [grade] in Course [N] + brief connection to department. On behalf of MIT Spokes 2026 and our lofty $50,000 fundraising effort: team of seven, 3 months biking DC to SF, teaching STEM to elementary students and incarcerated youth, stops at schools, libraries, camps for free Learning Festivals. [Sentence about a workshop related to the department; e.g., Course 4: workshop on the design process and prototyping with 3D printing.] Ask: help us hit $50,000 for equipment, travel and educational materials; any amount helps; also connections/referrals; see sponsorship package; offer to meet. Links: giving.mit.edu/search/node/spokes; tinyurl.com/spokesdonations (sponsorship package); giving.mit.edu/matching-gifts (corporate matching); mitspokes.com. Signed "[name, course] & Spokes 2026". (Sent to Paul Pettigrew, Course 4, for $150.)
## Family/friends email
Subject "I'm biking across the country!" - personal opening; MIT Spokes 2026, team of seven, DC to SF over ~3 months, free STEM workshops; raising about $50k for bikes, safety gear, travel, workshop materials; donation of any size or connections to people who support education/community projects; donation page + website. Text version: "I'm biking 4,000 miles across the country as part of MIT Spokes... fundraising $50K... any amount helps".
## Faculty printout (for FPC / faculty committee)
Short pitch, $50,000 goal, "We also rely heavily on MIT department funding if they can match you!" Crowdfunding link crowdfund.mit.edu/story/Spokes-S26; sponsorship package; corporate matching; website.
