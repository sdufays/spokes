LF Workshop Notes

  - Design:
      
      - Juvenile: no razor cutters, etc. pre-cut stuff, focus on assembly and prototyping (maybe throw in more random shapes to add to challenge/creativity to make up for not being able to cut things themselves)

      - Middle: Razors ok, how to transfer designs to cardboard via pushpins + cutting skills 
      - High: Introduce goal/objective (design a house with these limitations, etc), start with skills then move to free build time
      - General Notes: animal/habitat creation (kids love animals), offer different colors\! Have time at the end where they can look at each other’s designs and maybe trade parts, ideas which allows some other interactions and learning from each other’s

  - Chem/Bio: maybe do one or the other depending on age group

          - **\*\*The Monster Genome Project\*\***

      - Maybe use ball and stick models/ to explain how to balance reactions

Finances \[4\]

  - Note: This role was assigned to two people but basically just became the responsibility of the 4 card holders. I think this was because we didn’t fully understand the difference of this role from the Fundraising and Budget roles. I would just have the card holders automatically be the Finances team
  - Acquire travel and pro cards 
  - Make sure tax exemption forms are up to date
  - Keep track of receipts, purchasing before and during trip
  - Communicate with Edgerton center when they ask about purchases
  - Communicates with Gear team to purchase gear, and Learning Festival developers to purchase their supplies

Budget \[2-3\]

  - Note: We had 2 people on this, but I recommend 3 because Budget can be pretty complicated. This role often has to make decisions that affect the whole team about whether we can make purchases, and so more perspective is also a plus. 
  - Make itemized budget
  - Keep track of spending during trip
  - Finalize spending report at end of trip
  - Makes decision on purchases to ensure team stays within budget

Additional roles during the trip:

Driver \[alternates\]

  - Goes grocery shopping/other shopping during the day
  - Gives water stops to bikers when needed
  - Does the majority of van packing in the morning (recommended)
  - Does the laundry the night before their driving day
  - Writes the host a thank you card the morning of their driving day
  - Often will do other miscellaneous tasks that are needed

Daily Route Planner \[1-2, alternates\]
