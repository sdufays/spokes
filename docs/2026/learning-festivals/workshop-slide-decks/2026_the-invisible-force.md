# The Invisible Force! (Science of Electromagnetism)
What is a magnet (attract/repel) -> what is an electromagnet (a magnet we can control with electricity; on/off, reverse direction; what can electromagnets do that magnets can't?).
Build steps: 1) place solenoid near center of plate; 2) plug wires into solenoid (order doesn't matter); Experiment 1: plug in computer (driver) board, set knob to 1.0 Hz, place a compass by solenoid and observe; 3) press cylindrical magnet into wheel slot; 4) push axle into magnet wheel; 5) use flat piece as stand, wheel as close as possible to solenoid without touching, solenoid pointing at axle; 6) assemble bridge to support axle; 7) adjust wheel height to match solenoid. Experiment 2: find the frequency that works (probably 1-30 Hz), spin the wheel by hand, can you keep the motor spinning? Why?
