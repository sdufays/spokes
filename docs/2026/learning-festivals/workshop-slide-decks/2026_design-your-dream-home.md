# Design Your Dream Home! From 2D to 3D model (MIT Spokes LF 2026)
Leaders: two MIT students (sophomore Urban Planning & CS; senior Architecture) plus Nunu and Caroline.
Why design: ideas to real world, communication, iteration/prototyping, application across fields.
Outline: 1) intro exercise: how to build a box, tabs and line types (tabs for glue, dotted = fold, solid = cut); 2) 2D net exercise: materials ruler, graph paper (1/4 in grid), scissors, glue/tape; make a net for a 2x3x4 in box, a cylinder (3 in long, 3 in circumference), or a "triangle-ish" 2 in x 5 in; 3) interlocking shapes: make a tree (oak, willow, spruce, palm) by notching sheets; 4) transfer paper design to cardboard: (A) tape and cut around, (B) push-pin mark key points then connect dots; 5) build the dream home: template or DIY net, transfer (optional), color/construction paper, cut-fold-tape, add trees/furniture/people, add an LED light inside; 6) personalize.
Contact on last slide: spokes-2026@mit.edu, mitspokes.com, instagram/mitspokes.
