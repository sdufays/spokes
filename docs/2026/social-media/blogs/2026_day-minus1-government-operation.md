Author: Nunu. Day before departure, packing at Burton Conner (move-out deadline noon).
- Packing was chaotic; luggage ~10% done at 9am. Rental cargo van (to move bikes Boston to DC) picked up at 12:30 in Arlington, MA by Nate and the author; very large vehicle.
- Spokes 2026 minivan has no roof rails; the soft roof storage compartment (for tents, sleeping bags, pads, pillows) was hard to attach; 2025 Sarah advised but the 2025 car differed. Solution by Noah (Tatiana's boyfriend) with Nate: reconfigure straps longer and attach to the rear window. Sandeep (Aarushi's dad) helped too.
- Organization: all LF supplies fit in two large bins; kitchenware and cooking supplies in one bin; bike gear, first aid and misc in another. Labeled boxes. Minivan fits all boxes plus propane tank and giant hand sanitizer bottle. Pizza from Area 4 was ordered by Sandeep.
- Tatiana reported some of her gear stolen. Drew and Nate's LF supplies were picked up at Edgerton.
- Lessons: pack earlier; tidy shipping cardboard; test roof storage with the actual car in advance. In bed 3AM for a 6AM wakeup.
