- History: first Spokes team 2013 (MIT, Harvard, Columbia); Stanford joined 2018; entirely student-run; similar groups run other trips (e.g., Texas to Alaska).
- Why bike: on the ground with communities; staying at the homes of those visited.
- How LFs are arranged: direct outreach to principals, librarians, youth detention administrators; some prior-year contacts, some new. Lessons designed around each member's major/skills; 3 rotating workshop groups feeding a final project idea.
- Funding: reach out to everyone in personal and academic life, MIT departments, local companies (Cambridge Bikes and Trek were huge supports), larger companies.
- Hardest: cars too close, rain/mud, headwinds, tight schedule; day 1 steep >15% grades (didn't know about lower gears; after ~2 weeks everyone settles in).
- Stats: 2025: 10 learning festivals, ~400 students; thousands since 2013 (more with Stanford).
- Memorable: Tazewell VA first LF test-run after DC correctional facility (a girl said she was thankful at church the next week); connecting a college-applicant and an artist with resources; first 100-miler; crossing the Rockies; biking across Golden Gate Bridge.
- Asks: donations, housing along route, spreading the word; invite other schools to start teams.
