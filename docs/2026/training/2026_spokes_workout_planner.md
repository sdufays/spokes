- Weekly pattern: Mon Strength (Lower/Full/Upper rotating), Tue Cycling, Wed Rest, Thu Strength, Fri Rest, Sat Cycling; Week 5 = deload (half reps, half time). Cycling A1-A4: 60/70/80/90 min Zone 2 (145-155 bpm; get an HR monitor); B1 40 min intervals. Zone 2 tips: cadence 80-90 rpm with 5x30/45/60 s fast-pedaling surges; strength-focused rides 60-75 rpm, 3x10-20 min tempo.
- Strength (lower-body focused but "no weak noodle arms"): A1 goblet squats (heels elevated) 2-3 x 8-14, B1 reverse lunges 2-3 x 8-10, C1 DB single-leg hip thrusts 3 x 10-20, D1 leg extension 3 x 10-12 dropset, E1 single-leg calf raises, F1 cable crunch. RPE 8-10 (1-2 reps shy of failure); progressive overload: add reps to top of range then weight; rest 1:30-3:00; start 2 sets, move to 3.
- Session Record tab: per-week logs for Strength A/B and Cycling A/B/C.
