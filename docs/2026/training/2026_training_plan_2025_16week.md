**Goal:** endurance, strength, speed, recovery for consecutive long rides; 16 weeks (e.g., spring semester); focus on first weeks of the trip, not full 50-70 mile rides.
- **Phase 1 Base (wks 1-4):** 2-3 indoor rides/wk (20-30 min mountain terrain or 1 hr steady, 60-80% max HR, increase weekly); daily mobility (elephant walk, world's greatest stretch, deep squat hold, couch stretch w/ hip circles, plank); strength 1-2x/wk low volume (goblet squat, split squat, Romanian deadlift, plank, pullups, pushups/bench); 1+ rest day.
- **Phase 2 Build (wks 5-9):** spring break week off; indoor rides 1-2/wk (HIIT, hills, tempo 20-90 min); 1 outdoor ride/wk starting at 10+ miles, goal pace 18-22 mph, Saturday group rides organized by Ishaq; leg strength 1x/wk (back squat, single-leg press, deadlift, hip thrust) - avoid lifting before rides; optional upper body.
- **Phase 3 Peak (wks 10-13):** 3-4 rides/wk: endurance 1+ hr/20+ miles, indoor hill training, HIIT 4-6 x 3-5 min, tempo; outdoor rides progressively longer incl. consecutive-day rides.
- **Phase 4 Taper (wks 14-16):** 3 rides/wk, endurance easy, HIIT 3-5 x 2-4 min; reduce strength volume.
- Tracking: distance/duration (Strava), HR (max ~220-age), speed, power, cadence (80-100 rpm), strength reps/loads (Hevy). Glossary: endurance 60-80% HR; HIIT zone 4-5; tempo 76-90%; recovery <60%.
