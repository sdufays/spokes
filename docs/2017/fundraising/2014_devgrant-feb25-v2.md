MIT IDEAS Global Challenge:
Development Grant Request

	Spokes America: 2014

We would like to request a grant of $1000 to fund some of the training and materials costs for our team members as we prepare for the summer. The budget for how we would spend the money is given below. We will need to buy the most recent set of maps for the route that we will follow, as determined by the Adventure Cycling Association. These maps are necessary for coordinating the dates that we will visit each school.
At a minimum, four of our team members will be trained in First Aid and CPR during the semester – we plan on partnering with the MIT Arctan student group for a discounted rate on classes. Our other major cost is to get background checks for all team members. Our preliminary cost estimate for getting background checks through MIT is $75 per person, based on informal conversations with the SAO.

2014 edition Adventure Cycling Association maps | $20.00 | 10 | $200.00
