Spokes America 2017
Inspiring the next generation of scientists and engineers: Spokes America is committed to getting students across the country excited about STEM! We are a group of 8 MIT and Harvard students biking from Washington D.C. to San Francisco while hold nine learning festivals along the way. These learning festivals contain four workshops that focus on important and interesting parts of the STEM curriculum. Through hands-on, project-based discovery, we introduce concepts and possibilities that students may not otherwise see in their daily classrooms. We are picking up where the last four years of Spokes left off, each of which has made a huge impact on students across America.
To learn more, you can check out our website and our blog.

Spokes America: Who and Why
Across the United States, students in middle schools and high schools do not have access to programs in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math (STEM). This summer, a group of STEM students from MIT and Harvard will bike from Washington D.C. to San Francisco, inspiring students to discover new passions and learn what they love. The Spokes America team members come from a wide range of backgrounds—physics, mathematics, electrical engineering, computer science, and neuroscience—but we all share a common passion: getting the next generation excited about learning STEM.
Along our bike route, we will run nine Learning Festivals, where students will have the opportunity to touch, feel, design, build, and learn. They will discover the joy of designing and creating something new, and they’ll leave our workshops excited to learn more about STEM. These workshops are just meant to get them started.
By biking, we want to show students that scientists and engineers can also be athletic and live interesting lives, breaking down any negative stereotypes about being intelligent, nerdy, or interested in STEM.
As a SPONSOR of Spokes, you can help us make a lasting impact on the future of the United States. The U.S. needs more scientists, engineers, and programmers and you can help us make this difference!

The Impact
Spokes America means a lot to a lot of people. Students across the U.S. will be exposed to hands-on projects that show them that engineering is fun. Our workshops will also build off what students see in their everyday lives by peeling back layers and showing them the science behind circuits, how their computers work, and even the way that they think.
Spokes will also help science and engineering teachers make a difference by showing students the importance of what they are learning in STEM classes.
As for us, we’re just a group of college students who love STEM. Travelling across the United States, pushing our limits through biking, meeting all kinds of new people, and teaching what we’ve come to love will be one of the most formative experiences of our life.
Your sponsorship will help make this trip possible.
