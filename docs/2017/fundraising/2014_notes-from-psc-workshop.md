Notes from PSC Writing Workshop

There are slides to be sent out - I’ll move them into the drive when I get them!
Elizabeth Perolski is Alison Hynds’ grad assistant
word limits are only guidelines
abstract is for old man in Up - jargon free
SAY HOW MUCH FUNDING YOU REQUEST
make sure its consistent with your ask inside app
need - community
why do they need this?
describe their lives
focus on local, not global - who are you reaching, what are specific areas like?
WHO
WHY
WILL IT REALLY BENEFIT PEOPLE
HOW MUCH WILL YOU INVEST IN COMMUNITY
HOW MUCH DO YOU KNOW ABOUT COMMUNITY - are you in touch with them?
prove you’ve talked to community partner - that’s the point of the letter
success - what makes this project worth your while? how do you know you nailed it?
don’t use charts in work plan
length of time spent at location, given in weeks (min of 5)
how are you working with your teammates
when is everybody getting out to SF?
show your project is doable in the time available (route in miles, travel per day, number of days, plus buffer)
your specific role in work plan
be clear where things are happening - don’t spend too much time at MIT :)
how to get meetings/workshops on peoples’ calendars  asap
sustainability plan
how will it affect community?
DO NOT USE THE WORD “BETTER” - be specific about your impact without putting down the community for existing solutions
cultural context?
how will it impact you?
what experience do you have working in different cultures?
how will you prepare for these cultural contexts?
read local paper
are there MIT alums near where we’re going? (lodging, support, information?)
are you prepared to NOT be well received? how will you react?
SAFETY IS CRITICAL TO PROPOSAL
Alison is actually personally liable for project if it gets funded
main safety issues for our project?
what steps are you taking to address these?
safety implications for community we’re serving
show you have street smarts - can you look out for yourself?
show your awareness of biggest risks
consider this for domestic work too!
do research in areas - just a google search “student traveling to ____”
DO NOT IGNORE THE SCARY THINGS - it will look horrible
write this for your mom :(
no motorcycles
safety considerations:
MIT HAS EVACUATION INSURANCE - WHERE TO GET INFO?
dress code
securing belongings
phone
travel safety
Qualifications:
why will you make this a success?
relevant classes are okay (don’t talk about more than 4)
beyond resume - talk about your past experiences, clubs, projects, etc - stuff that might not be on resume
can you be trusted?
how will you learn to do the other parts?
are you a good match with the community?
languages you know? skill level in written/oral competencies
have you done this before?
Team work:
DO NOT COPY THE PARTS OF THE APPLICATION BETWEEN ME AND JAMIN
individual apps
discuss each person’s role!
they won’t interview/fund entire team (very rarely) - mainly 1-2 people will get funded
you can share money you win but you don’t have to
they’re trying to fund the max number of projects, not people
everyone applying has to be MIT student or VERY RECENT alum
motivation - be completely honest
personal outcomes - what do you plan to get out of this at the end?
end goals?
personality type
what are you?
how does this make you a good fit for this project?
what is your ability to work in this group?
Budget listing your needs
itemized
expenses must be very accurate
declare funding you already have, other intended places to apply
there are rules - you can’t give PSC Fellowship and Expedition Grants
they fund:
food, housing, transportation, ground transportation while there
ESSENTIAL materials - not unsustainable materials
funding range from $1500 - $4000 per person
research costs
how will you be fiscally conservative?
Kayak for flights
reasons for rejection:
rec letters/community letters can come a little late
community letter should clearly state the need for you to do this project
safety section is scary
you don’t understand the needs of your community
budget is depending on a lot of unsustainable materials (ipads, laptops)
project is a one-time thing, not sustainable - community must continue to benefit
mismatch between project needs and your qualifications
You can say you are going to learn more about how to do something!
show that you are innovative and awesome (YOU + PROJECT = SUCCESS)
show you understand customs of target community
do not lie about safety considerations - address them!
know how to deal with safety things
Stick with double space :)
you get the money before you go
check to your personal account linked to student account via MIT
in application, Jamin and Emma need to describe fully their roles (and each other’s roles) in their applications
also briefly summarize how the other 5 team members make the team awesome and likely to succeed at project
Do you take appendices (i.e. with detailed emergency plans?)
there will have to be weekly check ins during the project with the PSC if you win
will also need to do a blog or final report - blog is highly encouraged :)
share pictures
they feed a pre-departure dinner and a reflection dinner on return
ask you to wear MIT swag and take pictures
may be asked to speak to donors later
