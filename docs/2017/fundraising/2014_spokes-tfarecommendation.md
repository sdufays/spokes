Dear Ms. Hynd and the PSC Fellowship Committee,

My name is Joshua Gutierrez and I’m a Recruitment Manager for Teach For America (TFA) recruiting at five specific schools, one being MIT. I’m writing this recommendation on behalf of the Spokes team.

About a year ago I was approached by a couple of founding Spokes members about this project. As I learned more about Spokes that day, I immediately saw the incredible opportunities their organization was presenting to our students across the country. To have the opportunity to learn about science as fun, creative, and applicable from a group of MIT students would be both amazing and extremely critical for students considering the STEM field.

Since that day, TFA has fully endorsed Spokes. While collaborating with both our national marketing team and our regional offices, we have been working diligently to find schools all across the country for Spokes to host their “Learning Festivals.” At this point, we have connected with every region across the country and are nailing down exact schools that would benefit from these day long workshops.

TFA will also be offering teacher training to the entire Spokes team in order to adequately prepare them for the challenges of teaching in various classrooms across the country. Furthermore, the Spokes team will be visiting one of the TFA boot camps for new teachers in order to share the work that Spokes is doing, and to learn more about teaching from TFA.

We are extremely confident that this project will be successful. Through their passion for education, diligence towards this work, and partnership with our schools, Spokes will be transforming students into real-time scientists this summer!

If you’d like me to share any more information about TFA’s involvement with Spokes please feel free to call or email at any time.

Thank you,

-Joshua
