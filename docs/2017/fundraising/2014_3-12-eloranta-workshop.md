3/12 Notes on Eloranta Workshop

Melissa and Sarah (Melissa was giving the workshop)
Project is student’s own idea
something that can be completed over the summer
winning projects
doable
original
well thought out
engaging proposal
you clearly have the skills to accomplish project
read the website carefully!
can get the staff to read your proposal early
decisions come by 1st week of May
you will hear something by late april?
seniors are eligible to apply
if its a jumpstart towards a startup, masters, etc. they like that
or if this is something that you’ve always wanted to explore, then say that
competitive fellowship
2-4 per year
$6000 per person
usually get ~45 applications
engage committee
sell convincingly
make case for your skills and resources
make sure you have a faculty member recommenders
minimum 1 letter which has to come from faculty member who’s reviewed project and can speak to its merits and your ability to see it through
Sanjay is probably a great guy for this
judging on feasibility
understand where your work fits in target field
must be a well-written proposal!
no spelling mistakes, formatting errors, etc.
accept submissions by e-mail before 5pm April 4th
rec letters too
they also accept hard copies in 7-104
they allow for late rec letters but be sure to let them know letter will be late
can give supplementary materials, link to website, etc.
proposal word limit is 2000 words
abstract, overview, descriptions
APPENDICES DON’T COUNT
include a sample beginning and exit survey for students!!!
budget justifying use of $6000 - supplementary appendix
MUST HAVE THIS
living expense, travel expenses, interpreters, access to people, courses for team, supplies, materials, all are justifiable
cost ranges are acceptable, try to be exact as possible
make predictions as needed
Abstract:
What is the final product of your research? what is the expected deliverable?
clarity is key
may get asked follow up questions
should be able to answer everything with proposal and supplemental materials?
money is awarded for a lump sum
provide update at end of summer
send them materials you make - blog, video, etc
committee is looking for things that are different, new, unique
things you’ve always wanted to do but never had the opportunity to do before!!!
joint applications:
can split money, awarded to $3000 to each person
for a very expensive project, two people were awarded $6000, but that was once
generally only award $6000 per project
go to visit them to get feedback
e-mail eloranta-www@mit.edu to make an appointment or drop by office between 9-5 M-F to grab a staff member
Questions:
do you want to see the entire budget? not just $6000?
YES because it shows you understand the project
safety considerations
yes show you’ve thought about it
students who are coming back to MIT need to fill out MIT travel forms for UROP office
information on ISOS insurance etc
research with human subjects vetted by CUHES (count for students?)
