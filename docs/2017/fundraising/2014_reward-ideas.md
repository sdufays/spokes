$5 and up
Deep thanks from our team and inclusion on our Friends and Family email update list.

$25 and up
Deep thanks and appreciation from our team, inclusion in our Friends and Family email update list, and recognition of your contribution to our cause on the Spokes Website.

$35 and up
A postcard from one random state we visit along the way, plus all previous

Video greeting of team at a school stop, plus all previous.
Postcard from every state
Personal update from every school?

for a lot of money: a postcard from every state?

And so on up to infinity—only so many ways to say thank you. Personal letter from the team? Spokes bike jersey for big donor? Meet the team if you live along our bike route? All kinds of options...help me out here!

Postcards!! -- We can have an option where they get one for the summer (either of a random state or of a picture of us) or an option that they get one for every state that we visit etc. (AWESOME IDEA)

We promise to use letter stickers to spell out their names on our arms so that we get their names tanned onto our forearms, and then send them a photo.

Maybe access to an exclusive virtual photo album that gets updated regularly during the trip.

A random gift mailed to them from every state or a state of their choosing.

A video of us singing a song of their choosing around the campfire. Or a video of us singing as a group while riding on the road. (LOVE THIS)
