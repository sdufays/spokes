Hello from Spokes America!
We are a group of MIT, Harvard, and Columbia University science and engineering students, with a mission to encourage school children across the US to be self-driven, creative and curious in their approach to science and engineering. Our science and engineering outreach program will consist of a cross country cycling trip from San Francisco to Washington D.C. that exemplifies those qualities of self-driven creativity and curiosity. We are asking you, the JetBlue Foundation, to support us in our cause by helping us to travel from Boston to San Francisco so that we can begin our journey.
The American education system is struggling to produce enough scientists and engineers who can continue to drive technological development in the U.S., which is affecting key economic players including the airline industry and JetBlue. We will address this critical issue by entering classrooms and summer camps to connect students and teachers with new teaching resources like online programming tools and hobby electronics kits. We will use these tools in science and engineering workshops that encourage students to ask questions and use creative problem-solving to satisfy their own curiosity, with the end goal of inspiring them to become the next generation of scientists and engineers. We will be cycling in order to challenge the stereotype that students interested in STEM cannot also be athletic and active.
Sponsors of the Spokes America 2014 trip are enabling young people across the country to better realize their own creative problem-solving potential, as well as connecting teachers to new teaching resources for science and technology, all for the sake of building a stronger economic foundation for the nation. We are asking the JetBlue Foundation for a donation of flights from Boston or New York to San Francisco to help us begin our journey.  If you would like to learn more about our project, please feel free to contact me or visit our website at www.spokesamerica.org/.
We are excited to embark on this journey, and we hope that you will join us!

Sincerely,
Cali Warner and Spokes America
cwarner@mit.edu
