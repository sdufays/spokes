Lesson Plan Writing Worksheet
Title: How Computers Work: Building Circuits
Target Grade Levels: 7-12
What prior knowledge do you expect your students to have coming in?
Very basic knowledge of circuits (have to be closed, connect high to low in battery)
Understand how base 10 works. Understand exponents. (ex 365 = 3*10^2 + 6*10^1 + 5)
Maybe understand exponents to the power of 0.
Learning Objectives:
What do you want the students be able to do at the end of the lesson?
Build a circuit on a breadboard by following a breadboard diagram
Build a circuit on breadboard without a breadboard diagram (just a circuit diagram)
Use a voltmeter to check where a circuit is broken
Hopefully: Convert numbers from binary to base ten
Hopefully: Add numbers in binary
Maybe: Convert small number from base 10 to binary
What do you want the students to know at the end of the lesson?
Understand the basics of a breadboard and circuits
What holes connect, Where do you connect the battery terminals
Understand that low voltage (GND) is not the same as no input
Understand what the following components do: battery, LED, resistor, transistor (one type)
Understand V = IR and basics of voltage, current, and voltage drops
What each of the following logic gates does: AND, OR, XOR, maybe NAND
How to interpret a circuit diagram and predict where there will be high/low voltage
Transistors are the building blocks of computers
Maybe: transistors combine to form logic gates
How do you want your lesson to affect students?
Get excited about electrical engineering, math, and computer science
Have them want to build more circuits
Maybe send students home with an instructables link + diagram and links to the supplies they would need to buy to build a new circuit?
Feel like engineering is less intimidating
What do you want your students to tell their parents and friends when they go home?
“I made a circuit that adds numbers”
“I know how computers add numbers”
“I know binary, the language of computers”
“I can use transistors”
“I know what computers are made of”
Come home with a filled out worksheet that has diagram of transistor, logic gates and truth tables, diagram of circuits that they can look back at and hopefully understand.
Part #1: Intro to Breadboards, Circuits, and Transistors
Goals:
Build a circuit on a breadboard by following a breadboard diagram
Build a circuit on breadboard without a breadboard diagram (just a circuit diagram)
Use a voltmeter to check where a circuit is broken
Understand the basics of a breadboard and circuits
What holes connect, Where do you connect the battery terminals
Understand what the following components do: battery, LED, resistor, transistor (one type)
Understand V = IR and basics of voltage, current, and voltage drops ?

Introduction (8 min)
Ask Class: (3 min)
What is electricity?
What is a circuit?
What is a LED? Light emitting diode: show on board how it must be connected.
Explain broadly: V = IR or I = V/R. More voltage → more current. More resistance → less current. Too much current can damage things.
Set up circuit components at front of class and demo as we go.
Hand out breadboards and make partners. Introduce yourself to your partner, then tell your partner name + 3 fun facts about yourself.
Build simple circuit with LED, resistor and dip switch. (3 min)
Does it work if you turn the LED around?
Measure the voltage drop from positive to negative. Confirm that it is 4.5
Measure the voltage drop across the LED.
 If this is super easy for some kids, ask them to build a circuit with the switch such that the negative terminal of the LED is connected to the output of the switch and the LED turns on when the switch is OFF.

What is a transistor: (10 min)
Explain basic NPN-channel resistor with chart (truth table with 2 items). Demo with this.
Answer on worksheet with your partner: in which of the following circuits will the LED turn on??
If anyone is bored, have them make up a circuit on their paper with transistors. Or try to work on inverter circuit.

Building the Static-Electricity Detector circuit: (25min)
Flip over worksheet and look at diagram
Discuss: What is this circuit doing?
What are the components in this circuit?
What will happen when static charge (voltage) is applied to the antenna?
When will the circuit containing the lightbulb be closed?
Build circuit (20 min)
How can we generate more static? Use wool socks and comb. Have fun!
