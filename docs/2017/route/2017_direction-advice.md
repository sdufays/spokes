Hi Caity,
From East Bernstadt to Columbia is basically taking KY 80. The problems I see with 80 is that parts of it can and are very busy. The section between London and Somerset is the busiest with some of that being more busy than the rest.
The area in London where it crosses I-75.
The intersection of KY 461 and KY 80
The section from KY 461 to Somerset is really busy as you enter Somerset.
An alternate route (used Google Maps as a guide) could be
Leave East Bernstadt on KY 490.
Left onto US 25.
Right onto KY 2041.
Right onto KY 1956 (Somerset Rd).
Right onto KY 80.
Left onto Sandy Gap Rd. Sandy Gap Rd flows into KY 1675.
Cross KY 80 onto Leo Gilliland Rd.
Right onto Shopville Rd.
At the Shopville Elementary School turn left cross KY 80 onto KY 692.
Turn right onto KY 192.
Cross KY 914 (Southeastern Bypass).
Left onto E Mt Vernon St (Old KY 80).
Pick up W Mt Vernon St at the Courthouse in Somerset you will have to jog around the courthouse (Old KY 80).
Straight onto Ohio St (Old KY 80).
Left onto Ogden St (Old KY 80).
Cross US 27 This is a busy intersection with a traffic light.
Remain on KY Old 80 which will become KY 80 in a few miles after crossing KY 914 again.
Between Somerset and Nancy you have to cross water, which means a nice down hill followed by a climb. There is no other way without adding many many many many miles.
From Somerset to Russell Springs I do not recall a shoulder for the most part. Stay away from the edge and make motorist do a full lane change. Don't let them try to squeeze past you without changing lanes.
From Russell Springs follow the directions I have already sent.
NOTE: Other than the directions from Russell Springs to here I have not rode on any of the above.
