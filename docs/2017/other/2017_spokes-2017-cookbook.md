Spaghetti and Pasta Sauce
Compliments of Bruce and Vikki White
Fredericksburg, VA

Ingredients:
Spaghetti
A big jar of Prego Spaghetti Sauce
Diced Squash
Diced Tomatoes
Garlic Salt
Onion salt
Sweet Sausage

Directions:
Don't over cook the noodles and rinse them off to remove excess starch before serving
Cook the big jar of Prego Spaghetti Sauce and add diced squash, dice tomatoes, a little garlic salt, a little onion salt
If you are a meat lover, add sweet sausage cooked separately and ground fine
Drain off grease and add to sauce

Notes: This meal is best if you are cooking for a large group!

Stir Fry in a Wok
Compliments of Bruce and Vikki White
Fredericksburg, VA

Ingredients:
Brown Rice
Canola Oil
Diced Squash
Diced Tomatoes
Soy Sauce
Chicken Breasts
Salt and Pepper
Sweet Potatoes (optional)

Directions:
Start by cooking brown rice in a separate container and not too much water (soggy rice is bad)
Start in the wok with a little bit of canola oil and add diced squash, diced tomatoes, soy sauce and cook (the longer the better)
Pre-boil chicken breasts
For meat lovers, dice up chicken breasts and add to wok mix
When brown rice is finished, add it to the wok mix and stir (most like it when squash is cooked but still just a slight bit crunchy and not soggy)
Add a little salt and pepper to taste as needed
If you want, you can also add a sweet potato to the meal

Balela Salad
Compliments of Ann Boes
Farmington, MO

Ingredients:
2 (15 ounce) cans garbanzo beans, drained and rinsed
2 (15 ounce) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
1/2 cup (packed) chopped fresh mint
1/2 cup (packed) chopped fresh parsley
1/2 cup chopped onion
1 pint grape tomatoes, halved
1 jalapeno pepper, finely chopped
1 clove garlic, minced
1/4 cup olive oil
3 tablespoons lemon juice
5 tablespoons apple cider vinegar salt and ground black pepper to taste

Directions:
Chop the mint and parsley pretty fine
Dice the onion into very small pieces
In a salad bowl, lightly mix together the garbanzo beans, black beans, mint, parsley, onion, and grape tomatoes
Mince the garlic and jalapeno and add olive oil, lemon juice, and apple cider vinegar
Pour the dressing over the salad, and toss into mix; sprinkle with salt and black pepper to serve.

Ice-cream Sandwich Cake
Compliments of Neil Boes
Farmington, MO

Ingredients:
Ice-cream sandwiches (12 pack)
Cool Whip (8oz)
Toffee bits
Chocolate shell

Directions:
Lay down one layer of 4 sandwiches on cookie sheet
Fold toffee bits into Cool Whip and spread layer on top of sandwiches
Lay second layer of sandwiches and cool whip
Lay final layer of sandwiches and cover entire cake in cool whip concoction
Finally cover entire cake in chocolate shell
Freeze
