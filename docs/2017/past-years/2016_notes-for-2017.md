Biking and driving and finding place to stay:
We tried to start biking altogether at first but didn’t work because people went to bed and got up at different time and also needed varying amount of time to prepare for biking. In the end, each morning the last rider left within 2 hours after the first rider left and it worked.
In the morning, bikers eat, prepare and gather their personal belongings near the car and leave, driver of the day then will take down the tents, clean up the trash, and pack the car.
5 out of 9 members of the team can drive, and so 5 drivers take turn to drive, while 4 non-drivers have to do different things (washing dishes, locking bikes and cleaning up at night before bed) so that the responsibilities are well distributed among all members.
