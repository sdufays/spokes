- Want good wheels and GOOD TIRES; maybe Gatorskins. Wider/treadier wheels: is a cyclocross frame an option? (Not carrying much in panniers.)
- Groupset: Sora, Tiagra or hopefully 105; not critical.
- Sample bikes: Felt F24x $849 (Sora, cross); Felt FR60 $799 (Claris); Felt VR60 $899 (disc brakes, Claris, touring); Trek CrossRip 1 ~$1,100; Specialized Allez E5 Elite ~$1,150. Buying in bulk from a local shop probably means a discount, bringing these to about $1,000; name brand potentially important for supply/parts.
Outcome: team bought 8 Jamis Renegade Exile from Cambridge Bicycle.
