**Date:** 5/1/18. **Org:** Spokes America. **Contact:** Kate Pearce, kpearce@mit.edu, 224-567-2524. Website mitspokes.com. Mailing address: Spokes America, MIT Edgerton Center, 77 Massachusetts Ave Rm 4-408, Cambridge, MA 02139. Nonprofit 501(c)(3); Tax-ID 04-2103594 (MIT). Not part of government.

**Budget context:** current funds $6,000; project cost $25,000; proposed grant $4,000.

**Program:** 8 students, 8 bikes, 3,500 miles, 75 days, DC to San Francisco, 9 schools/community centers:
Louisa County Library VA (June 1); Challenger Learning Center KY (June 13-14); Lab: Revolution MO (June 23); St. Louis Homeschool Connection MO (June 26); aSTEAM Village MO (July 2); Newton Public Library KS (July 5); Loveland Public Library CO (July 14); Christa McAuliffe Space Center UT (July 21); Carson City Public Library NV (July 31).

Four workshops at each stop: "3...2...1...Build!", "Circuits Galore!", "Playing with Python", "Picture This: The Science of Photography", adaptable for elementary to high school.

**Team pitch:** 6th year; guidance from past teams; an experienced cyclist from MIT Cycling Club; planned entire trip themselves while in contact with MIT staff.

**Cost breakdown ($25,000):** ~$10,000 bikes + gear + camping; $6,000 van rental and gas; $8,000 food and misc supplies; $1,000 teaching materials and emergencies. Raised so far: $5,000 ($3,000 MIT departments, $2,000 individual donors).

**Benefits / recognition / success measures:** project-based STEM in rural areas, breaking stereotypes of engineers, promoting cycling. Club recognized on website, Instagram, Facebook. Success = making it across and teaching at 9 schools, surveying schools afterwards, being invited back (which has happened for 5 years).
