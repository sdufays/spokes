MIT SPOKES - 8 STUDENTS. 8 BIKES. 3,500 MILES. 75 DAYS.
8 MIT students biking across the country this summer to rethink STEM education. Website: mitspokes.wixsite.com/mitspokes.

**Who we are:** a team of 8 (freshmen, two juniors, a recent graduate) from MIT. Curricula complement each school's curriculum and highlight MIT's hands-on approach. Learning Festivals at schools, libraries and summer camps from D.C. to San Francisco.

**How you can help:** donations of any kind: teaching supplies, bike gear (or bikes), equipment and clothes, road snacks, water bottles, sunscreen, or money. In return sponsors are added to the Sponsors page on the MIT Spokes website.
