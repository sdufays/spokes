**Individual (1 per person):** bike; front/back lights; helmet; 2 bottle cages; 2 water bottles; 2 extra tubes; patch kit; bike shoes; 2 bike shorts; gloves; jerseys; sunglasses.

**Group:** car rack (2-3 bikes); 2 big cable locks; floor pump; frame pump; tire levers, 2 multitools, pedal wrench; tents (sleep 8); lighters; dish cloth/soap; 2 portable chargers (Lisa to bring her own two); laundry detergent; camping stove; pots/pans/utensils; bowls; propane.

**Emergency:** a couple of first aid kits; lots of tissues and baby wipes; pepper/dog spray (1 per person); duct tape; water purification tablets; small sewing kit.
