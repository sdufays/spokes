General ideas: break the build into sub-projects each with an observable output; build on prior steps; as much hands-on as possible, little lecturing. Target audience (General Notes, circuits folder): 11 to 15 years, middle school.

- Mini 1, LED circuit: how a breadboard works; resistors in series/parallel; Ohm's law; ground and power. Materials: Arduino, wires, resistor, LED, laptop, USB cable.
- Mini 2, LED circuit 2: introduce Arduino for flexibility; blink; change on/off times in prepared code.
- Mini 3, photoresistor: add photoresistor so light level dims/brightens LEDs (covering it raises resistance, dims LED).
- Mini 4, voltage divider with photoresistor: voltage divider equation; two resistors then one photoresistor; midpoint to LED/Arduino.
- Mini 6, motor basics: fidget spinner idea.
- Mini 7, electromagnetism: 18g copper wire (precut), AA/AAA batteries, neodymium magnets.
Planned kit sources: Elegoo Arduino kit and micro servos (Amazon); MakeCrate (Delia's mother's company) offered donated electronics kits (musical instrument kit).
