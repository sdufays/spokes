Contains the same text as "TBP Grant Formatted" with the funder's guidance prompts in italics:
- Project abstract: clear and concise, avoid jargon, include location, community partner, team members.
- Background and motivation: community, existing needs, impact and sustainability (lasting benefit after you leave).
- Project plan: technical details, goals, specific roles, how success is evaluated, figures, additional safety precautions beyond the safety statement.
- Proposed timeline: work plan in as much detail as possible, preparation on campus, milestones, continuation.
- Qualifications: skills, courses, teamwork experience.
- Personal statement: personal motivation and what you hope to learn.
Safety details mentioned: each team member undergoes a background check; an emergency contact at MIT; local and international travel forms filled out through the Student Activities Office.
