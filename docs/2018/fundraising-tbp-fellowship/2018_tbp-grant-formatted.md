**Spokes America 2018, Summer 2018.** Team: Delia Stephens, Kate Pearce, An Jimenez, Bethany Cates, Lisa Peng, Annie Phan, Matthew Luerman, Alex Tran.

**Abstract:** six-year-old student initiative; 8-hour Learning Festivals at schools, libraries, museums and camps; targets rural communities without strong STEM curricula; defies stereotypes.

**Background:** rural children lack STEM resources and role models; educators across the country are excited to have the program return, often promising large attendance; parents, teachers and hosts reached out, and many reported increased STEM engagement after past visits.

**Project plan:** DC to San Francisco, 3,500 miles in 75 days, 9+ Learning Festivals. Curriculum: Design Challenges, Circuit Building, Intro to Python, The Physics of Photography. Four pairs of instructors each run two 2-hour workshops per day so each student meets every concept; materials reused to save money. Staying in homes of hosts found through personal connections and Warmshowers.org. Working with MIT Student Activities Office on safety (helmets, lights, bright jerseys, first-aid kits).

**Timeline:** working since Feb 2018; fundraised through corporate sponsorships and MIT crowdfund; nearly all Warmshowers hosts confirmed; all 9 schools contacted, 7 confirmed, 2 awaiting dates; planning to finish by May 17 (almost two weeks before leaving DC).

**Qualifications highlights:** Kate (high school tutoring business, WiSE HS STEM outreach via MIT SWE); An (Global Teaching Labs, 200 students); Lisa (Exit 5 Robotics outreach, ~2000 visitors); Matthew (Navy ROTC, Physics TA); Annie (sMITe Ultimate captain); Delia (Air Force ROTC, MIT Varsity Field Hockey); Bethany (Physics TA, sMITe Ultimate captain).

**Personal statements** (excerpts): Bethany (low-income background; erase disparities); Kate ("Want to bike across the country this summer?" email); Annie; An; Delia (role model for young women); Matt; Lisa.
