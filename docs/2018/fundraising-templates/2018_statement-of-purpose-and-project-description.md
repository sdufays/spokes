Applicant: Kate Pearce (kpearce@mit.edu, 224-567-2524). Project: MIT Spokes America 2018.

Statement: Spokes brings hands-on STEM workshops to rural areas; the cross-country ride shows engineers are athletic, adventurous and community-focused.

Description: 8 students, 8 bikes, 3,500 miles, 75 days; schools: Louisa County Library VA (June 1), Challenger Learning Center KY (June 13-14), Lab: Revolution MO (June 23), St. Louis Homeschool Connection MO (June 26), aSTEAM Village MO (July 2), Newton Public Library KS (July 5), Loveland Public Library CO (July 14), Christa McAuliffe Space Center UT (July 21), Carson City Public Library NV (July 31). Four workshops each: "3...2...1...Build!", "Circuits Galore!", "Playing with Python", "Picture This".

Team roles: several on fundraising, a couple on schools, two on route/lodging, one on social media; mentorship from past teams (6th year); since 2013, 8-10 students per year to places like Hazard KY and Newton KS.

**Budget total $23,000:** Teaching materials $1,000; van rental + gas $6,000; food $6,000; bikes, gear, camping gear $11,000.
