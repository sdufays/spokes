Hello, I am Kate Pearce, a freshman in Course 20 from the Chicago area. This summer I will bike across the country with 7 other MIT students as part of Spokes America, DC to San Francisco, teaching self-designed STEM workshops at schools, many rural. I will run "Playing with Python". Details at mitspokes.wixsite.com/mitspokes. Could the MIT Club of Chicago or its members help fund Spokes? Trip costs about $25,000; donations via MIT Giving by searching "Spokes"; we will list donors as sponsors and send updates. kpearce@mit.edu.

Outcome: MIT Club of Chicago gave $500 (Projected Costs sheet, 2018).
