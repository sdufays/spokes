Hello from Spokes! We are a team of 8 MIT science and engineering students. This summer: a 3,500-mile bike ride from D.C. to San Francisco over 75 days to host 9 Learning Festivals in small towns. Spokes (mitspokes.com) is a non-profit, student-run organization rethinking STEM education. Team: 5 freshmen, 2 juniors, a recent graduate.

Learning Festival: an intensive 8-hour day with 4 STEM-focused workshops (Computer Science, Chemistry, Robotics, Design) targeted at schools and libraries in the middle of the U.S.

We will stay a night in [town] on [date]; could the local paper feature Spokes? The trip costs about $25,000 (bikes, gear, food, van, curriculum supplies); small donations to our crowdfund (crowdfund.mit.edu/project/10264) are appreciated, but even getting the word out helps. Signed An Jimenez and the MIT Spokes team.

Lesson: the news-tip form blast (10 Boston and Austin outlets) is in Potential Sponsors; this town-by-town version was written late (trip less than a month away) - send earlier.
