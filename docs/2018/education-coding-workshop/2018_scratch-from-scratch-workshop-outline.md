1. Opening (15-20 min): introduce ourselves; what is coding; applications of CS (picture handouts, discuss).
2. Introduce Scratch (5-10): what/why/what can you make.
3. Installation/sign-up (10): students create accounts and get Scratch running on their laptops.
4. Preview projects/options (10): demos on projector or kids browse by search terms; options: follow tutorial, modify someone else's project, start fresh.
5. Play time (30).
6. Sharing time (15): view other students' projects.
7. Group discussion (10): cool projects, easy/hard, what you learned, what next.
8. Play time round 2 (leave 5 min wrap-up).
9. Conclusion (5): resources for future learning; encourage exploring.
