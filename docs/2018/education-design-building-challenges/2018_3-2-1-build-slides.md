Instructors: An Jimenez and Bethany Cates. Overview: what is engineering, engineering design process, challenges. "Engineers use math and science to develop solutions to technical problems"; 200+ types of engineering.

- Challenge 1, Book Balance (10 min, 4 per group): using 8 sheets of paper, build a structure at least 6 inches off the ground that holds the most books.
- Challenge 2, DOTS Tower (30 min, groups of 4): tallest tower using only DOTS gummies and toothpicks; must stand 10 seconds.
- Challenge 3, Egg Drop (25 min, groups of 4): restricted-size device from provided materials to keep a raw large egg from breaking when dropped; egg must move freely in/out (no taping), no parachutes.
Wrap-up: discuss designs.
