**Time breakdown:** 8:30 AM team arrives and sets up; 9-11 Session 1; 11-1 Session 2; 1-2 lunch; 2-4 Session 3.

**Rooms:** Room 1 Intro to CS (all three sessions); Room 2 Design Challenge; Room 3 Picture This; Room 4 Circuit Building. Students rotate; sample schedule: Session 1 Intro to CS, Session 2 Picture This, Session 3 Circuit Building.

**Special requests:** Intro to CS: students need internet access and outlets. Design Challenge: outdoor access to test egg capsules. Picture This and Circuit Building: none stated.
