**Instructors:** Delia Stephens and Alex Tran. Based on pinholephotography.org camera obscura.

**Website description:** students construct a camera obscura (precursor of the camera) projecting images onto tracing paper, learning the physics of light and the chemistry of film photography.

**Plan (2 hours):** 0:00-0:30 Physics of light (what is light, how the eye works, camera obscura intro); 0:30-1:00 Build the camera (pass out boxes, cut holes, create tracing paper "cards"); 1:00-1:30 Breakdown (how a camera works, art and science of photography, what to change for better pictures); 1:30-2:00 Chemistry of an instant camera, take a picture with an instant camera.

**Materials:** cardboard boxes, scissors, tape, popsicle sticks, tracing paper (plus magnifying glasses and instant camera/film; per budget, workshop camera supplies cost $125.46).

Graphic design notes: MIT colors; header font Lemon/Milk.
