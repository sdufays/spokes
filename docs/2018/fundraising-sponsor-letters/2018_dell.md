**Problem statement:** rural STEM education is lacking; Carnegie Science Report: rural students rarely aware of STEM opportunities; the 2014 Condition of Education says rural students are less likely to attend college, complete a HS science sequence, or have qualified teachers.

**Project description:** Spokes is a 6-year-old program bringing MIT students cycling across the country with Learning Festivals; lasting partnerships between rural communities and sponsors; close STEM opportunity gaps.

**Request summary:** roughly $9,000 of $25,000 goal raised. MSDF funds would improve programming quality (educational materials, hands-on supplies). Largest cost is transportation (8 bikes and a rental van).

**Success factors:** later teams return to the same locations and gather subjective evaluations; two-part survey to educators for short- and long-term impact; hope for long-term boost in STEM interest.
(Outcome not recorded.)
