**Request:** grant of $6,000 for the transit van, rented through Budget Rent A Car (estimated about $6,000 long-term). The van carries educational supplies, outdoor gear, personal items and food; one team member drives ahead scouting meal spots or cooking breakfast, lunch and dinner. A larger van is needed because each workshop has a construction component.

**Fit with Ford:** improving underrepresented populations' access to STEM.

**Objectives:** share STEM enthusiasm with middle schoolers; empower self-driven learners; change perception of scientists as athletic, active, adventurous.

**Timeline:** acquiring equipment as quickly as possible.

**Other contributors listed:** MIT Edgerton Center; Spokes America 2017; Texas Instruments; MathWorks.

Tracker note: Ford grant portal required mission statement, overview and major achievements; "likely won't hear back until July". No award recorded.
