# [Arrived in Carson City, NV!](http://spokesamerica15.tumblr.com/post/125647922014/arrived-in-carson-city-nv)

We biked 60 miles today and arrived at our Warm Showers host in Carson City. We did something incredibly cool, and that was biking on these tall bikes that a person in town had built (i.e. two frames stacked on top of each other). They were scary to get on, but so cool to bike on!
