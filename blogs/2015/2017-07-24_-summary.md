These last two days have been an experience. Nevada is bizarre terrain. Long flat expanses where the next thing (read tree or rock) you see might be 20 miles away and a huge mountain expanse in the distance. Day one was long 83 miles with nothing in between. Today (day two) was 65 miles with nothing. We’ve climbed a few mountains and made it to Ely, NV. Car issues are our main concern, but we will try to make it to Carson City– wish us luck!
