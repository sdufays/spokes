# SPOKES

> **2015** · 2015-08-01
> Went through Eureka, NV, and camped in Austin, NV, in a public park for a second night. They showered at the public pool. A 9 pm siren in Eureka turned out to be a curfew for under-18s.
> 🏠 **Slept:** Public park, Austin NV (second night; showers at public pool)
> - 📌 Austin, NV: a public park worked as free camping, and the public pool provided showers.
> - 📌 Eureka, NV has a 9 pm siren for an under-18 curfew; it sounds like an emergency alarm, so warn riders in advance.
> Tags: camping, route, place:nevada
