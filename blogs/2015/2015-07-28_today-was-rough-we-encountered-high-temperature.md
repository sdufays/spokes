# SPOKES

> **2015** · 2015-07-28 · 85 mi
> Hard day of about 85 miles with high heat, strong headwinds, and no towns to refill water after a late start. The next day was a 92-mile ride with no towns. The post does not name the location; it mentions grasshoppers in Kansas.
> - 📌 Late-summer heat and headwinds made an ~85-mile day with no water refills very hard; start early and check the wind forecast.
> - 📌 Carry extra water on 85-to-92-mile legs with no towns; the team had zero refill points for the next day's 92 miles.
