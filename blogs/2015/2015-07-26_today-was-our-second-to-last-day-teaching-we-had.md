# SPOKES

> **2015** · 2015-07-26
> Second-to-last festival day in Utah: another group of students and a successful festival. The team toured the Temple in Salt Lake City, then watched sunset at the Great Salt Lake; the next day's ride was Spanish Fork to Delta, UT.
> 🧪 **Learning festival:** Full-day festival with a new group of students; activities and location not described in this post (likely the Leonardo, Salt Lake City, from the previous post).
> - 📌 Salt Lake City Temple and Great Salt Lake (sunset, Black Rock viewpoint) were a good evening outing after a festival day.
