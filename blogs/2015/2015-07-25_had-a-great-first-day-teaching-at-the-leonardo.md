# SPOKES

> **2015** · 2015-07-25
> First festival day at the Leonardo in Salt Lake City (post dated July 24, 2015): two 6-hour workshops for about 36 students. The Rockets workshop could not run because of a fire ban in Salt Lake City.
> 🏠 **Slept:** John and Jill Kennedy's home (Harvard alums), Salt Lake City, UT (host)
> 🧪 **Learning festival:** Two 6-hour workshops at the Leonardo, Salt Lake City, about 36 students; the Rockets workshop was cancelled due to a fire ban. Another full-day festival was planned for the next day.
> - 📌 Salt Lake City had a fire ban in late July 2015 that cancelled the Rockets (fire-based) workshop; check local fire restrictions before planning activities that need flame.
> - 📌 The Leonardo (Salt Lake City) hosted a two-day festival format: two 6-hour workshops on day one, about 36 students.
> Tags: learning-festivals, housing, place:utah, weather
