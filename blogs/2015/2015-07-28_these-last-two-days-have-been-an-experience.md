# SPOKES

> **2015** · 2015-07-28 · Ely, NV
> Two long Nevada days: 83 miles with no services, then 65 miles with nothing in between, with several mountain climbs, ending in Ely, NV. Car issues were the team's main worry.
> - 📌 Nevada has stretches of 65 to 83 miles with no towns or services between; carry enough food and water and plan stops before leaving the last town.
> - 📌 Nevada terrain is long flat basins with landmarks up to 20 miles away, then mountain passes; expect several climbs between Ely and Carson City.
> - 📌 The support car had issues on the Nevada stretch (Ely to Carson City); budget time for vehicle trouble.
> Tags: route, terrain, transport-logistics, place:nevada
