# Final Countdown

> **2014** · 2014-06-08
> Evan reports the whole team has assembled at a host family's backyard in Menlo Park, CA, pitching tents and doing laundry, bike shop stop, packing the car and a short ride before departure.
> 🏠 **Slept:** Caroline Wang and family's backyard, Menlo Park, CA (family host; 2014-06-15 post says San Francisco)

[Final Countdown](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/88193890320/final-countdown "Final Countdown")

The team has finally assembled in Menlo Park just outside San Francisco in the home of an awesome Spokes family- Caroline, Mike and Christopher have given us free reign over the backyard! Tents pitched, brown and blue tarps cast aside, our upturned bicycles are enjoying the rays of California sunshine as we continue to unpack boxes of accessory supplies and First Aid kits. In fact, the lively banter has already begun.

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/ed8b3da6f19ef43f089cfd9f27317591/tumblr_inline_n6v4wvdlD61s3e7lq.jpg)

Breaking News: The tent I am sharing with Caleb and Sam shall now be deemed, “The Bear Pad.” (The rather massive, or “less practical” tent in the middle as Sam calls it). 

The plan for today is to make sure everyone has their individual items (panniers, lights, Leatherman’s, etc.), do a laundry, stop by the bike shop for last supplies, pack the car and do some riding!

The energy here is exciting, as the journey to come feels more and more real. Spending some time with Caroline and Christopher last night at the dinner table and Mike this morning, I am feeling all the more invigorated about the journey to come!

Get psyched, get Spokes,

Evan

P.S. In case anyone needs a book for the summer, the ones I plan on working through in the first month are: Team of Rivals; Faraday, Maxwell, and Electromagnetic Field; The Design of Everyday Things; Endurance: Adventures of Shackleton.
