# Days 4 & 5: Through the Mountains and Desert Bound

> **2014** · 2014-06-14 · Woodfords, CA → Carson City, NV
> Riders climb to Carson Pass in the Sierra Nevada, descend a steep grade toward Woodfords CA, and ride into Carson City NV, where three nights and a first library workshop are planned.

> 🧪 **Learning festival:** First teaching workshop planned at Carson City Public Library, the following Monday

[Days 4 & 5: Through the Mountains and Desert Bound](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/88772130570/days-4-5-through-the-mountains-and-desert-bound "Days 4 & 5: Through the Mountains and Desert Bound")

A few days ago, day 3 I think it was, my bike received its name. We were passing Shadowfax Rd in Folsom, CA. One of my favorite lines from Lord of the Rings—"Run, Shadowfax, show us the meaning of haste"— of course popped into my head. I called it out loud to Emma, who was biking just ahead of me. My noble steed has been christened. 

A few days later, we’ve covered a lot of mileage, climbed some beautiful hills, and enjoyed the first sweet rewards of plunging into the valleys on the far side of a long climb. We summited yesterday, after 3 days of rollings hills and then long, grinding climbing, at Carson Pass in California’s Sierra Nevadas, elevation 8600 ft or so. 

At the top, we bought postcards and chatted with park ranger volunteers, one of whom, Kevin, is an avid cyclist. He might catch us later on in the trip when he’s doing a ride over in Colorado. We exchanged contact info in the hopes of maybe taking a ride with him later on.

On the back side of the pass, we enjoyed an exhilirating 4 miles 8% grade descent. The bowls we dropped into were stunningly beautiful—photos to follow once I can find my camera buried in the car.

We camped last night just west of Woodfords, CA. We’re getting our routine down pretty well—wake up at 4 or 4:30 a.m. to beat the heat, ride out of camp at 5:15, and, if all goes well, arrive at the next night’s campsite by noon. Then make lunch, then nap, then make dinner, then sleep around 8:30, then repeat. The system is working well so far, even if it is a little cold at 4:30 a.m. Cold in the morning for breakfast is definitely better than hot in the afternoon while biking.

We arrive today in Carson City, NV, where we will be spending tonight, Sunday night, and Monday night. We’ll be doing our first teaching at the Carson City Public Library on Monday, as well! Sunday will be spent putting our finishing touches on lessons.

Now that we’re into the meat of the trip, as well, we want to again thank our awesome sponsors—IAC, Texas Instruments, It’s About Time, and as well as Harvard SEAS and MIT—for making this spectacular experience possible.

Time to get back in the car and catch the day’s cyclists, who are now ahead of me! 

From Genoa, NV, the oldest settlement in Nevada, en route to Carson City,

Sam & Team Spokes 2014
