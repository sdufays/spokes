# Who drives the van?

> **2014** · 2014-06-11
> Answers a reader question: four team members with licenses alternate driving the van so everyone gets a rest day.
> - 📌 Van driving rota: four licensed team members alternate driving days; a driving day doubles as a rest day for the rider, so the whole team does not need to rest at once
> Tags: transport-logistics, team-roles

![Asker](http://assets.tumblr.com/images/anonymous_avatar_16.gif) Anonymous Asks:

Who drives the van?

![spokesamerica](http://31.media.tumblr.com/avatar_1caeada0d194_16.png) spokesamerica Said:

Four of us have drivers’ licenses and are alternating driving. That seemed the fairest way to do it and gives everyone a chance for a rest day without the whole team having to break for a day.
