# It builds character

> **2014** · 2014-05-19
> Jamin reflects on his fear of stepping outside his comfort zone before the trip and hopes the ride will build character and inspire students to try new things.
> Tags: reflection, press-media

[It builds character](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/86187025055/it-builds-character "It builds character")

To be perfectly honest, I am a bit of a wimp.

I’m saying that in the nicest way possible. It’s simply the way I am in my natural state: I am not particularly courageous (the thought of stepping outside my comfort zone is petrifying); I don’t like change, or the idea of putting myself under a spotlight. I often tell myself that it would be nice to just stay the way I am, have the world stay the way it is, and for me to just blend into the background.

So how did I end up here? What could have possibly possessed me to think that I would be able to bike across the country? And teach!

That’s what I want to know.

A few days ago, I sheepishly admitted to a friend that even though I have been working as the treasurer for Spokes for over three months now, the fact that I will be traveling by bike across the US hasn’t really sunk in yet. And even then, I was still terrified. Of what, he had asked. There were a myriad of things: of eating oatmeal everyday, of being dehydrated, of biking into a pole. I didn’t like the thought of my bum going numb from 8 hours of sitting on a stiff bike seat. But, above all else, I was scared that I would still be the last rider on the road and that at the end of the day, the van would have to backtrack to pick me up… except I’d be lost in the wilderness and I would have to eat bugs or something to survive.

You’re being silly, my friend laughed. And who cares if you have to be picked up by car at the end of the day? Does it really matter that much?

That was when I realized that what I was actually afraid of isn’t so much being uncomfortable or getting lost or even eating insects (thought that would be less than desirable). I was afraid of stepping outside my comfort zone. I was afraid that once I tried by best, I would come to realize that it’s still not enough. And it is so uncomfortable to admit that I am scared of trying because I am scared of failing.

So, again, why am I doing this? Why don’t I just step back into my little bubble? No one would call me out on that.

Because it sucks being afraid.

I don’t expect that biking across the country would make me undergo some majestic change such that by the end of the journey, I would suddenly be more courageous and less afraid of failure. But I figured it would build character and that might just be what I need. And maybe, just maybe, by going on this trip, we can inspire a student or two to be less afraid of trying something new.

– Jamin
