I’m going to take a moment to scold myself and apologize for the insane amount of time that has passed since I last updated you all on the status of Spokes. BAD CLAIRE. So much has happened with our team since the last update: we’ve had a run in with a car (but everyone’s ok!), lost and gained and lost a team member, taught three times, built a trebuchet and a waffle-making battle bear out of LEGOs, passed under the St.Louis arch (actually, we went around it - grass isn’t our bikes’ favorite terrain - but one way or another, we’re now in what’s considered “the east”), gone over the Missouri and Mississippi rivers, had a 21st birthday, visited a winery, followed the tracks of Lewis and Clark, swam in a “swimming hole” that really much more resembled a swamp, had a couple of run-ins with venomous snakes (much to Turner’s delight :)), milked goats (to Ethan’s EXTREME delight :P), had a goat in the car (sorry Dad), JUMPED HORSES!!!!! (bet you can’t guess who was excited about that one), met Kaitlyn and Turner’s parents (and eaten lots of Mexican food with both of them, as well as delicious Icelandic chocolates and Polish pastries), and met a whole lot of incredibly wonderful people. Our current whereabouts? Murphysboro, IL, which is <1,000 miles due east of DC! However, in true Spokes style, we’ll be taking a detour south to Nashville before making the final push to DC. The end really feels like it’s in sight though, and it’s weird! We’re all so excited to get back and see friends and family, but at the same time, we’ve become so accustomed to this way of life, each other, and the amazing families that we’ve been staying with that it’s hard to imagine life after Spokes. Safe to say, I absolutely love being on the trip, and can’t imagine anything else that I would want to do at this moment! 

Because so much has happened, I’m going to write a series of posts so you don’t have to commit to reading a novel. Before that, though, I figured I’d post a couple of recent team pictures for your viewing pleasure :)
