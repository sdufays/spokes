# When we hit Cedar City, UT, a couple of us just...

> **2013** · 2013-06-27
> At Cedar City, UT, a couple of team members stopped at Starbucks for Ethan's birthday mocha, and a local woman, Nancy, gave the team a gift card for future stops.
> - 📌 Starbucks in Cedar City, UT is a coffee stop on the route; Nancy's gift card was redeemed at a Starbucks in Montrose, CO on July 5.
> Tags: local-people, food-nutrition, place:utah

When we hit Cedar City, UT, a couple of us just couldn’t resist hitting up the Starbucks. It was Ethan’s birthday a couple of days before, and he was very much in need of a birthday mocha. We’re so glad that we went, because we met a great new friend, Nancy, who very kindly bought the team a Starbucks giftcard to use the next time that we come across one on our journey! More trail magic. :)
