# Miles of desert....and chocolate pudding jelly beans :)

> **2013** · 2013-06-22 · Ely, NV
> Over one week the team crossed Nevada on US-50 ('Loneliest Road in America'), received trail magic in Genoa, Middlegate and Ely, and ended the week with a host for the night in Ely.
> 🏠 **Slept:** Thomas and Jen's home, Ely, NV (offered after the team met Thomas at Economy Drugs)
> - 📌 US-50 in Nevada ('Loneliest Road in America'): over 300 miles from the state line with very few services; plan food and water for long gaps.
> - 📌 Middlegate, NV (population 17) has fast internet and giant burgers; a welcome stop on US-50.
> - 📌 Economy Drugs in Ely, NV sells espresso and general store items; Ely (about 4,000 people) was the biggest town in several days.

[Miles of desert….and chocolate pudding jelly beans :)](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/53555622870/miles-of-desertand-chocolate-pudding-jelly "Miles of desert….and chocolate pudding jelly beans :\)")

If I had to use two words to sum up the past week, I think they would have to be vastness and kindness :)

In Genoa, NV (the first settlement in NV, and the first place we encountered after crossing the border), we learned of a concept called trail magic: we were taking a break in a deli, all looking a bit worn out after both the final push over the Sierras (photo below of us at the top, receiving apples and cantaloupe from the park rangers!) and our first taste of the Nevada headwinds, when a woman approached us to ask what we were up to. After hearing the Spokes story, she insisted on buying us all free ice cream! She proceeded to tell us of a couple who had been walking from Mexico to Vancouver, who had coined the term “trail magic” after noticing how many people had offered help in one way or another on their trip. 

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/fcebc1ac4bd7fc4ff5a5f6ac93e8167e/tumblr_inline_morp5cRkcg1qz4rgp.png)

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/a8edc1d81ad3ba2608654c66200c4c49/tumblr_inline_morpvvkbxR1qz4rgp.jpg)

Since crossing the border, our team has traveled over 300 miles on Highway 50, aka “the Lonliest Road in America,” but despite the emptiness of the route, the trail magic hasn’t stopped by any means. One of our first stops along this road was at a place called Middlegate, where there is a total population of 17 people. There, we met some people walking across America! Talk about commitment - looks like their journey is going to end up taking 7 months! We quite enjoyed the facilities as Middlegate, including the surprisingly quick internet and, for the non-vegetarians among the group who had eaten just about enough of beans and oatmeal…….giant burgers :) The people who hosted us there really made us feel at home - quite the feat considering that we were surrounded by nothing more than sand and cows!

We’re currently sitting on the back porch of our new friends, Thomas and Jen in Ely, NV: the biggest town we’ve hit in days with 4,000 people. We met Thomas early this morning in a Economy drugs (sells everything, from espresso (!!!!!!!!), to standard drug store things, to chocolate pudding jelly beans, and when he learned of our quest for wifi, offered his home to us! We’ve had an amazing time getting to know this wonderful couple, as well as the kids next door…and their chickens :) 

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/9b307562eff948599180bfddf4fa504b/tumblr_inline_morqf9dvE01qz4rgp.jpg)

One more day in Nevada, and then we’ll be into Utah!!
