# The first part of our journey!

> **2013** · 2013-06-13 · Vallejo, CA
> The team ran its first two Learning Festivals in Oakland (Skyline High and Edna Brewer Middle), took the ferry from San Francisco to Vallejo to start riding, and camped at Lake Solano on night one while the van crew set up.
> 🏠 **Slept:** Lake Solano campground, CA (camping night 1; van crew set up camp)
> 🧪 **Learning festival:** Oakland, CA. Day 1 at Skyline High in Michelle Shankar's chemistry room (four one-hour periods): EyeWire brain-mapping game (Claire), procedurally generated plants (Ethan), The Science of Music (Jeff), mousetrap car (Kaitlyn). Day 2 at Edna Brewer Middle School with 12-13 year olds: four classes taught twice, plus Titiaan's grow-your-own-plants-in-a-bottle class. Students started a gardening club.
> - 📌 Expect to change lesson programs on the fly: LF #2 used a different program with younger kids (12-13) than the 14-18 target audience, and it worked.
> - 📌 The ferry from San Francisco to Vallejo was used as the first leg of the ride, with the van and the rest of the team going separately.
> - 📌 Davis, CA: Cloud Top Espresso and Juice was used as a mid-ride break stop.
> Tags: learning-festivals, curriculum, camping, route, transport-logistics, place:california

[The first part of our journey!](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/52833789069/the-first-part-of-our-journey "The first part of our journey!")

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/1ba5da3df088642803ac73646e35d26e/tumblr_inline_mob5ynpYsP1qz4rgp.jpg)

The Spokes journey has officially started! We’re excited to finally be together and on the road!

Our very first Learning Festival took place at Skyline high school in Oakland last Monday. We were kindly hosted in the chemistry classroom of Michelle Shankar, introduced to us by Teach for America’s Joshua Guiterrez and Melissa Moritz. For four periods of an hour each, a Spokes team member taught a workshop:

  * Claire O’Connell: EyeWire - a game to map the brain 

  * Ethan Sherbondy: Procedurally generated plants

  * Jeff Prouty: The Science of Music

  * Kaitlyn Bailey: How to build a mousetrap car?




The first teaching day served as a great learning experience for us all: we discovered how to engage students and received valuable feedback from Michelle on teaching didactics. 

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/dbf45caa529560b8f725a48a46018ae1/tumblr_inline_mob5zrGx841qz4rgp.jpg)

Later on Monday evening, [we hosted friends and family for the Spokes launch party at San Francisco’s beautiful Crissy Field](http://tmblr.co/ZDPEGsn4nQov). Friends and family from the Bay Area (and some from further away!) got together to meet the team, check out the bikes (including Nathan’s, which has been named and marked Supafly, in hot pink :)), and give last minute tips and hugs. From high school teachers, to horseback riding trainers, to friends from Italy, we had a diverse crowd out to see us off! We all had a great time meeting each other’s friends and catching up with our own. :) 

![](http://31.media.tumblr.com/dd5c72d2cf64e688c64b8939212c5d5c/tumblr_inline_mob5qkbrSK1qz4rgp.jpg)

_Jeff’s “Science of Music” class at Edna Brewer Middle School_

On Tuesday morning, we taught our second Learning Festival at the Edna Brewer middle school - again, in Oakland. The program of this festival was different than the program of the day before - good practice for altering lessons on the fly! At Learning Festival number two, we taught four different classes twice:

  * Ethan Sherbondy: Procedurally generated plants 

  * Jeff Prouty: The Science of Music 

  * Kaitlyn Bailey: How to build a mousetrap car?



  * Titiaan Palazzi: How to grow your own plants (in a bottle)? 




Despite being younger (12 - 13 year olds) than Spokes’ target audience (14 - 18 years old), students showed great initiative! In fact, students decided to start a gardening club after Titiaan’s vegetable class - possibly sparked more by his Dutch accent than class content!

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/eadb2c57b8ac93e52a5476cbe7903115/tumblr_inline_mob5rzPcqu1qz4rgp.jpg)

_Waiting for the ferry from San Francisco to Vallejo_  


On Tuesday afternoon, Kaitlyn, Titiaan, Jeff, Ethan and Nathan took the ferry from San Francisco to Vallejo, where they began the very first leg of the journey. In the meantime, Turner, Phillip and Claire packed up the van that is accompanying us on the journey, picked up some head lamps, filled Camelbacks (which we love!!), and headed to Lake Solano campground to set up for camping night #1. We were surprised to find peacocks! 

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/cb831447bfc45428f0b1b7e2c59c361f/tumblr_inline_mob5vmEtpE1qz4rgp.jpg)

_Camping ground, first night - set up by Turner & Phillip_

This morning, we cooked our first campground meal together and got on the road! We’re now taking a quick break from biking in Davis, CA at the Cloud Top Espresso and Juice bar to work on blog and video material, as we have our first Gopro footage of the team in action! Learning festival #3 tomorrow in Sacramento will be a bbq/demo event, and we can’t wait! After that, 40 miles to the base of the Sierras - and that’s where the fun really begins :) 

Updates will follow!
