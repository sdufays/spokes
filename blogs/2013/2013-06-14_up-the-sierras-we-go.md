[Up the Sierras we go! ](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/52969338865/up-the-sierras-we-go "Up the Sierras we go! ")

The spokes are rolling, and we’re having a ton of fun! :) At 5am on Wednesday morning, after staying at the KOA campground in Sacramento, our team headed out for a 10 mile ride to the PS7 middle school, which shares its campus with Sac high. The students were enjoying their very last day of school by rotating through a number of activities, and Spokes classes became part of that. 3 Spokes team members taught: 

  * Claire: EyeWire - a game to map the brain
  * Titiaan: Grow you own spouts in a cup
  * Kaitlyn: How to make a mousetrap car



We were really excited by how engaged the kids were with our activities, and it was heartwarming to hear a number of “come back next year, this was fun!” They really wanted to stay in touch, which has a number of us thinking about how we can get our mentorship program started asap - stay tuned for updates! 

![image](http://33.media.tumblr.com/74c28f8850de731ca2a8d391dc32211b/tumblr_inline_moedpc9Swh1qz4rgp.jpg)

Right after teaching, our team hopped on our bikes to head out of Sacramento toward the Sierras! I think we all kind of thought it would be like this…

![image](http://38.media.tumblr.com/eacb60d00e89b2e7758cc0bf3fd04533/tumblr_inline_moefc0K00U1qz4rgp.jpg)

…but tired as we were when we finally arrived at our campground just outside of Placerville, CA, the hills (so far) have proven doable :) 

We all were SO. HUNGRY. after the first ride on hills - talk about bottomless pits :P We’re managing to get by on a small food budget though, with lots of oatmeal, sandwiches, pasta, and our newest discovery: eggs! 

We’re about to head out of the Cozmic cafe in the super cute (and yes, those are Claire’s words ;)) town of Placerville, and internet access isn’t looking too reliable in the coming days. Never fear though - we’ll post another update soon! 

OH. Important. Bike names so far: 

  * Nathan’s: Supafly
  * Ethan’s: Mochamotion



And on the topic of mochas, we had mocha oatmeal this morning. Titiaan said it was “a bit like waking up to a chocolate bar,” so I fear I may have gone a bit overboard on the chocolate powder…live and learn :P 

Peace y’all!
