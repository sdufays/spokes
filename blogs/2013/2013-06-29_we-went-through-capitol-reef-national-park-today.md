# We went through Capitol Reef National Park today,...

> **2013** · 2013-06-29 · 2800 ft climbing
> The team rode through Capitol Reef National Park in 107-degree heat after a 2,800-ft climb to start the day, and reached 1,000 total miles.
> - 📌 Capitol Reef National Park (Utah): a 2,800-ft climb to start the day, then a scenic downhill through the park; at 107 degrees, start early.
> Tags: terrain, sightseeing, weather, place:utah, place:capitol-reef

We went through Capitol Reef National Park today, and it was, in Titiaan’s words, “epic!” Following a 2,800 foot climb to kickstart the day, the downhill route through the park was like being on an incredibly scenic roller coaster. The only downside? It was 107 degrees…thanks Utah! :P Exciting news though – Spokes hit 1,000 miles today!
