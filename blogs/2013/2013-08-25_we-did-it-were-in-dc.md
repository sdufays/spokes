# We did it! We're in DC!

> **2013** · 2013-08-25 · Washington, DC
> The team rode into Washington, DC at 11:24am on August 24, 2013, finishing the Pacific-to-Atlantic ride (post says more than 6,000 km), and planned celebrations, thank-you cards and a post-trip reflection series.
> - 📌 Food on the trip, per post: banana-honey-oatmeal, peanut butter sandwiches and pasta.
> Tags: route, food-nutrition, reflection, team-dynamics, place:washington-dc

[We did it! We’re in DC!](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/59304393351/we-did-it-were-in-dc "We did it! We’re in DC!")

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/fd5ae5ebb22523ac29707477ecf64628/tumblr_inline_ms3f4qcn6Y1qz4rgp.jpg)

We did it! Yesterday at 11.24am we rode into DC - what an amazing feeling! Exactly 50 years after Martin Luther King’s famous speech, we completed our own dream!

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/e7e30e73a45e080612eb8c11efdbd17d/tumblr_inline_ms3euft4JN1qz4rgp.jpg)

We cycled more than 6,000 kilometers from the Pacific to the Atlantic. We lived on banana-honey-oatmeal, peanut butter sandwiches and pasta. We fell asleep under starry skies and woke by the light of the sun (or rain, or sprinklers). It was an amazing adventure with an incredible team.**Find Titiaan’s best pictures[here](http://www.flickr.com/photos/75813579@N08/sets/72157634375352263/)!**

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/62b1dd61390cb28b119df1b6031dee70/tumblr_inline_ms3ewocH331qz4rgp.png)

Time to celebrate! We are meeting up with friends; cooking non-pasta dinners; writing thank you cards to all our supporters and remembering the epic experience we had. 

**Expect lots of reflections, cool videos and ideas for the future of Spokes!**
