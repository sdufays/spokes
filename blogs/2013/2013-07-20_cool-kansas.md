# Cool Kansas :)

> **2013** · 2013-07-20
> The team enjoyed the flat, scenic Kansas riding, visited the Cosmosphere in Hutchinson, had a bike repaired in Newton, and planned to meet an MIT friend who would guest-ride to St. Louis.
> - 📌 Newton, KS: a shop repaired a severely bent derailleur and the bike rode smoothly afterward.
> - 📌 Kansas heat: the team rode in the evening to avoid the worst of the day's heat.
> - 📌 Cosmosphere museum in Hutchinson, KS (aviation and space exhibits) was a good sightseeing stop.
> - 📌 All-you-can-eat buffets were used as cheap meals on the route in Kansas.
> - 📌 Kansas City was the next planned teaching stop.

[Cool Kansas :) ](http://spokesamerica.tumblr.com/post/55994276391/cool-kansas "Cool Kansas :\) ")

![](http://31.media.tumblr.com/9118df3bfecdb391d4b6499bd06e0544/tumblr_inline_mq97yiivrF1qz4rgp.jpg)

After the incredible scenery and riding in the first half of our trip, I think that we were all a little sad when mountainous Colorado began to disappear and we made our way eastward toward Kansas. Sure, flat riding sounded like a nice change in the physical sense (although I’ve developed a weird enjoyment of steep, long hills :P), but we thought that we were going to be in for a long bout of nothing in terms of scenery. I’ve actually loved our time in this state so far, and the riding has been beautiful :) 

Part of what has made the past few days so enjoyable has been the company that I’ve had while on the bike. One day, Titiaan, Ethan and I rode together for a good 50 miles, talking about neuroscience, childhood art projects, parenting (it sounds hard!), and just getting to know more about each other. Phillip, Titiaan and I had a long talk about ethics of meat productions (all of the cattle and a recent roadkill incident had ethical treatment of animals on our minds), and the team has decided to begin a biking book club! We’re all listening to the same book from Audible.com (currently Richard Dawkin’s _The Selfish Gene_), and plan to discuss our thoughts about it while on the road. 

Also noteworthy: we hit up our second all-you-can-eat buffet, and Nathan got creative ;) Davy Jone, pudding edition: 

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/2387c31f5be55c4922b6596010079376/tumblr_inline_mq97x5N2jG1qz4rgp.jpg)

One of our KS stops was Hutchinson, aka Smallville, which is Superman’s hometown on Earth! Noor, our new video pro (and stand-up-at-breakfast provider, and amazing dinner chef - have I mentioned we love him?), had the excellent idea of getting Superman capes from Walmart for us to wear while he got some footage, and we had a great time being pretend-movie-stars. :) 

![](http://33.media.tumblr.com/af6fa6af8ac7764fc50beba322c79826/tumblr_inline_mq99o9JAf11qz4rgp.png)

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/14bc133246fd5554679ce3a1530a3859/tumblr_inline_mq99oiaiFu1qz4rgp.png)

Noor took most of the footage at an museum called the [Cosmosphere](http://www.cosmo.org/), and we took a look at some of the planes and exhibits inside before hitting the road for the day. I couldn’t resist: 

![](http://31.media.tumblr.com/574be1d40dd1cfac502811a70768102b/tumblr_inline_mq99ot4HKX1qz4rgp.jpg)

Next stop was Newton, where my bike, which had sustained some mystery injuries (most notably, a severely bent derailer) got fixed up. So smooth now. :) 

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/93d1d9e93a084dac123822d44cfe58bb/tumblr_inline_mq97xf97SG1qz4rgp.jpg)

A few shots of what riding looks like. This was the first photo I actually took ON the bike: 

![](http://38.media.tumblr.com/57b621b579eb6d9821bceca7217328e9/tumblr_inline_mq97xvwklS1qz4rgp.jpg)

![](http://31.media.tumblr.com/8a66934c3a5d67e72e3d2863042a075e/tumblr_inline_mq97y3Cbog1qz4rgp.jpg)

Evening riding to avoid the heat: 

![](http://31.media.tumblr.com/dcdac0d9b699e86209e0cff6cd0187a0/tumblr_inline_mq97yaiWjc1qz4rgp.jpg)

We’ll be in Kansas city in a few days, our next teaching spot, and we’ll be meeting Chase Lambert, a good friend from MIT, who will “guest-cycle” with us to St. Louis. We can’t wait!
