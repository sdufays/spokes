### 2013-07-01 — [So. many. degrees. !!!](../2013/2013-07-01_so-many-degrees.md) — Hite, UT → Blanding, UT
On a 108-degree day the team rode Hanksville to Hite, UT, sheltered in shade until a 2 a.m. night ride of about 80 miles to Blanding, UT. The post also notes Nathan's video is out and the team is about to cross into Colorado.
- 📌 Hite, UT (Colorado River recreation area): the convenience store closes at 3:30 p.m., and the next services are about 80 miles away toward Blanding; stock up on water and food before. *(2013)*
- 📌 Hanksville to Hite is 50 miles of exposed desert with shade only at the base of the canyons. *(2013)*
- 📌 Heat: 108 degrees before noon at Hite; night riding (starting at 2 a.m.) worked. Plan early or night starts for Utah desert stretches in summer. *(2013)*
- 📌 The map-based climb profile was misleading: the final stretch before Blanding (miles 60-80) was the steepest climb yet. Check elevation profiles, not just mileage. *(2013)*
- 📌 Pat and Dan offered the team use of their motel pool in Blanding, UT. *(2013)*

### 2013-07-20 — [Cool Kansas :)](../2013/2013-07-20_cool-kansas.md)
The team enjoyed the flat, scenic Kansas riding, visited the Cosmosphere in Hutchinson, had a bike repaired in Newton, and planned to meet an MIT friend who would guest-ride to St. Louis.
- 📌 Newton, KS: a shop repaired a severely bent derailleur and the bike rode smoothly afterward. *(2013)*
- 📌 Kansas heat: the team rode in the evening to avoid the worst of the day's heat. *(2013)*
- 📌 Cosmosphere museum in Hutchinson, KS (aviation and space exhibits) was a good sightseeing stop. *(2013)*
