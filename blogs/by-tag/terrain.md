- 📌 Altitude: an EMT check in Fairplay showed Ethan's oxygen saturation was low while Jeff's was high. Take altitude symptoms seriously after Monarch Pass. *(2013)*
- 📌 Fairplay, CO: Java Moose cafe has wifi, excellent espresso and chai almonds, a good stop before the Bailey stretch. *(2013)*
- 📌 About 40 miles from Fairplay to Bailey, the team had no pre-booked camping because they had left the Adventure Cycling route to attend Spokes events; plan hosts or campsites in advance for off-route stretches. *(2013)*
- 📌 A derailleur worked loose on hills near Bailey and fell into the chain; with no proper tool or expertise, the team had to hitchhike for help. Carry the right tool and know the nearest bike shop. *(2013)*
- 📌 Cutthroat Cafe in Bailey, CO: owner offered the team indoor sleeping and breakfast; cafe hosting is a useful lodging option. *(2013)*
- 📌 Montrose to Sargent (July 5) was 106 miles, including a 6-mile detour for the Montrose Starbucks. *(2013)*
