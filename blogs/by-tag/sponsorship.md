### 2026-07-08 — [Day 38: So… steak for dinner?](../2026/2026-07-08_day-38-so-steak-for-dinner.md) — Lamar, CO → Lamar, CO

- 📌 Monster Genome leaves felt scraps everywhere and iodine clock needs one beaker per student; plan extra cleanup time. *(2026)*

### 2022-09-01 — [Day 80: Oh, the Places You'll Go!](../2022/2022-09-01_day-80-oh-the-places-you-ll-go.md) — Vacaville, CA → San Francisco, CA
Final ride, Vacaville to San Francisco (73.2 miles, 1,909 ft of gain), finishing over the Golden Gate Bridge at sunset with friends and family waiting; the post also tallies trip totals and names foam sleeping pads as the team's MVP.
- 📌 Foam sleeping pads were the team's MVP: a seat at lunch, a sleep pad, rain shelter, blanket, pillow, and car sunshade. Pack foam pads. *(2022)*
- 📌 Trip totals for five riders over about 80 days: Clif bars ~500, electrolyte tablets/packets ~500, peanut butter jars 10+ (some Costco-size), chamois butter packets ~10 (mostly unused), ice cream stops 10+, 8 state lines. Useful for food budgeting. *(2022)*
- 📌 A tiny hand pump got stuck and released all its air while the team helped cyclists in Sausalito. Carry a reliable pump. *(2022)*
- 📌 Bay Area suburbs had few public restrooms: the team tried three gas stations and a coffee shop, and ended at a Whole Foods. Plan restroom and water stops for the last stretch. *(2022)*
- 📌 The Golden Gate Bridge had gusts near 20 mph; bring a wind layer for the crossing. *(2022)*
