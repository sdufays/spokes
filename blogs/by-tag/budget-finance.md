### 2026-07-14 — [Day 44: MIT Paddles](../2026/2026-07-14_day-44-mit-paddles.md) — Idaho Springs, CO → Idaho Springs, CO
Rest day in Idaho Springs: a white water rafting and kayaking trip about 1.5 hours away (Class 2 rapids, with the kayaks spinning and flipping), a 30-foot cliff jump, and a food hall dinner. In the evening the team spent 2 to 3 hours on expense spreadsheets.
- 🏠 Slept: Airbnb in Idaho Springs CO (sauna and hot tub)

- 📌 Keep receipts and log expenses daily; the team spent hours reconciling the trip's receipts. *(2026)*

### 2025-08-22 — [Day 79: Dilly Dallying One Last Time](../2025/2025-08-22_day-79-dilly-dallying-one-last-time.md) — Humbug Mountain State Park, OR → Seattle, WA

- 📌 Cape Kiwanda food was expensive; Tillamook is the cheaper food stop. *(2025)*
- 📌 Tillamook Creamery was about to close when they arrived in the afternoon. Arrive earlier. *(2025)*
- 📌 Van with bike rack backed out of a Tillamook parking spot and pierced another car's tail lights. Note left, owner filed an insurance claim. Check rack clearance and back out slowly. *(2025)*
- 📌 Coast day of about 485 mi with stops ran past 3 pm. Plan fewer stops on long coastal driving days. *(2025)*

### 2024-09-12 — [Day 75: The REAL Spokes OGs](../2024/2024-09-12_day-75-the-real-spokes-ogs.md) — San Francisco, CA → San Francisco, CA
Rest/logistics day in San Francisco (0 mi): the team finished packing bikes at REI, struggled to ship them cheaply (FedEx and UPS were expensive, and REI boxes were oversized), then had dinner with original Spokes founder Turner and alumni Nathan and Kaitlyn, held a Spokes party, and some riders got matching tattoos of the route.
- 🏠 Slept: Friend's apartment, San Francisco CA (a rider's friend)
- 📌 Do not ship bikes from SF in REI boxes: they are huge and you pay to ship air. Costs were high at FedEx and UPS; Bike Flights made the shipping labels and saved money, but volume pricing was still expensive. *(2024)*
- 📌 Strong recommendation from this team: drive the rental car back to Boston with the bikes and gear. It is cheaper (no shipping and cheaper round-trip rental) and less complex. Past Spokes teams did this. *(2024)*
- 📌 REI SF will store half-packed bikes overnight if you cannot finish before closing, and staff will let you come back the next morning. *(2024)*
- 📌 Original Spokes origin: founder Turner recruited everyone he knew, applied for nearly every MIT grant, and ran a Kickstarter; the first trip happened about two years after the idea. *(2024)*
- 📌 Advice from alumni Rafael (met at Lake Tahoe): hold reunions with teammates; they share your context. The team planned to see each other at future weddings and reunions. *(2024)*

### 2022-09-01 — [Day 80: Oh, the Places You'll Go!](../2022/2022-09-01_day-80-oh-the-places-you-ll-go.md) — Vacaville, CA → San Francisco, CA
Final ride, Vacaville to San Francisco (73.2 miles, 1,909 ft of gain), finishing over the Golden Gate Bridge at sunset with friends and family waiting; the post also tallies trip totals and names foam sleeping pads as the team's MVP.
- 📌 Foam sleeping pads were the team's MVP: a seat at lunch, a sleep pad, rain shelter, blanket, pillow, and car sunshade. Pack foam pads. *(2022)*
- 📌 Trip totals for five riders over about 80 days: Clif bars ~500, electrolyte tablets/packets ~500, peanut butter jars 10+ (some Costco-size), chamois butter packets ~10 (mostly unused), ice cream stops 10+, 8 state lines. Useful for food budgeting. *(2022)*
- 📌 A tiny hand pump got stuck and released all its air while the team helped cyclists in Sausalito. Carry a reliable pump. *(2022)*
- 📌 Bay Area suburbs had few public restrooms: the team tried three gas stations and a coffee shop, and ended at a Whole Foods. Plan restroom and water stops for the last stretch. *(2022)*
- 📌 The Golden Gate Bridge had gusts near 20 mph; bring a wind layer for the crossing. *(2022)*
