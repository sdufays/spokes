### 2026-06-24 — [Day 24: 100 Things I Love About Spokes](../2026/2026-06-24_day-24-100-things-i-love-about-spokes.md) — Murphysboro, IL → St. Louis, MO
First century (101.1 miles) from Murphysboro IL to St. Louis MO, leaving by 7am, a team record start. Rest stops at a Lutheran church, a Dairy Queen and a coffee shop; the last stretch ran on the paved Metro Bike Link Trail past the Gateway Arch.
- 🏠 Slept: Alex's second apartment, St. Louis MO (host Alexander)
- 📌 Metro Bike Link Trail is paved and scenic into downtown St. Louis. *(2026)*
- 📌 Crossing the main bridge into St. Louis with cars right beside the riders was scary; look for a safer approach or a path. *(2026)*
- 📌 Chamois butter reapplied behind a building at the third rest stop prevented saddle chafing on century day. *(2026)*
- 📌 A tailwind made the century much faster than usual; plan for days when the wind is in your favor. *(2026)*
- 📌 MIT auditors reviewing team transactions added friction; keep receipts and transaction records organized as you go. *(2026)*


## 2024

### 2024-06-10 — [Day 4: Parallel Universes on the Spokes Team](../2024/2024-06-10_day-6.md) — Linden, VA → Shenandoah National Park, VA
Note: this file is the Day 4 post (title and date say Day 4 and 2024-06-10; the filename says day-6). Linden VA to Shenandoah NP: a 60.9-mile, 7,569 ft day where the support van's hybrid rental failed on Skyline Drive, leaving the support crew stranded for hours until a tow, a replacement rental, and a truck to move camping gear were arranged. Riders reached Big Meadows Campground after dark.
- 🏠 Slept: Big Meadows Campground, Shenandoah NP (camping). Support crew slept at Heather's home near Dulles after a 1am rental pickup.
- 📌 Only the primary driver on a company rental can authorize swaps and tows, so the primary driver must be present or reachable; add all riders as authorized drivers before leaving. *(2024)*
- 📌 Keep the support vehicle and camping gear with the riding group or within reach; the team had no camp gear for the night after the van broke down. *(2024)*
- 📌 Skyline Drive has an Appalachian Trail resupply point that sells MREs and soft serve. *(2024)*
- 📌 Front Royal VA: a Taco Bell parking lot was used as a waiting spot during a two-hour tow wait; the hybrid Chrysler Pacifica rental had an electrical fault that needed a mechanic's reset. *(2024)*
