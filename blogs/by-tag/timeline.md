## 2022

### 2022-08-16 — [Day 75: Last Day of Teaching, First Day of School](../2022/2022-08-16_day-75-last-day-of-teaching-first-day-of-school.md) — Fallon, NV → Fallon, NV
Last learning festival of the trip, at the Fallon Youth Club on a Sunday with low turnout (schools started the next day); the team reflected that their workshops moved from content-heavy to hands-on and engagement-driven.
- 📌 Circuits workshop lesson: 1.5-hour sessions were too short for younger students to retain advanced concepts like parallel circuits and Ohm's law. Skip most slides and go straight to hands-on building (add LEDs or buzzers, let kids pick LED colors). *(2022)*
- 📌 Timing: schools in Fallon NV started the day after this LF, so late-August events drew low turnout. Book learning festivals around the school calendar, and check when local schools start. *(2022)*
- 📌 Engagement beat content in every workshop (soap-bubble structural color demo; CAD demo where kids chose the design). Plan activities kids can make and keep. *(2022)*
- 📌 With one team member away, the team could only run part of their workshop lineup; plan for a missing team member by keeping workshops that one person can run alone. *(2022)*
