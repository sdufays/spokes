### 2013-01-31 — [Wilderness First Aid and The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants](../2013/2013-01-31_wilderness-first-aid-and-the-algorithmic-beauty-of.md)
Ethan Sherbondy took a two-day Wilderness First Aid course at Boston College to prepare for the ride, and shared first-aid takeaways such as not giving aspirin for a suspected stroke and cooling heat stroke without causing shivering. He also signed up to teach an intro L-systems plant-modeling class for MIT Spark.
- 📌 Two-day Wilderness First Aid course (SOLO field guide) was held at Boston College, a possible pre-trip training option for future riders. *(2013)*
- 📌 First-aid reminders from the course: do not give aspirin for a suspected stroke; designate one leader in an emergency; cool heat-stroke patients but avoid shivering, which raises body temperature. *(2013)*
