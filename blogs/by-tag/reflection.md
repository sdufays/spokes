### 2024-09-05 — [Day 74: THE FINALE](../2024/2024-09-05_day-74-the-finale.md) — Benicia, CA → San Francisco, CA
Final riding day from Benicia to San Francisco (42.3 mi, 2,513 ft): the team rode together, crossed three Bay Area bridges and a steep climb after Corte Madera, then reached the Golden Gate finish and spent the afternoon packing and shipping bikes at the San Francisco REI, which stayed open past closing while they finished.
- 🏠 Slept: Tony and Max's home, Benicia CA (host)
- 📌 Bay Area bridge day: Alfred Zampa Memorial Bridge (tall railing), then Richmond-San Rafael Bridge (longest, gradual climb, bike path with traffic running on a lower deck), then Golden Gate Bridge. *(2024)*
- 📌 Golden Gate Bridge bike path was crowded with pedestrians, so riders were braking more than pedaling; wind was less bad than feared. *(2024)*
- 📌 Steep climb after Corte Madera (turn onto Sausalito St): about 0.4 mi with up to 14% grade, Garmin max 24.9%. Lowest gear and steady pace worked; the riding-down twisty descent after it is a good skills check. *(2024)*
- 📌 A fence with a gap on the Bay Area path: the gap is too narrow for tall bikes (Hank had to lift his over). Keep riding a bit further to find a wider gap instead of crossing. *(2024)*
- 📌 Bike packing for shipping at REI SF took far longer than the team expected; allow at least a full extra day for packing and shipping in a city. *(2024)*
- 📌 Team arrived at the final day with little sleep and food; plan a real breakfast and snacks for the finish day. *(2024)*

### 2024-09-12 — [Day 75: The REAL Spokes OGs](../2024/2024-09-12_day-75-the-real-spokes-ogs.md) — San Francisco, CA → San Francisco, CA
Rest/logistics day in San Francisco (0 mi): the team finished packing bikes at REI, struggled to ship them cheaply (FedEx and UPS were expensive, and REI boxes were oversized), then had dinner with original Spokes founder Turner and alumni Nathan and Kaitlyn, held a Spokes party, and some riders got matching tattoos of the route.
- 🏠 Slept: Friend's apartment, San Francisco CA (a rider's friend)
- 📌 Do not ship bikes from SF in REI boxes: they are huge and you pay to ship air. Costs were high at FedEx and UPS; Bike Flights made the shipping labels and saved money, but volume pricing was still expensive. *(2024)*
- 📌 Strong recommendation from this team: drive the rental car back to Boston with the bikes and gear. It is cheaper (no shipping and cheaper round-trip rental) and less complex. Past Spokes teams did this. *(2024)*
- 📌 REI SF will store half-packed bikes overnight if you cannot finish before closing, and staff will let you come back the next morning. *(2024)*
- 📌 Original Spokes origin: founder Turner recruited everyone he knew, applied for nearly every MIT grant, and ran a Kickstarter; the first trip happened about two years after the idea. *(2024)*
- 📌 Advice from alumni Rafael (met at Lake Tahoe): hold reunions with teammates; they share your context. The team planned to see each other at future weddings and reunions. *(2024)*
