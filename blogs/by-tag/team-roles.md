### 2022-07-02 — [Day 28: St. Louis Blues](../2022/2022-07-02_day-28-st-louis-blues.md) — Perryville, MO → Chesterfield, MO
Rode 92 miles from Perryville, MO to Chesterfield, MO, a St. Louis suburb, in three sections. A flat from road debris, a gravel road being repaved that forced a walk downhill, and a noisy highway shoulder for the last stretch.
- 🏠 Slept: Noah's home, Chesterfield MO (family dog Moca)
- 📌 Google Maps sent the team onto a gravel road being paved near Perryville, MO. The surface was loose and a steep downhill had to be walked. Check the surface before trusting the route. *(2022)*
- 📌 Road debris (a glass base with a spike) caused a flat. Carry a spare tube, patch kit and levers. *(2022)*
- 📌 Google's 2-hour option for the last 30 miles to Chesterfield was a noisy highway shoulder, versus a 3-hour option. Check the shoulder before you commit. *(2022)*
- 📌 Water bottle filling is a weekly role. Doing it as a pipeline during breakfast kept departure quick. *(2022)*
