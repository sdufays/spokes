Zion National Park, UT to Cedar City, UT — 45.6 mi, 3737 ft

It was the best of times, it was the worst of times. 

If you recognize that line from Charles Dickens’ _A Tale of Two Cities_ (the namesake of this blog), you might have some foresight into how this day will play out. In this analogy, my day was akin to life in London and everyone else’s day was more like Paris: safety and peace vs chaos and hunger. Not to say I didn’t experience any chaos as well, but I escaped the worst part of the day as I took a driver day to spend some more time with my family. They had driven down from our home in California (12 hours!!) to visit me for our rest day in Zion. They were also staying an extra day beyond what they initially planned as well since our plan to hike the Narrows moved to today and we needed an extra car, since we had to split up yesterday to get Drew a new bike. All that to say, I wanted to spend some more quality time with them since they were doing so much to just see me. 🙂 I know my last couple of blogs haven’t been biking days… I bike, I swear.

Hiking the Narrows in the morning necessitated a 5 am wakeup, as we needed to pack up the minivan, pack up my family’s stuff, and prepare the airbnb for checkout. We moved fast, getting out of the door at 6:20 am. Our goal was to beat more of the line for the shuttles that we experienced yesterday before Angel’s Landing, so I ran in to hold our spot while the rest of our 11 person party meandered in, with some slightly dirty looks from the people behind us. Sometimes you gotta do what you gotta do.

Once on the shuttle there, I caught up with Aarushi + Carmen on their time in Vegas after we split up. They didn’t come back until 1 am, well after I was asleep, so it was literally the first time we had the chance to debrief our days. 

Our hike today in the Narrows took us through the Virgin River where it carves out a narrow slot canyon. Walking through the water meant we needed appropriate gear, available at the nearby outfitters that seemed to be having incredible business. I would say more than half the people in the Narrows had the distinguishable red rental boots and complimentary hiking pole, and each rental was $30 a pop 😳 I was initially going to just wear my hiking boots and steal a stick, but got convinced by the rest of the group to get a rental. My grandpa and dad did just fine though in their ordinary sneakers and scavenged sticks, so maybe I could’ve saved my money… not that I’ve made the best financial decisions on this trip anyways. 

The trail (river?) was beautiful, full of towering walls of rock around us and clear water that cooled our legs as we trudged through it. Since it was still early morning, the sun was not yet peaking through the top of the canyon, so we enjoyed the shade while we still could. On the way, Aarushi, Carmen, and I discussed the hypothetical godly parentage of our team as the resident audiobook listeners of Percy Jackson during some of our rides. You can see the final consensus on Aarushi’s Day 53 blog, which also may give you some insight of how backlogged our blogs are (oops).

We walked for around 1.5 miles until 10 am before we decided to turn back. We, being the entire group except Nate and Nunu, who wanted to go on for a while longer. There was a slight debate on what to do, as in usual Spokes fashion (had to give my family the full experience), but we ended up on this plan that Drew communicated to them when they were ahead of us. 

Our extremely long and unnecessarily complex plan:

  1. Nate + Nunu keep going and plan to finish ~ 1 hr later than us
  2. The rest of us leave the Narrows
  3. We ride a couple stops to Zion Lodge
  4. Eat lunch and refuel at the loge
  5. Drew + Tatiana go all the way back to the visitor center where the minivan is, and drive it to the lodge so that everyone can change into their biking gear and be ready to leave when Nate + Nunu meet back up with us


  * IMPORTANT NOTE: Cars are not usually allowed to drive to the lodge, due to the shuttle system. Thus, this entire plan hinges on being able to bypass the gate that prevents most cars from entering. Nate + Nunu (who aren’t with us) know the code, after acquiring it to go rescue Drew 2 days before. We have just a vague concept of the code. You might see where this is going…


  6. All leave the lodge
  7. Drop off our rental gear at the visitor center
  8. Get back in the minivan + my dad’s car back to the Airbnb to start the ride



As Nate beautifully put it the day before, “there are too many cooks in the kitchen, none of the cooks know what they want and the kitchen is also on fire.” This analogy doesn't exactly work for this scenario, but I do think it is very true for many of our days.

Once Drew came back and shared this plan they had come up with, the rest of us had some concerns, namely: what if we don’t know the code? Drew was under the impression that we didn’t need the code. We informed him that we didn’t have it, only guesses. That immediately threw a wrench in the plan, but without signal and with them being separated from us, we could only stick with what was agreed as to not cause more confusion. Not all of us wanted to eat at the lodge as well, since the food was a bit overpriced and _incredibly_ mid.

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-57.jpeg?w=902)Brady starting a war with Drew that he couldn’t win

Regardless, we got off at the lodge and lay in the shade of the tree. Aarushi ended up caving to her hunger and got a hot dog served in a pretzel bun?? Not sure what was going on there.

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-25.png?w=1024)Aarushi’s face says it all.

Now with glorious free wifi from the lodge, we reconnected with Tatiana and Drew, who let us know that lo and behold, they didn’t know the code and couldn’t get in ![:/](https://s1.wp.com/wp-content/mu-plugins/wpcom-smileys/uneasy.svg) And Drew got on the shuttle to try to get back to us, but ended up taking it in the wrong direction away from us? Nate and Nunu were still MIA and we were hot and hungry, so we decided to abandon the plan and just rendezvous at the visitor center. Funnily enough, as we got back on the shuttle towards the visitor center, Nate and Nunu were getting off that bus, back from the hike. This resulted in a very movie-like moment of our two groups looking at each other and not being able to fully communicate what happened due to the rush of getting on the shuttle, just a couple of “WE’RE GOING TO THE VISITOR CENTER” shouts before the doors closed. What would we do without our phones. 

Finally back at the visitor center, we stripped off our wet gear and returned it. At this point, my family had been waiting a long time for everyone and had already gone so much out of their way to be our chauffeur for the past 2 days, so I didn’t want them to wait another what was likely going to be at least 2 - 3 more hours. I decided to leave early with them to grab lunch and hang out for a bit before they went home, which the team were kindly supportive of. With that, I split off, and we headed for Cedar City.

At Cedar City, I got to indulge in some luxuries that hadn’t been available for a while: poke and boba. Our stretch of national parks have meant we’ve stayed away from a lot of major cities with diverse food options. As much as I think it was definitely worth it, it was also nice to get some different flavors in my mouth 😋

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-26.png?w=1024)I can’t wait until SF for even better asian food

Then we headed to the Southern Utah Museum of Art, a free museum that has a special place in my heart. I visited it in the past with my family, and its cool exhibits and the fact that it was free had a lasting impression on me. It was just as gorgeous as I remembered, especially the park outside! There was a scent-based exhibit that allowed you to interact with some curated smells, my personal favorite was the _Rockville Troposphere_ — beautifully floral and sweet. I also found some landscape paintings that truly blew my mind. This entire visit reaffirmed my itch to go home and just spend a day painting. I miss manipulating colors and form, almost as much as I miss my own bed.  


![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-27.png?w=1024) ![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-28.png?w=1024)

While we were there, we coincidentally ran in Carmen’s mom and sister, Tara and Simone! They were spending the night with us in Cedar City, so it was nice to meet them a little bit before everyone else. 

I said goodbye to my family, as they really needed to start driving to get home. I got dropped off at the airbnb Carmen’s family was generously providing us, and relaxed there for a bit as I waited for the rest of the team to arrive. It was 5:00 pm at this point, but to my surprise, they had only started the ride at 3:00 pm, so I had a long wait ahead of me. I made myself comfortable on my bunk bed and napped. Now I’ll give my best recount of the ride as I nap, according to survivor testimony. 

#### **Pre-ride (12 pm - 3 pm)**

  * Team debates the route. The most direct route is I-15, but several want to avoid the interstate at all costs. The route team manages to find some side roads that run parallel to the interstate to minimize time on it.
  * Everyone is slightly grumpy due to knowledge of hard ride ahead and 100+ degree heat
  * Change and wait out heat for a bit
  * Drive back from Zion NP to the airbnb to start the ride 



#### **Ride (3 pm - 10 pm)**

  * Ride is extremely hot, as expected, and also pretty uphill in the 1st leg
  * There were legitimate concerns of Aarushi passing out
  * The gas stations were like oases of AC and bathrooms
  * Direct quotes: 
    * “It felt like getting blasted in the face by a hairdryer”
    * “My phone overheated”
    * “It felt like my skin was melting”
    * “If a dragon blew fire on us it would be better than this”
    * “This is some type of cruel punishment”
    * “I hope Tatiana (the driver) isn’t happy when I get there or I’ll be mad”
    * And the likes

![](https://mitspokes2026.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/image-29.png?w=1024)A crazy gravel hill + I-15

Once they all arrived, we had an amazing dinner of homemade butternut squash pasta and chicken, with brownies for dessert. I still dream of that pasta to this day…
