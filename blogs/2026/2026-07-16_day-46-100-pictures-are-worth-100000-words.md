In honor of (my) first century, I finally brought out the digicam to take 105 pics for 105 miles!! This meant snapping a pic every 10 minutes today, so I hope this is like a pictoral version of my last blog post (how many steps of the spokes routine can you spot?) that provides you with a better sense of the ratio of time we spend on biking and eating and blogging and magic card tricks etc., and how quickly scenery can change in one day, from mountains to canyon to desert!
