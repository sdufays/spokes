### 2022-08-08 — [Day 61: Battling Sand and Night](../2022/2022-08-08_day-61-battling-mud-and-night.md) — Green River State Park, UT → Capitol Reef National Park, UT
A hard day: the team left late and was routed onto a sandy, almost non-existent road, then rode in the dark with no phone service and weak front lights. Support car Dora and Simone found them about 10 miles from the campsite. They camped near Capitol Reef with no showers, and a cyclist named James suggested stopping at Mesa Farm Market.
- 🏠 Slept: Campsite near Capitol Reef NP (no showers)
- 📌 Avoid the sandy unpaved road out of Green River State Park toward Capitol Reef. Tires sank in sand, and one section ran into a riverbed. It was the worst path of the trip. *(2022)*
- 📌 Mesa Farm Market is one of the only points of civilization on the route to Capitol Reef. Stock up there. *(2022)*
- 📌 No cell service on this stretch. A rider could not reach the support car; the team debated a satellite phone. *(2022)*
- 📌 Charge all front and rear lights before a day that may run late. The team pedaled in pitch dark with weak phone lights. *(2022)*
- 📌 A gas station along the route (location not stated) was carved into a rock and sold fruit ice-pop bars. *(2022)*
