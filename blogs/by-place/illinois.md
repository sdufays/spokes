### 2017-08-01 — [Bike Trails](../2017/2017-08-01_bike-trails.md)
Margalit broke down the 2,250 miles so far by surface (about 1,800 paved, 150 dirt, 300 trail) and described the trails the team rode, from the Mount Vernon trail out of DC to the Katy Trail and Denver-area paths, with thorny surfaces causing many flats.
- 📌 Katy Trail (Missouri): about 240 miles of crushed limestone rail trail across most of the state; the team rode about 220 miles of it. Flat and long, the team's favorite kind of riding. *(2017)*
- 📌 Flint Hills Nature Trail (Kansas): rough, thorny surface. The team rode only 5 miles but got many punctures over the next 3 days; bring puncture-resistant tires or extra tubes. *(2017)*
- 📌 A 5-mile 'Natural Earth' trail section was a thorny mess that caused 10+ punctures. *(2017)*
- 📌 Tunnel Hill State Rail Trail (Harrisburg, IL) is 56 miles; the team rode part of it. *(2017)*
- 📌 Denver to Fort Collins, CO: South Platte River Trail, Cherry Creek Trail and Denver-Boulder Bikeway, then bike lanes. Colorado is ranked 7th most bike-friendly state by the League of American Bicyclists. *(2017)*
- 📌 Between Kansas and Denver there were stretches of up to 50 miles of only farms or grassland with no trails and few cars. Carry water and plan for distance. *(2017)*

- 📌 Specific project ideas ('improve your school') got finished; vague ideas stalled. *(2013)*
