## 2026

### 2026-06-25 — [Day 25: Unreliable Narrator](../2026/2026-06-25_day-25-unreliable-narrator.md) — St. Louis, MO → Hermann, MO
Morning in St. Louis with a Forest Park tour from host Alex, then a Missouri ride on the Katy Trail. Caroline SAG'd out with an elbow nerve injury; a storm stranded the group near Augusta MO for about two hours, and Nate SAG'd the rest of the team to a pizza dinner and an Airbnb on the Katy Trail.
- 🏠 Slept: Airbnb along the Katy Trail (donated by Kate Engelmann)
- 📌 Katy Trail is a 240-mile rail trail across Missouri, good for steady riding; plan trailhead rest stops along it. *(2026)*
- 📌 Augusta MO (Katy Trail trailhead): the town is mostly abandoned storefronts with rotten floors. One rider fell through a floor of the 'HOSTEL' building, so do not enter abandoned buildings. *(2026)*
- 📌 Afternoon thunderstorms on the Katy Trail forced a roughly two-hour shelter. Have a bail-out plan and a SAG driver for storm days. *(2026)*
- 📌 Philly's Pizza in Marthasville MO was one of the best pizzas of the trip. *(2026)*
- 📌 Sophia M. Sach's Butterfly House near St. Louis: a fun first rest stop, with a snow cone truck and a carousel. *(2026)*
- 📌 Numbness in the pinky that spread up the arm to the elbow was serious enough to force a SAG; do not push through nerve symptoms. *(2026)*


## 2025

### 2025-06-29 — [Day 25: with(out) the sun - light rain and heavy legs](../2025/2025-06-29_day-25-without-the-sun-light-rain-and-heavy-legs.md) — St. Louis, MO → Jefferson City, MO
Long Katy Trail day in light rain from St. Louis to Jefferson City MO (125.1 mi, 3,840 ft as listed); riders went self-supported for about 50 miles before the support car shuttled ahead to cut mileage. The gravel went soft and muddy in the rain.
- 🏠 Slept: Sarah's home, Jefferson City MO (host; dog Barbara)
- 📌 Katy Trail (MO): about 260 mi rail-trail, mostly along the Missouri River, fine gravel; the route reaches it around mile 27 from St. Louis. *(2025)*
- 📌 Katy Trail has occasional bathrooms and benches but no water fountains; ask at restaurants (one restaurant refilled bottles around mile 80). *(2025)*
- 📌 Rain turns Katy Trail gravel soft and muddy: slower riding and mud up the back. *(2025)*
- 📌 Shuttle plan: ride self-supported first ~50 mi, then the support car shuttles ahead; riders can then finish the rest of the day on the bike. *(2025)*
- 📌 Riders drank less in cool rain, but still need water on the trail. *(2025)*

## 2024

### 2024-07-04 — [Day 25: No More Cars](../2024/2024-07-04_day-25-no-more-cars.md) — St. Louis, MO → Hermann, MO
Rode 83 miles from St. Louis suburbs onto the Katy Trail and ended at a treehouse in Hermann, MO; the rider ran out of food by mile 35 and arrived late.
- 🏠 Slept: Treehouse in Hermann, MO (host not named)
- 📌 First ~20 miles out of St. Louis are suburbs with highway exit ramps and a wrong turn risk; the Katy Trail starts after crossing the Missouri River. *(2024)*
- 📌 Katy Trail has long stretches with little food; a rider got very hungry by mile 35 with no food stop, so pack extra snacks and lunch. *(2024)*
- 📌 Hermann MO: ice cream shop and cafe next to each other were a team favorite. *(2024)*

### 2024-07-05 — [Day 26: Am I Dreaming?](../2024/2024-07-05_day-26-am-i-dreaming.md) — Hermann, MO → Jefferson City, MO
Easy 51-mile Katy Trail ride from Hermann to Jefferson City; the team had bike fit checks at a local shop, cooked Japanese curry for the host, and went to the host's brewery gig.
- 🏠 Slept: Host Sarah's house, Jefferson City, MO
- 📌 Katy Trail's crushed-stone surface takes more effort than pavement; 50 trail miles felt slower than expected. *(2024)*
- 📌 Jefferson City: ride up The Skywalk (spiral ramp) to reach the Jefferson City Bridge when entering the city from the trail. *(2024)*
- 📌 Jefferson City is worth a rest day; the team felt they did not explore it. *(2024)*

### 2024-07-06 — [Day 28: We have the Tour de France at home (jk)](../2024/2024-07-06_we-have-the-tour-de-france-at-home-jk.md) — Sedalia, MO → Osawatomie, KS
First century (118.85 miles) from Sedalia, MO to Osawatomie, KS, taking 13 hours; branches on the Katy Trail damaged derailleurs, and hosts Jeff and Sarah fit bikes and taught maintenance.
- 🏠 Slept: Hosts Jeff and Sarah's home, Osawatomie, KS
- 📌 Riding side by side on the Katy Trail's last stretch meant branches and twigs hit everyone; the team's derailleurs took the damage. Ride staggered on trails with debris. *(2024)*
- 📌 Sedalia to Osawatomie is 118 miles and took 13 hours with the Katy Trail's 60 miles; a 100+ mile day needs a planned early start and a driver for water and food. *(2024)*
- 📌 Osawatomie, KS: free bike fits and maintenance advice from the hosts; a good stop before the Flint Hills. *(2024)*
