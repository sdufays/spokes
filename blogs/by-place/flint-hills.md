### 2024-07-07 — [Day 29: Pick Your Poison](../2024/2024-07-07_day-29-pick-your-poison.md) — Osawatomie, KS → Council Grove, KS
Riders were caught in a severe thunderstorm on the Flint Hills Trail and then KS-68 (no shoulder, heavy semis), took refuge in a cafe, and added a 10-mile gravel detour around a flooded trail to reach Council Grove, KS.
- 🏠 Slept: Cottage House B&B, Council Grove, KS
- 📌 KS-68 out of Osawatomie has no shoulder and heavy semi-truck traffic; one rider pulled over after a close pass. Avoid it or plan a trail route. *(2024)*
- 📌 Flint Hills Trail turns muddy and slow after rain, and sections flood; a flooded stretch forced a 10-mile gravel detour that was rocky. *(2024)*
- 📌 The team had no agreed storm plan and spent about 30 minutes in indecision; agree on a storm protocol (when to pull off, where to shelter) before the trip. *(2024)*
- 📌 Hosts asked every week what the team would do in a storm; have a real answer ready. *(2024)*
