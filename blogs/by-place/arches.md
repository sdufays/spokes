### 2026-07-22 — [Day 52: I Spy 8 Breathtaking Arches](../2026/2026-07-22_day-52-i-spy-8-breathtaking-arches.md) — Moab, UT → Moab, UT
Rest day in Moab. The team visited Arches National Park, hiking Delicate Arch (a hat blew off the trail and was rescued) and Double Arch, then bought metal water bottles at a Moab gear shop after plastic insulated bottles melted their ice. They ate a second burger at Milt's and bought matching shirts.
- 🏠 Slept: Airbnb in Moab, UT
- 📌 Delicate Arch is a 3-mile round trip with switchbacks and exposed slickrock. Go early before the heat, and secure hats, since wind blew one off the trail. *(2026)*
- 📌 Metal water bottles beat plastic insulated ones in heat; plastic kept ice about 5 minutes. The first Moab shop had only small sizes; the second had full size. *(2026)*
- 📌 Double Arch is a 0.6 mile path with shade and climbing inside the arch. *(2026)*
