### 2026-05-31 — [Day 0: Half a Mile of Hell](../2026/2026-05-31_day-0-half-a-mile-of-hell.md) — Boston, MA → Washington, DC

### 2026-06-01 — [Day 1: Small Wins](../2026/2026-06-01_day-1-small-wins.md)
First learning festival at the DC Youth Services Center, a juvenile rehab facility. The team ran seven rotations of the Lego electric-motor workshop; only some students got motors working, though one class of boys all did.
- 🏠 Slept: Host's house in DC (Deepika and Nikunj's, per the Day 2 post)
- 📌 Lego motor builds are tricky: expect only a minority of students to get a working motor. The facility science teacher's advice was that small wins are the goal. *(2026)*
- 📌 Ask the host facility for staff or interns to help run stations; the judge's interns covered for the team. *(2026)*
- 📌 Costco in DC requires the card owner to be present at checkout; the team's borrowed-card workaround did not work reliably. *(2026)*

### 2026-06-02 — [Day 2: First SAG (and 2nd and 3rd and 4th)](../2026/2026-06-02_day-2-first-sag-and-2nd-and-3rd-and-4th.md) — Washington, DC → Linden, VA
Rode 88.6 miles from DC to Linden VA after a late, sleep-deprived start. Nate missed a turn and rode a four-lane highway with no shoulder before SAGging about 6 miles; Aarushi, Nunu and Caroline were SAG'd at mile 76.
- 🏠 Slept: Warmshowers host Jen and Jim's home, Linden VA
- 📌 Leaving DC at rush hour: the team left at 8:40 AM and reached the Lincoln Memorial at 10 AM. Leave earlier to avoid peak traffic. *(2026)*
- 📌 The W&OD trail out of DC is separated, shaded and green, with good riding to about mile 40. *(2026)*
- 📌 A right turn out of a rest-stop parking lot (about mile 55) sent a rider onto a parallel road with a 1000 ft climb and a four-lane 60 mph highway with no bike shoulder. Check shoulders on Street View before accepting a reroute. *(2026)*
- 📌 Jen warned that the next day's planned 60-mile, ~8000 ft route had sharp, low-visibility turns; the team swapped to a 30-mile, 2000 ft route to a different campground. Ask local hosts about the next day before finalizing. *(2026)*
- 📌 A SAG pickup spot had a 'No trespassers. Violators will be shot' sign; choose a different pickup point if one looks unsafe. *(2026)*
- 📌 Riding 88 miles after days of 2-4 hours of sleep caused most of the team to SAG. Consider a shorter opening ride. *(2026)*

## 2022

### 2022-06-03 — [First Day!](../2022/2022-06-03_first-day.md) — Washington, DC → Stafford, VA
Day one: the team left the Lincoln Memorial and rode the East Coast Greenway and busy Virginia roads toward Stafford, VA (about 58 miles planned). Sophia got leg cramps in the heat and rode in the support car for the rest of the day.
- 🏠 Slept: Warmshowers hosts Lauren and Steve, Stafford VA (basement, laundry)
- 📌 Electrolytes from day one: Nuun tablets (bought at Target) ended the leg cramps. Drink one plain water and one electrolyte bottle. *(2022)*
- 📌 95 F with a water shortage: add more bottle cages to the bikes early on. A collapsible 5-gallon water jug (bought at REI) was used for the car. *(2022)*
- 📌 East Coast Greenway out of DC has cold water fountains, but the route leaves the river breezes for busy roads and brutal hills near Stafford, VA. *(2022)*
