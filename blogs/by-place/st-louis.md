### 2026-06-25 — [Day 25: Unreliable Narrator](../2026/2026-06-25_day-25-unreliable-narrator.md) — St. Louis, MO → Hermann, MO
Morning in St. Louis with a Forest Park tour from host Alex, then a Missouri ride on the Katy Trail. Caroline SAG'd out with an elbow nerve injury; a storm stranded the group near Augusta MO for about two hours, and Nate SAG'd the rest of the team to a pizza dinner and an Airbnb on the Katy Trail.

- 📌 Philly's Pizza in Marthasville MO was one of the best pizzas of the trip. *(2026)*
- 📌 Sophia M. Sach's Butterfly House near St. Louis: a fun first rest stop, with a snow cone truck and a carousel. *(2026)*
- 📌 Numbness in the pinky that spread up the arm to the elbow was serious enough to force a SAG; do not push through nerve symptoms. *(2026)*

### 2024-06-30 — [Day 23: 💯](../2024/2024-06-30_day-23.md) — Gun Creek Campground, IL → St Louis, MO
Gun Creek Campground IL to St Louis MO (107 mi, 2,180 ft) in 100-plus-degree heat: riders started at 4:30 am, drafted in pairs for the first half, and one rider ran low on water at mile 60 before a homeowner refilled bottles; the team finished the century at 102 degrees and had dinner with an MIT alum.

- 📌 Water gaps in rural Illinois cornfields are real: a rider had to wait under a tree at a lonely house with no answer; a passing homeowner refilled bottles. *(2024)*
- 📌 Drafting within about 2 feet of the rider ahead saves effort; the team rotated lead every 5 miles. *(2024)*
- 📌 A gas station just outside St Louis was a good water stop. *(2024)*

Rode 92 miles from Perryville, MO to Chesterfield, MO, a St. Louis suburb, in three sections. A flat from road debris, a gravel road being repaved that forced a walk downhill, and a noisy highway shoulder for the last stretch.
- 🏠 Slept: Noah's home, Chesterfield MO (family dog Moca)

- 📌 Google's 2-hour option for the last 30 miles to Chesterfield was a noisy highway shoulder, versus a 3-hour option. Check the shoulder before you commit. *(2022)*
- 📌 Water bottle filling is a weekly role. Doing it as a pipeline during breakfast kept departure quick. *(2022)*
