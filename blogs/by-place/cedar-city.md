### 2026-07-31 — [Day 61: A Tale of Two Cities](../2026/2026-07-31_day-61-a-tale-of-two-cities.md) — Zion National Park, UT → Cedar City, UT
Driver day for Caroline. The Narrows hike was cut short after about 1.5 miles, and Drew and Tatiana could not get through the Zion Lodge car gate. Caroline left early with family. The rest of the team rode from 3 to 10 pm in 100+ degree heat to finish in Cedar City (45.6 miles, about 3,700 ft), with a rider nearly passing out.
- 🏠 Slept: Airbnb in Cedar City, UT (provided by Carmen's family)
- 📌 Zion Narrows: rental boots and walking poles cost about $30 each, and more than half the hikers rented them. Start early, around 6 am, to beat the shuttle line. *(2026)*
- 📌 Heat ride from Zion to Cedar City: gas stations were the only air conditioning and bathrooms. A side road parallel to I-15 cut time on the interstate. *(2026)*
- 📌 Zion Lodge gate code was not known to the team, so the plan to drive there failed. Confirm the access plan ahead of time. *(2026)*

### 2024-08-19 — [Day 60: A How-To Guide for Boring Rides](../2024/2024-08-19_day-60-a-how-to-guide-for-boring-rides.md) — Hurricane, UT → Milford, UT
A long, dull 100-mile day from Hurricane to Milford. The route went about 90 miles northeast, away from the West Coast. Riders had flats, a first lunch of Cedar City fast food, a drafting crash, and a team cheesesteak dinner after a night at a church.
- 🏠 Slept: Mountain View Baptist Fellowship, Milford, UT (church)
- 📌 Route check: this day's route went 90-some miles northeast before heading west. Verify the direction of long route segments before leaving. *(2024)*
- 📌 Cedar City, UT has McDonald's, Dairy Queen, Culver's and gas stations. It is a rare resupply on this stretch. *(2024)*
- 📌 Drafting: riding too close behind another rider caused a crash. Keep a safe gap, because the rider in back is the one who gets hurt. *(2024)*
