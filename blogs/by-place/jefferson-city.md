## 2025

### 2025-06-29 — [Day 25: with(out) the sun - light rain and heavy legs](../2025/2025-06-29_day-25-without-the-sun-light-rain-and-heavy-legs.md) — St. Louis, MO → Jefferson City, MO
Long Katy Trail day in light rain from St. Louis to Jefferson City MO (125.1 mi, 3,840 ft as listed); riders went self-supported for about 50 miles before the support car shuttled ahead to cut mileage. The gravel went soft and muddy in the rain.
- 🏠 Slept: Sarah's home, Jefferson City MO (host; dog Barbara)
- 📌 Katy Trail (MO): about 260 mi rail-trail, mostly along the Missouri River, fine gravel; the route reaches it around mile 27 from St. Louis. *(2025)*
- 📌 Katy Trail has occasional bathrooms and benches but no water fountains; ask at restaurants (one restaurant refilled bottles around mile 80). *(2025)*
- 📌 Rain turns Katy Trail gravel soft and muddy: slower riding and mud up the back. *(2025)*
- 📌 Shuttle plan: ride self-supported first ~50 mi, then the support car shuttles ahead; riders can then finish the rest of the day on the bike. *(2025)*
- 📌 Riders drank less in cool rain, but still need water on the trail. *(2025)*

### 2025-06-30 — [Day 26: Best Driver Ever](../2025/2025-06-30_day-26-best-driver-ever.md) — Jefferson City, MO → Sedalia, MO
Driving day from Jefferson City to Sedalia MO (69 mi, 3,250 ft): supermarket runs, a minor van accident in a parking lot, a rider-to-rider crash that left Charles and Greta with cuts and bruises, and a church sleepover.
- 🏠 Slept: Kingdom Life Church, Sedalia MO (church host; pews used as beds)
- 📌 Kingdom Life Church has no showers: riders showered at Pastor Steve's home and used church laundry. Plan showers in advance. *(2025)*
- 📌 The church kitchen had no pot lid, so rice cooked to porridge. Bring a lid. *(2025)*
- 📌 Two riders collided on the route (Charles and Greta): cuts, a sore pinky and a bump. The van's medical kit was used on the spot; keep first aid in the van. *(2025)*
- 📌 Driving days are heavy: the driver does morning cleanup and dinner planning. Build a shopping list before leaving so you don't need four supermarkets (this driver made four stops). *(2025)*


## 2024

### 2024-07-05 — [Day 26: Am I Dreaming?](../2024/2024-07-05_day-26-am-i-dreaming.md) — Hermann, MO → Jefferson City, MO
Easy 51-mile Katy Trail ride from Hermann to Jefferson City; the team had bike fit checks at a local shop, cooked Japanese curry for the host, and went to the host's brewery gig.
- 🏠 Slept: Host Sarah's house, Jefferson City, MO
- 📌 Katy Trail's crushed-stone surface takes more effort than pavement; 50 trail miles felt slower than expected. *(2024)*
- 📌 Jefferson City: ride up The Skywalk (spiral ramp) to reach the Jefferson City Bridge when entering the city from the trail. *(2024)*
- 📌 Jefferson City is worth a rest day; the team felt they did not explore it. *(2024)*

### 2024-07-06 — [Day 27: 41 Million Revolutions](../2024/2024-07-06_day-27-41-million-revolutions.md) — Jefferson City, MO → Sedalia, MO
Rode 60 miles from Jefferson City to Sedalia, MO in a flat, low-morale slog; the team had a famous barbecue lunch at Kehde's and slept in Antioch church with only a hose for showers.
- 🏠 Slept: Antioch church, Sedalia, MO (church host)
- 📌 Antioch church in Sedalia has plenty of floor space but no shower; the team showered with a garden hose. *(2024)*
- 📌 Kehde's Barbeque in Sedalia (seating in a train car) is a strong food stop; pulled pork and root beer floats; vegetarians may find it limited. *(2024)*
- 📌 Mid-trip Missouri days were tedious; the team suggested a rest day in Jefferson City. *(2024)*
