### 2024-08-28 — [Day 67: One Final Ass Whooping from Nevada](../2024/2024-08-28_day-67-one-final-ass-whooping-from-nevada.md) — Fallon, NV → South Lake Tahoe, CA

- 📌 The Fernley reroute (US-50 Alt) goes through Fernley. The state capitol and monuments in Carson City are a stop along the way. *(2024)*
- 📌 The California state line on US-50 has only a small green road sign, which is easy to miss. *(2024)*
