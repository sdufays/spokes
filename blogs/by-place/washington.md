### 2025-08-22 — [Day 79: Dilly Dallying One Last Time](../2025/2025-08-22_day-79-dilly-dallying-one-last-time.md) — Humbug Mountain State Park, OR → Seattle, WA
Drove the Oregon coast (Hwy 101) from Humbug Mountain State Park to Seattle, stopping at a near-empty beach, Sea Lion Caves, Thor's Well, Cape Kiwanda and Tillamook. A bike-rack hit on a parked car and a 1.5-hour advisor call delay pushed them into Seattle after midnight.
- 🏠 Slept: Charles' family house, Seattle WA (arrived after midnight)
- 📌 Sea Lion Caves (Oregon coast, Hwy 101): about $18 entry and a 200 ft descent; only about 30 sea lions in late August. *(2025)*
- 📌 Thor's Well (Oregon coast): best near high tide, but a wave nearly took two riders off the rocks. Stay on high ground. *(2025)*
- 📌 Little or no cell service on Hwy 101 between towns. A phone advisor call took over 30 minutes of troubleshooting. Make calls from towns. *(2025)*
- 📌 Cape Kiwanda food was expensive; Tillamook is the cheaper food stop. *(2025)*
- 📌 Tillamook Creamery was about to close when they arrived in the afternoon. Arrive earlier. *(2025)*
- 📌 Van with bike rack backed out of a Tillamook parking spot and pierced another car's tail lights. Note left, owner filed an insurance claim. Check rack clearance and back out slowly. *(2025)*
- 📌 Coast day of about 485 mi with stops ran past 3 pm. Plan fewer stops on long coastal driving days. *(2025)*

### 2025-08-23 — [Day 80: blue lakes, orange berries and the skies above us](../2025/2025-08-23_day-80-blue-lakes-orange-berries-and-the-skies-above-us.md) — Seattle, WA → Curlew Lake State Park, WA
Left Seattle and drove through North Cascades National Park (Gorge Creek Falls, a cold Diablo Lake swim, rowan berries), with a van gas-door problem and a failed propane stove along the way. They camped by Curlew Lake State Park in Washington.
- 🏠 Slept: Curlew Lake State Park campground, WA (campsite right on the lake)
- 📌 Propane camp stove would not work at Curlew Lake State Park; they made sandwiches instead. Stove problems continued for several more days. *(2025)*
- 📌 Van gas-tank door would not close after a fill-up; they taped it shut. It fixed itself a couple of days later. *(2025)*
- 📌 North Cascades National Park: the drive through the park with many overlooks ran behind schedule. Budget extra daylight. *(2025)*
- 📌 Sheri's Sweet Shop in Winthrop WA: smoothies and chocolates; long ice-cream lines. *(2025)*
- 📌 Roadside self-serve farm egg cooler on the highway near Winthrop; they paid in cash. *(2025)*
- 📌 Anderson's Grocery in Republic WA is a good grocery stop before the campsite. *(2025)*

### 2025-08-24 — [Day 81: Glacial Troubles](../2025/2025-08-24_day-81-glacial-troubles.md) — Curlew Lake State Park, WA → Duck Lake, MT
Woke up very cold at Curlew Lake, WA, then fought a van fuel-fill problem, a dirty windshield, and the stove failure. They drove up Going-to-the-Sun Road to Logan Pass, where a couple offered help, and reached Duck Lake, MT after dark.
- 🏠 Slept: Duck Lake Campground, MT (primitive, no running water)
- 📌 Northeast Washington mornings were very cold in late August. Their tent had no rainfly and they were cold; keep the rainfly on. The Apgar Visitor Center in Glacier sells beanies and thick socks. *(2025)*
- 📌 Gas pump near Kettle Falls WA: fuel cut off after about 5 seconds at two different stations. The problem later cleared up on its own. Allow time for fueling and try another pump. *(2025)*
- 📌 Colville WA self-serve car wash: a dirty windshield at sunrise was a glare hazard. Clean it before the mountain roads. *(2025)*
- 📌 Lake Pend Oreille (Idaho): no real overlooks; the path to the water is a steep, loose-rock descent. *(2025)*
- 📌 Going-to-the-Sun Road (Glacier NP): very narrow in places, with a rock wall on one side and cliff on the other. Drive it in daylight. *(2025)*
- 📌 Logan Pass visitor center closes in the evening; the ranger recommended a Hidden Lake sunset hike from there. *(2025)*
- 📌 Leaning Tree Campground (MT, near Duck Lake): showers and laundry; cell signal too weak for calls. *(2025)*
- 📌 Duck Lake campground (MT) is primitive with no running water; they arrived after dark. *(2025)*
