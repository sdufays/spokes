### 2017-08-20 — [Final Blog Post (and my first post!)](../2017/2017-08-20_final-blog-post-and-my-first-post.md) — San Francisco, CA
The team reached the Golden Gate Bridge and posted a final thank-you list and trip totals: 3,579 miles, about 110,000 ft of climbing, 54 riding days and 30 flat tires.
- 📌 Trip totals: 3,579 miles, about 110,000 ft of climbing, 54 riding days over 10 weeks, 30 flat tires, 25 WarmShowers hosts, 11 campgrounds, 16 McDonald's stops. *(2017)*
- 📌 Corporate sponsors: Texas Instruments, MIT Physics Department, MIT Edgerton Center, MathWorks, MIT Brain and Cognitive Sciences. The crowdfunding site was set up with the MIT Gift Office. *(2017)*
- 📌 Ace Wheelworks (Cambridge, MA) built and fitted all bikes; bike shops along the way fixed tires, derailleurs and disc brakes. *(2017)*
- 📌 Church and center stays: Al's Bike Hostel (Farmington, MO); Colby UMC (KS); Burlington UMC (CO); Colorado Mountain College (Steamboat Springs, CO); Assembly of God-Cavalry (Ely, NV); Epworth UMC (Fallon, NV, dinner and breakfast); Rancho Murieta Equestrian Center (CA). *(2017)*
- 📌 Last-minute hosts were found at dusk: Stacy and Mike (Wytheville, VA), Terry and Linda Crain (Murphysboro, IL), Steve Musser (Strasburg, CO). *(2017)*
- 📌 The route included US 50 (484 miles, called the loneliest road). *(2017)*
