### 2025-08-22 — [Day 79: Dilly Dallying One Last Time](../2025/2025-08-22_day-79-dilly-dallying-one-last-time.md) — Humbug Mountain State Park, OR → Seattle, WA
Drove the Oregon coast (Hwy 101) from Humbug Mountain State Park to Seattle, stopping at a near-empty beach, Sea Lion Caves, Thor's Well, Cape Kiwanda and Tillamook. A bike-rack hit on a parked car and a 1.5-hour advisor call delay pushed them into Seattle after midnight.
- 🏠 Slept: Charles' family house, Seattle WA (arrived after midnight)
- 📌 Sea Lion Caves (Oregon coast, Hwy 101): about $18 entry and a 200 ft descent; only about 30 sea lions in late August. *(2025)*
- 📌 Thor's Well (Oregon coast): best near high tide, but a wave nearly took two riders off the rocks. Stay on high ground. *(2025)*
- 📌 Little or no cell service on Hwy 101 between towns. A phone advisor call took over 30 minutes of troubleshooting. Make calls from towns. *(2025)*
- 📌 Cape Kiwanda food was expensive; Tillamook is the cheaper food stop. *(2025)*
- 📌 Tillamook Creamery was about to close when they arrived in the afternoon. Arrive earlier. *(2025)*
- 📌 Van with bike rack backed out of a Tillamook parking spot and pierced another car's tail lights. Note left, owner filed an insurance claim. Check rack clearance and back out slowly. *(2025)*
- 📌 Coast day of about 485 mi with stops ran past 3 pm. Plan fewer stops on long coastal driving days. *(2025)*
