### 2026-07-26 — [Day 56: a series of reflections](../2026/2026-07-26_day-56-a-series-of-reflections.md) — Capitol Reef National Park, UT → Escalante, UT
The trip's biggest climb, about 6,000 ft: from Capitol Reef through red rock, a wind-and-hail squall on a gravel climb, a cold lunch at 9,500 ft, and a 10,049 ft summit in the Dixie National Forest. Steep gravel descents led to a lake stop with kayaks, where two riders ran an hour late with no cell service. The team reached Escalante at dusk.
- 🏠 Slept: Pastor Richards' home (host), Escalante, UT

- 📌 Lunch at about 9,500 ft is cold. Bring a rain jacket and warm layer; the team wrapped in blankets. *(2026)*
- 📌 No cell service on the climbs. Two riders were an hour late and the team could not check on them, so set check-in points. *(2026)*
- 📌 Escalante to Bryce route: the host suggested a gravel road north of Hwy 12. The team chose the paved highway, the same as the 2025 team, because it had rest stops and a support crew could meet them. The gravel had no Street View and unclear elevation. *(2026)*
