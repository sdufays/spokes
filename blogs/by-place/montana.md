## 2025

### 2025-08-24 — [Day 81: Glacial Troubles](../2025/2025-08-24_day-81-glacial-troubles.md) — Curlew Lake State Park, WA → Duck Lake, MT
Woke up very cold at Curlew Lake, WA, then fought a van fuel-fill problem, a dirty windshield, and the stove failure. They drove up Going-to-the-Sun Road to Logan Pass, where a couple offered help, and reached Duck Lake, MT after dark.
- 🏠 Slept: Duck Lake Campground, MT (primitive, no running water)
- 📌 Northeast Washington mornings were very cold in late August. Their tent had no rainfly and they were cold; keep the rainfly on. The Apgar Visitor Center in Glacier sells beanies and thick socks. *(2025)*
- 📌 Gas pump near Kettle Falls WA: fuel cut off after about 5 seconds at two different stations. The problem later cleared up on its own. Allow time for fueling and try another pump. *(2025)*
- 📌 Colville WA self-serve car wash: a dirty windshield at sunrise was a glare hazard. Clean it before the mountain roads. *(2025)*
- 📌 Lake Pend Oreille (Idaho): no real overlooks; the path to the water is a steep, loose-rock descent. *(2025)*
- 📌 Going-to-the-Sun Road (Glacier NP): very narrow in places, with a rock wall on one side and cliff on the other. Drive it in daylight. *(2025)*
- 📌 Logan Pass visitor center closes in the evening; the ranger recommended a Hidden Lake sunset hike from there. *(2025)*
- 📌 Leaning Tree Campground (MT, near Duck Lake): showers and laundry; cell signal too weak for calls. *(2025)*
- 📌 Duck Lake campground (MT) is primitive with no running water; they arrived after dark. *(2025)*

### 2025-08-25 — [Day 82: Ancient Lands, Empty yet Grand](../2025/2025-08-25_day-82-ancient-lands-empty-yet-grand.md) — Duck Lake, MT → Lizard Creek Campground, WY
Drove from Duck Lake, MT through Browning and Helena, losing over two hours in Helena to failed stove troubleshooting before buying a working propane canister. They then rushed through Yellowstone to Old Faithful and reached Lizard Creek Campground in Grand Teton National Park after dark.
- 🏠 Slept: Lizard Creek Campground, Grand Teton NP, WY (arrived after dark)
- 📌 Duck Lake campground has no running water. Browning MT (Blackfeet Reservation headquarters) is the nearest stop with a cafe and water; the cafe is inside a casino. *(2025)*
- 📌 Montana Dinosaur Center (on the US-89 route in MT): worth a bathroom stop; touchable T. rex bone and an active dig. *(2025)*
- 📌 Helena MT has the only big hardware stores for many miles. The next major city after Helena is in South Dakota. Buy supplies there. *(2025)*
- 📌 Propane failure: the large refillable tank, adapter and a new stove did not fix the problem. A small camping propane canister from an outdoor store worked. Test the full cooking setup before the trip and carry a spare canister. *(2025)*
- 📌 Yellowstone: they only had about 3 hours, passing many springs and geysers without stopping. Old Faithful visitor center is about 15 min from the eruption. Allow a full day for the park. *(2025)*
- 📌 Lizard Creek Campground (Grand Teton NP, WY) was reached after dark. Plan an earlier arrival. *(2025)*
