### 2026-08-11 — [Day 72: GET OFF THE BEACH! THE BEAR IS COMING!](../2026/2026-08-11_day-72-get-off-the-beach-the-bear-is-coming.md) — South Lake Tahoe, CA → South Lake Tahoe, CA
Rest day in South Lake Tahoe. Drew drove about an hour each way to Reno to replace a blown rear tire at a Trek store. Meanwhile the team was dropped at Sand Beach State Park, where a ranger enforced a no-drop-off zone, a bear walked past an open dumpster, and the group split up without phones. The team cooked a last dinner together, and Drew got second-degree burns from frying burgers in hot oil.
- 📌 Sand Beach State Park in Lake Tahoe enforces no drop-off at the boat ramp. Arrange pickup from a parking lot. *(2026)*
- 📌 A black bear walked through the Sand Beach parking lot past an open dumpster. Keep food in the car, not in open bags. *(2026)*
- 📌 The Trek store is in Reno, NV, not North Lake Tahoe. Verify store addresses first; the drive is about an hour each way. *(2026)*
- 📌 The group split up with no phones and no meeting plan. Agree on a meeting point and check-in time before separating. *(2026)*
- 📌 Frying burgers in hot oil caused second-degree burns. Use the grill or oven for big batches. *(2026)*
