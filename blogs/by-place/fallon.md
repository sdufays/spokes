### 2024-08-28 — [Day 65: Me and the Flies Against the World](../2024/2024-08-28_day-65-me-and-the-flies-against-the-world.md) — Austin, NV → Fallon, NV
The longest ride of the trip, 112 miles on the Loneliest Road in America (US-50), in 99°F heat and headwinds. The rider went solo until the Middlegate stop at mile 64, then pushed on alone to the Fallon Youth Club for dinner with the host.
- 🏠 Slept: Fallon Youth Club, Fallon, NV
- 📌 US-50, Austin to Fallon: 16 mountain ranges across the Great Basin stretch. All the climbing is in the first half. The last 20 miles are flat, but with headwinds. *(2024)*
- 📌 Middlegate, NV (pop. 17): a bar with ice water and a rest stop at mile 64. There are no services for long stretches before it. *(2024)*
- 📌 Heat: the temperature reached 99°F in the afternoon. Start early and carry extra water. *(2024)*
- 📌 A subpar bike repair left a rider's bike unusable. Ask the mechanic to test-ride the bike and listen for the sounds you're concerned about. *(2024)*
- 📌 Knee pain: dropping a gear and pedaling faster reduced strain on one rider's knee. *(2024)*

### 2024-08-28 — [Day 66: The Oasis of Nevada](../2024/2024-08-28_day-66-the-oasis-of-nevada.md) — Fallon, NV → Fallon, NV
A learning festival day at the Fallon Youth Club: Strawberry DNA extraction, a Physics of Sound speaker workshop, and catch-and-run with students. The team also found out about US-50 road work between Fallon and Silver Springs. The detour decision was postponed to the morning.
- 🏠 Slept: Fallon Youth Club, Fallon, NV
- 📌 Strawberry DNA: dish soap breaks down membranes, salt neutralizes DNA charge so strands stick together, and rubbing alcohol makes DNA visible. Works well with students. *(2024)*
- 📌 Physics of Sound speaker kit: 3D-printed base, permanent magnet, copper wire wrapped on a paper cylinder, cardstock, and super glue. Have adults do the gluing so no one glues their fingers. Speakers sound louder when taped to a metal trash can. *(2024)*
- 📌 US-50 road work between Fallon and Silver Springs: one-way sections with pilot cars, 25 mph limits, delays up to 30 minutes, and sections up to 5 miles. The Nevada DOT phone line had closed by the time the team learned of it, so call when it opens at 8 am. *(2024)*
- 📌 Detour: US-50 Alt to Fernley adds about 15 miles and 500 ft of climbing. Few paved alternate roads exist in this part of Nevada. *(2024)*
