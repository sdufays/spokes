## 2025

### 2025-06-07 — [Day 3: BLUE RIDGE MOUNTAINS, SHENENDOAH RIVERRRR](../2025/2025-06-07_day-3-blue-ridge-mountains-shenendoah-riverrrrr.md) — Linden, VA → Big Meadows Campground, VA
Linden, VA to Big Meadows Campground (55.75 miles, 6,555 ft). Riders climbed about 10 miles and 3,000 ft into Shenandoah National Park in heat, and the group reached the campground at sunset.
- 🏠 Slept: Big Meadows Campground (Shenandoah National Park)
- 📌 The Skyline climb into Shenandoah is about 10 miles and 3,000 ft at grade 5. Some riders needed the van for part of it. *(2025)*
- 📌 Water ran out before the first rest stop in heat. Buy water at the cafe or gas stop before the climb. *(2025)*
- 📌 Big Meadows general store closes around sunset. Buy food and supplies before you arrive. *(2025)*
- 📌 Riders got separated at camp after dark. Agree on a regroup point and a plan for a late arrival. *(2025)*

### 2025-06-08 — [Day 4: thunderstruck⚡️](../2025/2025-06-08_day-4-thunderstruck-%e2%9a%a1%ef%b8%8f.md) — Big Meadows Campground, VA → Afton, VA
Big Meadows to Afton, VA (42.74 miles, 3,045 ft). Fog, blind curves, and heavy rain made the Skyline Drive descent unsafe, so riders and bikes were shuttled down by car, and the team reached the host by about 5 pm.
- 🏠 Slept: Pam Mendosa's home, Shannon Farm intentional community, Afton, VA (2 nights)
- 📌 Skyline Drive descent in fog, rain, and blind curves was unsafe for riders. Plan a rain-day bailout and a car shuttle for bikes. *(2025)*
- 📌 Two cars with two bike racks still did not fit all bikes in one trip. Count rack capacity before the shuttle. *(2025)*
- 📌 Pam Mendosa (Shannon Farm, Afton VA) is an excellent host. Her property is off-grid, and there is no wifi or cell service there. *(2025)*


## 2022

### 2022-06-08 — [Day 5: Into the Woods (and up the Mountain)](../2022/2022-06-08_day-5-into-the-woods-and-up-the-mountain.md) — Charlottesville, VA → Loft Mountain Campground, VA
Left Charlottesville for Loft Mountain Campground in Shenandoah National Park, a 3,717 ft gain. Paved country roads turned into steep gravel and bushes, then a long Skyline Drive climb and a final steep mile, with a driver meeting them at a halfway picnic table.
- 🏠 Slept: Loft Mountain Campground, Shenandoah National Park, VA
- 📌 The Loft Mountain approach is brutal: a gravel road into Shenandoah, a long Skyline Drive climb, then a final 1-mile steep road. The 3,717 ft gain was far more than the team expected from the route plan. *(2022)*
- 📌 The team's driver met them at a halfway picnic table near a stream. Plan a driver meeting point on long climb days. *(2022)*
- 📌 Pack food and gear carefully: lunch and oat milk were left at the host's home and had to be retrieved by car. *(2022)*
