### 2026-07-05 — [Day 35: The Wickedest Little Bugs in America](../2026/2026-07-05_day-35-the-wickedest-little-bugs-in-america.md) — Macksville, KS → Dodge City, KS

- 📌 Lunch ran from 4pm to 6pm; a long lunch cut into daylight. Keep rest stops short when the day is long. *(2026)*
- 📌 Bring bug spray for the evening in Kansas; mosquitoes bit the whole team at a park fireworks show. *(2026)*
- 📌 Windmills near Dodge City gave a strong tailwind for much of the ride. *(2026)*
- 📌 A halfway-point museum sits between New York and San Francisco; it is not the geographic center, but it has an old single-speed bike on display. *(2026)*


## 2024

### 2024-07-17 — [Day 33: Viva la Vida](../2024/2024-07-17_day-33-viva-la-vida.md) — Stafford, KS → Dodge City, KS
Flat 76-mile US-50 ride from Stafford to Dodge City, KS through a 17-mile wind farm; a rider got a first flat that was fixed roadside with a hand pump, and the team visited Paleteria La Michoacana and Boot Hill Museum.
- 📌 US-50 across Kansas is flat with wide shoulders and few turns; good for steady-pace riding but can have headwinds and thunderstorms. *(2024)*
- 📌 A 17-mile wind farm on US-50 near Dodge City took more than an hour with wind; expect slow miles there. *(2024)*
- 📌 Carry a hand pump: a flat on the US-50 shoulder was fixed roadside; a second rider also had a flat that day. *(2024)*
- 📌 Paleteria La Michoacana in Dodge City is a standout food stop (popsicles and esquites). *(2024)*
- 📌 Boot Hill Museum's gun show had ended before the team arrived; check show times. *(2024)*


## 2022

### 2022-07-16 — [Day 43: The Windy City is... Dodge City, KS](../2022/2022-07-16_day-43-the-windy-city-is-dodge-city-ks.md) — Great Bend, KS → Dodge City, KS
Rode 87.6 miles into a strong headwind from Great Bend to Dodge City KS, past wind farms and grain elevators. The four riders drafted in a tight line about 5 feet apart, rotating the lead every 30 minutes. Dinner was pizza and local beer at the Dodge City Brewery with hosts Lindy and Warren.
- 🏠 Slept: Hosts Lindy and Warren, Dodge City KS
- 📌 Strong headwind on Kansas prairie: drafting in a tight group (about 5 ft apart) made the wind manageable. The leader pulls about 30 minutes, then drops to the back. It needs attention to spacing and braking. *(2022)*
- 📌 Dodge City was a rough Wild West town, now a tame downtown with a pizzeria and brewery worth visiting. *(2022)*
- 📌 Grain towers were useful distance markers on the flat route. They signal a gas station or town ahead. *(2022)*

### 2022-07-16 — [Day 44: And Then There Were Three](../2022/2022-07-16_day-44-and-then-there-were-three.md) — Dodge City, KS → Lakin, KS
Rode 49.8 miles from Dodge City to Lakin KS with only three riders, shuttling the last 20 miles to a motel to avoid heat and wind. Host Lindy chased the team down to return four water bottles left behind. Lunch was El Salvadoran pupusas in Garden City KS.
- 🏠 Slept: Motel in Lakin KS
- 📌 No place to refill water between Dodge City and the lunch stop in Garden City. Start with full bottles and carry extras in jersey pockets if you run out of cages. *(2022)*
- 📌 Heat over 100 degrees by 1 pm in western Kansas. Leave as early as possible and plan to finish by early afternoon. *(2022)*
- 📌 Garden City KS has a large Latin American community. An El Salvadoran food truck there served pupusas and horchata. *(2022)*
- 📌 Team bedtime was about 8 pm with everything packed the night before for a 6 am start. *(2022)*
