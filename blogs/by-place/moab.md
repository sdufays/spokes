## 2026

### 2026-07-20 — [Day 50: 100 miles in 100 degrees](../2026/2026-07-20_day-50-100-miles-in-100-degrees.md) — Grand Junction, CO → Moab, UT
Hot ride of 100+ degrees from Grand Junction toward Moab: the team ran out of ice, a driver had to drive back to Grand Junction for more, and riders had mild heat exhaustion. Riders swam in the Colorado River, a storm forced a turn back, and bikes were locked at a campground to finish the next morning. The route file listed 122.5 miles, but the actual ride was 87.9 miles.
- 🏠 Slept: Moab, UT (team stayed in Moab, see next day)
- 📌 No ice at any of 5 gas stations within 30 miles on the Grand Junction to Moab route. Carry or pre-buy ice before the 30-mile gap. *(2026)*
- 📌 Shade is scarce past the Colorado National Monument cliffs; the first shade was a bridge about 60 miles later. Carry extra water for the 28-mile exposed stretch. *(2026)*
- 📌 Some pickup drivers deliberately tried to run riders off the road on this route. Unintentional drivers are also a real risk. *(2026)*
- 📌 Sandy, uneven bike path near the Utah welcome sign on I-70 is not safe for a vehicle. A rental minivan with about 3 inches of clearance struggled there. *(2026)*
- 📌 Gravel road detour near I-70 in Utah filled with dust from passing vehicles for over 30 minutes. Wait it out or avoid it. *(2026)*
- 📌 Colorado River swim near Moab: strong current and loose rock bed make it hard to stay still. Ride back in the dark only if the road is low traffic. *(2026)*

### 2026-07-21 — [Day 51: does it count as a rest day if we have to bike?](../2026/2026-07-21_day-51-we-have-to-bike-on-a-rest-day.md) — Hittle Bottom Campground, UT → Moab, UT

### 2026-07-22 — [Day 52: I Spy 8 Breathtaking Arches](../2026/2026-07-22_day-52-i-spy-8-breathtaking-arches.md) — Moab, UT → Moab, UT
Rest day in Moab. The team visited Arches National Park, hiking Delicate Arch (a hat blew off the trail and was rescued) and Double Arch, then bought metal water bottles at a Moab gear shop after plastic insulated bottles melted their ice. They ate a second burger at Milt's and bought matching shirts.
- 🏠 Slept: Airbnb in Moab, UT
- 📌 Delicate Arch is a 3-mile round trip with switchbacks and exposed slickrock. Go early before the heat, and secure hats, since wind blew one off the trail. *(2026)*
- 📌 Metal water bottles beat plastic insulated ones in heat; plastic kept ice about 5 minutes. The first Moab shop had only small sizes; the second had full size. *(2026)*
- 📌 Double Arch is a 0.6 mile path with shade and climbing inside the arch. *(2026)*


## 2024

### 2024-08-03 — [Day 48: Yay Utah](../2024/2024-08-03_day-48-yay-utah.md) — Grand Junction, CO → Moab, UT
Rode 106 miles (the post lists 106/127) from Grand Junction, CO to Moab, UT along the Colorado River canyons; the border was marked by gravel, three riders had flats within 15 minutes, and one rider had heat exhaustion in the last 10 miles.
- 🏠 Slept: Camped on the ground in Moab, UT (site not named)
- 📌 Grand Junction to Fruita is a paved Colorado River bike path, the best bike path of the trip; Fruita is very bike-friendly for a coffee stop. *(2024)*
- 📌 Sand-dune road from Fruita into Utah turns from pavement to gravel at the state line; expect flats (three riders had them within 15 minutes). *(2024)*
- 📌 UT-128 canyons along the Colorado River are very scenic; the last 10 miles into Moab are hot, so stop at JJ's Mercantile for cold drinks. *(2024)*
- 📌 Cisco, UT is a ghost town with a half-buried car and alien statues; the van waited there. *(2024)*

### 2024-08-05 — [Day 49: Delicate Knees at Delicate Arch](../2024/2024-08-05_day-49-delicate-knees-at-delicate-arch.md) — Moab, UT → Moab, UT
Rest day in Moab after a 100-mile day with six flats. The team cooled off in the Colorado River, got frozen yogurt, toured Arches, and hiked to Delicate Arch.

### 2024-08-07 — [Day 50: Sunsets and Moonrises](../2024/2024-08-07_day-50-sunsets-and-moonrises.md) — Moab, UT → Wingate Campground, UT
Ran errands in Moab (bike shop, supplies, library), then rode an evening 34-mile route to Wingate Campground and raced sunset without a front light.
- 🏠 Slept: Wingate Campground, Dead Horse State Park, UT
- 📌 Moab: Rim Cyclery checks bikes and sells water bottles, cages and spare tubes. Stock up on spare tubes before the desert. *(2024)*
- 📌 Interstate shoulders were wide and legal on the sections they rode, but debris caused about 10x more flats. Carry tubes. *(2024)*
- 📌 The evening ride from Moab into Dead Horse State Park ends in dusk. Bring a front light; one rider forgot theirs and barely made it before dark. *(2024)*

### 2022-08-06 — [Day 60: Today Was ...... MEH](../2022/2022-08-06_day-60-today-was-meh.md) — Moab, UT → Green River State Park, UT
Left Moab on a bike path, then hit one of the worst shoulders of the trip, with trucks, RVs and cars passing every minute. The team called Robert to shuttle them to the end of that road. The rest of the 52-mile day was smooth and they camped at Green River State Park.
- 🏠 Slept: Green River State Park campsite, UT
- 📌 Road out of Moab: the shoulder was too narrow for bikes, with heavy traffic. The team shuttled the worst stretch. *(2022)*
- 📌 The bike path out of Moab is pleasant, but it ends at the bad road. Plan for a shuttle or an alternative route there. *(2022)*
