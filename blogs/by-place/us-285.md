### 2024-07-22 — [Day 41: Mother Infrastructure](../2024/2024-07-22_day-41-mother-infrastructure.md) — Denver, CO → Kenosha Campground, CO
Tried to ride US-285 out of Denver and then a ten-mile Conifer climb; the 285 was too scary (one rider nearly hit by a truck on a downhill), so five riders shuttled to the Kenosha Campground, CO (the post lists 44.8 miles and 4,196 ft ridden against 74.6 miles and 7,556 ft planned).
- 🏠 Slept: Kenosha Campground, CO
- 📌 US-285 out of Denver: busy, with exit-only lanes to cross and a narrow shoulder on steep downhill curves; trucks passed within inches. The team recommends avoiding it. *(2024)*
- 📌 The Conifer climb (about 10 miles, High Grade Road section) is steep and hot; a house on the route sets out water for cyclists. *(2024)*
- 📌 Carry a spare derailleur hanger; this rider's was bent three times on the climbs. *(2024)*
- 📌 Denver's bike trails are busy and well built, with roundabouts and bike lanes. *(2024)*
- 📌 Kenosha Campground (CO) has no showers; the team used wipes. *(2024)*

### 2024-07-24 — [Day 42: Reckoning with Mortality](../2024/2024-07-24_day-42-reckoning-with-mortality.md) — Kenosha Campground, CO → Buena Vista, CO
Ride from Kenosha Campground to Buena Vista, CO (65.3 miles): gravel on Elkhorn Road was too loose so the riders turned back, the US-285 descents were terrifying, and after a flat fix and a wait at the CO-9 junction the group kept riding the 285 to Buena Vista.
- 📌 US-285 steep descent right out of the campground (near -10 percent grade), narrow shoulder, heavy traffic on the curves: avoid. *(2024)*
- 📌 Elkhorn Road (dirt per Google Street View) was 20 miles of loose gravel and dust; a driver confirmed the rest was the same. Check current conditions with locals, not satellite photos. *(2024)*
- 📌 The US-285 stretch between Fairplay and the US-24 junction has the widest shoulders on that road. *(2024)*
- 📌 Fairplay, CO: lunch with Palisade peaches and bowls was a good stop. *(2024)*
- 📌 A hand pump only reached about 40 psi; for curvy mountain descents, have support bring a floor pump. *(2024)*
- 📌 Cars graze riders on the last stretch of US-24 into Buena Vista; shuttle that part if the team is not comfortable. *(2024)*
