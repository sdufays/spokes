### 2024-08-03 — [Day 48: Yay Utah](../2024/2024-08-03_day-48-yay-utah.md) — Grand Junction, CO → Moab, UT
Rode 106 miles (the post lists 106/127) from Grand Junction, CO to Moab, UT along the Colorado River canyons; the border was marked by gravel, three riders had flats within 15 minutes, and one rider had heat exhaustion in the last 10 miles.
- 🏠 Slept: Camped on the ground in Moab, UT (site not named)

- 📌 Sand-dune road from Fruita into Utah turns from pavement to gravel at the state line; expect flats (three riders had them within 15 minutes). *(2024)*
- 📌 UT-128 canyons along the Colorado River are very scenic; the last 10 miles into Moab are hot, so stop at JJ's Mercantile for cold drinks. *(2024)*
