### 2024-09-05 — [Day 73: 28 Things You Have to Do Before We Both Die](../2024/2024-09-05_day-73-28-things-you-have-to-do-before-we-both-die.md) — Sacramento, CA → Benicia, CA
Sacramento to Benicia (72 miles). A non-hitch bike rack fell off the minivan on a speed bump and dragged two bikes. The rack was reattached and the bikes went to a shop. The riders also dealt with detours and flats. The rest of the post is a 28-item to-do list by state.
- 📌 Support van bike rack: the minivan has no hitch, so the non-hitch rack fell off over a speed bump and dragged bikes. A hitch-mounted rack is a safer choice for a long trip. *(2024)*
- 📌 A Sacramento-area bike shop replaced one wheel and repaired a derailleur. It could not fix a shredded tire and sanded rims on another bike, and ran out of time. *(2024)*
- 📌 Kentucky: aggressive dogs. Tactics that worked were sprinting, walking the bike, and barking back. *(2024)*
- 📌 Illinois: a campsite next to Rend Lake, with a warm swim. *(2024)*
- 📌 Colorado: the CR 742 descent from the Continental Divide, near Gunnison, was the team's favorite scenery. *(2024)*
- 📌 A detour near a chemical plant led police to be called on a rider. Be cautious near industrial sites. *(2024)*
