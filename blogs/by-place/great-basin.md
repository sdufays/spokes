### 2026-08-02 — [Day 63: Romanticizing the Mundane](../2026/2026-08-02_day-63-romanticizing-the-mundane.md) — Milford, UT → Baker, NV

- 📌 Great Basin Visitor Center hosts an evening stargazing talk with telescopes, starting about 8 pm. *(2026)*
