### 2022-07-11 — [Day 34: Fourth of July and Day 35: Our First Century](../2022/2022-07-11_day-34-fourth-of-july.md) — Lake of the Ozarks, MO → Springfield, MO
Fourth of July rest day at Lake of the Ozarks, MO: caving at Ozark Caverns to escape the heat, then a swim at the lake. The next day was the first century, 105 miles from the lake to Springfield, MO (the post says MI). A bumpy gravel road, a broken pump and long rolling hills pushed them to a 9 PM arrival at barber and cyclist Greg's home.
- 🏠 Slept: Lake of the Ozarks campground (July 4); Greg's home, Springfield MO (July 5)
- 📌 Early-July heat in Missouri came with a severe heat warning. Caves held at 56 F were an effective break. Caves need a 2-hour decontamination before you go into another cave (white-nose fungus). *(2022)*
- 📌 The century started at 7 AM and ended at 9 PM, about 8 hours of riding. Start earlier to beat the heat. *(2022)*
- 📌 A gravel road out of the lake shook the bikes, so the team let air out of the tires. The pump then broke, and the SAG wagon brought a replacement. Carry a backup pump. *(2022)*
