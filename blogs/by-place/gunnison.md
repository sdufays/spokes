### 2024-07-24 — [Day 44: Spokes vs the Continental Divide](../2024/2024-07-24_day-44-spokes-vs-the-continental-divide.md) — Buena Vista, CO → Gunnison, CO
Rode 68.6 miles from Buena Vista to Gunnison, CO over a 20-mile continuous climb to the Continental Divide, then a fast descent along the Taylor River; hosts Thalia and Jeff cooked dinner.
- 🏠 Slept: Hosts Thalia and Jeff's home, Gunnison, CO
- 📌 Continental Divide from Buena Vista: about 20 miles of continuous uphill with a steep final switchback; a high-grade climb in low gear. *(2024)*
- 📌 The summit is cold and gusty with snow patches; riders with only desert-weather clothing were under-dressed. Carry a warm layer in the van or on the bike. *(2024)*
- 📌 Taylor River descent from the Divide to Gunnison is fast (averaged near 30 mph over 5 miles) with hairpins; one rider scraped a knee on a hairpin. *(2024)*
- 📌 The reservoir lunch stop on the Divide descent is a good break. *(2024)*
