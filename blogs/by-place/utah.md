### 2026-07-20 — [Day 50: 100 miles in 100 degrees](../2026/2026-07-20_day-50-100-miles-in-100-degrees.md) — Grand Junction, CO → Moab, UT
Hot ride of 100+ degrees from Grand Junction toward Moab: the team ran out of ice, a driver had to drive back to Grand Junction for more, and riders had mild heat exhaustion. Riders swam in the Colorado River, a storm forced a turn back, and bikes were locked at a campground to finish the next morning. The route file listed 122.5 miles, but the actual ride was 87.9 miles.
- 🏠 Slept: Moab, UT (team stayed in Moab, see next day)
- 📌 No ice at any of 5 gas stations within 30 miles on the Grand Junction to Moab route. Carry or pre-buy ice before the 30-mile gap. *(2026)*
- 📌 Shade is scarce past the Colorado National Monument cliffs; the first shade was a bridge about 60 miles later. Carry extra water for the 28-mile exposed stretch. *(2026)*
- 📌 Some pickup drivers deliberately tried to run riders off the road on this route. Unintentional drivers are also a real risk. *(2026)*
- 📌 Sandy, uneven bike path near the Utah welcome sign on I-70 is not safe for a vehicle. A rental minivan with about 3 inches of clearance struggled there. *(2026)*
- 📌 Gravel road detour near I-70 in Utah filled with dust from passing vehicles for over 30 minutes. Wait it out or avoid it. *(2026)*
- 📌 Colorado River swim near Moab: strong current and loose rock bed make it hard to stay still. Ride back in the dark only if the road is low traffic. *(2026)*
