### 2025-06-23 — [Day 16: Kentucky Fried Chicken](../2025/2025-06-23_day-16-kentucky-fried-chicken.md) — Liberty, KY → Mammoth Cave National Park, KY
Liberty, KY to Mammoth Cave National Park (88.6 miles, 5,465 ft), the longest ride so far. Ruth was sick and rode in the van. Riders bought a bucket of Lee's Famous Chicken at a rest stop, a bike shop traded a pump for free, and the team had a campfire dinner at the Mammoth Cave campground.
- 🏠 Slept: Mammoth Cave National Park campground
- 📌 A rural stream crossing on this route is not rideable. Dismount and walk your bike across. *(2025)*
- 📌 Greta took a wrong turn and added 4 miles. Confirm turns when riders split up. *(2025)*
- 📌 Save A Lot did not have marshmallows, foil, or firewood. Buy them in a bigger store. *(2025)*
- 📌 Firewood was available at the Mammoth Cave campground. *(2025)*
- 📌 Lee's Famous Chicken bucket at a Campbellsville rest stop was the team's favorite rest stop. Plan a food stop there. *(2025)*

### 2025-06-24 — [Day 17: BUC-EES](../2025/2025-06-24_day-17-buc-ees.md) — Mammoth Cave National Park, KY → Bowling Green, KY
Short day from Mammoth Cave to Bowling Green (32.7 miles, 1,342 ft). The team took a self-guided Mammoth Cave tour, rode to the Buc-ee's rest stop in Smith's Grove, KY, then rode the last 17 miles into Bowling Green in rain.
- 🏠 Slept: Airbnb in Bowling Green, KY (provided by learning festival partners)
- 📌 Book a guided Mammoth Cave tour ahead. The self-guided line wasted time. *(2025)*
- 📌 Buc-ee's in Smith's Grove, KY is a full-service rest stop with clean restrooms, food, and a big bike rack area. The team loved it. *(2025)*
- 📌 Flash rain hit about 3 miles before Bowling Green. Carry rain gear on afternoon rides. *(2025)*
